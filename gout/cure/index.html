Part 5 · Living, care & the future

Toward a cure

Gout is curable in one precise sense: keep serum urate low for long enough and the urate crystals dissolve. This module shows what that takes, where the new drugs intercept the urate pathway and how strong the evidence is for each, and why the cure so rarely reaches people. Every route carries an evidence label.

Before treatment

Crystals sit in the joint

Gout is a disease of monosodium urate crystal deposits, and those deposits are reversible: long-term urate-lowering therapy dissolves them and so prevents flares and tophi. Plasma is saturated with urate at about 6.8 mg/dL (405 µmol/L). 84,85,250

In plain wordsAbove a certain level, urate comes out of solution as tiny needles. Bring the level down and the needles can melt back.
Exam hookGout is the one common arthritis with a removable cause: the crystal. 85

Remission: how often, and how soon

Show

Remission

Defined, and reached over years

Preliminary criteria from 2016 set five domains: serum urate below 0.36 mmol/L, no flares, no tophi, pain below 2/10 and patient global below 2/10, each measured at least twice; the experts split between a 6-month and a 12-month window. The G-CAN definition (2025/2026) keeps three: 12 months with no flares, no subcutaneous tophi and urate below 6 mg/dL. It is called clinical remission because no domain measures complete crystal dissolution. 89,394

In the CARES trial population, remission rose from 37.4% at year 1 to 63.1% at year 6. In NOR-Gout it was 7.7%, 45.4% and 58.6% at years 1, 2 and 5. In a Dutch pragmatic trial, treat-to-target gave 39.4% against 24.0% with symptom-driven care in months 18–24. 265,393,416

Exam hookRemission is not proof of a crystal-free joint: DECT still found urate deposits in 44% of people meeting the preliminary criteria. 83
In plain wordsRemission means a whole year with no flares, no lumps and a low urate. Most people get there, but it usually takes two years or more.

2 · Where new drugs intercept the pathway

Urate is made from purines by xanthine oxidase, leaves through the kidney, and forms crystals above saturation; crystals then switch on NLRP3 and IL-1β. Pick a route to see where it acts. Gold dots are urate; past the crystals they turn into the inflammatory signal. 39,84,251

Stage 1 · The supply

Purines in

Urate is the end product of purine breakdown in humans. Production was estimated at 591 mg a day in 123 people on haemodialysis without urine output, the first new estimate in over 50 years. 39,303

In plain wordsUrate comes from breaking down purines, which are in food and in every cell the body replaces.
Exam hookDiet explains at most 0.3% of the variation in serum urate; common genetic variation explains 23.9%. 245

Only three routes remove urate. Production, excretion and breakdown lower the urate pool, so they can dissolve crystals. NLRP3 and IL-1 blockers treat or prevent flares; they do not remove crystals. 84,85,251

3 · The implementation gap

The cure exists in principle; it rarely arrives. Among 94,759 people with new gout in Catalan primary care, followed for a median 5.44 years, 37.30% never received urate-lowering therapy and only 12.40% had good control. When care is organised, the gap closes. 95,318

Each square is 1% of the cohort. The two panels come from different studies and measures, labelled on each.

Real-world care

Most people never reach the target

In Catalonia, 37.30% of people with incident gout never received urate-lowering therapy and 12.40% kept urate below 6 mg/dL for at least 80% of follow-up, with no improvement from 2012 to 2023. In the UK in 2012, only 37.63% of people consulting for gout received urate-lowering therapy, and adherence was 39.66%. 223,318

Exam hookThe bottleneck is implementation, not chemistry: 12.4% good control in primary care. 318
In plain wordsThe medicines that can dissolve crystals already exist. Most people with gout just never get them, or never get the dose high enough.

Open questions

These are gaps the literature itself leaves open. Each one is a place where a study could change the picture.

01 · Lifelong treatment, or a holiday once crystals clear?

ACR 2020 conditionally favours indefinite urate-lowering therapy, at very low certainty. Perez-Ruiz proposed about 5 years of treatment then a pause guided by urate. GO TEST Finale (at least 278 people in remission) and STING (NCT06956885, about 450, not yet recruiting) are testing it. 135,283,310,315

02 · Is clinical remission the same as a crystal-free joint?

Of people meeting remission criteria, 44% still had DECT urate deposits. G-CAN calls its definition "clinical" for this reason, and no validated imaging-remission endpoint exists. 83,394

03 · Why do only some people with high urate form crystals?

About half of people with urate at or above 10 mg/dL stayed gout-free for 15 years. Silent deposits appear in 24% on DECT and 38.7% on ultrasound. The TIGER cohort is following people with asymptomatic hyperuricaemia to see who progresses. 79,82,387

04 · Should asymptomatic hyperuricaemia be treated?

FREED reported fewer composite events with febuxostat, driven by kidney impairment; CKD-FIX and PERL found no kidney benefit from allopurinol; ACR advises against routine treatment. 11,96,135,216

05 · Can new URAT1 and xanthine oxidase inhibitors beat allopurinol titrated to target?

Dotinurad 4 mg beat febuxostat 40 mg, a low febuxostat dose. EURELIA 2 (allopurinol up to 800 mg) and the dotinurad-versus-allopurinol trial are unreported or recruiting. 275,284,388

06 · Tolerance induction or methotrexate for uricase?

NASP reached 51% response against 8% on placebo with infusion reactions in 4%; pegloticase plus methotrexate reached 71.0% with 4.2%. The trials used different comparators, and there is no head-to-head trial. 15,27

07 · Oral NLRP3 inhibitors or IL-1 antibodies for flares?

Canakinumab and firsekibart have phase 3 and regulatory evidence. Oral NLRP3 inhibition has only an open-label phase 2a; the placebo-controlled dapansutrile trial is recruiting. 66,214,276,356

Where this leaves us

Gout already has a cure in the sense that matters most: urate held below target dissolves the crystals that cause it, and the drugs to do that have existed since 1966. The pipeline adds more potent and more convenient ways to lower urate, and better ways to stop a flare, but none of them changes the basic rule that crystals go only when urate stays low. 85,101,309

The larger gap is delivery. In primary care most people never reach the target, while organised nurse-led care gets almost everyone there with the same drugs. 95,318

In plain wordsThe cure for gout is keeping urate low for years. The new medicines may make that easier. The hard part is still getting the right dose to every person who needs it. This page is not advice about anyone's treatment.
