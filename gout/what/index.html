Part 1 · The disease

What gout is

Gout is a long-term disease of urate crystal deposits that shows itself as sudden, very painful flares, most often in the big toe. 84,85,105 High urate comes first, crystals form silently, flares follow, and years later some people develop tophi. Below: how common it is, the four stages, and where it strikes.

US adults

3.9%

NHANES 2015–2016, 5,467 adults: 3.9% said a doctor had diagnosed gout, about 9.2 million people; 5.2% of men and 2.7% of women. About 20% of both men and women had hyperuricaemia. Only 33% of people with gout were taking urate-lowering therapy (2007–2014). 41 Self-report overcalls gout: "self-report of gout or urate-lowering therapy" had 82% sensitivity but 72% specificity against crystal proof. 80

In plain wordsGout is common, and it is not spread evenly. Men get it about three times as often as women, and some peoples, such as Pasifika and Māori in New Zealand, have much higher rates. 146,405

The four stages

Dalbeth and Stamp proposed four stages in 2014. Not everyone moves through all of them, and lowering urate can stop the march at any point. Press play to watch one joint go from stage A to stage D. 77,85

Stage A

High urate, no crystals

Serum urate is high, but no crystals have formed and there are no symptoms. 77 This is common: about 20% of US men and women have hyperuricaemia, against 3.9% with gout. 41 Over 15 years, gout developed in 1.1% of people with baseline urate below 6 mg/dL (360 µmol/L) and in 49% at 10 mg/dL (≈ 595 µmol/L) or more. 82 The ACR 2020 guideline conditionally recommends against urate-lowering drugs at this stage. 135

In plain wordsHigh uric acid in the blood is not gout. Many people with high uric acid never have a flare.
Exam hookHyperuricaemia ≠ gout. 15-year gout incidence: 1.1% below 6 mg/dL, 49% at ≥ 10 mg/dL. 82

Where flares strike

In plain wordsGout usually starts in one joint of the foot or leg, most often the big toe. Later it can reach the hands, and lumps of crystals (tophi) can form at the elbow and ear. 84,290,337

Big toe (first MTP) 59–74%

Classic podagra at the first presentation fell from 74% (1989–1992) to 59% (2009–2010) in Olmsted County, Minnesota. 105 In the SUGAR study of crystal-proven gout in rheumatology clinics in 16 countries, first-MTP involvement was the commonest joint pattern, 39.4% of cases. 403 Why the foot: a cooler joint holds less urate in solution. In vitro, a 2 °C drop from 37 °C to 35 °C lowers solubility from 6.8 to 6.0 mg/dL. 249 Lower pH, minor trauma and osteoarthritis of the joint may add to it Hypothesis 337

Gout is crystals in joints, not just high uric acid. Where the urate comes from is in Where urate comes from; how it turns into crystals is in Crystals vs serum urate.

Words matter
  • Say gout flare, not "attack". The G-CAN consensus also advises against "chronic gout". 31
  • Calling gout "urate crystal arthritis" made the public see it as less under personal control and better suited to long-term medical treatment. Early evidence 63
