## Findings

### cure · NASP (SEL-212): the FDA's complete response letter is now on the public record
- The FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research sent Swedish Orphan Biovitrum a complete response letter for BLA 761438, "sirolimus for injectable suspension; pegadricase-sogo for injection". The letter is dated 26 June 2026 and says the FDA "cannot approve this application in its present form". The application was dated and received on 27 June 2025. (Established: regulatory record) [[FDA-CRL-BLA761438]]
- The published letter is redacted. The deficiency headings that can be read are "Product quality (pegadricase-sogo)", whose items are redacted in full, and "Facility inspections": deficiencies were conveyed to a facility after a CGMP and pre-license inspection, and to another after a Remote Regulatory Assessment. (Established: regulatory record) [[FDA-CRL-BLA761438]]
- The letter lists clinical pharmacology comments (CYP3A4 inhibitor dosing, a hepatic impairment study, population pharmacokinetic models) under "Additional comments", which it says "are not approvability issues". No clinical efficacy or safety deficiency can be read in the unredacted text. [[FDA-CRL-BLA761438]]
- The sponsor has one year from the date of the letter to resubmit or take another action under 21 CFR 601.3(b). [[FDA-CRL-BLA761438]]
- Context: the site already reports the phase 3 DISSOLVE results (51% and 43% response against 8% on placebo) and described NASP as "not approved" [[Baraf2026]]. That stays correct; this pass adds the regulatory reason and date from the primary record.

### Exam hooks
- NASP (pegadricase with nanoencapsulated sirolimus) had positive phase 3 trials but is not approved: the FDA issued a complete response letter on 26 June 2026, and the readable deficiencies concern product quality and manufacturing facilities. [[Baraf2026,FDA-CRL-BLA761438]]

### In plain words
- A newer uricase infusion called NASP worked in its main trials, but the US regulator has not approved it. In June 2026 the FDA asked the company to fix problems with how the medicine is made and with the factories before it can be considered again.

## Open questions and controversies
- Will NASP be resubmitted, and when? The letter gives one year to respond [[FDA-CRL-BLA761438]]. Status: open; no resubmission is on the public record.
