## Findings

### links · GLP-1 receptor agonists, tirzepatide and urate
- A systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD420261468511; PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL and ClinicalTrials.gov searched through July 2026) pooled 18 RCTs of at least 12 weeks (77 942 participants) of GLP-1 receptor agonists and the dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist tirzepatide. (Early evidence) [[Lee2026]]
- Incretin-based therapy lowered serum uric acid against control: weighted mean difference −0.44 mg/dL (95% CI −0.52 to −0.35) [≈ −26 µmol/L, converted]. Tirzepatide lowered it more than GLP-1 agonists alone (−0.62 vs −0.35 mg/dL, p = 0.001). (Early evidence) [[Lee2026]]
- Gout flares were less common (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.53–0.72), and so was new hyperuricaemia (OR 0.61, 95% CI 0.53–0.70). (Early evidence) [[Lee2026]]
- Weight loss moderated the urate fall (β = −0.041 mg/dL per kg, p < 0.001) and explained 84.7% of between-trial heterogeneity. A weight-independent intercept of −0.19 mg/dL remained. [[Lee2026]]
- The authors rate GRADE certainty as high for urate and moderate for gout flares (small-study effects) and for hyperuricaemia incidence. They say the clinical relevance, and the mechanism of any weight-independent part, "remains uncertain" and needs dedicated trials. [[Lee2026]]
- Context: the site already cites a cohort in which new gout was less common on SGLT2 inhibitors than on GLP-1 agonists (HR 0.64) [[Fralick2020]]. The two findings are compatible: both classes are linked to lower urate against control, and the SGLT2 effect on urate is larger (about −0.63 mg/dL) [[Zhao2018]].

### Exam hooks
- GLP-1 receptor agonists and tirzepatide lower serum urate modestly (about 0.44 mg/dL across 18 RCTs), mostly in step with weight loss. They are not urate-lowering therapy for gout. [[Lee2026]]

### In plain words
- Weight-loss and diabetes injections such as semaglutide and tirzepatide lower urate a little, and people taking them in trials had fewer gout flares. Most of this seems to come from losing weight. They do not replace gout medicines.

## Open questions and controversies
- Do GLP-1-based drugs lower flares in people who already have gout, and is any of the effect independent of weight loss? Lee2026 pools trials in type 2 diabetes, obesity and CKD, not gout trials [[Lee2026]]. Status: open.
