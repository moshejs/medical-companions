Where urate comes from

Urate is the end product of purine breakdown in humans: xanthine oxidase makes it, and the kidneys and gut clear it, about two thirds and one third. 39,302 In most people with gout the problem is the way out, not the way in. Press play to follow a purine from a cell to the urine.
