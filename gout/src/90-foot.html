Gout Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on gout, made for health-science study and for patient understanding.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should change a treatment without your own clinician. Guideline doses are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one disease each. Also there: FMF Companion, whose colchicine and inflammasome modules this guide builds on, Alzheimer's Companion, and Obesity Companion, on a condition that raises urate and the risk of gout.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
