Part 3 · Lowering urate

How urate-lowering drugs work

There are three ways to lower urate: make less of it, pass more of it out, or break it down. Press play to follow urate from purines to the blood and out through the kidney and gut, then pick a drug to light up the step it blocks.

Schematic. Dots are urate (gold), its precursors (muted) and allantoin (indigo); they are not to scale. The gauge applies the average change reported in one study of each drug to an illustrative starting urate of 8.5 mg/dL [about 506 µmol/L]. The studies differ in people, doses and timing, so the bars are not a head-to-head comparison.

Where urate goes. Xanthine oxidoreductase makes urate in two steps, hypoxanthine to xanthine and xanthine to uric acid. Humans have no working uricase, so urate is the end of the line. [[Chen2016,Oda2002]] About two thirds is excreted by the kidney and one third by the gut, and at least 90% of the urate filtered by the kidney is reabsorbed. [[Ohashi2023,Chen2016]] Most people with gout under-excrete urate through the kidney. [[PerezRuiz2002Renal,So2010]]

Which route first. ACR 2020 strongly recommends allopurinol as the first-line drug, including in CKD stage ≥3. It strongly prefers allopurinol or febuxostat over probenecid in CKD stage ≥3. It recommends pegloticase only when the other drugs have failed and flares or tophi persist, and strongly recommends against it as a first-line drug. [[FitzGerald2020]]

Why allopurinol depends on the kidney

23.3 hOxypurinol, the metabolite that does most of the work, has a half-life of 23.3 ± 6.0 h with normal kidney function and is cleared almost entirely in urine. [[Day2007]] Its clearance falls with creatinine clearance, which is why kidney disease raises exposure. Hande 1984 turned that into creatinine-clearance-based dosing after 78 cases of a severe toxicity syndrome. [[Hande1984]]

Later trials raised the dose above that cap to reach target, including in renal impairment. [[Stamp2011,Stamp2017RCT]] See Treat to target.

The enzyme humans lost

Most mammals have uricase, which turns urate into allantoin, about 100 times more soluble. [[Chen2016,So2010]] In humans, chimpanzees and orangutans a shared nonsense mutation silenced the gene, and gibbons lost it separately. [[Wu1992,Oda2002]] Resurrected ancestral enzymes show activity falling steadily along the primate line before the gene was switched off. [[Kratzer2014]]

Pegloticase puts a version of that enzyme back, by infusion. [[FDA-Krystexxa]]

When drug treatment starts

  • ACR 2020 strongly recommends urate-lowering therapy with ≥1 tophus, radiographic damage from gout, or ≥2 flares a year. [[FitzGerald2020]]
  • It conditionally recommends against it after a first flare, unless that flare comes with CKD stage ≥3, serum urate >9 mg/dL [>535 µmol/L] or kidney stones. [[FitzGerald2020]]
  • It conditionally recommends against drugs for asymptomatic hyperuricaemia; Japan's guideline considers them from 8–9 mg/dL. [[FitzGerald2020,Hisatome2021]]
  • EULAR 2016: consider it from the first presentation. [[Richette2017]]

Why some people respond less Established

ABCG2 exports urate in the kidney and gut. [[Matsuo2009,HalperinKuhns2021]] Its common Q141K variant (rs2231142) predicts serum urate staying ≥6 mg/dL despite more than 300 mg/day of allopurinol in adherent people (OR 2.71, 95% CI 1.70–4.48; replication meta-analysis OR 2.43). [[Roberts2017,Wallace2018]] A genome-wide study found that ABCG2 also transports allopurinol. [[Wen2015]]

In the pipeline Early evidence

New xanthine oxidase inhibitors and URAT1 inhibitors are in trials. Tigulixostat 200 mg brought 62.2% below 5 mg/dL at week 12 against 2.9% on placebo. [[Terkeltaub2023]] In China, dotinurad 4 mg brought 73.6% to ≤6 mg/dL at week 24 against 38.1% on febuxostat 40 mg. [[Sun2025]] Two placebo-controlled phase 3 trials of AR882 have completed. [[NCT06846515,NCT06439602]] More in Toward a cure.
