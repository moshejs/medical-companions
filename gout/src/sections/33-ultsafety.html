Part 3 · Lowering urate

Safety of urate-lowering drugs

Allopurinol is safe for most people, but a rare skin reaction can kill, and a gene test lowers that risk in some ancestries. Explore the numbers behind the reaction, the two big febuxostat heart trials, the interactions that matter and pegloticase's infusion rules.

This is a study aid, not a safety check for any person. Interactions and contraindications are reported as the US labels, UK and EU SmPCs, CPIC and ACR 2020 publish them. Decisions about a particular medicine belong with the prescriber. [[FDA-Zyloprim,FDA-Uloric,SmPC-Allopurinol-UK,EMA-Adenuric,CPIC-HLAB-Allopurinol,FitzGerald2020]]

Warning signs, as the labels state them. Labels say to stop allopurinol at the first sign of rash, and a person who has had a severe reaction must not take it again. [[FDA-Zyloprim,SmPC-Allopurinol-UK]] About 90% of hypersensitivity reactions begin within 60 days of starting. [[Ramasamy2013]]

How strong the gene link is

OR 580.3In Han Chinese, HLA-B*58:01 was present in all 51 people with allopurinol severe cutaneous reactions, against 15% of tolerant users. [[Hung2005PNAS]]

In Thailand: 100% (27/27) of Stevens–Johnson/TEN cases vs 12.96% of tolerant controls (OR 348.3). Sensitivity 100%, specificity 87%; at 0.2% prevalence the positive predictive value is only 1.52% and the negative predictive value 100%. [[Tassaneeyakul2009]]

What CPIC and ACR say

  • CPIC: allopurinol is contraindicated in HLA-B*58:01 carriers (strong); non-carriers follow standard dosing (strong). The 2015 update left this unchanged. [[Hershfield2013,Saito2016,CPIC-HLAB-Allopurinol]]
  • ACR 2020: test before allopurinol in people of Southeast Asian descent (for example Han Chinese, Korean, Thai) and African American people (conditional), and not universally in others; testing was cost-effective (ICER <$109,000/QALY). [[FitzGerald2020]]
  • After a prior allergic reaction, desensitisation is conditionally recommended only when no other oral drug is possible (very low certainty). [[FitzGerald2020]]

Other risk factors

In US Medicaid, relative risk against white or Hispanic people was 3.00 in Black, 3.03 in Asian and 6.68 in Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander people. Female sex (2.5-fold), age ≥60 (1.7-fold), CKD (2.3-fold) and a starting dose >100 mg/day (1.9-fold) added risk; older women of high-risk ancestry had RR 12.25. [[Keller2018]]

In Europe, allopurinol was the commonest cause of SJS/TEN (adjusted OR 18), with risk confined to the first 8 weeks and far higher at ≥200 mg/day (OR 36 vs 3.0). [[Halevy2008]]

Who gets it, and how bad

21%of those hospitalised in US Medicaid died; the mean stay was 9.6 days. [[Keller2018]]

In 901 published cases (1950–2012), all-cause mortality was 14%, 48% had renal impairment and 45% took diuretics. [[Ramasamy2013]]

3 · Febuxostat and the heart: CARES vs FAST

View

Real trial results. CARES rates come from the US febuxostat label's account of the trial; FAST rates are on-treatment. The two trials measured different endpoints in different people, so the bars sit side by side, not on one scale of risk. [[White2018,FDA-Uloric,Mackenzie2020]]

CARES (US-led)

Signal of harm

6190 people with gout and major cardiovascular disease, median 32 months, non-inferiority margin HR 1.3. Major cardiovascular events: 10.8% vs 10.4% (HR 1.03), non-inferior. Cardiovascular death HR 1.34 (95% CI 1.03–1.73); all-cause death HR 1.22 (1.01–1.47). 56.6% stopped the trial drug and 45.0% stopped follow-up. [[White2018]]

FAST (UK, Denmark, Sweden)

No excess found

6128 people aged ≥60 with ≥1 cardiovascular risk factor, allopurinol first optimised to target, open label, median follow-up 1467 days. On-treatment primary endpoint 1.72 vs 2.05 per 100 patient-years (adjusted HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.70–1.03), non-inferior. Deaths 7.2% vs 8.6%. Only 6.2% vs 5.5% were lost from all follow-up. [[Mackenzie2020]]

What changed after CARES

  • February 2019: the FDA added a boxed warning, "Cardiovascular death", and limited febuxostat to people with an inadequate response to maximally titrated allopurinol, intolerance, or when allopurinol is not advisable. [[FDA-Uloric,FDA-DSC-Febuxostat2019]]
  • ACR 2020 conditionally recommends switching people on febuxostat with a history of cardiovascular disease or a new event to another drug when possible. [[FitzGerald2020]]
  • The EU SmPC cites both trials: more fatal cardiovascular events in CARES in people with major cardiovascular disease, none in FAST. [[EMA-Adenuric]]

Why the trials disagree Early evidence

CARES lost 45% of follow-up; FAST lost about 6% but was open label and enrolled people already tolerating allopurinol. [[White2018,Mackenzie2020]] In Japan, febuxostat mortality did not differ from placebo or allopurinol in FEATHER and FREED. [[Hisatome2021,Kojima2019]]

5 · Pegloticase: the infusion rules

G6PD first

Pegloticase is contraindicated in G6PD deficiency, because of haemolysis and methaemoglobinaemia; people at risk are screened before treatment. [[FDA-Krystexxa]]

Boxed warning and the stopping rule

Anaphylaxis and infusion reactions can happen with any infusion, usually within 2 hours. Serum urate is checked before every infusion, and treatment stops if it rises above 6 mg/dL, particularly after two consecutive values above 6. [[FDA-Krystexxa]]

Loss of the urate response came before 79% (44/56) of infusion reactions, so the pre-infusion urate works as a stand-in for the antibodies that cause them. [[Lipsky2014]]

Methotrexate changed the numbers

71.0% vs 38.5%responded at month 6 with methotrexate vs placebo in MIRROR, and infusion reactions fell to 4.2% (4/96) from 30.6% (15/49). [[Botson2023]]

Without methotrexate and without the stopping rule, anaphylaxis occurred in 6.5% on every-2-week dosing and infusion reactions in 26% vs 5% on placebo. [[FDA-Krystexxa]]

Flares at the start

74% (every 2 weeks) and 81% (every 4 weeks) flared in the first 3 months, against 51% on placebo, so prophylaxis is given for at least the first 6 months. [[FDA-Krystexxa]]

6 · The other drugs

Probenecid

"May not be effective" at GFR ≤30 mL/min; contraindicated with uric acid kidney stones. [[FDA-Probenecid]] ACR 2020 conditionally recommends against checking urinary uric acid or alkalinising the urine on a uricosuric. [[FitzGerald2020]]

Benzbromarone

Withdrawn by Sanofi-Synthélabo in 2003 after reports of liver toxicity; the estimated risk in Europe is about 1 in 17,000 patients, possibly higher in Japan. [[Lee2008]]

Lesinurad

Boxed warning: "risk of acute renal failure, more common when used without a xanthine oxidase inhibitor". [[FDA-Zurampic]] In CLEAR 2, creatinine rose ≥1.5× baseline in 5.9% (200 mg) and 15.0% (400 mg) vs 3.4% on allopurinol alone. [[Bardin2017]]

Allopurinol and the kidney

Oxypurinol is cleared almost entirely by the kidney, so its levels rise in CKD. [[Day2007,Hande1984]] Yet ACR 2020 still names allopurinol first line in CKD stage ≥3, started low and titrated. [[FitzGerald2020]]

7 · Pregnancy and breastfeeding, as labels state them

Allopurinol

UK SmPC: the safety evidence in pregnancy is "inadequate"; use only when there is no safer alternative. Breast milk contained 1.4 mg/L allopurinol and 53.7 mg/L oxipurinol at 300 mg/day, and use while breastfeeding "is not recommended". [[SmPC-Allopurinol-UK]] The US label reports animal data and a case report of malformations, and advises caution in nursing. [[FDA-Zyloprim]]

In 31 prospectively followed first-trimester exposures the major malformation rate was 3.7%; the authors advise caution in the first trimester. [[Hoeltzenbein2013]]

Febuxostat

US label: data are insufficient to inform risk, and nothing is known about human milk. [[FDA-Uloric]] EU SmPC: it "should not be used during pregnancy". [[EMA-Adenuric]]

Probenecid and pegloticase

Probenecid crosses the placenta and appears in cord blood. [[FDA-Probenecid]] Pegloticase has no adequate studies in pregnancy and should not be used while breastfeeding unless the benefit is clear. [[FDA-Krystexxa]]

Exam hookAllopurinol hypersensitivity: first 8 weeks; risk factors HLA-B*58:01, CKD, high starting dose. CARES: CV death HR 1.34, all-cause death HR 1.22; FAST HR 0.85. Never combine azathioprine with febuxostat (US: contraindicated). [[Halevy2008,Keller2018,Stamp2012,White2018,Mackenzie2020,FDA-Uloric]]
In plain wordsAllopurinol is safe for most people. If you get a rash, fever, or sore mouth or eyes after starting it, stop it and get medical help the same day: this is rare but serious, and almost always happens in the first two months. If your family comes from China, Korea or Thailand, or you are of African descent, a gene test before allopurinol is advised. [[FDA-Zyloprim,Ramasamy2013,FitzGerald2020]]
