Nursing care

What the literature says a nurse does for a person with gout: assess the joint, give the medicines safely, teach, and keep urate on target. Every figure here is quoted from guidelines, drug labels and trials, not from local practice. Where the literature is silent, this module says so.

ScopeThis describes what guidelines, drug labels and published studies say. It is not a care plan, and it does not replace your service's protocols, your scope of practice or the prescriber. Doses are quoted as the labels and guidelines publish them, never as instructions.
