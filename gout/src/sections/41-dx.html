Diagnosis & the ACR/EULAR score

Seeing urate crystals in joint fluid settles the diagnosis; without them, the 2015 ACR/EULAR criteria add up points, and 8 or more classifies as gout. Turn the compensator to see the crystals change colour, score a case in the calculator, then step through the diagnostic pathway.

These are classification criteria, built so that research studies enrol comparable patients. Their authors say they are not meant for making a diagnosis in clinic, and that joint or tophus aspiration remains essential to establish one. [[Neogi2015]] There are no exclusion criteria, because gout can coexist with other diseases and microscopy can miss crystals. [[Neogi2015]]

Step 2 · Sufficient criterion

If yes, the person is classified as having gout and nothing is scored. [[Neogi2015]]

Step 3 · Clinical: joint pattern (ever)

First MTP, ankle or midfoot count only in a mono- or oligoarticular episode; in a polyarticular presentation they are not specific, as they also occur in rheumatoid arthritis. [[Neogi2015]]

Clinical: features of an episode (ever)

One point each, up to 3, scored whatever anti-inflammatory treatment was taken. [[Neogi2015]]

Clinical: time course (ever)

A typical episode has at least 2 of: maximal pain in under 24 hours; resolution within 14 days; complete resolution between episodes. [[Neogi2015]]

Laboratory: serum urate (required)

Measured by the uricase method. Score the highest value, whenever it was taken. Ideally it is measured off urate-lowering therapy and more than 4 weeks after a flare began; if practicable, retest under those conditions. At 10 mg/dL (600 µmol/L) or more there is no need to retest. [[Neogi2015]]

A primary-care rule without joint fluid

The Janssens (Nijmegen) rule was built in 328 Dutch primary-care patients with one hot joint, with crystals as the reference. Its 7 items had an AUC of 0.85 (0.81–0.90). [[Janssens2010]] It is a probability aid, not a substitute for aspiration when infection is possible. [[Lee2015]]

Weights and bands from Janssens 2010 as reproduced by Lee 2015. Band prevalences are from the derivation study, as reported by Lee 2015. Secondary-care validation: a score of 8 or more had a PPV of 0.87 and a score of 4 or less an NPV of 0.95 (AUC 0.86). [[Janssens2010,Lee2015,Kienhorst2015]]

Imaging that sees urate

Ultrasound: the double-contour sign

A bright, irregular line over the surface of the cartilage, running parallel to the bone line. A real one stays when the probe angle changes; an artefact disappears. [[Neogi2015]] It was seen in 92% of 37 crystal-proven gouty joints and in none of 33 controls. [[Thiele2007]] In SUGAR, any of double contour, tophus or snowstorm had sensitivity 76.9% and specificity 84.3%. [[Ogdie2017]]

It fades: it vanished in every joint where serum urate stayed under 6 mg/dL (360 µmol/L) for 7–18 months. [[Thiele2010]] EULAR names ultrasound as the first imaging choice when crystals cannot be sought. [[Richette2020]]

Dual-energy CT: colour-coded urate

Scans at 80 and 140 kV separate urate from calcium. Nail-bed, skin, motion, beam-hardening and vascular artefacts do not count. [[Neogi2015]] In one accuracy study sensitivity was 0.90 and specificity 0.83; all 4 false negatives were first flares with symptoms under 6 weeks, and all 7 false positives had advanced knee osteoarthritis. [[Bongartz2015]]

Pooled joint-level sensitivity was 0.83, but only 0.55 in disease of 6 weeks or less. [[Gamala2019]] Some artefact appeared in 90% of 50 scans. [[Mallinson2014]] Established A negative DECT does not exclude early gout. [[Gamala2019,Bongartz2015]]

X-ray: the overhanging edge

The criteria erosion is a cortical break with a sclerotic margin and an overhanging edge. DIP joints and the "gull wing" pattern are excluded because they occur in osteoarthritis. [[Neogi2015]] X-rays add little early; in late or severe gout, asymmetrical swelling (LR 4.13) and subcortical cysts without erosion (LR 6.39) help. [[Zhang2006Dx]]

Erosions are driven by tophus: all 56 CT erosions larger than 7.5 mm contained tophus. [[Dalbeth2009]]

Early evidence One meta-analysis found DECT more accurate overall (0.89/0.91), but ultrasound more sensitive in disease of 2 years or less (0.93 vs 0.75). [[Shang2022]]

The diagnostic pathway

A simplified teaching sequence drawn from the guidelines and studies cited in the notes. Real work-ups overlap and loop back.

Exam hookEntry: at least 1 episode of peripheral joint or bursa swelling, pain or tenderness. Sufficient: MSU crystals. Otherwise ≥8 of 23 points. Two items subtract: urate under 4 mg/dL (−4) and MSU-negative fluid (−2). Full criteria 0.92/0.89 (AUC 0.95); clinical-only 0.85/0.78 (AUC 0.89). [[Neogi2015]]
In plain wordsSeeing the crystals settles it. A doctor takes a little fluid from the swollen joint and looks at it under a special microscope; gout crystals look like thin needles. If that cannot be done, doctors add up points for where the pain is, how fast it came, the blood urate level and scan results. Your urate can look normal during a flare, so it is worth checking again about a month later.

Trained eyes agree

Trained observers identified MSU crystals with sensitivity 95.3%, specificity 97.2% and kappa 0.93; for CPP, 92.7%, 92.1% and 0.79. [[Lumbreras2005]] In an online test of 110 participants, only 39% met the primary outcome. [[Berendsen2017]]

Crystals stay between flares

In quiet, previously inflamed knees or first MTPs, crystals were found in all 43 people not on urate-lowering drugs and in 34 of 48 who were; 90% of positive samples showed them in the first 5 microscope fields. [[Pascual1999]] In untreated gout, 36 of 37 previously inflamed knees held crystals. [[Pascual1991]]

Handle the sample well

MSU crystal concentration falls over time at room temperature but not in the fridge, and dried cytospin preparations allowed delayed analysis for at least 12 months. [[Graf2013]] A lab report should state birefringence, shape, inside or outside cells, and number per high-power field. [[Jokic2020]]

A normal urate in a flare

In 41 patients, serum urate was normal during the flare in 20 (49%): mean 7.5 mg/dL (446 µmol/L) in flares vs 8.5 mg/dL (506 µmol/L) between them. [[Urano2002]] In 339 people, 14% had a urate of 6 mg/dL (357 µmol/L) or less at the flare. [[Schlesinger2009]]

Not on urate alone

EULAR 2018: gout should not be diagnosed on hyperuricaemia alone, and every person with suspected gout should have a search for crystals. [[Richette2020]] In people with asymptomatic hyperuricaemia, a first-MTP double contour was found in 0.31 (pooled). [[Stewart2019]] G-CAN calls this "asymptomatic MSU crystal deposition", not gout. [[Bursill2019ARD]]

How the older criteria did

In the same validation set, ACR 1977 had sensitivity 1.00 but specificity 0.51; Rome 0.97/0.78; New York 1.00/0.78; Mexico 1.00/0.44; Netherlands 0.95/0.59. [[Neogi2015]] Across older sets, sensitivity was better in established disease and specificity in early disease. [[Taylor2016]]
