Part 3 · Lowering urate

Treat to target

Urate-lowering therapy starts low and rises in small steps until a blood test shows serum urate below 6 mg/dL [360 µmol/L]. Scrub through two years of one imagined person: the dose staircase, the urate checks, the extra flares of the first months and the prophylaxis that covers them.

Doses here are quoted as published, not as instructions. The simulator follows the ACR 2020 guideline, the US labels and the trials named under each step; a dose for any person is set by their own clinician. [[FitzGerald2020,FDA-Zyloprim,FDA-Uloric]]

Most people need more than 300 mg

21–53%reached serum urate <6 mg/dL on allopurinol 300 mg/day across FACT, APEX, CONFIRMS and a Spanish study. [[Becker2005,Schumacher2008,Becker2010,PerezRuiz1998]]

Escalating above the creatinine-clearance dose brought 69% to target at 12 months against 32% on a fixed dose, in a trial where 52% had CrCl <60. [[Stamp2017RCT]] When the control group then escalated too, urate fell a further 1.1 mg/dL. [[Stamp2017OLE]]

Febuxostat by dose

Serum urate <6 mg/dL in 45% on 40 mg and 67% on 80 mg in CONFIRMS; 53% on 80 mg and 62% on 120 mg in FACT. [[Becker2010,Becker2005]]

In STOP Gout (940 veterans, at least a third with CKD stage 3), 80% reached target on either drug, and allopurinol was non-inferior for flares (36.5% vs 43.5% in the final phase). [[ODell2022]]

Starting during a flare

ACR 2020 conditionally recommends starting during a flare once treatment is indicated, rather than waiting. [[FitzGerald2020]] In a 57-man trial, allopurinol 300 mg started during a flare (with indomethacin and colchicine for all) did not worsen pain, and urate fell from 7.8 to 5.9 mg/dL by day 3. [[Taylor2012]] In a second trial the flare was not prolonged (15.4 vs 13.4 days, P = 0.5). [[Hill2015]]

The older probenecid label says to wait "until an acute gouty attack has subsided". [[FDA-Probenecid]]

Missed tablets Established

46%took at least 80% of their urate-lowering therapy, pooled across 13 studies; non-persistence ran from 54% to 87%. [[Scheepers2018SAR]]

A pharmacist-led, mostly automated telephone programme lifted adherence from 37% to 50% and target attainment from 15% to 30% at 1 year. [[Mikuls2019]] Only 12% of people on urate-lowering therapy knew about flares at the start. [[Harrold2012]]

Not too low? Unverified detail

EULAR 2016 sets <6 mg/dL [360 µmol/L], or <5 mg/dL [300 µmol/L] in severe gout. [[Richette2017]] Secondary sources also report EULAR advising against serum urate <3 mg/dL [180 µmol/L] over the long term; this guide could not confirm that line in the full text. [[Richette2017]] ACR 2020 sets <6 mg/dL for everyone on therapy (strong, high certainty). [[FitzGerald2020]]

How long to continue

ACR 2020 conditionally recommends continuing indefinitely (very low certainty). In the withdrawal series it cites, only 13% (27/211) stayed flare-free over 5 years off therapy. [[FitzGerald2020]]

Early evidence Perez-Ruiz 2006 suggested 5-year intermittent therapy for people with good control. [[PerezRuiz2006]] Hypothesis GO TEST Finale is randomising people in remission to continue or to taper and stop; only its protocol is published. [[Peeters2023]]
