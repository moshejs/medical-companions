The differential

A hot, swollen joint is not always gout: septic arthritis, CPP crystal arthritis and cellulitis can look the same, and infection can sit alongside crystals. Pick a clue to light up the conditions it points to, then sweep the joint-fluid white count to see how the odds of infection change.

Red flagsThink infection, and aspirate the joint
  • Fever, sweats or rigorsBut their absence does not exclude infection: fever had a sensitivity of only 57%. [[Margaretten2007]]
  • Prosthetic joint, diabetes, RA, skin infection or HIVEach is a risk factor for septic arthritis. [[Margaretten2007]]
  • A shoulderSeptic shoulders in crystal arthritis were bacteraemic in 90%. [[Papanicolas2012]]
  • A ruptured or discharging tophusRuptured, infected tophi were the commonest route in 30 people with septic gout. [[Yu2003]]
  • An atypical pictureEULAR 2018 says atypical presentations should be investigated further. [[Richette2020]]

Crystals do not exclude infection. Infection was found in 1.5% of 265 crystal-positive aspirates, and in 5% of crystal-positive joints in another series. [[Shah2007,Papanicolas2012]] Coexistence is very unlikely only when the Gram stain is negative, CRP is under 100 mg/L and the joint white count is under 10,000/µL. [[Papanicolas2012]]

Joint fluid: how likely is infection?

Likelihood ratios are pooled from 14 studies (6,242 patients, 653 with septic arthritis). Each LR belongs to its own cut-off (above or below it), not to the band between two cut-offs. [[Margaretten2007]]

Exam hookGout and septic arthritis can coexist: crystals in the fluid do not exclude infection (1.5–5%). Synovial white count: under 25,000/µL LR 0.32; over 50,000 LR 7.7; over 100,000 LR 28. Fever is absent in many: sensitivity 57%. [[Shah2007,Papanicolas2012,Margaretten2007]]
In plain wordsA hot, swollen joint with fever or chills needs a doctor the same day. A joint infection can look just like gout, and both can happen at once. The surest way to tell them apart is to take fluid from the joint and test it for crystals and for germs.

CPP crystal arthritis: its own criteria

The 2023 ACR/EULAR CPPD criteria have an entry step (joint pain, swelling or tenderness), exclude cases fully explained by another disease, and classify at a score above 56. Sensitivity and specificity were 92.2%/87.9% in derivation and 99.2%/92.5% in validation. Crowned dens syndrome is a sufficient criterion. [[Abhishek2023,Abhishek2023ARD]]

When gout turns septic

In 30 people with septic gout, 30% had a normal blood count and 10% a joint count under 6,000/mm³. S. aureus caused 16 cases, and a third had no fever. [[Yu2003]] A 50,000/µL cut-off missed 39% of 49 culture-proven septic joints, and 55% had a negative Gram stain. [[McGillicuddy2007]]

A gout score is not an infection test

Comparing 82 gout and 54 septic joints, the Janssens score averaged 8.6 vs 3.6 (AUC 0.933), but 3 of the 54 septic joints still scored "high". [[Lee2015]]

Cellulitis and its mimics

Cellulitis has no effective diagnostic test, and an organism is identified in about 15%. [[Raff2016]] Of 259 people admitted with "lower-limb cellulitis", 30.5% had another diagnosis. [[Weng2017]]

Rheumatoid and psoriatic arthritis

RA 2010: synovitis in at least 1 joint and a score of 6 or more of 10 from joint count, serology, acute-phase reactants and duration. [[Aletaha2010]] CASPAR: inflammatory joint disease plus 3 or more points (specificity 0.987, sensitivity 0.914). [[Taylor2006CASPAR]]

Reactive arthritis

Acute oligoarthritis of larger joints, 2–4 weeks after a gut or urogenital infection, with an incidence of 0.6–27 per 100,000 a year. HLA-B27 is not a diagnostic test. [[Hannu2011]]
