Sources

Every reference returned by the eight research passes behind this gout guide: articles, guidelines, drug labels and trial registrations. Each was validated against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov and the drug labels in October 2026. The small numbers throughout the app point here.

Research for each area was done separately, so a work cited under two names appears once, with its other key shown as “also”. Entries tagged check could not be fully confirmed against the original record, so treat them with extra care.
