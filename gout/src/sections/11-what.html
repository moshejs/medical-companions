Part 1 · The disease

What gout is

Gout is a long-term disease of urate crystal deposits that shows itself as sudden, very painful flares, most often in the big toe. [[Dalbeth2021,Dalbeth2019NRDP,Elfishawi2020]] High urate comes first, crystals form silently, flares follow, and years later some people develop tophi. Below: how common it is, the four stages, and where it strikes.

The four stages

Dalbeth and Stamp proposed four stages in 2014. Not everyone moves through all of them, and lowering urate can stop the march at any point. Press play to watch one joint go from stage A to stage D. [[Dalbeth2014,Dalbeth2021]]

Schematic, not to scale. The person's urate level, the symptom track and the timing are illustrative. Stage boundaries vary from person to person, and stages B and C can last for years.
