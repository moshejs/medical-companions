What it was called

Exam hookFive dates: 1679 van Leeuwenhoek sees needle crystals in a tophus · 1848 Garrod's thread test shows raised urate in gouty blood · 1961 McCarty and Hollander identify urate crystals by polarised light · 1966 allopurinol approved in the US (Nobel to Elion and Hitchings, 1988) · 2006 urate crystals shown to activate the NALP3 inflammasome. [[McCarty1970,Garrod1848,McCarty1961,DrugsFDA-Zyloprim,Elion1989,Martinon2006]]
In plain wordsPeople have described gout for more than 2,500 years. The crystals were seen in the 1600s, the chemistry was worked out in the 1700s and 1800s, and a daily pill that lowers urate arrived in the 1960s. Only in 2006 did scientists show how the crystals switch on the body's alarm.
