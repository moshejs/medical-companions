Gout, explained

Gout is the commonest inflammatory arthritis, and the one whose cause can be removed. Here it is in moving pictures: watch urate crystals grow and dissolve, follow a flare from a single crystal to a joint that cannot bear a bedsheet, and see why lowering urate to a target works and why it starts slowly.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.
