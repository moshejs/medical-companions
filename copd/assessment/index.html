Part 2 · Diagnosis & assessment

Symptoms, exacerbations & GOLD groups

Once spirometry has confirmed COPD, the assessment asks three more questions: how blocked are the airways, how much do symptoms weigh on daily life, and how many flare-ups were there last year? Confirm

Spirometry first

The assessment starts only after spirometry has confirmed COPD: a post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC below 0.7 12. GOLD 2023 then lists four goals: the GOLD grade, current symptoms, past exacerbations and other conditions 12.

In plain wordsFirst the breathing test has to show narrowed airways. Only then does the team grade how much COPD affects you.
Exam hookAssessment = grade + symptoms + exacerbation history + multimorbidity, after a post-BD ratio < 0.7 12.

Four questions after the diagnosis

After spirometry confirms COPD, GOLD 2023 lists four assessment goals: the severity of airflow limitation (the GOLD grade); the nature and size of current symptoms; previous moderate and severe exacerbations, which it calls "the best estimate of the risk of future exacerbations"; and the presence and type of other conditions (multimorbidity) 12.

Since GOLD 2017, the spirometric grade and the symptom-and-flare-up group are reported separately: the groups come only from symptoms and exacerbation history 463. That is because FEV1 tracks symptoms loosely (see ). Other conditions are covered in .

Breathlessness: the mMRC scale

The modified Medical Research Council (mMRC) scale is a five-point (0–4) self-rating of breathlessness during activity 252,395. The person picks the single grade that fits best; there are no sub-scores 491. It descends from the original MRC scale, which ran from 1 to 5 37. The grades below are paraphrased; the exact published wording is in Xiong and Liu 2026, Table 1 (open access) 491.

The mMRC numbers the original MRC scale’s steps from 0 instead of 1, so mMRC grade 2 corresponds to MRC grade 3. This mapping is inferred from the two numbering schemes, not stated by the sources.

Validity

A simple scale that tracks disability

In 100 rehabilitation patients graded MRC 3 (n = 32), 4 (n = 34) and 5 (n = 34), grade was significantly associated with shuttle-walk distance, SGRQ, CRQ, mood and extended activities of daily living 37. In 153 people with various respiratory diseases, the MRC scale, baseline dyspnoea index and oxygen-cost diagram correlated with one another (r = 0.48 to 0.70) with satisfactory agreement between observers 269.

Prognosis

Breathlessness predicted survival

In 227 Japanese patients followed for 5 years, the level of breathlessness predicted survival (p < 0.001), while FEV1-based staging did not reach significance (p = 0.08) 341.

Health status: the eight CAT domains

The COPD Assessment Test (CAT) has eight items, chosen by psychometric and Rasch analysis from 21 candidates in three studies (Europe and the USA, n = 1,503); Cronbach's α was 0.88, test–retest ICC 0.8, and correlation with the SGRQ r = 0.80 211. Each item is scored 0 to 5 between two opposite statements, and the total runs from 0 to 40; higher means worse health status 212,288.

The CAT is a copyrighted, licensed questionnaire, so its statements are not reproduced here. The sliders below name each item's domain and paraphrase what its two ends describe. The real wording is printed in Minov and colleagues 2015, Methods (open access) 288.

Scores rise during an exacerbation

CAAT

The Chronic Airways Assessment Test

The CAAT adapts the CAT by replacing "COPD" with "lung disease". Its items and scoring stay identical, and it is used in COPD and asthma 74.

Try it: the ABE group

GOLD 2023 replaced the ABCD tool with ABE. The symptom and exacerbation thresholds stayed the same, groups A and B stayed the same, and the old C and D merged into a single group E (for Exacerbations). GOLD says the practical value of the change "needs to be validated" 12. Exacerbation history counts the previous year 12.

Who ends up in which group

The split between groups depends on the population and on the symptom measure. In the UK ERICA cohort (n = 664), 57% of the people in group B by the CAAT were in group A by the mMRC 8. In COPDGene, the mMRC and the SGRQ (used as a CAT stand-in) agreed with κ = 0.77 on the older ABCD groups 176.

Blood eosinophils

A routine blood count includes eosinophils, a type of white blood cell. GOLD 2023 uses them to judge how likely an inhaled corticosteroid (ICS) is to help: below 100 cells/µL there is little or no effect, at 300 cells/µL or more there is a strong likelihood of benefit, and between the two the effect is a continuous gradation 12. IMPACT

Triple therapy by eosinophil count

The moderate/severe exacerbation rate ratio for triple therapy versus umeclidinium–vilanterol was 0.88 at eosinophils below 90 cells/µL and 0.56 at 310 cells/µL or more. Former smokers responded more to ICS than current smokers at any count 359.

Budesonide trials

A rising curve, not a switch

Pooling three budesonide–formoterol trials (4,528 patients, no asthma history), exacerbations rose non-linearly with eosinophil count on formoterol alone. At ≥ 0.10 × 10⁹ cells/L, adding budesonide reduced exacerbations (rate ratio 0.75) 30.

Population

Higher counts, more severe flare-ups

In the Copenhagen General Population Study (7,225 with spirometric COPD), eosinophils above 0.34 × 10⁹ cells/L carried adjusted incidence rate ratios of 1.76 for severe and 1.15 for moderate exacerbations 457.

Practicalities

Historical counts can be used

GOLD 2023 allows decisions to use historical eosinophil counts, as repeatability in a large primary-care population "appears reasonable", though variability is greater at higher thresholds 12. The GOLD 2019 science committee described eosinophils as a biomarker to target ICS at people who keep having exacerbations despite bronchodilators 411.

Beyond FEV1: the BODE index

BODE combines Body-mass index, airflow Obstruction, Dyspnoea and Exercise capacity into a 10-point score. In its validation cohort (625 patients, 162 deaths), each 1-point rise carried a hazard ratio of 1.34 for death from any cause and 1.62 for respiratory death; its C statistic was 0.74, against 0.65 for FEV1 66.

Walking tests

Method matters

The ERS/ATS field walking test standard covers the 6-minute walk and the incremental and endurance shuttle walks. Results are sensitive to small changes in method, and two tests should be done for the 6-minute walk and the incremental shuttle walk 194.

6-minute walk

A 30-metre fall matters

In ECLIPSE (n = 2,112), walk distance fell 29.7 m more over the year before death in those who died than in survivors. A fall of more than 30 m carried a hazard ratio of 1.93 for death, so 30 m is proposed as the minimal clinically important difference 371.

Hyperinflation

IC/TLC

Static hyperinflation measured as inspiratory capacity to total lung capacity predicted all-cause and respiratory mortality independently of BODE in 689 patients (183 deaths over a mean 34 months) 65. Why air gets trapped: .

Other scores

Many indices compared

A 2018 external validation compared BODE, updated BODE, ADO, e-BODE, BODEx, DOSE, SAFE, B-AE-D and GOLD-based scores for predicting COPD mortality 173. In the original BODE cohort, 61% of deaths were from respiratory insufficiency, 14% myocardial infarction, 12% lung cancer and 13% other causes 66.

Finding COPD: case-finding, not screening

GOLD 2023

Test people with symptoms or risk

Screening spirometry is "probably not indicated" in people without symptoms or significant exposure. In those with symptoms and/or risk factors (for example more than 20 pack-years, recurrent chest infections, prematurity or other early-life events), the yield is relatively high and spirometry "should be considered" 12.

USPSTF 2022

No screening without symptoms

The US Preventive Services Task Force recommends against screening asymptomatic adults for COPD (grade D), concluding with moderate certainty that screening has no net benefit 453.

Underdiagnosis

Most COPD is undiagnosed

In BOLD and other surveys (30,874 adults at 44 sites in 27 countries), 81.4% of spirometrically defined COPD was undiagnosed, from 50.0% in Lexington, Kentucky to 98.3% in Ile-Ife, Nigeria; only 26.4% had ever had a lung function test 241. A 2018 review estimates about 70% of COPD worldwide is undiagnosed 105.

CAPTURE

A case-finding questionnaire

CAPTURE (five questions plus peak flow; low flow < 350 L/min in males, < 250 L/min in females) had 89.7% sensitivity and 78.1% specificity in its development study 276. In 4,325 US primary-care patients, its sensitivity was 48.2% and specificity 88.6% (AUC 0.81) 279.

In plain words

Your doctor or nurse asks how breathless you get (the mMRC scale, 0 to 4) or gives you an 8-question form (the CAT, 0 to 40) 288,491. A change of 2 points on the CAT is about the smallest change people notice 230.

Flare-ups matter: two or more in a year, or one that put you in hospital, puts you in the highest-risk group, E 73.

A blood test that counts eosinophils (a type of white blood cell) helps decide whether an inhaled steroid is likely to help 12.

COPD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), emphysema and chronic bronchitis, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with COPD, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change an inhaler, a medicine, oxygen or a care plan without your own care team. The spirometry explainer, GOLD grade and group tools, mMRC and CAT scores, the blood-gas explainer and the other calculators are for learning, never for diagnosis, self-triage or treatment decisions. Doses, oxygen targets, inhaler routines and guideline statements are quoted as published (GOLD, NICE, ATS/ERS, BTS and drug labels), never as instructions, and survival and risk figures are population statistics. If breathing becomes much worse than usual, or there is confusion, drowsiness, chest pain or blue lips, follow your action plan or seek urgent care.
