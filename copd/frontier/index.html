Part 5 · Living & care

Research frontier

Where COPD research is heading in November 2026: injections that block the airway's alarm signals, the first licensed type 2 biologics, a new inhaled medicine, procedures through a bronchoscope, early-life lung growth, lung repair and new routes for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. Scrub through the routes, then filter the trial pipeline. Every route carries an evidence label.

Inhalers leave a gap. In COURSE, people already on triple inhaled therapy with at least two flare-ups in the previous year still had 2.11 moderate or severe exacerbations a year on placebo, and the newer add-on biologics target that unmet need 413. Three newer drugs have US labels for COPD so far: dupilumab and mepolizumab as add-on maintenance for adults with inadequately controlled COPD and an eosinophilic phenotype, and ensifentrine for maintenance treatment in adults 137,138,139. The eosinophil route is therefore established only for a subgroup: MATINEE notes that eosinophilic inflammation is present in 20 to 40% of patients with COPD 402.

Damage

Smoke and viruses injure the lining

Epithelial damage releases IL-33, an inflammatory cytokine implicated in COPD 378, and TSLP, whose expression is higher in COPD than in healthy people 413. Genetics pointed to IL-33 before the trials did: loss-of-function IL33 variants were associated with lower COPD risk, and gain-of-function variants in IL33 and IL1RL1 (the gene for ST2) with higher risk Early evidence 377. Downstream, signals such as IL-4/IL-13 and IL-5 drive type 2 inflammation 40,402.

In plain wordsDamaged airway cells send out alarm signals that switch on inflammation deeper in the airway wall 378,413.
Exam hookTSLP and IL-33 are epithelial "alarmins" at the top of the cascade; ST2 is the IL-33 receptor 377,413.

Alpha-1 routes: gene, RNA, protein

In alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency, the Z variant of the gene makes a protein that clumps in liver cells instead of reaching the lung. Augmentation replaces the protein; the newer routes act earlier, on the gene, the message or the folding step 70,236,264,435.

Schematic, not to scale. The blood gauge shows a direction of change, not a measured level. Base and RNA editing are first-in-human or animal results so far 236,293.

Gene

Rewrite the Z mutation

In the first liver biopsy after in vivo base editing (a 66-year-old PiZZ man in the YOLT-202 trial, NCT07193615), the PiZ variant was corrected in 54–57% of alleles at 10 weeks, with no off-target editing detected; circulating AAT stayed active Hypothesis: to early, a single case 236,337. BEAM-302, a lipid-nanoparticle base editor, is in a phase 1/2 trial for AATD lung and/or liver disease (about 192 planned, recruiting) 327.

In plain wordsResearchers are testing ways to fix the faulty gene itself, so the liver makes normal protein 236.
Exam hookIn vivo base editing corrected 54–57% of PiZ alleles in one patient's liver 236.

Open questions

AlarminsEarly evidence

Do eosinophils pick the responders?

Tezepelumab's effect grew across eosinophil strata (1.19 to 0.54), but tozorakimab and astegolimab cut exacerbations with no eosinophil criterion 355,403,413.

AlarminsEarly evidence

Does current smoking blunt anti-IL-33?

Itepekimab phase 2 helped only former smokers; AERIFY-1 (former smokers) was positive while AERIFY-2 was not, both registry-posted. Tozorakimab worked in the combined population 318,319,377,403.

BiologicsEarly evidence

How big is worthwhile?

Astegolimab's pooled 15% reduction met significance, but ARNASA alone did not; licensed type 2 biologics cut exacerbations by 20–34% 40,41,355,402,478.

Early COPDHypothesis

Should pre-COPD be treated?

Susceptible young adults developed COPD in 28% vs 8% over 10 years, but dual bronchodilators did not relieve symptoms in RETHINC 79,179.

ProceduresEarly evidence

Can devices beat sham on hard outcomes?

Cryospray and denervation improved symptoms or adverse-event windows, not all exacerbation endpoints 93,416. AIRFLOW-3 follows up to 2028 306.

AIEarly evidence

Will AI generalise?

CT mortality discrimination fell from 0.72 in COPDGene to 0.60 in ECLIPSE 164. Parametric response mapping suggests small-airway disease comes before emphysema 156.

In plain words. For some people who still have flare-ups on inhalers, injections called biologics can lower how often flare-ups happen; two are approved for people with high eosinophil counts 137,138. Newer injections that block the airway's alarm signals look promising but are not approved yet 403. Trial listings change often; a respiratory team can say which studies are open.

COPD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), emphysema and chronic bronchitis, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with COPD, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change an inhaler, a medicine, oxygen or a care plan without your own care team. The spirometry explainer, GOLD grade and group tools, mMRC and CAT scores, the blood-gas explainer and the other calculators are for learning, never for diagnosis, self-triage or treatment decisions. Doses, oxygen targets, inhaler routines and guideline statements are quoted as published (GOLD, NICE, ATS/ERS, BTS and drug labels), never as instructions, and survival and risk figures are population statistics. If breathing becomes much worse than usual, or there is confusion, drowsiness, chest pain or blue lips, follow your action plan or seek urgent care.
