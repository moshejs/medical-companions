COPD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), emphysema and chronic bronchitis, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with COPD, their families and caregivers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Cirrhosis Companion, on the liver disease that alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency can also cause, the Heart Attack Companion, on the heart disease that often comes with COPD, the CF Companion, on another lifelong airway disease, the TB Companion, on an infection that can leave lasting airflow obstruction, and the Sepsis Companion, on what can follow a severe chest infection.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
