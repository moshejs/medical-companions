For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. The spirometry explainer, GOLD grade and group tools, mMRC and CAT scores and the blood-gas explainer are teaching aids, quoted as published. Finish with flashcards, memory aids and a quiz.

For people with COPD, families and caregivers

Look for the In plain words boxes. COPD is common, often found late, and treatable: stopping smoking, the right inhaler used well, vaccines and pulmonary rehabilitation all change things. Bring questions to your own care team: the app reports what guidelines, labels and trials say, not what you should take, stop or change.
