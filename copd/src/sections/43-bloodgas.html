Get helpA flare-up can become an emergency. GOLD 2023 lists confusion or drowsiness, sudden worsening of breathlessness at rest, a high breathing rate, low oxygen saturation and new blue lips (cyanosis) among the reasons for hospital care. [[Agusti2023]] Anyone with these signs needs urgent medical help: in an emergency, call your local emergency number. BTS 2017 recommends oxygen alert cards for people who have had high carbon dioxide levels, to show to the ambulance crew. [[ODriscoll2017Summary]]

    Taking the sample

    Venous gas

    pH and HCO3⁻ yes, CO2 no

    In 234 flare-up patients, venous and arterial pH (mean difference 0.03; limits −0.05 to 0.11) and HCO3⁻ agreed well, and SpO2 agreed with SaO2 above 80%. Venous PCO2 should not be read as arterial PaCO2. Arterial sampling needed more attempts and hurt more (median pain 4 against 1). [[McKeever2016]]

    Earlobe gas

    Good for pH and CO2

    Arterialised earlobe gases measure pH and PCO2 accurately, but the PO2 "underestimates the oxygen tension by 0.5–1 kPa". Local anaesthetic should be used for all arterial samples except in emergencies (grade A). [[ODriscoll2017Summary]]

    Oximetry

    Not a substitute

    Pulse oximetry is less accurate than a blood gas and "may overestimate blood oxygen content among individuals with darker skin tones", which matters for an 88–92% target. [[Agusti2023]] Only 81.6% of admitted patients in the European COPD Audit had blood gases taken. [[Hartl2016]]

    Plain words

    What a blood gas is

    A blood gas test measures oxygen, carbon dioxide and acid in the blood, usually from an artery in the wrist. [[ODriscoll2017Summary]]

    Open questions

    Which CO2 line?

    6.0 or 6.5 kPa

    BTS/ICS uses pCO2 >6.5 kPa for starting NIV [[Davidson2016Summary]], while BTS 2017 oxygen guidance, Rome and the Cochrane review use >6 kPa (45 mmHg) [[ODriscoll2017Summary,Jacobson2023,Osadnik2017]]. All agree on pH <7.35, so the interpreter shows both lines.

    How long to wait?

    30 or 60 minutes

    BTS 2017 says acidosis persisting more than 30 minutes after initial treatment [[ODriscoll2017Summary]]; BTS/ICS says "despite optimal medical therapy" [[Davidson2016Summary]]; the Quality Standard sets 60 minutes from the qualifying gas. [[Davies2018]]

    Severe acidosis

    NIV below 7.25?

    Older practice treated pH <7.25 as a line for invasive ventilation, but Cochrane found similar NIV benefit below pH 7.30 [[Davidson2016Summary,Osadnik2017]], and BTS/ICS says severe acidosis alone does not preclude a trial where intubation is available. Failure risk is still much higher below 7.25. [[Davidson2016Summary,Confalonieri2005]]
