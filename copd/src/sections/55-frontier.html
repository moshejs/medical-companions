Research frontier

Where COPD research is heading in November 2026: injections that block the airway's alarm signals, the first licensed type 2 biologics, a new inhaled medicine, procedures through a bronchoscope, early-life lung growth, lung repair and new routes for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. Scrub through the routes, then filter the trial pipeline. Every route carries an evidence label.

Inhalers leave a gap. In COURSE, people already on triple inhaled therapy with at least two flare-ups in the previous year still had 2.11 moderate or severe exacerbations a year on placebo, and the newer add-on biologics target that unmet need [[Singh2025COURSE]]. Three newer drugs have US labels for COPD so far: dupilumab and mepolizumab as add-on maintenance for adults with inadequately controlled COPD and an eosinophilic phenotype, and ensifentrine for maintenance treatment in adults [[FDA-Dupixent,FDA-Nucala,FDA-Ohtuvayre]]. The eosinophil route is therefore established only for a subgroup: MATINEE notes that eosinophilic inflammation is present in 20 to 40% of patients with COPD [[Sciurba2025]].

Alpha-1 routes: gene, RNA, protein

In alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency, the Z variant of the gene makes a protein that clumps in liver cells instead of reaching the lung. Augmentation replaces the protein; the newer routes act earlier, on the gene, the message or the folding step [[Chapman2015,Strnad2022Fazirsiran,Lomas2021,Krooss2027]].

Open questions

AlarminsEarly evidence

Do eosinophils pick the responders?

Tezepelumab's effect grew across eosinophil strata (1.19 to 0.54), but tozorakimab and astegolimab cut exacerbations with no eosinophil criterion [[Singh2025COURSE,Sciurba2026,Papi2026]].

AlarminsEarly evidence

Does current smoking blunt anti-IL-33?

Itepekimab phase 2 helped only former smokers; AERIFY-1 (former smokers) was positive while AERIFY-2 was not, both registry-posted. Tozorakimab worked in the combined population [[Rabe2021,NCT04701983,NCT04751487,Sciurba2026]].

BiologicsEarly evidence

How big is worthwhile?

Astegolimab's pooled 15% reduction met significance, but ARNASA alone did not; licensed type 2 biologics cut exacerbations by 20–34% [[Papi2026,Wedzicha2026,Bhatt2023,Bhatt2024,Sciurba2025]].

Early COPDHypothesis

Should pre-COPD be treated?

Susceptible young adults developed COPD in 28% vs 8% over 10 years, but dual bronchodilators did not relieve symptoms in RETHINC [[Colak2024,Han2022RETHINC]].

ProceduresEarly evidence

Can devices beat sham on hard outcomes?

Cryospray and denervation improved symptoms or adverse-event windows, not all exacerbation endpoints [[Criner2026SPRAYCB,Slebos2019]]. AIRFLOW-3 follows up to 2028 [[NCT03639051]].

AIEarly evidence

Will AI generalise?

CT mortality discrimination fell from 0.72 in COPDGene to 0.60 in ECLIPSE [[Gonzalez2018]]. Parametric response mapping suggests small-airway disease comes before emphysema [[Galban2012]].

In plain words. For some people who still have flare-ups on inhalers, injections called biologics can lower how often flare-ups happen; two are approved for people with high eosinophil counts [[FDA-Dupixent,FDA-Nucala]]. Newer injections that block the airway's alarm signals look promising but are not approved yet [[Sciurba2026]]. Trial listings change often; a respiratory team can say which studies are open.
