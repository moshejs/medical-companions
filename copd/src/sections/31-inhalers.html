Bronchodilators

Bronchodilators relax the muscle wrapped around the airways. Watch one smooth-muscle cell as a reliever binds its β2 receptor and a long-acting blocker covers its M3 receptor, and see the airway open. Then compare the trials behind each class.

Study material, not a treatment planThis page reports what labels, guidelines and trials say. Which medicine a person takes is decided with their own clinician.

The bronchodilator classes

Two receptors on airway smooth muscle explain the two families. β2-agonists stimulate the β2-adrenergic receptors that predominate in bronchial smooth muscle [[FDA-VentolinHFA]]. Muscarinic antagonists block the M3 receptor, "leading to bronchodilation" [[FDA-SpirivaHandiHaler]]. The Ventolin label notes that β2-receptors also make up 10% to 50% of the β-receptors in the human heart, which "raises the possibility that even selective beta2-agonists may have cardiac effects" [[FDA-VentolinHFA]].

What the Cochrane reviews found

SABA vs placebo

A small, real lift

13 crossover trials: post-bronchodilator FEV1 +0.14 L (95% CI 0.04 to 0.25); breathlessness scores improved (SMD 1.33). The trials were too short and small to judge long-term harms or mortality [[Sestini2002]].

Established
Tiotropium vs placebo

Fewer people with flare-ups

22 studies, 23,309 participants: OR 0.78 (95% CI 0.70 to 0.87), a number needed to treat of 16 for 1 year at a placebo event rate of 44%. No overall mortality difference (Peto OR 0.98) [[Karner2014]].

Established
LABA vs placebo

Better quality of life

26 RCTs, 14,939 people: SGRQ −2.32 (95% CI −3.09 to −1.54); exacerbations needing hospital OR 0.73, 18 fewer per 1000 over about 7 months; no significant effect on mortality [[Kew2013]].

Established
Tiotropium vs LABA

The LAMA edges ahead

7 studies, 12,223 participants: fewer people with one or more exacerbations (OR 0.86; 95% CI 0.79 to 0.93) and fewer exacerbation-related hospitalisations (OR 0.87). Mortality did not differ [[Chong2012]].

Established

Open questions

Device safety

The soft mist signal

A Cochrane subgroup found more deaths with tiotropium Respimat (Peto OR 1.47; 95% CI 1.04 to 2.08) but not with HandiHaler [[Karner2014]]. TIOSPIR, 17,135 people over a mean 2.3 years, did not confirm it: Respimat 5 µg vs HandiHaler HR 0.96 for death [[Wise2013]].

Established: not confirmed
Dual vs single

Does LAMA+LABA cut flare-ups more than a LAMA?

Guidelines favour dual therapy for breathlessness [[Nici2020,Agusti2023]], but in DYNAGITO the exacerbation rate ratio of 0.93 missed its 0.01 significance target [[Calverley2018]].

Early evidence
LABA+LAMA or LABA+ICS?

Two answers

FLAME found LABA+LAMA superior [[Wedzicha2016]]; the 2023 Cochrane update found similar exacerbation odds (OR 0.91; 95% CI 0.78 to 1.06) [[Fukuda2023]]. GOLD 2023 sidesteps the comparison by preferring triple therapy, not LABA+ICS, when an ICS is indicated [[Agusti2023]].
