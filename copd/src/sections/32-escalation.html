Open question

Does triple therapy lower mortality?

IMPACT (HR 0.72 vs UMEC/VI) and ETHOS (HR 0.51 vs GLY/FOR, higher budesonide dose only) both reported fewer deaths than with LAMA/LABA. Both were secondary end points with low event rates, and neither was significant versus ICS/LABA [[Lipson2020,Martinez2021]]. GOLD 2023 includes both in its figure of evidence for reduced mortality [[Agusti2023]].

Early evidence
Guidelines

When triple is recommended

ATS 2020: "a conditional recommendation for the use of triple therapy ... over dual therapy with LABA/LAMA in patients with COPD and dyspnea or exercise intolerance who have experienced one or more exacerbations in the past year" [[Nici2020]]. GOLD 2023: with concomitant asthma, people "should be treated as if they have asthma" [[Agusti2023]].

Established

Inhaled steroids and pneumonia

The Cochrane review of 43 studies put the extra risk of serious (hospitalised) pneumonia in absolute terms. Each dot is one of 1000 people treated; red dots are the extra cases. The review found no significant change in overall mortality with either ICS, and pneumonia deaths were too rare to judge [[Kew2014]].

Beyond inhalers: add-on medicines

Roflumilast · oral PDE4 inhibitor

Fewer exacerbations, more side effects

The Daliresp label says roflumilast inhibits PDE4, "a major cyclic-3′,5′-adenosine monophosphate (cyclic AMP)-metabolizing enzyme in lung tissue", and that its exact mechanism "is not well defined" [[FDA-Daliresp]]. Indication: "to reduce the risk of COPD exacerbations in patients with severe COPD associated with chronic bronchitis and a history of exacerbations" [[FDA-Daliresp]]. Exacerbations 1.14 vs 1.37 per patient-year; weight −2.17 kg vs placebo [[Calverley2009]]. Label: diarrhoea 9.5% vs 2.7%, weight decreased 7.5% vs 2.1%, psychiatric adverse reactions 5.9% vs 3.3% [[FDA-Daliresp]]. REACT sat on the significance line (P=0.0529 primary, P=0.0424 sensitivity) [[Martinez2015]].

Established
Azithromycin · daily macrolide

Longer to the first flare-up

1142 people, 250 mg daily for 1 year: median time to first exacerbation 266 vs 174 days, and an exacerbation hazard ratio of 0.73. Hearing decrements 25% vs 20%; the effect on microbial resistance "is not known". People with hearing impairment, resting tachycardia or QTc risk were excluded [[Albert2011]]. No benefit in current smokers (HR 0.99) [[Han2014]]. Cochrane: prophylactic antibiotics cut the share of people with an exacerbation from 61% to 47% (NNTB 8) [[Herath2018]]. Azithromycin has no US label indication for COPD; its use comes from trials and guidelines.

Established
Dupilumab · anti-IL-4Rα

BOREAS and NOTUS

939 and 935 people on triple therapy with eosinophils ≥300/µL: rate ratios 0.70 and 0.66 [[Bhatt2023,Bhatt2024]]. FEV1 differences of 83 mL (BOREAS) and 82 mL (NOTUS) at week 12; SGRQ improved in BOREAS but not in NOTUS at 52 weeks [[Bhatt2023,Bhatt2024]]. FDA approval for COPD 2024-09-27; label dose "300 mg given every 2 weeks (Q2W)" [[FDA-Dupixent]].

Established for the trial populations
Mepolizumab · anti-IL-5

MATINEE

804 people randomised (806 in the registry) on triple therapy with eosinophils ≥300/µL: 0.80 vs 1.01 exacerbations per year (rate ratio 0.79); median time to first exacerbation 419 vs 321 days. Quality-of-life differences were not significant [[Sciurba2025,NCT04133909]]. Eosinophilic inflammation is present in 20 to 40% of people with COPD, the paper says [[Sciurba2025]]. FDA approval for COPD 2025-05-22; label dose "100 mg administered subcutaneously once every 4 weeks" [[FDA-Nucala]].

Established for the trial population
Ensifentrine · inhaled PDE3/PDE4 inhibitor

A nebulised newcomer

Inhibiting both enzymes raises intracellular cAMP and/or cGMP, the Ohtuvayre label says [[FDA-Ohtuvayre]]. ENHANCE-1 and -2 (760 and 789 people): FEV1 AUC0–12h +87 and +94 mL; exacerbation rate ratios 0.64 and 0.57 over 24 weeks [[Anzueto2023]]. FDA-approved 2024-06-26; label dose "3 mg (one unit-dose ampule) twice daily ... by oral inhalation using a standard jet nebulizer with a mouthpiece" [[FDA-Ohtuvayre]].
