An animated, cited guide for students, people with COPD, and their families and caregivers. Educational only, not medical advice. Your place is saved on this device.

Not medical adviceThe COPD Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, physiotherapist, pharmacist or the rest of a respiratory team. Spirometry bands, GOLD grades and groups, mMRC and CAT scores and the blood-gas explainer are teaching aids, never for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Doses, oxygen targets and inhaler routines are quoted as published, never as instructions. Survival and risk figures are population statistics, not predictions for any person.

Part 6 · Context & review

Sources

Every reference behind the COPD Companion: journal articles, randomised trials, cohort and registry studies, systematic reviews, GOLD, ATS, ERS, BTS and other guidance, US drug and device labels and trial registrations. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology, risk factors and history; mechanism, pathology and alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency; diagnosis and assessment; medicines for stable COPD and inhalers; exacerbations and hospital care; smoking cessation, vaccines, rehabilitation, oxygen and advanced therapies; living with COPD, comorbidities and nursing care; and the research frontier. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or the regulator's label, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked not matched in a research database are pages with no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are not cited for any fact in this guide.

COPD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), emphysema and chronic bronchitis, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with COPD, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change an inhaler, a medicine, oxygen or a care plan without your own care team. The spirometry explainer, GOLD grade and group tools, mMRC and CAT scores, the blood-gas explainer and the other calculators are for learning, never for diagnosis, self-triage or treatment decisions. Doses, oxygen targets, inhaler routines and guideline statements are quoted as published (GOLD, NICE, ATS/ERS, BTS and drug labels), never as instructions, and survival and risk figures are population statistics. If breathing becomes much worse than usual, or there is confusion, drowsiness, chest pain or blue lips, follow your action plan or seek urgent care. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
