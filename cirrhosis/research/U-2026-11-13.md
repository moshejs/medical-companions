# U · Update 2026-11-13

Pass date: 2026-11-13. Window: published or announced 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13 (PubMed entry dates 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-13), plus one catch-up item from 2026-10-20. Modules served: hepatitis, othercauses, frontier.

## Findings

### hepatitis · AASLD expert practice opinion on hepatitis D
- AASLD published an Expert Practice Opinion (EXPO) on HDV, written by subject experts for the AASLD Practice Guidelines Committee from a targeted literature review and group consensus. [[Lim2026AASLDHDV]]
- It states that all HBsAg-positive individuals should be tested at least once for HDV infection with HDV antibody, and that antibody-positive individuals should have HDV RNA testing to confirm active infection. [[Lim2026AASLDHDV]]
- All individuals with chronic HDV should have non-invasive fibrosis testing, preferably elastography; laboratory monitoring and HCC surveillance imaging are suggested every 6 months. [[Lim2026AASLDHDV]]
- All adults with chronic HDV viraemia and compensated liver disease should be considered candidates for bulevirtide for chronic virologic suppression, or bulevirtide plus peg-IFN alfa-2a for those without contraindications who want a finite regimen. Quoted as published, not as an instruction. [[Lim2026AASLDHDV]]

### hepatitis · Bepirovirsen (Hibsago) approved for chronic hepatitis B (catch-up, approved 2026-10-20)
- The FDA approved bepirovirsen (Hibsago, NDA221276, GlaxoSmithKline) on 2026-10-20, a new molecular entity under priority review. It is an HBV mRNA-directed antisense oligonucleotide given by subcutaneous injection. (Established) [[FDA-Hibsago]]
- Indication as published: in combination with nucleos(t)ide analogue therapy for chronic HBV infection in adult patients without cirrhosis who have been on NrtI therapy for at least 6 months with suppressed HBV DNA and who have HBsAg of 3,000 IU/mL or less. [[FDA-Hibsago]]
- Label dose as published: 300 mg twice a week in weeks 1 and 2, then 300 mg once a week in weeks 3 to 24. [[FDA-Hibsago]]
- Primary endpoint (HBV DNA below the lower limit of quantification and HBsAg not detected 24 weeks after stopping all chronic HBV treatment): 127/650 (20%) vs 0/328 (0%) in B-Well 1 and 106/570 (19%) vs 0/286 (0%) in B-Well 2. In participants with baseline HBsAg of 1,000 IU/mL or less: 25% and 28% vs 0%. [[FDA-Hibsago]]
- Boxed warning: on-treatment hepatic flares (ALT more than 20 to 40 times the upper limit of normal) and thrombocytopenia. Contraindicated in acute hepatic impairment, in cirrhosis (Child-Pugh A, B or C) and with a platelet count below 100 x 10^9/L. [[FDA-Hibsago]]
- This does not contradict the site's statement that nucleos(t)ide analogues and peginterferon rarely clear HBsAg [[Jeng2023Lancet]]; it adds a drug class that does so in about one in five selected people without cirrhosis.

### othercauses · ALP normalisation trials in PBC (IDEAL, ELSPIRE)
- IDEAL: phase 3, double-blind; 96 people with PBC on UDCA (or intolerant) with ALP between 1 and 1.67 x ULN were randomised 2:1 to seladelpar 10 mg daily or placebo for 12 months. ALP normalisation (ALP at or below ULN with at least a 15% fall) at month 12: 75.4% (49/65) vs 16.1% (5/31); stratum-adjusted difference 61.3% (95% CI 44.9 to 77.7; p < 0.0001). Serious adverse events 3.1% vs 12.9%. NCT06060665. (Early evidence) [[Levy2026IDEAL]]
- ELSPIRE: phase 3, double-blind; 92 people with PBC and ALP above 1 and below 1.67 x ULN after incomplete response or intolerance to UDCA were randomised 2:1 to elafibranor 80 mg (n=62) or placebo (n=30) for 52 weeks. ALP normalisation at week 52: 85.3% vs 23.3% (difference 62.0%; 95% CI 44.3 to 79.7; p < 0.0001). (Early evidence) [[Kowdley2026ELSPIRE]]
- Both trials measured a biochemical endpoint. Neither reports survival or decompensation, which the US labels say "have not been demonstrated". [[FDA-Iqirvo,FDA-Livdelzi]]

### frontier · Efinopegdutide phase 2b in MASH F2–F3
- Phase 2b, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 176 centres in 26 countries; 386 randomised, 381 treated; 63% F2 and 37% F3. Arms: efinopegdutide 4, 7 or 10 mg, placebo, or open-label semaglutide 2.4 mg, all weekly. NCT05877547. (Early evidence) [[Lawitz2026Efinopegdutide]]
- Primary endpoint, MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis at week 52: 56% (4 mg), 63% (7 mg) and 61% (10 mg) vs 13% on placebo (all p < 0.0001); 53% on semaglutide. (Early evidence) [[Lawitz2026Efinopegdutide]]
- Key secondary, fibrosis improvement of at least one stage without worsening of steatohepatitis: 43% (32/75), 52% (39/75) and 34% (26/77) vs 21% (16/77); the 10 mg difference was not significant (p = 0.072); 25% (16/65) on semaglutide. (Early evidence) [[Lawitz2026Efinopegdutide]]
- Adverse events leading to discontinuation: 11% (9/79) at 10 mg, 3–4% at the other doses and 1% on placebo. Funded by Merck Sharp & Dohme. [[Lawitz2026Efinopegdutide]]
- Not tested in cirrhosis. The site's caution that F2–F3 biopsy gains do not equal benefit in cirrhosis still applies. [[Loomba2023SemaCirrhosis,Noureddin2025SYMMETRY]]

### Exam hooks
- AASLD 2026 (EXPO): test every HBsAg-positive person at least once for HDV antibody; confirm with HDV RNA. [[Lim2026AASLDHDV]]
- Bepirovirsen: approved for adults without cirrhosis; contraindicated in cirrhosis; boxed warning for hepatic flares and thrombocytopenia. [[FDA-Hibsago]]
- PBC second-line drugs normalised ALP in people with ALP 1–1.67 x ULN: seladelpar 75.4% vs 16.1%, elafibranor 85.3% vs 23.3%. [[Levy2026IDEAL,Kowdley2026ELSPIRE]]

### In plain words
- Liver specialists in the US now say everyone with hepatitis B should be tested once for hepatitis D.
- A new injection for hepatitis B cleared the virus marker from the blood in about one in five people in its trials. It is not for people who already have cirrhosis.
- Two medicines for PBC brought a liver blood test back to normal in most people whose result was only slightly high. Whether that prevents liver damage is not yet known.

## Open questions and controversies
- Do ALP-normalising second-line drugs in PBC with ALP 1–1.67 x ULN improve clinical outcomes? Not shown [[Levy2026IDEAL,Kowdley2026ELSPIRE]]. Status: open.
- Will efinopegdutide's phase 2b histology hold in phase 3, and does it help in cirrhosis? Untested [[Lawitz2026Efinopegdutide]].
