Part 1 · Understanding cirrhosis

From injury to scar

Scrub through decades of repeated liver injury, whatever its cause: stellate cells switch on, collagen builds up, sinusoids lose their windows, scar bridges the lobule and nodules form. Then remove the cause and watch what the evidence says can heal, and what may not.

After year 30

Schematic. The stage moves at the median rate measured in untreated hepatitis C (0.133 METAVIR units a year), so F4 arrives at about year 30; real people progress much faster or slower, and many never reach cirrhosis. 366 The order of events (activation, capillarisation, septa, nodules) follows the reviews cited in each step; the timing between them is drawn, not measured.

Debates and limits

Staging

METAVIR vs Ishak

METAVIR has five fibrosis stages (F0–F4), Ishak seven (0–6, cirrhosis at 5–6), and NASH CRN five (0–4). The same biopsy carries different numbers in different systems, so a "stage 4" means cirrhosis in METAVIR but not in Ishak. All were built for progressing disease and are limited by their categories. 55,210,241,366

Sampling error

One core, one guess

In 124 people with hepatitis C biopsied in both lobes at once, 33.1% differed by at least one stage, and in 14.5% one lobe read as cirrhosis and the other as stage 3. A 15 mm core staged correctly 65% of the time, a 25 mm core 75%. 31,372

Controversy

Is there a point of no return?

Yes, sometimes: cross-linked, elastin-rich, vascularised septa and vascular lesions persist, and sinusoidal capillarisation did not reverse after hepatitis C cure. 92,171,211 Not necessarily: 74% of people with hepatitis B cirrhosis lost cirrhosis on biopsy at 5 years, and "for many patients, cirrhosis is not an end-stage condition anymore". 205,289 Status: unresolved; pathologists call for better, possibly AI-assisted, tools. 55,171

Early evidence

Does less scar mean fewer events?

After hepatitis C therapy, people whose cirrhosis regressed had 0 liver-related events per 100 patient-years versus 4 without regression, and 10-year transplant-free survival of 100% vs 74.2%, in a small retrospective cohort. The clinical meaning of regressive features "remains largely unstudied". 55,287

Established

Regressed is not risk-free

Cirrhosis that has regressed still carries molecular changes that can lead to liver cancer, so surveillance continues. Cirrhosis of recent onset, with thin septa, is more likely to improve. See Liver cancer surveillance. 205

Hypothesis

Scar-associated cells as targets

A single-cell atlas of human cirrhosis found scar-associated TREM2+CD9+ macrophages from circulating monocytes, and endothelial and PDGFRα+ collagen-producing cells confined to the scar. Whether targeting them helps people is untested. See Research toward better treatment. 369
