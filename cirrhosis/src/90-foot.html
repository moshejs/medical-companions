Cirrhosis Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cirrhosis, its staging scores and its complications, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with cirrhosis, their families and caregivers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the AUD Companion, a neighbouring guide on alcohol use disorder, one of the causes of cirrhosis and a treatable condition in its own right, and the CF Companion, on cystic fibrosis, a genetic cause of liver disease in children and young adults.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
