Cirrhosis, explained

Cirrhosis is long-term scarring of the liver, and it has many causes: fatty liver disease linked to metabolism, hepatitis B and C, alcohol, and autoimmune, inherited and bile-duct conditions. [[Gines2021Lancet]] More than 100 million people worldwide were estimated to live with compensated cirrhosis in 2017, a phase that usually causes no symptoms. [[GBD2020Cirrhosis,Gines2021Lancet]] Here is how scar forms, how cirrhosis is staged and scored, what its complications look like and what treating the cause can change, in animations you can scrub.

Cirrhosis at a glance

What it is
Cirrhosis is what a liver becomes after years of injury: healthy tissue is replaced by scar and nodules, and blood pressure in the portal vein rises. It has many causes, it is common, and for a long time it is silent. [[Gines2021Lancet]]
Who gets it
About 112 million people worldwide had early-stage (compensated) cirrhosis in 2017. [[GBD2020Cirrhosis]] In a US review, alcohol use disorder was present in about 45% of cases, fatty liver disease in 26% and hepatitis C in 41%; many people have more than one cause. [[Tapper2023JAMA]]
Main symptoms
Early cirrhosis usually has no symptoms. [[Gines2021Lancet]] In one US survey, 69% of adults with cirrhosis did not know they had liver disease. [[Scaglione2015]] Later signs include a belly swelling with fluid, confusion or sleepiness, vomiting blood or black stools, and yellow skin or eyes. [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII,Tapper2023JAMA]]
How it is diagnosed
A blood-test score called FIB-4 is the first-line check in AASLD 2023 [[Sterling2006FIB4,Rinella2023AASLD]], followed by a liver-stiffness scan when the risk stays high. [[Rinella2023AASLD,Shah2009FIB4,deFranchis2022BavenoVII]] About 40% of people are first diagnosed when a complication brings them to care. [[Tapper2023JAMA]]
Main treatments
Removing or suppressing the cause, such as curing hepatitis C, controlling hepatitis B or long-term abstinence from alcohol, can help the liver recover. [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]] An ultrasound, often with an AFP blood test, every 6 months can find liver cancer early. [[Singal2023AASLDHCC,Singal2014PLoSMed]] Liver transplant is the second most common solid-organ transplant. [[Asrani2019JHep]]
Outlook
The risk of death rises once fluid, confusion or bleeding begin. [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII,Gines2021Lancet]] Scarring can regress when the cause is removed. [[Kisseleva2021]] In England, average survival was 84% at 1 year and 66% at 5 years for people diagnosed as outpatients. [[Ratib2014]]

For people with cirrhosis, families and caregivers

Look for the In plain words boxes. Cirrhosis has many causes, from fatty liver disease and viral hepatitis to alcohol, genes and the immune system, and no one deserves blame for it. Survival figures here are population statistics, not a forecast for any one person. Bring questions to your own care team: the app reports what labels, guidelines and trials say, not what you should take or change.
