Measuring scarring without a biopsy

A free blood-test score first, a liver-stiffness scan next, and a specialist when the risk stays up. Follow people through the pathway, then try the FIB-4 and elastography explainers.

Schematic: the dots show the route people take, not the proportions in any study. Cut-offs as published. [[Rinella2023AASLD,Shah2009FIB4,deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]

Try it: a teaching FIB-4 calculator

A teaching tool, not a diagnosisFIB-4 estimates the chance of advanced scarring in groups of people. It cannot diagnose or rule out cirrhosis in one person, and it must not be used to make decisions about your care. Discuss any result with your care team, who will interpret it with your history, examination and scans.

Reading a FibroScan result: the kPa bands

A teaching tool, not a diagnosisLiver stiffness readings depend on the method, the cause of liver disease, recent meals and the quality of the scan: VCTE and MR elastography both report kPa but on different scales, a high reading is repeated while fasting, and nearly 1 in 5 readings cannot be interpreted. The bands below are quoted from Baveno VII and AASLD as published; a specialist interprets a real result. [[Rinella2023AASLD,deFranchis2022BavenoVII,Castera2010Pitfalls,Pavlov2016ALD]]

The tests compared

AUROC (area under the ROC curve) summarises how well a test separates people with and without a given stage of scarring: higher is better. Numbers from different populations and stages are not directly comparable, and one elastography meta-analysis cautioned that stage cut-offs varied widely and had not been validated. Worth knowing

Names

NAFLD is now MASLD

In 2023 NAFLD became metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD): steatosis plus at least 1 of 5 cardiometabolic risk factors. Many studies on this page use the older "NAFLD/NASH" names. [[Rinella2023MASLD]]

Not only fatty liver

Cut-offs depend on the cause

For alcohol-related liver disease a cut-off of about 12.5 kPa was suggested, with caution; in NAFLD a 13.6 kPa cut-off was found for cirrhosis. Baveno VII's rule of five is cause-agnostic and is used for prognosis. [[Pavlov2016ALD,Eddowes2019FibroScan,deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]

Two steps

Sequencing reduces biopsies, not to zero

FIB-4 (<1.3; ≥2.67) followed by VCTE (<8.0; ≥10.0 kPa) gave 66% sensitivity and 86% specificity for advanced fibrosis, with 33% still needing a biopsy. [[Mozes2022IPD]]

Over time

Stiffness can fall

Baveno VII counts a fall of 20% or more to below 20 kPa, or any fall to below 10 kPa, as a clinically meaningful decrease linked with lower risk. [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]
