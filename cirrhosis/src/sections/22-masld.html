Fatty liver disease (MASLD & MASH)

About 3 in 10 adults worldwide store too much fat in the liver alongside a metabolic risk factor such as a large waist, high blood pressure or diabetes. Most never develop cirrhosis; how much scar the liver builds decides who does. [[Younossi2023Hepatology,Angulo2015]]

Name it: the 2023 terms

In 2023, 236 panellists from 56 countries renamed non-alcoholic fatty liver disease. 61% and 66% of respondents felt "nonalcoholic" and "fatty" were stigmatising. Answer three questions to see which name fits. The labels below are the published definitions, used here for learning, not to diagnose anyone. [[Rinella2023MASLD]]

AASLD 2023

Diet, exercise and alcohol

Diets of different composition (low-carbohydrate, low-fat, Mediterranean, intermittent fasting) "appear comparable". At least 150 minutes of exercise a week can prevent or improve the condition. "Patients with clinically significant hepatic fibrosis (≥ F2) should abstain from alcohol use completely." Quoted as published. [[Rinella2023AASLD]]

Established

Bariatric surgery

With repeat biopsies after bariatric surgery, NASH resolved without fibrosis worsening in 84% at 5 years, and fibrosis fell in 70.2%. In SPLENDOR (1,158 people with NASH and obesity), 10-year major adverse liver outcomes were 2.3% after surgery vs 9.6% without (adjusted HR 0.12); 0.6% died of surgical complications in the first year. [[Lassailly2020,Aminian2021JAMA]]

Who is at risk

Diabetes and obesity

In people with type 2 diabetes, 55.5% had NAFLD, 37.3% NASH, and 17.0% of those with NAFLD had advanced fibrosis. AASLD 2023 puts advanced fibrosis at 6–19% in type 2 diabetes and 4–33% in medically complicated obesity, against 0.9–2% in the background population. [[Younossi2019T2D,Rinella2023AASLD]]

Testing

Finding the people with scar

Because fibrosis decides outcomes, the first job is to find out how much scar there is, usually without a biopsy. The FIB-4 score and elastography are explained, with a teaching calculator, in Measuring scarring without a biopsy.

The heart too

Most deaths are not from the liver

Per 1,000 person-years, people with NAFLD had 12.60 deaths from all causes, 4.20 from heart disease and 0.92 from liver disease. In a Swedish nationwide biopsy cohort, most excess deaths were from cancers outside the liver, then cirrhosis, cardiovascular disease and liver cancer. AASLD 2023 calls statins "safe and recommended" for heart risk, including in compensated cirrhosis. [[Younossi2023Hepatology,Simon2021Gut,Rinella2023AASLD]]

Established

Genes, and lean MASLD

PNPLA3 I148M impairs the breakdown of fat in liver droplets; TM6SF2, MBOAT7 and HSD17B13 also modify risk. Lean people with the condition are more often of Hispanic or Asian origin, partly because the variant is commoner. AASLD 2023: genetic testing "is currently not recommended, as it does not alter management". [[Rinella2023AASLD]]

Medicines for MASH

Two medicines are approved in the US for MASH with moderate to advanced scarring before cirrhosis. Both are accelerated approvals based on biopsy results; trials of clinical outcomes are still running. Doses and indications are quoted as published, not as advice. [[FDA-Rezdiffra,FDA-Wegovy]]

Approved (accelerated)

Resmetirom (Rezdiffra)

Approved 14 March 2024. The label indication: "in conjunction with diet and exercise for the treatment of adults with noncirrhotic metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) ... with moderate to advanced liver fibrosis (consistent with stages F2 to F3 fibrosis)." It says to "avoid use" in decompensated cirrhosis and in Child–Pugh B or C. In MAESTRO-NASH (966 people), NASH resolution was 25.9% (80 mg) and 29.9% (100 mg) vs 9.7% on placebo; fibrosis improved in 24.2% and 25.9% vs 14.2%. [[FDA-Rezdiffra,Harrison2024MAESTRO]]

Approved (accelerated)

Semaglutide (Wegovy)

The current label includes treatment of "noncirrhotic metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) ... with moderate to advanced liver fibrosis (consistent with stages F2 to F3 fibrosis) in adults"; Drugs@FDA lists the efficacy supplement on 15 August 2025. In ESSENCE (800 people, 2.4 mg weekly), steatohepatitis resolved in 62.9% vs 34.3%, fibrosis fell in 36.8% vs 22.4%, and weight fell 10.5% vs 2.0%. [[FDA-Wegovy,Sanyal2025ESSENCE]]

Early evidence

Tirzepatide

In the phase 2 SYNERGY-NASH trial (190 people, 52 weeks), MASH resolved without fibrosis worsening in 44%, 56% and 62% on 5, 10 and 15 mg vs 10% on placebo. Not approved for MASH. [[Loomba2024SYNERGY]]

Older trial

Vitamin E and pioglitazone

In PIVENS (adults with NASH without diabetes), vitamin E improved NASH more often than placebo (43% vs 19%). Pioglitazone did not meet the primary endpoint, and neither improved fibrosis. [[Sanyal2010PIVENS]]

Open question

And in cirrhosis?

EASL–EASD–EASO 2024: "No MASH-targeted pharmacotherapy can currently be recommended for the cirrhotic stage." In compensated NASH cirrhosis, semaglutide did not improve fibrosis (11% vs 29% on placebo). Resmetirom is being tested in Child–Pugh A MASH cirrhosis, with primary completion estimated for December 2026. [[EASL2024MASLD,Loomba2023SemaCirrhosis,NCT05500222]]

Research

Still to be proven

Approvals rest on biopsy endpoints. The outcome parts of MAESTRO-NASH (to January 2028) and ESSENCE (to April 2029) test whether the medicines prevent cirrhosis and its complications. See Research toward better treatment. [[NCT03900429,NCT04822181]]
