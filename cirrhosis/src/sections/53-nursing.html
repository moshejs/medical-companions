People with cirrhosis need "constant and rigorous monitoring both in and outside the hospital", and the LiverHope nursing project says nurses with liver expertise should be part of the inpatient and outpatient team, bridging clinicians, families and primary care. [[Fabrellas2020LiverHope]] Cirrhosis has many causes (MASLD, alcohol, viral hepatitis, autoimmune, cholestatic and genetic diseases); the nursing priorities below apply to all of them. Teaching, safety and support

Patient teaching

Diuretics

The spironolactone label says to start it in hospital in cirrhosis and titrate slowly, to check potassium within a week of starting, and warns of high potassium, low sodium, breast enlargement and, in cirrhosis with ascites, sudden fluid and electrolyte shifts that may worsen hepatic encephalopathy. [[FDA-Aldactone]] The furosemide label warns that sudden shifts may precipitate hepatic coma, and that potassium supplements or an aldosterone antagonist help prevent low potassium. [[FDA-Lasix]] Starting both together resolved ascites more often than one after the other (76% vs 56%), with less high potassium (4% vs 18%). [[Tapper2023JAMA]]

Patient teaching

Lactulose and rifaximin

The lactulose label targets "2 or 3 soft stools daily"; LiverHope's nursing table uses 3–4 bowel movements a day, reflecting European practice, so follow the prescriber's target. [[FDA-Enulose,Fabrellas2020LiverHope]] Gas, bloating and cramps affect about 20%; too much causes diarrhoea with fluid loss, low potassium and high sodium. [[FDA-Enulose]] Rifaximin's label: 550 mg twice daily to reduce the risk of overt hepatic encephalopathy recurring, with lactulose in 91% of the pivotal trial. [[FDA-Xifaxan]]

Patient teaching

Carvedilol and other beta-blockers

The carvedilol label says not to stop it abruptly, warns of slow heart rate, low blood pressure and fluid retention, and lists severe hepatic impairment as a contraindication, while Baveno VII names it the preferred beta-blocker in compensated cirrhosis with clinically significant portal hypertension. [[FDA-Coreg,deFranchis2022BavenoVII]] LiverHope suggests teaching home blood pressure and pulse checks. Baveno VII 7.7: with ascites, reduce or stop if systolic pressure stays under 90 mmHg or mean arterial pressure under 65 mmHg and/or with HRS-AKI. [[Fabrellas2020LiverHope,deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]

Safety

Falls with hepatic encephalopathy

In 122 outpatients the 1-year probability of a fall was 52.3% with cognitive dysfunction against 6.5% without (OR 10.2). [[Soriano2012]] After a fall, people with cirrhosis had higher adjusted risks of bleeding in the skull (2.33) and pelvic fracture (1.71). [[Ezaz2018]] Early evidence In LiveSMART, lactulose then tele-tai-chi cut injurious falls from 12% to 4%. [[Tapper2026LiveSMART]] LiverHope lists falls awareness among the three main teaching aims. [[Fabrellas2020LiverHope]]

Transplant evaluation

Supporting someone through assessment

AASLD 2021: frailty or sarcopenia should not be used as an absolute contraindication to transplant. [[Lai2021AASLD]] ACG 2024: selecting people with severe alcohol-associated hepatitis needs rigorous psychosocial evaluation by a multidisciplinary team, using tools such as SIPAT. [[Jophlin2024ACG]] AASLD 2022 asks for goals-of-care conversations during transplant evaluation. [[Rogal2022AASLD]] See liver transplant.

Readmission

Why discharge teaching matters

After a decompensation, 14% of 402 people were readmitted within a week and 37% within a month; 22% of early readmissions were judged possibly preventable. [[Volk2012Readmit]] Hepatic encephalopathy was most strongly linked with readmission (OR 1.77). [[Tapper2016Readmit]] A checklist in electronic ordering (goal-directed lactulose, rifaximin, SBP prophylaxis prompts) went with 40% lower adjusted odds of 30-day readmission. [[Tapper2016QI]]

Established

Albumin with large-volume paracentesis

A meta-analysis of 17 trials (1,225 patients) found albumin reduced circulatory dysfunction after paracentesis (OR 0.39), low sodium (OR 0.58) and death (OR 0.64) compared with alternatives. [[Bernardi2012Hepatology]] In 4,729 paracenteses severe bleeding occurred in 0.19%, unrelated to INR or platelets, mostly in people with severe liver failure and kidney dysfunction. [[Pache2005]]

Care models

Does nurse-coordinated care help?

Early evidence A coordinated outpatient model for ascites reported 12-month mortality of 23.1% vs 45.7% (non-randomised). [[Morando2013]] The ALFIE randomised trial of a liver-nurse-coordinated model found no reduction in overall liver-related emergency admissions (IRR 0.89), but fewer encephalopathy admissions and better quality-of-care measures. [[Wigg2025ALFIE]]
