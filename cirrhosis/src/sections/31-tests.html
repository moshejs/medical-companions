Why a normal-looking liver panel is not the end of the story

Silent phase

Compensated cirrhosis has no symptoms

Cirrhosis begins with an asymptomatic compensated phase; symptoms usually come with decompensation. Feeling well does not tell you how much scar there is. [[Gines2021Lancet]]

A misnomer

"Liver function tests" mostly measure injury

ACG calls "liver function tests" a misnomer and prefers "liver chemistries" or "liver tests". ALT and AST reflect liver-cell injury and alkaline phosphatase and bilirubin reflect bile flow; on their own they do not measure how well the liver works. [[Kwo2017ACG]]

Which "normal"?

Reference ranges differ

ACG gives a "true healthy normal" ALT of 29–33 IU/L for males and 19–25 IU/L for females and says levels above this should be assessed. For hepatitis B, AASLD 2018 recommends upper limits of 35 U/L (men) and 25 U/L (women) rather than local laboratory ranges. [[Kwo2017ACG,Terrault2018AASLD]]

Ratios and counts

The clue may be a ratio or the platelets

In chronic hepatitis B the mean AST/ALT ratio was 0.59 without cirrhosis and 1.02 with it. A platelet count below 160 ×10³/µL raised the likelihood of cirrhosis (LR 6.3). Neither depends on the enzymes being high. [[Williams1988ASTALT,Udell2012JAMA]]

Next step

Scores and scans stage the scar

FIB-4 combines age, AST, ALT and platelets, and elastography measures liver stiffness. The AASLD 2023 fatty liver guidance starts with FIB-4 and moves to elastography or ELF when FIB-4 is 1.3 or higher. [[Rinella2023AASLD]]

Bedside and laboratory clues, ranked

A meta-analysis of 86 studies (19,533 patients, 24% with biopsy-proven cirrhosis) asked which findings most change the odds. A likelihood ratio (LR) above 1 makes cirrhosis more likely; below 1, less likely. The clinician's overall impression was less informative than these individual findings. [[Udell2012JAMA]]
