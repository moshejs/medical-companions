Part 5 · Treatment & living

Living with cirrhosis

An ordinary week with cirrhosis: medicines, daily weights, meals with a late snack, appointments, symptoms and the people who help. Learn which changes mean "call your team" and which mean "get urgent care now", and keep a simple log of your own.

Cirrhosis has many causes, such as fatty liver linked to weight and diabetes, hepatitis viruses, alcohol, and autoimmune or inherited conditions. No cause makes a person less deserving of care. [[Gines2021Lancet]] This page is for people with cirrhosis and the people close to them; it is general education, and your own team's plan always comes first.

Illustrative week. The person, weights and events are invented. The thresholds and advice are quoted from the LiverHope nursing project, EASL 2019, AASLD and the companion's urgent-care wording; your team may give you different numbers. Urgent careVomiting blood, black or tarry stools, new confusion or drowsiness, or fever or belly pain with a swollen abdomen need urgent care: call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department.

Life beyond the liver

Stigma

Stigma and self-stigma

In a survey of 149 people with cirrhosis, 89% agreed with at least one stigma statement; more stigma went with less social support, more depression, worse quality of life and less care-seeking. [[VaughnSandler2014]] People with alcohol dependence are held more responsible for their condition than people with other mental disorders, and negative attitudes among health professionals reduce patients' engagement in care. [[Schomerus2011,vanBoekel2013]] In a US survey, 27.1% of people with cirrhosis reported discrimination in care. [[Rich2026Discrimination]] Stigma is a barrier to care, not a moral fact.

Carers

Carers carry a load too

In PAL-LIVER, 32% of caregivers had high burden, which went with lower quality of life and more depression in the patient. [[Orman2026Caregiver]] Among 127 caregivers of people with decompensated cirrhosis, 52% had significant anxiety and 35% depression symptoms, and over 85% had never discussed end-of-life preferences with the hepatologist. [[Li2026Caregivers]] AASLD 2022: "Caring for caregivers is a central component of providing palliative and hospice care". [[Rogal2022AASLD]]

Palliative care

Extra support, alongside treatment

AASLD 2022 describes palliative care as specialised care for the physical, spiritual and psychosocial needs of people with serious illness and their caregivers, which can be considered at any stage of cirrhosis alongside treatment aimed at the liver, including while waiting for a transplant. Yet "Only 11% of patients with cirrhosis receive specialty palliative care or hospice care referrals". [[Rogal2022AASLD]] Early evidence In a small pilot, early palliative consultation lowered the hazard of readmission (HR 0.36). [[Shinall2019COMPASS]]

Planning ahead

Talking early about what matters

AASLD 2022: "ACP is an iterative process that should start with a diagnosis of cirrhosis and preferably occur before hepatic decompensation and loss of decision-making capacity". It includes naming the person who should speak for you, in your preferred language with a professional interpreter if needed. [[Rogal2022AASLD]] In a survey of liver specialists, 81% thought advance care planning happens too late. [[Ufere2019Barriers]] Numbers about survival describe large groups of people, not what will happen to you.

Mental health

Feeling low is common, and treatable

In a US survey of 1,021 people with cirrhosis, 15.6% had moderately severe to severe depression and 42.6% high anxiety; fear of liver cancer went with both. [[Hernaez2022Depression]] In a Korean nationwide cohort, the risk of suicide was higher after a new diagnosis of cirrhosis (HR 2.37), most in the first 2 years. [[Jang2021Suicide]] If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line now.

Work and money

Costs beyond the clinic

In 104 people with cirrhosis, only 56% were employed; after hepatic encephalopathy 87.5% were unemployed against 19% without it. [[Bajaj2011Burden]] Among 453 US transplant candidates, 23.3% spent 10% or more of their income on care, which went with delayed or skipped care. [[Aby2026Financial]] Ask your team about social work and benefits support.

Intimacy

Sexual health

AASLD 2022: about two thirds of men and 80% of women with cirrhosis have sexual dysfunction; the first steps are reviewing medicines and substances that can cause it, and treating contributors such as depression and diabetes. [[Rogal2022AASLD]] Early evidence In one trial, tadalafil improved erectile function without changing portal pressure. [[Jagdish2023Tadalafil]]

Knowing more helps

Learning to self-manage

In 150 outpatients only 53% of self-management questions were answered correctly at first (most missed: the sodium in sea salt and the safety of paracetamol and statins); a short booklet raised that to 67%. [[Volk2013Knowledge]] Early evidence In a pilot app that tracked medicines, salt, daily weights and thinking tests, alerts were judged to have prevented 8 possible readmissions for hepatic encephalopathy.
