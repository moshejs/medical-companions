Other causes: genes, immunity & bile ducts

Cirrhosis has many causes beyond alcohol and fatty liver disease: iron or copper overload, a misfolded protein, the immune system turning on the liver or its bile ducts, medicines, and a heart circulation that backs up into the liver [[Gines2021Lancet]]. Inheritance: two copies needed

Wilson disease is autosomal recessive [[Ferenci2003Leipzig]], and HFE haemochromatosis is diagnosed from raised iron indices in people with two copies of C282Y [[EASL2022HH]]. Two altered copies means one from each parent; a carrier has one copy. Choose the parents to see each child's chances.

Which test points to which cause?

When liver tests are abnormal, the ACG workup for hepatocellular injury covers viral hepatitis, fatty liver disease, alcohol-related liver disease, haemochromatosis, autoimmune hepatitis, Wilson disease, alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency and a medicine history; a raised liver alkaline phosphatase prompts testing for PBC and PSC [[Kwo2017ACG]]. Treatment updates and open questions

PBC · 2024–25

Two new second-line drugs

For people with an inadequate response to ursodiol: in ELATIVE, elafibranor (a PPAR α/δ agonist) gave a biochemical response in 51% versus 4% on placebo [[Kowdley2024ELATIVE]]; in RESPONSE, seladelpar (a PPAR-δ agonist) in 61.7% versus 20.0%, and the itch score fell by 3.2 versus 1.7 points [[Hirschfield2024RESPONSE]].

The FDA approved Iqirvo (elafibranor) on 10 June 2024 and Livdelzi (seladelpar) on 14 August 2024, both under accelerated approval based on ALP reduction. Both labels state that improvements in survival or prevention of decompensation "have not been demonstrated" [[FDA-Iqirvo,FDA-Livdelzi]].

Two phase 3 trials published in November 2026 tested the same drugs in people whose ALP was above normal but below 1.67 × ULN. After a year, ALP was back to normal in 75.4% on seladelpar versus 16.1% on placebo (IDEAL, 96 people) and in 85.3% on elafibranor versus 23.3% (ELSPIRE, 92 people). Both measured a blood test, not survival or decompensation. Early evidence [[Levy2026IDEAL,Kowdley2026ELSPIRE]]

PBC · obeticholic acid

A drug that left the market

Obeticholic acid met POISE's biochemical endpoint in 46–47% versus 10% [[Nevens2016POISE]]. In the confirmatory COBALT trial, clinical outcomes occurred in 28.6% versus 28.9% on placebo (HR 1.01), although unblinding and crossover confounded it; only an external-control comparison favoured the drug [[Kowdley2025COBALT]]. Drugs@FDA now lists Ocaliva as Discontinued, and its PBC authorisations were withdrawn in the EU and US [[FDA-Ocaliva,Fiorucci2026OCA]].

PBC · response

Why the ALP number matters

In the GLOBAL PBC study of 4,845 patients, 84% with ALP ≤2.0 × ULN after a year of treatment survived 10 years without transplant, against 62% above it; for bilirubin ≤1.0 × ULN, 86% against 41%. These are population figures [[Lammers2014]].

Alpha-1Early evidence

Silencing the faulty protein

Fazirsiran, an RNA interference drug, lowered liver Z-AAT by a median of 83% in an open-label trial, with fibrosis regressing in 7 of 15 people [[Strnad2022Fazirsiran]]. In the placebo-controlled SEQUOIA trial, serum Z-AAT fell by 61%, 83% and 94% across doses [[Clark2024SEQUOIA]]. No phase 3 outcome data yet.

Wilson

Chelators compared

In CHELATE (53 stable adults), trientine tetrahydrochloride was non-inferior to penicillamine for maintenance therapy [[Schilsky2022CHELATE]]. With early diagnosis and treatment, the outlook is described as good [[Czlonkowska2018]].

CryptogenicEarly evidence

When no cause is found

In 70 people with "cryptogenic" cirrhosis, 73% had type 2 diabetes, obesity or both, suggesting that NASH (now MASH) plays an under-recognised role [[Caldwell1999]]. Under the 2023 nomenclature, steatotic liver disease with no metabolic risk factors and no known cause is called cryptogenic steatotic liver disease [[Rinella2023MASLDD]]. See Fatty liver disease.

Get urgent care for: vomiting blood, black or tarry stools, new confusion or drowsiness, or fever or belly pain with a swollen abdomen. Call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department.
