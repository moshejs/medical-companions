Part 3 · Diagnosis & staging

Child–Pugh & MELD scores

Two families of scores describe how advanced cirrhosis is. Child–Pugh adds up five findings into classes A, B and C; MELD turns three lab values into a number, later refined with sodium, sex and albumin.

Child–Pugh points per item are drawn as 1, 2 or 3 without their thresholds: we could not verify those cut-offs against the original 1973 table. MELD numbers are calculated live from the published formulas. [[Costa2023ChildB,InfanteRivard1987,Kim2008MELDNa,Kim2021MELD3]]

A teaching tool, not for clinical or allocation decisionsThese are the formulas as published, to show how each lab value moves the score. Real scores are calculated by transplant centres and registries with their own rules and data checks. This page must never be used to judge prognosis, treatment or a place on a transplant list. Units here are mg/dL for bilirubin and creatinine, mmol/L for sodium and g/dL for albumin, as in the published formulas.

Child–Pugh: the five items

Child and Turcotte built the original classification in 1964 to estimate surgical risk before portal-hypertension surgery, using ascites, encephalopathy, nutritional status, bilirubin and albumin. Pugh and colleagues replaced nutritional status with prothrombin time (now often INR) in 1973. Each item scores 1 to 3 points, so the total runs from 5 to 15. Reading the numbers fairly

Groups, not people

Scores describe populations

Child–Pugh and MELD scores describe how sick the liver is for groups of people. They cannot tell any one person what will happen. Across 119 studies, the two had similar prognostic value in most settings. [[Kim2021MELD3,Peng2016ChildMELD]]

Anchors

What the published bands say

On the US waiting list in 1999–2001, 3-month mortality was 1.9% with MELD below 9 and 71.3% with MELD 40 or more. At a MELD 3.0 of 20, predicted mortality was 1.9% at 30 days and 5.4% at 90 days. These are population statistics. [[Wiesner2003,Kim2021MELD3]]

Fairness

Why sex and albumin were added

MELD 3.0 added female sex and albumin. In early UNOS data after adoption, female sex was no longer a significant risk factor for missing transplant or for waitlist death; these results are preliminary. Early evidence [[Kim2021MELD3,Akabane2025MELD3]]

Beyond transplant

Other uses of MELD

MELD also predicts survival with infection, variceal bleeding, fulminant liver failure and alcohol-associated hepatitis, and is used for risk before non-transplant surgery. [[Kamath2007MELD]]

When to get urgent careVomiting blood, black or tarry stools, new confusion or drowsiness, or fever or belly pain with a swollen abdomen need urgent care: call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department.
