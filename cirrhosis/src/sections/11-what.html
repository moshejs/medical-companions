Part 1 · Understanding cirrhosis

What cirrhosis is

Cirrhosis is what a liver becomes after years of injury: healthy tissue is replaced by scar and lumpy regenerating nodules, and blood pressure in the portal vein rises. It has many causes, it is common, and for a long time it is silent. [[Gines2021Lancet]]

The word, the liver, the body

The word

"Cirrhosis" means tawny

René Laennec named it in 1819, in a brief footnote to his book on listening to the chest (De l'auscultation médiate). He took the Greek kirrhos, an orange-yellow, tawny colour, for the look of the scarred liver. The lesion itself had been recognised in England more than a century earlier, and the eponym "Laennec's cirrhosis" spread in North America through William Osler's textbook. [[Duffin1987]]

The liver

Scar, nodules and pressure

Long-lasting inflammation replaces healthy liver tissue with fibrous scar and regenerative nodules. Blood that should flow easily through the liver meets resistance, so pressure rises in the portal vein: portal hypertension. Portal hypertension, body-wide inflammation and failing liver function drive what happens next. [[Gines2021Lancet]] See how scar forms in From injury to scar.

Two phases

Compensated, then decompensated

Compensated cirrhosis means no present or past complications; the liver is coping and most people feel well. Decompensated cirrhosis begins with overt ascites, overt hepatic encephalopathy or variceal bleeding, and the risk of death rises with that step. [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII,Gines2021Lancet]] The course is told in Compensated to decompensated.

Many causes, not one

Cirrhosis follows fatty liver disease linked to excess weight and diabetes (MASLD), high alcohol intake, hepatitis B or C, autoimmune and cholestatic (bile-duct) diseases, and iron or copper overload. It is not caused by alcohol alone. [[Gines2021Lancet]] Causes also overlap: in a US review, alcohol use disorder was present in about 45% of cases, fatty liver disease in 26% and hepatitis C in 41%, adding to more than 100% because many people have more than one. [[Tapper2023JAMA]]

In 2023 a Delphi process of 236 panellists from 56 countries renamed non-alcoholic fatty liver disease as metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), diagnosed with at least 1 of 5 cardiometabolic risk factors, and created MetALD for people with MASLD who also drink more alcohol. [[Rinella2023MASLD]]

Two things are true at once. The number of deaths from cirrhosis rose from 1990 to 2017, while the age-standardised death rate fell from 21.0 to 16.5 per 100,000: populations grew and aged. Symptoms, in plain words

Often nothing at first

Compensated cirrhosis is the phase without symptoms; symptoms usually appear with decompensation. [[Gines2021Lancet]] About 40% of people are first diagnosed when they arrive with a complication such as confusion or fluid in the belly. [[Tapper2023JAMA]] Day to day, many people notice muscle cramps (about 64%), poor sleep (63%), sexual problems (53%) or itch (39%). Many of these can be treated, so they are worth raising. [[Tapper2023JAMA]]

Signs that the liver is struggling

  • A swelling belly from fluid (ascites)
  • Confusion, sleepiness or personality change (hepatic encephalopathy)
  • Vomiting blood or black stools from bleeding varices
  • Yellow skin or eyes (jaundice)

Words matter

Most people with cirrhosis feel judged at some point: in a survey of 149 people with cirrhosis, 89% agreed with at least one of 18 stigma statements, and more stigma went with less social support, more depression and less care-seeking. [[VaughnSandler2014]] Tap a card to turn a label into language that describes a person, not a verdict.

Older papers keep their published titles, such as "alcoholic liver disease"; this guide uses current terms in its own words.
