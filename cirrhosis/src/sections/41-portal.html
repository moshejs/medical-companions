Portal hypertension & varices

Scar makes it hard for blood from the gut to pass through the liver, so pressure builds in the portal vein. Watch the pressure gauge climb, blood find detours, a varix bleed, and treatment bring the pressure down.

Urgent careVomiting blood, black or tarry stools, new confusion or drowsiness, or fever or belly pain with a swollen abdomen need urgent care: call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department.

Schematic, not to scale. The needle follows one illustrative person; the coloured zones are the published thresholds: above 5 mmHg is portal hypertension and 10 mmHg or more is clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH) [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]; no variceal bleeding occurred below 12 mmHg in a 1985 series [[GarciaTsao1985Hepatology]]; above 20 mmHg at the time of a bleed is one criterion for pre-emptive TIPS [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]].

The pressure thresholds

Doctors measure portal pressure indirectly as the hepatic venous pressure gradient (HVPG): a balloon catheter is passed through the jugular vein into a hepatic vein, and the free pressure is subtracted from the wedged pressure [[DiazSoto2022TAG]]. Baveno VII prefers a compliant balloon-occlusion catheter and at least 1 minute of stabilisation for the wedged reading [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]].

In primary biliary cholangitis an extra pre-sinusoidal component means HVPG may underestimate portal hypertension [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]. Without a catheter, liver stiffness and the platelet count can rule CSPH in or out: see the endoscopy rule below and Measuring scarring without a biopsy.

A variceal bleed: the first hours, as the guidelines lay them out

Bleeding from varices is an emergency. Each bleed carries a 6-week mortality of about 15–25% in the EASL guideline, and 15–20% in recent series [[EASL2018Decomp,DiazSoto2022TAG]]. Hospitals treat it as a bundle: medicine through a drip, antibiotics, a camera test to put bands on the veins, and careful blood transfusion [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]. Who needs a screening endoscopy? The Baveno VII rule

Teaching toolThis shows how a published rule is built. It is never for diagnosis or for deciding anyone's care: only a care team can do that.

Rule as stated by Baveno VII for people who cannot take NSBBs: endoscopy if liver stiffness is ≥20 kPa or platelets ≤150×10⁹/L; below both thresholds, endoscopy can be avoided and elastography and platelets repeated yearly [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]].

Preventing problems before they start

Baveno VII

Skipping the camera test

People on NSBBs to prevent decompensation do not need screening endoscopy, because the result would not change management. Those who cannot take NSBBs can avoid it if liver stiffness is below 20 kPa and platelets above 150×10⁹/L [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]. In the ANTICIPATE cohort (518 people), stiffness plus platelets picked out 30% of patients with a <5% risk of varices needing treatment [[Abraldes2016Anticipate]].

Medicine

Carvedilol

Baveno VII names carvedilol the preferred NSBB in compensated cirrhosis: it adds anti-α1-adrenergic vasodilation to non-selective β-blockade and lowers HVPG more [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII,DiazSoto2022TAG]]. In a meta-analysis of 4 trials (352 people with CSPH) it lowered decompensation (SHR 0.506) and death (SHR 0.417) [[Villanueva2022Carvedilol]]. Against band ligation (152 people), first bleeds were 10% vs 23%, with no difference in mortality [[Tripathi2009Hepatology]]. Its US label warns against stopping abruptly and lists bradycardia, hypotension and fluid retention; it is contraindicated in severe hepatic impairment [[FDA-Coreg]].

Established

Treating the cause lowers the pressure

Removing or suppressing the cause, such as hepatitis C cure, hepatitis B suppression or long-term abstinence from alcohol, produces potentially meaningful falls in HVPG in most people and substantially reduces the risk of decompensation [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]. Cirrhosis has many causes, including MASLD, viral hepatitis, genetic and autoimmune disease: see fatty liver disease and hepatitis B and C.

Not for everyone

When β-blockers do not help

Timolol did not prevent varices in unselected people with HVPG ≥6 mmHg (39% vs 40%) and caused more serious adverse events (18% vs 6%) [[Groszmann2005NEJM]]. Baveno VII states there is no indication for NSBBs without CSPH [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]. In people with ascites it advises reducing or stopping them if systolic pressure falls below 90 mmHg, mean arterial pressure below 65 mmHg, or HRS-AKI develops [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]].

Early evidence

Statins

Statins may lower portal pressure. Baveno VII encourages them for people with cirrhosis who have an approved indication, at a lower dose in Child–Pugh B/C (as published, simvastatin up to 20 mg/day) [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII,DiazSoto2022TAG]]. See Research toward better treatment.

How often

Varices and first bleeds

Oesophageal varices are present in 50–60% of people with compensated cirrhosis and up to 85% with decompensated cirrhosis. A first bleed occurs at 10–15% a year, depending on risk factors [[DiazSoto2022TAG]]. In 206 people with no or small varices, new varices appeared in 5% at 1 year and 28% at 3 years [[Merli2003JHepatol]]. Gastric varices occur in about 20% and bleed less often but more severely [[EASL2018Decomp,Sarin1992Hepatology]].

Where the evidence is still argued

Controversy

Pre-emptive TIPS in Child–Pugh B

A 34-centre observational study found no mortality benefit in Child–Pugh B with active bleeding, while in Child–Pugh C 1-year mortality was 22% vs 47% [[HernandezGea2019Hepatology]]. An individual-patient meta-analysis of 1327 patients did find a benefit, without more encephalopathy [[NicoaraFarcau2021Gastro]]. Baveno VII includes Child–Pugh B >7 with active bleeding [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]].

Controversy

Restrictive transfusion in Child–Pugh C

In the 2013 trial the survival benefit of a restrictive threshold was significant in Child–Pugh A/B (HR 0.30) but not in Child–Pugh C (HR 1.04), and portal pressure rose with liberal transfusion [[Villanueva2013NEJM]]. Baveno VII keeps a target of 7–8 g/dl, adjusted for heart disease, age, haemodynamics and ongoing bleeding [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII]].

Rescue

When bleeding will not stop

Up to 10–15% of people keep bleeding or rebleed early despite drugs, ligation and antibiotics; salvage covered TIPS is the rescue therapy [[EASL2018Decomp,deFranchis2022BavenoVII]]. Balloon tamponade or a self-expanding oesophageal stent is only a bridge, and EASL limits tamponade to 24 hours [[deFranchis2022BavenoVII,EASL2018Decomp]]. In a 28-patient trial the stent succeeded more often (66% vs 20%) with fewer serious adverse events [[Escorsell2016Hepatology]].

The survival numbers on this page describe large groups of people and cannot predict what will happen to any one person. Care has improved a lot: at one French centre, in-hospital mortality after a variceal bleed fell from 42.6% in 1980 to 14.5% in 2000 [[Carbonell2004Hepatology]].
