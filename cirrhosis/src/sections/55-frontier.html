Reading research news

How to read a headline

"Reverses fibrosis" in whom, measured how?

  • Which stage? Most positive drug trials enrolled F2–F3. In compensated cirrhosis, semaglutide showed no fibrosis benefit at 48 weeks, and efruxifermin missed its week-36 goal. [[Loomba2023SemaCirrhosis,Noureddin2025SYMMETRY]]
  • Biopsy or outcomes? A one-stage biopsy improvement is a surrogate. Outcome trials in cirrhosis report from about 2026 to 2030. [[NCT05500222,NCT06528314]]
  • Against what? Placebo groups improve too: 14.2% in MAESTRO-NASH, 22.4% in ESSENCE. [[Harrison2024MAESTRO,Sanyal2025ESSENCE]]
  • Primary or secondary? BLEPS missed its main goal (rebleeding); its survival signal was secondary. [[Abraldes2016BLEPS]]
  • Open-label and small? MATCH was a small open-label trial; a larger blinded one is needed. [[Brennan2026MATCHLTFU]]
Established

The best-proven antifibrotic is removing the cause

Treating hepatitis B or C, stopping alcohol and losing weight are among the best-proven ways to let scar regress. Curing or controlling the primary disease is the first of four antifibrotic strategies. See From injury to scar. [[Kisseleva2021,Lee2015Gut,Marcellin2013,VilarGomez2015]]

Joining research

Asking about a trial

Trials listed here are registered on ClinicalTrials.gov, where each NCT number shows who can take part, where and whether it is still recruiting. A liver team can say whether a trial fits someone's stage and health, and what taking part would involve. Questions worth asking: what is being compared, how long it lasts, and what happens at the end.

Caution

Label and guideline wording

The US resmetirom label covers adults with noncirrhotic MASH and moderate to advanced fibrosis (F2–F3) under accelerated approval, and says to avoid use in decompensated cirrhosis. EASL–EASD–EASO 2024: "No MASH-targeted pharmacotherapy can currently be recommended for the cirrhotic stage." Quoted as published. [[FDA-Rezdiffra,EASL2024MASLD]]
