Part 2 · Causes

Other causes: genes, immunity & bile ducts

Cirrhosis has many causes beyond alcohol and fatty liver disease: iron or copper overload, a misfolded protein, the immune system turning on the liver or its bile ducts, medicines, and a heart circulation that backs up into the liver 180. Genetic · iron

Haemochromatosis

Two copies of the HFE C282Y variant are common: 0.44% (about 1 in 227) of non-Hispanic white people in HEIRS 7 and 1 in 156 people of European descent in UK Biobank 354. Far fewer become ill, and men far more than women: iron-overload disease in 28.4% of homozygous men versus 1.2% of women in Melbourne 12. Ferritin tracks the danger: the adjusted probability of cirrhosis was 7.4% with ferritin below 1,000 µg/L and 72% above it 315. Phlebotomy is the mainstay of treatment 244; EASL 2022 targets ferritin below 50 µg/L during induction and below 100 during maintenance, and people with advanced fibrosis enter HCC screening 108.

In plain wordsHaemochromatosis means the body stores too much iron. Regular blood removal, like giving blood, lowers the iron and can prevent liver damage 108.
Exam hookIn HFE haemochromatosis, ferritin below 1,000 µg/L makes cirrhosis unlikely 244,315.

Inheritance: two copies needed

Wilson disease is autosomal recessive 154, and HFE haemochromatosis is diagnosed from raised iron indices in people with two copies of C282Y 108. Two altered copies means one from each parent; a carrier has one copy. Choose the parents to see each child's chances.

haemochromatosis · HFE

25% of children with two copies

The chances come from Mendel's rules: each parent passes on one of their two copies at random, so each child is one square of the Punnett square. They are the same for every pregnancy, whatever happened to brothers or sisters.

Two copies of C282Y are needed for HFE haemochromatosis to be diagnosed from raised iron indices 108, but two copies are not the disease: iron-overload disease developed in 28.4% of homozygous men and 1.2% of homozygous women followed for about 12 years in Melbourne 12, and by the end of UK Biobank follow-up haemochromatosis had been diagnosed in 21.7% of homozygous men and 9.8% of women 354.

Which test?

Haemochromatosis

Iron studies are the screen. EASL 2022 states that in C282Y homozygotes, TSAT above 45% with ferritin above 200 µg/L in females, or TSAT above 50% with ferritin above 300 µg/L in males and postmenopausal women, are enough to diagnose haemochromatosis 108. A ferritin below 1,000 ng/mL at diagnosis identifies people at low risk of advanced fibrosis (ACG 2019) 244.

Treatment updates and open questions

PBC · 2024–25

Two new second-line drugs

For people with an inadequate response to ursodiol: in ELATIVE, elafibranor (a PPAR α/δ agonist) gave a biochemical response in 51% versus 4% on placebo 245; in RESPONSE, seladelpar (a PPAR-δ agonist) in 61.7% versus 20.0%, and the itch score fell by 3.2 versus 1.7 points 200.

The FDA approved Iqirvo (elafibranor) on 10 June 2024 and Livdelzi (seladelpar) on 14 August 2024, both under accelerated approval based on ALP reduction. Both labels state that improvements in survival or prevention of decompensation "have not been demonstrated" 136,138.

PBC · obeticholic acid

A drug that left the market

Obeticholic acid met POISE's biochemical endpoint in 46–47% versus 10% 330. In the confirmatory COBALT trial, clinical outcomes occurred in 28.6% versus 28.9% on placebo (HR 1.01), although unblinding and crossover confounded it; only an external-control comparison favoured the drug 246. Drugs@FDA now lists Ocaliva as Discontinued, and its PBC authorisations were withdrawn in the EU and US 141,159.

PBC · response

Why the ALP number matters

In the GLOBAL PBC study of 4,845 patients, 84% with ALP ≤2.0 × ULN after a year of treatment survived 10 years without transplant, against 62% above it; for bilirubin ≤1.0 × ULN, 86% against 41%. These are population figures 257.

Alpha-1Early evidence

Silencing the faulty protein

Fazirsiran, an RNA interference drug, lowered liver Z-AAT by a median of 83% in an open-label trial, with fibrosis regressing in 7 of 15 people 440. In the placebo-controlled SEQUOIA trial, serum Z-AAT fell by 61%, 83% and 94% across doses 80. No phase 3 outcome data yet.

Wilson

Chelators compared

In CHELATE (53 stable adults), trientine tetrahydrochloride was non-inferior to penicillamine for maintenance therapy 407. With early diagnosis and treatment, the outlook is described as good 90.

CryptogenicEarly evidence

When no cause is found

In 70 people with "cryptogenic" cirrhosis, 73% had type 2 diabetes, obesity or both, suggesting that NASH (now MASH) plays an under-recognised role 57. Under the 2023 nomenclature, steatotic liver disease with no metabolic risk factors and no known cause is called cryptogenic steatotic liver disease 383. See Fatty liver disease.
