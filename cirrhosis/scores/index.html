Part 3 · Diagnosis & staging

Child–Pugh & MELD scores

Two families of scores describe how advanced cirrhosis is. Child–Pugh adds up five findings into classes A, B and C; MELD turns three lab values into a number, later refined with sodium, sex and albumin.

Five items, 1–3 points each

Child and Turcotte's classification, made to estimate surgical risk in portosystemic shunt surgery, used ascites, encephalopathy, nutritional status, bilirubin and albumin. Pugh and colleagues replaced nutritional status with prothrombin time/INR. Each of the five items scores 1 to 3 points. Two of them, ascites and encephalopathy, are judged at the bedside and so are partly subjective. 86,218,351,367

In plain wordsChild–Pugh adds up points for three blood tests and two things a clinician sees: fluid in the belly and confusion. More points means the liver is struggling more.

Child–Pugh: the five items

Child and Turcotte built the original classification in 1964 to estimate surgical risk before portal-hypertension surgery, using ascites, encephalopathy, nutritional status, bilirubin and albumin. Pugh and colleagues replaced nutritional status with prothrombin time (now often INR) in 1973. Each item scores 1 to 3 points, so the total runs from 5 to 15. 76,86,351,367

Reading the numbers fairly

Groups, not people

Scores describe populations

Child–Pugh and MELD scores describe how sick the liver is for groups of people. They cannot tell any one person what will happen. Across 119 studies, the two had similar prognostic value in most settings. 236,351

Anchors

What the published bands say

On the US waiting list in 1999–2001, 3-month mortality was 1.9% with MELD below 9 and 71.3% with MELD 40 or more. At a MELD 3.0 of 20, predicted mortality was 1.9% at 30 days and 5.4% at 90 days. These are population statistics. 236,496

Fairness

Why sex and albumin were added

MELD 3.0 added female sex and albumin. In early UNOS data after adoption, female sex was no longer a significant risk factor for missing transplant or for waitlist death; these results are preliminary. Early evidence 10,236

Beyond transplant

Other uses of MELD

MELD also predicts survival with infection, variceal bleeding, fulminant liver failure and alcohol-associated hepatitis, and is used for risk before non-transplant surgery. 222

Before this: · Complications:

Cirrhosis Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cirrhosis, its staging scores and its complications, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with cirrhosis, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, a diet or a care plan without your own care team. FIB-4, the elastography bands and the Child–Pugh and MELD calculators are for learning, never for diagnosis, prognosis or transplant decisions, or for reading your own results. Doses, sodium and protein targets, paracetamol limits and guideline statements are quoted as published, never as instructions, and survival figures are population statistics, not a forecast for any one person.
