An animated, cited guide for students, people with cirrhosis, and their families and caregivers. Educational only, not medical advice. Your place is saved on this device.

Not medical adviceThe Cirrhosis Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, dietitian, pharmacist or other clinician. FIB-4, the elastography bands and the Child–Pugh and MELD calculators are teaching tools, never for diagnosis, prognosis or transplant decisions, and doses, sodium and protein targets, paracetamol limits and guideline statements are quoted as published, never as instructions. Vomiting blood, black or tarry stools, new confusion or drowsiness, or fever or belly pain with a swollen abdomen need urgent care: call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department.

ACLF (acute-on-chronic liver failure)A severe form of acutely decompensated cirrhosis with organ failure and high short-term mortality, driven by an excessive inflammatory response to triggers such as infection 109. 28-day mortality 33.9% in CANONIC 319.
AcamprosateAn AUD medicine that is not metabolised by the liver; contraindicated in severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance 30 mL/min or less) 129.
Advance care planningAn iterative process that AASLD says should start at the diagnosis of cirrhosis, before decompensation and loss of capacity 392.
AFP (alpha-fetoprotein)A blood marker used with ultrasound for HCC surveillance; at 20 ng/mL it is about 60% sensitive, and over 40% of HCCs have normal AFP 434.
AKI (acute kidney injury)In cirrhosis, a creatinine rise of 0.3 mg/dl or more within 48 h, or 50% or more within 7 days 106. It affects about 20% of people hospitalised with cirrhosis 171.
ALBI gradeA score using only albumin and bilirubin; it performed at least as well as Child–Pugh in more than 6,000 people with HCC 219.
AlbuminThe most abundant protein the liver secretes, about 55% of plasma protein; the liver makes about 10.5 g a day 266,472.
Albumin infusionUsed for SBP, AKI beyond stage 1A, large-volume paracentesis and with terlipressin for HRS-AKI, per Baveno VII 97.
Alcohol-associated hepatitisAn acute syndrome of jaundice and liver failure in people drinking heavily; severe cases carry 20–50% 1-month mortality (ACG 2024) 220.
Alcohol-related liver disease (ARLD)A spectrum from fatty liver to steatohepatitis, fibrosis, cirrhosis and in some people HCC 420. AASLD uses "alcohol-associated liver diseases" 88.
Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiencyIn Pi*ZZ, misfolded Z protein builds up in hepatocytes and can cause liver fibrosis 445.
ALT (alanine aminotransferase)A liver enzyme marking hepatocellular injury; ACG gives a "true healthy normal" of 29–33 IU/L for males and 19–25 IU/L for females 251.
AmmoniaMostly made in the gut; cleared by the urea cycle, and in liver disease partly by muscle, which is why sarcopenia raises HE risk 397. A raised level alone does not diagnose HE 490.
Animal naming testNaming animals in 1 minute: fewer than 10 (age- and education-adjusted) is abnormal and predicts overt HE 60.
APRIAST-to-platelet ratio index; at 1.0, 76% sensitive and 72% specific for cirrhosis in hepatitis C 272.
AscitesFluid in the abdomen, the most common decompensating event, from renal sodium retention plus portal hypertension 106.
AsterixisA flapping tremor: brief loss of muscle tone while holding a posture, first called the "liver flap" 287. A sign of West Haven grade II HE 490.
AST/ALT ratioOften rises above 1 as cirrhosis develops in chronic hepatitis; above 2 is usual in alcohol-related liver disease 503.
Atezolizumab–bevacizumabFirst-choice first-line systemic therapy for BCLC C HCC with preserved liver function 379; HR for death 0.58 vs sorafenib in IMbrave150 159.
Autoimmune hepatitisImmune attack on the liver, diagnosed from transaminases, IgG, autoantibodies and interface hepatitis; treated with steroids and azathioprine 312.
BaclofenAn AUD medicine with an RCT in cirrhosis: abstinence 71% vs 29% 8.
Band ligationEndoscopic rubber bands on varices: the recommended endoscopic treatment for oesophageal variceal bleeding 97.
Bariatric surgeryIn MASH, resolution without fibrosis worsening in 84% at 5 years 260; major adverse liver outcomes 2.3% vs 9.6% at 10 years in SPLENDOR 13.
Baveno VIIThe 2021 consensus on portal hypertension, latest in a series begun in Groningen in 1986 97.
BCLC stagingBarcelona Clinic Liver Cancer stages 0, A, B, C and D link HCC stage to treatment 379.
Beta-blockers (NSBBs)Propranolol, nadolol or carvedilol lower portal pressure; PREDESCI cut decompensation or death from 27% to 16% 97,488.
BilirubinA pigment the liver conjugates and excretes; a raised conjugated bilirubin implies liver disease or biliary obstruction 251.
BulevirtideAn HDV entry inhibitor; FDA accelerated approval on 22 May 2026 for chronic HDV without cirrhosis or with compensated cirrhosis 135.
cACLDCompensated advanced chronic liver disease; liver stiffness under 10 kPa rules it out and over 15 kPa is highly suggestive 97.
Caput medusaeVisible swollen veins around the navel from paraumbilical collaterals in portal hypertension. As collaterals form, gut blood flow rises 101.
CarvedilolA non-selective β-blocker with anti-α1 vasodilation; Baveno VII's preferred NSBB in compensated cirrhosis 97.
Caudate lobe hypertrophyIn cirrhosis the right lobe shrinks and the caudate enlarges; the caudate-to-right-lobe ratio separated cirrhotic livers with 100% specificity in a small series 192.
Child–Pugh scoreBilirubin, albumin, INR, ascites and encephalopathy, 1–3 points each: A 5–6, B 7–9, C 10–15 86,208.
CholangiocytesThe cells lining bile ducts, the second most abundant epithelial cell of the liver 472.
Cholestatic patternAlkaline phosphatase raised out of proportion to AST and ALT 251.
CholestyramineFirst-line treatment for cholestatic itch in AASLD guidance 392.
CIWA-ArA 10-item scale for alcohol withdrawal severity 446.
CLDQChronic Liver Disease Questionnaire: 29 items in six domains of quality of life 512.
CollagenCollagens I and III dominate the scar; advanced fibrosis holds about 6 times normal matrix 28.
Compensated cirrhosisCirrhosis with no present or past complications; split by the absence or presence of CSPH 97.
Covert hepatic encephalopathyMinimal HE plus grade I 490; pooled prevalence 40.9% 289.
Cryptogenic cirrhosisCirrhosis without a known cause; 73% of one series had diabetes, obesity or both, pointing to MASH 57.
CSPH (clinically significant portal hypertension)HVPG 10 mmHg or more 97.
Cyclosporin AThe immunosuppressant Calne reported in 1979, which made transplant practical 58.
DAAs (direct-acting antivirals)Hepatitis C tablets targeting NS3/4A, NS5A and NS5B; cure in more than 90% 293.
DecompensationOvert ascites, overt HE (West Haven II or higher) or variceal bleeding 97.
DiureticsSpironolactone, then furosemide, for ascites; EASL quotes 100–400 mg and 40–160 mg a day, as published 106.
DILI (drug-induced liver injury)A diagnosis of exclusion; antimicrobials, supplements and anticancer drugs are the common causes in the West 65.
Dry weightBody weight corrected for ascites (subtract 5%, 10% or 15%) and oedema, used for nutrition targets 107,257.
Early liver transplant (alcohol-associated hepatitis)Transplant before 6 months of abstinence in highly selected people: 6-month survival 77% vs 23% 298.
ELF testEnhanced Liver Fibrosis panel of hyaluronic acid, TIMP-1 and PIIINP; 9.8 or more marks higher risk in NAFLD 386,398.
EntecavirA preferred HBV antiviral; label 0.5 mg daily compensated, 1 mg decompensated, as published 128,464.
Erectile dysfunctionReported in 79% of men with cirrhosis in a meta-analysis 511.
Extracellular matrix (ECM)The protein scaffold, mainly collagen, that builds up in fibrosis 28.
FazirsiranAn RNAi drug cutting liver Z-AAT in alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (Early evidence) 80,445.
FenestraePores of 50–180 nm in sinusoidal endothelial cells that let plasma reach hepatocytes 472.
FIB-4Age × AST / (platelets × √ALT); in MASLD under 1.3 is low risk and over 2.67 high 386,443.
FibrosisExcessive build-up of extracellular matrix in chronic liver disease 28.
Fontan-associated liver diseaseLiver fibrosis from chronically high venous pressure after Fontan surgery; cumulative cirrhosis 32.2% at 20 years or more 202.
FrailtyReduced physical function; 17–43% of outpatients with cirrhosis 257.
Further decompensationA second portal hypertension event or jaundice, recurrent bleeding, ascites or HE, SBP or HRS-AKI: a stage with higher mortality 97.
Gastric varicesPresent in about 20% of people with cirrhosis; they bleed less often than oesophageal varices but more severely 106,410.
GLP-1 receptor agonistsSemaglutide and related drugs; ESSENCE showed MASH resolution 62.9% vs 34.3% 409.
Haemochromatosis (HFE)Iron overload; C282Y homozygosity in about 1 in 156 to 1 in 227 people of European descent 7,359. Phlebotomy is the mainstay 245.
HBeAgHepatitis B e antigen, a marker of the replicative phase 125.
HBsAgHepatitis B surface antigen; global prevalence 3.2% (257.5 million) in 2022 365.
HCC (hepatocellular carcinoma)The main primary liver cancer; about 1–4% of people with cirrhosis develop it each year 458.
Hepatic arterial buffer responseWhen portal flow falls, arterial flow rises and can buffer 25–60% of the loss 117.
Hepatic encephalopathy (HE)Brain dysfunction from liver insufficiency or portosystemic shunting, from subclinical change to coma 490.
Hepatitis BA virus that can persist for life, especially when caught at birth; antivirals suppress it and can reverse cirrhosis 294,464.
Hepatitis CA virus now curable in about 99% with 8–12 weeks of tablets 140,154.
Hepatitis D (delta)A defective virus needing HBV; the most aggressive viral hepatitis 110.
Hepatocellular patternAST and ALT raised out of proportion to alkaline phosphatase 251.
HepatocytesThe main liver cells, doing most of its metabolic and synthetic work 472.
HepatostatThe liver's ability to restore 100% of the liver-to-body-weight ratio after loss 311.
HospiceComfort-focused care when life expectancy is measured in months; underused in cirrhosis 392.
HRS-AKI (hepatorenal syndrome)Kidney failure from renal vasoconstriction without major structural damage; treated with vasoconstrictors plus albumin, and ideally transplant 180.
HVPG (hepatic venous pressure gradient)Wedged minus free hepatic venous pressure, measured by a balloon catheter; the gold standard for portal pressure 97,101.
HyponatraemiaLow blood sodium: 130 mmol/L or less in 21.6% of people with cirrhosis 16.
INRInternational normalised ratio, a clotting test; part of Child–Pugh and MELD 86,222.
Ishak scoreA fibrosis staging system from 0 to 6; 5–6 is cirrhosis 211,294.
Kayser–Fleischer ringsCopper in Descemet's membrane of the eye in Wilson disease 90.
Kupffer cellsThe liver's resident macrophages, 80–90% of the body's tissue macrophages 42.
LactuloseA non-absorbable sugar, first choice for overt HE, titrated to 2–3 soft stools a day 131,490.
Large-volume paracentesis (LVP)Draining all the fluid in one session; first-line for grade 3 ascites 106.
Late-evening snackA bedtime snack that shortens the overnight fast; 710 kcal at night added about 2 kg of lean tissue in a year 362.
Leipzig scoreCombines signs, copper tests and mutations for Wilson disease; 4 or more establishes the diagnosis 90,155.
LI-RADSA system grading liver nodules from LR-1 (benign) to LR-5 (HCC) 75,434.
Lille modelBilirubin change after a week of steroids, with other variables; 0.45 or more means non-response 284.
Liver biopsyA tissue sample; prone to sampling error, with major bleeding about 6 per 1,000 377,499.
Liver Frailty IndexGrip strength, chair stands and balance; frail at 4.4 or more 255,257.
Liver stiffness measurement (LSM)Elastography reading in kPa; stiffer usually means more scar 167.
Living donor liver transplantPart of a living person's liver; adjusted mortality HR 0.56 vs waiting 33.
LobuleThe hexagonal unit with a central vein and portal triads at the corners 472.
LSEC (liver sinusoidal endothelial cell)The most permeable endothelial cells in mammals; they keep portal pressure low and stellate cells quiet 363.
Maddrey discriminant functionAn older alcohol-associated hepatitis score; 32 or more defined severe disease 290,433.
Machine perfusionPumping blood or fluid through a donor liver; cut graft injury and discard by 50% vs cold storage 323.
MalnutritionReported in 20% of compensated and more than 50% of decompensated cirrhosis 107.
MASHMetabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis, formerly NASH 387.
MASLDMetabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease: steatosis plus at least 1 of 5 cardiometabolic risk factors 387.
MELDModel for End-stage Liver Disease: bilirubin, creatinine and INR 222.
MELD 3.0MELD-Na plus female sex and albumin, with a creatinine cap of 3.0 mg/dL; US allocation from July 2023 237,252.
MELD-NaMELD plus serum sodium (2008) 234.
MelaenaBlack, tarry stools from upper GI bleeding; caregivers are taught to recognise it 122.
METAVIRFibrosis staging F0 to F4 (cirrhosis) and activity A0 to A3 30,371.
MetALDMASLD plus 140–350 g a week of alcohol (women) or 210–420 g (men) 387.
Metabolic zonationDivision of liver functions along the lobule from periportal zone 1 to pericentral zone 3 472.
Milan criteriaOne HCC 5 cm or less, or up to 3 each 3 cm or less, with no macrovascular invasion 300.
Minimal hepatic encephalopathyAbnormal psychometric tests without clinical signs 490; linked to car crashes 24.
MMPs and TIMPsMatrix metalloproteinases break down scar; TIMPs block them. A fall in TIMP-1 permits regression 28,210.
MR elastographyMagnetic resonance stiffness imaging; 5 kPa or more suggests cirrhosis 386,436.
MyofibroblastAn activated, collagen-producing stellate cell 476.
NaltrexoneAn AUD medicine; a VA cohort found no probable drug-induced liver injury in cirrhosis (Early evidence) 469.
Nodule (regenerative)A ball of regenerating liver tissue ringed by scar, the defining feature of cirrhosis 416.
NSAIDsIbuprofen-type medicines; AASLD advises avoiding systemic NSAIDs in cirrhosis 392.
Oesophageal varicesSwollen veins in the oesophagus from portal hypertension; first bleed at 10–15% a year 101.
Overt hepatic encephalopathyWest Haven grades II–IV; occurs in 30–40% during the course of cirrhosis 490.
Palliative careSpecialised care for symptoms and planning, possible at any stage alongside treatment 392.
Paracetamol (acetaminophen)AASLD's preferred first-line analgesic, up to 2 g a day in cirrhosis, as published 392.
PBC (primary biliary cholangitis)Autoimmune small bile duct disease; antimitochondrial antibodies plus cholestatic tests 105.
PNPLA3 I148MThe strongest common genetic risk variant for alcohol-related cirrhosis and MASLD 52,395.
Portal hypertensionRaised pressure in the portal vein from scar, nodules and vascular tone 101.
Portal triadA branch of the portal vein, hepatic artery and bile duct at each lobule corner 472.
Pre-emptive TIPSA covered TIPS within 72 h of a variceal bleed in high-risk patients 97,169.
PSC (primary sclerosing cholangitis)Bile duct strictures, often with IBD; high cholangiocarcinoma and colorectal cancer risk 231,521.
RecompensationCause removed, complications resolved off treatment for 12 months, stable albumin, INR and bilirubin 97.
RegressionShrinkage of scar after the cause is removed (Established) 241.
ResmetiromA thyroid hormone receptor-β agonist approved for noncirrhotic MASH with F2–F3 fibrosis 144,194.
RifaximinA gut-acting antibiotic added to lactulose to prevent HE recurrence; 550 mg twice daily on the label 27,153.
Rule of 5Baveno VII: liver stiffness of 10, 15, 20 and 25 kPa marks rising risk 97.
SAAGSerum–ascites albumin gradient; 1.1 g/dl or more means portal hypertension 106,400.
SarcopeniaLoss of muscle mass; L3 skeletal muscle index under 50 cm²/m² in men and 39 cm²/m² in women 63.
SBP (spontaneous bacterial peritonitis)Infection of ascites without a surgical source; ascitic neutrophils above 250 cells/µl 106.
SinusoidsLow-pressure capillaries where portal and arterial blood mix on the way to the central vein 117,472.
Space of DisseThe gap between hepatocytes and sinusoidal cells where stellate cells live 166.
SpironolactoneAn aldosterone antagonist for ascites; the label advises starting in hospital in cirrhosis 127.
StatinsSafe for cardiovascular risk in MASLD including compensated cirrhosis 386; LIVERHOPE-EFFICACY did not reduce ACLF 369.
Stellate cellA vitamin A-storing cell that becomes the main scar-maker when activated 166,476.
SteatosisFat in the liver; develops in about 90% of heavy drinkers 433.
SVR (sustained virological response)Hepatitis C cure: no virus 12 weeks after treatment 293.
TacrolimusThe main anti-rejection drug after liver transplant; boxed warning for serious infections and malignancies 143.
TenofovirAn HBV antiviral (TDF or TAF); 5 years of TDF reversed cirrhosis in 74% on biopsy 150,151,294.
TerlipressinA vasoconstrictor for variceal bleeding and HRS-AKI; boxed warning for respiratory failure 106,147.
TGF-β1The strongest fibrogenic cytokine 28.
ThrombocytopeniaLow platelets, seen in up to 76% of people with chronic liver disease 9.
TIPSTransjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt, a stent through the liver that lowers portal pressure 97,383.
Transient elastography (FibroScan)An ultrasound-based pulse measuring liver stiffness; AUROC 0.94 for cirrhosis 167.
Ursodiol (UDCA)First-line PBC treatment; label 13–15 mg/kg/day, as published 149.
Variceal bleedingBleeding from varices, with 15–25% 6-week mortality per episode 106.
West Haven criteriaGrades of HE from minimal to grade IV coma 490.
Wilson diseaseAutosomal recessive copper overload from ATP7B mutations 90,155.
XenotransplantationUsing a gene-edited pig liver; first human cases are Hypothesis-stage 451,523.
Zone 3The pericentral zone of the lobule, with the least oxygen 233,472.

Cirrhosis Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cirrhosis, its staging scores and its complications, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with cirrhosis, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, a diet or a care plan without your own care team. FIB-4, the elastography bands and the Child–Pugh and MELD calculators are for learning, never for diagnosis, prognosis or transplant decisions, or for reading your own results. Doses, sodium and protein targets, paracetamol limits and guideline statements are quoted as published, never as instructions, and survival figures are population statistics, not a forecast for any one person.
