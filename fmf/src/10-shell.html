Familial Mediterranean Fever, explained

Familial Mediterranean fever is the most common inherited autoinflammatory disease. Here it is in moving pictures: watch a single protein misfire, follow an attack hour by hour, see why colchicine takes months to show its full effect, and look honestly at what a cure would take.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Schematic. Each spike is an attack. Colchicine starts at month 12.
