What it was called

Exam hookFive dates: 1945 Siegal, "benign paroxysmal peritonitis" · 1958 named FMF at Tel Hashomer (Heller, Sohar, Sherf) · 1972 colchicine (Goldfinger) · 1997 MEFV cloned, protein named pyrin and marenostrin · 2016 RhoA–PKN–14-3-3 keeps pyrin switched off. [[Siegal1945,Heller1958,Goldfinger1972,IntFMF1997,FrenchFMF1997,Park2016]]
In plain wordsFor half a century doctors could describe FMF but not stop it, and many patients died of kidney failure from amyloidosis. A daily pill, colchicine, changed that in the 1970s. The gene was found in 1997, and only in the last decade did scientists work out what the protein actually senses.
