Each of the four boxes is an equally likely outcome for every pregnancy. "Two variants" means the genotype, not certain illness: penetrance is incomplete.

Pseudodominance. Because carriers are so common, an affected parent often has a carrier partner. Then half the children inherit two variants, and FMF appears in every generation, as if it were dominant. Cazeneuve and colleagues documented this pseudodominant transmission in Armenian families. [[Cazeneuve1999]]
