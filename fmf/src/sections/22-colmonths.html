Part 2 · Colchicine

Colchicine over months

Colchicine is not a painkiller and does not stop an attack that has already started. Its benefit builds as the drug settles into white cells and as weeks pass without the inflammation that would have happened. Drag through two years, then zoom out to decades.

Scenario

Illustrative model. No study has published a week-by-week curve of SAA or attack rate after starting colchicine. The shapes follow what is published: leukocyte pharmacokinetics, response judged over 3–6 months, SAA falling after dose increases, and long-term cohort outcomes. It is not a prediction for any person.
