Sources

Every reference returned by the six research passes behind this app: journal articles, guidelines, drug labels, trial registrations and a few database or search records. The small numbers throughout the app point here.

Research for each area was done separately, so a few works appear under a merged entry. Entries marked check could not be fully verified against the original, so treat them with extra care.

In October 2026 every flagged entry was re-checked against Europe PMC, Crossref, DOAJ and the ClinicalTrials.gov registry. Six turned out to name the wrong authors, the wrong journal or the wrong paper, and two gave a paraphrased title. Those entries now carry a short note saying what was corrected, and several paywalled links were replaced with free copies.
