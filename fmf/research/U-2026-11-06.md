## Findings

### ddx · A PFAPA-like throat phenotype before FMF is diagnosed
- In a population-based matched case-control study in Leumit Health Services, Israel, 3324 people with FMF (identified by colchicine dispensing) were matched 1:4 by birth year and sex to 13,296 controls. (Early evidence) [[Magen2026]]
- A PFAPA diagnostic code was recorded in 2.6% of FMF patients and 0.1% of controls (OR 25.4, 95% CI 14.3–48.5). (Early evidence) [[Magen2026]]
- Up to 20 years before the FMF diagnosis, future FMF patients had more acute tonsillitis (35.6% vs 27.2%; OR 1.48, 1.37–1.61), viral-labelled tonsillitis (OR 2.65), oral aphthae (OR 1.72) and cervical lymphadenitis (OR 1.62), all with FDR q < 0.05. These associations persisted after diagnosis. (Early evidence) [[Magen2026]]
- Streptococcal sore-throat coding was not increased (lifelong OR 1.03, 0.94–1.14). Antibiotic dispensing and ENT visits were higher. Tonsillectomy was more frequent (OR 2.51), but the surgical comparisons did not survive FDR correction. (Early evidence) [[Magen2026]]
- The authors conclude that recurrent pharyngotonsillitis with aphthae or cervical adenitis, without proven streptococcal disease, "may be a clinical cue" to consider FMF in at-risk populations. (Early evidence) [[Magen2026]]
- Limits: the study used diagnosis codes and dispensing records, not chart-confirmed PFAPA. Cases were defined by colchicine dispensing. The data come from one Israeli insurer, so they may not generalise to low-prevalence populations.

### Exam hooks
- FMF and PFAPA can overlap. In an Israeli population study, a PFAPA code was about 25 times more common in people later treated for FMF, and non-streptococcal tonsillitis, mouth ulcers and neck-node swelling were more common up to 20 years before the FMF diagnosis. [[Magen2026]]

### In plain words
- Some people with FMF had years of sore throats, mouth ulcers and swollen neck glands that were not caused by strep before anyone named FMF. In a family or community where FMF is common, repeated episodes like this can be a reason to ask about FMF.

## Open questions and controversies
- Is the PFAPA-like phenotype an early FMF expression, a co-occurring disease, or a coding and labelling artefact? Magen2026 cannot separate these. It is a registry study without genotype or chart review [[Magen2026]]. Status: open.
