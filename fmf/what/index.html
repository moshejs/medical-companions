Part 1 · The disease

What FMF is

Familial Mediterranean fever is the most common inherited autoinflammatory disease 268: short, self-limited attacks of fever with inflammation of the lining of the belly, chest or joints. Pick a population and press play to see how common the gene variants are, and why far fewer people get sick than the variants alone would predict.

Population
Iraqi Jews: 29% carriers in an Israeli screen that tested three variants (Kogan 2001); 1 in 3.5 on a four-variant panel (Gershoni-Baruch 2001). V726A is the main variant here (12.8% carry it). 78,135Simple estimate of two-variant births, assuming random partnering and ignoring penetrance: (29.0% ÷ 2)² ≈ 1 in 48.Observed: phenotype III, 1 in 25 135; FMF in endemic countries, 1:500 to 1:1,000 (regional, not specific to this group) 268.

Each figure is 1 person in 100. Carrier rates come from different studies that tested different variant panels by different methods, so they are not one comparable series. The predicted figure is a simple estimate: it assumes random partnering, counts only the variants that study tested, and ignores reduced penetrance, so it is not a disease rate.

Step 1 · Carriers

One changed copy, no illness

Where FMF is common, roughly 1 person in 5 carries one MEFV variant. A meta-analysis of 16,756 chromosomes from 14 populations found a mean carrier rate of 0.186. Carriers are usually healthy, because FMF is classically recessive. 77,209

In plain wordsMany people carry one changed copy of the FMF gene without knowing it. One copy alone usually causes no illness.
Exam hookMEFV carrier rates in Jewish, Arab, Armenian and Turkish populations (about 10% or more) are far above cystic fibrosis carriers in North Americans of European ancestry (about 4%). 211

Why are carriers so common?

Disease variants usually stay rare because they are a burden. FMF variants reached carrier rates of about 1 in 5 in several populations. One explanation is that carrying one copy once helped people survive an infection. Drag through two thousand years.

About 2,100–2,200 years ago

A new variant appears

Established The variants are ancient. Carrier chromosomes from populations "separated for centuries" share the same surrounding DNA. Dating 4,626 haplotypes from 2,313 Turkish people put M694V at about 2,108 years by a hidden Markov model (95% CI 1,798–2,501) and V726A at about 2,192 years (1,826–2,754). The same paper's Gamma method dated the most recent common ancestor further back, about 3,541 years for M694V and 3,372 years for V726A, so "how old" depends on the method. 110,211

In plain wordsEach common FMF variant started once, in one person, more than two thousand years ago.
Exam hookShared haplotypes mean founder variants. Five founders (M694V, V726A, M694I, M680I, E148Q) explain about 74% of FMF chromosomes in typical patients. 265

What supports it Early evidence

Three frequent variants shared across several Mediterranean populations "strongly suggests a heterozygote advantage". 3 Park 2020 added the selection signal, the YopM mechanism and the mouse survival data. 211

Counterpoint

An Israeli study found no perceptible health advantage for Ashkenazi carriers today. 135 A benefit against plague would not necessarily show up in a modern population. Drift in isolated populations remains the competing explanation: the pooled meta-analysis found the strongest isolation and drift in Jewish populations, the candidate group for MEFV founder effects. 209

An older idea: tuberculosis Hypothesis

A 2003 letter asked whether carriers had lower tuberculosis mortality. It was never tested directly. 32

Carriers run slightly hot

Healthy heterozygotes have higher basal and peak CRP and SAA than people without variants. 143 That fits a primed, but not disease-causing, innate immune system.

An attack at a glance

How often each site is involved, in the Turkish national registry of 2,838 patients. 269 Tap a bar to see where it is and what it feels like.

In plain wordsAlmost every attack brings fever, and most bring belly pain. About half of patients have joint attacks at some point. Between attacks most people feel completely well. 77,269

Peritonitis 93.7%

Sudden belly pain that can look exactly like a surgical emergency, with guarding and rebound. It settles completely within 24–72 hours. Many patients have had unnecessary appendectomies or laparotomies. 268

Fever and peritonitis together are the classic attack. For what happens hour by hour, with CRP and SAA curves, see An attack, hour by hour.

Key numbers

Onset80–90%

have their first symptoms before age 20 248,268, and more than half begin in the first decade 219. First attacks after 40 are rare and usually mild 268.

Sex ratio1.2 : 1

male to female in the Turkish registry of 2,838 patients 269. Older Israeli data gave about 1.7, read as reduced penetrance in females 230.

Diagnostic delay6.9 years

mean time from first symptoms to diagnosis in Turkey, shortening by about 2 years each decade since 1981 269. Very early onset goes with M694V homozygosity, more severe disease and a longer delay 81,259.

Prevalence1:500–1:1,000

in endemic countries 268. The highest field figure is 1 in 395 in Sivas, Central Anatolia 198.

Japan192 of 601

Japanese patients with unexplained fever were diagnosed with FMF in a 2009–2015 study, which called it "no longer a rare disease in Japan" 177. A 2009 survey had estimated only 292 patients. Japanese FMF starts later, is milder, often involves E148Q/M694I, and colchicine worked in 91.8% 176.

WorldwideNo verified total

No primary source gives a checked global count; figures like "over 100,000" circulate unverified. Turkey's e-health records alone list 160,897 people with FMF codes 237. Turkey has the most patients, then Israel and Armenia 268.

Three phenotypes
  • Phenotype 1: recurrent attacks of fever with serositis or synovitis.
  • Phenotype 2: AA amyloidosis as the first sign, without prior attacks. At most 0.3% in the Turkish registry, under 2% across cohorts.
  • Phenotype 3: two variants, no symptoms and no amyloidosis.

Between attacksWell, mostly

Patients usually feel completely well between attacks 77,219. Yet median SAA between attacks was 6 mg/L against 693 mg/L during them, and many patients never fully normalize: attacks are the "tip of the iceberg" 143.

Exam hookBefore colchicine, more than half of FMF patients died of amyloidosis 268. See AA amyloidosis.
