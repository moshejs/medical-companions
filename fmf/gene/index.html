Level 1 · Chromosome

Chromosome 16

In 1992, linkage analysis in 27 non-Ashkenazi Jewish families placed the FMF gene on the short arm (p) of chromosome 16, with a maximum LOD score of 19.86. 214

Where it is common
The commonest variant in non-Ashkenazi Jews; predominant in North African Jews and absent in Ashkenazim. 77,145 51.4% of variant alleles in 1,090 genotyped Turkish patients 264, 38.1% in Greek patients 81, 39.6% in pooled data 206. Estimated age about 2,108 years by hidden Markov model, or a most recent common ancestor about 3,541 years ago by the Gamma method in the same paper, with a positive-selection signal. 208
Severity and notes
M694V/M694V meant earlier onset, more arthritis, more erysipelas-like erythema and higher colchicine needs than other common genotypes (Shinar 2000). 239 In 90 Armenian patients M694V homozygosity was linked to renal amyloidosis (Cazeneuve 1999) 33, but in the Turkish registry of 2,838 patients it was not (Tunca 2005). 264 Among M694V homozygotes, male sex and arthritic attacks raised amyloidosis risk. 78

Step 1 · Parents

Two copies each

Each person has two copies of MEFV, one on each chromosome 16. FMF is classically autosomal recessive: disease usually needs a variant on both copies. 71,76

In plain wordsEveryone has two copies of the gene. Usually both must be changed for FMF.
Exam hookRecessive: two hits needed. Carriers (one hit) are usually healthy.

Pseudodominance. Because carriers are so common, an affected parent often has a carrier partner. Then half the children inherit two variants, and FMF appears in every generation, as if it were dominant. Cazeneuve and colleagues documented this pseudodominant transmission in Armenian families. 33

Beyond the textbook pattern

Phenotype II: amyloidosis first

AA amyloidosis as the first or only sign, in someone who never had attacks. 76,171 It is rare, and estimates differ: at most 0.3% of the 2,838-patient Turkish registry, about 0.6% in a later review whose authors doubt it is a separate entity, and no biopsy-proven case among 461 relatives of FMF-amyloidosis patients. 89,171,264 SHARE advises following people with two pathogenic variants and no symptoms, and considering treatment when amyloidosis risk factors are present (level B). 82,200

One variant, real symptoms

Up to about 25% of people with FMF have only one identifiable variant 76,168; 12% in one Turkish cohort 141 and 32% in a referral cohort. 253 Booty 2009 resequenced the whole 15-kb region in 46 such patients and found no second variant; one variant plus typical symptoms was judged enough to diagnose FMF and try colchicine. 26 Jéru 2013 saw heterozygosity as a susceptibility factor rather than Mendelian FMF. 112 Gene dose matters. 64

Truly dominant variants

  • M694del: dominant FMF with variable penetrance (Booth 2000); one patient had AA amyloidosis. 25 A UK screen of about 3,500 patients found 21, all North-Western European, sharing a founder about 550 years old; median onset 18, excellent colchicine response. 227
  • T577N/S/A, just before B30.2: dominant autoinflammation; T577N was absent from about 6,800 controls; colchicine-responsive. 250
  • One lab also lists I692del as dominant with reduced penetrance. 6

Genotype and severity

M694V homozygotes: earlier onset, more arthritis (Shinar 2000). 239 Amyloidosis link: yes in 90 Armenians (Cazeneuve 1999) 33, no in 2,838 Turks, where amyloidosis still affected 12.9% of the cohort (Tunca 2005). 264 Among 124 M694V homozygotes, male sex (OR 2.37) and arthritic attacks (OR 3.27) raised amyloidosis risk. 78

Exam hookStudies disagree on M694V and amyloidosis; country of origin is itself a risk factor in SHARE. 82

What panels test

Many labs test in two steps: common pathogenic variants first, then the full coding sequence. Screening covers exons 2, 3, 5 and 10; searching other exons in clinically definite FMF adds little. 240

  • Exon 2: E148Q, E167D, T267I, R202Q
  • Exon 3: P369S, R408Q · Exon 5: F479L · Exon 9: I591T
  • Exon 10: M680I, M694V, M694I, V726A, A744S, R761H, I692del, K695R

Limits of testing

About 10–20% of FMF patients carry no MEFV variant, with a classic but milder picture than M694V homozygotes. 19 One lab estimates full sequencing sensitivity at 75–90%; deep intronic and regulatory variants are missed, and large deletions or duplications have not been reported as causes. 6 FMF remains a clinical diagnosis (SHARE, level B). 82

PAAND is not FMF

S242R removes a 14-3-3 binding site directly, so one copy causes a dominant disease with neutrophilic dermatosis, prolonged fever and myalgia. It responds to IL-1-targeted therapy rather than behaving as colchicine-responsive FMF. 169,234
