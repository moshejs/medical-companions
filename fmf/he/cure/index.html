מדריך מונפש ומבוסס מקורות לסטודנטים, למטופלים ולבני משפחה. לצורכי לימוד בלבד, לא ייעוץ רפואי. המקום שבו עצרתם נשמר במכשיר הזה.

לא ייעוץ רפואימלווה FMF הוא סיכום לימודי של הספרות המדעית שפורסמה, ללמידה ולהבנת המחלה. הוא אינו מחליף את הצוות הרפואי. מינונים והמלצות מתוך הנחיות מובאים כפי שפורסמו, ולא כהוראות לאדם מסוים.

חלק 4 · טיפול והעתיד

לקראת ריפוי

אין היום טיפול מאושר שמרפא FMF. קולכיצין וחסימת IL-1 שולטים במחלה, לכל החיים. הפרק הזה בכל זאת לוקח את השאלה ברצינות: איפה במסלול אפשר להתערב, אילו ראיות יש לכל דרך, ומה עומד בדרך. לכל טענה יש תווית ראיות. לחצו על הפעלה כדי לשלוח את האות לאורך המסלול, ואז בחרו דרך כדי להציב לפניו מחסום.

Stage 1 · The gene

MEFV, and the alleles that matter

The FMF-associated gain-of-function variants cluster in the C-terminal B30.2 domain of pyrin: M694V, M680I, V726A. Those are the alleles any genetic cure would have to correct or silence. MEFV messenger RNA in blood is restricted to myeloid cells, and the gene switches on as CD34+ progenitors differentiate down the granulocyte line, which puts the target inside the bone-marrow stem and progenitor compartment. 35,210

במילים פשוטותThe fault is a small spelling change in one gene, and the cells that read that gene are made in the bone marrow.
לבחינהFMF is hematopoietic and cell-autonomous in mice: bone marrow transplantation corrected the knock-in phenotype. That is why marrow-directed cures are even discussed. 37

Hypothesis for FMF as a strategy

FMF is recessive, and Mefv-null mice show no spontaneous autoinflammation while B30.2 knock-in mice do. So partial correction of the myeloid compartment might be enough, and monoallelic correction or knockout of the mutant allele should in principle restore the brake. That is a hypothesis: there is no validated surrogate for "enough corrected myeloid cells" in FMF, unlike the fetal-haemoglobin threshold in sickle cell disease. SAA and attack rate are the only candidate readouts. 31,37,206,210

Early evidence human data

One FMF patient has been transplanted and reported: a girl transplanted at age 4 for congenital dyserythropoietic anaemia, with FMF as a comorbidity rather than the indication, attack-free off colchicine at about 2 years. The paper is in Blood, 2002, and the word "cure" in its title is the authors' framing of a single observation. No confirmatory case has appeared in 24 years. In mevalonate kinase deficiency, 9 allogeneic transplants produced 7 complete remissions but also grade II–IV acute GvHD in 4 patients and two transplant-related deaths. 116,179

Early evidence the autologous route

Both clinically validated stem-cell cures, exa-cel and BEAM-101, require busulfan myeloablation before infusion, so the conditioning toxicity, infertility risk and secondary-malignancy concern largely persist even without a donor. 8,31

Exam hookThe risk-benefit is the whole argument. Life expectancy on colchicine plus IL-1 blockade is near normal, and myeloablative transplant carries double-digit treatment-related mortality. The commentary accompanying the mouse data says plainly that transplant risks currently preclude its use in human FMF. 37,292
In plain wordsReplacing the bone marrow would replace the cells that misbehave. It would also be a far more dangerous treatment than the tablets and injections that already control the disease in almost everyone.

מה ריפוי היה צריך להשיג. שני דברים בבת אחת, לא אחד. הוא היה צריך לעצור את ההתקפים, וגם לכבות את הדלקת השקטה שביניהם, כי עמילואידוזיס מסוג AA נגרמת מ-SAA גבוה באופן כרוני ולא מההתקפים עצמם. העדכון של EULAR/PReS משנת 2024 קובע שליטה בדלקת התת-קלינית כיעד טיפולי מפורש. ריפוי היה צריך גם להגיע לתאים הנכונים: ה-RNA השליח של MEFV בדם מוגבל לתאים מיאלואידיים, והגן נדלק כשתאי אב CD34+ מתמיינים לגרנולוציטים, ולכן המטרה נמצאת בתוך מאגר תאי הגזע ותאי האב של מח העצם. החלבון פירין דווח גם בתאים דנדריטיים ובפיברובלסטים של הקרום הסינוביאלי, כך שתיקון של תאי הדם בלבד עלול להשאיר מאגר מאחור. 35,55,206

שלוש-עשרה שאלות פתוחות

אלה הפערים שהספרות עצמה משאירה פתוחים, לא שאלות רטוריות. כל אחד מהם הוא מקום שבו מחקר יכול לשנות את התמונה.

01 · Is FMF fully hematopoietic?

Pyrin protein has been reported in dendritic cells and in synovial fibroblasts, which raises the possibility that a myeloid-only correction leaves residual disease. No human study has quantified the non-hematopoietic contribution. 35,55

02 · Would correcting one allele be enough?

Recessive inheritance and the benign Mefv-null mouse predict yes, but the clean experiment, a corrected and uncorrected iPSC-macrophage pair from the same patient, has not been published. 37,93

03 · What editing efficiency would be therapeutic?

Sickle cell disease has a measurable fetal-haemoglobin threshold. FMF has no validated surrogate for "enough corrected myeloid cells"; SAA and attack rate are the only candidates. 31,206

04 · Can conditioning be avoided?

Every clinically validated stem-cell cure so far requires busulfan myeloablation, which is hard to justify in a disease with near-normal survival on colchicine plus IL-1 blockade. 8,31,292

05 · Is the single transplant case reproducible?

One patient, about 2 years of follow-up, improvement that began during conditioning, and no reported correlation between chimerism and symptoms. No confirmatory case in 24 years. 179

06 · Does a 14-3-3/pyrin molecular glue exist?

It would be the most target-faithful FMF drug imaginable, and it is the most conspicuous gap in the chemistry literature. 158,210

07 · Are gasdermin D inhibitors worth a trial in FMF?

Two approved drugs hit the executioner downstream of the pyrin inflammasome, and one of them, dimethyl fumarate, is reported to alleviate FMF in mice. No FMF patient has been given either, and no trial of either is registered. 102,103,156

08 · Does IL-18 blockade add anything to IL-1 blockade?

IL-18 elevation in FMF is inconsistently reported, and tadekinig alfa has never been given to an FMF patient in a published study. 28,96,186

09 · Why do JAK inhibitors appear to work?

No mechanistic account links JAK/STAT to pyrin inflammasome activation. The effect may act on amplification loops, or may be publication bias in series of four patients or fewer. 121

10 · Are statins harmful in FMF?

The RhoA and geranylgeranyl pyrophosphate mechanism predicts that statins activate the pyrin inflammasome, yet patients with cardiovascular indications routinely receive them and no clinical study has looked. 210

11 · Is the microbiome causal?

All FMF microbiome findings are cross-sectional and confounded by colchicine. No faecal transplant, defined-consortium or longitudinal pre/post-treatment study exists. 48,127

12 · What about amyloidosis that is already established?

Every route on this page targets inflammation upstream. None addresses deposits already in an organ, and the regression reports on anakinra and tocilizumab are uncontrolled. 99,206

13 · Would an attack-free but still-mutant patient be cured?

The field has no agreed definition. Treatment-free remission, normalised SAA, absent subclinical inflammation and a corrected genotype are four different bars. 206

איך ניסוי הבא יכול להיראות

קראו את החלק הזה בתשומת לב. שלושת המבנים שלהלן הם המחשות שנכתבו לדף הזה. הם אינם ניסויים רשומים, אינם הצעות של חוקר כלשהו, ואינם ייעוץ. נכון לסריקה של ClinicalTrials.gov באוקטובר 2026, לא רשום אף ניסוי ב-FMF של עריכה גנטית, טיפולי RNA, השתלת מח עצם (HSCT) או מולקולה קטנה המכוונת לפירין. 43

A: repurposing a gasdermin D inhibitor

Illustration onlyEarly evidence

Shape. A small randomised crossover in colchicine-resistant FMF of an already-approved drug that inhibits gasdermin D, added to stable colchicine, with each patient spending equal time on drug and on placebo in random order and a washout between.

Endpoints. Attack rate per month, which is the endpoint the anakinra and rilonacept trials used, and SAA between attacks, which is the marker the 2024 update treats as the damage-prevention target.

What would count as a signal. A within-patient fall in attack rate of the size the rilonacept trial saw (2.00 to 0.77 a month), together with SAA moving toward the under-10 mg/L range, in a trial too small to be definitive but large enough to justify a proper phase 2.

Why it is only an illustration. No gasdermin D inhibitor has ever been given to an FMF patient in any published study, and this page is not suggesting anyone take one. 94,102,103,156,206

B: the preclinical gate before any editing trial

Illustration onlyHypothesis

Shape. Not a clinical trial at all. An isogenic pair of induced pluripotent stem cell lines from the same M694V patient, one corrected and one not, differentiated into macrophages side by side.

Endpoints. Constitutive and stimulated IL-1β release, ASC speck formation with and without microtubule disruption, and the microtubule-independence that FMF variants confer.

What would count as a signal. Correction of one allele restoring the healthy-donor phenotype, which would answer open question 2 and give the field its first dose-response estimate for how much correction is enough.

Why it is only an illustration. Patient iPSC lines with M694V exist and have been differentiated into macrophages, but without an isogenic corrected control; a Koç University thesis describes a CRISPR system for MEFV in such cells with no published efficiency or rescue data. 93,133,210,275

C: an IL-18-high subgroup study

Illustration onlyHypothesis

Shape. Screen colchicine-resistant patients for serum IL-18, enrol only those in the high stratum, and test recombinant IL-18 binding protein added to existing treatment against placebo.

Endpoints. Attack rate, SAA, and the IL-18 level itself as the pharmacodynamic check that the drug engaged its target.

What would count as a signal. Benefit concentrated in the high-IL-18 stratum, which would turn an inconsistent biomarker into a stratifying one. A flat result across strata would be informative too: it would close the question.

Why it is only an illustration. IL-18 elevation in FMF is reported inconsistently, and tadekinig alfa has been tested in NLRC4 gain-of-function and XIAP deficiency but never in FMF. 28,96,186,188

האזהרה לגבי סטטינים, וגבולותיה. סטטינים מדלדלים גרניל-גרניל פירופוספט, וכך מונעים מ-RhoA להיקשר לקרום התא, ובניסויים בתאים זה משפעל את אינפלמזום הפירין; שחרור IL-1β שנגרם מסטטינים התהפך כשהוסיפו בחזרה גרניל-גרניל פירופוספט או כששפעלו את PKN. זהו מנגנון במבחנה. המשמעות הקלינית שלו אינה ידועה: אף מחקר קליני לא בדק אם סטטינים משפיעים על FMF, ומטופלים עם התוויה קרדיווסקולרית מקבלים אותם באופן שגרתי. אין לשנות או להפסיק שום תרופה על סמך זה, וכל שאלה על סטטין שייכת לרופא שרשם אותו. היא מופיעה כאן כאחת השאלות הפתוחות, לא כהמלצה. 210

Where this leaves us

No approved therapy cures FMF today. Colchicine and IL-1 blockade control it, and they do so well enough that in one Turkish cohort of 587 patients survival did not differ significantly from the general population, though an Israeli cohort of adolescents did find higher mortality; both point to amyloidosis as the risk that matters. Every genuinely curative route on this page is either untested in FMF or untested in humans at all, and the ones with the clearest mechanism, editing the gene or replacing the marrow, are also the ones whose current form demands myeloablative conditioning. 2,31,43,271,292

That is not a pessimistic reading. It is the reason the research agenda in the literature looks the way it does: short-path repurposing at the bottom of the pathway, a chemistry gap at the top, and a long list of questions that a handful of well-designed studies could answer. 29

In plain wordsNothing available now removes FMF. What is available controls it, for most people, for life. The work on a cure is real but early, and no part of this page is advice about anyone's treatment.
