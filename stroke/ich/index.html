Not medical adviceThe Stroke Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. If you think someone is having a stroke, call your local emergency number. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, physiotherapist, occupational therapist, speech and language therapist or the rest of a stroke team. Scores such as the NIHSS, ICH score, ABCD2, CHA2DS2-VASc and ASPECTS are teaching aids, never for an individual's diagnosis, prognosis or treatment. Doses, thresholds and guideline statements are quoted as published, never as instructions. Outcomes are population statistics; they are not predictions for any person.

Part 3 · Acute treatment

Treating intracerebral haemorrhage

In one large trial, several things done early and together shifted outcomes. Run the first hour of that care bundle, then explore the score, the blood pressure trials, reversal, surgery and restarting.

AHA/ASA 2022

Replaces the 2015 guideline. Its take-home messages cover regional systems, expansion markers, smooth blood pressure control, updated reversal, and therapies that give no benefit or harm 370,425. ENRICH and SWITCH, and the FDA action on andexanet, came after it.

ESO/EANS 2025

37 questions and 208 studies. Strong for organised stroke units and secondary prevention with blood pressure lowering. Strong against anti-inflammatory drugs outside trials. Weak against routine rFVIIa, platelet transfusion for antiplatelet-associated ICH, prophylactic anti-seizure medicines, and general policies limiting treatment within 24 hours of onset 943.

Arrival: the clock starts

Speed matters because a bleed can keep growing, predominantly within the first 3 hours of onset 885. AHA/ASA 2022 includes a baseline severity measure at first evaluation (Class 1, B-NR) 370. INTERACT3 tested a bundle of four targets in 121 hospitals and 7036 people within 6 hours of onset 612.

This is what happened to a group in one 2001 cohort, in a small sample. It should not be the sole basis for forecasting one person's prognosis or for limiting care (Class 3: No Benefit, B-NR) 370.

ABC/2: A is the greatest diameter on CT, B the diameter at 90 degrees to A, C the number of slices with haemorrhage times slice thickness 514. Volume of 60 cm³ or more with GCS 8 or less predicted 91% 30-day mortality, under 30 cm³ with GCS 9 or more predicted 19% 131.

Exam hookICH score items: GCS (0 to 2), age 80 or over, infratentorial, volume 30 cm³ or more, IVH 423.

A score must not set the limits of care. AHA/ASA 2022 recommends a baseline severity measure at first evaluation (Class 1, B-NR), but says a score should not be the sole basis for forecasting individual prognosis or limiting life-sustaining treatment (Class 3: No Benefit, B-NR). Postponing new DNAR orders or withdrawal of support until at least the second full day of hospitalisation is reasonable (Class 2a, B-NR). Limiting other interventions because someone has DNAR status is Class 3: Harm 370.

How good is it?

In INTERACT2 (2556 people), discrimination for 90-day poor outcome was 0.68 for the original score, and all scores had poor sensitivity and positive predictive value for death 416. In a prospective study of 243 people, the score stratified 12-month function, and many people kept improving even after 6 months 424.

Where the figures come from

The 2001 abstract reports scores 0 to 5 only: all 26 people at score 0 survived and all 6 at score 5 died, with mortality rising steadily (P<0.005) 423. The intermediate rates are a secondary quotation 22. Score 6 is possible by addition, but no outcome is reported for it 423.

In plain wordsA risk score is a starting point. It should not be the only thing used to decide about someone's care 370.

Andexanet

Thrombotic events were 10.3% versus 5.6% in the ANNEXA-I paper 198. The FDA notice quotes 14.6% versus 6.9% through day 30, and thrombosis-related death 2.5% versus 0.9%. In December 2025 the FDA said it considers the risks to outweigh the benefits. AstraZeneca sought voluntary licence withdrawal for commercial reasons, with US sales ending 22 December 2025 294.

Drugs that stop bleeding

Reducing growth has not translated into better function. FAST: rFVIIa, growth 11% versus 26%, poor outcome 29% versus 24% 643. FASTEST: mRS aOR 1.09, thromboembolic complications 15 versus 4, stopped for futility, with testing in those at greatest risk ongoing Early evidence 133. Tranexamic acid: no change in 90-day function (acOR 0.93), lower day 7 mortality (0.70) and expansion (0.81) 664,936.

In plain wordsBlood thinners usually can be reversed in hospital. Tell staff what you take and when you last took it 370.
Exam hookFour-factor PCC beats FFP for INR correction in warfarin-related ICH 942. Platelet transfusion worsened outcome in PATCH 71. rFVIIa slows growth without improving function 133,643.

STICH

Early surgery versus initial conservative treatment. Favourable outcome 26% versus 24% (OR 0.89, 0.66 to 1.19) 659.

A meta-analysis to 2019 (21 trials, 4145 people, only 4 of highest quality) gave good outcome RR 1.40 (1.22 to 1.60) for any surgery and 1.47 (1.26 to 1.72) for minimally invasive surgery, looking better earlier (p=0.04) 932. In an INTERACT3 China secondary analysis, surgery went with lower poor outcome (OR 0.71), but this is observational 447.

AHA/ASA 2022: cerebellar ICH with deterioration, brainstem compression and/or hydrocephalus, or volume 15 mL or more, immediate removal (Class 1, B-NR). Large IVH with impaired consciousness, external ventricular drain (Class 1, B-NR). Minimally invasive evacuation for supratentorial bleeds over 20 to 30 mL, GCS 5 to 12, can reduce mortality (2a, B-R), with an effect on function that is uncertain (2b, B-R). Craniotomy for most is uncertain (2b, A) and lifesaving if deteriorating (2b, C-LD). Decompressive craniectomy may be considered (2b, C-LD) 370. ENRICH and SWITCH were published after this guideline.

Exam hookCerebellar ICH 15 mL or more, or with brainstem compression or hydrocephalus, is a Class 1 indication for surgery in AHA/ASA 2022 370.

Restarting antithrombotics

A person with atrial fibrillation who survives a bleed faces two risks that pull opposite ways. Compare them in PRESTIGE-AF (319 people, median age 79, median follow-up 1.4 years) 1006. Early evidence

Rates per 100 patient-years. For recurrent ICH the trial did not meet non-inferiority (HR 10.89, 90% CI 1.95 to 60.72). Not a recommendation for anyone.

The other risk

With anticoagulation, more repeat bleeding

Recurrent ICH: 5.00 per 100 patient-years on a DOAC against 0.82 without. Non-inferiority was not met (HR 10.89, 90% CI 1.95 to 60.72). Deaths 10% versus 13% 1006.

In plain wordsRestarting a blood thinner after a bleed is a careful choice between preventing clots and risking another bleed. Trials are still settling this 1006.
