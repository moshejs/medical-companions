Part 6 · Context & review

History & people

For centuries people called stroke "apoplexy", meaning struck down. Treatment that opens the blocked artery is only about 30 years old 280,737. Scrub through the words, the pictures and the trials that changed that, up to the 2026 guidelines.

Drawings are schematic; the time axis is not linear. People and dates are as the cited sources give them. Textbook shorthand simplifies the record 70,486.

Antiquity · Struck down

"Apoplexia" means struck down with violence, a picture known to Greek physicians at least 2500 years ago. Its first recorded appearance is in the Hippocratic Corpus: "It is impossible to remove a strong attack of apoplexy, and not easy to remove a weak attack". Galen called it "a privation of sense and motion in all the nerves" 280. Understanding rested on Galen and humoral pathology for centuries 1083.

In plain wordsFor centuries people called stroke "apoplexy", meaning struck down 280.
Exam hookApoplexy was an umbrella term, covering non-vascular and even non-cerebral events such as myocardial infarction and pulmonary embolism 280.

People

1658

Johann Jakob Wepfer

Distinguished non-traumatic intracranial bleeding, splitting the unitary concept for the first time 280. A re-reading of his text shows his thinking stayed rooted in Galenic theory and iatrochemistry, so "first modern" overstates it 486.

1664

Thomas Willis

Published Cerebri anatome in London and explained the pathological and functional meaning of the circular arterial anastomosis named after him 51. The source marks the 350th anniversary in 2014; 1664 is calculated from that. Module 1.2 shows the circle.

1852

Rudolf Virchow

Created the terms thrombosis and embolism 70,280. A review casts doubt on the triad in its usual form: he was routinely credited with it only 100 years after his work 70.

1927

Egas Moniz

Showed the first cerebral angiogram on 28 June 1927, of a pituitary tumour in a young man 53. He described the first carotid occlusion in 1936 758. His 1949 Nobel Prize was for prefrontal leucotomy, now unacceptable 53.

1951, 1965

Miller Fisher

Carotid occlusions were thoroughly described in English only with his 1951 paper 315,758. His 1965 paper described lacunes as small deep infarcts 316. Through serial sections at necropsy he linked them to penetrating artery disease 163.

1971

Hounsfield, Cormack, Ambrose

Godfrey Hounsfield of EMI built the system; Allan Cormack, with earlier reconstruction work, shared the Nobel Prize with him. James Ambrose led the first scan of a live patient 905. The 1973 papers describe the system and clinical use 34,440.

1975

Symon, Astrup and colleagues

The penumbra was found in four experiments in September 1975 in Symon's London laboratory 60. In baboon cortex, electrical failure came at about 15 ml/100 g per minute and massive potassium release at a lower flow, median 6 58.

From clock to tissue

DAWN and DEFUSE 3 moved selection from clock time toward tissue, the clinical use of the penumbra idea 418,1083. That link is an interpretation, not a claim of the trials themselves.

2025 and 2026: where the story stands

2026

New early-management guideline

The 2026 AHA/ASA guideline replaces the 2018 guideline and its 2019 update. Stated key updates include thrombolytic choice and eligibility and thrombectomy eligibility 809,811. A correction has been issued, and outside authors have proposed further corrections 18,812.

2026

New rehabilitation guideline

Richards 2026 replaces the 2016 guideline. Rehabilitation should begin in the acute phase, and "uncertainty remains regarding the best treatment approaches, their timing, and dosing" 843,1053.

4 March 2025

Tenecteplase approved in the US

FDA approval of TNKase for acute ischaemic stroke in adults was reported, resting on the AcT trial 297,348. Module 3.1 has the trials.

Early evidence2026

Later still

LATE-MT (336 patients, 24 to 72 hours, salvageable tissue on imaging): common OR 1.74 (1.19-2.55), with symptomatic haemorrhage 4.8% against 0 and deaths 6.0% against 1.8%. A single trial, not yet reflected in the 2026 guideline 1081.

2025

Smaller vessels: no gain

ESCAPE-MeVO (530 patients): mRS 0 to 1, 41.6% against 43.1%, with mortality 13.3% against 8.4% 364. DISTAL (543 participants): common OR 0.90 (0.67-1.22) 815. Thrombectomy's success did not simply extend downstream.

Established, single phase 3 trial2026

A new class for prevention

OCEANIC-STROKE (12,327 people): asundexian against placebo, ischaemic stroke 6.2% against 8.4% (HR 0.74, 0.65-0.84), major bleeding 1.9% against 1.7% 916. Module 5.4 covers it.

2025

Fifty years of the penumbra

Astrup's personal account dates the discovery to four experiments in September 1975 60.
