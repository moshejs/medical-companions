Prevention

Factor XI and XIa inhibitors

A new target for blood thinners, tested after non-cardioembolic stroke and in AF.

  • PACIFIC-StrokeEarly evidence
    Phase 2b, 1808 people, asundexian 10, 20 or 50 mg vs placebo. Covert infarct or ischaemic stroke at 26 weeks in 19%, 22% and 20% vs 19%. Bleeding pooled HR 1.57 (90% CI 0.91-2.71) 924.
  • Stated hypothesisHypothesis
    Can anticoagulant effect be separated from bleeding? The LIBREXIA-AF design paper states that milvexian "may offer similar anticoagulant efficacy with less bleeding risk" 462.
  • AXIOMATIC-SSPEarly evidence
    Phase 2, 2366 people, milvexian on dual antiplatelet therapy. Composite at 90 days 16.8% with placebo vs 15.3-16.7% across doses, no dose-response 917.
  • OCEANIC-AFEstablished
    14,810 people with AF, asundexian 50 mg vs apixaban, stopped early. Stroke or systemic embolism 1.3% vs 0.4% (HR 3.79, 95% CI 2.46-5.83). Major bleeding 0.2% vs 0.7% (HR 0.32, 95% CI 0.18-0.55) 796.
  • AZALEA-TIMI 71Established
    Established for bleeding only. 1287 people with AF, abelacimab vs rivaroxaban. Bleeding 3.2 and 2.6 vs 8.4 events per 100 person-years. Stroke efficacy is not reported as a result in the abstract 869.
  • OCEANIC-STROKEEstablished
    A single positive phase 3 trial. 12,327 people after non-cardioembolic stroke or high-risk TIA, asundexian 50 mg vs placebo. Ischaemic stroke 6.2% vs 8.4% (cause-specific HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.65-0.84). Major bleeding 1.9% vs 1.7% (HR 1.10, 95% CI 0.85-1.44) 918.
  • LIBREXIA-STROKEHypothesisNo results shown
    Milvexian vs placebo after stroke or high-risk TIA. Active, not recruiting, 12,523 enrolled, primary completion listed 2026-10-15, no results posted 722.
  • LIBREXIA-AFHypothesisNo results shown
    Milvexian vs apixaban. Active, not recruiting, 20,284 enrolled (the design paper planned 15,500), primary completion listed 2026-12-08, no results posted 462,724.
  • LILAC-TIMI 76HypothesisNo results shown
    Abelacimab vs placebo in AF unsuitable for oral anticoagulation. Recruiting, 2500 estimated, primary completion listed 2027-12-30 723.
  • LILAC (Novartis)HypothesisNo results shown
    A second record under the same name. Not yet recruiting, completion listed 2030-12-30 728.
In plain wordsA newer type of blood thinner, asundexian, lowered the chance of another clot-related stroke in one very large study, without more serious bleeding. It is one study, and a similar medicine is still being tested. The same medicine did worse than a standard blood thinner for people with AF, so a medicine that helps one group may not help another 722,796,918.
Exam hookAsundexian failed against apixaban in AF but reduced ischaemic stroke against placebo after non-cardioembolic stroke: mechanism of stroke and comparator matter 796,918.

Factor XI versus factor X: why bleeding could be less

The comparators in these trials, apixaban and rivaroxaban, act at factor X. Factor XI inhibitors act further up the chain. The hope is a drug that prevents clots while leaving more of the body's ability to seal a tear. That hope is a Hypothesis under test, and the trials have given more than one answer 462,796,918.

OCEANIC-STROKE was the first completed trial of factor XIa inhibition for prevention after non-cardioembolic stroke or TIA. Mean age was 68 and 67% were male. Index strokes were 43% large artery, 22% small vessel, 30% undetermined and 2% cardioembolic. Dual antiplatelet therapy was planned in 63% 919. Entry was within 72 hours of the index event, per the registry record, which lists no posted results even though the trial is published 721. On 9 November 2026 the US FDA approved asundexian (Sunharbo) 50 mg tablets, used with antiplatelet therapy, to reduce the risk of stroke in adults after a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischaemic stroke or a high-risk TIA. The FDA notes that major bleeding was similar overall but higher with the drug in people older than 75 and in the first 90 days, that gastrointestinal bleeding was more common, and that it is not approved for stroke prevention in AF 297. Guideline status was not established when this guide's research was done.

How a promising signal holds up

Pick a route to see the arms of its trials side by side. Watch for three traps: a subgroup that does not repeat, an open-label gain that vanishes against sham, and a change that does not reach a better outcome.

Open arguments

Where trials disagree, both sides are shown. None of these is settled.

Hypothesis

Will factor XI inhibition become standard after stroke?

For: OCEANIC-STROKE 918. Caution: both phase 2 trials missed their composite outcome 917,924. Status: LIBREXIA-STROKE has no reported result 722.

Hypothesis

Could a factor XI drug still work in AF?

Asundexian failed against apixaban 796. LIBREXIA-AF and LILAC have not reported 723,724.

Early evidence

Is nerinetide real in very early treatment with reperfusion?

For: a post hoc pooled analysis and 1-year early-window data 7,993. Against: the primary results of all three trials were neutral 188,428,429. It needs a prospective trial.

Early evidence

Does remote conditioning work?

For: RICAMIS and SERIC-EVT 180,378. Against: the sham-controlled Danish RESIST and SERIC-IVT 109,377. The meta-analysis found better favourable outcomes only without reperfusion 535, which conflicts with SERIC-EVT 378.

Early evidence

Is colchicine useful after stroke?

For: a pooled fall in ischaemic stroke 310. Against: CONVINCE missed significance and CHANCE-3 was neutral 491,570. CHANCE-3 measured 90 days, while CONVINCE reported long-term follow-up.

Early evidence

Do GLP-1 receptor agonists prevent stroke?

Yes, in diabetes: stroke HR 0.84 (95% CI 0.76-0.93) across 7 trials 521. No difference: in a wider analysis of 21 trials 342.

Early evidence

Can trials from one country be generalised?

BAST and TASTE-2 are positive or borderline in Chinese centres 1022,1028, and the RIC meta-analysis raises regional generalisability 535. Replication elsewhere was not found when this research was done.

Early evidence

Is intra-arterial lysis after thrombectomy useful?

CHOICE and ANGEL-TNK were positive with small samples 675,840. POST-TNK and POST-UK were not significant 448,591.

Established, debated

Should people be screened for AF, and how hard?

STROKESTOP found a small net benefit 953. LOOP did not reach significance, and its authors suggest "not all atrial fibrillation is worth screening for" 952.

Exam hookA drug that improves a marker or a time may still not improve function: FASTEST slowed haematoma growth without functional benefit 133, AI cut door-to-groin time with no shown functional gain 637, and clazosentan reduced vasospasm without better function 25. Open-label gains can vanish with sham control: SB623 versus ACTIsSIMA 711,941. Fluoxetine reduces depression but does not improve disability after stroke, and raises fracture risk 566,603.
In plain wordsScientists are trying to protect brain cells during a stroke. Many drugs looked good in animals but have not clearly helped people yet 188,429. Stem cell treatments have been safe in trials so far, but have not clearly improved recovery in controlled studies 426,440,711. Early results from brain and spinal cord implants are hopeful. They come from very small groups and need bigger studies 75,225. This guide does not recommend any treatment.
