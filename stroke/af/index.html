CHA2DS2-VASc: how the scale scores

A teaching tool, never for an individual's diagnosis, prognosis or treatment. Choose inputs to see how the published scale adds points, 0 to 9 756. It was refined from the 2006 Birmingham/NICE schema and tested in 1084 Euro Heart Survey patients. Its C-statistic was modest (0.606) 586.

CHA2DS2-VASc: 2 of 9

In the Danish cohort, a score of 2 went with 3.71 thromboembolic events per 100 person-years at 1 year 756. With the sex point removed, this person scores CHA2DS2-VA 2.

A broader outcome than stroke. This count includes peripheral embolism, ischaemic stroke and pulmonary embolism, in people admitted to hospital. So it runs higher than stroke rates alone 756.

Population figures from one cohort 756. They are not a prediction for anyone.

Rates vary between cohorts. Olesen records 0.78 per 100 person-years at score 0 where Lip recorded no events, and its 1-year rates exceed its own 10-year rates at every score (9.27 vs 6.46 at score 4) 586,756. In 182 678 Swedish people with AF, C-statistics were 0.67 for CHA2DS2-VASc and 0.66 for CHADS2 335.

Why drop the sex point? In 229 565 Finns with new AF, female sex was linked to a 20% to 30% higher ischaemic stroke rate in 2007 to 2008, but this attenuated to non-significance by 2018 970. In the Swedish cohort female sex was an independent predictor (HR 1.17) 335.

Exam hookCHA2DS2-VASc has two 2-point items: age 75 or over and previous thromboembolism 756. HAS-BLED, developed from Euro Heart Survey data, had a C-statistic of 0.72 for major bleeding 798. The 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS guideline added new recommendations on risk assessment, anticoagulation and appendage occlusion 463.

Warfarin and antiplatelets

A relative reduction read another way: for every 100 strokes without treatment, how many remain? This is arithmetic on the published percentage, drawn for teaching.

Adjusted-dose warfarin reduced stroke by 64% (49 to 74) vs control, in 6 trials and 2900 participants 404.

DOACs against warfarin

DOACs prevented more stroke and embolism than warfarin: RR 0.81 (0.73 to 0.91), DOACs vs warfarin, 71 683 participants 867.

When to start a DOAC after a stroke

Four randomised trials and a pooled analysis have tested how soon a DOAC can start. Move the cursor through the first two weeks to see each trial's arms.

ELAN's early start was within 48 hours for minor or moderate stroke and day 6 or 7 for major. Windows drawn from the published definitions; composites differ between trials.

ELAN

2013 people. Composite at 30 days 2.9% vs 4.1% (risk difference -1.18 points, -2.84 to 0.47). Symptomatic intracranial haemorrhage 0.2% in both 314.

TIMING

888 people, registry-randomised in Sweden. Composite at 90 days 6.89% vs 8.68% (P=0.004 for noninferiority). No symptomatic intracerebral haemorrhage in either group 755.

OPTIMAS

3621 people in the UK. Composite at 90 days 3.3% in each group (noninferior, p=0.0003). Symptomatic intracranial haemorrhage 0.6% vs 0.7% 1037.

START

200 people, response-adaptive design. No clearly superior day was identified, but the evidence favoured earlier starts in the first 2 weeks 1027.

CATALYST: the pooled answer

TIMING, ELAN, OPTIMAS and START, 5441 people. Within 4 days vs day 5 or later: 30-day composite 2.1% vs 3.0% (OR 0.70, 0.50 to 0.98), recurrent ischaemic stroke 1.7% vs 2.6% (OR 0.66, 0.45 to 0.96), symptomatic intracerebral haemorrhage 0.4% in each (OR 1.02, 0.43 to 2.46) 226.

Exam hookEarly DOAC (within 4 days) is safe and reduces recurrent ischaemic stroke in pooled data 226.

The dual antiplatelet window

These trials enrolled people after a minor stroke or high-risk TIA. Aspirin itself acts early: pooling 12 trials and 15 778 people, it reduced 6-week recurrent ischaemic stroke by about 60% (HR 0.42, 0.32 to 0.55), with no benefit beyond 12 weeks in the aspirin-only comparison 863. Adding clopidogrel widens the early gain 771.

Published anchors at day 21 and 90 771; the curves between them are schematic. POINT reported most events in the first week 466.

How long? A BMJ meta-analysis of 3 trials and 10 447 people: non-fatal recurrent stroke RR 0.70 (0.61 to 0.80), about 20 fewer per 1000. Stopping within 21 days, and possibly as early as 10 days, is likely to maximise benefit and minimise harm 396. Long-term dual therapy harms: in SPS3, over a mean 3.4 years, recurrent stroke HR 0.92, major haemorrhage HR 1.97 and all-cause mortality HR 1.52 91.

Which regimen, for whom? This remains open. CHANCE, INSPIRES and CHANCE-2 all enrolled in China, and CHANCE-2's authors limit their conclusion to Chinese carriers 1017. For long-term single agents: CAPRIE, clopidogrel vs aspirin, annual risk 5.32% vs 5.83% (RRR 8.7%) 164; PRoFESS, aspirin with dipyridamole vs clopidogrel, 9.0% vs 8.8%, neither superior, with more intracranial haemorrhage (HR 1.42) on the former 874.

In plain wordsAfter a small stroke or TIA, the first days carry the most risk, so early treatment matters 466,863. Two blood-thinning tablets can help for a short time, but staying on both for years raises bleeding risk 91,771.
Exam hookDual antiplatelet benefit sits in the first 21 days after minor stroke or high-risk TIA 771; long-term therapy in lacunar stroke raised bleeding and death 91.

Closing the left atrial appendage

Schematic device and access route. Each result panel has its own scale.

CHAMPION-AF

3000 people suitable for anticoagulation. At 3 years the efficacy difference was 0.9 points (-0.8 to 2.6), noninferior with a 4.8-point margin. Bleeding HR 0.55 252.

The closure trials disagree by population. OPTION and CHAMPION-AF, in people who could take anticoagulants, were noninferior on efficacy with less later bleeding. CLOSURE-AF, in older people at high risk of both stroke and bleeding, failed noninferiority 252,536,1036. Earlier trials showed numerically more ischaemic stroke with closure 830. This question is open.

Device-detected AF and embolic stroke of undetermined source

Device-detected AF

NOAH-AFNET 6, edoxaban vs placebo: composite HR 0.81 (0.60 to 1.08), safety HR 1.31 505. ARTESiA, apixaban vs aspirin: stroke or embolism 0.78% vs 1.24% per patient-year (HR 0.63), major bleeding 1.71% vs 0.94% (HR 1.80) 414.

Together: ischaemic stroke RR 0.68, major bleeding RR 1.62, no reduction in death 648.

Looking for AF

LOOP, 6004 older people: loop recording found AF in 31.8% vs 12.2% but stroke or embolism was not significantly reduced (HR 0.80, 0.61 to 1.05) 950. CRYSTAL AF, after cryptogenic stroke: AF found in 12.4% vs 2.0% by 12 months 884.

ESUS: a prediction that failed

ESUS was proposed in 2014, and the authors predicted anticoagulants would beat antiplatelets 405. NAVIGATE ESUS: HR 1.07, more major bleeding (HR 2.72) 407. RE-SPECT ESUS: HR 0.85 (0.69 to 1.03) 242. ARCADIA: HR 1.00, stopped for futility 484.
