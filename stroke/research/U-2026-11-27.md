## Findings

### frontier · OCEANIC-STROKE results are now posted on ClinicalTrials.gov
- The registry record for OCEANIC-STROKE had results first posted on 2026-11-20 (submitted 2026-08-24). Until now the site said the record listed no posted results. (Established: registry fact) [[NCT05686070]]
- The posted results agree with the published trial and with the numbers on the site: 6162 people started asundexian and 6165 placebo; ischaemic stroke in 384 vs 518 (HR 0.739, 95% CI 0.647–0.843); ISTH major bleeding in 117 of 6124 vs 107 of 6130 treated (HR 1.104, 95% CI 0.849–1.435). No number on the site changes. [[NCT05686070,Sharma2026]]

### frontier · Lp(a)HORIZON: the peer-reviewed paper replaces the topline (catch-up)
- The frontier module still said that full Lp(a)HORIZON data were awaited and that no numbers were available. The trial was published on 2026-11-07, before this window; the 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-20 passes did not update this passage.
- 8350 people with cardiovascular disease and Lp(a) of at least 70 mg/dL were randomised to pelacarsen 80 mg monthly under the skin or placebo; 8322 were in the efficacy analysis. Lp(a) at 12 months was 64.4 vs 233.4 nmol/L. Dose quoted as published. [[Cho2026HORIZON]]
- Over a median 4.7 years the primary composite (cardiovascular death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke or urgent coronary revascularisation) occurred in 511 people (12.3%) with pelacarsen and 503 (12.1%) with placebo: HR 1.02 (95% CI 0.90–1.15; P = 0.76). (Early evidence for Lp(a) lowering as a treatment: one neutral outcome trial) [[Cho2026HORIZON]]
- The abstract gives no separate figure for stroke. The registry record still lists no posted results [[NCT04023552]].

### Exam hooks
- Lp(a)HORIZON: pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) (64.4 vs 233.4 nmol/L at 12 months) but the primary composite was 12.3% vs 12.1%, HR 1.02. [[Cho2026HORIZON]]

### In plain words
- The official trial register now shows the results of the asundexian trial. They match the published paper.
- A medicine that lowers a blood fat called Lp(a) did lower it, but people taking it had just as many heart attacks and strokes as people taking a dummy injection.
