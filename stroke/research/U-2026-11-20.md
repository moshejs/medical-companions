## Findings

### frontier · The asundexian (Sunharbo) label is now posted
- Last week's pass cited the FDA news release only, because the label was not yet on DailyMed or Drugs@FDA. The US prescribing information is now on DailyMed (published 2026-11-13) and openFDA (set_id f48aee87-69a8-45a6-afa5-0917807efb70, effective 2026-11-09); Drugs@FDA lists NDA221291, approved 2026-11-09, priority review, new molecular entity. (Established: regulatory fact) [[FDA-Sunharbo]]
- Indication: with antiplatelet therapy, to reduce the risk of stroke in adults with recent non-cardioembolic ischaemic stroke (NIH Stroke Scale 15 or lower) or high-risk TIA. Recommended dosage 50 mg orally once daily, quoted as published. Contraindications: none. The label has no boxed warning and no Warnings and Precautions section. [[FDA-Sunharbo]]
- Safety population in OCEANIC-STROKE: 6,124 on asundexian and 6,130 on placebo, median 14.2 months. ISTH major bleeding 117 vs 107 people, 1.6 vs 1.5 per 100 person-years (HR 1.1, 95% CI 0.8-1.4). Within 90 days of starting: 4.5 vs 3.1 per 100 person-years (HR 1.46, 95% CI 0.99-2.16). Older than 75 years (25% of the population): 2.6 vs 1.7 per 100 person-years. High-risk TIA subgroup (5%): 2.9 vs 1.0 per 100 person-years. Planned dual antiplatelet therapy: 1.9 vs 1.5; not planned: 1.3 vs 1.4. [[FDA-Sunharbo]]
- Gastrointestinal bleeding 186 vs 147 people, 2.7 vs 2.1 per 100 patient-years (HR 1.3, 95% CI 1.0-1.6). Symptomatic intracranial haemorrhage 41 vs 36 (0.6 vs 0.5 per 100 patient-years; HR 1.2, 95% CI 0.7-1.8). Fatal bleeding 14 vs 8 (HR 1.8, 95% CI 0.7-4.2). Diarrhoea 3.4% vs 2.8%. [[FDA-Sunharbo]]
- Efficacy as in the label: ischaemic stroke 384 of 6,162 (6.2%) vs 518 of 6,165 (8.4%), HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.65-0.84). All-cause mortality 4.0% vs 4.1% (HR 0.98, 95% CI 0.83-1.17). These match the published trial [[Sharma2026]]. The crude major-bleeding proportions on the site (1.9% vs 1.7%) correspond to the label's 117 of 6,124 and 107 of 6,130. [[FDA-Sunharbo]]
- Other label statements: interrupt at least 24 hours before elective surgery or invasive procedures; withhold for at least 24 hours after thrombolysis for acute ischaemic stroke; avoid combined P-gp and strong CYP3A4 inducers; not recommended in moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B and C); breastfeeding not recommended. Newly diagnosed atrial fibrillation required permanent discontinuation in the trial. [[FDA-Sunharbo]]

### Exam hooks
- Asundexian label: 50 mg once daily with antiplatelet therapy after non-cardioembolic ischaemic stroke (NIHSS 15 or lower) or high-risk TIA; major bleeding 1.6 vs 1.5 per 100 person-years, higher in the first 90 days and over age 75. [[FDA-Sunharbo]]

### In plain words
- The official US instructions for asundexian are now published. Serious bleeding was about as common as with a dummy tablet overall, but more common in the first three months and in people older than 75. Stomach and bowel bleeding was somewhat more common.

## Open questions and controversies
- Will factor XI inhibition become standard after non-cardioembolic stroke? FDA-approved on one phase 3 trial [[FDA-Sunharbo,Sharma2026]]; LIBREXIA-STROKE is active, not recruiting, with no results posted [[NCT05702034]]. Guideline status not established. EU status not established.
