Part 5 · Recovery and care

Nursing care

Nurses run the first three days of stroke care. Walk through 72 hours block by block, follow the swallow screen, count the observations after thrombolysis, and tap around the body for what the evidence says about each bedside task.

Protocols here are quoted as published, as teaching material for students and families. None is a care plan. The AHA replaced its 2009 nursing statement [[Summers2009]] with three updates in 2021: prehospital and acute care [[Ashcraft2021]], endovascular and ICU postinterventional care [[Rodgers2021]], and posthyperacute care up to discharge [[Green2021]]. The last notes knowledge gaps in many areas and calls for continued nurse-led research [[Green2021]]. The 2026 AHA/ASA guideline replaces the 2018 guideline and its 2019 update [[Prabhakaran2026,Powers2019]]. Full texts of those statements could not be read for this page, so no class of recommendation and no AHA observation schedule is quoted.

Fewer checks: weakly non-inferior

mRS 2 to 6 at 90 days was 31.7% with low-intensity vs 30.9% with standard (RR 1.03, 0.92 to 1.15; p for non-inferiority 0.057). Symptomatic haemorrhage 0.2% vs 0.4%. Serious adverse events 11.1% vs 11.3% [[Anderson2025F]].

The non-inferiority margin was an RR of 1.15, which the upper confidence limit reached. The authors call this "weak evidence" and say hospitals could consider the approach according to local circumstances [[Anderson2025F]]. Early evidence It applies only to stable people with NIHSS under 10.

Exam hookACE inhibitor use more than doubles the odds of angio-oedema with alteplase (OR 2.3) [[Hurford2015]].

The label

"During and following Activase administration for the treatment of acute ischemic stroke, frequently monitor and control blood pressure" [[FDA-Activase]].

The label reports angioedema "during and up to 2 hours after" infusion [[FDA-Activase]].

A disagreement on the window

The latest onset of 189 minutes starts the clock at the start of alteplase [[Hurford2015]], while the label counts up to 2 hours after infusion [[FDA-Activase]]. The authors say angio-oedema may be delayed and occurs more often than previously reported. The angio-oedema window is one reason for caution with fewer checks.

Mobility

The AVERT protocol had low-arousal dependent people do active sitting with assistance, 10 to 30 minutes a session. Passive sitting in a chair did not count [[Bernhardt2016]]. Module 3.5 shows the trial.

Continence and outcome

People with initial incontinence had higher 2-year case fatality (67% vs 20%) and institutional care (39% vs 16%) [[Patel2001]]. In Copenhagen, urinary incontinence went from 36% fully and 11% partly on admission to 8% and 11% at 6 months [[Nakayama1997]]. Catheter infection was commoner with stroke than without (6.0% vs 1.7%) [[Retelski2017]].

Talking with a person who has aphasia

Aphasia was found in a median 30% of mixed acute stroke, and 62% of acute ischaemic stroke arriving within 3 h [[Flowers2016]]. It carried greater odds of in-hospital death (OR 2.7, 2.4 to 3.1) and a longer stay [[Flowers2016]]. In general acute care, people with communication problems were three times more likely to have a preventable adverse event (OR 3.00, 1.43 to 6.27) [[Bartlett2008]].

Supported Conversation training rests on revealing the competence of the person through a skilled partner. Trained volunteers scored higher than untrained on acknowledging and revealing competence [[Kagan2001]]. All 25 studies in a 2016 review reported positive changes from partner training, mostly in chronic aphasia, with more research needed in acute settings [[SimmonsMackie2016]]. The 2026 AHA/ASA rehabilitation guideline lists supported communication training (Class 1, B-NR), and the 2026 guideline supersedes earlier rehabilitation guidance [[Richards2026]].

Practical tips

  • Say you know they know. Speak to an adult, not a patient to be managed.
  • One idea at a time. Short sentences. One question, then wait.
  • Give time. Silence is not a no.
  • Offer other ways to answer. A pen, written key words, pictures, pointing, nods.
  • Check you got it. Say it back and ask if it is right.
  • Talk to the person first, not over them to family.
  • Note what works in the record, so the next nurse can use it.

    Going home

    COMPASS: a negative trial

    A pragmatic trial at 40 US hospitals with 6024 adults. Stroke Impact Scale-16 at 90 days was 80.6 vs 79.9, not significantly different [[Duncan2020]]. But 58% of hospitals delivered it without interruption, and 35% of patients attended a clinic visit. Whether the model or the delivery failed is unresolved [[Duncan2020]].

    Early supported discharge

    14 trials, 1957 people: stay about seven days shorter, and death or dependency OR 0.80 (0.67 to 0.97). Benefits were no longer significant at five years [[Fearon2012]].

    Carers

    18 studies of caregiver and dyad interventions: 11 with significant caregiver outcomes, most often burden, and 6 with significant survivor outcomes. Too limited for firm conclusions [[Bakas2022]]. Carers of people who fell repeatedly were more stressed (53% vs 18%) [[Forster1995]].

    Readmission

    A 2010 review found no risk-standardised model of readmission after stroke, and few characteristics consistently associated with it [[Lichtman2010]].
