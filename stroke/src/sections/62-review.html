%t:cards.mnemonics%

BE-FAST, ABCD2 and FeSS are published names, and their cards are tagged so. Every other card is a memory aid written for this guide: a short phrase to hold a published fact in mind. None of those is an official grouping from any guideline, drug label or stroke organisation, and none replaces the source it points to. Doses, thresholds and schedules are quoted as published, never as instructions.

Mnemonic in motion: ABCD2

ABCD2 is a published score for the early risk of stroke after a TIA [[Johnston2007]]. Play the animation to walk through its five letters as the points add up to 7, then see the three published risk bands and why NICE says the score should not decide urgency [[NICE-NG128]]. A teaching tool that explains how a published scale scores its items. It is never for an individual's diagnosis, prognosis or treatment. The drawings are schematic; the bands are population figures. [[Johnston2007,Wardlaw2015]]

These cards are for learning, not for deciding anything about one person's care. Survival, risk and outcome figures are population statistics from registries and trials, and they cannot predict what will happen to any one person. If you think someone is having a stroke, call your local emergency number [[Harbison2003,Ekundayo2013]].

Sources

Every reference behind the Stroke Companion: randomised trials, meta-analyses, registries, reviews, AHA/ASA, ESO, NICE and other guidance, US drug labels and trial registrations. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology and history; blood supply, the ischaemic cascade, causes and TIA; haemorrhagic stroke; recognition, imaging and the NIHSS; thrombolysis and thrombectomy; stroke unit and nursing care; secondary prevention; rehabilitation and life after stroke; and the research frontier. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or the regulator's label, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Several guideline full texts could not be read, including parts of the 2026 AHA/ASA early management guideline. Where that matters, modules say the statement is reported through a secondary source. Entries marked %t:sources.check% are pages with no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed.
