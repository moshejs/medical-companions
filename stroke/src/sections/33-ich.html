Part 3 · Acute treatment

Treating intracerebral haemorrhage

In one large trial, several things done early and together shifted outcomes. Run the first hour of that care bundle, then explore the score, the blood pressure trials, reversal, surgery and restarting.

AHA/ASA 2022

Replaces the 2015 guideline. Its take-home messages cover regional systems, expansion markers, smooth blood pressure control, updated reversal, and therapies that give no benefit or harm [[Greenberg2022,Hemphill2015]]. ENRICH and SWITCH, and the FDA action on andexanet, came after it.

ESO/EANS 2025

37 questions and 208 studies. Strong for organised stroke units and secondary prevention with blood pressure lowering. Strong against anti-inflammatory drugs outside trials. Weak against routine rFVIIa, platelet transfusion for antiplatelet-associated ICH, prophylactic anti-seizure medicines, and general policies limiting treatment within 24 hours of onset [[Steiner2025]].

The ICH score: a teaching calculator

The ICH score adds five items to a severity number [[Hemphill2001]]. Set them below. The result shown is what happened to a whole group in the 2001 study, not a forecast for anyone.

Teaching tool only. It explains how a published scale scores its inputs. It is never for an individual's diagnosis, prognosis or treatment. Results are population figures from a small 2001 cohort.

A score must not set the limits of care. AHA/ASA 2022 recommends a baseline severity measure at first evaluation (Class 1, B-NR), but says a score should not be the sole basis for forecasting individual prognosis or limiting life-sustaining treatment (Class 3: No Benefit, B-NR). Postponing new DNAR orders or withdrawal of support until at least the second full day of hospitalisation is reasonable (Class 2a, B-NR). Limiting other interventions because someone has DNAR status is Class 3: Harm [[Greenberg2022]].

How good is it?

In INTERACT2 (2556 people), discrimination for 90-day poor outcome was 0.68 for the original score, and all scores had poor sensitivity and positive predictive value for death [[Heeley2015]]. In a prospective study of 243 people, the score stratified 12-month function, and many people kept improving even after 6 months [[Hemphill2009]].

Where the figures come from

The 2001 abstract reports scores 0 to 5 only: all 26 people at score 0 survived and all 6 at score 5 died, with mortality rising steadily (P<0.005) [[Hemphill2001]]. The intermediate rates are a secondary quotation [[Alkhars2026]]. Score 6 is possible by addition, but no outcome is reported for it [[Hemphill2001]].

In plain wordsA risk score is a starting point. It should not be the only thing used to decide about someone's care [[Greenberg2022]].

Andexanet

Thrombotic events were 10.3% versus 5.6% in the ANNEXA-I paper [[Connolly2024]]. The FDA notice quotes 14.6% versus 6.9% through day 30, and thrombosis-related death 2.5% versus 0.9%. In December 2025 the FDA said it considers the risks to outweigh the benefits. AstraZeneca sought voluntary licence withdrawal for commercial reasons, with US sales ending 22 December 2025 [[FDA-Andexxa2025]].

Drugs that stop bleeding

Reducing growth has not translated into better function. FAST: rFVIIa, growth 11% versus 26%, poor outcome 29% versus 24% [[Mayer2008]]. FASTEST: mRS aOR 1.09, thromboembolic complications 15 versus 4, stopped for futility, with testing in those at greatest risk ongoing Early evidence [[Broderick2026]]. Tranexamic acid: no change in 90-day function (acOR 0.93), lower day 7 mortality (0.70) and expansion (0.81) [[Menon2026,Sprigg2018]].

In plain wordsBlood thinners usually can be reversed in hospital. Tell staff what you take and when you last took it [[Greenberg2022]].
Exam hookFour-factor PCC beats FFP for INR correction in warfarin-related ICH [[Steiner2016]]. Platelet transfusion worsened outcome in PATCH [[Baharoglu2016]]. rFVIIa slows growth without improving function [[Mayer2008,Broderick2026]].

Surgery trials explorer

STICH, STICH II and MISTIE III were neutral on function, ENRICH was positive, driven by lobar bleeds, and SWITCH gave weak evidence. Choose a trial to see where the bleeds were, what was done and what it found. Unsettled Selection and timing remain open [[Pradilla2024,Beck2024,Sondag2020]].

Schematic. The drawing shows the idea of each approach only. Bars are the published figures in each arm.

A meta-analysis to 2019 (21 trials, 4145 people, only 4 of highest quality) gave good outcome RR 1.40 (1.22 to 1.60) for any surgery and 1.47 (1.26 to 1.72) for minimally invasive surgery, looking better earlier (p=0.04) [[Sondag2020]]. In an INTERACT3 China secondary analysis, surgery went with lower poor outcome (OR 0.71), but this is observational [[Hu2025]].

AHA/ASA 2022: cerebellar ICH with deterioration, brainstem compression and/or hydrocephalus, or volume 15 mL or more, immediate removal (Class 1, B-NR). Large IVH with impaired consciousness, external ventricular drain (Class 1, B-NR). Minimally invasive evacuation for supratentorial bleeds over 20 to 30 mL, GCS 5 to 12, can reduce mortality (2a, B-R), with an effect on function that is uncertain (2b, B-R). Craniotomy for most is uncertain (2b, A) and lifesaving if deteriorating (2b, C-LD). Decompressive craniectomy may be considered (2b, C-LD) [[Greenberg2022]]. ENRICH and SWITCH were published after this guideline.

Exam hookCerebellar ICH 15 mL or more, or with brainstem compression or hydrocephalus, is a Class 1 indication for surgery in AHA/ASA 2022 [[Greenberg2022]].

AHA/ASA 2022: restart anticoagulation early with a mechanical valve or LVAD (2a, C-LD). Antiplatelet may be reasonable (2b, B-R). Anticoagulation in non-valvular AF may be considered (2b, B-NR), at about 7 to 8 weeks (2b, C-LD). Left atrial appendage closure if ineligible (2b, C-LD) [[Greenberg2022]].

Recurrence risk ran 1.3 to 7.4% a year across nine studies, higher after lobar bleeds in two of three hospital studies [[Poon2014]]. In 1145 survivors at a single centre, recurrence per 1000 person-years was 84 with inadequate versus 49 with adequate blood pressure control after lobar ICH, and 52 versus 27 after non-lobar ICH [[Biffi2015]].

In plain wordsRestarting a blood thinner after a bleed is a careful choice between preventing clots and risking another bleed. Trials are still settling this [[Veltkamp2025]].
