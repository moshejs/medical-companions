Part 6 · Context & review

History & people

For centuries people called stroke "apoplexy", meaning struck down. Treatment that opens the blocked artery is only about 30 years old [[Engelhardt2017,NINDS1995]]. Scrub through the words, the pictures and the trials that changed that, up to the 2026 guidelines.

Drawings are schematic; the time axis is not linear. People and dates are as the cited sources give them. Textbook shorthand simplifies the record [[Karenberg1998,Bagot2008]].

People

1658

Johann Jakob Wepfer

Distinguished non-traumatic intracranial bleeding, splitting the unitary concept for the first time [[Engelhardt2017]]. A re-reading of his text shows his thinking stayed rooted in Galenic theory and iatrochemistry, so "first modern" overstates it [[Karenberg1998]].

1664

Thomas Willis

Published Cerebri anatome in London and explained the pathological and functional meaning of the circular arterial anastomosis named after him [[ArraezAybar2015]]. The source marks the 350th anniversary in 2014; 1664 is calculated from that. Module 1.2 shows the circle.

1852

Rudolf Virchow

Created the terms thrombosis and embolism [[Engelhardt2017,Bagot2008]]. A review casts doubt on the triad in its usual form: he was routinely credited with it only 100 years after his work [[Bagot2008]].

1927

Egas Moniz

Showed the first cerebral angiogram on 28 June 1927, of a pituitary tumour in a young man [[Artico2017]]. He described the first carotid occlusion in 1936 [[Oliveira2018]]. His 1949 Nobel Prize was for prefrontal leucotomy, now unacceptable [[Artico2017]].

1951, 1965

Miller Fisher

Carotid occlusions were thoroughly described in English only with his 1951 paper [[Oliveira2018,Fisher1951]]. His 1965 paper described lacunes as small deep infarcts [[Fisher1965]]. Through serial sections at necropsy he linked them to penetrating artery disease [[Caplan2015]].

1971

Hounsfield, Cormack, Ambrose

Godfrey Hounsfield of EMI built the system; Allan Cormack, with earlier reconstruction work, shared the Nobel Prize with him. James Ambrose led the first scan of a live patient [[Schulz2021]]. The 1973 papers describe the system and clinical use [[Hounsfield1973,Ambrose1973]].

1975

Symon, Astrup and colleagues

The penumbra was found in four experiments in September 1975 in Symon's London laboratory [[Astrup2025]]. In baboon cortex, electrical failure came at about 15 ml/100 g per minute and massive potassium release at a lower flow, median 6 [[Astrup1977]].

Thrombectomy: failure, then the wave

Three neutral trials in 2013 nearly closed the question. Then retrievable stents and better selection changed the answer in 2015. Choose a group. Each row runs from the control group to the thrombectomy group.

HERMES, 2016, 1287 patientsNNT 2.6

To reduce disability by at least one mRS level. Adjusted common OR 2.49 (1.76-3.53), with no heterogeneity across subgroups, including age 80 or over [[Goyal2016]].

MR CLEAN, 201513.5 points

Absolute difference in functional independence, 32.6% against 19.1%. Retrievable stents were used in 81.5% of the intervention group [[Berkhemer2015]].

From clock to tissue

DAWN and DEFUSE 3 moved selection from clock time toward tissue, the clinical use of the penumbra idea [[Yilmaz2025,Heiss2011]]. That link is an interpretation, not a claim of the trials themselves.

In plain wordsDoctors once saw stroke as something they could do little about. Clot-busting drugs, stroke units and clot-removal procedures changed that [[NINDS1995,Langhorne2020,Goyal2016]].

2025 and 2026: where the story stands

2026

New early-management guideline

The 2026 AHA/ASA guideline replaces the 2018 guideline and its 2019 update. Stated key updates include thrombolytic choice and eligibility and thrombectomy eligibility [[Prabhakaran2026,Powers2019]]. A correction has been issued, and outside authors have proposed further corrections [[Prabhakaran2026Correction,Albers2026]].

2026

New rehabilitation guideline

Richards 2026 replaces the 2016 guideline. Rehabilitation should begin in the acute phase, and "uncertainty remains regarding the best treatment approaches, their timing, and dosing" [[Richards2026,Winstein2016]].

4 March 2025

Tenecteplase approved in the US

FDA approval of TNKase for acute ischaemic stroke in adults was reported, resting on the AcT trial [[Genentech2025,FDA-TNKase]]. Module 3.1 has the trials.

Early evidence2026

Later still

LATE-MT (336 patients, 24 to 72 hours, salvageable tissue on imaging): common OR 1.74 (1.19-2.55), with symptomatic haemorrhage 4.8% against 0 and deaths 6.0% against 1.8%. A single trial, not yet reflected in the 2026 guideline [[Yang2026]].

2025

Smaller vessels: no gain

ESCAPE-MeVO (530 patients): mRS 0 to 1, 41.6% against 43.1%, with mortality 13.3% against 8.4% [[Goyal2025]]. DISTAL (543 participants): common OR 0.90 (0.67-1.22) [[Psychogios2025]]. Thrombectomy's success did not simply extend downstream.

Established, single phase 3 trial2026

A new class for prevention

OCEANIC-STROKE (12,327 people): asundexian against placebo, ischaemic stroke 6.2% against 8.4% (HR 0.74, 0.65-0.84), major bleeding 1.9% against 1.7% [[Sharma2026]]. Module 5.4 covers it.

2025

Fifty years of the penumbra

Astrup's personal account dates the discovery to four experiments in September 1975 [[Astrup2025]].

Where the shorthand breaks

Textbook lineThe record
"Wepfer was the first modern"Credited with showing apoplexy results from brain haemorrhage or vessel occlusion, yet his text stays rooted in Galenic theory [[Engelhardt2017,Karenberg1998]]
"Virchow's triad"Doubted in its usual form; routinely credited to him only 100 years after his work [[Bagot2008]]
"Moniz won the Nobel for angiography"It was for prefrontal leucotomy [[Artico2017]]
"CT arrived in 1975"One review says CT entered clinical practice in 1975 [[Artico2017]]; the first patient scan was 1 October 1971 [[Schulz2021]]
"Fisher named the lacune"Dechambre introduced the word in 1838; Fisher's work gave it its modern meaning, and lipohyalinosis is attributed to a 1968 Fisher paper [[Engelhardt2017,Caplan2015]]
"Medieval medicine added nothing"One account says medieval authors added no novel concepts [[Engelhardt2017]]; another says Islamic scholars expanded the knowledge and addressed contradictions [[Yilmaz2025]]
