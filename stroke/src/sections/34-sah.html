Part 3 · Acute treatment

Treating subarachnoid haemorrhage

After an aneurysm bursts, each stretch of the next three weeks has its own danger. Scrub from day 0 to day 21, then compare coiling with clipping and see what the evidence says about nimodipine and life afterwards.

AHA/ASA 2023

Replaces the 2012 guideline [[Hoh2023,Connolly2012]]. Recommends timely transfer to higher-volume centres (Class 1, B-NR), with no numeric threshold in the recommendation [[Hoh2023]]. Neurocritical Care Society guidance appeared the same year [[Treggiari2023]].

ESO/EANS/ESMINT 2026

15 evidence-based recommendations and 37 expert consensus statements. High-level evidence backs only oral nimodipine, and regular coiling when both options are equally suitable in good-grade patients. Consensus: treat in a centre with at least 70 aSAH patients a year (35 in remote areas), by a physician treating at least 30 aneurysms a year [[Vergouwen2026]].

What raises rebleeding risk

Systolic pressure over 160 mm Hg had OR 3.1 [[Ohkuma2001]]. High SBP 1.93, Hunt-Hess III to IV 3.43, intracerebral or intraventricular haematoma 1.65, posterior circulation 2.15, size over 10 mm 1.70 [[Tang2014]]. It is among the strongest predictors of poor outcome: in-hospital death aOR 7.69 [[Stienen2018]], unfavourable discharge OR 7.41 [[Galea2017]].

Stopping the clot breaking down?

ULTRA (955 people, tranexamic acid up to 24 h): good outcome 60% versus 64%, rebleeding 10% versus 14% (OR 0.71, 0.48 to 1.04) [[Post2021]]. AHA text reads ULTRA as no significant reduction [[Hoh2023]]. Meta-analyses show less rebleeding with no gain in function [[Roos2003,AlSalihi2025,Vergouwen2026]]. Both guidelines agree: not routinely (AHA 3: No Benefit, A) [[Hoh2023,Vergouwen2026]].

In plain wordsBlood around the brain can cause trouble days later, so people often stay in hospital for up to two weeks and take a medicine called nimodipine [[Hoh2023,Pickard1989]].

Open Both guidelines favour treatment within 24 hours [[Hoh2023,Vergouwen2026]]. ESO moved from the 2013 target of within 72 hours, yet stresses that the evidence for under 24 hours is low-quality observational, and very early treatment might worsen early brain injury and DCI. No randomised trial has settled it [[Vergouwen2026]].

ESO 2026 suggests reserving stent-assisted coiling for when other options are not feasible, and flow diversion as a last line. ISAT-2 stopped early for poor enrolment [[Vergouwen2026]]. A non-randomised 24-centre comparison (n=9159) linked coiling to higher 14-day case fatality and non-superior 90-day outcome, as quoted in a review [[Claassen2022]]. AHA/ASA 2023 recommends follow-up cerebrovascular imaging after repair (1, B-NR) [[Hoh2023]].

Exam hookISAT: 1-year death or dependence about 7% less with coiling, late rebleeding more common [[Molyneux2005]].

Monitoring in AHA/ASA 2023: CT angiography or perfusion (2a), transcranial Doppler (2a), continuous EEG in high grade (2a), invasive monitoring may be considered (2b) [[Hoh2023]]. Early evidence DCI is now understood as multifactorial: microcirculatory dysfunction, glymphatic impairment, inflammation and neuroelectric disruption alongside vasospasm [[Dodd2021]]. In research, main outcomes are cerebral infarction and functional outcome, while vasospasm can be proof of concept only [[Vergouwen2010]].

Flagged discrepancy

36 of 49 is 73.5%, the abstract prints 71.4%

Both figures are shown because they do not match in the source: SIAD in 36 of 49, printed as 71.4% [[Hannon2014]]. This series found no cerebral salt wasting. A review instead treats SIAD or cerebral salt wasting as causes that are hard to separate, says fluid restriction can be harmful, and focuses management on euvolaemia [[Claassen2022]].

AHA/ASA 2023: goal-directed euvolaemia (2a, B-R); mineralocorticoids reasonable for natriuresis and hyponatraemia (2a, B-R) [[Hoh2023]].

Exam hookHyponatraemia after SAH is mostly SIAD in the best prospective series [[Hannon2014]].

For hydrocephalus, AHA/ASA 2023 recommends urgent CSF diversion when acute and symptomatic (1, B-NR), an EVD bundle (1, B-NR) and permanent diversion when chronic and symptomatic (1, B-NR). Routine lamina terminalis fenestration is not indicated (3: No Benefit, C-LD) [[Hoh2023]]. EVD or lumbar puncture before securing did not clearly raise rebleeding: HR 1.0 (0.4 to 2.7) and 0.7 (0.1 to 6.4) [[Hellingman2007]].

Outcomes over time

Each line joins two published points only. Nothing between them is measured here.

Other series agree on direction but differ on size. In 33 studies, case fatality ranged 8.3 to 66.7%, falling 0.8% a year, 17% over 1973 to 2002, despite mean age rising from 52 to 62 [[Nieuwkamp2009]]. An earlier review gave 32 to 67% over 1960 to 1992 [[Hop1997]]. In the 1980s International Cooperative Study of 3521 people, 26% had died at 6 months and 58% had fully recovered [[Kassell1990]]. AHA text gives prehospital mortality of 22 to 26% and US inpatient mortality of 13.7% in 2006 and 13.1% in 2018 [[Hoh2023]]. The ESO lay summary says about 40% die within 3 months and roughly one third regain independence [[Vergouwen2026]]. A Swiss registry found in-hospital mortality of 20%, or 13.9% once those with active treatment discontinued were excluded [[Stienen2018]]. In 3078 one-year survivors, 20-year excess mortality was 17%, including young people and those with good recovery [[Huhtakangas2015]].

After hospital: long-term issues

Figures are rounded to whole numbers of 100. Each study has its own size and setting.

Hidden problems

People who look normal at discharge may have hidden cognitive deficits that interfere with daily function [[Nussbaum2021]]. Memory, executive function and language are often affected, compounded by depression, anxiety, fatigue and sleep disturbance [[AlKhindi2010]].

Mood

AHA text: depression in about one-third of survivors, anxiety and post-traumatic stress disorder in about 15 to 20%, cognitive impairment in 40 to 70% [[Hoh2023]].

Fatigue

Among 96 good-outcome people with fatigue, it ranked in their three most disabling symptoms, described as low motivation, mental fatigue and sensitivity to stress [[Western2021]].

What guidelines ask for

AHA/ASA 2023: validated screening for physical, cognitive, behavioural and quality-of-life deficits before discharge (1, B-NR), depression and anxiety screening (1, B-NR), early multidisciplinary rehabilitation (1, B-NR) [[Hoh2023]]. Long-term data remain limited [[Rinkel2011]].

In plain wordsMany people who survive still feel tired or find thinking and memory harder, even when they look well. This is real, common and worth raising with the care team [[AlKhindi2010,Western2021]].
