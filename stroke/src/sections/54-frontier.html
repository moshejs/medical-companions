Research frontier

Where stroke research is heading: a new class of blood thinner, drugs to protect the brain, implants that may help recovery, and new ideas for bleeds. Filter the map, pick a route, and scrub through its trials year by year. Every claim carries an evidence label.

Research, not adviceMost of what is here is experimental or used only in trials. Nothing here is a reason to start, stop or change a treatment. Taking part in a trial is a personal choice, and a care team can explain whether a trial may fit. Trial status was read from registries on 8 November 2026 and will change.

Factor XI versus factor X: why bleeding could be less

The comparators in these trials, apixaban and rivaroxaban, act at factor X. Factor XI inhibitors act further up the chain. The hope is a drug that prevents clots while leaving more of the body's ability to seal a tear. That hope is a Hypothesis under test, and the trials have given more than one answer [[Jain2024,Piccini2025,Sharma2026]].

OCEANIC-STROKE was the first completed trial of factor XIa inhibition for prevention after non-cardioembolic stroke or TIA. Mean age was 68 and 67% were male. Index strokes were 43% large artery, 22% small vessel, 30% undetermined and 2% cardioembolic. Dual antiplatelet therapy was planned in 63% [[Sharma2026Design]]. Entry was within 72 hours of the index event, per the registry record, which lists no posted results even though the trial is published [[NCT05686070]]. Regulatory and guideline status were not established when this guide's research was done.

Open arguments

Where trials disagree, both sides are shown. None of these is settled.

Hypothesis

Will factor XI inhibition become standard after stroke?

For: OCEANIC-STROKE [[Sharma2026]]. Caution: both phase 2 trials missed their composite outcome [[Shoamanesh2022,Sharma2024]]. Status: LIBREXIA-STROKE has no reported result [[NCT05702034]].

Hypothesis

Could a factor XI drug still work in AF?

Asundexian failed against apixaban [[Piccini2025]]. LIBREXIA-AF and LILAC have not reported [[NCT05757869,NCT05712200]].

Early evidence

Is nerinetide real in very early treatment with reperfusion?

For: a post hoc pooled analysis and 1-year early-window data [[Tymianski2025,Adams2026]]. Against: the primary results of all three trials were neutral [[Hill2020,Hill2025,Christenson2025]]. It needs a prospective trial.

Early evidence

Does remote conditioning work?

For: RICAMIS and SERIC-EVT [[Chen2022,Guo2026]]. Against: the sham-controlled Danish RESIST and SERIC-IVT [[Blauenfeldt2023,Guo2025]]. The meta-analysis found better favourable outcomes only without reperfusion [[Lam2026]], which conflicts with SERIC-EVT [[Guo2026]].

Early evidence

Is colchicine useful after stroke?

For: a pooled fall in ischaemic stroke [[Fiolet2024]]. Against: CONVINCE missed significance and CHANCE-3 was neutral [[Kelly2024,Li2024CHANCE3]]. CHANCE-3 measured 90 days, while CONVINCE reported long-term follow-up.

Early evidence

Do GLP-1 receptor agonists prevent stroke?

Yes, in diabetes: stroke HR 0.84 (95% CI 0.76-0.93) across 7 trials [[Kristensen2019]]. No difference: in a wider analysis of 21 trials [[Galli2025]].

Early evidence

Can trials from one country be generalised?

BAST and TASTE-2 are positive or borderline in Chinese centres [[Wang2023,Wang2026TASTE2]], and the RIC meta-analysis raises regional generalisability [[Lam2026]]. Replication elsewhere was not found when this research was done.

Early evidence

Is intra-arterial lysis after thrombectomy useful?

CHOICE and ANGEL-TNK were positive with small samples [[Renu2022,Miao2025]]. POST-TNK and POST-UK were not significant [[Huang2025,Liu2025]].

Established, debated

Should people be screened for AF, and how hard?

STROKESTOP found a small net benefit [[Svennberg2021]]. LOOP did not reach significance, and its authors suggest "not all atrial fibrillation is worth screening for" [[Svendsen2021]].

Exam hookA drug that improves a marker or a time may still not improve function: FASTEST slowed haematoma growth without functional benefit [[Broderick2026]], AI cut door-to-groin time with no shown functional gain [[MartinezGutierrez2023]], and clazosentan reduced vasospasm without better function [[Alsakti2026]]. Open-label gains can vanish with sham control: SB623 versus ACTIsSIMA [[Steinberg2019,NCT02448641]]. Fluoxetine reduces depression but does not improve disability after stroke, and raises fracture risk [[Lundstrom2020,Legg2021]].
In plain wordsScientists are trying to protect brain cells during a stroke. Many drugs looked good in animals but have not clearly helped people yet [[Hill2025,Christenson2025]]. Stem cell treatments have been safe in trials so far, but have not clearly improved recovery in controlled studies [[Hess2017,Houkin2024,NCT02448641]]. Early results from brain and spinal cord implants are hopeful. They come from very small groups and need bigger studies [[Baker2023,deFreitas2026]]. This guide does not recommend any treatment.
