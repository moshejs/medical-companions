Stroke Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on stroke, made for medical, nursing and therapy courses and for people who have had a stroke, their families and carers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Heart Attack Companion, on arterial disease that strokes and heart attacks share, the Alzheimer's Companion, on the dementia that vascular cognitive impairment can overlap with, and the Sepsis Companion, on the critical care that some people with severe stroke also need, and the Obesity Companion, on a risk factor for high blood pressure, diabetes and stroke.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
