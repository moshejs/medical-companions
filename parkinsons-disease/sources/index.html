Parkinson's Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on Parkinson's disease, made for health-science students, people living with Parkinson's, and the families who care for them.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should change a treatment without your own clinician. Parkinson's medicines should never be stopped suddenly. Guideline doses are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
