Part 3 · Treatment

Deep-brain stimulation

A thin lead in the subthalamic nucleus or the globus pallidus delivers small, rapid electrical pulses from an implanted pulse generator. Step through the surgery and the first switch-on, raise the amplitude to see how far the field spreads, and watch the abnormal beta rhythm settle.

What it helps

Mostly what levodopa helps, more evenly

  • Off-time and swings: on-time without troubling dyskinesia rose 4.6 hours a day against none on medical therapy. 369
  • Dyskinesia: in people followed for about 17 years, time with dyskinesia was 75% lower than before surgery and dopaminergic medication about half. 39
  • Tremor, even when levodopa does not control it. 44
  • Quality of life over medical therapy in randomised trials. 81,324,379
What it does not fix

Axial signs, speech, thinking

  • Freezing of gait, postural instability and cognitive decline often develop over time. 44
  • Speech was the only motor item not improved at 5 years, and STN stimulation can worsen speech and gait in some people. 44,179
  • Verbal fluency falls moderately after STN-DBS. 279
  • No treatment, DBS included, is rated clinically useful for slowing progression. 111

What the trials found

Six randomised trials and two long-term cohorts carry most of the evidence. The bars show each trial's own outcome, so they cannot be compared across cards. The MDS evidence review rates bilateral STN and GPi stimulation "clinically useful" for both motor fluctuations and dyskinesia. 111

The other device-aided route: infusions

For advanced fluctuations, a pump can replace pills with a continuous supply. NICE advises against DBS when best medical therapy controls symptoms, and best medical therapy can include apomorphine infusion. 241 The details of how levodopa levels swing, and how infusions flatten them, are in Levodopa over time.
