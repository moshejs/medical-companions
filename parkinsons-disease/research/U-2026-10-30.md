## Findings

### cure · Repurposed drugs and supplements
- NOPARK (nicotinamide riboside 500 mg twice daily vs placebo, 52 weeks; 410 randomised with early PD at 11 Norwegian centres, 393 analysed; NCT03568968, phase 3): MDS-UPDRS I–III +2.45 vs −0.27, adjusted difference 2.72 (95% CI 0.47–4.98; P=.02) favouring placebo. NMSS worse with nicotinamide riboside (difference 4.22; P=.009). Serious adverse events 8.3% vs 14.1%. Established negative [[Brakedal2026]].
- Published 8 Oct 2026, before the previous pass's window; added here because the site did not cover it.

### Exam hooks
- NOPARK: nicotinamide riboside did worse than placebo on MDS-UPDRS in phase 3 [[Brakedal2026]].

### In plain words
- A large trial tested nicotinamide riboside, a vitamin B3 supplement, as a way to slow Parkinson's. People taking it did slightly worse than people on a dummy pill [[Brakedal2026]].
