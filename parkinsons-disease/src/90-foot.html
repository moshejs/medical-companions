Parkinson's Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on Parkinson's disease, made for health-science students, people living with Parkinson's, and the families who care for them.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one disease each. Also there: Alzheimer's Companion, a related guide to the other common neurodegenerative disease, and FMF Companion, on Familial Mediterranean Fever.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
