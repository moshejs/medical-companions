Diagnosis and the MDS criteria

There is still no single test for Parkinson's. The diagnosis is clinical: a specialist confirms parkinsonism, looks for anything that points elsewhere, and weighs the warning signs against the supportive ones. Tick the features below, or load an example, and the 2015 MDS criteria give their verdict. Then see what a scan or a synuclein test adds.

The MDS clinical diagnostic criteria were built for research, but may serve as a general guide in clinical practice. [[Fox2025,Marsili2018]] NICE guidance in the UK, which predates them, still bases diagnosis on the UK Brain Bank criteria and advises referring people with suspected Parkinson's "quickly and untreated" to a specialist. [[NICE2017]] This calculator is a teaching tool, not a diagnosis.

The test toolbox

FDA approved

DaT-SPECT (ioflupane)

Approved in 2011, with suspected dementia with Lewy bodies added in 2022, as "an adjunct to other diagnostic evaluations". It shows loss of dopamine terminals, but PD, MSA and PSP all have that, and the scan "was not designed to distinguish among the conditions". It changed management in 54% and the diagnosis in 31% of people with suspected parkinsonism. NICE suggests considering it for tremor when essential tremor cannot be told from parkinsonism clinically. [[FDA-DaTscan,Bega2021,NICE2017]]

Debated

When the scan is normal (SWEDD)

In the ELLDOPA trial, 19 of 142 imaged people with clinically diagnosed early PD (about 13%) had no dopaminergic deficit. Under MDS 2015 a normal scan is an absolute exclusion, so they cannot count as PD. In PPMI, synuclein SAA was positive in 9% of these people against 4% of controls. Some experts object that early scans can be of low quality. [[Fahn2004,Marsili2018,Siderowf2023]]

Established

Cardiac MIBG

Against other neurodegenerative parkinsonism, the delayed heart-to-mediastinum ratio gave 89.7% sensitivity and 82.6% specificity; in early PD, 94.1% and 80.2%. Cardiac sympathetic denervation on MIBG is one of the MDS supportive criteria, alongside smell loss, but no autopsy series in a 2025 review reported it, a sign of limited availability. [[Orimo2012,Postuma2015,Fox2025]]

Established

MRI signs

  • Loss of the nigrosome-1 "swallow tail": sensitivity 0.94, specificity 0.90, but only against healthy adults. [[Chau2020]]
  • Hummingbird sign for PSP: specificity 99.5%, sensitivity only 51.6%. [[Mueller2018]]
  • Hot-cross-bun sign and putaminal diffusivity (90% sensitivity, 93% specificity against PD) are MRI markers in the 2022 MSA criteria. [[Wenning2022]]
  • NICE advises against structural MRI to diagnose PD itself. [[NICE2017]]
Research use

Synuclein seed amplification (SAA)

In PPMI spinal fluid, SAA found PD with 87.7% sensitivity and 96.3% specificity against controls. Positivity was 98.6% in sporadic PD with smell loss, 78.3% without it, and 67.5% in LRRK2 PD. The FDA has endorsed it for enriching clinical trials, which is not a diagnostic approval; access is "mostly limited to research studies". [[Siderowf2023,Stephenson2026,Fox2025]]

Early evidence

Skin biopsy

Phosphorylated synuclein in skin was found in 92.7% of PD, 98.2% of MSA, 96.0% of DLB, 100% of pure autonomic failure and 3.3% of controls. The authors say it still needs validation "in unselected clinical populations". The commercial test is not FDA-approved as a diagnostic for PD. [[Gibbons2024,Hospitalist2024]]

Advised against

Levodopa and apomorphine challenges

Sensitivity for established PD was 0.86 for apomorphine, 0.75 for an acute levodopa test and 0.91 for chronic levodopa therapy. The review concluded that the acute tests add nothing to chronic treatment and cause adverse events, and NICE advises against them. [[Clarke2000,NICE2017]]

Established

How often the clinical diagnosis is right

In 100 brains of people diagnosed with PD by neurologists in 1992, only 76 had PD. A meta-analysis put the accuracy of clinical diagnosis at 80.6%, and it had not improved over 25 years. In a London community study, at least 15% of people with a PD diagnosis did not meet strict criteria. [[Hughes1992,Rizzo2016,Schrag2002]]
