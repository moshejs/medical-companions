Hallucinations and delusions: the published approach

NICE NG71 and the 2019 MDS evidence review set out a sequence: ask and look for causes, leave well-tolerated symptoms alone, reduce the medicines that may have triggered them, and only then add a medicine for psychosis. Impulse-control problems, which are more common on dopamine agonists, are covered in The other medicines.
