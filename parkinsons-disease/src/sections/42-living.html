What the research found

Freezing of gait and cueing

In freezing of gait the feet seem glued to the floor, often after steps grow shorter and faster. External cues, a rhythm to step to or lines to step over, are used to get walking going again; the RESCUE trial tested home training with rhythmic cues. [[Nieuwboer2007]] Choose a cue and play.

Palliative care: what the trial found

Palliative care is care focused on quality of life and symptoms, alongside usual treatment, at any stage. One randomised trial tested an outpatient team approach for people with Parkinson's and related disorders. [[Kluger2020]]

Planning ahead and the end of life

Guideline

Talking about the future

NICE recommends offering discussions about prognosis and advance care planning, including advance decisions to refuse treatment, do-not-resuscitate decisions and lasting power of attorney, and considering referral to palliative care at any stage. [[NICE2017]]

Consensus

Palliative care in neurology

A European consensus for progressive neurological disease recommends early integration of palliative care, a multidisciplinary team, symptom management, end-of-life care and carer support, mostly at evidence level C; symptom management is level B. [[Oliver2016]]

Cohort

How people die with Parkinson's

In an incident cohort, 55% had died by 10 years. Death certificates listed Parkinson's as a substantial contributor in only 20%, and pneumonia was the commonest cause of death. [[Williams-Gray2013]] Swallowing problems are under-reported: 35% when asked, 82% on testing. [[Kalf2012]]

Cohort

Twenty years on

In the Sydney cohort, 74% had died by 20 years. Among survivors, 83% had dementia, 87% had falls and 48% lived in nursing homes; only one person lived independently. [[Hely2008]] See Stages and symptoms and Beyond movement.

Survey

Non-motor symptoms weigh more

In 411 patients the Non-Motor Symptoms Scale tracked quality of life more closely than the motor score (correlation 0.70 against 0.58), and was its best predictor. [[Martinez-Martin2011]] In 1,072 patients, 98.6% reported at least one non-motor symptom. [[Barone2009]]

In hospital

Medicines on time

Parkinson's medicines should not be stopped abruptly, because of the risk of acute akinesia or neuroleptic malignant syndrome, and should be given at the right times in hospital, which may mean allowing self-medication. [[NICE2017]] See Nursing care.
