The dopamine pathway

Movement depends on a loop that runs from the cortex through the basal ganglia and thalamus and back. Dopamine from a small strip of the midbrain keeps that loop tilted toward "go". Step through the loop, then take the dopamine away and watch the brake win.

The dopamine reserve: how much is gone before symptoms

Symptoms do not start when the first nigral neuron dies. Loss builds silently for years, and the nerve endings in the striatum go faster than the cell bodies in the midbrain.
