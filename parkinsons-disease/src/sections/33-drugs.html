Kidneys, liver and interactions: label information

What the US prescribing information says, reported as published for study. It is not dosing advice for any person; doses are set with a prescriber.

Never change or stop a Parkinson's medicine without a clinician. NICE says antiparkinsonian medicines "should not be withdrawn abruptly or allowed to fail suddenly due to poor absorption", and advises against drug holidays, because of the risk of acute akinesia or neuroleptic malignant syndrome. Labels describe a neuroleptic malignant syndrome-like reaction after levodopa is cut or stopped, and parkinsonian crisis after amantadine is stopped abruptly; tapering a dopamine agonist produced a withdrawal syndrome in 19% of tapers in one series. [[NICE2017,LevodopaLabels2026,AmantadineLabels2026,Rabinak2010]]
