Part 2 · Course and diagnosis

The differential

Several conditions look like Parkinson's at first, and some are far more common than people expect. In one US county, Parkinson's disease made up only 42% of new cases of parkinsonism and drugs caused 20%. [[Bower1999]] Follow the questions a specialist asks, watch a rest tremor and an action tremor side by side, then compare any look-alike with Parkinson's feature by feature.

A simplified teaching sequence from published criteria and guidance. Real assessments ask these questions together, not one at a time, and loop back as the illness unfolds.

Rest tremor or action tremor?

A key early question is whether a shaking hand is a parkinsonian rest tremor or the action tremor of essential tremor. Play a short examination: arm resting, lifting, held out, finger to nose, and back to rest.

Why the differential is hard

Established

Parkinsonism has many causes

Among 364 new cases of parkinsonism in Olmsted County, Minnesota: 42% Parkinson's disease, 20% drug-induced, 17% unspecified, 14% parkinsonism in dementia and 7% other causes. Men had higher incidence for every type except drug-induced. [[Bower1999]]

Established

Even experts are sometimes wrong

Of 100 people diagnosed with PD by neurologists, 24 had another condition at autopsy, including PSP, MSA, Alzheimer's disease and vascular disease. Pooled accuracy of the clinical diagnosis is 80.6%. [[Hughes1992,Rizzo2016]]

Established

More than one disease at once

At the UK Brain Bank, 37.2% of people with PD also had co-pathology such as DLB, MSA or Alzheimer pathology, which complicates any single-diagnosis accuracy figure. Functional symptoms can also coexist with PD. [[Fox2025,AmbarAkkaoui2020]]

Early is hardest

Atypical disorders hide at first

Under the 2008 criteria, sensitivity for MSA at the first visit was only 41% for possible and 18% for probable MSA. For PSP under the older NINDS-SPSP criteria, the median was 24%. In early unclassifiable parkinsonism, the MRI hummingbird and morning-glory signs caught 35.3%. [[Wenning2022,Hoglinger2017,Mueller2018]]

Guidance

What NICE recommends

Suspect PD with "tremor, stiffness, slowness, balance problems and/or gait disorders", refer quickly and untreated to a specialist, and review the diagnosis every 6 to 12 months. NICE advises against PET outside trials, acute levodopa or apomorphine challenge tests and routine smell testing for the differential. [[NICE2017]]

Established

Missed in the community

In a London population study, at least 15% of people with a PD diagnosis did not meet strict criteria, and about 19% of people found to have PD had not been diagnosed with it. [[Schrag2002]]

Exam hookEssential tremor is a bilateral action (postural and kinetic) tremor of the arms; small doses of alcohol may reduce it, though not consistently enough for the definition. Parkinsonian tremor is a rest tremor that is suppressed when movement starts. Early falls and vertical gaze palsy suggest PSP; early autonomic failure and stridor suggest MSA; dementia before or within a year of parkinsonism suggests DLB.
