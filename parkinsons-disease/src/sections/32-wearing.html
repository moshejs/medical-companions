The words used

Off

Wearing-off

Symptoms return in a regular way towards the end of each dose. Predictable wearing-off is the most frequent type of fluctuation. [[Freitas2017]]

Off

On-off ("yo-yoing")

Rapid, abrupt switching between on and off. Unpredictable "sudden offs" come regardless of dose timing, are commoner in advanced disease and can develop within a few seconds. On-off fluctuation is described as now rare, because doses are lower. [[Freitas2017]]

Delay

Delayed-on and partial on

A dose takes much longer than usual to work, or works only partly. Both are put down mainly to erratic absorption, [[Freitas2017]] including slow stomach emptying. [[Jenner2026]]

Off

No-on (dose failure)

A dose does not work at all. Like delayed-on, it is attributed mainly to erratic levodopa absorption. [[Freitas2017]]

Dyskinesia

Peak-dose dyskinesia

Involuntary movements at the time of peak levodopa action. Chorea is the most common form. [[Freitas2017]]

Dyskinesia

Diphasic dyskinesia

Movements as levels rise and again as they fall, at the start and end of a dose: the "dystonia-improvement-dystonia" pattern. It mainly affects the legs and occurs in about 15 to 20% of people. [[Freitas2017]] Low levels can cause dyskinesia, as well as high ones. [[Contin2010]]

Off

Off-period dystonia

Dystonia during off periods or at the switch from on to off, often painful and especially in the legs. Some people also have worse symptoms at the start of a dose, or a rebound at the end that is worse than before treatment. [[Freitas2017]]

Diary

How on and off are counted

The Hauser home diary records each half-hour as asleep, off, on without dyskinesia, on with non-troublesome dyskinesia, or on with troublesome dyskinesia. Off-time and on-time with troublesome dyskinesia count as "bad time". Completion fell off after 3 days. [[Hauser2004]]

Infusions and devices

When tablets can no longer give a smooth day, the options deliver medicine continuously or change the circuit itself. NICE asks for best medical therapy first, which may include apomorphine, and DBS only when that does not control symptoms. [[NICE2017]] See Deep-brain stimulation.

Approved

Levodopa-carbidopa intestinal gel

Duopa: pumped into the jejunum through a PEG-J tube over 16 hours, up to 2000 mg of levodopa a day; US approval 9 January 2015. [[DrugsFDA2026F]] Against oral levodopa: off-time −1.91 hours a day, good on-time +1.86. Adverse events were mostly tube-related. [[Olanow2014]] The label lists bezoar, intussusception, obstruction, perforation, pancreatitis, peritonitis and sepsis, and polyneuropathy in 5% of trial patients. [[DrugsFDA2026F]]

Approved

Foslevodopa-foscarbidopa

Vyalev (Produodopa in the EU): a 24-hour infusion under the skin by a small pump; US approval 16 October 2024. [[DrugsFDA2026F,AbbVie2024]] Against oral levodopa: good on-time +1.75 hours, off-time −1.79. Site redness 27%, pain 26%, cellulitis 19%; 22% stopped for adverse events against 1%. [[Soileau2022]] NICE (TA934) reserves it for when apomorphine or DBS cannot be used or no longer work. [[NICE2017]]

Not approved in the US

ND0612

Continuous subcutaneous levodopa-carbidopa. In BouNDless: +1.72 hours good on-time (95% CI 1.08 to 2.36) and 1.40 hours less off-time; site reactions in 83% during the open-label run-in. [[Espay2024]] Both arms started on ND0612, so the "gain" reflects losing benefit after switching to tablets. [[Espay2024]] The FDA issued complete response letters in June 2024 and on 23 October 2025. [[PharmaLetter2025,DrugsFDA2026F]]

Approved

Apomorphine infusion

A dopamine agonist given under the skin through the waking day. TOLEDO (107 people): off-time −2.47 versus −0.58 hours with placebo. [[Katzenschlager2018]] Onapgo, US approval 3 February 2025: off-time −2.55 versus −0.90 hours; common reactions included infusion-site nodules, nausea and sleepiness. [[AgonistLabels2026,DrugsFDA2026F]]

Sweden

Levodopa-entacapone-carbidopa gel

Lecigon adds entacapone to the intestinal gel: in an 11-person crossover, a 20% lower levodopa dose gave the same exposure. [[Senek2017]] In the Swedish registry (150 people) complications were mainly tube-related (30%); 13.3% stopped. [[Othman2025]]

Surgery

Deep-brain stimulation

In the VA trial, bilateral STN or GPi stimulation gave 4.6 more hours a day of on-time without troubling dyskinesia, against 0 with best medical therapy. [[Weaver2009]] The MDS review rates both targets clinically useful for fluctuations and dyskinesia. [[Fox2018]] See Deep-brain stimulation.

Reading the evidence

Disputed

Does the window really narrow?

The picture of a window closing from above, with the dyskinesia threshold falling towards the on threshold, is widely taught. In 20 people followed from their first levodopa for 4 years, dyskinesia and benefit always began and ended together, which the authors judged not consistent with a falling threshold. [[Nutt2010]] No source gives the window's width in numbers, so the model above is a drawing, not a measurement.

Established

The buffer runs down

In 18 people tested over 4 years, the long-duration response decayed faster after 3 days off levodopa and the short-duration response grew, while its duration did not change. Dyskinesia became more severe. [[Nutt2002]] How the body handles levodopa does not change; what changes is in the brain. [[Fabbrini1987]]

Established

Dose and the young

In STRIDE-PD (745 analysed), dyskinesia and wearing-off became more likely as the levodopa dose rose (p<0.001 for both). Young onset was the strongest predictor of dyskinesia. [[WarrenOlanow2013]] Starting with entacapone did not prevent dyskinesia: 42% versus 32% at week 134. [[Stocchi2010]]

A common myth

"Save levodopa for later"

The Parkinson's Foundation answers the belief that levodopa stops working after five years: it works for decades, and works best taken on time, just before the previous dose wears off. [[PFMyths]] LEAP found no harm from starting early. [[Verschuur2019]]

Guidance

On time, every time

NICE's standard is levodopa within 30 minutes of the person's own prescribed time in hospital or a care home. [[NICE2018QS]] Parkinson's UK says a delay of as little as 30 minutes can decide whether someone can move, walk, talk or swallow. [[PUK2023]] See Nursing care.

The short version

In plain wordsEarly on, levodopa works all day. Later, each dose wears off sooner, and the gap between too little and too much gets narrower. Smaller, more frequent doses, longer-acting forms, add-on medicines, pumps and surgery all aim to keep the level steady. Any change is decided with the treating team, and Parkinson's medicines are never stopped suddenly.
