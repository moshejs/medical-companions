Other medicines: topiramate, gabapentin, baclofen and more

Beyond the four US-approved products, many medicines have been tested for alcohol use disorder. Some are approved for AUD in other countries; others are used off-label. Build the evidence ladder row by row, then filter by situation. [[KranzlerSoyka2018]]

Read this firstNone of the medicines on this page is approved by the US FDA for AUD. Doses appear only as the arms of named trials, never as instructions. Baclofen, gabapentin and sodium oxybate can cause sleepiness, and mixing alcohol with sedating medicines, opioids or sleeping pills can be dangerous. If a person drinks heavily every day, get medical help to stop safely. [[KranzlerSoyka2018,Agabio2023Cochrane,Leone2010Cochrane,FDA-Naltrexone]]

    Baclofen: why the trials disagree

    Each row uses its own trial's outcome; the rows are not directly comparable. [[Addolorato2007Lancet,Rigal2020Bacloville,Reynaud2017Alpadir,Beraha2016Baclofen]]

    Baclofen · contrast

    Titrated, fixed, high or low?

    Bacloville titrated doses in primary care for a year and helped more people reach no or low-risk drinking, but with more serious adverse events: 85 people on baclofen (7 deaths) vs 36 on placebo (3 deaths). ALPADIR, aiming for a fixed target after detoxification, showed no difference in abstinence, though craving fell more. In the Dutch trial only 15.5% of the high-dose group reached the top dose. [[Rigal2020Bacloville,Reynaud2017Alpadir,Beraha2016Baclofen]]

    A meta-analysis of 13 trials found low doses did no worse than high doses, and high doses were poorly tolerated. The 2023 Cochrane review (17 trials, 1,818 people) found baclofen probably lowers relapse risk (RR 0.87) and increases percent days abstinent (MD 9.07), mainly in detoxified participants, with no effect on heavy drinking days. Established [[Pierce2018Baclofen,Agabio2023Cochrane]]

    In plain wordsBaclofen seems to help some people stay off alcohol, but the best dose is not settled and higher doses bring more side effects. [[Agabio2023Cochrane,Pierce2018Baclofen]]
    Exam hookBaclofen is a GABA-B agonist with an RCT in cirrhosis (71% vs 29% abstinent). France: temporary use up to 300 mg/day from 2014, capped at 80 mg/day in July 2017, marketing authorisation October 2018. [[KranzlerSoyka2018,Addolorato2007Lancet,deBeaurepaire2022TRU,Payet2026Baclofen]]

    Everything side by side: a network meta-analysis

    Risk ratio for total abstinence vs placebo (1 = no difference). Bahji 2022, 156 trials, 27,334 people. [[Bahji2022NMA]]

    Comparing every medicine at once, the network meta-analysis found that GHB, baclofen, disulfiram, gabapentin, acamprosate and oral naltrexone improved total abstinence over placebo, and that disulfiram, baclofen, acamprosate and oral naltrexone reduced heavy drinking. [[Bahji2022NMA]]

    The authors concluded that acamprosate, disulfiram, baclofen and oral naltrexone had the best evidence. Nalmefene (RR 3.26), fluvoxamine (3.08) and topiramate (2.18) caused more dropouts for adverse events than placebo. [[Bahji2022NMA]]

    Network estimates combine direct and indirect comparisons from very different trials, so they guide questions rather than settle them. The approved medicines are on the previous page.

    What "not approved" means

    Guidelines

    Not approved is not the same as never used

    The APA 2018 guideline, as quoted in reviews, suggests topiramate or gabapentin for patients who prefer them, or who cannot tolerate or have not responded to the FDA-approved medicines. The US VA/DoD guideline lists topiramate among appropriate first-line treatments. [[Reus2018APA,Kranzler2019Gabapentin,KranzlerSoyka2018]]

    Other countries

    Approved elsewhere

    The EU authorised nalmefene to reduce drinking in adults with alcohol dependence at a high drinking-risk level (men >60 g/day, women >40 g/day). Sodium oxybate has been approved in Italy since 1991 and Austria since 1999. France authorised baclofen in October 2018. [[vandenBrink2013Nalmefene,Guiraud2022SMO,Payet2026Baclofen]]

    French baclofen starts peaked at 98,552 people in 2014 and fell to 48,471 in 2021; the 2018 authorisation had no impact on starts. [[Payet2026Baclofen]]

    Safety

    Sedation and misuse

    About 1% of the general population has misused gabapentin, sometimes with alcohol, which reviewers cite against first-line use. Sodium oxybate (GHB) is a central nervous system depressant with a risk of addiction or misuse. Baclofen causes sleepiness. With alcohol or other sedatives, each is a safety concern. [[KranzlerSoyka2018,Leone2010Cochrane,Agabio2023Cochrane]]

    Genes

    Gene-guided treatment is not ready

    Carriers of the OPRM1 Asp40 allele did better on naltrexone in retrospective analyses (in COMBINE, 87.1% vs 54.8% good outcome), but a prospective genotype-stratified trial found no interaction (P = .32). Two reviews conclude the evidence does not support gene tests to choose a medicine. Early evidence [[Anton2008OPRM1,Oslin2015JAMAPsych,KranzlerSoyka2018,Fairbanks2020Mayo]]

    Frontier

    GLP-1 medicines

    Once-weekly exenatide did not reduce heavy drinking days overall in 127 patients; low-dose semaglutide (48 people, 9 weeks) reduced lab drinking, drinks per drinking day and craving. Early evidence More on the research frontier. [[Klausen2022Exenatide,Hendershot2025Semaglutide]]

    In plain wordsSome medicines made for other conditions, like topiramate and gabapentin, can also help with drinking. Whether one fits a particular person is a decision for that person and their clinician. [[Reus2018APA,KranzlerSoyka2018]]

    Need help now?If you or someone else may have alcohol poisoning or severe withdrawal (seizure, confusion, hallucinations), call emergency services. For help with drinking in the US, SAMHSA's National Helpline is free and confidential: 1-800-662-4357. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123).
