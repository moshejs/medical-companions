Which marker answers which question?

Pick a question. The markers that can answer it light up, with their accuracy as published. Many of the figures come from studies in people with liver disease, where the review found PEth, urine EtG and hair EtG most useful. [[Arnts2021LiverBiomarkers]]

Reading a PEth value: a teaching band reader

Teaching only. Cut-offs differ between studies and settings, and a single PEth value gives only an approximate estimate of how much and how recently someone drank. Self-report and PEth can disagree in both directions. Results belong in a conversation with the care team, never a quick judgment. [[Helander2019PEthDose,Hahn2016PEth]]

Bands as proposed by Ulwelling and Smith; other cut-offs as published. Decay uses mean half-lives in AUD withdrawal treatment (7.24 days; range of individual half-lives 3.7–10.4 days in another detox study). Good to know

Direct vs indirect

Formed only with ethanol

EtG, EtS, PEth and FAEE are formed only when ethanol is present. GGT, MCV and CDT are indirect: affected by age, sex, other substances and other illness, and they miss parts of the drinking timeline. [[Wurst2015Metabolites]]

Moderate drinking

PEth sees what others miss

After a week of one or two glasses of wine a day (16 or 32 g), all 21 participants had measurable PEth while CDT, AST, ALT and GGT changed little. In a 3-month randomised study, PEth was the only marker that clearly separated moderate drinking from abstinence. [[Walther2026PEth,Kechagias2015PEth]]

Used well

Markers support the conversation

Combining self-report with a highly specific biomarker raises sensitivity without losing specificity. Traditional markers also help detect complications, motivate change and monitor treatment. [[Hahn2016PEth,Conigrave2003Markers]]

Not for driving

No calculation says it is safe to drive

Blood alcohol estimates carry real uncertainty (one analysis proposed a ±21% coefficient of variation), and published models disagree. Virtually all drivers are impaired on some measures at 0.05. [[Searle2015,Posey2007Widmark,Fell2014BAC]]

Safety. If you or someone else may have alcohol poisoning or severe withdrawal (seizure, confusion, hallucinations), call emergency services. Stopping suddenly after long, heavy daily drinking can be dangerous: get medical help to stop safely. Next: Alcohol withdrawal: the timeline.
