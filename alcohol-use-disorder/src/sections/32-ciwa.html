Part 3 · Withdrawal & acute care

CIWA-Ar: scoring withdrawal

The CIWA-Ar puts a number on how severe alcohol withdrawal is: ten items, a total from 0 to 67. Try the teaching calculator, then watch how symptom-triggered care uses the score to give less medicine for a shorter time than a fixed schedule.

SafetyStopping suddenly after long, heavy daily drinking can be dangerous: get medical help to stop safely. A seizure, confusion or hallucinations during withdrawal are an emergency: call emergency services. [[ASAM2020Withdrawal]]

The ten items: a teaching calculator

CIWA-Ar is a 10-item shortened version of the CIWA-A, designed to be efficient while keeping its clinical usefulness, validity and reliability. Nine items score 0–7 and orientation scores 0–4, so the maximum is 67. [[Sullivan1989CIWA,Mirijello2015]] Before scoring, compare: the PAWSS predicts risk before symptoms start; CIWA-Ar measures withdrawal once it has started. [[ASAM2020Withdrawal]]

Teaching toolThis calculator shows how the published scale is built and how the bands are read. It is for learning only: it is not a self-test, not for scoring yourself or a family member, and not a substitute for a clinician's assessment. In practice the score is taken by trained staff and used only within their own clinical protocols. It never says what medicine or dose to give.

When CIWA-Ar doesn't fit

Not a diagnosis

Other conditions raise the score

ASAM 2020 Recommendation I.6: withdrawal scales including CIWA-Ar "should not be used as a diagnostic tool", because conditions other than withdrawal can raise the score. [[ASAM2020Withdrawal]] Tremor, sweating, a fast pulse or anxiety can have other causes.

Delirium & ICU

It needs a patient who can answer

Recommendation VI.9: CIWA-Ar is not recommended in delirium, because it relies on patient-reported symptoms; CAM-ICU, DDS, RASS or MINDS are used instead. [[ASAM2020Withdrawal]] CAM-ICU in 111 ventilated ICU patients: sensitivity 100% and 93%, specificity 98% and 100% for two nurses, about 2 minutes to give. [[Ely2001CAMICU]] RASS runs from +4 ("combative") to −5 ("unarousable"). [[Sessler2002RASS]]

Medical & surgical wards

Trust low scores, question high ones

Recommendation VII.16: in general medical or surgical patients, low scores can usually be trusted but high scores should be read with caution; fever from infection, some medicines and communication problems can all confound the score. [[ASAM2020Withdrawal]]

Misuse in practice

Half the protocols were misapplied

Of 124 randomly chosen Mayo Clinic inpatients on a CIWA-Ar protocol, only 60 (48%) met both requirements: recent drinking and intact verbal communication. 9 drank but could not communicate, 35 could communicate but had not been drinking, and 20 met neither. [[Hecksel2008]]

Other scales

SAWS and GMAWS

The Short Alcohol Withdrawal Scale is a 10-item self-administered scale for ambulatory care that records the prior few hours. The Glasgow Modified Alcohol Withdrawal Scale has 5 items (maximum 10): ≥1 gave sensitivity 100%, specificity 12%, and ≥2 gave 98% and 39% for predicting CIWA-A ≥8. [[ASAM2020Withdrawal]]

Get helpIf you or someone else may have alcohol poisoning or severe withdrawal (seizure, confusion, hallucinations), call emergency services. For help with drinking in the US, SAMHSA's National Helpline is free and confidential: 1-800-662-4357. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123).
