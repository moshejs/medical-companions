AUD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on alcohol use disorder and alcohol withdrawal, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with alcohol use disorder, their families and friends.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: Bipolar Companion and ADHD Companion, two neighbouring guides on conditions that often occur alongside alcohol use disorder, and the Cirrhosis Companion, on the liver scarring that long-term heavy drinking can cause, and the Depression Companion, on major depressive disorder, which often occurs alongside alcohol use disorder.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
