AUD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on alcohol use disorder and alcohol withdrawal, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with alcohol use disorder, their families and friends.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: Bipolar Companion and ADHD Companion, two neighbouring guides on conditions that often occur alongside alcohol use disorder, and the Cirrhosis Companion, on the liver scarring that long-term heavy drinking can cause.
