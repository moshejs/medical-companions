Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Need help now?If you or someone else may have alcohol poisoning or severe withdrawal (seizure, confusion, hallucinations), call emergency services. For help with drinking in the US, SAMHSA's National Helpline is free and confidential: 1-800-662-4357. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123).

For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. The AUDIT, the criteria counter, CIWA-Ar and the other calculators are teaching aids, not self-tests. Finish with flashcards, memory aids and a quiz.

For people with alcohol use disorder, families and friends

Look for the In plain words boxes. Alcohol use disorder is a treatable medical condition, not a moral failing, and the Recovery module shows, with sources, how drinking changes for people over the years. Bring questions to your own care team: the app reports what labels, guidelines and trials say, not what you should take or change.

How sure is each claim?
