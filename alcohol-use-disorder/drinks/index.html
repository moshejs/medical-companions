Not medical adviceThe AUD Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, pharmacist, counsellor or other clinician. The AUDIT, the criteria counter, CIWA-Ar and the other calculators are teaching aids, not self-tests, and doses, benzodiazepine and thiamine regimens and CIWA-Ar thresholds are quoted as published, never as instructions. Stopping suddenly after long, heavy daily drinking can be dangerous: get medical help to stop safely. If you or someone else may have alcohol poisoning or severe withdrawal (seizure, confusion, hallucinations), call emergency services. For help with drinking in the US, SAMHSA's National Helpline is free and confidential: 1-800-662-4357. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123).

Grams, not glasses

This evening is four US standard drinks of about 14 g of ethanol each, one an hour. 261 Ethanol is absorbed quickly from the gut: peak blood alcohol after a dose usually comes 10–60 minutes later. 213 How fast depends on the drinking pattern, the drink's strength and, most of all, whether the person has eaten. 347 Press After a meal to compare.

How many standard drinks is it?

A "standard drink" is a fixed amount of pure ethanol, not a glass. Of 75 national governments examined, 37 had a definition: most often 10 g, ranging from 8 to 20 g. 217 The US standard drink is 0.6 oz of ethanol, for example 12 oz of 5% beer, 5 oz of 12% wine or 1.5 oz of 40% spirits; 0.6 fl oz is about 14 g (the research team's arithmetic: 0.6 × 29.57 mL × 0.789 g/mL). 261 GBD and the WHO AUDIT assume 10 g. 137,179 The UK counts in 8 g units (the Cochrane brief-intervention review gives 244 g a week as 30.5 UK units). 220 The sum is always the same: volume (mL) × ABV ÷ 100 × 0.789 g/mL = grams of ethanol.

14.0 g of ethanol

Binge, heavy and high-intensity drinking: the published definitions

TermDefinition as publishedSource
US standard drink0.6 oz of ethanol: 12 oz of 5% beer, 5 oz of 12% wine or 1.5 oz of 40% spirits.261
Binge drinking (NIAAA)A pattern that brings blood alcohol to about 0.08 g%: 5 or more drinks for men or 4 or more for women. An NIAAA task force recommended adding "within about 2 hours"; in one comparison, the older measure without a time limit predicted negative consequences at least as well.68
Binge drinking (NSDUH surveys)5 or more standard drinks for males, or 4 or more for females, on the same occasion.261
Heavy alcohol use (NSDUH surveys)Binge drinking on 5 or more days in the past month.261
High-intensity drinking8 or more drinks for women, 10 or more for men: two or more times the binge thresholds.356

In 2016, 26.2% of US adults reported at least one binge-drinking day in the past month and 6.6% reported heavy alcohol use. 261 These are population statistics. Most people who drink heavily do not have alcohol dependence: in NSDUH 2009–2011, 10.5% of binge drinkers met DSM-IV dependence criteria. 102 That is not reassurance. Binge drinking still accounted for 76.7% of the US cost of excessive drinking in 2010. 408 For how alcohol use disorder itself is defined, see Diagnosis: DSM-5-TR criteria.

A teaching blood alcohol calculator

This calculator cannot tell anyone whether it is safe to drive or to drink

It draws a textbook curve from published average values. Real people differ: elimination rates vary about four-fold between healthy people 96, five published Widmark-type models give different answers for the same evening 368, and one proposed error band is ±21% (others argue the error must be worked out case by case) 424. It is not for working out your own level, and no number here means "fine". Fatal-crash risk rises steadily with BAC in every age and sex group 511, and a 2014 review found virtually all drivers impaired on at least some driving measures at 0.05. 125

Alcohol poisoning is an emergency: call emergency services. Signs progress from poor judgement, slurred speech, poor coordination and vomiting to stupor, coma and death. 221 Call if someone cannot be woken, is breathing slowly or irregularly, or has a seizure. 1,221 Do not leave them alone; cold can contribute (hypothermia was a contributing cause in 6.0% of US alcohol poisoning deaths). 221 Mixing alcohol with opioids, benzodiazepines or other sedatives is dangerous. 465

What changes the curve, and what does not

Established

Food in the stomach

Ten men drank 0.80 g/kg once fasting and once straight after breakfast. Peak BAC was 104 mg/dL fasting and 67 mg/dL fed; the 0–6 h area under the curve was 398 against 241 mg/dL·h, and the dose was cleared about 2 h sooner after the meal. 211 Eating lowers the peak; it does not make heavy drinking safe.

Established

Body water and sex

Ethanol does not bind to plasma proteins and spreads in total body water 347: about 55–60% of body weight in non-obese men and 50–55% in women 213. After the same amount, women reach higher BACs than men even allowing for body size. 132

Debated

First-pass metabolism in the stomach

Some alcohol is oxidised by gastric ADH before it reaches the blood. In women without alcohol dependence, first-pass metabolism was 23% and gastric ADH activity 59% of men's; in women with alcohol dependence it was "virtually abolished". 132 How much this matters overall is debated; body water is the better-established factor. 59,347

Established

Heavy drinking speeds clearance

CYP2E1 is inducible, so people who drink heavily clear ethanol faster 347: 25–35 mg/100 mL per hour during detoxification against about 15 in moderate drinkers. 212 In 20 people admitted for detoxification, blood ethanol on arrival was 238–489 mg/dL. Tolerance does not remove danger. 210

Established

Acetaldehyde: the toxic step

Acetaldehyde is a mutagen and an animal carcinogen that damages DNA; saliva holds 10–20 times more than blood because mouth bacteria make it. 50 About 3.6% of cancers worldwide derive from chronic drinking. 426 See Genes, family & risk for the flushing variant, and the whole body for cancer risk.

Established

No amount is good for health

GBD 2016 found the level that minimised harm across health outcomes was zero standard drinks a week. 137 After bias adjustment, low-volume drinkers had no lower all-cause mortality than lifetime non-drinkers (RR 0.93). 517 Genetic evidence calls the apparent stroke protection "largely non-causal". 330 The research does not support drinking for health.

AUD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on alcohol use disorder and alcohol withdrawal, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with alcohol use disorder, their families and friends.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine or a plan for stopping drinking without your own care team. The AUDIT, the criteria counter, CIWA-Ar, PAWSS and the blood alcohol and liver calculators are for learning, not self-diagnosis or reading your own results, and the blood alcohol calculator can never tell anyone whether it is safe to drive. Doses and regimens are quoted as published in labels and guidelines, never as instructions. Alcohol poisoning and severe withdrawal are emergencies.
