After the ward

Established

The first 3 months after discharge

Suicide rates after psychiatric discharge are highest in the first 3 months (1132 per 100,000 person-years, a population statistic), so follow-up contact should be arranged before the person leaves. Our sources do not set a specific number of days for the first contact; they say active follow-up after discharge "should be routine". [[Chung2017JAMAPsych,Mann2021Suicide]] See Suicide risk & crisis safety.

NICE CG185

What a discharge plan contains

  • recovery goals
  • a crisis plan with early warning signs and triggers
  • a mental-state assessment
  • a medication plan with review dates and monitoring

The person and their GP each get a copy, and the person is encouraged to share it with carers. Consider intensive case management for people likely to disengage. [[NICE2014CG185]]

Established

Safewards

In a cluster RCT on 31 wards, ten simple staff-patient relationship interventions reduced conflict events by 15% and containment events by 23.2%. [[Bowers2015]]

Early evidence

A standard of nursing care for acute mania

A Dutch three-round Delphi study of professionals, patients and informal caregivers reached consensus on all 89 statements for a standard of nursing care in acute mania on a closed ward. It is consensus, not a trial. [[Goossens2022]]

Physical health

Why metabolic checks matter

Metabolic syndrome affects 37.3% of people with bipolar disorder, 45.3% of those taking antipsychotics and 32.4% of those not taking them. [[Vancampfort2013]] Severe mental illness carries higher cardiovascular disease incidence (HR 1.78) and cardiovascular death (HR 1.85). [[Correll2017CVD]] NICE recommends a combined healthy-eating and physical-activity programme for people on antipsychotics and long-term medicines. [[NICE2014CG185]]

Gaps in practice

Monitoring is often missed

In a UK audit of people on lithium for a year or more, NICE standards were met for 30% of lithium levels, 55% of renal checks and 50% of thyroid checks. [[Collins2010]] Before guidelines, baseline glucose was checked in 44.3% of people starting antipsychotics, and lipids stayed low even afterwards (28.9%). [[Mitchell2012Monitoring]]

In plain words. On the ward, safety comes first, then food, fluids and sleep, then a calm space and calm talk, then learning and planning for home. Looking after the heart matters too: regular checks of weight, blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol are part of good care. [[NICE2014CG185,NICE-NG10,Correll2017CVD]]
