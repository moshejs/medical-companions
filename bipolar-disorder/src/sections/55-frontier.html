Reading the research

Checklist

How to read a trial headline

Ask how many people took part: the ketamine trials that reported 71% and 79% response had 18 and 15 participants, and a network meta-analysis gave ketamine a response ratio of 12.49 with a 95% interval from 3.06 to 50.93, wide because the trials were few and small. Ask what it was compared with, how long it lasted and whether the effect lasted: the first ketamine benefit lasted through day 3. Ask whether a larger trial agreed: a meta-analysis of 8 small trials favoured anti-inflammatory add-ons, but larger trials of minocycline, celecoxib and aspirin were negative. And check that a result exists: an inhaled esketamine trial finished in 2021 with no results posted. [[Diazgranados2010,Zarate2012,Bahji2021,Rosenblat2016,Husain2020,Bruun2026,NCT03965871]]

Prediction is not benefit

Accurate is not the same as helpful

Phone-call voice features told manic or mixed states apart with an AUC of 0.89, yet a randomised trial of daily smartphone self-monitoring did not reduce symptoms, and a 12-month passive-sensing study raised too many false alarms for clinical use. A good classifier still has to be tested in a trial that measures whether people do better. [[FaurholtJepsen2016,FaurholtJepsen2015,Ludwig2025]]

Taking part

How to join research safely

Look the study up on ClinicalTrials.gov by its NCT number: the record shows who runs it, what it is compared with, how many people it needs and whether results have been posted. Talk to your own care team before changing or stopping any medicine for a study. When lithium is stopped, more than half of new episodes come within 10 weeks, and NICE advises that rapid discontinuation may raise the risk of relapse. [[Suppes1991,NICE2014CG185E,NCT06372964]] For your current options, see Lithium, Antipsychotics and Treating bipolar depression.

Early evidence

Light and sleep, already near the clinic

The 2025 ISBD chronobiology task force judged adjunctive bright light likely efficacious for bipolar depression, with anti-manic cover and clinical monitoring. Sleep loss has also been proposed as a pathway into mania, which is why chronotherapy is given by specialist teams alongside medicines. [[Geoffroy2025,Wehr1987,Benedetti2014]] See Brain, sleep & the body clock and Living well.
