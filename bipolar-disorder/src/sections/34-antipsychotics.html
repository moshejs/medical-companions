Metabolic monitoring: what is checked, and when

Some antipsychotics raise weight, blood sugar and blood fats. Metabolic syndrome affects 20–65% of people with bipolar disorder, and weight gain contributes to "upwards of 60%" of treatment non-adherence. Side effects compared

Across 18 antipsychotics in 100 trials (in schizophrenia, median 6 weeks), weight change vs placebo ranged from −0.23 kg with haloperidol to 3.01 kg with clozapine. Olanzapine and clozapine had the worst metabolic profiles; aripiprazole, brexpiprazole, cariprazine, lurasidone and ziprasidone the most benign. Pick a medicine to see what the sources in this guide report for it. [[Pillinger2020]]

Not only for psychosis

Why "antipsychotic" doesn't mean psychosis

The name comes from their first use, but CANMAT/ISBD 2018 lists quetiapine, asenapine, aripiprazole, paliperidone, risperidone and cariprazine among first-line treatments for acute mania, alongside lithium and divalproex, whether or not there is psychosis. Several also treat bipolar depression. [[Yatham2018,Yildiz2023]] Mania is linked to raised striatal dopamine D2/3 receptor availability, one target these medicines act on. Established as a model; mechanism Early evidence [[Ashok2017]]

Long-acting injectables

An injection instead of daily tablets

Risperidone long-acting injection prolonged time to an elevated-mood episode (p<0.001) but not a depressive one; aripiprazole once-monthly 400 mg cut 52-week recurrence to 26.5% vs 51.1% on placebo, "predominantly on manic episodes". CANMAT/ISBD 2018: if psychosocial strategies do not fix non-adherence, "long‐acting injectable medications should be offered". [[Quiroz2010,Calabrese2017AOM,Yatham2018]]

Labels, as published: Risperdal Consta 25 mg IM every 2 weeks; Abilify Maintena 400 mg IM monthly, after tolerability is established with oral aripiprazole. [[FDA-RisperdalConsta,FDA-AbilifyMaintena]]

Staying well

Maintenance

In Trial 144 quetiapine prolonged time to any mood event vs placebo (HR 0.29), as did lithium (HR 0.46). In the olanzapine maintenance trial, relapse was 46.7% vs 80.1% on placebo. After an antipsychotic plus lithium or divalproex treats mania, continuing it for 6 months offered "clear benefit", but "the benefits beyond 6 months remain uncertain". [[Weisler2011Quetiapine,Tohen2006Olanzapine,Yatham2018]]

Boxed warnings

What the labels warn about

Every antipsychotic label read for this guide carries a boxed warning of increased mortality in elderly people with dementia-related psychosis. Those approved for depression also carry the antidepressant-class warning on suicidal thoughts and behaviours in children, adolescents and young adults, with close monitoring for worsening. [[FDA-Seroquel,FDA-Latuda,FDA-Vraylar,FDA-Caplyta,FDA-OlanzapineFluoxetine,FDA-AbilifyMaintena,FDA-RisperdalConsta]]

Part of good care

Healthy eating and activity

NICE recommends offering a combined healthy-eating and physical-activity programme, "especially those taking antipsychotics and long‑term medication", and advises against regular combined antipsychotics "except for short periods (for example, when changing medication)". [[NICE2014CG185F]]
