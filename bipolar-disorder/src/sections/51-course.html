The conditions that come along

In the worldwide surveys, three in four people with bipolar spectrum disorder met criteria for at least one other disorder. [[Merikangas2011]] Each of these can be treated, so it is worth telling the care team about them. Choose a condition.

The mortality gap

All-cause mortality in bipolar disorder is about twice that of the general population (SMR 2.05; risk ratio 2.02 in a larger 2023 meta-analysis). [[Hayes2015,Biazus2023]] Natural causes matter: cardiovascular mortality RR 1.76, respiratory 3.18, while cancer mortality was not raised. [[Biazus2023]] Pooled life expectancy was 66.88 years, 70.51 for women and 64.59 for men. [[Chan2022]] In Danish registers, unaffected siblings had no shortening of life, pointing to the illness rather than shared family factors. [[Kessing2021LifeExp]] The authors conclude that preventing suicide and promoting physical health are both needed. [[Biazus2023]] These are group averages, not a forecast for anyone.

Need help now?If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123).

Specifier

Rapid cycling

Rapid cycling means 4 or more mood episodes in a year. In 8 studies of consecutively admitted patients, 16.3% had it, slightly more often women and people with bipolar II. [[Kupka2003]] It is linked to suicide attempts and poorer response to mood stabilisers. [[Miola2023]] With lithium, 34% of people with rapid cycling had no further recurrences against 47% without it, and partial response was common in both (59% and 65%). [[Kupka2003]] Hypothyroidism was associated with current rapid cycling, and whether antidepressants cause it is unproven Hypothesis. [[Kupka2003]] See specifiers.

Later life

Older age

Older adults with bipolar disorder are a growing group; late onset, physical illness and cognitive decline are the main open questions. [[Sajatovic2015]] Even when mood is steady, small to moderate cognitive differences are found (effect sizes 0.26–0.63), not explained by residual symptoms or medicines. [[Bourne2013]] Dementia is more common (odds ratio 2.96 in 10 studies), and the number of mood episodes predicted it. [[Velosa2020]] In observational studies, lithium treatment went with lower dementia risk (OR 0.51) Early evidence. [[Velosa2020]] In the GERI-BD trial in people aged 60 or older with mania, 9-week response was 79% with lithium and 73% with divalproex. [[Young2017GERIBD]]
