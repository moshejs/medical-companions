Bipolar Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on bipolar disorder and lithium therapy, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people living with bipolar disorder, their families and carers.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: BPD Companion and ADHD Companion, two neighbouring guides on conditions that are sometimes mistaken for bipolar disorder or occur alongside it, and AUD Companion, on alcohol use disorder, which often occurs alongside bipolar disorder.
