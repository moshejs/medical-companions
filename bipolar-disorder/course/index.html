Year 0 · first episode

A first mania

This illustration starts with a first hospital admission for mania. In the McLean-Harvard First-Episode Mania Study (166 people), 98% reached syndromal recovery within 2 years, half of them by 5.4 weeks. 486 Over up to 25 years of follow-up in another cohort, the median mood episode in bipolar I lasted 13 weeks, and more than 75% of people recovered from their episodes within a year of onset; episodes with a severe start, such as psychosis, took longer. 465

In plain wordsMost episodes end. For most people a first mania settles within weeks to a few months with treatment.

The conditions that come along

In the worldwide surveys, three in four people with bipolar spectrum disorder met criteria for at least one other disorder. 329 Each of these can be treated, so it is worth telling the care team about them. Choose a condition.

Comorbidity · 45% lifetime

Anxiety disorders

Across 40 studies (14,914 people), 45% of people with bipolar disorder had an anxiety disorder in their lifetime, about three times the population rate (RR 3.22), with no difference between bipolar I and II. 400 Even while mood was steady, 34.7% met criteria for a current anxiety disorder (RR 4.60 against controls). 401 Panic attacks were the most common comorbidity in the worldwide surveys. 329

Why it mattersComorbid anxiety lengthens the delay to diagnosis and is linked to suicide attempts, 260,442 so it is worth naming and treating in its own right.

More about the long-term course

Population statistic

The mortality gap

All-cause mortality in bipolar disorder is about twice that of the general population (SMR 2.05; risk ratio 2.02 in a larger 2023 meta-analysis). 46,213 Natural causes matter: cardiovascular mortality RR 1.76, respiratory 3.18, while cancer mortality was not raised. 46 Pooled life expectancy was 66.88 years, 70.51 for women and 64.59 for men. 72 In Danish registers, unaffected siblings had no shortening of life, pointing to the illness rather than shared family factors. 269 The authors conclude that preventing suicide and promoting physical health are both needed. 46 These are group averages, not a forecast for anyone.

Specifier

Rapid cycling

Rapid cycling means 4 or more mood episodes in a year. In 8 studies of consecutively admitted patients, 16.3% had it, slightly more often women and people with bipolar II. 284 It is linked to suicide attempts and poorer response to mood stabilisers. 340 With lithium, 34% of people with rapid cycling had no further recurrences against 47% without it, and partial response was common in both (59% and 65%). 284 Hypothyroidism was associated with current rapid cycling, and whether antidepressants cause it is unproven Hypothesis. 284 See specifiers.

Later life

Older age

Older adults with bipolar disorder are a growing group; late onset, physical illness and cognitive decline are the main open questions. 436 Even when mood is steady, small to moderate cognitive differences are found (effect sizes 0.26–0.63), not explained by residual symptoms or medicines. 51 Dementia is more common (odds ratio 2.96 in 10 studies), and the number of mood episodes predicted it. 505 In observational studies, lithium treatment went with lower dementia risk (OR 0.51) Early evidence. 505 In the GERI-BD trial in people aged 60 or older with mania, 9-week response was 79% with lithium and 73% with divalproex. 530

Bipolar Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on bipolar disorder and lithium therapy, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people living with bipolar disorder, their families and carers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine without your own care team. Criteria, screening tools and the lithium level explainer are for learning, not self-diagnosis or reading your own results. Doses, levels and monitoring schedules are quoted as published in labels and guidelines, never as instructions; suspected lithium toxicity is an emergency.
