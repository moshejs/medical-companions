Antipsychotics in bipolar disorder

Some of the most effective treatments for mania, and several for bipolar depression, are antipsychotic medicines. Watch the evidence build drug by drug, then see how weight, blood sugar and lipids are checked, and how the side effects differ.

Acute mania

Top of the table

Haloperidol (SMD −0.56), risperidone (−0.50) and olanzapine (−0.43) came out on top. The authors concluded that "antipsychotic drugs were significantly more effective than mood stabilisers" and that risperidone, olanzapine and haloperidol "should be considered as among the best of the available options". 82

Metabolic monitoring: what is checked, and when

Some antipsychotics raise weight, blood sugar and blood fats. Metabolic syndrome affects 20–65% of people with bipolar disorder, and weight gain contributes to "upwards of 60%" of treatment non-adherence. Before starting

A baseline

NICE CG185: before starting an antipsychotic, measure and record weight or BMI, pulse, blood pressure, fasting blood glucose or HbA1c, and blood lipid profile. 373

In plain wordsA first set of measurements gives a starting point, so any change later can be spotted early.

Side effects compared

Across 18 antipsychotics in 100 trials (in schizophrenia, median 6 weeks), weight change vs placebo ranged from −0.23 kg with haloperidol to 3.01 kg with clozapine. Olanzapine and clozapine had the worst metabolic profiles; aripiprazole, brexpiprazole, cariprazine, lurasidone and ziprasidone the most benign. Pick a medicine to see what the sources in this guide report for it. 416

Side effects

Olanzapine

  • Highest BMI change of 18 antipsychotics (1.07 kg/m²); with clozapine, the worst metabolic profile. 416
  • Maintenance trial: mean 3.1 kg gained on open-label olanzapine; another 1.0 kg in those who continued it, while those switched to placebo lost 2.0 kg. 488
  • Effective for mania (SMD −0.43) and maintenance (relapse 46.7% vs 80.1%), but CANMAT/ISBD places it second-line for maintenance "because of safety issues such as metabolic syndrome". 82,488,525
In plain wordsEvery medicine has trade-offs. Side effects differ a lot between antipsychotics, so if one is hard to live with, there are often others to discuss with the prescriber.
Not only for psychosis

Why "antipsychotic" doesn't mean psychosis

The name comes from their first use, but CANMAT/ISBD 2018 lists quetiapine, asenapine, aripiprazole, paliperidone, risperidone and cariprazine among first-line treatments for acute mania, alongside lithium and divalproex, whether or not there is psychosis. Several also treat bipolar depression. 525,528 Mania is linked to raised striatal dopamine D2/3 receptor availability, one target these medicines act on. Established as a model; mechanism Early evidence 16

Long-acting injectables

An injection instead of daily tablets

Risperidone long-acting injection prolonged time to an elevated-mood episode (p<0.001) but not a depressive one; aripiprazole once-monthly 400 mg cut 52-week recurrence to 26.5% vs 51.1% on placebo, "predominantly on manic episodes". CANMAT/ISBD 2018: if psychosocial strategies do not fix non-adherence, "long‐acting injectable medications should be offered". 67,422,525

Labels, as published: Risperdal Consta 25 mg IM every 2 weeks; Abilify Maintena 400 mg IM monthly, after tolerability is established with oral aripiprazole. 126,135

Staying well

Maintenance

In Trial 144 quetiapine prolonged time to any mood event vs placebo (HR 0.29), as did lithium (HR 0.46). In the olanzapine maintenance trial, relapse was 46.7% vs 80.1% on placebo. After an antipsychotic plus lithium or divalproex treats mania, continuing it for 6 months offered "clear benefit", but "the benefits beyond 6 months remain uncertain". 488,518,525

Boxed warnings

What the labels warn about

Every antipsychotic label read for this guide carries a boxed warning of increased mortality in elderly people with dementia-related psychosis. Those approved for depression also carry the antidepressant-class warning on suicidal thoughts and behaviours in children, adolescents and young adults, with close monitoring for worsening. 126,127,131,134,135,136,137

Part of good care

Healthy eating and activity

NICE recommends offering a combined healthy-eating and physical-activity programme, "especially those taking antipsychotics and long‑term medication", and advises against regular combined antipsychotics "except for short periods (for example, when changing medication)". 373
