Trial entry

REGAIN: who took part

REGAIN enrolled 125 adults with AChR-antibody-positive refractory generalised MG (MGFA class II to IV, MG-ADL 6 or more): 62 on eculizumab and 63 on placebo for 26 weeks, at 76 sites in 17 countries. Refractory meant failure of at least 2 immunosuppressants, or 1 plus chronic IVIg or plasma exchange, for 12 months. Meningococcal vaccination was required before the first dose. 244

In plain wordsEveryone in this trial had MG that had not settled on several older treatments. Half received the drug and half a dummy infusion.
Exam hookEculizumab binds C5, the last common step before the membrane attack complex, so the complex cannot assemble at the junction. 245

How big is the benefit?

Most pivotal trials met their primary endpoint (REGAIN's prespecified worst-rank analysis did not), yet the placebo arms improved too, and many drug-minus-placebo differences sit below the 2-point MG-ADL change that best predicted a person's own sense of improvement. RAISE

Zilucoplan: −2.09 at week 12

MG-ADL −4.39 vs −2.30, difference −2.09 (−3.24 to −0.95). A model-based meta-analysis of 9 phase 3 trials gave zilucoplan the highest MG-ADL maximum effect (net 2.5). 248,462

In plain wordsEach bar is how many more MG-ADL points the drug group improved than the placebo group, on a 24-point scale. The dashed line is the 2-point change that best matched a person feeling better.
Exam hookPlacebo arms in MG trials improve by about 1 to 3 MG-ADL points, so a drug-minus-placebo difference is usually smaller than the drug arm's own change.

One readingReal, fast and lasting benefit

  • Every placebo-controlled pivotal C5 and FcRn trial beat placebo on MG-ADL. Pooled estimates over placebo are −2.17 points (95% CI −2.67 to −1.67) in a 2023 meta-analysis and about 1.7 points across 6 AChR-positive trials in a 2026 one. 178,470
  • The 2-point MCID describes one person's change; responder analyses, which count individuals, separate more clearly (zilucoplan 73.1% vs 46.1%; ADAPT 68% vs 30%). 173,246,369
  • Onset is fast: complete C5 inhibition after the first ravulizumab infusion, and, in one network meta-analysis, efgartigimod, zilucoplan and rozanolixizumab already ahead of placebo at week 1. 540,573
  • Extensions show durable control: eculizumab exacerbations fell 75.2%, ravulizumab held −4.0 to week 164, and complement-inhibitor extensions showed steroid sparing to 156 weeks. 178,370,541

Another readingModest gains over a strong placebo

  • Placebo arms improve by about 1 to 3 points, and several group differences (ravulizumab −1.6, gefurulimab −1.6, nipocalimab −1.45, satralizumab −1.02) sit below 2 points. 21,218,221,369
  • REGAIN missed its prespecified primary analysis (p=0.0698); its approval is cited as an example of how the choice of analysis shapes a trial's reading. 165,244
  • Benefit wanes once IgG recovers: 4 weeks after the last dose no biologic beat placebo on QMG in one network meta-analysis. 573
  • Extensions have no placebo, a key comparison is sponsor-funded and depends on timepoint, and zilucoplan's only week-24 comparison is a sponsor model, because RAISE had no placebo data after week 12. 99,530
  • A published model put eculizumab at $3,338,000 and efgartigimod at $1,987,000 per QALY, far above usual thresholds. 319

Newer agents

Phase 3 · approved in the US

Inebilizumab (MINT)

An anti-CD19 antibody that depletes B cells. In 238 people (190 AChR+, 48 MuSK+), with a mandatory steroid taper, MG-ADL improved by −1.9 versus placebo at week 26. The FDA approved it for AChR+ or MuSK+ adults in December 2025. 17,398

Phase 3 · development stopped

Satralizumab (LUMINESCE)

An anti-IL-6-receptor antibody. The week-24 MG-ADL difference was −1.02, which the authors called small; the sponsor stopped development and ended the extension. 221

Phase 3 · under FDA review

Cemdisiran ± pozelimab (NIMBLE)

Cemdisiran is an siRNA that silences C5 production, injected every 12 weeks; pozelimab is a C5 antibody. Week-24 differences: −2.3 alone, −1.7 combined. FDA and EMA accepted applications in June 2026 (press). 388,543

Phase 3 (China) · not approved

Batoclimab

An FcRn blocker: day-43 difference −1.9 and sustained responders 58.2% vs 31.3%. In phase 2a, albumin fell by about 12 g/L and cholesterol rose, reversing after stopping. 397,559

Phase 3 · EU opinion

Gefurulimab (PREVAIL)

A C5 and albumin binding nanobody given weekly under the skin: week-26 difference −1.6, with a CHMP positive opinion in September 2026. 116,218

Mixed evidence

Rituximab and tocilizumab

In BeatMG (mild to moderate AChR+ gMG), rituximab missed its steroid-sparing goal: 60% vs 56%, a futility result. In a network meta-analysis it did not significantly improve MG-ADL: −0.92 (95% CI −2.24 to 0.39). In new-onset generalized MG, RINOMAX found the opposite: minimal manifestations at 16 weeks in 71% vs 29%. 396,425,470

Tocilizumab has case reports and one non-randomised prospective cohort in refractory AChR+ gMG (20 treated), with no randomised MG trial. 275,463

Mechanism, safety and comparisons

Established mechanism

Why block C5

In AChR-antibody MG, IgG1 and IgG3 bound to receptors recruit C1q and start the classical complement pathway, ending in C5b-9, the membrane attack complex, which destroys the junctional folds. MAC was found at every endplate in all 30 patients of one biopsy study. C5 is the last common step before that complex, and blocking it leaves upstream C3 coating intact. 6,245,380

MuSK antibodies are mainly IgG4, which cannot activate complement; the C5 inhibitor trials and labels in our sources cover AChR-positive disease. 244,248,259,539

In plain wordsOne group of drugs stops the damaging protein cascade at its last step, so it cannot punch holes in the muscle side of the junction.
Established mechanism

Why block FcRn

FcRn rescues IgG from breakdown: in the acidic endosome it binds IgG and returns it to the blood, while unbound IgG goes to the lysosome. That is why IgG and albumin last about 19 to 21 days in the blood, against at most 5 to 7 days for IgA. Block FcRn and IgG is degraded faster. In healthy volunteers, repeated efgartigimod lowered IgG by an average of 75% without changing albumin or other immunoglobulins, and IgG recovered about 8 weeks after the last dose. 435,460,524

In plain wordsThe other group makes the body clear its antibodies faster, the harmful ones included. When the drug stops, antibody levels climb back.
Exam hookC5 inhibitors stop the membrane attack complex (AChR+ disease) and carry a boxed warning for meningococcal infection, so vaccination comes first. FcRn blockers lower all IgG by stopping its recycling, and need no meningococcal vaccine.
Label information

Meningococcal risk, vaccination and REMS

  • Soliris, Ultomiris and Zilbrysq carry a boxed warning for serious meningococcal infection. The labels call for vaccination against serogroups A, C, W, Y and B at least 2 weeks before the first dose, antibacterial prophylaxis if treatment cannot wait, and access only through a REMS. 165,167,173
  • Vaccination does not eliminate the risk: fatal infections have occurred in vaccinated patients. CDC surveillance linked eculizumab with a 1,000- to 2,000-fold higher incidence; 14 of 16 US cases had at least 1 vaccine dose, and 11 were non-groupable strains. 165,343
  • The patient safety card is carried until 3 months after the last eculizumab dose, 8 months after ravulizumab and 2 months after zilucoplan; it lists fever, headache with stiff neck, rash, light sensitivity and confusion as reasons for immediate evaluation. 165,167,173
  • For prophylaxis in penicillin allergy, the guidance notes that macrolides and fluoroquinolones can worsen MG. 381

In US claims data only 28% of eculizumab recipients had a serogroup B dose. 53

Separately from infection, new trouble breathing or swallowing in a person with MG can signal a crisis and is treated as an emergency: see Myasthenic crisis. 353

Label information

How they are given

  • Eculizumab: IV over 35 minutes in adults, with at least 1 hour of monitoring afterwards. 165
  • Efgartigimod IV: 10 mg/kg over 1 hour weekly for 4 weeks. The SC syringe takes 20 to 30 seconds and can be self-injected. 171,172
  • Rozanolixizumab: SC by infusion pump at up to 20 mL/h, weekly for 6 weeks. 163
  • FcRn labels advise against live vaccines during treatment and an immunisation review before each cycle. 144,163,171

Published label dosing, quoted for education. Doses and schedules for any one person are set by the treating clinician.

Mixed evidence

Comparing drugs across trials

No head-to-head trials exist, and indirect comparisons disagree. One model-based meta-analysis found FcRn agents ahead of C5 agents on QMG (posterior probability 99.7%) but not on MG-ADL (52.5%); another network analysis ranked batoclimab first on QMG with very wide intervals; a third found only efgartigimod, zilucoplan and rozanolixizumab ahead of placebo at week 1. A 2023 network meta-analysis found no complement-versus-FcRn difference on MG-ADL (p=0.16) but a larger QMG effect with FcRn agents. Designs differ: cyclic trials peak at weeks 4 to 6, continuous ones report weeks 12 to 26. 330,462,470,573

Guidelines disagree

Cost and where guidelines place them

An ICER review judged that eculizumab and efgartigimod both give a net health benefit over conventional therapy, but a published model put them at $3,338,000 and $1,987,000 per QALY. 319,514

Guidelines place these drugs at different steps. The 2020 international consensus update says eculizumab can be considered in severe, refractory AChR+ gMG, generally after other immunotherapies fail; it predates the FcRn approvals. The German guideline (2023) recommends C5 or FcRn inhibitors in highly active AChR+ disease, defined by activity criteria rather than by treatment failure. The Nordic guidance (2024) lists both classes as second-line options for severe, difficult-to-treat MG, depending on approval and cost, and the 2025 ABN update describes their role as still being established. 200,268,381,549

A reminder. Labels, approved populations and access rules differ between countries and change over time. The figures here are for understanding the evidence, not for choosing a treatment; that choice belongs with the treating clinician.
