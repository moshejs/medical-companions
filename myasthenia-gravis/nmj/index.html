The receptor: five subunits, two keys

The muscle receptor is a ring of five subunits: α2βδε in adults, α2βγδ before birth. The γ-to-ε switch changes how the channel conducts and gates. 364,494

The two ACh sites sit at the α–δ interface and at the α–ε (adult) or α–γ (fetal) interface. They are not equal: ACh leaves one about 100 times faster than the other. 494 The main immunogenic region, the target of half or more of MG antibodies, sits at the top of α1. 328,494 Antibodies at the junction picks up from there.

Established

Crests and troughs

The folds split the membrane into two domains. Crests carry AChR with rapsyn (the 43 kDa protein); troughs carry Na⁺ channels with ankyrin. 180,497

Rapsyn is needed for clustering itself: rapsyn-deficient mice form no AChR clusters. 190 Human junctions are small, fragmented into coin-shaped patches, and stable from age 34 to 92. 274

Established

The safety factor

SF = EPP ÷ E_AP, where E_AP is the step from resting potential to threshold. Trough Na⁺ channels raise the safety factor by lowering that threshold. 467

Frogs get their margin by releasing a lot of transmitter. Humans release little and rely on elaborate postsynaptic folds that amplify each quantum. 497,555

How big is it normally? 2–4 times what is needed (Slater), 3–5 in adult mammals (Wood & Slater), 4–5 times (Kouyoumdjian). 301,497,555
Measured in people 2.98 in control intercostal muscle, 1.09 in MG. 467

The clean-up crew

Acetylcholinesterase (AChE) is held in the basal lamina by a collagen tail, ColQ. Mice without ColQ have no asymmetric AChE at the junction at all. 176

With AChE working, each quantum acts on its own patch of membrane. When the enzyme is inhibited, quanta overlap and add up, and the synaptic current lasts longer. 226 That is the idea behind pyridostigmine, a cholinesterase inhibitor. 150 Too much ACh action has a cost: ColQ mutations prolong it and damage the postsynaptic membrane. 497

The nerve turns up the volume

In MG muscle and in rats with experimental MG, quantal content was raised over control. The smaller an endplate's mEPPs, the larger its increase: a muscle-to-nerve compensation. 427 The signals behind it are not fully known. 428

In mice given MuSK antibodies this compensation did not happen. 294

Established

Rundown during a train

At 10–100 Hz, quantal content and EPP fall as the ready-to-release vesicle store is used up; healthy EPPs stay above threshold. 301,497 After 1–2 seconds a second store refills the first, giving the "U" shape of the decrement. 301

When the margin is thin, rundown pushes fibres below threshold one by one. That is the decrement on repetitive nerve stimulation and the weakness that grows with use in Weakness over a day.

Exam hookIn MG the endplate potential still forms but sits close to threshold: the safety factor fell from 2.98 to 1.09 in human muscle. 467 Two things shrink it: fewer AChRs make a smaller EPP, and loss of trough Na⁺ channels raises the threshold. With rundown during repeated firing, some fibres fail, which is the decrement and the fatigable weakness. MG is in the end the failure of the EPP to reach threshold. 494
In plain wordsThe nerve throws handfuls of a chemical messenger across a tiny gap. Catchers on the muscle side pick it up, and when enough are caught the muscle fires. A healthy junction catches about three times more than it needs. In MG there are fewer catchers, so the muscle only just fires, and after a few rapid signals it may not fire at all.
