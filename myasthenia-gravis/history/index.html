Clinic & names

1672 · Thomas Willis

Thomas Willis gave the first recognisable account, in Latin, of patients who were well in the morning and fatigued by noon, including a woman whose speech failed after long talking. It went largely unnoticed until 1903. 100,257

In plain wordsMore than 350 years ago a doctor noticed people who were strong in the morning and weak by midday.

In plain wordsDoctors described MG for more than two centuries before anyone knew it was the immune system attacking the muscle's receptors. The first useful drug came in 1934, surgery on the thymus soon after, and breathing machines in intensive care made it far less deadly. Since 2017 new targeted drugs have been approved in most years.

Names it has carried

For a time after Goldflam's 1893 review the disease was called the Erb-Goldflam syndrome. Jolly's 1895 name, "myasthenia gravis pseudoparalytica", was later shortened to the one used today. 100,257

1936 or 1939? The Blalock date

Blalock removed a thymic tumour from a patient with MG in 1936 and published it in 1939, so timelines disagree by three years about the same operation. His planned-thymectomy series in patients without thymoma followed in 1941. German reviews credit Sauerbruch's 1911 thymectomy, done for thyrotoxicosis in a young woman with MG, as coming first. 49,50,100,413,495

Three experiments that proved it

  • 1973 Rabbits immunised with eel AChR became weak: the receptor is the target. 418
  • 1973 MG junctions carry fewer receptors. 131
  • 1975 Patient IgG injected into mice reproduces the defect: the antibody is enough. 519
  • 1976 The antibody test: positive in 87% of 71 patients, 0 of 175 controls. 322

From often fatal to near-normal life expectancy

In 1934, 70% of the few people diagnosed died of respiratory failure or pneumonia within two years. 64 Deaths per crisis fell from 42% in the early 1960s to 6% in the late 1970s, with ventilation and intensive care. 84,100 Disease-specific mortality is now under 5% with nearly normal life expectancy. 276 Older cohorts still fare worse: 12% of ventilated crises in Germany, 2006–2015 (median age 72). 384

Cell therapy Early evidence

The first person with MG treated with CAR-T cells was reported in 2023. By 2026 small series reported remission off immunotherapy for at least 19 months, and a 26-person randomised trial of an mRNA CAR-T met its primary endpoint. Larger trials are still running. 223,230,542 Toward a cure
