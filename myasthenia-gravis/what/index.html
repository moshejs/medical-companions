Part 1 · The disease

What myasthenia gravis is

Myasthenia gravis (MG) is an autoimmune disease of the nerve-muscle junction: muscles tire with use and recover with rest. Press play to watch weakness spread from the eyes to the breathing muscles while the age-of-onset charts fill in, then switch between ocular and generalized disease and the onset groups.

In MG, antibodies attack proteins on the muscle side of the neuromuscular junction: the acetylcholine receptor (AChR), muscle-specific kinase (MuSK) or related proteins. Weakness, local or general, is the main symptom. Patients are grouped by antibody, by which muscles are affected, by age at onset and by thymus pathology. 198 The nerves and the muscles themselves are healthy, which is why sensation, reflexes and pupils stay normal. 276,367

Distribution
Onset group
Early onset means onset before 50 107. Before 40, women outnumber men about 3:1 107 (7:3 in another review 276). Thymic hyperplasia, seen in about 65% of MG overall 276, mainly affects women of childbearing age (9:1) 64. In people of European descent, early onset is linked to the HLA A1-B8-DR3 haplotype 107. In an Italian cohort, 24% of early-onset AChR patients reached complete stable remission 130.

First signs · the eyes

Drooping lids and double vision

Ocular weakness, with asymmetric ptosis (a drooping upper lid) and double vision that goes away when one eye is covered, is the most common first presentation 276. About two-thirds of patients start with eye symptoms, and the lid or eye muscles are involved at some point in nearly all 128. In a Japanese national survey of AChR-positive patients without thymoma, ptosis was a first symptom in 74.9% and double vision in 47.4% 567.

In plain wordsFor most people the first sign is a drooping eyelid or seeing double, often worse in the evening.
Exam hookFatigable, fluctuating weakness of skeletal muscle with normal sensation and reflexes; the eyes are the commonest first sign. 276,367

Which antibody? The subtypes

Most people with MG have antibodies against the acetylcholine receptor. A minority have MuSK antibodies, a few have LRP4 antibodies, and some have none that standard tests find. Thymoma is not an antibody type: it overlaps the AChR group. Tap a subtype.

Rounded to 100 people: AChR about 80% 107,423, MuSK 7-10% 107, and the remaining 12 squares double seronegative; published estimates for that group are 6-10% 564, so rounding makes it look a little larger here. LRP4 squares are derived (18.7% of the double-seronegative group 577), so they are drawn open. Estimates vary by country and assay.

Subtype

AChR antibodies

about 80%

AChR antibodies are found in about 80% of all people with MG 107,423 and about 85% of generalized MG 367, but in only about half of ocular MG 461. The first radioimmunoassay series (1976) found them in 87% of 71 patients and in none of 175 controls 322. How they damage the junction is animated in Antibodies at the junction.

How common? The estimates disagree

Pooled studies do not agree on how common MG is. Why the numbers rise

Prevalence was about 1 in 200,000 before 1934, about 1 in 20,000 after the first effective drugs and about 1 in 17,000 after AChR antibodies were found 64,107. Patient numbers have more than doubled in 20 years, mainly through rising incidence in older people; better diagnosis, treatment and survival are the likely reasons 64. Pooled frequency also rose with study year and quality, read as better case-finding 70.

Course and prognosis, then and now

Older figures come from an era before immunotherapy and intensive care. Modern figures are lower, for conversion from eye-only to generalized disease and for deaths. The measures differ from study to study, so read each bar on its own.

The natural course

Amsterdam, 1926-1965

22% complete remission, 18% much improved, 16% moderately improved, 16% unchanged, 29% died; 2 patients were worse. 73 patients treated mostly with anticholinesterases only, outcome in 1985; thymoma did worse 409.

Worst earlyfirst 1-2 years

The most severe weakness and the highest mortality came in the first 1-2 years in 1,976 patients followed 1940-2000 210. Almost two-thirds reach maximum severity within the first year 276.

Remission24% vs 4%

complete stable remission in early-onset vs very-late-onset Italian AChR patients (onset 2000-2020); minimal manifestations or better on 5 mg/day prednisone or less in 60% and 48% 130. Before steroids about a quarter remitted spontaneously, for 4.6 years on average 107. Guidelines set remission or minimal manifestations as the goal 198,491.

Mortality today

Disease-specific mortality is under 5% with near-normal life expectancy 276; other reviews give 5-9% 64,107. Before immunomodulators it was about 30% 276. A single Israeli tertiary centre found 14% mortality at 5 years and 21% at 10, above matched controls, mainly from infections linked to chronic steroid use 291.

Crisis15-20%

lifetime risk in AChR MG 107,367 (about 10% per MedlinePlus 345). In 73 crises, median time from onset to first crisis was 8 months and mortality 4% per episode 512. Difficulty breathing or swallowing is an emergency. See Myasthenic crisis.

Living with it

Fatigue, as distinct from muscle fatigability, affects about 60% of patients 69. Pooled prevalence was 36% for depression and 33% for anxiety 378. Quality of life is lower in generalized than ocular MG and falls with severity 341. See Living with MG.

Other autoimmune disease and family

Another autoimmune disease13%

of people with MG, in a review of 25 studies; autoimmune thyroid disease was the most common, and coexisting autoimmunity was more frequent in women and AChR-positive patients 335. The natural-history cohort found 25% 409.

Thyroid firstabout 10%

have autoimmune thyroiditis 64. Next come lupus and rheumatoid arthritis 377. Thyroid disease can also worsen MG 276, and a 2021 diagnostic review advises thyroid function tests in all definite or probable MG 461.

Families5.6%

of 1,032 North American AChR-positive patients reported a relative with MG; 26.6% of patients and 28.4% of their relatives had an autoimmune disease 209. In an Israeli cohort 5.7% had familial MG, which started younger (median 44.5 vs 58 years) 234. Identical twins are concordant in about 35.5%, non-identical twins in 4-5% 441.

HLA

Early-onset MG in people of European descent is linked to the A1-B8-DR3 haplotype (HLA-B*08:01); late onset to other markers such as HLA-DRB1*15:01 in Norwegian patients over 60; MuSK MG to HLA-DQB1*05 and DRB1*14/*16. Thymoma MG shows no consistent HLA link 64,107.

Checkpoint-inhibitor MG

Cancer drugs that release immune brakes can trigger MG: 0.12% of nivolumab-treated Japanese patients 509. In 65 reported cases onset came a median 4 weeks after starting, often with myositis, and 38% died 471.

The clue: what stays normal

SensationNormal. Sensory nerves are not involved. 128,367
ReflexesDeep tendon reflexes are normal. 367
PupilsNormal, even when the lids droop and the eyes cannot move together. 276
PainThe weakness is painless. 276
Exam hookFluctuating, fatigable weakness of eye, bulbar, limb or breathing muscles, with normal sensation, reflexes and pupils, points to the neuromuscular junction. Abnormal pupils point to a mimic such as botulism (dilated pupils in 37% of confirmed cases) 442; lost reflexes with a dry mouth point to Lambert-Eaton syndrome 290,516. See The differential.
In plain wordsThe nerves and muscles are healthy. The message between them is weakened, so muscles tire quickly with use and recover with rest. Feeling, reflexes and the pupils are untouched.
