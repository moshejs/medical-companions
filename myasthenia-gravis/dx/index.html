Step 1 · Suspicion · generalized

The clinical picture

Diagnosis is straightforward in most people with typical symptoms and a positive antibody test. 198 The work-up starts with an examination for fatigable weakness: ocular, bulbar and limb-girdle. 220 At the bedside, ptosis that worsens on sustained upgaze shows the lid muscle tiring, and ice held over the eye for 2 to 5 minutes counts as positive with 2 mm or more of improvement, a cut-off Rousseff calls "somewhat arbitrary". 461,564 In generalized MG the weakness also reaches bulbar, neck or limb muscles. The ice pack test was studied in people with ptosis, so its figures belong to the ocular setting.

In plain wordsIt starts with what the person notices and what the examiner sees: muscles that tire with use and recover with rest.
Exam hookFatigability is the clue: ptosis that worsens on sustained upgaze and improves with ice or rest.
Exam hookAChR antibodies are found in about 85% of generalized but only about half of ocular MG. Single-fibre EMG is the most sensitive test and a positive AChR antibody the most specific. RNS is read as abnormal with a decrement of more than 10% from the 1st to the 4th or 5th response at 2 to 5 Hz, though one review says that cut-off has never been validated. 1,461,564
In plain wordsIn the published pathways, a blood test for the antibodies comes first. If it is negative, nerve tests that measure how the muscle tires can show the problem. Reviews advise a chest scan for everyone with MG, to look at the thymus gland.

Before and after the result

Sensitivity 85% finds 26 of the 31 with MG and misses 5. Specificity 95% clears 66 of the 69 without MG, but 3 test positive anyway. So after a positive result the probability of MG is 88%, and after a negative result 6.6%.

In plain wordsA positive result means more when MG was already likely. A negative result rules MG out best when the test rarely misses it.
Exam hookSensitivity and specificity belong to the test; the post-test probability also depends on who is tested. A normal concentric-needle jitter study in a weak muscle had a negative predictive value of about 98%. 461

Bedside tests

Established

Ice pack test

Ice over the drooping eye for 2–5 minutes; 2 mm or more of improvement counts as positive. In 155 consecutive ptosis referrals, sensitivity was 86% and specificity 79%. A systematic review of 20 studies found sensitivity from 38.5% to 100%, with no adverse effects reported. Rousseff calls the 2 mm cut-off "somewhat arbitrary", since heat changed the lid in some patients too. 194,431,461

Ice, rest or heat

Mean ptosis improvement was 2.3 mm with ice, 1.3 mm with rest and 0.33 mm with heat. In one study ice alone was 28% sensitive and 100% specific, against 73% and 97% when combined with 2 minutes of sustained upgaze. 564

Sleep test

Ptosis or eye-movement weakness resolves right after a 30-minute sleep and comes back over the next 30 seconds to 5 minutes. Its authors called it safe and "moderately sensitive, and specific", without numbers in the abstract. 403

Lid signs

  • Cogan's lid twitch: sensitivity 50–99% and specificity 75–100% across 7 studies. 426
  • Forced eyelid closure test: 94% sensitivity, 91% specificity in 48 patients. 22
  • Enhanced ptosis (the curtain sign) is described clinically, but no diagnostic-accuracy study was found, so no figure is given here.
Established

Edrophonium: why it is rarely used now

Edrophonium (Tensilon) works within 30 seconds and lasts about 5 minutes. Serious adverse events occur in under 0.2% but include slowing of the heart to asystole, bronchospasm and seizures, and the test may worsen MuSK-MG. Since 2018 it has not been marketed in the US as a test for MG; reviews cite false positives and cardiac risk, and it has not been used in the UK in recent years. 461,564

Edrophonium and neostigmine numbers

A 2026 Vienna study (182 MG, 324 controls, 1991–2024) found edrophonium sensitivity 83.5% and specificity 87.7%, lower odds of a positive test in MuSK-MG, and adverse events in 11.5%, mostly mild. Intramuscular neostigmine starts in 5–15 minutes and lasts hours; one 47-patient study gave 83% sensitivity and 97% specificity. 289,564

When the tests mislead

False-positive antibodies

  • Unexplained positive AChR binding results in about 5% of people with Lambert-Eaton or ALS; 2 of 22 ALS patients in the Mayo series, against 0 of 427 with other neurological disease. 243,365
  • Recent general anaesthesia with muscle relaxants gave false positives in the blocking and modulating assays. 243
  • LRP4 antibodies in up to 23% of ALS. 461

Positive, but not (yet) MG

  • AChR antibodies can be positive in thymoma without clinical MG; up to a third of people with thymoma develop MG. 461
  • Some people have raised titres years before symptoms, found in stored blood, which may explain some apparent false positives. 461
  • Two of 8 chronic mimics treated as refractory MG for years were AChR-antibody positive. 225

Misdiagnosis, in numbers

8 of 61

people reviewed once single-fibre EMG became available locally had been wrongly diagnosed and treated as refractory MG, for a mean of 9 years; two had had a thymectomy. Of 80 people labelled double-seronegative MG, 36% had another diagnosis, most often a functional disorder (45%), then OPMD. 225,331

When every test is negative

If all tests are negative but there is clearly fluctuating, asymmetric ptosis and double vision, ocular MG is "probable", with observation and retesting. People negative on all objective tests yet still symptomatic are unlikely to have MG; such people, undiagnosed after 2 years at a specialist clinic, may be over 10% of referrals. 461 See The differential.

Jitter is not specific

Increased jitter also occurs in other junction disorders, neuropathies, some myopathies and especially motor neuron disease. Botulinum toxin raises jitter even in distant muscles for 6 months or more, and Rousseff writes that cosmetic or therapeutic toxin use must be excluded. 461,477

Severity is not the titre

High jitter (over 100 µs) and a decrement over 10% go with more severe disease, while the serum AChR antibody level does not track clinical severity. 564
