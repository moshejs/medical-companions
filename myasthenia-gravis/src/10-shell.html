Myasthenia gravis, explained

In myasthenia gravis the nerves and the muscles are healthy, but the message between them is under attack. Here it is in moving pictures: one nerve impulse in slow motion, the antibodies that block it, why weakness grows through the day, how each treatment works, and what to watch for in a crisis.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.
