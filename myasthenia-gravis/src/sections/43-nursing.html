Nursing care

A 24-hour ward shift in myasthenia gravis: pyridostigmine timed to meals, breath counts and swallow checks, and when to escalate. Press play, or switch the scenario.

ScopeThis page reports what published studies, drug labels, NHS safety documents and patient charities say. It is not a care plan, and it does not replace local protocols, your scope of practice or the prescriber. Doses and schedules are set by the treating clinician. Where the literature has no answer, the page says so.
EmergencyNew or worsening difficulty breathing, swallowing or speaking in a person with MG is an emergency that needs urgent medical help. The pyridostigmine teaching literature tells patients to contact the prescriber urgently for exactly these signs, and the MGFA emergency alert card warns first responders that breathing or swallowing difficulty may signal a crisis.
