The differential

How Lambert-Eaton, botulism, ALS, eye-muscle disease, brainstem lesions, Miller Fisher, congenital and drug-induced myasthenia are told apart from MG. Press play to sweep the features column by column, ending with the nerve test that separates MG from Lambert-Eaton; pick a condition to compare it with MG.

MG weakens muscles that are moved on purpose and spares the pupils, the reflexes and sensation. A finding outside that pattern points away from MG: a dilated pupil, absent reflexes, a dry mouth, numbness, weakness since birth, or a muscle response that jumps after 10 seconds of effort. Exam hookLambert-Eaton: proximal leg weakness, dry mouth and areflexia; a low first CMAP that rises 60% or more (classically 100%) after 10 s of exercise or 50 Hz stimulation; P/Q-type VGCC antibodies in 91–100%; small-cell lung cancer in about half. MG: normal first CMAP, low-rate decrement of 10% or more, normal reflexes and pupils. [[Kesner2018,Oh2005,Hatanaka2008,Lennon1995,Titulaer2011,Oh2017,AAEM2001,Juel2007,MSDManual2026]]
In plain wordsSeveral other conditions also cause drooping lids or tiring muscles. MG leaves the pupils, the reflexes and feeling alone, and comes and goes over the day. Reflexes, pupils, how the illness started, and a nerve test that compares the muscle's response before and after a short effort help tell them apart.

Lambert-Eaton: the tumour score

The DELTA-P score (Dutch-English LEMS Tumour Association Prediction) gives one point for each item present at or within 3 months of onset. It was built to predict small-cell lung cancer in people with LEMS. It is a published research score, shown here to illustrate the study's numbers; it is not a diagnosis. [[Titulaer2011b]]

Look-alikes in brief

Lambert-Eaton (LEMS)

A presynaptic disease: antibodies against P/Q-type voltage-gated calcium channels cut acetylcholine release. Found in 100% of 32 LEMS with cancer and 91% of 33 without; N-type antibodies in 49%. Low titres also appear in 23% of sporadic ALS and 24% of people with cancer but no neurological disease. [[Lennon1995]]

About half have small-cell lung cancer. After exercise, the increment was largest with 5–10 s of effort (about 243–244%) and smallest with 30 s (84%). [[Titulaer2011,Hatanaka2008]]

Botulism

Cranial nerve palsies, then a symmetric descending flaccid paralysis in an alert, afebrile person. Ptosis 81%, diplopia 75%, dysphagia 85%, dilated pupils 37%. 87% of those intubated were intubated on hospital day 1 or 2. Asymmetry (6–15%) does not exclude it. [[Rao2021,ChathamStephens2017]]

Thyroid eye disease, alone or with MG

Proptosis, pain and restricted, often asymmetric eye movements with enlarged muscles on imaging. The two can coexist: 0.23% of 6,576 TED patients also had MG, where ptosis and exotropia were the clues. When both are present, one positive test may not be enough. [[Keene2022,Abumanhal2026,Claytor2021]]

Drug- and checkpoint-inhibitor MG

D-penicillamine induces true AChR-antibody MG in up to 7%, mostly mild and ocular. Checkpoint inhibitors cause MG in about 0.5%, mostly with PD-1/PD-L1 blockade, a median 4 weeks after the first dose, often with myositis or myocarditis; mortality 20–30%. [[Krenn2020,Safa2019]] Drugs that can worsen MG

Mimics that were treated as MG

In one series, 8 of 61 patients had been wrongly treated as refractory MG for a mean of 9 years; 2 had a thymectomy. Of 80 people first labelled double-seronegative MG, 36% had another diagnosis, most often a functional disorder (45%), then OPMD. [[Harrison2023,Maccora2025]]

Magnesium: a chemical look-alike

A high blood magnesium produces a LEMS-like presynaptic picture that tends to spare the eye muscles. [[Krenn2020]]

Congenital myasthenic syndromes: one drug, opposite effects

Congenital myasthenic syndromes (CMS) are inherited, not autoimmune: 32 genes are known (8 presynaptic, 4 synaptic, 15 postsynaptic, 5 glycosylation). Most respond to acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, 3,4-DAP, salbutamol, ephedrine or fluoxetine, but which drug helps depends on the gene. [[Finsterer2019]]
