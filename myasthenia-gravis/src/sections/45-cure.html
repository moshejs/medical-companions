Toward a cure

No therapy has yet been shown to cure myasthenia gravis reliably. This module follows the immune chain that makes the harmful antibodies and shows where each research route cuts it, with an evidence label. Press play to move the cut along the chain, or pick a route.

Corrections and unverified reports.

  • No riliprubart MG trial. The C1s inhibitor riliprubart has data in cold agglutinin disease and phase 3 programmes in CIDP, but no myasthenia gravis trial was found in PubMed or ClinicalTrials.gov on 20 October 2026. The C1s route in MG is an extrapolation, not an MG programme. [[DSa2024]]
  • MuSK-CAART has no posted results. Its phase 1 (7 enrolled) is listed as completed, with no results on the registry and no peer-reviewed report. [[CTMuSKCAART]]

The press releases on the first CAR-T-treated MG patient give different ages (33 and 34). [[NeurologyLiveKYV2026,MGNewsTelitacicept2025,EMJTelitacicept2025,KyvernaPR2023,UMMagdeburg2023]]

What would count as a cure

Definitions

Remission has a precise meaning

The MGFA research standards define post-intervention categories including complete stable remission, pharmacologic remission and minimal manifestations. [[Jaretzki2000]] The 2020 consensus sets the treatment goal as minimal manifestation status or better, with no more than mild medication side effects. Minimal manifestation status means no symptoms or functional limits but some weakness on examination; remission means no symptoms or signs, and excludes people taking daily cholinesterase inhibitors that help. [[Narayanaswami2021]]

A 2026 review proposes that a cure would be complete remission without ongoing treatment, with a high chance of success, low relapse risk, repeatability after relapse, proportionate toxicity and cost-effectiveness. [[ElWahsh2026]]

Under conventional care, lasting drug-free remission is rare: in 421 people followed about 10 years, 12.2% after thymectomy and 4.3% without reached complete stable remission with all treatment stopped for at least a year. [[Zhang2026]]

Exam hookClinical remission is not the same as serological cure: after anti-CD19 CAR-T and after autologous transplant, people were symptom-free while AChR antibodies stayed detectable. [[Hegelmaier2026,Schlatter2023]]
