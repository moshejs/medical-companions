How big is the benefit?

Most pivotal trials met their primary endpoint (REGAIN's prespecified worst-rank analysis did not), yet the placebo arms improved too, and many drug-minus-placebo differences sit below the 2-point MG-ADL change that best predicted a person's own sense of improvement. Pick a view, then a row, to see what the number is and how it is read. Specialists disagree on how to weigh this, so both readings are shown below.
