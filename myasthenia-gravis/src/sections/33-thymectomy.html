Thymectomy over the years

Thymectomy removes the thymus, a gland behind the breastbone. Follow the MGTX trial month by month: muscle scores, prednisone doses and remission over 3 and 5 years. Press play, switch the gland view or the surgical approach, and extend the timeline to 5 years.

Published trial and guidance information only. Everything here reports what the MGTX trial, its extension and the consensus guidance published. Whether surgery suits a particular person, and when, is decided by the person with their neurologist and thoracic surgeon.

Who the guidance says it is for

The strongest evidence comes from one randomised trial in a narrow group: adults aged 18 to 65 with generalized, AChR-antibody-positive MG without a thymoma, within 5 years of onset. Outside that group, guidance rests on cohort studies and expert consensus. [[Wolfe2016,Narayanaswami2021]]
