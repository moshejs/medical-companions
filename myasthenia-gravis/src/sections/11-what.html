Part 1 · The disease

What myasthenia gravis is

Myasthenia gravis (MG) is an autoimmune disease of the nerve-muscle junction: muscles tire with use and recover with rest. Press play to watch weakness spread from the eyes to the breathing muscles while the age-of-onset charts fill in, then switch between ocular and generalized disease and the onset groups.

In MG, antibodies attack proteins on the muscle side of the neuromuscular junction: the acetylcholine receptor (AChR), muscle-specific kinase (MuSK) or related proteins. Weakness, local or general, is the main symptom. Patients are grouped by antibody, by which muscles are affected, by age at onset and by thymus pathology. [[Gilhus2019]] The nerves and the muscles themselves are healthy, which is why sensation, reflexes and pupils stay normal. [[Juel2007,MSDManual2026]]

Which antibody? The subtypes

Most people with MG have antibodies against the acetylcholine receptor. A minority have MuSK antibodies, a few have LRP4 antibodies, and some have none that standard tests find. Thymoma is not an antibody type: it overlaps the AChR group. Tap a subtype.

Rounded to 100 people: AChR about 80% [[Dresser2021,Pevzner2012]], MuSK 7-10% [[Dresser2021]], and the remaining 12 squares double seronegative; published estimates for that group are 6-10% [[Yoganathan2022]], so rounding makes it look a little larger here. LRP4 squares are derived (18.7% of the double-seronegative group [[Zisimopoulou2014]]), so they are drawn open. Estimates vary by country and assay.

How common? The estimates disagree

Pooled studies do not agree on how common MG is. Their prevalence estimates differ by more than twofold, mostly because of how a case is defined (insurance claims, laboratory results or clinical records) and when the studies were done. [[Carr2010,Sciancalepore2025,Shelly2025]] The chart shows each estimate with its own interval, not one blended number.

Filled dots are published point estimates; thick bars are 95% confidence intervals; thin lines are the range of the studies pooled; pale bars are ranges quoted by reviews. Per-100,000 figures are converted to per million. [[Carr2010,Salari2021,Sciancalepore2025,Dresser2021,MedlinePlusMG2026,Shelly2025,Lotan2020]]

Course and prognosis, then and now

Older figures come from an era before immunotherapy and intensive care. Modern figures are lower, for conversion from eye-only to generalized disease and for deaths. The measures differ from study to study, so read each bar on its own.

All values are published. Each bar or dot is a different measure from a different study, so they are not one series. [[Dresser2021,Juel2007,Hendricks2019,Kupersmith2003,Bubuioc2021,Deymeer2021,Alshekhlee2009]]

The clue: what stays normal

SensationNormal. Sensory nerves are not involved. [[MSDManual2026,Estephan2022]]
ReflexesDeep tendon reflexes are normal. [[MSDManual2026]]
PupilsNormal, even when the lids droop and the eyes cannot move together. [[Juel2007]]
PainThe weakness is painless. [[Juel2007]]
Exam hookFluctuating, fatigable weakness of eye, bulbar, limb or breathing muscles, with normal sensation, reflexes and pupils, points to the neuromuscular junction. Abnormal pupils point to a mimic such as botulism (dilated pupils in 37% of confirmed cases) [[Rao2021]]; lost reflexes with a dry mouth point to Lambert-Eaton syndrome [[Kesner2018,Titulaer2008]]. See The differential.
In plain wordsThe nerves and muscles are healthy. The message between them is weakened, so muscles tire quickly with use and recover with rest. Feeling, reflexes and the pupils are untouched.
