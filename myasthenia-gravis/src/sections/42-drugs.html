Drugs that can worsen MG

Some medicines can weaken the nerve-to-muscle signal or set off the immune attack in MG. Press play to light each site at the junction, then search the checker for a drug's evidence badge and its US label wording.

A listed drug is not automatically forbidden. The MGFA cautionary-drugs list says these associations "do not necessarily mean" a drug should not be prescribed, and that some drugs may be necessary and "should not be deemed 'off limits'". A 2021 review stresses that infections in MG must be treated aggressively, while avoiding macrolides, aminoglycosides and fluoroquinolones where possible. Choices are made by the prescriber, with closer monitoring when a listed drug is needed. [[MGFA2024,Sheikh2021]] New or worsening weakness, trouble swallowing or trouble breathing after a new medicine is an emergency. Nursing teaching material tells patients to contact the prescriber urgently for trouble swallowing, speaking or breathing, and the ciprofloxacin medication guide warns of worsening MG symptoms "including muscle weakness and breathing problems". How big is the risk in practice?

Antibiotic courses

2.4% vs 1.3%Exacerbation followed 2.4% of ciprofloxacin, 1.6% of levofloxacin and 1.5% of azithromycin courses, against 1.3% of amoxicillin courses (p = 0.68). Infection itself was a confounder in 88.2% of these exacerbations. [[Uysal2025]]

Overridden alerts

12.7%of 55 hospital encounters where an MG drug alert was overridden were followed by an exacerbation, all after IV magnesium or IV labetalol; in 5 of 7 events another risk factor was present. [[Petrucelli2024]]

A national database

89.6%of 14,459 French people with MG received a "contraindicated" drug during follow-up, and exposure was not linked to ICU admission for MG (HR 1.04). For statins, long-term mortality benefit likely outweighs the short-term MG risk, the authors suggest. [[Sole2026]]

Usually considered safe

One review lists cephalosporins, sulfa drugs, clindamycin, tetracyclines, polymyxin B and nitrofurantoin as antibiotics that can be given safely in MG. [[Sheikh2021]]

Starting steroids and checkpoint inhibitors

Steroid start Label warning

42% of 55 people given prednisone 40-80 mg worsened within 4 weeks, more often with older age, severe bulbar symptoms and more severe MG; published rates range from 25% to 75%, mostly in the first 2 weeks, and 8.6% severe worsening needing ventilation in one table. The prednisone label warns that anticholinesterases together with corticosteroids may produce severe weakness in MG. Giving IVIG first was followed by worsening in 2.2% of 45 patients. The consensus says it may be reasonable to let PLEX or IVIG act for several days before starting steroids in impending crisis. Doses for a person are set by the treating clinician. [[Bae2006,Sheikh2021,Krenn2020,DiezPorras2020,Sanders2016,FDAPrednisone2026]]

Checkpoint inhibitors Label warning

MG appeared in 0.12% (12 of 9,869) on nivolumab and 0.24% (14 of 5,898) in another study. In 65 cases, median onset was 4 weeks; 37% also had myositis, 45% respiratory failure, and 38% died (23% from MG); IVIG or PLEX first did better than steroids alone (95% vs 63% improved). In-hospital mortality of the myositis-myocarditis-MG overlap is close to 40%. The 2020 consensus: existing MG is not an absolute contraindication, and avoiding anti-CTLA-4 plus anti-PD-1/PD-L1 combinations may be prudent. [[Suzuki2017,Sheikh2021,Safa2019,Furlepa2025,Narayanaswami2021]]

Exam hookContraindicated on the US label: telithromycin and quinine. Boxed warning: fluoroquinolones (MG added February 2011). Label warnings: macrolides, aminoglycosides, procainamide, quinidine, timolol, botulinum toxin, D-penicillamine, checkpoint inhibitors, non-depolarizing blockers ("small doses … profound effects"). Magnesium sulfate is not recommended for eclampsia in MG. Starting steroids can cause transient worsening.
In plain wordsSome common medicines make the weak nerve-to-muscle signal weaker, and a few can switch on the immune attack. Being on a list does not mean a drug is banned: the prescriber weighs why it is needed, and the person is watched more closely. Guidance for children with MG suggests giving families a list of medicines that may worsen it. [[OConnell2020]]
