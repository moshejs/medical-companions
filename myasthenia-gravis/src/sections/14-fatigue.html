Part 1 · The disease

Weakness over a day

In MG, weakness grows with use and through the day, and eases with rest and cold. Press play to run a waking day beside the junction's electrical rundown, then switch between a healthy and an MG junction and try rest breaks, an ice pack and heat.

Fatigability is the hallmark of MG. Weakness fluctuates from day to day and hour to hour, is generally worse later in the day, and can be increased by sustained exercise and a higher body temperature. [[Juel2007]] It eases with rest and recurs with use, and it lessens in the cold. [[MSDManual2026]] The cause sits at the junction: each nerve impulse releases a little less acetylcholine than the one before, and in MG the margin for that loss has almost gone. [[Kouyoumdjian2023,Ruff2008]]

The safety factor, in numbers

Bedside tests

Three quick tests use fatigability itself. Sustained upgaze provokes ptosis, an ice pack relieves it, and a rest lets it recover. Pick a test and press play; the accuracy figures are the published ones.

What makes it worse

Sudden difficulty breathing or swallowing is an emergency, not ordinary fatigue. See Myasthenic crisis, and for medicines, Drugs that can worsen MG.

Exam hookFatigability is the hallmark. The decrement on repetitive nerve stimulation is the electrical picture of the same rundown: a reproducible fall of 10% or more from the 1st to the 4th or 5th response at 2-5 Hz. [[AAEM2001,Kouyoumdjian2023]]
In plain wordsEach time a nerve fires in quick succession, it releases a little less messenger. With fewer catchers on the muscle, a few fibres stop responding, and the muscle tires. Rest lets the nerve ending restock.
