# F. Complement, FcRn & B-cell biologics
Scope: the placebo-controlled pivotal trials, open-label extensions (OLEs), pharmacodynamics, safety and regulatory status of the targeted biologics for generalised myasthenia gravis (gMG). Complement C5: eculizumab, ravulizumab, zilucoplan, gefurulimab, cemdisiran ± pozelimab. FcRn: efgartigimod IV and SC, rozanolixizumab, nipocalimab, batoclimab. B-cell and IL-6: inebilizumab, satralizumab, with short notes on rituximab and tocilizumab. Also covered: meningococcal risk with C5 inhibition, FcRn class effects, network meta-analyses, cost-effectiveness, and the debate over placebo response and clinical meaningfulness. These numbers feed the week-by-week "drug vs placebo MG-ADL change" animation. **Every anchor point is tagged with where it came from: paper (abstract or open-access full text), FDA label, EMA page, or press release/congress abstract.**

## Facts

### How to read the animation numbers (endpoint conventions)
- MG-ADL runs 0 to 24 and QMG runs 0 to 39; higher means worse. All changes below are least-squares (LS) mean change from baseline, and a negative number means improvement. [FDASoliris2026; FDAZilbrysq2026]
- Two different designs are being compared. Continuous-dosing trials report change at a fixed week: C5 inhibitors at week 12 or 26, nipocalimab averaged over weeks 22-24, inebilizumab at week 26. Cyclic FcRn trials report one 4- or 6-week cycle and peak near week 4 to 6. A single-axis animation should mark these timepoints, because the endpoints are not interchangeable. [Saccà2023; Roy2026]
- A 2-point MG-ADL improvement best predicted clinical improvement in the validation study. This is the basis of the "≥2-point" MCID used by ADAPT and later trials. [Muppidi2011]
- For QMG, MCID cut-offs from an IVIg-vs-placebo dataset were below a 3.0-point change; trials such as REGAIN and RAISE use stricter responder thresholds (≥3 MG-ADL, ≥5 QMG). [Katzberg2014; Muppidi2019]

### Eculizumab (Soliris) – REGAIN, NCT01997229
- **Population:**
  - AChR-antibody-positive refractory gMG, MGFA class II-IV, MG-ADL ≥6.
  - Refractory meant failure of at least 2 immunosuppressants, or 1 immunosuppressant plus chronic IVIg or plasma exchange, for 12 months.
  - Meningococcal vaccination was required before treatment.
  - Excluded: thymoma, thymectomy within 12 months, IVIg/PLEX within 4 weeks, rituximab within 6 months.
  [Howard2017]
- n=125 randomised and treated (62 eculizumab, 63 placebo), 76 sites in 17 countries, 26 weeks. [Howard2017]
- **Dosing:** IV 900 mg on day 1 and weeks 1, 2 and 3; 1200 mg at week 4; then 1200 mg every 2 weeks. [Howard2017; FDASoliris2026]
- **Primary endpoint failed.** The worst-rank ANCOVA of MG-ADL change at week 26 was not significant (LS mean rank 56.6 vs 68.3; rank difference −11.7, 95% CI −24.3 to 0.96; p=0.0698). The authors called the worst-rank method "an important limitation" because the secondary and sensitivity analyses disagreed with it. [Howard2017]
- **Week-26 anchor (label, repeated-measures analysis):** [FDASoliris2026]
  - MG-ADL −4.2 (SEM 0.49) eculizumab vs −2.3 (0.48) placebo; difference −1.9 (95% CI −3.3 to −0.6), p=0.006.
  - QMG −4.6 vs −1.6; difference −3.0 (−4.6 to −1.3), p=0.001.
- **Source disagreement on the worst-rank p value.** The US label lists p=0.014 "in ranks at Week 26 using a worst rank analysis". The paper reports p=0.0698 for its prespecified worst-rank primary analysis. The label does not explain the difference. [FDASoliris2026; Howard2017]
- **Responders:** the label says ≥3-point MG-ADL and ≥5-point QMG responder rates (without rescue) were significantly higher on eculizumab, but gives no percentages. It also states that "clinical response is usually achieved by 12 weeks". Responder percentages from the paper were not verified (unverified). [FDASoliris2026]
- **REGAIN safety:** [Howard2017]
  - Headache and URTI: 16% eculizumab vs 19% placebo for each.
  - MG exacerbations: 10% vs 24%.
  - Rescue therapy: 10% vs 19%.
  - No deaths and no meningococcal infections.
- **Label adverse reactions (eculizumab vs placebo):** musculoskeletal pain 15% vs 8%, herpes simplex infection 8% vs 2%, abdominal pain 8% vs 5%. [FDASoliris2026]
- **OLE durability (Muppidi 2019):** [Muppidi2019]
  - 117 patients; median exposure 22.7 months; 227 patient-years.
  - Exacerbation rate fell 75.2%, from 102.4 to 25.4 per 100 patient-years compared with the year before REGAIN (p<0.0001).
  - 56% reached minimal manifestations or pharmacological remission.
- **Animation anchor (placebo patients switched to open-label eculizumab):** [Muppidi2019]
  - MG-ADL −1.6 at week 1 (p<0.0001).
  - MG-ADL −3.8 from open-label baseline at month 30 (n=15).
  - Over half of the improvement came in the first 3 months.
- **OLE safety:** headache 37.6%, nasopharyngitis 31.6%. One meningococcal infection occurred after the interim cut-off and resolved with antibiotics. [Muppidi2019]
- **Responder timing (REGAIN plus OLE, n=98):** [Howard2021b]
  - MG-ADL response (≥3 points): 67.3% by week 12 and 84.7% by the end of the OLE.
  - QMG response (≥5 points): 56.1% and 71.4%.
  - Some first responses came after week 12.

### Ravulizumab (Ultomiris) – CHAMPION-MG, NCT03920293
- **Population:** AChR-antibody-positive gMG, MGFA II-IV, MG-ADL ≥6, not restricted to refractory disease. n=175 (86 ravulizumab, 89 placebo), 26 weeks. [Vu2022; FDAUltomiris2025]
- **Dosing (IV, weight-based):** loading dose of 2400/2700/3000 mg on day 1; maintenance of 3000/3300/3600 mg on day 15 and every 8 weeks after. A supplemental dose is required after PLEX, plasmapheresis or IVIg. [Vu2023; FDAUltomiris2025]
- **Week-26 anchor (paper and label):** [Vu2022; FDAUltomiris2025]
  - MG-ADL −3.1 vs −1.4; difference −1.6 (95% CI −2.6 to −0.7), p<0.001.
  - QMG −2.8 vs −0.8; difference −2.0 (−3.2 to −0.8), p<0.001.
- **Onset:** improvement in both scores "within 1 week" of starting, sustained to week 26. [Vu2022]
- **Responders at week 26:** QMG ≥5 points 30.0% vs 11.3% (p=0.005); MG-ADL ≥3 points 56.7% vs 34.1%. [Vu2022; FDAUltomiris2025]
- **Time to response (post hoc, up to week 60):** [Habib2024]
  - Median time to a ≥2-point MG-ADL improvement: 2.1 weeks. To ≥3 points: 4.1 weeks.
  - Median time to ≥3-point QMG: 4.1 weeks. To ≥5-point QMG: 18.3 weeks.
- **Pharmacodynamics:** free C5 was below 0.5 µg/mL (complete inhibition) immediately after the first infusion and stayed there through 26 weeks in all patients. [Vu2023; FDAUltomiris2025]
- **Safety (label):** [FDAUltomiris2025]
  - Diarrhoea 15% vs 12%; URTI 14% vs 8%; dizziness 9% vs 3%.
  - Serious adverse reactions 23% vs 16%; serious infections ≥9% vs 4%.
  - One fatal COVID-19 pneumonia on ravulizumab.
- **OLE durability:** [Meisel2023; Vu2025]
  - Ravulizumab-from-start arm: MG-ADL −4.0 at week 60 (interim) and −4.0 (95% CI −5.3 to −2.8) at week 164 (final), from RCP baseline.
  - Placebo-switch arm: −1.7 at week 60 and −2.1 after 138 weeks, from OLE baseline.
  - 88.1% achieved a ≥2-point MG-ADL improvement; 41.8% of those reached MG-ADL 0-1.
  - No meningococcal infections reported.

### Zilucoplan (Zilbrysq) – RAISE, NCT04115293
- **Population:** AChR-antibody-positive gMG, ages 18-74, MGFA II-IV, MG-ADL ≥6 and QMG ≥12. n=174 (86 zilucoplan, 88 placebo), 12 weeks. Mean baseline MG-ADL was 10.6 (label). [Howard2023; FDAZilbrysq2026]
- **Dosing:** a 0.3 mg/kg macrocyclic peptide C5 inhibitor, self-injected subcutaneously once daily. Label doses by weight: 16.6 mg (<56 kg), 23 mg (56 to <77 kg), 32.4 mg (≥77 kg). [Howard2023; FDAZilbrysq2026]
- **Week-12 anchor:** [Howard2023; FDAZilbrysq2026]
  - MG-ADL −4.39 (95% CI −5.28 to −3.50) vs −2.30 (−3.17 to −1.43); difference −2.09 (−3.24 to −0.95), p=0.0004.
  - QMG −6.19 vs −3.25; difference −2.94 (−4.39 to −1.49) (label).
- **Responders at week 12, without rescue (label):** MG-ADL ≥3 points 73.1% vs 46.1%; QMG ≥5 points 58% vs 33%. [FDAZilbrysq2026]
- **Pharmacodynamics:** complement inhibition (sheep red-cell lysis assay) was 97.5% by the end of week 1 and held through 12 weeks. [FDAZilbrysq2026]
- **RAISE safety:** [Howard2023; FDAZilbrysq2026]
  - Injection-site bruising 16% vs 9% (paper).
  - Label: injection-site reactions 29% vs 16%; URTI 14% vs 7%; diarrhoea 11% vs 2%; lipase increased 7% vs 0%; amylase increased 5% vs 1%.
  - One death per arm, neither judged drug-related.
- **Label warnings beyond meningococcal risk:**
  - Pancreatitis and pancreatic cysts (baseline amylase and lipase required).
  - Morphea in 10/213 (5%) OLE patients, mostly with onset after more than 1 year.
  [FDAZilbrysq2026]
- **OLE (RAISE-XT, n=200, median exposure 1.2 years):** [Howard2024a]
  - Zilucoplan-from-start: MG-ADL −6.06 at week 24 and −6.04 at week 60, from double-blind baseline.
  - Placebo-switch: −6.46 at week 24 and −6.51 at week 60.
  - Commonest adverse events: MG worsening 26%, COVID-19 25%.
- **No placebo data exist beyond week 12.** A Bayesian model-informed analysis using registry and trial controls predicted week-24 MG-ADL of −4.55 with zilucoplan vs −2.00 with control (difference −2.55). It gave an 82.8% probability that the difference exceeds 2 points. This is a modelled value, not observed. [delaBorderie2024]

### Newer C5-pathway agents (2026 results)
- **Gefurulimab (PREVAIL, NCT05556096):** [Gwathmey2026]
  - Population: AChR-antibody-positive gMG, MGFA II-IV, MG-ADL ≥5. n=260 (131 vs 129).
  - Drug: a dual-binding nanobody that binds C5 and albumin. Self-injected subcutaneously: loading dose of 600 or 900 mg, then 300 or 600 mg weekly by weight, for 26 weeks.
  - Baseline: MG-ADL 9.0, QMG 14.8.
- **Gefurulimab week-1 anchor:** MG-ADL difference −1.3 (95% CI −1.9 to −0.8) at week 1, the first assessment. [Gwathmey2026]
- **Gefurulimab week-26 anchor:** [Gwathmey2026]
  - MG-ADL −4.2 vs −2.6; difference −1.6 (−2.4 to −0.8).
  - QMG −4.5 vs −2.4; difference −2.1 (−3.1 to −1.1).
  - Responders: MG-ADL ≥3 points 65.6% vs 52.1% (p=0.04); QMG ≥5 points 46.8% vs 28.0% (p=0.002).
- **Gefurulimab safety:** injection-site reactions 9.9% vs 3.1%; no meningococcal infections. [Gwathmey2026]
- **Gefurulimab regulatory:** CHMP positive opinion on 17 September 2026 (brand Klygefa), as add-on therapy for adults with AChR-antibody-positive gMG. No FDA decision was found. [EMAKlygefa2026]
- **Cemdisiran ± pozelimab (NIMBLE, NCT05070858):** [Vu2026b]
  - Population: AChR- or LRP4-antibody-positive gMG, MG-ADL ≥6. n=284 randomised.
  - Arms: cemdisiran (a C5-silencing siRNA) 600 mg SC every 12 weeks; pozelimab 200 mg every 4 weeks; the combination; or placebo; for 24 weeks.
- **NIMBLE week-24 anchor (MG-ADL):** [Vu2026b]
  - Cemdisiran −4.5, combination −4.0, placebo −2.2.
  - Placebo-adjusted differences: −2.3 (95% CI −3.6 to −1.0) for cemdisiran and −1.7 (−3.0 to −0.4) for the combination.
  - No serious or meningococcal infections in the cemdisiran arm.
- **NIMBLE secondary results (press, not in the abstract):** QMG −4.2 vs −1.5 for cemdisiran; MG-ADL ≥3-point responders 76.6% vs 44.1%. [NeurologyLiveCemdisiran2026]
- **Cemdisiran regulatory:** FDA and EMA accepted the cemdisiran-monotherapy applications in June 2026, with an FDA priority-review target date in November 2026 (press). [NeurologyLiveCemdisiran2026]

### Meningococcal risk with C5 inhibition (class)
- **Boxed warning:** all approved C5 inhibitors (Soliris, Ultomiris, Zilbrysq) carry a boxed warning for serious meningococcal infections. Labels require: [FDASoliris2026; FDAUltomiris2025; FDAZilbrysq2026]
  - Completing or updating vaccination against serogroups A, C, W, Y and B at least 2 weeks before the first dose, following ACIP.
  - Antibacterial prophylaxis if treatment cannot wait.
  - Access only through a REMS: the "ULTOMIRIS and SOLIRIS REMS" or the "ZILBRYSQ REMS".
- **Vaccination is not enough on its own:** the labels state that vaccination "does not eliminate the risk", that fatal infections have occurred in vaccinated patients, and that non-groupable strains are included. [FDASoliris2026; FDAZilbrysq2026]
- **CDC surveillance (all indications):** [McNamara2017]
  - Eculizumab is associated with a 1,000- to 2,000-fold higher incidence of meningococcal disease.
  - 16 US cases were found in 2008-2016; 11 were non-groupable N. meningitidis, and 14 patients had at least 1 documented vaccine dose.
- **US claims data:** only 28% of eculizumab recipients had received at least 1 serogroup B dose, and 7% received antibiotics for at least half of the at-risk period. [Bozio2020]
- **EU risk minimisation (zilucoplan):** controlled access requires the prescriber to declare vaccination in writing. Patients starting within 2 weeks of vaccination take antibiotics for 2 weeks, and patients carry an alert card. [EMAZilbrysq2026]
- **In the MG trials:** REGAIN, CHAMPION-MG through its final OLE, and PREVAIL reported no meningococcal infections. The REGAIN OLE had 1 case, which resolved. [Howard2017; Muppidi2019; Vu2025; Gwathmey2026]

### Efgartigimod IV (Vyvgart) – ADAPT, NCT03669588; ADAPT+, NCT03770403
- **Population:** gMG of any antibody status, MG-ADL ≥5 (more than 50% non-ocular), stable on at least 1 gMG therapy, IgG ≥6 g/L. n=167 (84 vs 83); 129 (77%) were AChR-antibody-positive. Median baseline MG-ADL 9, QMG 16 (label). [Howard2021; FDAVyvgart2026]
- **Cycle design:** [Howard2021; FDAVyvgart2026]
  - 10 mg/kg IV once weekly for 4 infusions = 1 cycle (1200 mg cap at ≥120 kg).
  - Retreatment was by clinical response, no sooner than 8 weeks after the previous cycle started (28 days after the last infusion), over a 26-week study.
  - Median time to a second cycle was 50 days after the last infusion of cycle 1 (label).
- **Primary endpoint (AChR+):** MG-ADL responders in cycle 1, defined as ≥2 points sustained for ≥4 weeks: 68% (44/65) vs 30% (19/64); odds ratio 4.95 (95% CI 2.21-11.53), p<0.0001. [Howard2021; FDAVyvgart2026]
- **QMG responders in cycle 1 (≥3 points sustained for ≥4 weeks; label):** 63.1% vs 14.1%; odds ratio 10.84 (4.18-31.20). [FDAVyvgart2026]
- **Week-4 anchor, cycle 1 (press release; manufacturer conference data, not the paper):** [argenxADAPT2020]
  - Mean MG-ADL change −4.6 from cycle baseline.
  - Minimal symptom expression (MG-ADL 0-1): 40.0% vs 11.1% placebo.
  - 84.1% of responders had onset within the first 2 weeks.
  - The placebo week-4 mean was not obtained (unverified).
- **Pharmacodynamics:** [argenxADAPT2020; Howard2023b]
  - ADAPT: mean maximum reduction of 61.3% in total IgG and 57.6% in AChR antibody at week 4 (press release).
  - ADAPT+ cycle 1: maximum mean total-IgG reduction 55.9% in AChR+ participants, measured at week 3.
  - IgG returned to baseline between weeks 7 and 11.
- **Effect wanes after IgG recovers.** At the post-last-dose week-4 timepoint, no biologic differed significantly from placebo in a QMG network meta-analysis. [Zhong2024]
- **ADAPT safety:** [Howard2021; FDAVyvgart2026]
  - Headache 29% vs 28%; nasopharyngitis 12% vs 18%; serious adverse events 5% vs 8%; no deaths.
  - Label: respiratory infection 33% vs 29%; UTI 10% vs 5%; below-normal lymphocyte counts 28% vs 19%.
- **ADAPT+ durability:** [Howard2023b]
  - 145 treated; up to 17 cycles; 217.6 participant-years.
  - MG-ADL −5.0 at week 3 of cycle 1; −5.3 to −7.5 in cycles 2-10 (AChR+).
  - Annualised cycles: mean 4.7 per year (median 5.0, range 0.5-7.6).
  - Headache 24.8%, COVID-19 15.2%.
- **Seronegative expansion (ADAPT SERON, NCT06298552):** [FDAVyvgart2026; Howard2026SERON]
  - n=119 (58 vs 61). Of these, 61% were triple-seronegative, 34% MuSK+ and 5% LRP4+.
  - Week-4 MG-ADL difference −1.46 (95% CI −2.50 to −0.41), p=0.0068.
  - Week-4 values were −3.35 vs −1.90 (AAN 2026 abstract, which reports 90% CIs).
- **FDA indication change:** on 8 May 2026 the FDA label changed from "anti-AChR antibody positive" to "adult patients with generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG)", the first label without an antibody restriction. [FDAVyvgart2026; FDADrugsFDA2026]

### Efgartigimod alfa + hyaluronidase SC (Vyvgart Hytrulo; Vyvdura in Japan) – ADAPT-SC, NCT04735432
- **Design:** a pharmacodynamic noninferiority bridge rather than a placebo trial. n=110: 1000 mg SC weekly ×4 (n=55) vs 10 mg/kg IV ×4 (n=55). The primary endpoint was the percentage reduction in total IgG at week 4. [Howard2024b; FDAVyvgartHytrulo2026]
- **IgG reduction at week 4:** −66.4% SC vs −62.2% IV; difference −4.2% (95% CI −7.73 to −0.66), which met noninferiority within a 10% margin. [Howard2024b]
- **IgG time course:** −40.1% (SC) and −39.6% (IV) at week 1; −58.1% to −63.5% at week 4; near baseline by week 10. [Howard2024b]
- **Clinical anchors at week 4 (no placebo arm):** [Howard2024b]
  - MG-ADL −5.1 (SC) vs −4.7 (IV); QMG −6.1 vs −5.2.
  - MSE at any time (AChR+): 45.5% vs 41.3%.
- **Injection-site reactions:** 38% in ADAPT-SC (label). In the final ADAPT-SC+ OLE they were 46.1% (n=180; mean follow-up 2.6 years; up to 33 cycles), all mild or moderate. [FDAVyvgartHytrulo2026; Meisel2026F]
- **ADAPT-SC+ minimal symptom expression:** 59.2% of AChR+ and 34.2% of AChR− participants reached MSE. [Meisel2026F]

### Rozanolixizumab (Rystiggo) – MycarinG, NCT03971422
- **Population:** AChR+ or MuSK+ gMG, MGFA II-IVa, MG-ADL ≥3 (non-ocular), QMG ≥11, IgG ≥5.5 g/L. n=200 (66 at 7 mg/kg, 67 at 10 mg/kg, 67 placebo); 89.5% AChR+ and 10.5% MuSK+. [Bril2023; FDARystiggo2026]
- **Dosing:** SC infusion once weekly ×6 = 1 cycle. Label doses by weight: 420 mg (<50 kg), 560 mg (50 to <100 kg), 840 mg (≥100 kg). Safety of starting a new cycle sooner than 63 days after the previous start is not established. [FDARystiggo2026]
- **Day-43 (week 6) anchor:** [Bril2023; FDARystiggo2026]
  - MG-ADL −3.37 (7 mg/kg) and −3.40 (10 mg/kg) vs −0.78 placebo; differences −2.59 (95% CI −4.09 to −1.25) and −2.62 (−3.99 to −1.16), both p<0.0001.
  - QMG −5.4 and −6.7 vs −1.9 (label).
  - This placebo change (−0.78) is the smallest among the pivotal trials.
- **Responder rates:** not obtained in text (unverified).
- **Pharmacodynamics:** total IgG fell up to 79% (stated in the pooled paper). Median change at day 43 ranged from −71.45% (cycle 1) to −59.29% (cycle 10), with reductions seen as early as day 3. [Bril2026]
- **Trial safety:** [Bril2023; FDARystiggo2026]
  - Headache 45% (7 mg/kg) and 38% (10 mg/kg) vs 19%; diarrhoea 25% and 16% vs 13%; pyrexia 13% and 20% vs 1%.
  - Serious adverse events 8% and 10% vs 9%; no deaths.
  - Label: hypersensitivity 11% vs 5%.
- **Label warning – aseptic meningitis:** listed as a Warning and Precaution. Across the trial and extensions, 48% had infections, 4% serious infections, and 3 fatal pneumonias (2 COVID-19). [FDARystiggo2026]
- **Cycle frequency:** about 4 cycles per year in extensions, with a median 98 days between cycle starts. [FDARystiggo2026]
- **Cyclic durability:** day-43 MG-ADL change ranged from −3.2 to −6.0 across cycles 1-13. Headache occurred in 50.0% of 188 patients. [Bril2026; Bril2025b]
- **Chronic weekly dosing (MG0004, n=70):** no albumin or lipid abnormalities. Maximum MG-ADL reduction was 3.1 (7 mg/kg) and 4.1 (10 mg/kg). [Bril2025F]

### Nipocalimab (Imaavy) – Vivacity-MG3, NCT04951622
- **Population:** gMG inadequately controlled on standard care, MG-ADL ≥6, MGFA II-IV. n=199 enrolled and 196 treated (98 vs 98). The primary population was the 153 antibody-positive patients (AChR, MuSK or LRP4); 88% AChR+ and 10% MuSK+. [Antozzi2025; FDAImaavy2026]
- **Dosing:** IV 30 mg/kg loading dose, then 15 mg/kg every 2 weeks, continuously, added to standard care for 24 weeks. [Antozzi2025; FDAImaavy2026]
- **Primary anchor (MG-ADL averaged over weeks 22-24):** −4.70 vs −3.25; difference −1.45 (95% CI −2.38 to −0.52), p=0.0024. [Antozzi2025]
- **QMG anchor (averaged over weeks 22 and 24; label):** −4.9 vs −2.1; difference −2.8 (−4.2 to −1.4). [FDAImaavy2026]
- **The placebo arm improved by 3.25 points**, the largest placebo response among the pivotal FcRn trials. The trial ran 24 weeks with standard care in both arms. [Antozzi2025]
- **Onset and sustained response (post hoc):** response categories were reached as early as week 1. Sustained composite response for ≥8 weeks had an odds ratio of 6.3, and sustained MSE was 4-fold more likely than on placebo. [Vu2026a]
- **Pharmacodynamics:** up to about 75% reduction in median total IgG (company release reporting the Lancet Neurology paper; exact table value not verified). [PharmacyTimesNipocalimab2025]
- **Trial safety:** [Antozzi2025]
  - Adverse events 84% in both arms; infections 43% in both; headache 14% vs 17%; serious adverse events 9% vs 14%.
  - One death from myasthenic crisis on nipocalimab.
- **Label safety:** [FDAImaavy2026]
  - Peripheral oedema 12% vs 2%; muscle spasm 12% vs 3%.
  - Infections in 71% across the double-blind period and OLE (7% serious).
  - 2 serious EBV-related events in the extension, 1 fatal.
  - Fasting total cholesterol ≥240 mg/dL in 24% and LDL ≥160 mg/dL in 11%, peaking at week 4 and then plateauing.
- **Phase 2 (Vivacity-MG):** n=68. Dose response was significant only as a linear trend (p=0.031); no single dose significantly improved MG-ADL at day 57. [Antozzi2024]

### Batoclimab (not approved for MG as of October 2026)
- **China phase 3 (NCT05039190):** n=132 antibody-positive gMG; 680 mg SC weekly ×6 per cycle. [Yan2024]
  - Sustained MG-ADL improvement (≥3 points for ≥4 weeks): 58.2% vs 31.3%; odds ratio 3.45 (1.62-7.35).
  - Separation from placebo by week 2.
  - Day 43: −3.6 vs −1.7; difference −1.9 (−2.8 to −1.0).
  - Treatment-related adverse events 70.1% vs 36.9%.
- **Class concern seen with batoclimab:** in the phase 2a trial albumin fell by about 12 g/L at week 4 (680 mg dose), and total cholesterol, LDL and HDL rose dose-dependently. Both returned toward baseline after stopping. [Nowak2024]
- **Regulatory status:** no NMPA, FDA or EMA approval for MG was found (searched October 2026). This is unverified.

### FcRn class effects (summary)
- **Effect on IgG:** FcRn blockers lower IgG only (not IgM, IgA or IgE), and the effect reverses. Reductions are about 55-66% for efgartigimod, up to about 79% for rozanolixizumab, and about 70-75% for nipocalimab. [Howard2024b; Bril2026; PharmacyTimesNipocalimab2025]
- **Vaccines:** all FcRn labels advise against live vaccines during treatment and an immunisation review before each cycle. None requires meningococcal vaccination. [FDAVyvgart2026; FDARystiggo2026; FDAImaavy2026]
- **Shared signals:** infections, headache (most marked with rozanolixizumab, 38-45%), and hypersensitivity.
- **Agent-specific signals:** lipid rises (nipocalimab label; batoclimab) and albumin falls (batoclimab). Rozanolixizumab binds away from the albumin site, and pooled data showed no meaningful albumin or lipid change.
[FDARystiggo2026; FDAImaavy2026; Nowak2024; Bril2026]

### B-cell and IL-6 agents
- **Inebilizumab (Uplizna) – MINT, NCT04524273:** an anti-CD19 B-cell-depleting antibody. [Nowak2025]
  - n=238 (119 per arm; 190 AChR+ and 48 MuSK+).
  - IV 300 mg on days 1 and 15, plus day 183 for AChR+ participants.
  - Mandatory glucocorticoid taper from week 4 to 5 mg/day by week 24.
  - Entry MG-ADL threshold not verified (unverified).
- **MINT week-26 anchor:** [Nowak2025]
  - MG-ADL −4.2 vs −2.2; difference −1.9 (95% CI −2.9 to −1.0).
  - QMG −4.8 vs −2.3; difference −2.5 (−3.8 to −1.2).
- **MINT week-52 and safety (press release):** [AmgenUplizna2025]
  - AChR+ week-52 MG-ADL difference −2.8 (95% CI −3.9 to −1.7) (exploratory).
  - MuSK+ week-26 MG-ADL difference −2.2; MuSK+ QMG not significant (p=0.13).
  - Infusion reactions 10.1%; 87.4% vs 84.6% reached ≤5 mg/day prednisone.
- **Inebilizumab regulatory:** FDA approved it for adults with AChR+ or MuSK+ gMG, announced 11 December 2025. Dosing is 2 loading doses, then every 6 months. [AmgenUplizna2025]
- **Satralizumab – LUMINESCE, NCT04963270:** an anti-IL-6-receptor antibody. [Habib2025F]
  - Population: seropositive gMG, MGFA II-IV, MG-ADL ≥5, ages ≥12. n=188; 166 AChR+ in the primary analysis.
  - Dosing: SC 120 or 180 mg at weeks 0, 2 and 4, then every 4 weeks.
  - Week 24: MG-ADL −3.59 vs −2.57; difference −1.02 (−1.88 to −0.16), p=0.012. The authors called this "small".
  - The sponsor stopped development and terminated the OLE.
- **Rituximab:** detail is in pass D. In BeatMG (mild-to-moderate AChR+ gMG on prednisone ≥15 mg/day), the steroid-sparing primary outcome was met by 60% on rituximab vs 56% on placebo, a futility result. In a network meta-analysis, rituximab did not significantly improve MG-ADL (−0.92, 95% CI −2.24 to 0.39). [Nowak2022; Saccà2023]
- **Tocilizumab:** evidence is limited to case reports, for example 2 rituximab-refractory AChR+ patients who improved within months. There is no randomised MG trial. [Jonsson2017]

### Comparative evidence (network and indirect comparisons) – disagreements
- **Saccà 2023 (pairwise and network meta-analysis):** [Saccà2023]
  - Pooled MG-ADL effect vs placebo −2.17 (95% CI −2.67 to −1.67).
  - No complement-vs-FcRn difference on MG-ADL (p=0.16).
  - QMG effect larger with FcRn agents (−4.78 vs −2.60).
  - Efgartigimod ranked most likely best.
- **Ma 2024 (Bayesian NMA on QMG):** ranked batoclimab first, then eculizumab. Only batoclimab separated significantly from placebo, and nipocalimab (phase 2 data) ranked last. The authors flagged very wide credible intervals. [Ma2024]
- **Zhong 2024 (NMA at standardised times):** [Zhong2024]
  - At week 1, only efgartigimod, zilucoplan and rozanolixizumab beat placebo.
  - At maximal response, 6 drugs did.
  - At 4 weeks after the last dose, none did.
- **Filippakopoulou 2026 (6 RCTs, n=739, AChR+ only):** both classes improved MG-ADL by 1.7 points (95% CI 1.1-2.3) and QMG by 2.7 points vs placebo. They concluded that only complement-inhibitor OLEs showed durable benefit to 156 weeks with steroid sparing. [Filippakopoulou2026]
- **Roy 2026 (model-based meta-analysis of 9 phase 3 trials):** [Roy2026]
  - Zilucoplan had the highest MG-ADL maximum effect (net 2.5).
  - Batoclimab led on QMG; efgartigimod had the fastest onset (≤1.6 weeks).
  - FcRn agents beat C5 agents on QMG (posterior probability 99.7%) but not on MG-ADL (52.5%).
- **van Steen 2024 (sponsor-funded matching-adjusted comparison, ADAPT vs CHAMPION):** [vanSteen2024]
  - Efgartigimod was better on MG-ADL at week 4 (−1.9, 95% CI −3.3 to −0.5).
  - No difference in 26-week area under the curve (−8.7, −36.1 to 18.8).
  - The result depends on timepoint and is funded by the efgartigimod maker.

### Placebo response and clinical meaningfulness (debate)
- **Placebo arms improve by about 1-3 points**, and the gap between drug and placebo is often near or below the 2-point MCID. Placebo MG-ADL change by trial: [Bril2023; Vu2022; Howard2017; Nowak2025; Vu2026b; Howard2023; Habib2025F; Gwathmey2026; Antozzi2025]
  - −0.78 (MycarinG)
  - −1.4 (CHAMPION-MG)
  - −2.2 (MINT, NIMBLE)
  - −2.3 (REGAIN, RAISE)
  - −2.57 (LUMINESCE)
  - −2.6 (PREVAIL)
  - −3.25 (Vivacity-MG3)
- **Group mean differences vs the 2-point MCID:** ravulizumab −1.6, gefurulimab −1.6, nipocalimab −1.45, satralizumab −1.02, efgartigimod (seronegative) −1.46 and inebilizumab −1.9 all fall below the 2-point individual-patient MCID. This is the core of the meaningfulness debate. The counter-argument is that MCID is defined for individuals, and that responder analyses (for example zilucoplan 73.1% vs 46.1%) separate more clearly. [Muppidi2011; Gwathmey2026; FDAZilbrysq2026]
- **The REGAIN case:** the trial is often cited as an example of how the analysis choice (worst-rank vs repeated-measures) can turn a "negative" trial into a regulatory approval. [Howard2017; FDASoliris2026]
- **Long placebo periods:** zilucoplan's 12-week placebo period was shortened by design. Its sponsor argues for model-based extrapolation (modelled control −2.00 at week 24) to reduce time on placebo. [delaBorderie2024]
- **Cost-effectiveness:**
  - The ICER 2021 review (New England CEPAC) judged that eculizumab and efgartigimod both give a net health benefit over conventional therapy. [Tice2022]
  - The published model found $3,338,000 per QALY for eculizumab (refractory AChR+) and $1,987,000 per QALY for efgartigimod (AChR+), far above usual thresholds. [Lien2024]
  - The ICER health-benefit price benchmark for eculizumab ($13,200-$19,400 per year) comes from secondary press coverage only and was not verified in the report itself (unverified).
- **Guideline position:** the 2020 international consensus update added recommendations on eculizumab (alongside rituximab and methotrexate). It predates FcRn approvals, so no guideline-level ranking among the newer agents exists in this source. [Narayanaswami2021]
