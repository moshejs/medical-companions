Thymectomy over the years

Thymectomy removes the thymus, a gland behind the breastbone. Follow the MGTX trial month by month: muscle scores, prednisone doses and remission over 3 and 5 years. Press play, switch the gland view or the surgical approach, and extend the timeline to 5 years.

Published trial and guidance information only. Everything here reports what the MGTX trial, its extension and the consensus guidance published. Whether surgery suits a particular person, and when, is decided by the person with their neurologist and thoracic surgeon.

Month 0 · Randomisation

The gland and the trial

MGTX randomised 126 people at 36 sites between 2006 and 2012: 66 to extended transsternal thymectomy plus a standard alternate-day prednisone protocol, and 60 to the same prednisone protocol alone. Everyone was 18 to 65, with generalized MG of less than 5 years, AChR antibodies and no thymoma; 6,958 people were screened to find them. Median age was 32 and 33. Mean QMG at entry was 11.40 in the thymectomy arm and 12.35 with prednisone alone. 552 In early-onset MG the thymus often looks like a lymph node, with ectopic germinal centres and muscle-like myoid cells that carry AChR, where antibody-making B cells can be primed. 87,282

In plain wordsThe thymus is a gland behind the breastbone. In many younger people with MG it holds small antibody-making centres that a healthy thymus does not have.

Who the guidance says it is for

The strongest evidence comes from one randomised trial in a narrow group: adults aged 18 to 65 with generalized, AChR-antibody-positive MG without a thymoma, within 5 years of onset. Outside that group, guidance rests on cohort studies and expert consensus. 381,552

Around the operation, thymoma, and the evidence on approach

Guidance and trials

Before and after the operation

Thymectomy is elective: the 2016 guidance gives its long delay in onset of effect as the reason. Guidance says it should be done when the person is stable and judged safe for an operation in which pain and chest mechanics can limit breathing, and recommends postponing it until after pregnancy. 381,476

Whether to give IVIG or plasma exchange first is debated. Guidance lists them for preparing people with significant bulbar weakness. In a double-blind trial of 47 well-controlled patients, routine IVIG before surgery was judged unnecessary; a 2026 review suggests risk-based use rather than treating everyone. In a small trial of 24, plasma exchange was followed by longer intubation and surgery than IVIG. 10,11,188,476

Post-operative myasthenic crisis is now reported below 10%. Risk factors include bulbar symptoms, previous crisis, lower lung function, high AChR antibody levels and thymoma. In 352 US operations for non-thymomatous MG there were no deaths within 30 days; Japan's registry reports 0.26% hospital mortality. 10,71,324,379

New trouble breathing or swallowing in a person with MG can signal a crisis and is treated as an emergency: see Myasthenic crisis. 353

Pathology

Thymoma: types, stages and antibodies

About 10 to 20% of people with MG have a thymoma (a pooled 21% across 49 studies, higher in men and after 40), and 30 to 44% of people with a thymoma have MG. WHO types run A, AB, B1, B2, B3; MG is rare with type A and common with cortical type B. 336,408,459

Masaoka stage I is encapsulated, II through the capsule, III into neighbouring organs, IVa spread over pleura or pericardium, IVb spread by lymph or blood; Koga later rebalanced stages I and II. 295,340

Titin antibodies occur in about 95% and RyR antibodies in about 70% of thymoma MG, against 58% and 14% in late-onset and 10% and 0% in early-onset MG. In someone under 60 they strongly suggest a thymoma. Thymomas usually lack AIRE, a gene the thymus needs to teach tolerance. 459,507

MG can start after a thymoma is removed: 8% across 13 cohorts, more often with AChR antibodies before surgery (risk ratio 5.53). In one series 10 of 33 seropositive patients developed it, against 0 of 90 seronegative. 510,558

No randomised comparison

Open, VATS or robotic

A meta-analysis of 26 studies (3,588 patients) found no difference in complete stable remission at 1, 3 or 5 years between open and minimally invasive thymectomy, with shorter stays and less blood loss but longer operations for minimally invasive surgery. A 4-centre cohort found no difference in QMG or prednisone over 2 years, with a hospital stay of 2.5 against 5 days. 40,312

In a matched single-centre comparison, robotic surgery had fewer in-hospital complications than open surgery (6.8% against 21.1%). 498

Where sources disagree: the consensus panel is permissive about less invasive approaches in experienced centres, while the AAN advisory says people should be told it is uncertain whether MGTX's benefit carries over. 212,381

Mechanism

Why removing a gland can help

The hyperplastic early-onset MG thymus behaves like a tertiary lymphoid organ, with new vessels, chemokines and ectopic germinal centres; its myoid cells carry AChR and can present it to B cells. Regulatory T cells from MG thymus are functionally defective. More ectopic germinal centres in the removed gland predicted a better outcome after surgery. 30,87,282,479

The thymus is thought to play little part in MuSK MG: most MuSK thymi resemble normal ones. 314,339

In plain wordsIn many younger people with MG, the thymus seems to train and house the cells that make the harmful antibodies. Taking it out removes one source.
After MGTX

Cost, uptake and long-term remission

A UK lifetime model built on MGTX data found thymectomy plus prednisolone both cheaper and better than prednisolone alone: 0.52 more quality-adjusted life years and £13,014 saved per person. 326

Yet uptake has not grown: in US surgical data thymectomy for MG rose until about 2019 to 2020 and then fell; in Japan it fell from 375 cases in 2001 to 97 in 2023. 71,379

Drug-free remission is not guaranteed: in a post hoc MGTX analysis, 64% of the thymectomy arm against 38% with prednisone alone reached sustained minimal manifestations with prednisone stopped over 5 years, and a review notes that many people in routine care never reach complete stable remission. 311,444 The Toward a cure module puts this beside newer approaches.

History

Seventy-five years to a trial

Sauerbruch removed thymic tissue from a young woman with MG in 1911, and Blalock removed a thymic tumour in 1936 and reported a planned series in 1941. For decades the only evidence was retrospective: the 2000 AAN review found 21 flawed cohorts, and a 2013 Cochrane review found no randomised trials. MGTX in 2016 was the first. 49,50,72,211,495,552
