Sources

Every reference returned by the nine research passes behind this guide: journal articles, guidelines, drug labels, regulatory documents and trial registrations. The small numbers throughout the guide point here.

Each research area was searched separately, so a few works appear under a merged entry. Every journal entry was checked against its PubMed, Crossref or Europe PMC record for authors, title, journal, year and identifiers; trial entries against ClinicalTrials.gov. Entries marked check could not be fully verified against the original, so treat them with extra care.
