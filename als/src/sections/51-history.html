History & people

ALS was described in Paris in the 19th century, became known to the public through a baseball player in 1939, and was explained at the level of genes and proteins mostly after 1993. In plain wordsALS was named in France about 150 years ago. For a century doctors could describe it but knew little about its causes. Most of what is known about the genes and proteins involved was found in the last 30 years, and in 2023 a medicine aimed at one gene, for SOD1-ALS, was approved in the US.
