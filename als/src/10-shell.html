ALS, explained

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis is a disease of the motor neurons, the nerve cells that carry every movement from the brain to the muscles. Here it is in moving pictures: watch the two neurons fail, see a protein leave its place in the cell, score the ALSFRS-R, follow breathing and weight over months, and look honestly at what each treatment and each research route can and cannot do.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.
