ALS Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, made for health-science students and for people living with ALS and their families.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should change a treatment or a care decision without your own clinical team. Doses, criteria and guideline thresholds are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one disease each. Also there: Alzheimer's Companion and FMF Companion.
