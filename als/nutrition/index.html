Swallowing and feeding tubes

Weak mouth and throat muscles make swallowing slow and less safe, and weight falls. Scrub the timeline to watch one swallow repeat as the muscles weaken, with weight and FVC on the same months. Choose when the feeding tube (gastrostomy) is placed and how, and see what the ProGas study found.

Weight, energy and diet

Weight

Weight loss and survival

  • In 92 people in Limoges, a 5% loss from usual weight at diagnosis carried a 30% higher risk of death (RR 1.30, 95% CI 1.08-1.56); each further 5% lost during follow-up raised it by 34%, and each BMI unit lost by 24%. 321
  • ESPEN summarises cohort data: at diagnosis each BMI point lost raised the risk of death by 9-23%, more than 5% weight loss roughly doubled it (HR 1.92), and 10% loss raised it by 45%. 84
  • In 55 people, 16.4% were malnourished, with a 7.7-fold higher risk of death; in 79 Japanese people a BMI fall of 2.0 kg/m2 a year or more predicted shorter survival. 141,468
  • ESPEN suggests aiming for weight gain if BMI is below 25, stability at 25-35 and loss above 35 (good-practice points). 84
Established: consistent cohort associations
Energy

Hypermetabolism

  • Measured resting energy use was above the predicted value in 47.5% of 61 people, and 80% kept the same metabolic status over time. 71
  • Hypermetabolism was found in 41% of 58 people with ALS against 12% of matched controls (adjusted OR 5.4), with faster ALSFRS-R decline (−0.68 against −0.39 points a month) and a higher risk of death (HR 3.2). 486
  • The survival link is disputed: Bouteloup 2009 found no correlation, and ESPEN cites a study with no association. 71,84
  • ESPEN estimates needs at about 30 kcal/kg a day without ventilation and 25-30 kcal/kg on NIV, which lowered resting energy use by 7% in one study. 84
Early evidence: survival link disputed
ESPEN 2018

What the nutrition guideline recommends

  • Screen for malnutrition and dysphagia at diagnosis and about every 3 months; videofluoroscopy, videofluoromanometry and FEES can detect early dysphagia. 84
  • Texture adaptation and postures such as chin tuck in moderate dysphagia (good-practice points). 84
  • Supplements when needs are not met by an enriched diet, while stating there is insufficient evidence that oral supplements improve survival. 84
  • Reports PEG placed under NIV: in one study 35 people with FVC below 50% had survival similar to 40 above 50%; spirometry can underestimate breathing in bulbar weakness. 84
Evidence

Does the tube prolong life?

  • The 2023 Cochrane review found no randomised or quasi-randomised trials comparing tube feeding with continued oral feeding, and judged such trials unlikely for ethical reasons. 488
  • AAN 2009 graded PEG Level B to stabilise weight and prolong survival, from 2 Class II and 7 Class III studies; 4 Class III studies found no survival benefit. 332
  • ESPEN reports that EFNS found no convincing survival evidence. 84
  • A 2013 meta-analysis suggested RIG or PIG might be safer than PEG; the larger, prospective ProGas found similar 30-day mortality, but its groups differed in FVC and weight loss. 431,484
Early evidence: observational only
Diet trials

High-calorie diets

Wills 2014

Phase 2, double-blind, 24 people already tube-fed (20 started). The high-carbohydrate hypercaloric group had fewer serious adverse events (0 against 9) and no deaths against 3 of 7 controls over 5 months (p=0.03); the authors called this preliminary. 540

Early evidence
LIPCAL-ALS (2020)

201 people, a 405 kcal/day fatty supplement or placebo with riluzole: survival probability at 28 months 0.37 against 0.39 (HR 0.97, p=0.44), no benefit overall. 304

Established: no overall benefit

A survival benefit in fast progressors was found only in a post hoc analysis (effect size not read). 304

Hypothesis
LIPCAL-ALS II (ongoing)

An ultra-high-calorie fatty diet against placebo, survival as the primary outcome, in people with disease under 24 months and ALSFRS-R loss of 0.33 points a month or more; 399 enrolled, active and not recruiting, estimated completion April 2028. EAN 2024 makes no recommendation specific to high-calorie diets. 297,507

Texture

Texture modification and IDDSI

  • The IDDSI framework (released November 2015) grades drinks and foods on an 8-level continuum, 0 to 7, each level with a number, text label, colour code, definition and a way to test it. 113
  • It was built from surveys of 2050 respondents in 33 countries and 3190 in 57 countries. 113
  • Videofluoroscopy may identify textures a person can handle; in a FEES cohort, 44% refused thickeners or PEG. 332,408

Background: IDDSI is a shared vocabulary for textures, not a treatment, and it is used across conditions, not only in ALS.

Where the sources disagree

The FVC 50% rule. AAN (one Class III study) and EFNS link higher risk to FVC below 50%, but ESPEN, Canada and EAN cite procedures under NIV, RIG or PIG with similar outcomes below 50%, and note that spirometry is unreliable in bulbar weakness. 84,332,471,507

PEG, RIG or PIG. A 2013 meta-analysis suggested RIG and PIG might be safer; ProGas found similar 30-day mortality, but it was not randomised and its groups differed in FVC and weight loss. 431,484

Hypermetabolism and survival. Steyn 2018 linked it to shorter survival; Bouteloup 2009 did not. 71,84,486

Multidisciplinary clinics (observational evidence). Attending a specialist clinic was linked to longer survival in Ireland (median 7.5 months longer; HR 0.59 across Ireland and Northern Ireland) and better mental quality of life in the Netherlands, but Cochrane found no RCT, so the evidence is observational. 398,454,499,508
