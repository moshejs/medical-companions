## Findings

### cure · HEALEY regimens A to G
- HEALEY Regimen F (NCT05740813; ABBV-CLS-7262, which the site calls fosigotifator, an eIF2B activator) is now published in full. It ran at 74 US centres between 23 March 2023 and 3 October 2024. 310 people were randomised 2:1:1 to ABBV-CLS-7262 120 mg (n=155), 240 mg (n=79) or regimen placebo (n=76) once daily for 24 weeks; 50 participants on placebo from other regimens were shared, giving 126 on placebo in total. Established negative (peer-reviewed platform-trial regimen; authors include AbbVie and Calico employees) [[AjroudDriss2026]].
- Primary outcome, the disease rate ratio (DRR) from the Bayesian shared-parameter model of ALSFRS-R and survival, 120 mg vs shared placebo: 1.06 (95% credible interval 0.85-1.32). Below 1 would favour the drug. The 240 mg dose was exploratory, and the abstract gives no DRR for it [[AjroudDriss2026]].
- Secondary outcomes at 24 weeks were not statistically significant between drug and placebo. Adverse event rates were similar, with no unexpected treatment-related risks [[AjroudDriss2026]].
- CSF FGF21, described as a putative marker of integrated stress response activity, fell at week 24 on both doses: mean (SD) change −6.8 (28.6) pg/mL on 120 mg, −8.3 (15.9) pg/mL on 240 mg and 0.6 (11.1) pg/mL on placebo. The authors conclude the drug "attenuated the ISR but not ALS progression" [[AjroudDriss2026]].
- The registry record agrees: DRR 1.06 (0.850-1.324), posterior probability that the DRR is below 1 of 0.3117, 155, 79 and 76 started [[NCT05740813]].
- The site said the primary endpoint was not met "as reported in January 2025, no peer-reviewed report", citing a news report [[LongevityTech2025]]. That statement is now outdated. The site now gives the DRR and cites the paper. The direction of the result is unchanged, and nothing on the site is overturned.

### Exam hooks
- HEALEY Regimen F, ABBV-CLS-7262 (fosigotifator) 120 mg: DRR 1.06 (95% CrI 0.85-1.32), no slowing of progression, although CSF FGF21 fell [[AjroudDriss2026]].

### In plain words
- The full results of a trial of a stress-response drug in ALS are now published. The drug reached its target in the spinal fluid, but people taking it declined at about the same rate as people on a dummy pill [[AjroudDriss2026]].
