## Findings

### genes · Genetic testing and counselling guidelines
- The International Alliance of ALS/MND Associations convened an international, multidisciplinary Genetics Task Force, which published global consensus recommendations for genetic counselling, genetic testing and laboratory practice in ALS. They were developed through a structured, iterative consensus process with lived-experience input and approved by anonymous vote, and they set "both minimum and aspirational best practices" across resource settings (Established: published consensus guideline) [[Salmon2026]].
- The 2023 US consensus remains the source for the minimum gene panel and the counts of recommendations [[Roggenbuck2023]]; the 2026 document's specific recommendations were not read (abstract only).

### In plain words
- In 2026 ALS groups from around the world agreed on basic and ideal standards for gene testing and counselling, including for countries with fewer resources [[Salmon2026]].
