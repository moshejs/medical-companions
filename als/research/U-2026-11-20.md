## Findings

### drugs · Edaravone: later studies
- ADORE (NCT05178810, phase 3, Ferrer) is now published in full. Adults with ALS within 24 months of symptom onset, vital capacity at least 70% and an ALSFRS-R decline of 0.35 to 1.50 points per month were randomised 2:1 to oral edaravone (FNP122) 100 mg per day or placebo at 37 hospitals in 12 European countries. 313 were randomised (209 vs 104); nine people from one site found non-compliant with Good Clinical Practice were excluded and one person in each group was never treated, leaving 302 analysed (205 vs 97). Established negative (peer-reviewed phase 3 RCT, sponsor-funded) [[vanEijk2026]].
- Primary endpoint, least squares mean change in ALSFRS-R total score at week 48: −12.6 (SE 0.7) on edaravone vs −13.4 (SE 1.0) on placebo; difference 0.78 points (95% CI −1.44 to 3.00; p=0.49). The primary endpoint was not met [[vanEijk2026]].
- Safety: treatment-emergent adverse events in 180 of 205 (88%) on edaravone and 82 of 97 (85%) on placebo. No deaths were considered related to study treatment [[vanEijk2026]].
- The authors write that the results "do not support its use at the tested dose" for modifying disease progression in the general ALS population [[vanEijk2026]].
- The site already said ADORE missed its primary endpoint, citing the registry results (unadjusted mean change −11.2 vs −10.8 in the 171 and 76 people with week-48 data, no statistical analysis posted) [[CTgov_ADORE]]. The paper confirms the negative result and supplies the modelled estimate, confidence interval and p value. The site now shows the paper's figures and cites both. Nothing on the site is overturned.
- EAN 2024 recommended against IV or oral edaravone outside a clinical trial as an interim recommendation, pending this European phase 3 [[VanDamme2024EAN]]. No revised EAN statement was found; the guideline wording on the site is unchanged.

### Exam hooks
- Oral edaravone (ADORE, 48 weeks): ALSFRS-R change −12.6 vs −13.4 on placebo, difference 0.78 (95% CI −1.44 to 3.00; p=0.49); primary endpoint not met [[vanEijk2026]].

### In plain words
- A year-long European trial of a daily edaravone drink is now published. People taking it lost function at about the same rate as people taking a dummy drink [[vanEijk2026]].
