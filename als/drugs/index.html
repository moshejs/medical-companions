Part 3 · Treatment & care

Riluzole, edaravone and others

The drugs approved to slow ALS act on steps that are thought to injure motor neurons, but none stops the disease. Play the synapse to see two of those steps, then lay a drug over it. Below are three decades of approvals and withdrawals, the published label details and what each trial found.

Step 1 · A normal signal

Glutamate carries the signal

A glutamate-releasing nerve terminal excites the motor neuron. Glutamate opens AMPA and NMDA receptors on the motor neuron, and the astrocyte next to the synapse clears it from the cleft through its transporter EAAT2 (called GLT-1 in rodents). 458,493

In plain wordsNerve cells pass a signal with a chemical called glutamate. A helper cell, the astrocyte, mops it up after each signal.

What the trials found, in absolute numbers

RiluzoleEstablished

About 2 to 3 more months of median survival

HR 0.8495% CI 0.698 to 0.997

Cochrane 2012, four trials (974 on riluzole, 503 on placebo), tracheostomy-free survival on 100 mg/day. One-year survival 49% on placebo vs 58% on riluzole; median survival 11.8 vs 14.8 months. 334

No benefit on muscle strength. The EU label says there is no evidence of effect on motor function, lung function or strength, and none in late-stage ALS. 162,334

RiluzoleControversy

Real-world studies claim much more

6 to 19months, in 8 of 15 studies

Andrews 2020 reviewed 15 population studies; 8 reported median survival 6 to 19 months longer in people treated with riluzole. 22

These are not randomised. People who are offered and keep taking a drug differ from those who are not (confounding by indication), and time before treatment starts can be counted in the treated group's favour (immortal-time bias). The trial figure of 2 to 3 months remains the reference. 22,334

EdaravoneNegative

First phase 3 (MCI186-16): no difference

0.65points, p = 0.411

ALSFRS-R change over 24 weeks: −5.70 on edaravone (n = 100) vs −6.35 on placebo (n = 99). 1

A post hoc search found a subgroup (vital capacity at least 80%, every ALSFRS-R item at least 2, onset within 2 years) where the difference was larger. That subgroup became the entry criteria for the next trial. 152

EdaravoneSelected group only

MCI186-19: positive in the post hoc–selected group

2.49points, 95% CI 0.99 to 3.98

137 people randomised (69 vs 68), chosen to match the subgroup above. ALSFRS-R change at 24 weeks −5.01 vs −7.50, about a third less decline. More than 90% were also taking riluzole. 176,325

The authors saw "no indication" of benefit outside these criteria. 325 In grade 3 disease (MCI186-18) there was no benefit. 153

EdaravoneNot confirmed

Later studies found no added benefit

  • Germany, 116 vs 116 matched people: −0.91 vs −0.85 points/month (P = 0.37); survival also no different. 543
  • Oral FAB122 (ADORE, 313 people): −11.2 vs −10.8 at 48 weeks, primary not met. 122
  • Daily oral vs on/off dosing (384 people): stopped for futility. 124

The EU application was withdrawn in 2019, and EAN 2024 recommends against IV or oral edaravone outside a clinical trial. 158,507

AMX0035Phase 3 negative

CENTAUR positive, PHOENIX negative

0.42points/month, 95% CI 0.03 to 0.81

CENTAUR (phase 2, 137 people): ALSFRS-R slope −1.24 vs −1.66 points/month over 24 weeks (P = 0.03); strength, breathing and survival outcomes did not differ. 415 An open-label survival follow-up reported 25.0 vs 18.5 months (HR 0.56), comparing early with later starting. 416

PHOENIX (phase 3, 664 people, 48 weeks) missed its primary endpoint (p = 0.667) and every secondary endpoint. Withdrawal began on 4 April 2024, about 19 months after US approval. 17

MasitinibRefused in the EU

A narrowed population

3.4points, 95% CI 0.65 to 6.13

In 394 people on riluzole, 4.5 mg/kg/day gave this ALSFRS-R difference only in the prespecified "normal progressor" group; there was no effect in the broader cohort, and serious adverse events were 31% vs 18%. 347 EMA refused it twice, citing inspection findings and methods. 161

MethylcobalaminJapan only

Ultra-high-dose vitamin B12 injections

1.97points, 95% CI 0.44 to 3.50

JETALS: 130 people within 1 year of onset, 50 mg intramuscular twice weekly for 16 weeks; ALSFRS-R −2.66 vs −4.63. 407 Backed for ALS by the Japanese committee in August 2024; no FDA or EMA approval was found. 428

Exam hookRiluzole (FDA 1995): HR 0.84, about 2 to 3 months of median survival. Edaravone: 2.49 ALSFRS-R points over 24 weeks, only in a post hoc–selected early group; not authorised in the EU. AMX0035: approved on phase 2 (CENTAUR), withdrawn in 2024 when phase 3 (PHOENIX) failed. 17,325,334
In plain wordsThe approved drugs slow ALS a little; none stops it. Riluzole adds a few months on average. Other drugs looked promising in one trial and then did not hold up in the next, bigger one.

What the guidelines say

EAN 2024: "Offer lifelong riluzole to all people with ALS at diagnosis" (strong), with dose reduction and then stopping considered if adverse events persist. It recommends against edaravone outside a clinical trial, did not recommend AMX0035 outside trials before phase 3 data (it was written before PHOENIX), and recommends offering tofersen first-line in progressive SOD1-ALS. 507

Earlier: AAN 2009 says riluzole should be offered to slow progression (Level A), and EFNS 2012 advises starting it as early as possible. 20,332

Some drugs treat symptoms rather than slowing ALS. Dextromethorphan-quinidine (approved by the FDA for pseudobulbar affect in 2010) improved the CNS-LS score by 3.3 points more than dextromethorphan alone over 28 days. 76,149 Symptom care is in Living with ALS and Breathing and NIV.
