Part 2 · Diagnosis & course

ALSFRS-R and the course

The ALSFRS-R adds up 12 everyday abilities into one score from 48 down to 0. Press Play to follow three illustrative courses past the milestones of King's staging, then score the 12 items yourself in the calculator below.

Domain order

The lines are arithmetic, 48 minus rate × months, using the lower quartile, median and upper quartile of ΔFS at diagnosis in a French cohort: 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 points a month. 217 For comparison, PRO-ACT trial participants fell by 1.02 a month on average 29 and a UK and Australian clinic cohort by 0.58. 66 They are illustrations, not observed courses. Stage bands and event markers are mean months from onset in people who had died in a London clinic cohort: means, not medians, and other cohorts differ. 451 The order in which the four domains fall is schematic.

King's 1 · Month 0

Onset

Symptoms begin in one region: weakness, wasting, stiffness, slurred speech or trouble swallowing. King's stage 1 is the first region involved; the regions are bulbar, upper limb, lower limb and diaphragmatic. 451 The ALSFRS-R starts at or near 48, the score for normal function. It has 12 items in four domains (bulbar, fine motor, gross motor, respiratory), each scored 0 to 4. 66,79 Domain sub-scores may begin to fall before the date a person gives as onset. 455

In plain wordsThe scale adds up 12 everyday abilities, each worth up to 4 points. 48 means no loss yet.
Exam hookALSFRS-R: 12 items × 0-4 = 48 (normal). Four domains: bulbar, fine motor, gross motor, respiratory.

The commonly quoted bands

On the bands of below 0.5, 0.5 to 1.0 and above 1.0 points a month, these inputs fall in the slower (below 0.5) group. 217,330

These three bands are widely used, but who first proposed them could not be traced: Kimura 2006, which introduced ΔFS, gives no bands in its abstract, and later studies use them without naming an origin. 217,265,280

Balendra and colleagues mapped ALSFRS-R domains to King's regions, with gastrostomy or NIV as the stage 4 proxy; agreement with clinical staging was excellent (ICC 0.92). 34 The items used are 1-3 (bulbar), 4 and 5a (upper limb), 8 (lower limb), 5b (gastrostomy) and 10 and 12 (breathing). 9 The exact cut-offs are in the Balendra paper, which could not be read for this guide. The cut-offs used here, any mapped item below 4 for a region, item 5b in use for 4A, and item 10 at 0 or item 12 at 3 or less for 4B, come from secondary descriptions in trial documents and have not been checked against the original paper. MiToS counts the domains in which independence is lost: walking or self-care, swallowing, communicating and breathing. Stage 0 is involvement without loss of independence, stages 1-4 the number of domains lost, and stage 5 death. 111 It uses items 1 and 4 (communicating), 3 (swallowing), 6 and 8 (movement), and 10 and 12 (breathing) 9; one trial analysis plan combines them as walking or dressing, swallowing, speech and handwriting, and breathlessness or breathing support. 122 The item cut-offs could not be verified from the original paper, so this calculator shows the mapped items and does not give a MiToS stage.

Prognosis: the ENCALS model

ENCALS is a published model that sorts people into five risk groups. The figures below are medians for groups of people in the development data, as published. They describe populations, not what will happen to one person, and the model's own inputs come from a clinical assessment.

Data came from 14 ALS centres in nine European countries, for people seen between 1992 and 2016. The endpoint combined three events, counted from symptom onset: non-invasive ventilation for more than 23 hours a day, tracheostomy, or death. Of 16 candidate predictors, eight entered the model. 535

Tested in external data, the model separated longer from shorter times fairly well (c statistic 0.78, 95% CI 0.77-0.80) and its predictions matched observed times (calibration slope 1.01). 535

What the numbers mean, and their limits

How much change matters

In a UK and Australian clinic cohort, the minimal important difference over about 3 months was 3.8 points on the usual summed scale (2 on an interval version); other methods gave 2.02 to 5.43. 66

90% of 65 surveyed clinicians and researchers rated a 20% slowing of decline as at least somewhat meaningful, and 93% rated a 50% slowing as very meaningful. 91 In a survey of 522 people with ALS, the median minimum slowing expected from a drug was 20%. 330

One total, or four domains?

In an Irish register cohort, bulbar and motor subscore slopes fitted prognostic models better than the total slope, and the respiratory subscore added nothing once the other two were known. The authors argue for reporting domains. 455

The scale is analysed as if each point were equal, although its items are categories; an interval (Rasch) transformation has been proposed. 66

Trials and clinics differ

Pooled trial data average about 1 point a month (PRO-ACT 1.02, ±2.3). 29 A clinic cohort averaged 0.58, and fast progressors were more likely to drop out of follow-up. 66 Survival from onset ranged from 4 months to 11.9 years in one German cohort. 270

King's and MiToS measure different things

King's counts regions involved and then the need for gastrostomy or NIV; MiToS counts domains in which independence is lost. In LiCALS trial data they agreed only fairly (weighted kappa 0.21): King's gives more detail early, MiToS late, and the authors propose using both. 111,168,451

Losing at least one MiToS domain, or an ALSFRS-R fall of 6 to 9 points, over 6 months was the best cut-off for predicting death, tracheotomy or more than 23 hours a day of NIV at 12 to 18 months. 495

Exam hookALSFRS-R: 12 items × 0-4 = 48. ΔFS = (48 − score) / months since onset; a higher ΔFS at diagnosis predicts shorter survival. King's 1-3 count regions, 4A is gastrostomy, 4B is NIV. 265,280,451
In plain wordsA 48-point score tracks everyday abilities. How quickly it falls says more about the future than any one score, and the fall is rarely a perfectly straight line.
