History & people

ALS was described in Paris in the 19th century, became known to the public through a baseball player in 1939, and was explained at the level of genes and proteins mostly after 1993. Clinic & places

1850 · Progressive muscular atrophy

François-Amilcar Aran published a memoir describing and naming "progressive muscular atrophy". Duchenne de Boulogne later claimed priority, but a historian credits Aran with both the clinical description and the name, hence "Aran-Duchenne" disease 68.

In plain wordsBefore ALS had its name, doctors in Paris described people whose muscles slowly wasted away.

In plain wordsALS was named in France about 150 years ago. For a century doctors could describe it but knew little about its causes. Most of what is known about the genes and proteins involved was found in the last 30 years, and in 2023 a medicine aimed at one gene, for SOD1-ALS, was approved in the US.

Aran or Duchenne?

Two Paris physicians described progressive muscular atrophy around 1850, and both claimed it. A historian credits Aran with the clinical description and the name, which is why the condition was long called "Aran-Duchenne" disease 68.

One disease, several names

ALSCharcot's diseaseLou Gehrig's diseasemotor neuron disease (MND)

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, Charcot's disease in many countries 275, Lou Gehrig's disease in the United States, and motor neuron disease (MND), the umbrella term used in the UK. Studies of "motor neuron disease" may also count PLS and PMA 197.

Guam: a cluster that faded Hypothesis, contested

ALS was far more common on Guam from the mid-1950s, then declined over 40 years 424,425. Macaques fed the cycad amino acid BMAA developed motor and parkinsonian features 481, and eating flying foxes that feed on cycad seeds was proposed as a route 121. One laboratory found no free BMAA in brains of Chamorros with the disease 344.

Approval is not the last word

AMX0035 went from approval in 2022 to withdrawal after its phase 3 trial failed 17. Edaravone was approved in the US, while its EU application was withdrawn 149,158 and the 2024 EAN guideline does not recommend it 507. Tofersen's approval is conditional on the confirmatory ATLAS trial 61.

The long tail

Median survival is 2-4 years 180, yet some people live far longer. In the Lombardy register 7% were alive at 10 years and 3% at 15 435. Stephen Hawking lived about 55 years after his diagnosis 221.
