Privacy

Short version: no accounts and no ads. Your place in a guide is kept on your own device. We measure how the guides are used with Vercel Web Analytics and Google Analytics. Google Analytics sets cookies, except for visitors in the UK, the EU, the European Economic Area and Switzerland.

What stays on your device

Each guide remembers a few things in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off: the module you were on, whether you chose the light or dark theme, which tab you last opened in some modules, and, on the home page, which "I'm here because…" option you chose. These are stored under names that start with the guide's short name (for example fmfc:) or mc:.

They are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

What we count

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies, and it does not identify you or follow you to other sites. We see totals, such as how many people opened a guide, which country they were in and what kind of device they used.

We also use Google Analytics to understand which guides and modules people read and how they move through them. It works differently depending on where you are:

  • In the UK, the EU, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google Analytics stores nothing on your device. Your browser still tells Google that a page was viewed, without a cookie or an identifier that links your visits together.
  • Everywhere else, Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_Z5BS957R33. They hold a random identifier, so Google can tell a returning browser from a new one. They don't contain your name, email address or anything you type into the site.

Google receives your IP address and browser details with each page view and handles them under Google's privacy policy. We see reports, not individual people. You can block Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, with your browser's tracking protection or a content blocker, or by deleting this site's cookies. The guides work the same with it blocked.

Who else is involved

Vercel (hosting)
The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, its servers receive your IP address and browser details to deliver each page and keep the service secure. See Vercel's privacy policy.
Google (analytics)
Google Analytics runs as described under "What we count". The script is loaded from Google's servers, so Google sees each page view.
Typefaces
The typefaces are open-licence fonts served from this site, by Vercel like the pages, so your browser does not ask Google Fonts or any other font service for them.
Links to sources
Opening a source link takes you to PubMed, a journal or another site, whose own privacy policy then applies.

If you email us

If you write to hello@medcompanions.org, we use your message and address only to reply and to fix what you report. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your message.

Children

The guides are educational. They don't ask anyone, children included, for a name, an email address or any other contact details.

Changes and questions

If this changes, this page will say so, with a new date. Questions: hello@medcompanions.org.
