Part 3 · Emergency treatment

Antiplatelets & anticoagulants

A heart attack (myocardial infarction, MI) is usually a clot on a torn plaque. Medicines that calm platelets and slow clotting stop that clot growing, but every one of them also makes bleeding more likely. Watch how aspirin and the P2Y12 blockers work, then see the trials that weighed fewer heart attacks against more bleeding.

Platelet panel is schematic. Bars are the published trial percentages; each trial compared different patients, outcomes and follow-up, so compare within a row, not between rows. Percentages are population statistics. 202,473,478,488

Why two kinds of medicine?

When a plaque tears, platelets stick to it, switch on and clump, and the clotting (coagulation) proteins make thrombin and fibrin that bind the clump together. Antiplatelet tablets (aspirin and a P2Y12 inhibitor) act on the first part; injected anticoagulants (heparin, enoxaparin, bivalirudin, fondaparinux) act on the second. The 2023 ESC guideline puts it plainly: “A combination of antiplatelet and anticoagulant therapy is indicated acutely for patients with ACS.” 46

Taking aspirin and a P2Y12 inhibitor together is called dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT). The 2023 ESC default is “DAPT including aspirin and a potent P2Y12 receptor inhibitor (prasugrel or ticagrelor)”, with clopidogrel kept for when those “are contraindicated/not available.” 46 The ACC/AHA 2025 guideline also prefers ticagrelor or prasugrel to clopidogrel after PCI (as summarised by its chair). 356,367 How the artery itself is reopened is covered in Reperfusion: PCI & clot-busting drugs.

Closer looks

Genes

CYP2C19 and clopidogrel

Clopidogrel must be switched on by the liver enzyme CYP2C19. About 2% of White and 4% of Black patients are poor metabolisers, and more Asian patients are (for example 14% of Chinese patients). 131

In TRITON-TIMI 38, clopidogrel-treated carriers of a reduced-function gene had more cardiovascular death, MI or stroke (12.1% vs 8.0%) and about three times the stent thrombosis (2.6% vs 0.8%). 288 In POPular Genetics, choosing the drug by genotype was noninferior and cut bleeding (9.8% vs 12.5%). 64

Atrial fibrillation

When an anticoagulant is needed too (AUGUSTUS)

In AUGUSTUS (4614 people with AF and a recent ACS or PCI, all on a P2Y12 inhibitor), aspirin raised major or clinically relevant bleeding from 9.0% to 16.1%, and apixaban bled less than a vitamin K antagonist (10.5% vs 14.7%). Ischaemic events were similar. 271

ESC 2023: a NOAC plus a single antiplatelet “is recommended as the default strategy for up to 12 months after up to 1 week of TAT”, and “The use of prasugrel or ticagrelor as part of TAT is not recommended.” 46

Stomach

Proton pump inhibitors

In COGENT, omeprazole cut gastrointestinal events on aspirin plus clopidogrel (1.1% vs 2.9%), with no significant change in cardiovascular events (4.9% vs 5.7%), though the authors say this does “not rule out a clinically meaningful difference”. 30 ESC 2023 recommends PPIs for patients on DAPT “at higher-than-average risk of gastrointestinal bleeds”. 46

In the cath lab

Heparin, bivalirudin, fondaparinux

Fondaparinux bled less than enoxaparin in OASIS-5 (2.2% vs 4.1%) 491, but ESC 2023 says it “is not recommended in patients with STEMI undergoing PPCI.” 46 Bivalirudin trials disagree: HEAT-PPCI favoured heparin, MATRIX and VALIDATE-SWEDEHEART were neutral, BRIGHT-4 favoured bivalirudin with a high-dose infusion after PCI. 114,261,402,463 Debated

After year one

Which single drug long term?

HOST-EXAM (clopidogrel vs aspirin, 5.7% vs 7.7% for its composite) and SMART-CHOICE 3 (4.4% vs 6.6% death, MI or stroke at 3 years) both favoured clopidogrel. 59,244 Both were open-label Korean trials, and a 2026 review says they “suggest that P2Y12 monotherapy may be better than aspirin”. 367 Early evidence

Surgery & dentists

Never stop on your own

Stopping a P2Y12 inhibitor early raises the risk of heart attack and stent clotting; the labels give planned stop times before surgery (clopidogrel and ticagrelor 5 days, prasugrel at least 7 days), set by the doctors involved. 121,123,131 In the DAPT trial, stent thrombosis and MI rose in the 3 months after stopping. 283

Bleeding to report: black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, blood in the urine, or bleeding that will not stop.
