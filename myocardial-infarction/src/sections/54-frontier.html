Research toward better treatment

Calming inflammation, lowering Lp(a), protecting heart muscle, combining pills, teaching computers to read the ECG: here is where each idea stands, which trials worked, which did not, and what is still due. Every programme carries an evidence label.

Research, not adviceMost of these treatments are experimental or used only in trials. Nothing here is a reason to start, stop or change a medicine: talk to your own care team. Status checked against trial registries and papers up to 4 November 2026.

A marker appears in the year of a trial's main publication or registry completion date; markers after November 2026 are registry estimates, not results. Stages are a reading aid, not an official classification. Evidence labels follow the research pass. [[NCT05021835,NCT06118281,NCT05581303,NCT03705234,NCT05030428,NCT05665608]]

Read the headline: absolute and relative

A hazard ratio of 0.77 sounds like a 23% cut. What it means depends on how often the event happened at all. Pick a trial to see its primary result as people per 1,000.

Why so many promising ideas fail

Hypothesis

Protecting muscle at reperfusion

Remote ischaemic conditioning with an arm cuff did not reduce cardiac death or heart failure admission in 5,401 people (9.4% vs 8.6%), and cyclosporine before PCI did not help in 970 people with anterior STEMI. No drug or conditioning strategy has yet shown clinical benefit on top of timely PCI. [[Hausenloy2019CONDI2,Cung2015CIRCUS]]

Hypothesis

Rebuilding muscle with cells

A Cochrane review of 41 trials found no change in long-term mortality with bone marrow cells after MI (RR 0.93), and LVEF differences of 2–5% judged not clinically relevant. BAMI, the largest trial, under-recruited and could not test its hypothesis. [[Fisher2015Cochrane,Mathur2020BAMI]]

Early evidence

A better number is not a better outcome

Pelacarsen lowered Lp(a), but Lp(a)HORIZON's topline result showed no reduction in the primary cardiovascular endpoint; full data are awaited. [[Sabouret2026]] Olpasiran nearly abolished Lp(a) in a phase 2 trial, and its outcome trial is not due until 2028. [[ODonoghue2022,NCT05581303]]

Established

Benefit can come with a cost

CANTOS proved that lowering inflammation without lowering lipids cuts MI, stroke or cardiovascular death (HR 0.85), but fatal infections were more common and all-cause mortality did not differ. [[Ridker2017CANTOS]] Early defibrillators cut arrhythmic deaths in DINAMIT and IRIS while other deaths rose, so total mortality did not fall. [[Hohnloser2004DINAMIT,Steinbeck2009IRIS]]

Early evidence

Two good trials can disagree

COLCOT found fewer ischaemic events with colchicine after MI (HR 0.77); the larger, longer CLEAR SYNERGY found none (HR 0.99). Its role early after MI is contested. [[Tardif2019COLCOT,Jolly2025]]

Tested, missed

Preventing heart failure after MI

Empagliflozin (EMPACT-MI), dapagliflozin (DAPA-MI) and sacubitril-valsartan (PARADISE-MI) each missed their main hard endpoint after MI; fewer first heart failure admissions with empagliflozin was a component finding only. These drugs have separate heart failure indications. [[Butler2024EMPACT,James2024DAPAMI,Pfeffer2021PARADISE]]

How to read a trial headline

Primary or secondary?

VEST's wearable defibrillator did not significantly reduce arrhythmic death, its primary outcome (1.6% vs 2.4%; P=0.18). Fewer all-cause deaths (3.1% vs 4.9%) was a secondary outcome with an uncorrected P value, so it cannot rescue the trial. [[Olgin2018VEST]]

A component is not the whole

EMPACT-MI's composite was not reduced (HR 0.90), even though first heart failure admission, one of its parts, was (HR 0.77). [[Butler2024EMPACT]]

Biomarker or outcome?

Ziltivekimab cut CRP by up to 92% in a phase 2 trial. That is a blood test result; whether it prevents heart attacks is what ZEUS and ARTEMIS are for. [[Ridker2021RESCUE,NCT05021835,NCT06118281]]

Accuracy is not benefit

An AI ECG model found occlusion MI with 80.6% sensitivity, against 32.5% for STEMI criteria and 73.0% for expert readers, with lower specificity (93.7% vs 97.7%). No randomised trial has yet shown that AI ECG triage improves outcomes. [[Herman2024,AlZaiti2023]]

Topline or paper?

A press topline comes before peer review. Lp(a)HORIZON is known so far only from a commentary and news; ClinicalTrials.gov lists no posted results. [[Sabouret2026,NCT04023552]]

Who was in it?

LoDoCo2 tested colchicine in chronic coronary disease (HR 0.69), not in the weeks after an MI, so it answers a different question from COLCOT and CLEAR SYNERGY. [[Nidorf2020LoDoCo2,Tardif2019COLCOT,Jolly2025]]

In plain words: researchers are testing medicines that calm inflammation, lower a blood fat called Lp(a), and AI tools that read the heart tracing. Some have worked in trials and some have not. [[Tardif2019COLCOT,Jolly2025,Sabouret2026,Herman2024]]
