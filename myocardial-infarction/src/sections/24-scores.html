Risk scores: HEART, TIMI & GRACE

Emergency teams add up a few findings to sort people with chest pain into lower and higher risk. Watch the HEART score being built item by item, see what each band meant in the studies, then try teaching versions of HEART and TIMI and see what GRACE weighs.

Teaching tool, not for triageThese scores are used by clinicians after an ECG and blood tests, never by a person at home to decide whether to seek care. If you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU).

GRACE: what it weighs

GRACE was built from a large international registry of acute coronary syndromes to predict death, not to sort undiagnosed chest pain. Its in-hospital model uses eight variables, which together carried 89.9% of the prognostic information; its c-statistic was 0.83 in derivation and 0.79 when tested in GUSTO-IIb. [[Granger2003]] Later GRACE models predict death after discharge to 6 months (9 variables, c-statistics 0.81 and 0.75) and death or MI from admission to 6 months (9 predictors including heart failure and peripheral vascular disease; c-statistics 0.81 for death and 0.73 for death or MI). [[Eagle2004,Fox2006]] The point tables and formula are not reproduced here. The 2023 ESC guideline lists a GRACE risk score above 140 as a high-risk criterion favouring an early invasive strategy within 24 hours. [[Byrne2023ESC]] What that strategy involves is in Reperfusion.

Head to head

HEART discriminated best in emergency cohorts

In 1748 Dutch emergency patients, at a sensitivity of at least 98%, HEART identified 381 people as low risk (0.8% of events missed) and GRACE 231 (2.2% missed); TIMI identified none. [[Poldervaart2017IJC]] That fits what each score was built for: HEART for undifferentiated chest pain, TIMI and GRACE for people already diagnosed with an acute coronary syndrome.

Exam hookHEART for the emergency department; TIMI and GRACE were derived in diagnosed ACS. GRACE >140 favours invasive care within 24 h. [[Byrne2023ESC]]

Does using a score change care?

Established

HEART Pathway trial

In 282 patients, HEART plus troponin at 0 and 3 hours cut objective cardiac testing at 30 days (56.7% vs 68.8%) and shortened hospital stay by 12 hours (9.9 vs 21.9 h). [[Mahler2015]]

Established

A stepped-wedge trial in 9 hospitals

In 3648 patients, HEART-guided care was non-inferior for 6-week events, with 2.0% in low-risk patients, but did not reduce use of health-care resources much. Benefit depends on whether clinicians act on the score. [[Poldervaart2017AIM]]

Small print

The HEART age item

The printed table in Six 2008 says "≤65" for 2 points while the Methods text says 65 or older; this page follows the Methods. In the 120-patient derivation, endpoint risk was 2.5%, 20.3% and 72.7% across the three bands. [[Six2008HEART]]

Troponin first

Scores sit beside the blood test

Rapid troponin pathways now rule many people out within 1 to 2 hours, and the T in HEART depends on the assay's normal limit. See Troponin & rapid rule-out and the ECG. [[Byrne2023ESC]]
