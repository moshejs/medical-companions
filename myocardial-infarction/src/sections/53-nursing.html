Nursing care

Nurses are with a person having a heart attack (myocardial infarction, MI) from the first ECG to the discharge talk. Follow one patient's journey and the evidence behind each nursing priority, then practise with an access-site flip set, a bleeding sorter and six priority questions.

Call nowIf you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU).

Schematic journey. Curve shapes are drawn for illustration; the numbers on them are published trial and registry figures [[Yiadom2022,Hofmann2017,Stub2015,Kubica2016,Valgimigli2015MATRIX,Mehta2009APEX,Mehran2013]]. Local protocols decide care; this is not a protocol.

Wrist or groin? An access-site flip set

PCI reaches the heart through an artery at the wrist (radial) or in the groin (femoral). Tap a card to flip it and compare the two. The 2023 ESC guideline lists "Radial artery approach as default vascular access" among strategies to reduce bleeding with PCI. [[Byrne2023ESC]]

Bruise or bleed? A recognition sorter

Teaching toolEach finding comes from a study of patients after catheterisation or PCI. Sort it as common and expected (teach, record, keep watching) or as something to report promptly. Real escalation follows local protocols and the clinical picture, never a sorter.

Priority questions, NCLEX style

Six scenarios. Choose the best nursing action; each answer explains the evidence. Doses and label wording are quoted as published, not as instructions.

Teaching before discharge

GTN

Nitroglycerin, as the label states

The sublingual nitroglycerin label: "Administer one tablet under the tongue or in the buccal pouch at the first sign of an acute anginal attack … One additional tablet may be administered every 5 minutes until relief is obtained. No more than three tablets are recommended within a 15-minute period. If the pain persists after a total of 3 tablets in a 15-minute period, or if the pain is different than is typically experienced, seek prompt medical attention." [[FDA-Nitrostat]]

It also says to take it "at rest, preferably in the sitting position", to keep tablets "in the original glass container" tightly capped, and never with PDE-5 inhibitors such as sildenafil or tadalafil. [[FDA-Nitrostat]]

DAPT

Dual antiplatelet adherence

The ticagrelor label's boxed warning: "If possible, manage bleeding without discontinuing BRILINTA. Stopping BRILINTA increases the risk of subsequent cardiovascular events." Breathlessness affected about 14–21% of patients in the trials, usually mild to moderate. [[FDA-Brilinta]]

In PARIS, unplanned disruption raised the risk of major events (HR 1.50), highest in the first 7 days (HR 7.04); stopping under a doctor's guidance did not (HR 0.63). [[Mehran2013]] In PREMIER, 13.6% had stopped by 30 days, with 11-month mortality 7.5% vs 0.7%. [[Spertus2006]]

Discharge

A discharge checklist that works

  • Medicines: names, purpose, a plan to fill every prescription; counselling was linked to filling them. [[Jackevicius2008]]
  • Rehab referral: people who stopped antiplatelets early were less often referred or given medicine instructions. [[Spertus2006]]
  • Early follow-up: two-thirds of 30-day readmissions happen within 15 days. [[Dharmarajan2013]]
  • Mood and smoking: depression screening is advised by the AHA; quitting is linked to a 36% lower risk of death. [[Lichtman2008,Critchley2003]]
  • Driving: written advice was often missing in UK audits; check local licensing rules. [[Bharaj2021,Dyson2025]]

Discharge programmes with many components that support self-care cut 30-day readmission (RR 0.82). [[Leppin2014]]

Nurse-led

Nurse-led prevention clinics

In a Scottish trial of 1,173 people with coronary heart disease, nurse-led clinics in general practice improved six of eight quality-of-life domains and cut hospital admissions (OR 0.64) at 1 year. [[Campbell1998]] At 4 years, deaths were 14.5% vs 18.9% (HR 0.75). [[Murchie2003]] The nurse-coordinated, family-based EUROACTION programme improved diet, blood pressure and statin prescribing. [[Wood2008EUROACTION]]
