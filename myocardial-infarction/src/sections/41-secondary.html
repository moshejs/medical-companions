Medicines after a heart attack

After a heart attack (myocardial infarction, MI), most people leave hospital with several medicines, each aimed at a different way another heart attack or heart failure can happen. Watch them arrive one by one with the trial behind each, including the recent beta-blocker trials that changed the picture for people whose heart still pumps normally.

Start windows are those used in the trials cited, not instructions. Time axis is stretched (logarithmic) so the first days are visible. Hazard ratios below 1 favour the medicine; each is from a different population and outcome. [[ISIS2_1988,Schwartz2001MIRACL,Pfeffer1992SAVE,Pfeffer2003VALIANT,Munkhaugen2025BETAMI,Butler2024EMPACT]]

Beyond the tablets

Adherence

Many people stop within two years

In US Medicare patients aged 66–75 who filled a high-intensity statin after MI, 58.9% were still taking it with high adherence at 6 months and 41.6% at 2 years. [[Colantonio2017]] In the PREMIER registry, 184 of 1521 people had stopped aspirin, beta-blocker and statin by 1 month, and they had lower 1-year survival (88.5% vs 97.7%; an observational association). [[Ho2006]]

Gaps were larger for African American and Hispanic patients, which points to access and support, not personal failings. Full drug coverage raised adherence by 4–6 points in MI FREEE. [[Colantonio2017,Choudhry2011FREEE]]

Polypill

Three medicines in one tablet (SECURE)

SECURE gave 2499 people within 6 months of MI a polypill (aspirin 100 mg, ramipril 2.5/5/10 mg, atorvastatin 20/40 mg) or usual care. Major cardiovascular events were 9.5% vs 12.7% over a median 36 months (HR 0.76), and patient-reported adherence was higher. [[Castellano2022SECURE]]

Vaccination

A flu jab after a heart attack (IAMI)

In IAMI (2571 people, nearly all shortly after MI), influenza vaccine cut death, MI or stent thrombosis at 12 months from 7.2% to 5.3% (HR 0.72), and all-cause death from 4.9% to 2.9%. [[Frobert2021IAMI]] It stopped early because of COVID-19, so the effect may be overestimated, but it agrees with an earlier meta-analysis (RR 0.45 after a recent ACS). [[Frobert2021IAMI,Udell2013]] Early evidence

Smoking

Quitting is one of the biggest steps

Across 20 cohort studies of people with coronary heart disease, those who quit smoking had a 36% lower risk of death than those who continued (RR 0.64). These are observational data, so some confounding is possible. [[Critchley2003]] Help works: varenicline started in hospital after ACS raised abstinence at 24 weeks to 47.3% from 32.5%. [[Eisenberg2016EVITA]]
