Complications & cardiogenic shock

What can go wrong in the hours, days and weeks after a heart attack (myocardial infarction, MI): dangerous rhythms, heart block, shock, tears in the heart wall, clots, pericarditis and heart failure. Scrub through the first 90 days, then see what the shock trials found.

Call nowIf you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU).

Cardiogenic shock: when the pump cannot keep up

Cardiogenic shock is end-organ hypoperfusion caused by heart failure, often with failure of several organs at once. [[vanDiepen2017]] In the US it affects 40,000 to 50,000 people a year and is the leading cause of in-hospital death after acute MI. [[Samsky2021]] The SCAI scheme stages it from A to E. Defibrillators

Why an ICD is not fitted straight away

In VALIANT, sudden death or resuscitated arrest was most likely in the first 30 days: 1.4% a month, and 2.3% a month with LVEF of 30% or less. [[Solomon2005VALIANT]] Yet two trials of early implantable cardioverter-defibrillators (ICDs), DINAMIT (6–40 days) and IRIS (5–31 days), found no fall in overall mortality: arrhythmic deaths fell but other deaths rose. [[Hohnloser2004DINAMIT,Steinbeck2009IRIS]] Later, in people with a prior MI and LVEF of 0.30 or less, MADIT-II showed lower mortality with an ICD (14.2% vs 19.8%; HR 0.69). [[Moss2002MADIT]]

Wearable defibrillator

VEST: bridging the gap

The VEST authors note that ICDs are contraindicated until 40 to 90 days after MI. In 2,302 people with LVEF of 35% or less, a wearable defibrillator did not significantly lower arrhythmic death at 90 days (1.6% vs 2.4%). All-cause death was 3.1% vs 4.9%, a secondary outcome. Median wear time was 18 hours a day, and only 12 of the 48 people in the device group who died were wearing it. [[Olgin2018VEST]]

Early evidence

Is the ICD benefit shrinking?

In 32,214 people with LVEF of 35% or less after MI (observational data), the mortality reduction linked to primary-prevention ICDs fell from HR 0.54 (1995–2004) to 0.89, not significant, in 2015–2020. [[SepehriShamloo2026]] The randomised PROFID EHRA trial is testing optimal medical therapy without an ICD against therapy with one, with estimated primary completion in November 2027. [[Dagres2026PROFID,NCT05665608]]

Long-term outlook

Survival has improved

In Sweden (105,674 people with STEMI), 1-year mortality fell from 22.1% in 1995 to 14.1% in 2014, as primary PCI rose from 4.5% to 78.0% and statin use from 14.1% to 93.6%. Gains levelled off from about 2008. [[Szummer2017STEMI]] In Framingham, 5-year mortality after a first MI fell from 41.1% (1970s) to 17.3% (1990s). [[Velagaleti2008]] These are population statistics: they cannot predict what will happen to one person.

After discharge

Events and readmission

Among 97,254 Swedish MI survivors, 18.3% had a non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke or cardiovascular death in the first year, and 20.0% of those event-free at 1 year had one in the next 36 months. Heart failure and no revascularisation raised that risk. [[Jernberg2015]] In US Medicare, 19.9% were readmitted within 30 days, at a median of 10 days, and only 10.0% of readmissions were for another MI. [[Dharmarajan2013]] Recovery support is covered in Cardiac rehabilitation.

Heart failure

Living longer, with a weaker heart

As more people survive the MI itself, more live on with heart failure: in Framingham, 5-year heart-failure incidence rose from 27.6% to 31.9% while mortality fell. [[Velagaleti2008]] In Olmsted County, 5-year survival with post-MI heart failure improved from 54% to 61%, against 82% without it (2001–2010). [[Gerber2016]] Medicines that protect the heart are in Medicines after a heart attack.
