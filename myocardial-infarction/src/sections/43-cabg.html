Stents or bypass? Revascularisation choices

Narrowed coronary arteries can be opened from the inside with a balloon and stent (percutaneous coronary intervention, PCI) or bypassed with a graft in heart surgery (coronary artery bypass grafting, CABG). Step from one blocked artery to three, to the left main, to diabetes, and watch what the trials found.

Bars are the published percentages from each trial; each trial measured its own endpoint over its own follow-up, so compare the two arms within a row, not rows with each other. The heart team: which evidence speaks to which factor?

Even the big trials disagree in places: for left main disease, EXCEL found no significant difference in its composite while NOBLE favoured CABG. [[Stone2019EXCEL,Holm2020NOBLE]] That is one reason the choice is made by a heart team with the patient. Stable disease is different

ISCHEMIA: not a heart attack

ISCHEMIA enrolled 5,179 people with stable coronary disease and moderate or severe ischaemia. An initial invasive strategy did not reduce ischaemic events or death over a median 3.2 years compared with medicines first: 5-year event rates 16.4% vs 18.2%, and deaths 145 vs 144. Early events were higher with the invasive strategy (5.3% vs 3.4% at 6 months), largely from procedure-related MI. [[Maron2020ISCHEMIA]] It studied stable patients, so its result does not apply to STEMI or high-risk NSTE-ACS, where trials show benefit from timely reperfusion or invasive care. [[Maron2020ISCHEMIA,Keeley2003,FRISCII1999]]

After surgery

Recovery after bypass

In New York State, in-hospital mortality was lower the later CABG was done after an acute MI: 11.8% within 6 hours, 9.5% at 6 hours to 1 day and 2.8% after 1 day; the authors suggested that "waiting in some may be warranted" (observational data). [[Lee2001CABGtiming]] Cardiac rehabilitation reaches people after bypass far more often than after an MI without a procedure: 55.3% vs 7.1% of eligible US Medicare patients. [[Ritchey2020]] See Cardiac rehabilitation.

Labels, as published

Blood thinners and surgery

The BRILINTA (ticagrelor) boxed warning states: "Do not start BRILINTA in patients undergoing urgent coronary artery bypass graft surgery (CABG)." [[FDA-Brilinta]] The Plavix (clopidogrel) label states: "Discontinue 5 days prior to elective surgery that has a major risk of bleeding." [[FDA-Plavix]] The Effient (prasugrel) label states: "When possible, discontinue Effient at least 7 days prior to any surgery"; CABG-related TIMI major or minor bleeding was 14.1% with prasugrel vs 4.5% with clopidogrel. [[FDA-Effient]] These are quoted, not instructions: never stop these medicines without the prescribing team. More in Antiplatelets & anticoagulants.

Grafts

One mammary graft or two?

A graft from the internal mammary (internal thoracic) artery to the LAD is the graft linked to long-term survival: patients with vein grafts only had 1.61 times the risk of death over 10 years. [[Loop1986]] Adding a second mammary graft did not help further in ART: 10-year death 20.3% with two vs 21.2% with one (HR 0.96). [[Taggart2019ART]]

Emergencies

When surgery is urgent

ESC 2023: "Emergency coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) surgery should be considered for patients" in whom PCI is not feasible or has failed (sentence quoted in part). [[Byrne2023ESC]] In the SHOCK trial, people with cardiogenic shock in the early revascularisation group had angioplasty in 55% and CABG in 38%. [[Hochman2001SHOCK1y]] More in Complications & cardiogenic shock.

Several arteries during a heart attack

Fixing the other narrowings

In COMPLETE (4,041 people with STEMI and multivessel disease after successful culprit PCI), complete revascularisation reduced cardiovascular death or MI at a median 3 years (7.8% vs 10.5%; HR 0.74), whether the other PCI was during or after the hospital stay. [[Mehta2019COMPLETE]] In cardiogenic shock the opposite held: culprit-only PCI first (CULPRIT-SHOCK). [[Thiele2017CULPRIT]] How the artery is first reopened is in Reperfusion.
