Troponin & rapid rule-out

Troponin is a protein inside heart muscle cells. When they are damaged it leaks into the blood, where a test can measure it. Watch one illustrative curve rise and fall over a week, switch between assays and thresholds, then see how the ESC 0/1-hour algorithm sorts patients in the emergency department.

Call nowIf you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU). Do not wait for a blood test or to see if symptoms pass.

Injury or infarction? The UDMI4 categories

Teaching toolThis explainer walks through the published definitions in the Fourth Universal Definition of Myocardial Infarction (UDMI4). It is not a diagnosis and must never be used for self-triage or to decide whether to seek care. Clinicians adjudicate these categories with the full clinical picture.

Evidence of acute myocardial ischaemia (tick any)

Paths follow the published UDMI4 definitions; type 3 MI (death before troponin can be measured) is described in the note. [[Thygesen2018UDMI4C,Domienik2021UDMI4]]

Raised troponin without a heart attack

A single raised value is not an MI. [[Byrne2023ESC]] Many conditions damage heart muscle; without clinical evidence of acute ischaemia they are called myocardial injury, not MI. [[Domienik2021UDMI4]] In 372,310 South Australian emergency episodes tested with high-sensitivity troponin T, 5.12% were acute MI, 1.86% acute injury with coronary artery disease, 8.66% acute injury without it, 14.79% chronic injury and 69.58% no injury. [[Chuang2021Injury]]

What the research says

Established

Why serial samples

UDMI4 advises sampling at presentation (0 h) and again "3–6 h later, or earlier with hs-cTn assays"; people who arrive more than 12–18 h after onset may take longer to show a changing pattern. The ESC recommends the 0 h/1 h algorithm as the "best option" and 0 h/2 h as the "second-best option". [[Thygesen2018UDMI4C,Byrne2023ESC]]

Established

Single-sample rule-out, with a time condition

Very low values at presentation gave NPVs around 99.5%: hs-cTnI <5 ng/L applied to 49% of 22,457 patients (NPV 99.5%), and to 61% of those without MI in High-STEACS (NPV 99.6%). [[Chapman2017,Shah2015]] An hs-cTnT below the limit of detection plus a non-ischaemic ECG classed 30.6% as low risk (sensitivity 98.7%). [[Pickering2017]] The ESC limits the very-low single 0 h rule-out to chest pain that began more than 3 h before the sample. [[Byrne2023ESC]]

Debate

Sex-specific thresholds

UDMI4 recommends sex-specific 99th percentiles for hs-cTn assays, because values are lower in women; age-specific cut-offs are not recommended. [[Thygesen2018UDMI4C]] In one study, 16 ng/L for women and 34 ng/L for men increased diagnoses of myocardial injury by 42% in women and 6% in men, but afterwards women with injury were less often revascularised (15% vs 34%) or given dual antiplatelet therapy (26% vs 43%). [[Lee2019]] High-STEACS implementation did not lower 1-year MI or cardiovascular death among those reclassified (adjusted OR 1.10). [[Shah2018]]

Established

Type 2 MI and injury are not benign

Among 2,122 Edinburgh patients with raised troponin I, 55.2% had type 1 MI, 20.2% type 2 MI and 24.6% myocardial injury. Five-year death was 36.7% after type 1 MI, 62.5% after type 2 and 72.4% after injury, mostly from non-cardiovascular causes. These are population figures, not predictions. [[Chapman2018Type2]] No treatment strategy has been proven for type 2 MI and injury. [[DeFilippis2019Type2]]

Established

Sensitive assays find MI earlier

In 718 emergency patients, sensitive assays at presentation had an AUC of 0.95–0.96 against 0.90 for a standard assay, and 0.92–0.94 vs 0.76 in those arriving within 3 h. [[Reichlin2009]] The ESC says CK-MB, myosin-binding protein C and copeptin add only limited value on top of troponin in most situations. [[Byrne2023ESC]]

Early evidence

Faster pathways

In COMPASS-MI (22,651 patients), hs-cTnI <6 ng/L at presentation plus a change <4 ng/L after 45–120 min gave an NPV of 99.5% and classed 56.5% as low risk. [[Neumann2019]] In BACC, hs-TnI <3 ng/L with a low-risk ECG ruled out 28% with an NPV of 100%. [[Neumann2017]] Risk scores used alongside troponin are covered in HEART, TIMI & GRACE.
