Part 3 · Emergency treatment

Antiplatelets & anticoagulants

A heart attack (myocardial infarction, MI) is usually a clot on a torn plaque. Medicines that calm platelets and slow clotting stop that clot growing, but every one of them also makes bleeding more likely. Watch how aspirin and the P2Y12 blockers work, then see the trials that weighed fewer heart attacks against more bleeding.

Platelet panel is schematic. Bars are the published trial percentages; each trial compared different patients, outcomes and follow-up, so compare within a row, not between rows. Percentages are population statistics. [[ISIS2_1988,Yusuf2001CURE,Wiviott2007TRITON,Wallentin2009PLATO]]

Why two kinds of medicine?

When a plaque tears, platelets stick to it, switch on and clump, and the clotting (coagulation) proteins make thrombin and fibrin that bind the clump together. Antiplatelet tablets (aspirin and a P2Y12 inhibitor) act on the first part; injected anticoagulants (heparin, enoxaparin, bivalirudin, fondaparinux) act on the second. The 2023 ESC guideline says people with ACS should get both kinds of drug, antiplatelet and anticoagulant, in the acute phase. [[Byrne2023ESC]]

Taking aspirin and a P2Y12 inhibitor together is called dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT). The 2023 ESC default pairs aspirin with one of the stronger P2Y12 inhibitors (prasugrel or ticagrelor), and keeps clopidogrel for people who cannot take those or cannot get them. [[Byrne2023ESC]] The ACC/AHA 2025 guideline also prefers ticagrelor or prasugrel to clopidogrel after PCI (as summarised by its chair). [[Restrepo2026,Rao2025ACC]] How the artery itself is reopened is covered in Reperfusion: PCI & clot-busting drugs.

Closer looks

Genes

CYP2C19 and clopidogrel

Clopidogrel must be switched on by the liver enzyme CYP2C19. About 2% of White and 4% of Black patients are poor metabolisers, and more Asian patients are (for example 14% of Chinese patients). [[FDA-Plavix]]

In TRITON-TIMI 38, clopidogrel-treated carriers of a reduced-function gene had more cardiovascular death, MI or stroke (12.1% vs 8.0%) and about three times the stent thrombosis (2.6% vs 0.8%). [[Mega2009CYP2C19]] In POPular Genetics, choosing the drug by genotype was noninferior and cut bleeding (9.8% vs 12.5%). [[Claassens2019POPGEN]]

Atrial fibrillation

When an anticoagulant is needed too (AUGUSTUS)

In AUGUSTUS (4614 people with AF and a recent ACS or PCI, all on a P2Y12 inhibitor), aspirin raised major or clinically relevant bleeding from 9.0% to 16.1%, and apixaban bled less than a vitamin K antagonist (10.5% vs 14.7%). Ischaemic events were similar. [[Lopes2019AUGUSTUS]]

ESC 2023: after up to 1 week of triple therapy (TAT), the default for up to 12 months is a NOAC plus one antiplatelet drug, and prasugrel or ticagrelor should not be part of triple therapy. [[Byrne2023ESC]]

Stomach

Proton pump inhibitors

In COGENT, omeprazole cut gastrointestinal events on aspirin plus clopidogrel (1.1% vs 2.9%), with no significant change in cardiovascular events (4.9% vs 5.7%), though the authors say this does “not rule out a clinically meaningful difference”. [[Bhatt2010COGENT]] ESC 2023 recommends PPIs for people on DAPT whose risk of stomach or gut bleeding is above average. [[Byrne2023ESC]]

In the cath lab

Heparin, bivalirudin, fondaparinux

Fondaparinux bled less than enoxaparin in OASIS-5 (2.2% vs 4.1%) [[Yusuf2006OASIS5]], but ESC 2023 says it “is not recommended in patients with STEMI undergoing PPCI.” [[Byrne2023ESC]] Bivalirudin trials disagree: HEAT-PPCI favoured heparin, MATRIX and VALIDATE-SWEDEHEART were neutral, BRIGHT-4 favoured bivalirudin with a high-dose infusion after PCI. [[Shahzad2014HEAT,Valgimigli2015MATRIXE,Erlinge2017VALIDATE,Li2022BRIGHT4]] Debated

After year one

Which single drug long term?

HOST-EXAM (clopidogrel vs aspirin, 5.7% vs 7.7% for its composite) and SMART-CHOICE 3 (4.4% vs 6.6% death, MI or stroke at 3 years) both favoured clopidogrel. [[Koo2021HOSTEXAM,Choi2025SMARTCHOICE3]] Both were open-label Korean trials, and a 2026 review says they “suggest that P2Y12 monotherapy may be better than aspirin”. [[Restrepo2026]] Early evidence

Surgery & dentists

Never stop on your own

Stopping a P2Y12 inhibitor early raises the risk of heart attack and stent clotting; the labels give planned stop times before surgery (clopidogrel and ticagrelor 5 days, prasugrel at least 7 days), set by the doctors involved. [[FDA-Plavix,FDA-Brilinta,FDA-Effient]] In the DAPT trial, stent thrombosis and MI rose in the 3 months after stopping. [[Mauri2014DAPT]]

Bleeding to report: black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, blood in the urine, or bleeding that will not stop.

Call nowIf you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU).
