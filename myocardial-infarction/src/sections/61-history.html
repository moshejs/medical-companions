The people behind the ideas

The first account

William Heberden

Gave the first account of classic angina pectoris; Prinzmetal's report of variant angina came nearly two centuries later. [[Sternbach1991Heberden]]

1903

Willem Einthoven

Published galvanometric recording of the human electrocardiogram in Pflügers Archiv. [[Einthoven1903]]

1912

James B. Herrick

Described the clinical features of sudden obstruction of the coronary arteries in JAMA: the clinical description of acute MI. [[Herrick1912,Braunwald2012History]]

1920

Harold E. B. Pardee

Published "An electrocardiographic sign of coronary artery obstruction" in Archives of Internal Medicine. [[Pardee1920]]

1948–1961

The Framingham investigators

The study examined its first volunteer in 1948, its origins tied to President Roosevelt's death in 1945. Dawber and colleagues described its approach in 1951; Kannel and colleagues' "Factors of risk" report followed in 1961. [[Mahmood2014Framingham,Dawber1951Framingham,Kannel1961Framingham]]

1961

Desmond Julian

His Lancet paper on treating cardiac arrest in acute myocardial ischaemia and infarction began the coronary care unit era. [[Julian1961CCU,Braunwald2012History]]

1978–1979

Andreas Grüntzig

Reported transluminal dilatation of a coronary stenosis in a 1978 Lancet letter. In his 1979 series of 50 patients the procedure succeeded in 32, reducing stenosis from a mean of 84% to 34%. [[Gruntzig1978Lancet,Gruntzig1979NEJM]]

1980

DeWood and colleagues

Found total occlusion in 87% of patients studied within 4 hours of transmural MI, and retrieved thrombus by catheter in 52 of 59 patients whose angiograms suggested it, which established thrombosis as the cause. [[DeWood1980]]

1989–1991

Katus and colleagues

Described an immunoassay for cardiac troponin T, raised in all 50 patients with transmural AMI, then showed it in serum within 3 hours of pain in 50% of patients, staying raised for more than 130 hours. [[Katus1989TnT,Katus1991TnT]]

2000–2018

Thygesen, Alpert and colleagues

Authored the 2000 ESC/ACC redefinition around troponin and the universal definitions of 2007, 2012 and 2018, which introduced and refined the five types. [[Alpert2000Redefined,Thygesen2007UDMI,Thygesen2012UDMI3,Thygesen2018UDMI4]]
