Heart attack, explained

%HOMELEDE%

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Call nowIf you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU). Do not wait to see if it passes, and do not use this guide to decide.

Schematic, not to scale.

For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. ECG criteria, troponin algorithms and the HEART, TIMI and GRACE calculators are teaching aids, quoted as published. Finish with flashcards, memory aids and a quiz.

For patients, families and caregivers

Look for the In plain words boxes. Heart attacks are common and treatable, and the recovery modules show, with sources, what rehabilitation and the first year can look like. Survival figures are population statistics, not predictions for one person. Bring questions to your own care team: the app reports what labels, guidelines and trials say, not what you should take or change.

How sure is each claim?

Research claims carry a label: Established Early evidence Hypothesis. Small numbers link to the Sources list.
