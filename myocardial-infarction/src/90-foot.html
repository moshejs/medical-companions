Heart Attack Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on heart attack (myocardial infarction) and acute coronary syndromes, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people who have had a heart attack, their families and caregivers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Sepsis Companion, which covers cardiogenic shock alongside septic and the other types of shock, and the Stroke Companion, on the same arterial disease in the brain, and the Obesity Companion, on one of the main causes of the coronary disease behind heart attacks and on the weight-loss medicines now tested in cardiovascular trials.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
