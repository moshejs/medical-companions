## Findings

### frontier · LIBREXIA-ACS: milvexian after an acute coronary syndrome (catch-up)
- LIBREXIA-ACS was a phase 3, randomised, placebo-controlled trial of milvexian, an oral factor XIa inhibitor, 25 mg twice daily, added to standard antiplatelet therapy within 7 days after an acute coronary syndrome event. Dose quoted as published. [[Gibson2026LIBREXIA]]
- The trial was terminated for futility after a planned interim analysis based on 556 adjudicated efficacy end points. 14,194 people had been enrolled (7094 milvexian, 7100 placebo). [[Gibson2026LIBREXIA]]
- After a median follow-up of 12.2 months, cardiovascular death, MI or ischaemic stroke had occurred in 384 people (5.4%) with milvexian and 365 (5.1%) with placebo: HR 1.05 (95% CI 0.91–1.21; P = 0.50). (Hypothesis: factor XIa inhibition has not been shown to prevent events after an acute coronary syndrome) [[Gibson2026LIBREXIA]]
- The principal safety outcome, BARC type 3c or 5 bleeding (intracranial bleeding, intraocular bleeding that compromises vision, or fatal bleeding), occurred in 23 people (0.3%) with milvexian and 22 (0.3%) with placebo (P = 0.88). [[Gibson2026LIBREXIA]]
- The paper was published online on 2026-08-29 and in print on 2026-10-22, before this window. The site had no mention of factor XIa inhibitors; the trial was added to the frontier trial table as a completed neutral trial.

### frontier · ARTEMIS enrolment is now an actual figure

Status is still Active, not recruiting, with estimated primary completion 2027-01-29 and no results posted. [[NCT06118281]]

### Exam hooks
- LIBREXIA-ACS: milvexian (factor XIa inhibitor) plus antiplatelet therapy after ACS, stopped for futility; 5.4% vs 5.1%, HR 1.05; BARC 3c or 5 bleeding 0.3% vs 0.3%. [[Gibson2026LIBREXIA]]

### In plain words
- A new kind of blood thinner, milvexian, was added to the usual antiplatelet tablets after a heart attack or unstable angina. It did not prevent more heart attacks, strokes or deaths than a dummy tablet, and it did not cause more of the most dangerous bleeds.
