# U · Update 2026-11-13

Pass date: 2026-11-13. Window: published or announced 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13 (PubMed entry dates 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-13). This window includes the AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 (6 to 9 November). Modules served: frontier, lipids, cabg, call.

## Findings

### frontier, lipids · Lp(a)HORIZON: pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) but not cardiovascular events
- Lp(a)HORIZON was an international, randomised, placebo-controlled trial in people with pre-existing cardiovascular disease and Lp(a) of at least 70 mg/dL. Pelacarsen 80 mg was given under the skin monthly. 8350 people were randomised and 8322 were in the efficacy analysis (4157 pelacarsen, 4165 placebo); mean age 59.7 years, 27.0% women, median baseline Lp(a) 236 nmol/L. [[Cho2026HORIZON]]
- At 12 months Lp(a) was 64.4 nmol/L with pelacarsen and 233.4 nmol/L with placebo. [[Cho2026HORIZON]]
- Over a median 4.7 years, the primary endpoint (cardiovascular death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke or urgent coronary revascularisation) occurred in 511 people (12.3%) with pelacarsen and 503 (12.1%) with placebo: HR 1.02 (95% CI 0.90–1.15; P = 0.76). In people with baseline Lp(a) of at least 90 mg/dL it was 12.3% vs 12.3% (HR 1.00, 95% CI 0.87–1.15). (Early evidence for Lp(a) lowering as a treatment: one neutral outcome trial) [[Cho2026HORIZON]]
- Serious adverse events: 37.3% with pelacarsen and 41.1% with placebo. [[Cho2026HORIZON]]
- This replaces the topline-only statement on the site, which rested on a commentary [[Sabouret2026]]. The registry record still lists no posted results [[NCT04023552]].

### frontier · ZEUS: ziltivekimab lowered inflammation markers but not cardiovascular events
- ZEUS was a multinational, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled trial in adults with established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, chronic kidney disease and high-sensitivity CRP of at least 2 mg/L. Ziltivekimab 15 mg was given under the skin once monthly. The full analysis population was 6376 (3184 ziltivekimab, 3192 placebo); mean age 69.4 years, 27.5% women. [[Ridker2026ZEUS]]
- At 24 months, the between-group difference in median percent change was -76 percentage points for free interleukin-6 and -64 percentage points for high-sensitivity CRP. [[Ridker2026ZEUS]]
- Over a median 33 months, 3-point MACE (cardiovascular death, non-fatal MI or non-fatal stroke) occurred in 561 people (17.6%) with ziltivekimab and 565 (17.7%) with placebo: HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.88–1.11; P = 0.84). (Hypothesis: IL-6 inhibition has not been shown to prevent events) [[Ridker2026ZEUS]]
- Serious adverse events were 54.6% vs 54.8%; serious infections were 20.4% vs 17.9%. [[Ridker2026ZEUS]]
- ARTEMIS, the ziltivekimab trial in acute MI, is still active with primary completion listed as January 2027 [[NCT06118281]].

### cabg · ISCHEMIA-EXTEND: long-term survival in stable coronary disease
- Extended follow-up of the 5179 ISCHEMIA participants (stable coronary disease with moderate or severe ischaemia) to a median of 9 years recorded 1071 deaths. All-cause mortality at 11 years was 25.8% with an initial invasive strategy and 26.9% with an initial conservative strategy (difference -1.0 percentage point, 95% CI -4.2 to 2.1; adjusted HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.86–1.09). [[Hochman2026EXTEND]]
- Cardiovascular mortality at 11 years was 15.8% vs 17.1% (adjusted HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.75–1.02); non-cardiovascular mortality was 9.1% vs 8.1% (adjusted HR 1.18, 95% CI 0.96–1.45). [[Hochman2026EXTEND]]
- This extends the result already on the site [[Maron2020ISCHEMIA]]. It studied stable disease, not heart attack.

### call · RACE-CARS: a community and EMS programme for cardiac arrest
- RACE-CARS was a registry-based, cluster-randomised trial in North Carolina counties. The intervention had four parts: training 911 telecommunicators to recognise cardiac arrest quickly, training them to give CPR instructions to bystanders, community CPR training, and wider use of automated external defibrillators by first responders or bystanders before EMS arrival. [[MaltaHansen2026]]
- From 1 July 2022 to 30 June 2026, 28 clusters with 11,615 patients were assigned to the intervention and 27 clusters with 10,844 patients to usual care. Median age was 64.0 years and 36.7% were women. [[MaltaHansen2026]]
- Survival to hospital discharge with a good neurological outcome (Cerebral Performance Category 1 or 2) was 9.0% (1048 of 11,608) vs 9.4% (1017 of 10,839): adjusted OR 0.97 (95% CI 0.75–1.26; P = 0.80). [[MaltaHansen2026]]
- The trial tested a strategy for rolling these steps out across counties. It did not test whether CPR or defibrillation helps the person who receives them; the observational studies on the site address that [[HasselqvistAx2015,Weisfeldt2010,Kragholm2017]].

### antithrombotic, complications · DAPT-SHOCK-AMI (recorded, not added to the site)
- Double-blind trial at 29 hospitals in five European countries: 605 people with acute MI and cardiogenic shock were assigned to intravenous cangrelor (298) or crushed ticagrelor 180 mg (307). [[Motovska2026]]
- Platelet reactivity index below 50% at the end of primary PCI, in the subset tested: 56 of 56 (100%) with cangrelor vs 15 of 68 (22%) with ticagrelor (p < 0.0001). [[Motovska2026]]
- Death, MI or ischaemic stroke at 30 days: 112 of 298 (38%) vs 126 of 307 (41%); non-inferiority was not demonstrated (difference -4 percentage points, 90% CI -10 to 3; P for non-inferiority = 0.13). Major bleeding at 30 days was 6% vs 5%. (Early evidence) [[Motovska2026]]

### Exam hooks
- Lp(a)HORIZON: pelacarsen cut Lp(a) (64.4 vs 233.4 nmol/L at 12 months) but MACE was 12.3% vs 12.1%, HR 1.02. [[Cho2026HORIZON]]
- ZEUS: ziltivekimab in ASCVD with CKD and hsCRP ≥2 mg/L, MACE 17.6% vs 17.7%, HR 0.99; serious infections 20.4% vs 17.9%. [[Ridker2026ZEUS]]
- ISCHEMIA-EXTEND: 11-year all-cause mortality 25.8% vs 26.9% in stable coronary disease. [[Hochman2026EXTEND]]

### In plain words
- A medicine that lowers a blood fat called Lp(a) did lower it, but people taking it had just as many heart attacks and strokes as people taking a dummy injection.
- A medicine that blocks an inflammation signal called IL-6 lowered inflammation in blood tests, but it did not prevent heart attacks or strokes in its first large trial.
- A large programme to train dispatchers and communities in CPR did not raise survival across whole counties. CPR and defibrillators still matter for the person whose heart has stopped.

## Open questions and controversies
- Lp(a) as a treatment target: pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) without lowering events [[Cho2026HORIZON]]. OCEAN(a)-Outcomes (olpasiran) is due around 2028 [[NCT05581303]]. Status: open.
- IL-6 inhibition: neutral in chronic disease with CKD [[Ridker2026ZEUS]]; untested result in acute MI until ARTEMIS reports [[NCT06118281]]. Status: open.
