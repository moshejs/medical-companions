Research toward better treatment

Calming inflammation, lowering Lp(a), protecting heart muscle, combining pills, teaching computers to read the ECG: here is where each idea stands, which trials worked, which did not, and what is still due. Every programme carries an evidence label.

Research, not adviceMost of these treatments are experimental or used only in trials. Nothing here is a reason to start, stop or change a medicine: talk to your own care team. Status checked against trial registries and papers up to 4 November 2026.

Step 4 · 2025 to November 2026

Colchicine contested, Lp(a) setback

CLEAR SYNERGY gave colchicine to 7,062 people soon after MI and found no difference over a median 3 years: 9.1% versus 9.3% (HR 0.99). Its role early after MI is now contested. Early evidence 216 The 2025 ACC/AHA guideline lists inclisiran among non-statin options for lowering LDL cholesterol, as summarised in two 2026 papers; its outcome trials are still running. 165,356,360,479 Lp(a)HORIZON's September 2026 topline showed that pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) but did not reduce the primary endpoint; full data are awaited. 385 ZEUS, the ziltivekimab outcome trial, is listed as completed in June 2026 with no results posted. In plain wordsA second, bigger colchicine trial did not find a benefit, so experts now disagree. A medicine that lowers a fat called Lp(a) did not reduce heart events in its first big trial.

Read the headline: absolute and relative

A hazard ratio of 0.77 sounds like a 23% cut. What it means depends on how often the event happened at all. Pick a trial to see its primary result as people per 1,000.

COLCOT (colchicine after MI): ischaemic cardiovascular events, 5.5% with treatment versus 7.1% with placebo over median 22.6 months (HR 0.77). 436 Dot positions are illustrative; each grid is 1,000 people scaled from the trial's percentages.

Teaching toolCrude percentages from each trial's abstract, scaled to 1,000 people. It shows how to read a result, not what a treatment would do for you.

Reading the headline

A real effect, in absolute terms

The relative figure (23%) sounds large; the absolute difference is 16 people per 1,000 over median 22.6 months. Both are true. Absolute numbers depend on how common the event is in the people studied, so they change from one group to another. 436

In plain words"23% fewer" and "16 fewer people in every 1,000" describe the same result. Ask for both.
Exam hookAbsolute risk reduction = control rate − treatment rate; NNT = 1 / ARR (for the trial's follow-up). 436

Why so many promising ideas fail

Hypothesis

Protecting muscle at reperfusion

Remote ischaemic conditioning with an arm cuff did not reduce cardiac death or heart failure admission in 5,401 people (9.4% vs 8.6%), and cyclosporine before PCI did not help in 970 people with anterior STEMI. No drug or conditioning strategy has yet shown clinical benefit on top of timely PCI. 80,183

Hypothesis

Rebuilding muscle with cells

A Cochrane review of 41 trials found no change in long-term mortality with bone marrow cells after MI (RR 0.93), and LVEF differences of 2–5% judged not clinically relevant. BAMI, the largest trial, under-recruited and could not test its hypothesis. 140,282

Early evidence

A better number is not a better outcome

Pelacarsen lowered Lp(a), but Lp(a)HORIZON's topline result showed no reduction in the primary cardiovascular endpoint; full data are awaited. 385 Olpasiran nearly abolished Lp(a) in a phase 2 trial, and its outcome trial is not due until 2028. 324,333

Established

Benefit can come with a cost

CANTOS proved that lowering inflammation without lowering lipids cuts MI, stroke or cardiovascular death (HR 0.85), but fatal infections were more common and all-cause mortality did not differ. 370 Early defibrillators cut arrhythmic deaths in DINAMIT and IRIS while other deaths rose, so total mortality did not fall. 195,421

Early evidence

Two good trials can disagree

COLCOT found fewer ischaemic events with colchicine after MI (HR 0.77); the larger, longer CLEAR SYNERGY found none (HR 0.99). Its role early after MI is contested. 216,436

Tested, missed

Preventing heart failure after MI

Empagliflozin (EMPACT-MI), dapagliflozin (DAPA-MI) and sacubitril-valsartan (PARADISE-MI) each missed their main hard endpoint after MI; fewer first heart failure admissions with empagliflozin was a component finding only. These drugs have separate heart failure indications. 45,208,344

How to read a trial headline

Primary or secondary?

VEST's wearable defibrillator did not significantly reduce arrhythmic death, its primary outcome (1.6% vs 2.4%; P=0.18). Fewer all-cause deaths (3.1% vs 4.9%) was a secondary outcome with an uncorrected P value, so it cannot rescue the trial. 335

A component is not the whole

EMPACT-MI's composite was not reduced (HR 0.90), even though first heart failure admission, one of its parts, was (HR 0.77). 45

Biomarker or outcome?

Ziltivekimab cut CRP by up to 92% in a phase 2 trial. That is a blood test result; whether it prevents heart attacks is what ZEUS and ARTEMIS are for. 322,326,371

Accuracy is not benefit

An AI ECG model found occlusion MI with 80.6% sensitivity, against 32.5% for STEMI criteria and 73.0% for expert readers, with lower specificity (93.7% vs 97.7%). No randomised trial has yet shown that AI ECG triage improves outcomes. 3,186

Topline or paper?

A press topline comes before peer review. Lp(a)HORIZON is known so far only from a commentary and news; ClinicalTrials.gov lists no posted results. 321,385

Who was in it?

LoDoCo2 tested colchicine in chronic coronary disease (HR 0.69), not in the weeks after an MI, so it answers a different question from COLCOT and CLEAR SYNERGY. 216,330,436

In plain words: researchers are testing medicines that calm inflammation, lower a blood fat called Lp(a), and AI tools that read the heart tracing. Some have worked in trials and some have not. 186,216,385,436
