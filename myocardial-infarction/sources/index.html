An animated, cited guide for students, people who have had a heart attack, and their families and caregivers. Educational only, not medical advice. Your place is saved on this device.

Not medical adviceThe Heart Attack Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, pharmacist, paramedic or other clinician. ECG criteria, troponin algorithms, the HEART, TIMI and GRACE scores and the risk calculators are teaching aids, never for self-triage or for deciding whether to seek care, and doses, aspirin wording, CPR steps and guideline statements are quoted as published, never as instructions. Survival and mortality figures are population statistics, not predictions for one person. If you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU).

Part 6 · Context & review

Sources

Every reference behind the Heart Attack Companion: journal articles, Cochrane and other systematic reviews, guidelines, drug labels and trial registrations. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Eight research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology, burden, types and history; atherosclerosis, plaque rupture, thrombosis, ischaemia and risk factors; presentation, ECG, troponin, risk scores and the differential diagnosis; the first minutes, emergency services and reperfusion; antithrombotic therapy; complications, cardiogenic shock, prognosis and research; secondary prevention medicines and LDL lowering; and cardiac rehabilitation, living after a heart attack and nursing care. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked not matched in a research database are pages with no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are not cited for any fact in this guide.

Heart Attack Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on heart attack (myocardial infarction) and acute coronary syndromes, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people who have had a heart attack, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine or a care plan without your own care team. ECG criteria, troponin algorithms, the HEART, TIMI and GRACE scores and the risk calculators are for learning, never for self-triage or for deciding whether to seek care. Doses, aspirin wording, CPR steps and guideline statements are quoted as published in labels and guidelines, never as instructions, and survival and mortality figures are population statistics. If you think you or someone else is having a heart attack, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU). Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
