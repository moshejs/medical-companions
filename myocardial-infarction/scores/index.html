Risk scores: HEART, TIMI & GRACE

Emergency teams add up a few findings to sort people with chest pain into lower and higher risk. Watch the HEART score being built item by item, see what each band meant in the studies, then try teaching versions of HEART and TIMI and see what GRACE weighs.

H · 0–2 points

History

HEART stands for History, ECG, Age, Risk factors and Troponin; each scores 0, 1 or 2, for a total of 0 to 10. History scores 0 if the story is slightly suspicious for an acute coronary syndrome, 1 if moderately and 2 if highly suspicious. 407

In plain wordsThe first item is the doctor's judgement of how much the story sounds like a heart problem.

Teaching tool, not for triageThe TIMI risk score was built from trial patients already diagnosed with unstable angina or NSTEMI. Its seven items each score one point, as published in Antman 2000. 9

TIMI 0 of 7

4.7%

In the TIMI 11B test cohort, the 14-day rate of death, MI or urgent revascularisation was 4.7% for a score of 0/1, rising from 4.7% at 0/1 to 40.9% at 6/7. 9 In the JAMA review, TIMI 5–7 had a likelihood ratio for ACS of 6.8 and TIMI 0–1 of 0.31. 116

GRACE: what it weighs

GRACE was built from a large international registry of acute coronary syndromes to predict death, not to sort undiagnosed chest pain. Its in-hospital model uses eight variables, which together carried 89.9% of the prognostic information; its c-statistic was 0.83 in derivation and 0.79 when tested in GUSTO-IIb. 168 Later GRACE models predict death after discharge to 6 months (9 variables, c-statistics 0.81 and 0.75) and death or MI from admission to 6 months (9 predictors including heart failure and peripheral vascular disease; c-statistics 0.81 for death and 0.73 for death or MI). 107,143 The point tables and formula are not reproduced here. The 2023 ESC guideline lists a GRACE risk score above 140 as a high-risk criterion favouring an early invasive strategy within 24 hours. 46 What that strategy involves is in Reperfusion.

Highlight

Odds ratios are per the unit shown and come from the derivation model; they are not points and cannot be added. 168

GRACE in-hospital model · all 8 variables

Eight variables, one prediction

The model weighs how sick the person is now (cardiac arrest, Killip class, blood pressure, heart rate), the ECG, kidney function, cardiac enzymes and age. Odds ratios from the derivation: cardiac arrest 4.3, ST deviation 2.4, Killip 2.0 per class, age 1.7 per 10 years, enzymes 1.6, systolic BP 1.4 per 20 mm Hg lower, heart rate 1.3 per 30 bpm, creatinine 1.2 per 1 mg/dL. 168

In plain wordsGRACE estimates how likely death is for someone already diagnosed with a heart attack or unstable angina. A higher score points the team toward earlier angiography.
Exam hookGRACE >140 is an ESC high-risk criterion for an invasive strategy within 24 h. 46

Head to head

HEART discriminated best in emergency cohorts

In 1748 Dutch emergency patients, at a sensitivity of at least 98%, HEART identified 381 people as low risk (0.8% of events missed) and GRACE 231 (2.2% missed); TIMI identified none. 349 That fits what each score was built for: HEART for undifferentiated chest pain, TIMI and GRACE for people already diagnosed with an acute coronary syndrome.

Exam hookHEART for the emergency department; TIMI and GRACE were derived in diagnosed ACS. GRACE >140 favours invasive care within 24 h. 46

Does using a score change care?

Established

HEART Pathway trial

In 282 patients, HEART plus troponin at 0 and 3 hours cut objective cardiac testing at 30 days (56.7% vs 68.8%) and shortened hospital stay by 12 hours (9.9 vs 21.9 h). 275

Established

A stepped-wedge trial in 9 hospitals

In 3648 patients, HEART-guided care was non-inferior for 6-week events, with 2.0% in low-risk patients, but did not reduce use of health-care resources much. Benefit depends on whether clinicians act on the score. 348

Small print

The HEART age item

The printed table in Six 2008 says "≤65" for 2 points while the Methods text says 65 or older; this page follows the Methods. In the 120-patient derivation, endpoint risk was 2.5%, 20.3% and 72.7% across the three bands. 407

Troponin first

Scores sit beside the blood test

Rapid troponin pathways now rule many people out within 1 to 2 hours, and the T in HEART depends on the assay's normal limit. See Troponin & rapid rule-out and the ECG. 46
