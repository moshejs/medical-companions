Troponin & rapid rule-out

Troponin is a protein inside heart muscle cells. When they are damaged it leaks into the blood, where a test can measure it. Watch one illustrative curve rise and fall over a week, switch between assays and thresholds, then see how the ESC 0/1-hour algorithm sorts patients in the emergency department.

Symptoms begin · 0 h

Below the 99th percentile

UDMI4: "Detection of an elevated cTn value above the 99th percentile URL is defined as myocardial injury." Each assay has its own 99th percentile upper reference limit (URL), and UDMI4 recommends sex-specific values for high-sensitivity (hs) assays because values are lower in women. 453 At the very start of a heart attack the level may still be normal, so one early normal result cannot rule MI out.

In plain wordsTroponin is a protein that leaks out of damaged heart muscle. Right at the start of a heart attack it may not have reached the blood yet.
Exam hookMyocardial injury = cTn above the 99th percentile URL; acute if it rises and/or falls. 453

Where the 100 ended up

In prospective validation of the 1-hour hs-cTnT algorithm, 59.5% were ruled out, 16.4% ruled in and 24.1% observed. NPV was 99.9% (95% CI 99.3–100%) and PPV 78.2% (72.1–83.6%). 364 Filled dots in the rule-in box: 13 of 16 had an MI. In the rule-out box, about 0.1 of 60 had an MI (rounded to whole dots).

Type 1 MI

UDMI4 type 1 MI: "Detection of a rise and/or fall of cTn values with at least one value above the 99th percentile URL" plus at least one ischaemic criterion, caused by acute atherothrombosis (plaque rupture or erosion). 453 Among 2,122 Edinburgh patients with raised troponin I, 55.2% had type 1 MI. 57

In plain wordsA raised troponin means some heart muscle has been damaged. Only when there are also signs that it was starved of oxygen is it called a heart attack.
Exam hookType 2 MI = supply–demand imbalance without acute atherothrombosis. Type 4a needs cTn >5× URL, type 5 >10×. 453

Raised troponin without a heart attack

A single raised value is not an MI. 46 Many conditions damage heart muscle; without clinical evidence of acute ischaemia they are called myocardial injury, not MI. 101 In 372,310 South Australian emergency episodes tested with high-sensitivity troponin T, 5.12% were acute MI, 1.86% acute injury with coronary artery disease, 8.66% acute injury without it, 14.79% chronic injury and 69.58% no injury. 63

What the research says

Established

Why serial samples

UDMI4 advises sampling at presentation (0 h) and again "3–6 h later, or earlier with hs-cTn assays"; people who arrive more than 12–18 h after onset may take longer to show a changing pattern. The ESC recommends the 0 h/1 h algorithm as the "best option" and 0 h/2 h as the "second-best option". 46,453

Established

Single-sample rule-out, with a time condition

Very low values at presentation gave NPVs around 99.5%: hs-cTnI <5 ng/L applied to 49% of 22,457 patients (NPV 99.5%), and to 61% of those without MI in High-STEACS (NPV 99.6%). 56,400 An hs-cTnT below the limit of detection plus a non-ischaemic ECG classed 30.6% as low risk (sensitivity 98.7%). 345 The ESC limits the very-low single 0 h rule-out to chest pain that began more than 3 h before the sample. 46

Debate

Sex-specific thresholds

UDMI4 recommends sex-specific 99th percentiles for hs-cTn assays, because values are lower in women; age-specific cut-offs are not recommended. 453 In one study, 16 ng/L for women and 34 ng/L for men increased diagnoses of myocardial injury by 42% in women and 6% in men, but afterwards women with injury were less often revascularised (15% vs 34%) or given dual antiplatelet therapy (26% vs 43%). 256 High-STEACS implementation did not lower 1-year MI or cardiovascular death among those reclassified (adjusted OR 1.10). 401

Established

Type 2 MI and injury are not benign

Among 2,122 Edinburgh patients with raised troponin I, 55.2% had type 1 MI, 20.2% type 2 MI and 24.6% myocardial injury. Five-year death was 36.7% after type 1 MI, 62.5% after type 2 and 72.4% after injury, mostly from non-cardiovascular causes. These are population figures, not predictions. 57 No treatment strategy has been proven for type 2 MI and injury. 86

Established

Sensitive assays find MI earlier

In 718 emergency patients, sensitive assays at presentation had an AUC of 0.95–0.96 against 0.90 for a standard assay, and 0.92–0.94 vs 0.76 in those arriving within 3 h. 362 The ESC says CK-MB, myosin-binding protein C and copeptin add only limited value on top of troponin in most situations. 46

Early evidence

Faster pathways

In COMPASS-MI (22,651 patients), hs-cTnI <6 ng/L at presentation plus a change <4 ng/L after 45–120 min gave an NPV of 99.5% and classed 56.5% as low risk. 328 In BACC, hs-TnI <3 ng/L with a low-risk ECG ruled out 28% with an NPV of 100%. 327 Risk scores used alongside troponin are covered in HEART, TIMI & GRACE.
