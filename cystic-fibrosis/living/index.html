Part 5 · Living & care

Living with CF

CF is lived one day at a time: treatments around meals and school or work, the effort of keeping them up, and the feelings that come with a lifelong condition. Scrub through a day of treatments, then the whole life course.

Step 1 · treatment burden

A day of treatments

In 204 US adults with CF, the median number of daily therapies was 7 (IQR 5–9) and the mean time spent on treatment was 108 minutes a day (SD 58). On the day before the survey, people reported a median of 3 inhaled and 3 oral therapies, and only 49% had done airway clearance that day 400. The workload did not differ by gender, age or FEV1: it stays high whatever the disease severity 400. The dial sweeps one illustrative day built to match those averages.

In plain wordsDaily CF care can take about two hours a day. Feeling tired of treatments is common and worth telling your team about 400.

School years

  • Families preferred the CF care team to start conversations about school 203.
  • ECFS standards describe CF nurse specialists visiting schools, with parental permission, to teach staff about nutrition, enzymes and other medicines, time off for clinic visits, and adherence 74.
  • Haggie summarises the infection-control guideline's advice that children with CF at the same school should avoid contact and shared facilities 193,392.

Treatment burden and quality of life

The Cystic Fibrosis Questionnaire (teen and adult version, age 14 and older) has 44 items on 12 generic and CF-specific scales, and child and parent versions exist 349. Its revised respiratory scale (CFQ-R) is the usual quality-of-life outcome in trials. In the Sawicki study, the mean CFQ-R treatment-burden score was 52.3 out of 100 (higher is better); two or more nebulised medicines, and airway clearance of 30 minutes or more, were each linked to higher perceived burden 400.

Mental health

Screening, as published

Every year from age 12

"The ICMH recommends annual screening for depression and anxiety with the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 for adolescents and adults with CF (ages 12–adulthood)." The committee also recommends offering annual screening to at least one primary caregiver of children aged 0–17 (PHQ-9/GAD-7, PHQ-8/GAD-7 or PHQ-2/GAD-2, depending on resources). Its 15 recommendations each had 100% consensus 353.

Stepped care, ages 12+

Matched to severity

  • Mild symptoms: education and rescreening
  • Moderate: CBT or interpersonal therapy (IPT)
  • Severe depression: psychological treatment plus antidepressants
  • Severe anxiety: exposure-based CBT

The ICMH names citalopram, escitalopram, sertraline and fluoxetine as appropriate first-line SSRIs for most people with CF aged 12 and older who need medication, with close monitoring for drug–drug interactions 353. Children aged 7–11 are evaluated when a caregiver's scores are raised or the child has significant symptoms, and psychological interventions come first 353.

Why it matters

Mood, adherence and survival

The consensus lists worse adherence among the outcomes linked to psychological symptoms 353. Among 1005 US TIDES participants, 25% screened positive for depression; the adjusted 5-year hazard ratio for death was 1.6 in adults (95% CI 1.0–2.4) and 2.0 in the severe range. These are associations in a population, not predictions for any person 402.

Modulators and mood

What the label says

The Trikafta label (section 5.4) warns of "Serious neuropsychiatric events, including symptoms of anxiety, depression, suicidal ideation and behavior, and sleep disturbances", which "may occur within the first three months"; it advises assessing baseline symptoms and monitoring for new or worse ones 140. The Alyftrek and Kalydeco labels carry the same class warning 132,135.

A case series described 6 people with "mental fogginess" in the first 3 months of ETI Early evidence 208, while a 48-week cohort found psychosocial health improved overall and anxiety unchanged, with women, people with lower lung function and those on psychotropic medicines possibly more vulnerable 479. Whether modulators cause these symptoms is unresolved Early evidence.

SupportIf you feel foggy, sad or anxious, or have trouble sleeping after starting a CFTR modulator, tell your care team. Do not stop the medicine on your own 140,208. If you ever feel unsafe or think about ending your life, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line now.

Everyday rules and choices

Cross-infection

Six feet apart

The 2013 CFF/SHEA guideline widened the recommended distance between people with CF from 3 to 6 feet 193,392. Haggie summarises its social advice: at least 2 m apart, no in-person meetings, and no shared facilities for children with CF at the same school 193,392. In hospital, people with CF should not share rooms, bathrooms or toilets 74.

Community

Meeting online

Of 138 US centres with indoor in-person events, 45% allowed one person with CF to attend and 51% allowed none; outdoors, 84% allowed more than one 393. Because in-person contact is discouraged, online contact is a main route to peer community 193,438. Only 53% of US programmes had adopted the inpatient 6-foot rule in writing 451.

Exercise

Fitness, not FEV1

A Cochrane review (24 trials, 875 people) found that activity programmes over 6 months probably improve exercise capacity a little (peak oxygen uptake MD 1.60), and may have no effect on FEV1 or the CFQ-R respiratory score Established 355. The specialist physiotherapist leads airway clearance, exercise and inhalation therapy 74.

Travel

Flying with CF

In 79 adults, a pre-flight algorithm sorted people by resting and walking oxygen saturation; only 21 (27%) would have needed a hypoxia-altitude test. Like everything here it is a teaching example, not individual advice 6.

Research priorities

What the community asked

The 2023 James Lind Alliance refresh drew on 1417 people with CF, family members and clinicians; in the second survey, 62% of respondents with CF were taking modulators 383.

Palliative care

From diagnosis, not just at the end

The CFF consensus says CF teams should deliver primary palliative care "at the time of diagnosis, and throughout the disease course" 222. Palliative care means extra support for comfort and quality of life, at any stage 222. More in Advanced lung disease & transplant.
