Part 1 · Understanding CF

Variant classes & genotype

There are more than 2,000 known changes in CFTR. Some cause CF, some cause milder problems and some are harmless 81,95. Follow one protein down the cell's production line and see where each class of variant breaks it, then look up a variant yourself.

Seven ways to break one protein

The functional class system began with Welsh and Smith in 1993, who grouped CFTR mutations by how they disrupt the chloride channel 498. Most teaching uses six classes; a seventh has been proposed Proposed 91,261. Pick a class or press Play.

The production line

Healthy CFTR, start to finish

A working CFTR protein has to be made from its mRNA, fold correctly, travel to the cell surface, open when it is switched on, let chloride and bicarbonate through and stay there. The class system groups variants by which of these steps fails 261,498. By 1995 many of the more than 550 known mutations could already be grouped into five classes by their molecular consequences 519.

In plain wordsDifferent gene changes break the CFTR protein in different ways. Some make no protein, some make a misfolded protein and some make a protein that does not open well 261.
Exam hookClass I = no protein, II = misfolding, III = gating, IV = conductance, V = reduced amount, VI = unstable at the membrane 23,261.

What goes wrong
The protein misfolds and is destroyed; the little that reaches the surface is unstable and gates poorly, so it "could be assigned to at least three classes" 81.
Where it is common
About 70% of CF chromosomes worldwide 231,307: 66.8% across Europe, from 87.2% in Denmark to 26.3% in Algeria 120; 12–31% of CF alleles in Asia, and not seen on CF alleles of East Asian ancestry 23.
What it means
In a 1990 cohort, 99% of F508del homozygotes were pancreatic insufficient 233. The phase 3 elexacaftor–tezacaftor–ivacaftor trial enrolled people with F508del and a minimal-function variant, a group earlier modulators had not helped 295.

Teaching toolThis explorer explains what a variant does. It is not a diagnosis, a prognosis or an eligibility check: diagnosis rests on clinical features and CFTR function, confirmed with a sweat chloride test 55,128, and drug labels list responsive variants themselves (module 4.2).

How many variants, and which cause CF

a change in the DNApossibly harmless

A 2019 count of 2,075 reported variants by type 261. An earlier classification of the CF Mutation Database found 40% single amino-acid substitutions and 36% RNA-processing changes (nonsense, frameshift and splicing) 81.

CFTR2

Clinical and functional evidence

CFTR2 collected genotype and phenotype data on 39,696 people with CF in North America and Europe. A variant was called disease-causing on clinical grounds if mean sweat chloride in at least 3 carriers of it was 60 mmol/L or more; below 10% of wild-type CFTR function was used as a conservative threshold for CF features 431.

ACMG 2020

Three categories, and a caution

The CFTR2 categories are CF-causing, varying clinical consequence and non-CF-causing; "non-CF-causing" does not rule out a CFTR-related disorder such as male infertility or pancreatitis. About 5% of variants cannot be assigned. The ACMG warns against relying on ClinVar assertions made outside CFTR2 95.

In practice

Use the latest classifications

The CF Foundation 2017 diagnosis guideline recommends using the latest CFTR2 variant classifications to help with diagnosis 128. CFTR2 is updated over time, so counts differ by date: one 2019 review reports 432 annotated variants, 352 with confirmed disease liability 261.

Minimal function, residual function and theratypes

"Minimal function" and "residual function" are genotype groups used in trials and drug labels. Lopes-Pacheco describes classes I and II as minimal function and classes IV, V and VI as residual function 261; Bell and colleagues state that class I to III variants cause little or no CFTR function and a more severe phenotype, including exocrine pancreatic insufficiency 23. Residual-function variants can cause milder disease, sometimes affecting a single organ 420.

A theratype is "a recently invented term used to classify disease-associated DNA variants according to the molecular-based treatment to which they respond" 81. Knowing both CFTR variants matters because some medicines work only for certain ones 81,140.

Established

Testing drugs on cells

Theratyping uses CFTR modulators in laboratory models to define a variant's defect and predict drug response: Fischer rat thyroid (FRT) cell lines, human bronchial and nasal epithelial cells, and rectal organoids 66.

Established

A label widened by lab data

Ivacaftor potentiated several of 54 missense CFTR forms in FRT cells 483. In 2017 the FDA expanded the ivacaftor label to rare variants on the basis of FRT data instead of clinical trials 112. The Kalydeco label calls a variant responsive on clinical response and/or FRT data showing chloride transport raised by at least 10% over baseline, as a percentage of normal 135.

US labels

Responsiveness lists

The Trikafta label covers people aged 2 years and older with at least one CFTR variant responsive on clinical and/or in vitro data, or that "results in production of CFTR protein", with a non-exhaustive list 140. The Alyftrek label uses the same structure for people aged 6 and older 132.

Early evidence

417 variants in a dish

In FRT cells, about 76% of 417 CFTR variants reached the 10%-of-wild-type threshold with vanzacaftor–tezacaftor–ivacaftor, including 50 previously non-responsive or borderline to elexacaftor–tezacaftor–ivacaftor. This is in vitro work 29.

Caution

The dish is not the person

FRT data have predicted response for many missense variants, but for G970R the in vitro response did not match the clinical response to ivacaftor 66.

Genotype and phenotype

Genotype predicts the pancreas well and the lungs poorly. Pancreatic status follows genotype closely 4,233, whereas for none of the genotypes it studied could the Genotype–Phenotype Consortium predict the severity or course of lung disease 59.

Toronto cohort of 293 people with CF, 1990: pancreatic insufficiency by number of F508del copies 233. In that cohort 52% were homozygous, 40% heterozygous and 8% had other genotypes 233.

R117H and the polyT tract. R117H's effect depends on the polyT tract on the same chromosome (in cis). With 5T plus a CF-causing variant on the other copy it generally gives nonclassic CF; with 7T it may range from no symptoms to absence of the vas deferens, and very rarely nonclassic CF 95,238. In France only about 3.1% of an estimated 3,650 people with [R117H;T7] and F508del were known to have CF-related symptoms, and the penetrance of classical CF was estimated at 0.03% 470. Thauvin-Robinet and colleagues argued for withdrawing R117H from screening panels; the ACMG kept it, with reflex polyT testing 470,497.

Modifier genes and the environment

Two people with the same CFTR genotype can have very different lungs. Twin and sibling studies show how much comes from other genes and how much from surroundings and chance.

Variation in lung function, from 134 identical twins and 272 non-identical twins and siblings: genetic factors 50%, unique environmental or chance factors 36%, shared environment 14% 70.

Established

Secondhand smoke

Smoke exposure at home (23.2% of 812 participants) was linked to a 9.8 percentile-point lower lung function, and to 22.7 points in people homozygous for the TGFB1 −509 TT genotype 69.

Established

Lung modifier loci

A genome-wide study of 1,978 F508del homozygotes found a modifier near EHF and APIP on chromosome 11p13 504. A meta-analysis of 6,365 people found five: MUC4/MUC20, SLC9A3, HLA class II, AGTR2/SLC6A14 and EHF/APIP 77.

Established

Gut and diabetes

Meconium ileus affects about 15% of newborns with CF, and identical twins were much more concordant for it than non-identical twins and siblings 31. Type 2 diabetes risk genes (TCF7L2, CDKAL1, CDKN2A/B, IGF2BP2) modify the age at onset of CF-related diabetes 81.

In plain wordsYour genes do not decide everything. Other genes and your surroundings, like smoke exposure, also shape how CF affects the lungs 69,70.
