An animated, cited guide for students, people with cystic fibrosis (CF), and their families and caregivers. Educational only, not medical advice. Your place is saved on this device.

Not medical adviceThe CF Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, physiotherapist, dietitian, pharmacist, genetic counsellor or the rest of a CF care team. Sweat-chloride bands, FEV1 percent predicted, genotype and eligibility tables and the carrier calculator are teaching aids, never for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Doses, airway-clearance routines, enzyme dosing and infection-control guidance are quoted as published, never as instructions. Survival and life-expectancy figures are registry population statistics that are changing quickly with CFTR modulators; they are not predictions for any person.

Sources

Every reference behind the CF Companion: journal articles, randomised trials, registry studies, systematic reviews, CF Foundation, ECFS and other guidance, US drug labels and trial registrations. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Research passes ran in parallel, one per area: the CFTR gene, inheritance and variants; CFTR physiology and the lungs; the digestive system, other organs and nutrition; newborn screening, diagnosis and monitoring; CFTR modulators and the research frontier; airway clearance, infection and transplant; and living with CF, fertility, pregnancy and nursing care. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or the regulator's label, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked not matched in a research database are pages with no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are not cited for any fact in this guide.

CF Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cystic fibrosis (CF) and CFTR modulators, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with CF, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, an airway-clearance routine, enzyme dosing or a care plan without your own CF care team. Sweat-chloride bands, the diagnostic flow, FEV1 percent predicted, genotype tables, the carrier calculator and the modulator eligibility explainer are for learning, never for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Doses, airway-clearance routines, enzyme dosing, infection-control guidance and guideline statements are quoted as published by the CF Foundation, ECFS, NICE and drug labels, never as instructions, and survival figures are registry population statistics, not predictions for any person. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
