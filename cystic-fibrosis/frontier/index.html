Part 5 · Living & care

Research frontier

About 1 in 10 people with CF have variants that modulators cannot help, and modulators do not suit everyone else. This is where research is trying to reach them: adding a working gene, sending the CFTR message, reading past a stop, editing the DNA, or working around CFTR altogether. Every entry carries an evidence label.

Nonsense variants cause CF in about 10% of people, and drugs that act on the CFTR protein cannot help when no protein is made 236. A 2023 Lancet review notes that a health outcome gap has opened for people who are not eligible for modulators, cannot tolerate them, or cannot get them 467. As of November 2026 no genetic therapy for CF is approved 8,388,436.

Gene addition · step 1 of 5

A working CFTR gene, inhaled

Gene addition delivers a working copy of the CFTR gene into airway cells. Three kinds of carrier are in trials: a non-viral lipid complex (pGM169/GL67A), adeno-associated viruses (AAV: 4D-710 and SP-101) and a lentivirus (BI 3720931) 8,317,320,321.

In plain wordsThe idea is to breathe in a tiny delivery package that carries a correct copy of the CFTR instructions into the airway lining 8.

Gene editing · baseEarly evidence (lab)

Adenine base editing

In an organoid biobank from 664 people, about 20% carried variants base editing could theoretically correct; repair in all 4 tested samples, no off-target mutations in 2 lines 166.

Gene editing · primeEarly evidence (lab)

Prime editing

Corrected F508del in 58% of immortalised bronchial cells and 25% of patient airway cells, restoring over 50% of wild-type CFTR function 436; also corrected L227R and N1303K in organoids and nasal cells 43.

Around CFTREarly evidence: negative

ENaC inhibitor BI 1265162

A "mutation-agnostic" approach. BALANCE-CF 1 showed no relevant clinical benefit (+1.5% ppFEV1 at 200 µg twice daily vs placebo); development terminated 175.

Cell biologyEstablished discoveryHypothesis as a target

Pulmonary ionocytes

A rare FOXI1-positive cell that is the major source of CFTR transcripts in mouse and human airways; knocking out Foxi1 caused CF-like fluid and mucus changes in mice 304,341.

Personalised testingEarly evidence

Organoid theratyping

Forskolin-induced swelling of rectal organoids measures CFTR function, and organoid responses correlated with trial outcomes and, prospectively, with FEV1 and sweat chloride 24,98,99. Still a research tool 99.

In plain words. Researchers are testing gene therapy, mRNA and gene editing for the people modulators cannot help, but none is a proven treatment yet 8,236,388. Trial listings change often; a CF care team can say which studies are open.

CF Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cystic fibrosis (CF) and CFTR modulators, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with CF, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, an airway-clearance routine, enzyme dosing or a care plan without your own CF care team. Sweat-chloride bands, the diagnostic flow, FEV1 percent predicted, genotype tables, the carrier calculator and the modulator eligibility explainer are for learning, never for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Doses, airway-clearance routines, enzyme dosing, infection-control guidance and guideline statements are quoted as published by the CF Foundation, ECFS, NICE and drug labels, never as instructions, and survival figures are registry population statistics, not predictions for any person.
