Sweat, diabetes, bones & fertility

Beyond the lungs and gut, CFTR matters in the islets of the pancreas, the sweat glands, the sinuses, the bones, the kidneys and the reproductive tract. Start with CF-related diabetes, the commonest of these as people with CF live longer, then explore the body map.

CFRD at a glance, as the guidelines state it

QuestionWhat the guideline says
Who is screened, and howAnnual screening by age 10 in everyone with CF who does not have CFRD, with a 2-hour 75-g OGTT (ADA/CFF 2010) [[Moran2010CFRD]]; yearly OGTT at least by age 10 (ISPAD 2022) [[Ode2022ISPAD]]; glucose tolerance testing from 10 years (ESPEN-ESPGHAN-ECFS) [[Turck2016Nutrition]]
Why not HbA1c?Not recommended as a screening test because it is not sensitive enough [[Moran2010CFRD]]
Diagnostic thresholds2-hour OGTT glucose ≥200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L), fasting glucose ≥126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L), or HbA1c ≥6.5% [[Moran2010CFRD]]
During exacerbationsWith IV antibiotics or systemic steroids, screen with fasting and 2-hour post-meal glucose for the first 48 hours [[Moran2010CFRD]]
PregnancyOGTT at 12–16 and 24–28 weeks for pregnant women with CF not known to have CFRD [[Moran2010CFRD,Ode2022ISPAD]]
Liver diseaseWith advanced CF liver disease, start CFRD screening at diagnosis of the liver disease, even before 10, then yearly [[Sellers2024]]
TreatmentInsulin; oral agents are not as effective and not recommended outside research trials. HbA1c quarterly, with a goal of <7% for many, individualised [[Moran2010CFRD]]. Insulin pumps and continuous glucose monitoring where appropriate [[Ode2022ISPAD]]
ComplicationsYearly microvascular screening from 5 years after diagnosis [[Moran2010CFRD,Ode2022ISPAD]]
DietKeep the high-calorie, high-salt, high-fat CF diet; calorie restriction is almost never appropriate [[Moran2010CFRD,Ode2022ISPAD]]

A gap that closed. In Minnesota in 1987–2002, median survival was 49.5 years in males without diabetes and 47.4 with it, but 47.0 in females without diabetes and 30.7 with it [[Milla2005]]. By 2003–2008 the sex difference in CFRD mortality had disappeared, and only 2 of 61 people diagnosed in that period died; the authors credit early diagnosis and aggressive treatment [[Moran2009Trends]]. Why women fared worse is unclear Hypothesis: the authors speculated about hormones and inflammation [[Milla2005]]. These are cohort statistics from their era, not predictions.

Up to 80% of older people with severe genotypes have CFRD; incidence is about 4–9% a year in Denmark and 2.7 cases per 100 person-years in Minnesota [[Ode2022ISPAD]]. Whether highly effective modulators change this is open: ISPAD 2022 finds too little evidence yet to change screening for people taking them [[Ode2022ISPAD]].
