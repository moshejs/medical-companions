CFTR modulators: how they work

Modulators are pills that work on the faulty CFTR protein itself, not on the mucus or the infection downstream. Follow one misfolded F508del protein as correctors refold it, the cell ships it to the surface and a potentiator props its gate open. Switch each drug class off to see what it adds.

There are two main kinds of modulator. Correctors improve folding so that more CFTR reaches the cell surface; potentiators increase the function of CFTR that is already there [[Liu2019Potentiator]]. Most modern regimens combine both [[FDA-Trikafta]]. Which one helps depends on the defect: a gating variant such as G551D reaches the surface but opens poorly, while F508del is mostly lost before it gets there [[VanGoor2009VX770,VanGoor2011VX809]].

Which drug acts on which defect

Potentiator

Ivacaftor · deutivacaftor

Raises the channel open probability of CFTR already at the surface [[FDA-Kalydeco]]. In cells it increased open probability for both the G551D gating variant and F508del [[VanGoor2009VX770]]. Alone, it is the treatment for gating variants: in STRIVE (G551D) ppFEV1 rose 10.6 points over placebo [[Ramsey2011STRIVE]]. Deutivacaftor is a once-daily potentiator used in Alyftrek [[Uluer2023VTD,FDA-Alyftrek]].

Type I corrector

Lumacaftor · tezacaftor

Bind a hydrophobic pocket in TMD1, linking four unstable helices Established [[Fiedorczuk2022Cell]]. Combined with ivacaftor for people homozygous for F508del, the gain was modest: ppFEV1 +2.6 to 4.0 points (lumacaftor-ivacaftor) and +4.0 points (tezacaftor-ivacaftor) over placebo [[Wainwright2015TRAFFIC,TaylorCousar2017EVOLVE]].

Next-generation corrector

Elexacaftor · vanzacaftor

Bind a different site from tezacaftor, with an additive effect on processing and trafficking, as the labels state [[FDA-Trikafta,FDA-Alyftrek]]. Elexacaftor is also a co-potentiator in vitro Early evidence [[Veit2021ELX]]. With tezacaftor and ivacaftor, ppFEV1 rose 14.3 points over placebo in people with one F508del allele [[Middleton2019ETI]].

Not helped

Variants that make no protein

Drugs that act on the protein cannot help when no protein is made, as with many nonsense variants, which cause CF in about 10% of people [[Kerem2014Ataluren]]. Approaches for them are in Research frontier.

Liver: the boxed warning and the test schedule

Boxed warning (Trikafta and Alyftrek)"Drug-induced liver injury and liver failure." The Trikafta label reports cases of liver failure leading to transplantation and death, in people with and without prior liver disease [[FDA-Trikafta]]. Alyftrek carries the same boxed warning, based on cases seen with ETI [[FDA-Alyftrek]].

What the trials showed

In Trikafta label Trial 1, transaminases rose above 8×, 5× and 3× the upper limit of normal in 1%, 2% and 8% on ETI, vs 1%, 1% and 5% on placebo; total bilirubin above 2× the upper limit of normal in 4% vs under 1% [[FDA-Trikafta]].

In SKYLINE, the same thresholds (3×, 5×, 8×) were reached in 6%, 2.5% and 1.3% on VTD, vs 3.1%, 1.2% and 0.2% on ETI [[FDA-Alyftrek]].

After approval

US FDA adverse event reports from late 2019 to 2022 held 452 reports of drug-induced liver injury linked to ETI, 2.1% of all its reports, with a reporting odds ratio of 2.82 and median onset at 50.5 days. Reports like these cannot give an incidence [[Shi2024DILI]].

How often severe injury happens, and why, is still unknown Early evidence [[Shi2024DILI,FDA-Trikafta]].

Drug interactions: CYP3A

All three ETI components are broken down by the liver enzyme CYP3A, and ivacaftor is a sensitive substrate, so drugs that speed up or slow down CYP3A change modulator levels [[FDA-Trikafta]].

Other label safety points

Eyes

Cataracts in children

Non-congenital lens opacities have been reported in children treated with ivacaftor-containing regimens; the labels recommend baseline and follow-up eye examinations in children [[FDA-Trikafta,FDA-Alyftrek]]. In juvenile rats ivacaftor caused cataracts, not seen in older animals [[FDA-Kalydeco]].

Mood and sleep

Neuropsychiatric events

A class warning since 03/2026 covers anxiety, depression, suicidal ideation and behaviour, and sleep disturbance. The Trikafta label notes these "may occur within the first three months", in people with and without a prior history, and advises a baseline assessment and monitoring [[FDA-Trikafta]].

Evidence is mixed: a sponsor-funded pooled analysis found depression-related events at 3.32 vs 3.24 per 100 person-years (ETI vs placebo) [[Ramsey2024Depression]]; a UK centre saw symptoms in 7.1% of 266 adults, improving in 10 of 13 after dose reduction [[Spoletini2022MentalHealth]]; a 7-centre US study saw mental health diagnoses and medication rise after ETI [[McKinzie2026MentalHealth]]. Causality is unsettled Early evidence.

Skin and muscle

Rash and creatine phosphokinase

Rash in Trikafta Trial 1: 10% vs 5% on placebo, more in females (16%) than males (5%); in Trial 5, 23% vs 2%. A role of hormonal contraceptives "cannot be excluded". Creatine phosphokinase above 5× the upper limit of normal: 10% vs 5% [[FDA-Trikafta]].

In plain words. These medicines can affect the liver, so blood tests are checked often, especially in the first months. Children have eye checks. Changes in mood, anxiety or sleep are worth telling the care team about early. Many other medicines, and grapefruit, change modulator levels, so the CF team and pharmacist should know about every medicine and supplement [[FDA-Trikafta]].
