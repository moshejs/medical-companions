Common misunderstandings and teaching points

Misunderstanding

"Gloves replace hand washing"

Hand hygiene applies before and after every patient contact, regardless of glove use [[Haggie2020,Saiman2014IPC]]. In a survey across 25 centres, inpatient nurses had generally lower knowledge scores and adherence to selected practices than other groups [[Saiman2022Barriers]].

Misunderstanding

"Two people with CF can share a bay if both are well"

ECFS standards say people with CF should not share rooms, bathrooms or toilets in hospital, or be in contact in clinic, ward, pharmacy or radiology waiting areas [[Conway2014ECFS]].

Misunderstanding

"Enzymes are only for big meals"

The Creon label plans for about three meals plus two or three snacks, with snacks at about half the meal dose; capsules are swallowed whole, not crushed or chewed [[FDA-Creon]].

Misunderstanding

"Diabetes means cutting calories"

People with CFRD keep the high-calorie, high-salt, high-fat CF diet; calorie restriction is almost never appropriate [[Moran2010CFRD,Ode2022ISPAD]]. CFRD is treated with insulin [[Moran2010CFRD]].

Teaching point

Once-daily is not weaker

Once-daily and thrice-daily IV aminoglycosides were equally effective, and once-daily probably caused less nephrotoxicity in children Established [[Bhatt2019Cochrane,Smyth2005TOPIC]]. Evening dosing raised a urinary kidney-injury marker more than morning dosing [[Prayle2016]].

Teaching point

Hearing loss can be silent

Reported tobramycin ototoxicity rates range from 0 to 56%, depending on how hearing is measured [[Harruff2021Ototox]]. In children given IV aminoglycosides, extended high-frequency audiometry found ototoxicity in 24% [[AlMalky2015]].

Teaching point

Ports last, but fail in known ways

165 ports in 109 people had a median survival of 1441 days and 0.58 complications per 1000 catheter-days: occlusion (33 devices), infection (23) and leakage (16) [[Royle2008]]. At an Irish centre, 14 candidaemia cases were associated with lower FEV1 and more exacerbations [[McCarthy2018TIVAD]].

Teaching point

Modulators have not ended exacerbations

ECFS 2024: monitoring and treating airway infection, inflammation and exacerbations "remains important" despite modulators [[Burgel2024Standards]].

The CF nurse specialist

The ECFS framework lists the core team as consultants, clinical nurse specialist, microbiologist, physiotherapist, dietitian, pharmacist, clinical psychologist, social worker and clinical geneticist [[Conway2014ECFS]]. The nurse specialist provides education, advocacy and psychosocial support at key moments: the screening result and diagnosis, the first admission and IV course, a second diagnosis such as CFRD, transition, reproductive and pre- and postnatal care, and transplant and end of life [[Conway2014ECFS]].

Routine visits for stable disease are every 2–3 months, with an annual review that includes a full medical, dietetic, physiotherapy and psychosocial review and surveillance blood tests [[Conway2014ECFS]]. Glucose tolerance testing starts at age 10 and bone density at 8–10 [[Turck2016Nutrition]]. The CFF palliative-care guideline recommends screening for unmet palliative-care needs with the IPOS every year from age 12 [[Kavalieratos2021Palliative]].
