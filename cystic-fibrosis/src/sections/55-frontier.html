Part 5 · Living & care

Research frontier

About 1 in 10 people with CF have variants that modulators cannot help, and modulators do not suit everyone else. This is where research is trying to reach them: adding a working gene, sending the CFTR message, reading past a stop, editing the DNA, or working around CFTR altogether. Every entry carries an evidence label.

Nonsense variants cause CF in about 10% of people, and drugs that act on the CFTR protein cannot help when no protein is made [[Kerem2014Ataluren]]. A 2023 Lancet review notes that a health outcome gap has opened for people who are not eligible for modulators, cannot tolerate them, or cannot get them [[TaylorCousar2023Lancet]]. As of November 2026 no genetic therapy for CF is approved [[Alton2015pGM169,Rowe2023MRT5005,Sousa2025Prime]].

Gene addition · non-viralEarly evidence

pGM169/GL67A

UK CF Gene Therapy Consortium, 140 people, nebulised monthly for a year: relative ppFEV1 benefit 3.7% over placebo (95% CI 0.1 to 7.3; p=0.046), reflecting stabilisation against decline; "significant, albeit modest" [[Alton2015pGM169]].

Gene addition · AAVEarly evidence

4D-710

Inhaled AAV (capsid 4D-A101) carrying a CFTR transgene, open-label phase 1/2 in adults, recruiting [[NCT05248230]].

mRNAEarly evidence

MRT5005

Inhaled CFTR mRNA in lipid nanoparticles, 42 adults: 14 febrile reactions in 10 treated people and 2 hypersensitivity reactions; FEV1 stable, no FEV1 benefit [[Rowe2023MRT5005]].

mRNAEarly evidence

VX-522

Inhaled mRNA for adults whose genotype does not respond to modulators. Phase 1/2 (26 enrolled) listed as completed on 9 Sep 2026, no results posted [[NCT05668741]].

Read-throughEstablished: negative

Ataluren

Phase 3 (238 people): relative ppFEV1 −2.5% vs −5.5% (p=0.12), raised creatinine 15% vs under 1% [[Kerem2014Ataluren]]. ACT CF (279): difference 0.6 points (p=0.54); "Development of a nonsense-mutation CF therapy remains elusive" [[Konstan2020ACTCF]].

Nonsense suppressionHypothesis

ELX-02

Subcutaneous eukaryotic ribosome-selective glycoside, with and without ivacaftor, in adults with at least one G542X allele; phase 2, 17 enrolled, completed 2022, no results posted [[NCT04135495]].

NCT04135495

Gene editing · baseEarly evidence (lab)

Adenine base editing

In an organoid biobank from 664 people, about 20% carried variants base editing could theoretically correct; repair in all 4 tested samples, no off-target mutations in 2 lines [[Geurts2020ABE]].

Gene editing · primeEarly evidence (lab)

Prime editing

Corrected F508del in 58% of immortalised bronchial cells and 25% of patient airway cells, restoring over 50% of wild-type CFTR function [[Sousa2025Prime]]; also corrected L227R and N1303K in organoids and nasal cells [[Bulcaen2024Prime]].

Around CFTREarly evidence: negative

ENaC inhibitor BI 1265162

A "mutation-agnostic" approach. BALANCE-CF 1 showed no relevant clinical benefit (+1.5% ppFEV1 at 200 µg twice daily vs placebo); development terminated [[Goss2022BALANCE]].

Cell biologyEstablished discoveryHypothesis as a target

Pulmonary ionocytes

A rare FOXI1-positive cell that is the major source of CFTR transcripts in mouse and human airways; knocking out Foxi1 caused CF-like fluid and mucus changes in mice [[Montoro2018,Plasschaert2018]].

Personalised testingEarly evidence

Organoid theratyping

Forskolin-induced swelling of rectal organoids measures CFTR function, and organoid responses correlated with trial outcomes and, prospectively, with FEV1 and sweat chloride [[Dekkers2013FIS,Dekkers2016Organoids,Berkers2019Organoids]]. Still a research tool [[Dekkers2016Organoids]].

In plain words. Researchers are testing gene therapy, mRNA and gene editing for the people modulators cannot help, but none is a proven treatment yet [[Kerem2014Ataluren,Rowe2023MRT5005,Alton2015pGM169]]. Trial listings change often; a CF care team can say which studies are open.
