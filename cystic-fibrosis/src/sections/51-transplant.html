    Complications of advanced lung disease

    Haemoptysis

    Massive haemoptysis

    Average annual incidence 0.87%, and 4.1% of people overall in US registry data. Nearly 60% of those affected had FEV1 <40%, and it carried higher 2-year mortality [[Flume2005Hemoptysis]].

    Pneumothorax

    Pneumothorax

    Average annual incidence 0.64% (about 1 in 167 people a year), and 3.4% overall; nearly 75% of those affected had FEV1 <40%. Risk was higher with P. aeruginosa (OR 2.3), B. cepacia (OR 1.8) and FEV1 <30% (OR 1.5) [[Flume2005Pneumothorax]].

    How the advice was made

    Expert consensus

    The CFF haemoptysis and pneumothorax recommendations were built by Delphi expert consensus, because the evidence was insufficient for evidence-based grading [[Flume2010]].

    Where you live

    US and Canada differ

    Among people with FEV1 <40%, US residents had a higher risk of death without transplant than Canadians (HR 1.79) and a lower transplant rate (HR 0.66). The gaps were larger for people with Medicaid/Medicare and for non-white people [[Ramos2021ALD]]. In France, 50% of CF deaths occurred without transplant [[Ramos2019Referral]].

    Palliative care, alongside everything else

    Palliative care is extra support for comfort and quality of life. It can start at any age and go alongside all other CF care [[Kavalieratos2021Palliative]]. The CF Foundation's 2021 consensus guidelines recommend, as published:

    From diagnosis

    Primary palliative care in usual care

    CF care teams deliver primary palliative care as part of usual CF care "at the time of diagnosis and throughout the disease course" [[Kavalieratos2021Palliative]].

    Specialists

    When transplant is on the table

    Specialty palliative care consultation "when an individual with CF is considering or declines transplantation" [[Kavalieratos2021Palliative]].

    Screening

    Symptoms and carers

    Screen people aged 12 and over with the Integrated Palliative Care Outcome Scale (IPOS) every year and at disease milestones, and offer at least one primary caregiver annual screening with the Brief Assessment Scale for Caregivers [[Kavalieratos2021Palliative]].

    Planning ahead

    Advance care planning

    Goals-of-care discussions and advance care planning across the lifespan, and caregiver support from diagnosis through bereavement [[Kavalieratos2021Palliative]].

    Palliative care and transplant are not opposites. The guideline places specialist palliative input exactly when transplant is being considered or declined, so the conversations about goals and the transplant work-up run side by side [[Kavalieratos2021Palliative,Ramos2019Referral]].
