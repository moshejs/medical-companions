Pancreas, gut & liver

The same faulty CFTR channel that thickens airway mucus also clogs the tubes of the pancreas, the bowel and the bile ducts. Scrub through a lifetime: the pancreas from birth, meconium ileus in the newborn, DIOS and constipation, the liver, and bowel cancer screening in adulthood.

Pancreatic sufficient or insufficient: the genotype decides

In 538 people with CF whose pancreatic status was documented, each complete CFTR genotype was linked only with pancreatic insufficiency (PI) or only with pancreatic sufficiency (PS), never both. PS came with one or two "mild" alleles (for example R117H, R334W, R347P, A455E, P574H); PI came with two "severe" alleles (for example F508del, G542X, W1282X, G551D) [[Kristidis1992]]. The same group estimates that about 10% of CF alleles may confer sufficiency [[Kristidis1992]].

The CFF infant guideline recommends measuring pancreatic status by faecal elastase or coefficient of fat absorption in all infants with CF, and rechecking it in pancreatic-sufficient infants who lose weight or develop gut symptoms [[Borowitz2009]]. The ESPEN-ESPGHAN-ECFS guideline recommends a yearly assessment, including faecal elastase, for people who are pancreatic sufficient [[Turck2016Nutrition]]. Healthy babies, even premature ones, have faecal elastase in the adult normal range by two weeks of age [[Borowitz2009]].

Pancreatitis is a disease of the sufficient pancreas Established. It needs surviving gland tissue, so it is a complication of PS, not PI [[Ooi2011Pancreatitis,Singh2017PI]]. Among 277 pancreatic-sufficient people, 22% had pancreatitis; people with it were diagnosed with CF later (median 14.9 vs 9.3 years) and had lower sweat chloride (74.5 vs 82.8 mmol/L) [[Ooi2011Pancreatitis]].

The liver: what the 2024 guideline asks for

screening

Every year, from diagnosis

Liver blood tests (total bilirubin, AST, ALT, alkaline phosphatase, GGT, platelets) and an abdominal examination for an enlarged liver or spleen; an abdominal ultrasound at least every 2 years from age 3 (or diagnosis) until late adolescence [[Sellers2024]].

definitions

Two levels of disease

Advanced CF liver disease: a nodular liver, advanced fibrosis (F4), multilobular cirrhosis or non-cirrhotic portal hypertension. Hepatobiliary involvement: milder findings such as an enlarged liver, fatty liver, focal biliary cirrhosis, persistent enzyme rises, gallstones or sclerosing cholangitis [[Sellers2024]].

treatment

Ursodeoxycholic acid

A Cochrane review of 4 trials (137 participants) found "insufficient evidence to justify its routine use" [[Cheng2017]]; earlier use did not change severe liver disease in France [[Boelle2019]], and CFF 2024 recommends against routine use to prevent advanced disease [[Sellers2024]].

modulators

CFTR modulators and the liver

CFF 2024 recommends modulators for people with hepatobiliary involvement under close monitoring, but not in decompensated advanced liver disease; trials reported transaminase rises above 3 times the upper limit of normal in about 5% of participants [[Sellers2024]].

cirrhosis

Linked screening

Children with cirrhosis: yearly liver cancer screening with ultrasound and alpha-fetoprotein. Advanced liver disease: start CFRD screening at diagnosis, even before 10. With advanced lung and liver disease, combined lung-liver transplant may be considered [[Sellers2024]].

On CFTR modulators: does the gut recover?

In PROMISE-GI (438 people aged 12 and over), after 6 months of elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor, gut symptom scores improved only slightly and faecal calprotectin fell by 66.2 µg/g, but faecal elastase and steatocrit did not meaningfully change [[Schwarzenberg2023PROMISEGI]]. In children aged 6 to under 12 there was no significant change in faecal elastase categories [[Green2026PROMISEPeds]].

Younger children may be different Early evidence: with ivacaftor from 12 to under 24 months (ARRIVAL), faecal elastase-1 rose and immunoreactive trypsinogen fell [[Rosenfeld2018ARRIVAL]], and in children aged 2 and over on elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor, rises in faecal elastase were maintained to 96 weeks, with 9 children above the sufficiency threshold [[Goralski2026]].
