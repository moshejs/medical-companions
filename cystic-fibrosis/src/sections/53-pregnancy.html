Partner testing: work out the chances

Choose your status and your partner's test result. The squares follow the autosomal recessive rule: CF needs a variant on both copies [[Deignan2020ACMG,Shteinberg2021]]. When both parents are carriers, each pregnancy has a 1 in 4 chance of CF, 2 in 4 of a carrier child and 1 in 4 of neither [[Deignan2020ACMG]].

Paths to parenthood

These are options people with CF and their partners have used. They are listed without ranking; which, if any, fits is a personal choice, ideally made with a genetic counsellor and the CF team [[Kazmerski2022Family]].

Counselling

Genetic counselling

Because CF is inherited, genetic counsellors have an important role as people with CF consider their reproductive options [[Kazmerski2022Family]]. ACOG advises offering counselling when both partners are carriers [[ACOG2017CO691]].

Testing in pregnancy

Prenatal diagnosis

Carrier screening can lead to prenatal diagnosis: one laboratory found 1 affected fetus per 25,000 carrier screens [[Strom2011]]. Couples can be tested one after the other or both at once; testing together may be preferred during pregnancy [[Deignan2020ACMG]].

Assisted reproduction

Sperm retrieval, IVF and ICSI

For males with CF, biological parenthood needs sperm retrieval followed by ICSI [[Kazmerski2022Family]]. The risk of having a child with CF must be considered when planning assisted reproduction [[Shteinberg2021Fertility]].

Other families

Adoption, fostering, step-parenthood, surrogacy

People with CF have pursued and qualified for each of these routes to parenthood [[Kazmerski2022Family]].

A choice too

Declining testing

ACOG states that a patient may decline any or all carrier screening [[ACOG2017CO691]].

Planning a pregnancy

Diabetes, as published

Glucose tests

The CFF/ADA guideline: women with CF planning a pregnancy or confirmed pregnant should be screened for pre-existing CFRD with a 2-h 75-g OGTT if they have not had a normal screen in the last 6 months. Women with gestational diabetes should have an OGTT 6–12 weeks after the pregnancy ends [[Moran2010CFRD]]. Published HbA1c targets are <6.5% at conception and <6.0% in pregnancy [[Montemayor2022Pregnancy]].

Risk

Who is higher risk

Higher-risk disease is defined as FEV1 ≤60% predicted and/or BMI ≤21 kg/m² before pregnancy; more severe disease is associated with preterm and caesarean delivery and low birthweight. A preconception BMI of at least 22 kg/m² is described as the ideal goal [[Montemayor2022Pregnancy]]. In the Italian registry, the caesarean rate was 54.9% overall and 81.2% with CFRD [[Salvatore2026Registry]].

Contraception

Safe, but underused

The CFF position paper says contraception is safe overall but underused in people with CF [[Jain2026CFFReproductive]]. In the 561-person survey, modulators did not appear to reduce the effectiveness of hormonal contraception [[Godfrey2025Contraception]].

Talking early

Education from adolescence

Men first told about CF infertility after age 20 were more likely to be "very upset" than those told before 16 [[West2022SRH]]. Testosterone deficiency is also more common in men with CF [[Yoon2019Male]]. The position paper says fertility may be improving on modulators for females but not for males [[Jain2026CFFReproductive]].

After the birth

Breastfeeding and new parents

Clinicians report that more than half of people with CF choose to breastfeed, and calorie needs in lactation are higher than the roughly 500 kcal/day extra advised without CF [[Montemayor2022Pregnancy]]. Parenting adds burden on top of CF treatments [[Shteinberg2021Fertility]]. A review suggests eye screening and considering liver tests for exposed infants [[Montemayor2022Pregnancy]].
