Part 3 · Screening, diagnosis & monitoring

Lung function & monitoring

CF care is a rhythm of regular checks: blowing tests of the lungs, airway cultures, scans and a yearly review of other organs. Watch one spirometry blow build FEV1, FVC and percent predicted, then see why a gentler test, the lung clearance index, spots early disease that FEV1 misses.

Schematic: the curve shapes are drawing proportions and the litres are made-up numbers for the arithmetic. Reference equations and standards: [[Quanjer2012,Graham2019,Bhakta2023]]

Try it: FEV1 percent predicted bands

A teaching tool, not an assessmentSeverity bands quoted as published in a CF research paper: mild >70% FEV1, moderate 40–69%, severe <40%. [[Gee2003]] A single number says little alone: trends, symptoms, other tests and the care team's judgement matter, and bands differ between sources.

The 30% marker: in a Toronto cohort of 673 patients (1977–1989), FEV1 below 30% predicted was linked to a 2-year mortality above 50%, and each 10% fall doubled the 2-year risk of death (RR 2.0, 95% CI 1.9–2.2). This is a historic population statistic from before modern treatment and cannot predict what happens to any one person. [[Kerem1992]]

Mean FEV1 decline by age group, an epidemiologic cohort Established

Each segment is one age group's mean baseline and yearly decline, drawn across the group's ages; groups, not people. A high baseline was itself a risk factor for faster decline. [[Konstan2007]]

Lung clearance index: seeing early disease

In multiple-breath washout a person breathes a tracer gas in and then washes it out with ordinary breaths; the lung clearance index (LCI) reflects how unevenly ventilation is spread. It can be done across a wider age range than spirometry and is more sensitive to early damage. A 2013 ERS/ATS consensus set its standards. [[Robinson2013]]

Research tool

Valuable in trials

A CF Foundation workshop (2015) judged LCI a valuable trial outcome for preschool children and for older patients with normal FEV1, but found insufficient data to support it in routine clinical management. [[Subbarao2015]]

Modulators

LCI moves when FEV1 may not

ETI improved median LCI in children aged 6–11 from 3 months on (−1.0 for F/MF, −0.8 for F/F), with better MRI scores. [[Stahl2024]] In Danish children aged 6–17, mean LCI improved by 1.7 units at 12 months. [[Henriksen2025]] Lumacaftor–ivacaftor improved LCI (−1.6) without a significant FEV1 change. Early evidence [[Graeber2021]]

Imaging

AREST CF

Bronchiectasis starts in infancy

In 127 infants diagnosed by screening and followed with CT and lavage, bronchiectasis rose from 29.3% at 3 months to 61.5% at 3 years. Risk factors: meconium ileus (OR 3.17), respiratory symptoms (2.27), free neutrophil elastase in lavage (3.02) and gas trapping (2.05). [[Sly2013]]

CT technique

Lung volume matters

Expiratory CT scans had poor sensitivity (0.46) for bronchiectasis in young children; lung volume during CT should be standardised. [[Mott2013]]

MRI

Without radiation

Chest MRI scores of morphology and perfusion respond to CFTR modulators and are used as radiation-free research outcomes. Early evidence [[Stahl2024,Graeber2021]]
