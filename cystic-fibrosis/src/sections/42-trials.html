Modulator trials & eligibility

Fifteen years of trials, one bar at a time: how much each modulator moved lung function and sweat chloride, and against what. Then a teaching tool that shows what the US labels say for a genotype and an age.

Two numbers recur. ppFEV1 is the lung function test result (the volume blown out in the first second) as a percentage of the predicted value; trials report the change in percentage points against a comparator. Sweat chloride measures CFTR function directly in the skin. Across studies the two move together, but within a single ivacaftor study a person's fall in sweat chloride did not predict their FEV1 gain, so sweat chloride predicts response for a population, not an individual [[Fidler2017SwCl,Durmowicz2013SwCl]].

Read the comparator. A bar against placebo, a bar against another modulator and a before-and-after change in the same people are not the same kind of number, so they cannot be ranked against each other. Effects are trial averages for groups, not predictions for one person [[Fidler2017SwCl]]. STRIVE sweat chloride is through week 48; SKYLINE sweat chloride is from the Alyftrek label (Table 9) [[Ramsey2011STRIVE,FDA-Alyftrek]].

Eligibility as the US labels state it: a teaching tool

Teaching tool, not a treatment decisionThis tool summarises what the current US labels say, as this guide's sources describe them. Each label's variant list is long and "non-exhaustive", the Trikafta label says eligible variants "should not be the sole determinant" for treatment and that a clinical diagnosis of CF is required [[FDA-Trikafta]]. Only a CF care team can decide on treatment.

About 1 in 10 have no modulator option. Nonsense variants cause CF in about 10% of people, and variants that make no protein cannot be helped by drugs that act on the protein [[Kerem2014Ataluren]]. Nearly 90% of people with CF carry at least one F508del allele [[Middleton2019ETI]]; a later paper puts it at about 80%, and such estimates vary by registry [[Castellani2026RareVariants]]. Work for people without an option is in Research frontier.

How variants get onto a label. Every label counts a variant as responsive in vitro when chloride transport in Fischer rat thyroid cells rises by at least 10% of normal over baseline [[FDA-Kalydeco,FDA-Trikafta]]. On 17 May 2017 the FDA first expanded a label this way, for ivacaftor, without a new clinical trial [[Durmowicz2018]]. Access is another matter: in 2022 about 12% of people with CF worldwide (19,516 of 162,428) were receiving triple therapy [[Guo2022Access]].
