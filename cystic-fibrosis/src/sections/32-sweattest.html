The sweat test & genetic testing

The sweat test has been the cornerstone of CF diagnosis since 1959: make a small patch of skin sweat, collect enough of it, and measure its chloride. Genetic testing then names the CFTR variants, and CFTR2 says which of them cause CF.

Schematic, not to scale. The coil and meter fill are drawings; the minimum, the bands and the 88.0 mmol/L needle (PROMISE's mean before ETI, ages 12 and over) are published values. [[Farrell2008Dx,Farrell2017Dx,Nichols2022PROMISE]]

What the band means

Exam hookThe 2017 update lowered the bottom of the intermediate band from 40 to 30 mmol/L, because registries follow many people with confirmed CF whose sweat chloride is 30–40. [[Sosnay2017Nonscreened]]

Doing the test well

PointAs published
When, in a screened newbornAt ≥2 weeks of age, weight >2 kg and >36 weeks gestation at birth, ideally bilaterally; symptomatic newborns (e.g. meconium ileus) from 48 hours if enough sweat is collected. [[Farrell2008Dx]] A 2023 review summarises CF Foundation practice as testing after a positive screen by 4 weeks of age and not before 48 hours. [[DeCelieGermana2023]]
How much sweat"At least 75 mg of sweat obtained by the Gibson-Cooke method or at least 15 μL obtained by the Macroduct coil collection method". [[Farrell2008Dx]]
QualityQNS rate below 5% for infants over 3 months; detect chloride down to 10 mmol/L; never pool samples from two sites. [[Farrell2008Dx]] Mean QNS in infants ≤3 months: 7.2% (±7.6), 0–40% across centres, whatever the collection method. [[LeGrys2010]]
What is measuredSweat chloride is "the only analyte on which a diagnosis of CF should be based"; conductivity or osmolality "should not be used for diagnosing CF at the present time". [[Farrell2008Dx]] Conductivity is a screening test: false-positive rate 16.7% at a >50 mmol/L cutoff, best cutoff 69 mmol/L. [[Domingos2015]]

Genetic testing

CFTR2

Which variants cause CF

CFTR2 collected genotype and phenotype data on 39,696 people with CF. Of 159 variants with allele frequency ≥0.01%, 127 (80%) met clinical and functional criteria for causing disease. [[Sosnay2013CFTR2]] As of September 2024 it had interpreted 1167 variants and deemed 1085 CF-causing: these are expected to cause CF when in trans with another CF-causing variant. [[McGarry2025NBS]]

Testing

Panels, then the whole gene

ACMG updated its CFTR testing standard in 2020, noting that expanded panels and sequencing had become feasible. [[Deignan2020ACMG]] A laboratory that sequences but reports only a pre-set list is still running a "variant panel". Sequencing misses structural and deep intronic variants unless they are targeted. [[McGarry2025NBS]]

Interpretation

Genes are evidence, not the verdict

Two CF-causing variants in trans count as evidence of CFTR dysfunction; variants of variable or unknown consequence do not. [[Sosnay2017Genetics]] Even then, a screened baby's diagnosis is confirmed by showing CFTR dysfunction. [[Farrell2017Screened]]

Other tests of CFTR function

TestWhat it showsStatus
Nasal potential differenceIn 1981, NPD in 24 people with CF was more than 3 SD above controls and heterozygotes, and amiloride lowered it more. [[Knowles1981]] The CF pattern: a high baseline and a very low response to zero-chloride perfusate with isoproterenol; cutoffs differ between centres. [[Farrell2008Dx,Rowe2011]]Established
Intestinal current measurementOn rectal biopsies, validated in 130 subjects; a chloride secretory response cutoff of 34 µA/cm² separated pancreatic-sufficient CF from controls. Of 61 people with an unclear diagnosis, 6 were classed as CF and 55 as CF unlikely. [[Derichs2010]]Established
Rectal organoid swellingForskolin-induced swelling is strongly reduced in CF and tracks the currents in rectal biopsies, making it a candidate functional test. [[Dekkers2013FIS]]Early evidence

The CF Foundation's review of four decades of diagnosis warns against using NPD and ICM too early, before they are validated and standardised. [[Farrell2017History]]

Sweat chloride on CFTR modulators

Modulators lower sweat chloride, a sign the CFTR channel is working better. [[Nichols2022PROMISE]] In PROMISE, 487 people aged 12 and over starting elexacaftor–tezacaftor–ivacaftor (ETI) by prescription saw sweat chloride fall by 41.7 mmol/L (95% CI −43.8 to −39.6) at 6 months. [[Nichols2022PROMISE]]
