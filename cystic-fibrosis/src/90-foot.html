CF Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cystic fibrosis (CF) and CFTR modulators, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with CF, their families and caregivers.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Cirrhosis Companion, on the liver disease CF can cause, the Colorectal Cancer Companion, on a cancer that adults with CF are screened for earlier, and the TB Companion, on the mycobacteria family that also includes the NTM that can infect CF lungs.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
