Cystic fibrosis, explained

Cystic fibrosis (CF) is an inherited condition, affecting at least 100,000 people worldwide, in which a faulty CFTR channel leaves mucus thick and sticky in the lungs, pancreas and other organs. Once a disease of infancy, it is now a condition of adults too: in many countries more than half of people with CF are adults. [[Shteinberg2021,Elborn2016,Scotet2020]]

For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. The sweat-test bands, diagnostic flow, Punnett squares, FEV1 explainer and modulator tables are teaching aids, quoted as published. Finish with flashcards, memory aids and a quiz.

For people with CF, families and caregivers

Look for the In plain words boxes. Carrier status is common and nobody's fault, and CF care has changed fast: survival figures are registry population statistics, not predictions for one person. Bring questions to your own CF care team: the app reports what labels, guidelines and trials say, not what you should take, stop or change.
