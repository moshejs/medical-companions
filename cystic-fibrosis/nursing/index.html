Admission and assessment

ECFS standards call for admission within 24 hours for urgent cases and within 7 days for non-urgent ones 75. The CF nurse specialist's role includes education, advocacy and psychosocial support at key moments, among them the first admission and first IV course 75. During an exacerbation treated with IV antibiotics or systemic steroids, the ADA/CFF guideline advises screening with fasting and 2-hour post-meal glucose for the first 48 hours 307.

In plain wordsAn admission is a planned stretch of extra treatment. The team checks breathing, blood sugar and how you are coping 75,307.

Common misunderstandings and teaching points

Misunderstanding

"Gloves replace hand washing"

Hand hygiene applies before and after every patient contact, regardless of glove use 194,393. In a survey across 25 centres, inpatient nurses had generally lower knowledge scores and adherence to selected practices than other groups 395.

Misunderstanding

"Two people with CF can share a bay if both are well"

ECFS standards say people with CF should not share rooms, bathrooms or toilets in hospital, or be in contact in clinic, ward, pharmacy or radiology waiting areas 75.

Misunderstanding

"Enzymes are only for big meals"

The Creon label plans for about three meals plus two or three snacks, with snacks at about half the meal dose; capsules are swallowed whole, not crushed or chewed 135.

Misunderstanding

"Diabetes means cutting calories"

People with CFRD keep the high-calorie, high-salt, high-fat CF diet; calorie restriction is almost never appropriate 307,327. CFRD is treated with insulin 307.

Teaching point

Once-daily is not weaker

Once-daily and thrice-daily IV aminoglycosides were equally effective, and once-daily probably caused less nephrotoxicity in children Established 28,429. Evening dosing raised a urinary kidney-injury marker more than morning dosing 344.

Teaching point

Hearing loss can be silent

Reported tobramycin ototoxicity rates range from 0 to 56%, depending on how hearing is measured 200. In children given IV aminoglycosides, extended high-frequency audiometry found ototoxicity in 24% 7.

Teaching point

Ports last, but fail in known ways

165 ports in 109 people had a median survival of 1441 days and 0.58 complications per 1000 catheter-days: occlusion (33 devices), infection (23) and leakage (16) 389. At an Irish centre, 14 candidaemia cases were associated with lower FEV1 and more exacerbations 281.

Teaching point

Modulators have not ended exacerbations

ECFS 2024: monitoring and treating airway infection, inflammation and exacerbations "remains important" despite modulators 48.

The CF nurse specialist

The ECFS framework lists the core team as consultants, clinical nurse specialist, microbiologist, physiotherapist, dietitian, pharmacist, clinical psychologist, social worker and clinical geneticist 75. The nurse specialist provides education, advocacy and psychosocial support at key moments: the screening result and diagnosis, the first admission and IV course, a second diagnosis such as CFRD, transition, reproductive and pre- and postnatal care, and transplant and end of life 75.

Routine visits for stable disease are every 2–3 months, with an annual review that includes a full medical, dietetic, physiotherapy and psychosocial review and surveillance blood tests 75. Glucose tolerance testing starts at age 10 and bone density at 8–10 476. The CFF palliative-care guideline recommends screening for unmet palliative-care needs with the IPOS every year from age 12 223.

CF Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cystic fibrosis (CF) and CFTR modulators, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with CF, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, an airway-clearance routine, enzyme dosing or a care plan without your own CF care team. Sweat-chloride bands, the diagnostic flow, FEV1 percent predicted, genotype tables, the carrier calculator and the modulator eligibility explainer are for learning, never for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Doses, airway-clearance routines, enzyme dosing, infection-control guidance and guideline statements are quoted as published by the CF Foundation, ECFS, NICE and drug labels, never as instructions, and survival figures are registry population statistics, not predictions for any person.
