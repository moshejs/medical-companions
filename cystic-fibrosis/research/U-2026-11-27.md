## Findings

### clearance · Stopping nebulised mucoactive therapy on ETI for a year (CF STORM)
- CF STORM was a multicentre, open-label, randomised, controlled non-inferiority trial in 40 UK specialist CF centres, with eligibility, consent, randomisation and data collection inside the UK Cystic Fibrosis Registry. People aged 6 or over, on ETI for at least 3 months, with ppFEV1 of at least 40%, were randomised 1:1 to stop or continue their daily nebulised mucoactive therapy (dornase alfa, hypertonic saline or both). ISRCTN14081521. [[Davies2026STORM]]
- 1716 screened, 684 eligible, 591 randomised (297 stop, 294 continue) between July 2021 and August 2024. Median age 15.5 years (IQR 12.7–29.4); mean baseline ppFEV1 89.4% (SD 18.2). [[Davies2026STORM]]
- Primary outcome, change in ppFEV1 from baseline to 52 weeks, in those with data (195 per group): 0.15 (SD 6.85) after stopping vs −0.79 (SD 8.01) after continuing; adjusted mean difference 0.97 points (95% CI −0.51 to 2.45). The non-inferiority margin was −3 points, so stopping was non-inferior (Early evidence) [[Davies2026STORM]].
- Pulmonary exacerbations were more frequent after stopping: 0.90 vs 0.67 per person-year, incidence rate ratio 1.34 (95% CI 1.06 to 1.71). Exacerbations treated with intravenous antibiotics: 0.12 vs 0.08 per person-year, IRR 1.55 (95% CI 0.78 to 3.08), not significant. [[Davies2026STORM]]
- No serious adverse reactions to dornase alfa and no related serious adverse events with hypertonic saline; one death, unrelated to the intervention, in the stop group. [[Davies2026STORM]]
- The authors conclude that stopping is unlikely to worsen respiratory function in this population and recommend a more cautious approach for people with ppFEV1 of 40–69%. [[Davies2026STORM]]
- Limits visible in the abstract: open-label; 390 of 591 randomised (66%) had primary outcome data; exacerbations were registry-recorded.

### Exam hooks
- CF STORM: stopping dornase alfa or hypertonic saline on ETI was non-inferior for ppFEV1 at 52 weeks (+0.97 points), with more pulmonary exacerbations (IRR 1.34). [[Davies2026STORM]]

### In plain words
- A UK study followed people on a modulator for a year after they stopped their mucus-thinning nebulisers. Their lung function held up as well as in people who kept going, but they had somewhat more chest flare-ups. Any change is something to decide with the CF team. [[Davies2026STORM]]

## Open questions and controversies
- Do people on ETI still need daily mucoactive inhalers? SIMPLIFY (6 weeks) and CF STORM (52 weeks) both found stopping non-inferior for ppFEV1 [[MayerHamblett2023SIMPLIFY,Davies2026STORM]]; CF STORM recorded more pulmonary exacerbations after stopping and advises caution at ppFEV1 40–69% [[Davies2026STORM]]. Status: lung function looks stable over a year; the exacerbation signal and lower lung function remain open.
