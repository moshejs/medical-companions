Modulator trials & eligibility

Fifteen years of trials, one bar at a time: how much each modulator moved lung function and sweat chloride, and against what. Then a teaching tool that shows what the US labels say for a genotype and an age.

Two numbers recur. ppFEV1 is the lung function test result (the volume blown out in the first second) as a percentage of the predicted value; trials report the change in percentage points against a comparator. Sweat chloride measures CFTR function directly in the skin. Across studies the two move together, but within a single ivacaftor study a person's fall in sweat chloride did not predict their FEV1 gain, so sweat chloride predicts response for a population, not an individual 111,143.

Read the comparator. A bar against placebo, a bar against another modulator and a before-and-after change in the same people are not the same kind of number, so they cannot be ranked against each other. Effects are trial averages for groups, not predictions for one person 143. STRIVE sweat chloride is through week 48; SKYLINE sweat chloride is from the Alyftrek label (Table 9) 132,360.

2011 · the first modulator

STRIVE: a potentiator for G551D

STRIVE randomised 167 people aged 12 or older with at least one G551D variant to ivacaftor or placebo for 48 weeks. Through week 24, ppFEV1 was 10.6 percentage points higher with ivacaftor; through week 48, sweat chloride fell 48.1 mmol/L, weight rose 2.7 kg more and the chance of a pulmonary exacerbation was 55% lower. The effect appeared by week 2 360. In children aged 6–11 (ENVISION), ppFEV1 rose 12.5 points despite near-normal baseline values 86.

In plain wordsFor people whose CFTR reaches the surface but stays shut, a gate-opening pill improved breathing tests within two weeks 360.

Eligibility as the US labels state it: a teaching tool

Teaching tool, not a treatment decisionThis tool summarises what the current US labels say, as this guide's sources describe them. Each label's variant list is long and "non-exhaustive", the Trikafta label says eligible variants "should not be the sole determinant" for treatment and that a clinical diagnosis of CF is required 140. Only a CF care team can decide on treatment.

Alyftrek

vanzacaftor-tezacaftor-deutivacaftor · from 6 years

Depends on the label’s variant table

Needs a variant in the label's responsive table (about 560 listed); the phase 2 trial in F/MF adults raised ppFEV1 14.2 points 132,476.

About 1 in 10 have no modulator option. Nonsense variants cause CF in about 10% of people, and variants that make no protein cannot be helped by drugs that act on the protein 235. Nearly 90% of people with CF carry at least one F508del allele 295; a later paper puts it at about 80%, and such estimates vary by registry 57. Work for people without an option is in Research frontier.

How variants get onto a label. Every label counts a variant as responsive in vitro when chloride transport in Fischer rat thyroid cells rises by at least 10% of normal over baseline 135,140. On 17 May 2017 the FDA first expanded a label this way, for ivacaftor, without a new clinical trial 112. Access is another matter: in 2022 about 12% of people with CF worldwide (19,516 of 162,428) were receiving triple therapy 188.

CF Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on cystic fibrosis (CF) and CFTR modulators, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with CF, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, an airway-clearance routine, enzyme dosing or a care plan without your own CF care team. Sweat-chloride bands, the diagnostic flow, FEV1 percent predicted, genotype tables, the carrier calculator and the modulator eligibility explainer are for learning, never for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Doses, airway-clearance routines, enzyme dosing, infection-control guidance and guideline statements are quoted as published by the CF Foundation, ECFS, NICE and drug labels, never as instructions, and survival figures are registry population statistics, not predictions for any person.
