Pass date: 2026-11-13. Window: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13. Modules served: cure.

## Findings

### cure · Tau antibodies
- TOGETHER (NCT04867616, phase 2, UCB) is now published in full. 466 people aged 50 to 80 with prodromal-to-mild AD were randomised 1:1:1 to IV bepranemab 45 mg/kg (n=154), 90 mg/kg (n=155) or placebo (n=157) every 4 weeks for 76 weeks, at 85 sites; 460 were in the full analysis set. Established negative (peer-reviewed phase 2 RCT, sponsor-authored) [[Barton2026]].
- Primary endpoint, LS mean change in CDR-SB from baseline to week 80: 2.15 (90 mg/kg), 1.85 (45 mg/kg), 1.81 (placebo). Differences vs placebo: 0.34 (95% CI −0.09 to 0.78, p=0.12) and 0.04 (−0.40 to 0.47, p=0.87). Higher is worse, so neither dose slowed decline. The primary endpoint was not met [[Barton2026]].
- The authors describe the differences favouring bepranemab on tau PET and ADAS-Cog14 as "hypothesis-generating", to be tested in trials powered for tau PET and stratified by baseline tau burden and APOE ε4 status. The abstract gives no numbers for them [[Barton2026]].
- Safety: treatment-emergent adverse events in 139 (91%), 140 (91%) and 142 (90%) for 45 mg/kg, 90 mg/kg and placebo. No treatment-related neuroinflammatory signal clinically or on MRI. Adverse events leading to death: 4 (1%) in the combined bepranemab groups, 6 (4%) on placebo, none judged related [[Barton2026]].
- The site already said bepranemab missed its CDR-SB primary endpoint, citing a news report of the company's conference data and a conference abstract [[UCB2024,Maguire2025]]. The peer-reviewed paper confirms that and supplies the numbers. Nothing on the site is overturned.

### Exam hooks
- Bepranemab (mid-region tau antibody, TOGETHER): CDR-SB change at week 80 was 2.15 and 1.85 on drug vs 1.81 on placebo; primary endpoint not met [[Barton2026]].

### In plain words
- The full results of a trial of the tau antibody bepranemab are now published. After 80 weeks, people on the drug had declined about as much as people on a dummy drip [[Barton2026]].
