# U · Research update 2026-11-06

Pass date: 2026-11-06. Window: 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-06. Modules served: cure.

## Findings

### cure · Tau antibodies
- AuTonomy (NCT04619420, phase 2, Janssen): posdinemab (JNJ-63733657), given IV at 1000 mg or 3000 mg vs placebo, in 523 people with early AD (CDR global 0.5) and a positive tau PET. Primary endpoint, change in iADRS-MCI at week 104: 1000 mg −11.3, 3000 mg −11.6, placebo −12.1; LS mean differences 1.0 (95% CI −2.01 to 4.08) and 0 (−3.09 to 3.04), P=.986. Status: terminated, reason given as "Posdinemab did not achieve statistical significance in slowing clinical decline". Results first posted 5 Nov 2026. Established negative (registry results, not peer-reviewed) [[CTgovAuTonomy]].
- Secondary outcomes at week 104 were also null: CDR-SB differences −0.33 (−0.89 to 0.22) and 0.14 (−0.42 to 0.70); ADAS-Cog13 −0.2 and 0.6; ADCS-ADL-MCI 1.1 and 0.7, all CIs crossing 0. Tau PET: no difference in the composite region; meta-temporal SUVR lower on 3000 mg (difference −0.076, 95% CI −0.137 to −0.014, nominal P=.016), an unadjusted secondary result [[CTgovAuTonomy]].
- Serious adverse events in the double-blind period: 34/171 (1000 mg), 42/175 (3000 mg), 43/175 (placebo) [[CTgovAuTonomy]].
- Posdinemab is a humanised IgG1 antibody that binds tau phosphorylated at threonine 217 (pT217) in the proline-rich (mid) domain [[Galpern2024]]. That makes it the second mid-region tau antibody, after bepranemab, to miss its primary endpoint. The site says newer antibodies "aim at a different part of the protein", and that now needs qualifying.

### Exam hooks
- Mid-region tau antibodies have also failed: bepranemab (TOGETHER) and posdinemab (AuTonomy, pT217, iADRS at 104 weeks) [[UCB2024,CTgovAuTonomy]].

### In plain words
- A drug company tested a tau antibody that grabs the middle of the tau protein. After two years, people on it declined at the same rate as people on a dummy drip [[CTgovAuTonomy]].
