# U · Research update 2026-10-30

Pass date: 2026-10-30. Window: 2026-10-29 to 2026-10-30. Modules served: genes.

## Findings

### genes · GWAS
- Multi-ancestry GWAS (109,479 cases, 74,141 proxy cases, 2,131,799 controls and 499,708 proxy controls, diverse ancestries): 118 loci in the multi-ancestry analysis and 9 more in ancestry-specific analyses, 48 of them new. Genes upregulated in microglia and downregulated in neurons, in cases compared with controls, were enriched for AD genetic risk (Established) [[Uffelmann2026]].
- The same study improved polygenic prediction and estimated SNP heritability at 16%, "nearly threefold higher than previous estimates" (Early evidence) [[Uffelmann2026]]. SNP heritability is not the twin estimate (58–79%) in the same module and is not shown there.
- This replaces the claim that Bellenguez 2022 (75 loci) is the largest study [[Bellenguez2022]].

### Exam hooks
- Largest AD GWAS (2026, multi-ancestry): 118 loci plus 9 ancestry-specific, 48 new; risk converges on microglia [[Uffelmann2026]].

### In plain words
- A study of more than 2.8 million people from many backgrounds found over 100 places in our DNA linked to Alzheimer's risk. Many point to the brain's immune cells [[Uffelmann2026]].
