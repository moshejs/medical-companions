## Findings

### bpsd · Medicines for agitation
- Auvelity (dextromethorphan–bupropion) US label, efficacy supplement approved 30 Apr 2026: new indication 1.2, "treatment of agitation associated with dementia due to Alzheimer's disease". Limitation of use: "not indicated as an as needed ("prn") treatment". Boxed warning: suicidal thoughts and behaviours (antidepressant class) [[FDAAuvelity2026]].
- Study 3 (NCT03226522), 5 weeks: CMAI change −14.9 on Auvelity (N=152, baseline 60.8) vs −11.6 on placebo (N=156, baseline 59.3); LS mean difference −3.3 (95% CI −5.8 to −0.8). A bupropion-alone arm (N=49) was stopped early for futility. Study 4 (NCT04947553), randomised withdrawal in responders: time to relapse significantly longer on Auvelity; no hazard ratio in the label text. Established (regulatory approval) [[FDAAuvelity2026]].
- Approved 30 Apr 2026, before the previous passes' windows; added here because the site named brexpiprazole as the only approved option. Brexpiprazole remains the first drug approved for this.

### Exam hooks
- Two US-approved drugs for agitation in Alzheimer's dementia: brexpiprazole (2023, antipsychotic, mortality boxed warning) and dextromethorphan–bupropion (2026, antidepressant boxed warning); neither is for prn use [[FDARexulti2026,FDAAuvelity2026]].

### In plain words
- Since April 2026 a second medicine is approved in the US for agitation in Alzheimer's. It is not an antipsychotic. In its trial, agitation scores improved a little more than on a dummy pill [[FDAAuvelity2026]].
