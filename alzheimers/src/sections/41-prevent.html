What the prevention trials found

A risk factor linked to dementia in observational studies is not proof that changing it prevents dementia. Randomised trials test that. Play the plot to bring in each trial's main result; many are null or small.

Dots are estimates; lines are 95% confidence intervals. A line that crosses the dashed no-effect line means the result is compatible with no effect. Units differ between rows, so compare each with its own zero.
