What the history leaves open

Debated

Why a separate disease?

Historians still debate why Kraepelin made a new disease from so few cases; one explanation is the rivalry between the Munich and Prague schools. The line between "presenile" Alzheimer's and "senile dementia" was dropped in 1978. [[BermejoPareja2024]]

Debated

The amyloid cascade, three decades on

Hardy and Higgins proposed the amyloid cascade hypothesis in 1992. A 2024 history review notes that the dominant amyloid hypothesis has so far yielded "poor therapeutic results". [[HardyHiggins1992,BermejoPareja2024]] See Amyloid, animated for the evidence on both sides.

Now established

Mixed causes in the oldest old

The same 2024 review concludes that mixed dementia, with Alzheimer's changes alongside other brain diseases, is now established as the most frequent cause of dementia in the oldest old. [[BermejoPareja2024]]

Sources disagree

How old was Auguste D.?

One account gives her age as 50 when her symptoms began in 1901; another says she was 56 when she died in 1906. The sources disagree slightly. [[Hippius2003,BermejoPareja2024]]
