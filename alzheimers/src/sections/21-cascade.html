Part 2 · Course and diagnosis

Twenty years before symptoms

Alzheimer's changes in the brain long before anyone notices. Biomarkers turn abnormal in a broadly predictable order: amyloid, then tau and nerve-cell damage, then thinking. Play the clock, and switch between families with a dominant gene and the common late-onset form.

Where is this person on the 2024 map?

In 2024 an Alzheimer's Association workgroup proposed two scales: a biological stage from biomarkers (A to D, by how far tau has spread on PET) and a clinical stage from symptoms (0 to 6). The International Working Group (IWG) disagreed about what to call people without symptoms. Set the biomarkers and symptoms, or pick an example. [[Jack2024,Dubois2024]]

How the model and the definitions changed

Hypothetical model

Jack 2010, revised 2013

The 2010 model put amyloid first and neurodegeneration (CSF tau, MRI atrophy, FDG) after a variable lag, tracking symptoms. The 2013 update indexed people by time, added person-to-person variation, reordered some markers, and proposed that tau may start on its own in sporadic disease and be accelerated by amyloid. [[Jack2010,Jack2013]]

Measured

DIAN, and its caveats

DIAN's numbers come from 128 mutation carriers placed on a clock set by the parent's age at onset. Longitudinal data from 217 people confirmed amyloid changes first, but disagreed with the cross-sectional timing of CSF tau by more than 10 years and showed CSF p-tau181 falling near onset. The authors warn the results may not apply to sporadic Alzheimer's. [[Bateman2012,McDade2018]]

Definitions moved

2011, 2018, 2024

In 2011, diagnosis was clinical and biomarkers were not advised for routine use. In 2018, a research framework defined Alzheimer's biologically and required A+ and T+. In 2024, one abnormal Core 1 result, such as amyloid alone, is enough. [[McKhann2011,Jack2018,Jack2024]]

Modelled

How long each phase lasts

Across 6 cohorts (3,268 people), someone with preclinical Alzheimer's at age 70 was estimated to spend about 10 years preclinical, 4 with MCI and 6 with dementia. Total duration ranged from 24 years starting at 60 to 15 years starting at 80. [[Vermunt2019]]

Exam hookAmyloid is necessary for the biological label but tracks symptoms poorly; neurodegeneration tracks symptom severity, and tau stage carries prognosis. In cognitively unimpaired people, against A−T−, the hazard ratio for progressing to dementia was 1.5 for stage A, 5.6 for medial temporal tau and 39.9 for neocortical tau. [[Jack2010,Jack2024]]
