Anti-amyloid antibodies over time

Lecanemab and donanemab are antibodies infused into the blood that help the brain's immune cells clear amyloid plaque. Follow the published trials month by month: plaque falls fast, ARIA (brain swelling or small bleeds on MRI) shows up early, and the clinical benefit appears slowly and stays modest.

Published trial and label information only. Everything here reports what the trials, labels and regulators published. Whether a particular person is eligible, and whether treatment is right for them, are decisions for specialist clinicians together with the person and their family.
