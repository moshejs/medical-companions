Scoring a brain at autopsy: the ABC score

The 2012 NIA-AA guidelines rate three things and combine them into one level of Alzheimer's neuropathologic change. Choose a rating for each and read the result. [[Montine2012,Hyman2012]]

A · amyloid plaques (Thal phase)
B · tangles (Braak stage)
C · neuritic plaques (CERAD)
How the levels combine

Not, Low, Intermediate, High

A0 is always "Not". With B0 or B1 the level is at most "Low". "High" needs A3, B3 and C2 or C3 together. "Intermediate" or "High" is considered a sufficient explanation for dementia. [[Montine2012]]

The guidelines recognise that Alzheimer's changes can be present without cognitive impairment, and they set protocols for common co-pathologies: Lewy body disease, vascular brain injury, hippocampal sclerosis and TDP-43 inclusions. [[Hyman2012,Montine2012]]

In plain wordsAfter death, a pathologist scores how much amyloid, how far the tangles have spread and how many damaged plaques there are. Together they say how likely Alzheimer's explains the dementia.
