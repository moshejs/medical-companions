Words matter

Dementia Australia's language guidelines ask writers to put the person first. The table shows some of the swaps they suggest. [[DementiaAustralia2021]]

Stigma is common. In a global survey of almost 70,000 people, 62% of healthcare practitioners thought dementia is a normal part of ageing, 35% of carers had hidden a relative's diagnosis from at least one person, and over 85% of people living with dementia said their opinion had not been taken seriously. [[ADI2019]]

Where sources disagree. The guidelines advise against "carer burden", preferring neutral wording such as "the impact of supporting someone". Research still measures "caregiver burden", for example with the Zarit Burden Interview used in trials. This page uses "impact" in its own words and "burden" only when naming a research measure.
