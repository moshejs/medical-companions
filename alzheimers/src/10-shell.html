Alzheimer's disease at a glance

What it is
Dementia means problems with memory and thinking that get in the way of everyday tasks such as driving, cooking or cleaning. Alzheimer's disease is its most common cause. [[AA2025Facts]] In the brain, tangles of a protein called tau first appear at the entrance to the memory circuit [[Braak1991]], while deposits of amyloid start in the outer cortex. [[Thal2002]]
Who gets it
Age is the greatest risk factor; the other two are the APOE e4 gene and family history. [[AA2025Facts]] About one in three Americans aged 85 or older has Alzheimer's dementia. [[AA2025Facts]] Fewer than 1% of all cases are passed down directly in families. [[Bateman2011]]
Main symptoms
Complex tasks such as paying bills or planning meals become hard. [[Reisberg1988,AA2025Facts,Jack2024,OBryant2008,Perneczky2006]] Later, help is needed with dressing, then bathing, then using the toilet. [[Reisberg1988,Jack2024]]
How it is diagnosed
Diagnosis starts with a history of decline, confirmed by someone who knows the person well. [[Arvanitakis2019,McKhann2011]] A brain scan looks for strokes, tumours and other causes. [[Knopman2001,Arvanitakis2019,Frisoni2010,Nakajima2021]] Amyloid PET scans, spinal fluid tests and, since 2025, a blood test for specialised care can detect the disease itself. [[Clark2012,Hansson2019,FDA2025,Palmqvist2024,Arvanitakis2019]]
Main treatments
Cholinesterase inhibitors boost the remaining brain signal; their labels state no evidence that they change the course of the disease. [[Noetzli2013,FDAAricept2021,FDAExelonPatch2024,FDAGalantamineER2025]] In trials of antibodies that clear amyloid, lecanemab slowed decline by 27% over 18 months, with swelling on MRI in 12.6% [[vanDyck2023,Eisai2022CTAD,LeqembiLabel2026,Perry2026]], and donanemab by 29%, though treated groups in both trials still declined. [[Sims2023,KisunlaLabel2025]]
Outlook
People diagnosed at 65 or older live 4 to 8 years on average, and some live 20 years. [[AA2025Facts]] Pneumonia is the most common immediate cause of death. [[AA2025Facts,Brookmeyer2002]]
