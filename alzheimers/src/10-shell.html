Alzheimer's disease, explained

Alzheimer's disease is the most common cause of dementia. Here it is in moving pictures: watch a protein get cut the wrong way, follow tangles spreading through the brain, see the changes that begin many years before the first forgotten name, and look honestly at what the newest drugs do and do not achieve.

Schematic. Amyloid rises first, tau follows, and memory changes come last.
