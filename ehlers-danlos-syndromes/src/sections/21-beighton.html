Beighton score

Nine points from five quick manoeuvres: four done on both sides, one for the trunk. Watch each joint pass (or miss) its threshold, then try the teaching calculator with the 2017 age-adjusted cut-offs and the five-part questionnaire.

How common is a "high" score?

Generalised joint hypermobility is commonest in young children, falls quickly through childhood and more slowly in adulthood, and is more common in girls and women at every age. Studies differ in cut-off and method, so compare within a study, not across them. [[Malek2021C,Remvig2007,Clinch2011]]

Each figure is 1 in 100 people, rounded. Population statistics, not a forecast for any one person.

The nine points, item by item

Item (points)How it is measuredCommon measurement problemsReliability reported
1. Little finger (2: left and right)Passive dorsiflexion of the fifth finger at the knuckle (metacarpophalangeal joint) to 90° or more. [[Thwaites2022,Malfait2017]]Sources word the threshold differently ("beyond 90°" or "≥ 90°"). Two-thirds of the scored joints are in the upper limbs. [[Malek2021C,Thwaites2022]]Item kappa 0.60 or more for the fifth fingers, lower than most items (above 0.80). Video estimates against a goniometer: rho 0.786. [[Juul-Kristensen2007,Sabo2024]]
2. Thumb to forearm (2)Passive apposition of the thumb to the flexor (volar) surface of the same-side forearm. [[Thwaites2022,Carroll2023]]Only one plane of motion is tested, and the item says nothing about how severe the hypermobility is. [[Malek2021C]]Item kappas were generally above 0.80 in a three-phase study. [[Juul-Kristensen2007]]
3. Elbow (2)Hyperextension of the elbow by 10° or more. [[Thwaites2022,Carroll2023]]A 10° angle is hard to judge by eye; the score was shown valid in children "when goniometry is used". [[Smits-Engelsman2011]]Item kappa 0.60 or more for the elbows. With a structured protocol in degrees, raters agreed within 5°. Video rho 0.722. [[Juul-Kristensen2007,Schlager2018,Sabo2024]]
4. Knee (2)Hyperextension of the knee by 10° or more. [[Thwaites2022,Carroll2023]]Major joints such as the shoulders, hips and ankles are not scored at all. [[Malek2021C]]Video estimates against a goniometer: rho 0.608. [[Sabo2024]]
5. Palms flat (1)Forward flexion of the trunk so both palms rest flat on the floor, knees locked straight, feet together. [[Thwaites2022,Carroll2023]]Can be trained (as in ballet) and is limited by short hamstrings: 84.2% of over 200 hypermobile people (score 5 or more) could not do it. [[Malek2021C]]Whole-score inter-rater ICC 0.72–0.82 in 49 healthy adults measured in degrees. [[Schlager2018]]

How far can the score be trusted?

Established

Reliable, but not perfect

A 2021 systematic review (24 studies, 1,333 people aged 4–71) found 12 of 19 inter-rater kappas (63.2%) substantial to almost perfect. A narrative review reports most kappas at 0.4–0.8, and below 0.6 has been called inadequate. A 2017 COSMIN review rated every study "fair" or "poor". [[Bockhorn2021,Malek2021C,Juul-Kristensen2017]]

Validity

Not the whole picture

The original paper gives no evidence-based reason for its joints or for the usual adult cut-off of 4. In over 100 people strongly suspected of EDS, 45 scored below 4, yet their shoulder abduction was still 20° above normal. Agreement with clinicians' judgement of systemic laxity was poor (kappa 0.39–0.45). [[Malek2021C,Bernhoff2022]]

Age and sex

Scores fall with age

The chance of meeting GJH fell by 5.5% per year of age in one dataset; among 200 people over 70, nobody scored above 2. In one cohort most diagnosed people over 33 no longer reached 4. That is why the 2017 cut-off drops to 4 after 50 and why past flexibility can be asked about. [[Malek2021C,Fairweather2025]]

Early evidence

Apps, video and self-report

A video-based tool flagged 91.9% of people with GJH but its specificity was only 42.4%. Self-reported line drawings matched blinded goniometry well in 50 people (sensitivity 0.87, specificity 0.99). Neither is validated for diagnosis. [[Sabo2026,Cooper2018]]

Other scales

Beyond nine points

The Upper Limb Hypermobility Assessment Tool scores 12 items (cut-point 7 or more; ICC 0.92). The paediatric working group recommends it and the Lower Limb Assessment Score for detailed phenotyping beyond the Beighton score. [[Nicholson2018,Tofts2023]]
