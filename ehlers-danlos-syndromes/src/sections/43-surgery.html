Surgery & anaesthesia

Fragile skin and vessels, unstable joints, a jaw that can dislocate and local anaesthetics that may wear off early: an operation in EDS needs planning. What matters differs by type, and much of the advice rests on case series and expert opinion. [[Wiesmann2014,Amaral2026]]

Evidence base: EDS anaesthesia knowledge "is fragmented and consists mostly of case reports, small case series and expert opinion". The 2026 modified-Delphi panel (15 experts, 3 rounds) produced the first consensus-based anaesthesia guidelines for EDS, with type-specific advice, so vEDS precautions are not applied to every type by default. Teeth, gums and jaw

Periodontal EDS

A complement gene, not collagen

pEDS is autosomal dominant, caused by variants in C1R or C1S (subunits of complement C1). It brings rapidly progressive gum disease from childhood or the teens, lack of attached gingiva, darker patches on the shins and easy bruising. [[KapfererSeebacher2016]]

Outcomes

Early tooth loss

In 35 adults with confirmed pEDS, 94% had stage 3–4 periodontitis or no teeth. The first permanent tooth was lost at a median age of 21.5 years (range 13–43). [[Lepperdinger2022]]

Care as published

A periodontist from early childhood

GeneReviews advises regular care by a periodontist from early childhood, with supportive care (re-evaluation, hygiene instruction, debridement) every 3–6 months. Prosthetic rehabilitation after tooth loss is challenging because most alveolar bone is destroyed. [[KapfererSeebacherGR-pEDS]]

hEDS

Palate, crowding and the jaw joint

GeneReviews lists orthodontic, maxillofacial and ENT management for a narrow palate, crowded teeth and TMJ laxity; periodontal disease gets standard treatment. Oral and TMJ involvement varies across types and interacts with wider musculoskeletal problems. [[Hakim2024GeneReviews,Mitakides2017]]

Early evidence

Dental numbing

In a survey, 88% of people with EDS recalled inadequate dental pain prevention vs 33% without EDS. Among named agents, articaine had the highest reported success (30%), then bupivacaine (25%) and mepivacaine (22%). Recall and selection bias apply. [[Schubart2019LA]]

Tell the dentist

Plan ahead

Wiesmann advises recording any history of failed local anaesthesia, for example during dental work, at pre-operative assessment. [[Wiesmann2014]] In a controlled trial, lidocaine numbed people with EDS as well as controls at 5 minutes but appeared to wear off sooner. [[Bourne2026RAPM]]
