Mind, belief and community

Established

Anxiety and low mood

In a Swedish nationwide cohort (1,771 people with EDS against 17,710 matched comparators), EDS was associated with depression (RR 3.4), ADHD (RR 5.6), autism (RR 7.4), bipolar disorder (RR 2.7) and attempted suicide (RR 2.1), but not with suicide or schizophrenia. The cause is unknown; siblings without EDS also had raised risks of ADHD and depression. [[Cederlof2016]]

A meta-analysis of 14 studies found higher odds of anxiety (OR 4.39), depression (OR 4.10) and panic disorder (OR 6.72) with benign joint hypermobility syndrome. [[Smith2014Rheum]]

Established

Pain, not hypermobility, tracks distress

In a chart review of 106 people with classical or hypermobile EDS, 42.5% had a psychiatric disorder. Having any pain symptom was strongly associated with one (OR 9.68); joint hypermobility itself was not. A Consortium review recommends integrated psychiatric and psychological care alongside physiotherapy. [[Hershenfeld2016,Bulbena2017]]

Validation

Being believed

In a study across Europe and North America, 73% of survey respondents questioned the extent or reality of their own pain and linked it to repeated dismissal, often from childhood. The authors found clinicians can reinforce self-doubt without meaning to when they talk about diagnostic uncertainty, and recommend "epistemic humility". Perinatal guidance calls for "compassionate communication validating hEDS/HSD experiences". [[Halverson2025,Pezaro2024]]

Community

Peer support and the zebra

The zebra is the EDS community's symbol. In a survey of 102 members of EDS support groups recruited through Facebook, 448 social connections were mapped; in-person contact was the main mode, and EDS was discussed daily. In interviews, people named building social networks and learning about the condition as ways of coping that worked. [[Callen2025,Bennett2021]]

Early evidence

Online programmes

A two-week online mindfulness pilot (76 completers) improved the SF-36 mental component score, without a control group. The HOPE online pain programme for hEDS/HSD was judged feasible and acceptable; benefits seemed stronger for people newer to their diagnosis. [[Lattimore2023,Chew2025H]]

Vascular EDS

Travel

The international vEDS consensus recommends an individual emergency plan, a "vascular EDS passport" carrying the diagnosis and management plan, for use when travelling. GeneReviews adds a MedicAlert bracelet or necklace or an emergency contact letter. In the UK national service every patient receives an emergency information card. [[Byers2017,Byers2025GeneReviews,Bowen2023vEDS]]

Not sourced

Driving

The research pass behind this module found no validated, EDS-specific source on driving, so this guide gives no driving advice. Rules differ by country and are set by licensing authorities, not by these papers.

Gap

Growing older

A scoping review found no study aimed at ageing in hEDS or HSD, and notes that older adults with several conditions may have undiagnosed hEDS/HSD. [[Anderson2023]]
