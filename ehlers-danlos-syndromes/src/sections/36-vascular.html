Vascular EDS

Vascular EDS (vEDS) is a rare, autosomal dominant type caused by changes in COL3A1, the gene for type III collagen, a major protein of artery walls and hollow organs such as the bowel and the uterus. It affects an estimated 1 in 50,000 to 1 in 200,000 people. Problems are rare in childhood and become common in adult life. [[Byers2017,Pepin2000]]

If you or someone you care for has vEDS

GeneReviews advises that people with vEDS seek immediate medical attention for sudden, unexplained pain, and carry documentation of the genetic diagnosis, such as a MedicAlert bracelet, an emergency letter or a "vascular EDS passport". This page is educational and does not replace that plan. [[Byers2025GeneReviews,Byers2017]]

Medicines that may lower the risk

Each icon is one randomised participant; coloured icons had the primary outcome. [[Ong2010,Jeunemaitre2025]]

Early evidence

Celiprolol and BBEST

Celiprolol is a β1-blocker with β2-agonist action. BBEST randomised 53 people with clinical vEDS (25 celiprolol, 28 no treatment) at 9 centres in France and Belgium. The protocol gave it twice daily, uptitrated by 100 mg every 6 months to a maximum of 400 mg/day. Arterial rupture or dissection: 20% vs 50%, HR 0.36 (95% CI 0.15–0.88), mean follow-up 47 months. [[Ong2010,NCT00190411]]

Limitations

Why BBEST is not the last word

  • Open-label (only the endpoints were assessed blind). [[Ong2010]]
  • Stopped early for benefit after a mean 47 months, so the number of events is small. [[Ong2010]]
  • Small: 53 people, of whom only 33 had a confirmed COL3A1 variant. [[Ong2010,Shalhub2020]]
  • The 2022 ACC/AHA guideline says the data were considered insufficient for FDA approval; celiprolol has no FDA approval for any indication. [[Isselbacher2022,openFDA-Celiprolol]]
  • Not available in several countries, and dizziness or low blood pressure sometimes limit it. [[Buso2024,Saputra2025]]
Early evidence

Cohorts on treatment

In a French referral cohort (144 people), 90.3% were on celiprolol by the end; those treated survived better (p=0.0004), and 400 mg/day did better than lower doses. In a Swedish cohort the annual major-event risk on celiprolol was 4.7%, close to BBEST's treated arm (5%) and below its control arm (12%). These are observational: medicine and overall specialist care cannot be separated. [[Frank2019JACC,Baderkhan2021]]

Early evidence

ARCADE: adding irbesartan

A double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of irbesartan in 57 adults already on celiprolol: the composite arterial outcome occurred in 27.6% vs 53.6% over 2 years (HR 0.42, 95% CI 0.17–0.99). The benefit was not explained by the 5.4 mm Hg fall in systolic pressure. [[Jeunemaitre2025,NCT02597361]]

Ongoing

DiSCOVER (NCT05432466)

A phase 3, double-blind, decentralised trial of celiprolol 200 mg twice daily versus placebo in COL3A1-positive vEDS, ages 15–64. Status: recruiting; started November 2022, estimated enrolment 150, estimated primary completion March 2029. Primary endpoint: time to the first vEDS-related event needing medical attention. [[NCT05432466]] More in Toward better treatment and Medicines.

Hypothesis

Mouse models disagree

In two vEDS mouse models, blocking ERK1/2 or PKCβ signalling prevented aortic rupture. In those models celiprolol "accelerated rather than reduced" death from aortic rupture, while another model suggested benefit. Animal data only. [[Bowen2020JCI,Buso2024]]

The plan: card, blood pressure, imaging, pregnancy

Emergency card

What the card or passport carries

  • The diagnosis and documentation of the COL3A1 result. [[Byers2025GeneReviews]]
  • The individual management plan and how to reach the specialist team, for use when travelling. [[Byers2017]]
  • Sudden, unexplained pain needs immediate medical attention. [[Byers2025GeneReviews]]
  • As published: arteriography only to find life-threatening bleeding before surgery; no routine colonoscopy without concerning symptoms; elective surgery only if the benefit is substantial. [[Byers2025GeneReviews]]
  • For anaesthetists: avoid central and arterial lines where possible, use ultrasound if a central line is unavoidable, and avoid neuraxial blocks. [[Wiesmann2014]] See Surgery & anaesthesia.
Blood pressure & life

Keeping strain off the arteries

The 2017 consensus says to "maintain blood pressure in the normal range and treat hypertension aggressively", adapt lifestyle to minimise injury and build a care team at an experienced centre. GeneReviews lists collision sports, heavy lifting and extreme weight training as things to avoid. Stopping smoking and controlling hypertension are stressed for heritable aortic disease. [[Byers2017,Byers2025GeneReviews,CalderonMartinez2025]]

Surveillance imaging

Imaging as published

  • 2017 consensus: Doppler ultrasound, CTA (low-radiation) or MRA "if feasible on an annual basis" (expert consensus). [[Byers2017]]
  • ACC/AHA 2022, typical protocol: baseline head-to-pelvis CT or MRI; yearly for any dilated or dissected segment; every 2 years if the first imaging is normal. The right interval is uncertain. [[Isselbacher2022]]
  • An Italian expert centre: every 12 to 18 months with office and 24-hour blood pressure, echocardiography and whole-body CT or MR angiography. [[Buso2024]]
  • European expert centres all monitor, but intervals and methods differ. [[vanDeLaar2022]]
Pregnancy

A high-risk pregnancy

Pregnancy-related deaths occurred in 30 of 565 deliveries (5.3%) in family pedigrees, and a 2024 review found 5.7% maternal mortality per pregnancy. In the recent UK cohort, 10% (5 of 48) of women without later-onset variants had a life-threatening or fatal vascular event, and none had a uterine rupture; older data may overestimate today's risk. Planning with a specialist team matters. [[Murray2014GenetMed,Haem2024,Bowen2023vEDS]] See Pregnancy & parenthood.

Diagnosis

Confirm the gene

The consensus recommends finding the causative COL3A1 variant before using the label. In 11 US institutions, 9.8% of people diagnosed clinically had non-pathogenic changes, and the authors wrote that a diagnosis without confirmatory testing "cannot be trusted". Testing is prompted by a family history, arterial rupture or dissection under 40, unexplained sigmoid colon rupture, or spontaneous pneumothorax with other features. [[Byers2017,Shalhub2020,Wang2026OJRD]] See Look-alikes & red flags.

Children

Found early, through family

In London's national service, 13 of 63 children with vEDS had events in childhood, the first at a median age of 11. 55% of the whole cohort were diagnosed through family genetic testing. Each child of an affected parent has a 50% chance of inheriting the variant. [[Wilkinson2025,Byers2025GeneReviews]] See Inheritance & genetic testing.
