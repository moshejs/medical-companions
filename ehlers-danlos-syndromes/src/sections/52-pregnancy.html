The evidence disagrees for hEDS

Early evidence

A volunteer survey: raised risks

An international online survey of 947 women with hEDS or HSD (1,338 pregnancies) reported preterm birth in 15.45% of babies (population figure quoted 7.8%), preterm rupture of membranes in 7.03% (under 3%), precipitate labour under 3 hours in 34.29%, postpartum haemorrhage in 20.60% (6.38%) and hyperemesis gravidarum in 25.48% (3%). These are self-reports from volunteers recruited online. [[Pearce2023]]

Established

A national register: no excess

A Swedish nationwide register study of 314 births to women with joint hypermobility syndrome or EDS found no increase in preterm birth (aOR 0.6, 95% CI 0.3–1.2), caesarean section (aOR 0.9) or stillbirth. Induction of labour (aOR 2.6) and amniotomy (aOR 3.8) were more common among the 62 births to women with an EDS code. [[Sundelin2017]]

US inpatient data (EDS in 7 per 100,000 births, all types including vascular) showed raised odds of preterm birth (OR 1.47), cervical incompetence (OR 3.11) and caesarean delivery (OR 1.55), and of maternal death (OR 9.04, 95% CI 1.27–64.27), an interval that wide because there were very few deaths. None of these studies separates EDS types well except the vEDS-specific ones. In a case series of eight women with hEDS, most pregnancies were uncomplicated under multidisciplinary care. [[Spiegel2022,Karthikeyan2018]]
