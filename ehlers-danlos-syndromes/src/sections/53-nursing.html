Active stand: plotting lying and standing observations

A teaching chart, not a diagnostic tool. The 2019 NIH consensus defines the heart-rate criterion for POTS as a sustained rise of at least 30 beats/min within 10 minutes of standing (at least 40 at ages 12–19), without a sustained systolic fall of 20 mmHg or more, with orthostatic symptoms for at least 3 months. It notes that POTS can usually be identified by recording heart rate and blood pressure lying down and during a 10-minute stand. [[Vernino2021]]

Examples

Example values are invented for teaching. In 45 young women, POTS on an active stand was found in 47% with generalised HSD, 27% with hEDS and 13% of controls, but head-up tilt gave different figures (43%, 7% and 7%): the method matters. [[Peebles2022]] See POTS & the autonomic link.

Risk of skin damage from adhesives and cuffsInspect skin; record bruises and where they came from.Adhesive tapes and dressings should be easy to remove, or avoided. Be aware that repeated non-invasive cuff inflations can cause bruising and haematoma.Fragile skin risks severe damage.[[Wiesmann2014]]
Risk of subluxation or dislocation during handlingAsk about past dislocations and which joints are unstable.Position and mobilise carefully; pad to reduce shear and pressure; use wedges, pillows and bed adaptations; take care with the jaw during mask ventilation.Subluxations are common and may occur with minimal trauma; physical treatments must avoid causing them.[[Wiesmann2014,Pezaro2024,Hakim2024GeneReviews]]
Acute and chronic painValidated tools used in EDS research: Multidimensional Pain Inventory and Sickness Impact Profile (adults); PedsQL 4.0 and Pediatric Pain Questionnaire (children). Parent report is a reasonable proxy (r = 0.6–0.84).Validate the person's experience; use multidisciplinary care, including equipment such as cushions, compression garments and braces.Nearly 90% report chronic pain in one study cited by the 2017 pain review.[[Rombaut2011,Pacey2015,Chopra2017,Pezaro2024]]
Local anaesthetic that fails or wears offAsk about earlier failed local anaesthesia, for example at the dentist.Record the history before procedures; perinatal guidance states that "higher doses of local anesthesia may be needed".Lidocaine numbed 53% of people with EDS at 30 minutes vs 80% of controls.[[Wiesmann2014,Pezaro2024,Bourne2026RAPM]]
Orthostatic intoleranceHeart rate and blood pressure lying down and over a 10-minute stand.Report against the published criteria; non-drug care in hEDS includes education, avoiding triggers, fitness and hydration.Orthostatic intolerance in 74% of 39 women with EDS-HT vs 34% of controls.[[Vernino2021,Hakim2017,DeWandele2014Mech]]
vEDS: risk of arterial, bowel or uterine ruptureSudden, unexplained pain; signs of bleeding or perforation; the emergency card.Treat sudden unexplained pain as an emergency and escalate. Arteriography is discouraged except to find life-threatening bleeding before surgery; avoid central venous access and arterial puncture whenever possible; ultrasound guidance is "mandatory" if a central line is needed.Arterial dissection or rupture, bowel perforation or organ rupture are the presenting signs in most adults; invasive procedures are "potentially dangerous".[[Byers2025GeneReviews,Wiesmann2014,Bowen2023vEDS,Corrao2026]]
vEDS: blood pressureRegular blood pressure.The consensus recommends keeping blood pressure in the normal range and treating hypertension aggressively; avoid invasive arterial monitoring in non-emergency surgery.Expert consensus.[[Byers2017,Wiesmann2014]]
Anxiety, low mood and self-doubtAsk about mood and past experiences of care.Integrated psychological care alongside physiotherapy; compassionate, validating communication.Depression RR 3.4 in a national cohort; 73% questioned their own pain after dismissal.[[Bulbena2017,Cederlof2016,Halverson2025]]
