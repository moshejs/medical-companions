A teaching map, not a decision aid: the choice to test belongs to a genetics team. Gene-panel testing by massively parallel sequencing is the current standard for confirming the monogenic types, and careful clinical assessment decides what to test and how to read the result. [[VanDijk2024]]

For families

Genetic counselling

A negative family history does not rule out vEDS

About half of people with vEDS have an affected parent; in the other half the variant is new, in the person or in an apparently unaffected parent with mosaicism. About 60% of people diagnosed before 18 are found through family history. [[Byers2025GeneReviews]] In a London cohort of 63 diagnosed in childhood, 55% were found through family testing, and the authors recommend offering childhood testing when there is a family history. [[Wilkinson2025]]

Testing relatives

Cascade testing

For vEDS, evaluating first-degree relatives is appropriate: it starts clinically and, even without clinical signs, goes on to testing for the family's known COL3A1 variant. [[Byers2025GeneReviews]] A vEDS review lists cascade testing as part of lifelong management, with pregnancy and psychosocial care. [[Corrao2026]]

Having children

Prenatal and preimplantation options

Once a family's variant is known, prenatal and preimplantation genetic testing are possible for vEDS and cEDS. They are not possible for hEDS, because no gene is known. [[Byers2025GeneReviews,Malfait2024GeneReviews,Hakim2024GeneReviews]] The UK Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority has approved vEDS for preimplantation genetic diagnosis. [[Angwin2023H]] More in Pregnancy & parenthood.

Early evidence

The hEDS gene search

The cause of hEDS is unknown. One group found KLK15 variants enriched in 200 people with hEDS; this has not been replicated and is not a clinical test. The common MTHFR variants were not enriched, so online claims of an "hEDS gene" test are not supported. [[Malfait2020,Gensemer2025KLK15]]

Established

Results can change

Variant interpretation is revised as evidence grows. COL3A1 glutamic acid-to-lysine variants were reclassified from uncertain to pathogenic in seven families, and a COL1A1 arginine-to-cysteine variant had been wrongly called uncertain. [[Ghali2019GenetMed,Venable2023]]
