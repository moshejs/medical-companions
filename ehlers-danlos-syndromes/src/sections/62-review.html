Mnemonic in motion: four pairs and a bow

Play the timeline to watch a Beighton score build one manoeuvre at a time: four paired items worth 2 points each (little fingers, thumbs, elbows, knees) and one forward bow worth 1, for 9 in all. Then see how the same total reads against the age cut-offs of the 2017 hEDS criteria, 6-5-4. The figure is a teaching example; a score is measured by a clinician and is never a diagnosis on its own.

Sources

Every reference behind the EDS Companion: journal articles, consensus statements, GeneReviews chapters, drug labels, trial registrations and society pages. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Eight research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology, history and the classification; genetics, collagen biology and the 13 types; mechanisms in tissue, joints, skin, pain and fatigue; diagnosis, criteria, the differential and assessment tools; physiotherapy, exercise and psychological care; medicines, surgery and anaesthesia; course, comorbidity and outcomes; and living, family, nursing and research toward better care. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked check are society pages and announcements that have no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. Nothing in the app cites them; they are listed so you can see what was left out and why.
