Toward better treatment

The pipeline: where each candidate stands

Most candidates are still in mice or cells. Results in animals often fail to carry over to people: in one vEDS mouse model celiprolol "accelerated rather than reduced death from aortic rupture", while in another it strengthened the aorta, and in people it lowered events in an open trial. How to read EDS research claims

For patients, families and students

Seven questions to ask of any headline

  1. People, or mice and cells?Hydralazine kept 97% of vEDS mice alive to 45 days; no human trial has tested it. Doxycycline repairing hEDS cells in a dish is explicitly "not a treatment recommendation". [[Bowen2020JCI,Chiarelli2021]]
  2. Which type of EDS?A vascular EDS result says nothing about hypermobile EDS, and almost all exercise trials enrolled hEDS or HSD. [[Malfait2017,Palmer2021]]
  3. Was there a comparison group, and was it blinded?BBEST had no placebo; DiSCOVER is testing the same drug against placebo. [[Ong2010,NCT05432466]]
  4. How many people, and was the diagnosis confirmed?Only 33 of BBEST's 53 participants had a confirmed COL3A1 variant. [[Ong2010]]
  5. Has anyone repeated it?The KLK15 candidate gene still awaits independent replication; earlier candidate genes failed to replicate. [[Gensemer2025,Scicluna2022]]
  6. Is the trial registered, and are results posted?Registries show when a trial is suspended ("Indefinite hold by company") or stopped for poor recruitment. [[NCT05463679,NCT05405257]]
  7. Who paid, and who owns it?An online Pilates trial studied a programme owned by a co-author's organisation; a taping trial used donated tape. Declared conflicts are not proof of bias, but worth knowing. [[Russek2026,Tudini2024]]
Early evidence

A blood test for hEDS?

Several groups are looking for molecular markers. Plasma fibronectin and collagen-fragment patterns were shared by hEDS and HSD and differed from other EDS types and arthritis, but the test is not validated. Serum proteomics found complement proteins lower in 29 women with hEDS than in controls. Fibroblast RNA and serum protein studies found no clear molecular line between hEDS and HSD. [[Ritelli2025,Griggs2025,Ritelli2022,Cinquina2026]]

For vascular EDS, an AI facial-analysis model separated vEDS from controls and from hEDS (AUC ≥0.97, trained on 30 people), and vertebral tortuosity was linked to cardiovascular events. Both need validation. [[Murdock2025,Stephens2023]]

The 2017 criteria remain the published standard. A revision of the criteria has been announced by the Ehlers-Danlos Society; until it is published, see The 2017 hEDS criteria. [[Malfait2017]]
