For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail, from collagen biochemistry to the 2017 criteria. Finish with flashcards, mnemonics and a quiz.

For people with EDS or HSD, and their families

Look for the In plain words boxes. The Beighton score, the criteria checklist and the other tools here are for learning: they cannot tell anyone whether they have EDS. Bring questions to your own doctor, nurse or physiotherapist.

How sure is each claim?

Research claims carry a label: Established Early evidence Hypothesis. Associations such as POTS and mast-cell problems are labelled too. Small numbers link to the Sources list.

EDS Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on the Ehlers-Danlos syndromes and hypermobility spectrum disorders, made for health-science and nursing courses and for patient understanding.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose: the Beighton score, the criteria checklist and the other tools are teaching aids, not a test, and nothing here should change a treatment or care decision without your own clinician. Criteria, doses and guideline statements are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
