Glossary

A searchable glossary of EDS terms, from ADAMTS-2 and the D-period to the 13 type abbreviations, Feature A, G-HSD, POTS, the "20% + 2" tryptase rule, celiprolol and the vEDS passport. Type to filter; the arrow on each entry jumps to the module where the term matters most.

156 of 156 terms

5PQ (five-part questionnaire)Five yes/no questions about present and past hypermobility; 2 or more "yes" answers is positive (84% sensitivity, 89% specificity in its development cohort) 196.
AcrogeriaFine wrinkling of the face, hands and feet seen with thin, translucent skin in vascular EDS 449.
Active stand testHeart rate and blood pressure recorded lying down and then during a 10-minute stand; it usually identifies POTS 488.
ADAMTS-2The procollagen I N-proteinase that trims the N-propeptide; biallelic loss of ADAMTS2 causes dermatosparaxis EDS 99.
AEBP1 (ACLP)A protein that binds fibrillar collagens and enhances their polymerisation; biallelic variants cause classical-like EDS type 2 47.
AneurysmA localised widening of an artery. In vEDS arteries may rupture or dissect even without significant dilation 219.
ArachnodactylyLong, slender fingers; in the hEDS criteria (Feature A item 9) it means a positive wrist or thumb sign on both sides 147.
ARCADE trialA double-blind RCT of irbesartan added to celiprolol in vEDS: composite arterial outcome 27.6% vs 53.6% over 2 years (HR 0.42) 226.
ARFIDAvoidant/restrictive food intake disorder. In 680 people with hEDS/HSD, an ARFID fear-of-eating screen predicted needing nutrition support 465.
Arm span-to-height ratioA ratio of 1.05 or more is one of the 12 Feature A items of the hEDS criteria 147.
Arterial dissectionA tear in an artery's inner lining that lets blood split the wall. Arterial rupture or dissection under 40 is a vEDS minimal criterion 496.
ArteriographyImaging by catheter inside the arteries. In vEDS it is discouraged except to find life-threatening bleeding before surgery 68.
Arthrochalasia EDS (aEDS)Autosomal dominant type caused by COL1A1 or COL1A2 variants that delete exon 6; hallmark congenital bilateral hip dislocation 66,480.
ArticaineA dental local anaesthetic with the highest reported success rate (30%) among EDS survey respondents who named their agent 419.
Atrophic scarA thin, depressed scar. hEDS Feature A counts atrophic scars at 2 or more sites without the papyraceous or haemosiderotic scars of classical EDS 147.
Autosomal dominantOne changed copy is enough; each child of an affected parent has a 50% chance of inheriting it (cEDS, vEDS, aEDS, pEDS) 68,240,293.
Autosomal recessiveTwo changed copies are needed; children of two carriers have a 25% chance of being affected (kEDS, clEDS, mcEDS and others) 250,390,481.
BBEST trialThe open, randomised celiprolol trial in 53 people with vEDS: arterial events 20% vs 50% (HR 0.36), stopped early for benefit 345.
Beighton scoreA 9-point screen of joint hypermobility from 5 manoeuvres, devised in 1973 on 1,083 Tswana Africans; a screening tool, not a diagnostic test 33,288.
Bendy StudyAn RCT in 119 hypermobile children: tailored physiotherapy plus occupational therapy added nothing over standard advice at 12 months 25.
Berlin nosologyThe 1986 EDS classification (published 1988) with 11 subtypes numbered in Roman numerals 34,166.
BiallelicAffecting both copies of a gene, as in recessive types such as dermatosparaxis EDS 99.
BIoH (Bristol Impact of Hypermobility)A 55-item questionnaire developed with people with JHS; its smallest detectable change is 42 points 355,356.
BMP-1Bone morphogenetic protein-1, the same protein as procollagen C-proteinase, which removes the C-propeptides 244.
Brighton criteriaThe 1998 criteria for benign joint hypermobility syndrome, published in 2000, folded into hEDS and HSD in 2017 187,461.
Brittle cornea syndrome (BCS)Autosomal recessive type caused by ZNF469 or PRDM5: thin cornea that may rupture, keratoconus or keratoglobus, blue sclerae 125,480.
Cardiac-valvular EDS (cvEDS)Autosomal recessive type from biallelic COL1A2 null variants: heart valve disease without bone involvement 425.
Carotid-cavernous sinus fistulaAn abnormal connection between the carotid artery and the cavernous sinus; without trauma it is a vEDS major criterion 480.
Carter-Wilkinson scoreThe 1964 joint laxity score that Beighton adapted in 1973 73,288.
Cascade testingTesting relatives for a known familial variant; in vEDS, first-degree relatives can be tested even without clinical signs 68.
CeliprololA β1 antagonist with β2 agonist action used in vEDS, uptitrated to 400 mg/day in BBEST; not FDA-approved 219,345.
Central sensitisationAn amplified pain response in the central nervous system; signs were found in people with JHS/hEDS without nerve damage (Early evidence) 128.
Chiari malformation type IDescent of the cerebellar tonsils. 12.7% of 2,813 people with Chiari I met criteria for EDS or a related disorder in a surgical series (Early evidence) 303.
CHST14Dermatan-4-sulfotransferase 1; its loss abolishes dermatan sulfate and causes musculocontractural EDS 134.
Classical EDS (cEDS)Autosomal dominant type, about 1 in 20,000, mostly COL5A1/COL5A2: hyperextensible skin, atrophic scarring and GJH 480.
Classical-like EDS (clEDS)Type 1 is recessive TNXB deficiency with hyperextensible skin but no atrophic scars; type 2 is caused by AEBP1 47,413.
Closed kinetic chain exerciseExercise with the foot or hand fixed against a surface; an 8-week home programme improved knee proprioception in 16 of 18 people with JHS 160.
COL1-related overlap disorderA proposed EDS-OI overlap phenotype in people with COL1A1/COL1A2 variants; not one of the 13 types 315,480.
COL3A1The gene for type III procollagen; heterozygous pathogenic variants cause vascular EDS 67.
COL5A1 / COL5A2Genes for type V collagen chains; variants cause most classical EDS 456.
CollagenThe main structural protein: about one-third of human protein and three-quarters of the dry weight of skin; 28 types are known 432.
Compression garmentsElastic clothing. Added to physiotherapy in hEDS, they improved dynamic balance more, while pain fell equally in both groups 38.
Conditioned pain modulationThe body's ability to dampen one pain with another; it was weaker in classical EDS than in controls (Early evidence) 102.
Craniocervical instability (CCI)Proposed instability between the skull and upper neck; it has no validated imaging criteria, and surgical evidence is level III–V 278.
Criterion 1Generalised joint hypermobility on the Beighton score with age cut-offs of 6, 5 and 4, or one point short with a positive 5PQ 460,464.
Criterion 2Two or more of Features A, B and C in the 2017 hEDS criteria 147,460.
Criterion 3No unusual skin fragility, and exclusion of other connective tissue disorders and alternative causes of hypermobility 147,460.
Cross-linkA covalent bond between collagen molecules, made possible by lysyl oxidase; it gives fibrils strength 279,432.
Cutis laxaLoose, redundant skin from fragmented elastic fibres; unlike classical EDS skin, it does not snap back 46,293.
D-periodThe 67 nm repeating band of a collagen fibril, from quarter-staggered molecules 4.46 D long 432.
De novo variantA genetic change new in the person, not inherited; about half of vEDS and cEDS cases 68,293.
DecorinA proteoglycan whose dermatan sulfate chain helps organise collagen; in CHST14 deficiency collagen bundles form poorly 309.
Dermatosparaxis EDS (dEDS)Autosomal recessive type from ADAMTS2 loss: extreme skin fragility and redundant skin; prevalence under 1 in 1,000,000 99,480.
Desmopressin (DDAVP)A drug that roughly halved bleeding time in children with EDS (11.26 to 5.95 minutes) 298.
DGBI (functional GI disorder)Disorders of gut-brain interaction such as functional dyspepsia and IBS; joint hypermobility is more common in them than in organic GI disease 162.
DiSCOVER trialA recruiting phase 3, double-blind trial of celiprolol (ACER-002) versus placebo in COL3A1-positive vEDS 333.
DislocationA joint fully out of place. In classical EDS, 34.1% of adults vs 3.2% of children had had one 385.
Dominant-negativeA variant whose faulty protein disrupts the normal one; 76.8% of variants in a Dutch vEDS cohort were predicted to act this way 118.
Ectopia lentisDislocation of the eye's lens; with aortic root aneurysm it is a cardinal feature of Marfan syndrome 276.
Ehlers-Danlos syndromes (EDS)A group of heritable connective tissue disorders with joint hypermobility, skin hyperextensibility and tissue fragility; 13 types in 2017 291.
EmbolisationBlocking a bleeding artery from inside; the main endovascular approach when vEDS arteries needed intervention (13.4%) 429.
Epistemic humilityClinicians acknowledging the limits of what is known; recommended after 73% of people with EDS reported doubting their own pain 205.
Exon skippingA splice change that drops an exon from the message; COL1A1/COL1A2 exon 6 skipping causes arthrochalasia EDS 247.
FBN1The fibrillin-1 gene; variants cause Marfan syndrome 126.
Feature ASystemic signs of a more generalised connective tissue disorder: at least 5 of 12 items, such as soft skin, striae and arachnodactyly 147,460.
Feature BA first-degree relative who independently meets the hEDS criteria 147.
Feature CMusculoskeletal complications: limb pain daily for 3 months, chronic widespread pain, or atraumatic recurrent dislocation or instability 460.
FibrilThe rope-like assembly of collagen molecules; tendon fibrils reach up to 1 cm long and about 500 nm across 432.
FibromyalgiaA chronic widespread pain condition; since 2016 it can be diagnosed alongside another condition, and pain alone cannot separate it from hEDS 147,508.
FKBP14An ER folding catalyst for type III collagen; biallelic variants cause kyphoscoliotic EDS with normal urinary pyridinolines 30,183.
FludrocortisoneA mineralocorticoid used off-label in POTS; it has not been tested in randomised trials in POTS 431.
G-HSD (generalised HSD)GJH on an objective score plus at least one secondary musculoskeletal manifestation; its main differential is hEDS 72,78.
Generalised joint hypermobility (GJH)Hypermobility in 5 or more joints across the limbs and axial skeleton; common, and not a diagnosis on its own 72,98.
Glycine substitutionReplacement of a helix glycine by a bulkier residue, which stalls triple-helix folding; the commonest vEDS variant type 169,432.
GoniometerA protractor for measuring joint angles; Beighton scoring in children is valid "when goniometry is used" 438.
H-HSD (historical HSD)GJH reported on the 5PQ with a negative Beighton score, plus a secondary musculoskeletal manifestation 72.
Haemosiderotic scarA brown, iron-stained scar typical of classical EDS (54.7% of confirmed cases) 385.
Haploinsufficiency (null allele)One gene copy makes no protein, halving the amount; COL3A1 null variants delay vEDS complications by almost 15 years 262.
Hartmann's procedureResection of the bowel with an end colostomy and no join; favoured after vEDS sigmoid perforation because reperforation was rarer 142.
HEDGE studyThe Hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Genetic Evaluation, begun in 2018 to whole-genome sequence 1,000 adults meeting the 2017 hEDS criteria 132,480.
Hereditary alpha-tryptasemia (HαT)Extra TPSAB1 copies raising basal tryptase; not more prevalent in hypermobility disorders than in the general population 485.
Hess testThe Rumpel-Leede capillary fragility test; it may be abnormal in EDS when clotting tests are normal 120.
High-load trainingStrength training at high resistance; in HSD shoulders it beat low load at 16 weeks, with exercise pain below 0.5/10 268,271.
HyperalgesiaIncreased sensitivity to pain; in hEDS pressure-pain thresholds were low even at pain-free sites 398.
Hypermobile EDS (hEDS)Likely the commonest heritable connective tissue disorder, and the only EDS type with no known gene; diagnosed clinically with the 2017 criteria 291,461.
Hypermobility spectrum disorders (HSD)The 2017 labels for symptomatic joint hypermobility that does not fit hEDS or another syndrome 78.
HypertelorismWidely spaced eyes; part of the Loeys-Dietz triad 275.
Inspiratory muscle trainingBreathing-muscle training; 6 weeks raised 6-minute walk distance from 455 to 515 m in women with hEDS 379.
IrbesartanAn angiotensin receptor blocker that reduced arterial events when added to celiprolol in ARCADE 226.
IvabradineA heart-rate-lowering drug labelled for heart failure, used off-label in POTS; its label warns of fetal toxicity 151.
Joint hypermobility syndrome (JHS)The pre-2017 label, with benign joint hypermobility syndrome (BJHS), now replaced by hEDS and HSD 72.
KinesiophobiaFear of movement; 93% of 42 people with JHS/EDS-HT scored above the Tampa Scale cut-off 82.
KLK15A kallikrein gene with a recurrent variant reported in hEDS families; Early evidence from one group and not a clinical test 178.
Kyphoscoliotic EDS (kEDS)Autosomal recessive type from PLOD1 or FKBP14: congenital hypotonia, early kyphoscoliosis and GJH; about 1 in 100,000 480.
L-HSD (localised HSD)Hypermobility at single joints or groups of joints, with a secondary manifestation in the same region 72.
Local anaesthetic failureNumbing that fails or wears off early. In a crossover trial, 53% of people with EDS vs 80% of controls were still numb 30 minutes after lidocaine 54.
Loeys-Dietz syndrome (LDS)A dominant TGF-β pathway aortopathy that can mimic vEDS but tolerates vascular surgery well 275,277.
Lysyl hydroxylase 1 (PLOD1)The enzyme that hydroxylates lysines for cross-links and sugars; its deficiency causes PLOD1-kyphoscoliotic EDS 512.
Lysyl oxidase (LOX)The enzyme that oxidises lysines so collagen and elastin can cross-link 279.
Marfan syndromeAn FBN1 disorder whose cardinal features are aortic root aneurysm and ectopia lentis; about 1 in 5,000–10,000 276,368.
Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)Episodic mast-cell mediator symptoms confirmed by a tryptase rise; its link with hEDS is Hypothesis to Early evidence 254,473.
Mesenteric arteriesThe gut's arteries, the bed most often affected in genetically confirmed vEDS (31.7%) 429.
MidodrineAn alpha-agonist labelled for orthostatic hypotension with a boxed warning for supine hypertension; off-label in POTS 156.
Mitral valve prolapseBillowing of the mitral valve; mild or greater on strict echocardiographic criteria counts as Feature A item 11 147.
Molluscoid pseudotumourA fleshy, fibrotic nodule up to 3 cm at pressure points such as elbows and knees; highly diagnostic of classical EDS 385,449.
Multidirectional instabilityShoulder instability in several directions; common in hEDS/HSD, with altered scapular movement 444.
Musculocontractural EDS (mcEDS)Autosomal recessive type from CHST14 or DSE: a dermatan sulfate defect with dislocations and large haematomas 250,305.
Myopathic EDS (mEDS)Caused by COL12A1 variants, dominant or recessive; congenital hypotonia improves with age 480,517.
Neuraxial anaesthesiaSpinal or epidural anaesthesia; avoided in vEDS where possible 505, while perinatal hEDS/HSD guidance prefers epidural over spinal 367.
Nociplastic painPain arising from altered pain processing rather than tissue or nerve damage; one of the mechanisms mixed in hypermobility-related pain 79.
Obstructive sleep apnoea (OSA)Repeated airway collapse in sleep; 32% in EDS vs 6% in controls 173.
Occipitocervical fusionSurgery fixing the skull to the upper spine; uncontrolled series only, with a call for a prospective trial 208,296.
Orthostatic intoleranceSymptoms on standing that ease on lying down; 74.4% in hEDS vs 34.3% in controls 124.
Osteogenesis imperfecta (OI)A COL1A1/COL1A2 disorder whose hallmark is bone fragility, unlike EDS 297.
P-HSD (peripheral HSD)Hypermobility limited to the hands and/or feet with a secondary manifestation; peripheral JH may be a clue to vEDS 72.
PacingPlanning activity to avoid boom-and-bust; recommended by patients and clinicians but untested alone in hEDS/HSD (Hypothesis) 40.
Papyraceous scarA paper-thin "cigarette paper" scar typical of classical EDS (65.3% of confirmed cases) 385.
Pelvic organ prolapseDescent of pelvic organs; associated with joint hypermobility (pooled OR 2.37) 486.
Periodontal EDS (pEDS)Autosomal dominant type from C1R or C1S: early severe periodontitis and lack of attached gingiva; first tooth loss at a median 21.5 years 238,263.
Piezogenic papulesFat herniating into the heel skin on standing; common in healthy children (72%), so not specific 483.
POTSPostural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome: a sustained heart-rate rise of 30 beats/min or more on standing (40 at ages 12–19) without orthostatic hypotension 431,488.
Precipitate labourBirth in 3 hours or less, reported in 28–36% in hEDS/HSD perinatal guidance 367.
Preimplantation genetic diagnosis (PGD)Testing embryos for a known familial variant; possible in vEDS and cEDS, not in hEDS 68,199.
PREVEnt trialA phase 3 trial of the PKCβ inhibitor enzastaurin in vEDS, suspended on an "indefinite hold by company" 334.
ProcollagenThe precursor of collagen, with propeptides at both ends that keep it soluble until they are cut off 217,432.
Prolyl 4-hydroxylaseThe ER enzyme that turns proline into 4-hydroxyproline in the Yaa position 324,432.
Propranolol (low dose)A beta-blocker used off-label in POTS; 20 mg improved symptoms more than 80 mg 373.
ProprioceptionThe sense of joint position and movement; poorer in the lower limbs in benign joint hypermobility syndrome (meta-analysis of 254 people) 436.
Pyridinoline ratioThe urinary deoxypyridinoline to pyridinoline ratio, raised in PLOD1-kEDS 390.
PyridostigmineAn acetylcholinesterase inhibitor labelled for myasthenia gravis, used off-label in POTS 155,431.
Revised Ghent nosologyThe 2010 Marfan criteria built on aortic root aneurysm, ectopia lentis, FBN1 testing and a systemic score 276.
Skin hyperextensibilitySkin that stretches beyond 1.5 cm at the forearm and back of the hand, or 3 cm at the neck, elbows and knees 385,449.
Small-fibre neuropathyDamage to thin sensory and autonomic nerve fibres; found on biopsy in 61% of 31 people with hEDS, though findings disagree 128,218.
Spondylodysplastic EDS (spEDS)Autosomal recessive type from B4GALT7, B3GALT6 or SLC39A13: short stature, hypotonia and bowed limbs 480.
Stickler syndromeA type II/XI collagenopathy with myopia, retinal detachment, hearing loss and cleft palate 317.
StriaeStretch marks; in hEDS Feature A they count only at set sites and without major weight change 147.
Subcutaneous spheroidA hard, pea-sized calcified fat lobule over the ulna or tibia, seen in classical EDS 449.
SubluxationA partial dislocation; reported in 29.3% of people with classical EDS 385.
Systemic score (Ghent)A Marfan scoring system with a maximum of 20; 7 or more is significant 368.
Tampa Scale for KinesiophobiaA questionnaire for fear of movement used in hypermobility trials 82,445.
Tenascin-XA matrix protein that regulates collagen deposition; its recessive deficiency (TNXB) causes classical-like EDS type 1 295,413.
Thumb sign (Steinberg)The whole distal phalanx of the thumb sticks out past the clenched fist 368.
Tilt-table testHead-up tilt to provoke orthostatic changes; 41.0% of an hEDS subsample had postural tachycardia 124.
Tranexamic acidAn antifibrinolytic that can be considered before surgery in EDS with a bleeding history 505.
Triple helixThree left-handed chains coiled into a right-handed helix, with glycine at every third position 432.
Tryptase "20% + 2" ruleAn event-related tryptase rise of at least 20% plus 2 ng/mL over baseline, the consensus marker of mast-cell activation 473.
Type III collagenA homotrimer of α1(III) chains found in skin, blood vessels and intestine; its gene is COL3A1 432.
Type V collagenA minor fibrillar collagen that co-assembles with type I and controls fibril number; too little causes classical EDS 502,503.
ULHATThe Upper Limb Hypermobility Assessment Tool: 12 items, cut-point 7 or more 341.
Variant of uncertain significance (VUS)A genetic change whose effect is not yet known; some are later reclassified 181,498.
Vascular EDS (vEDS)Autosomal dominant COL3A1 type, about 1 in 50,000 to 200,000, with arterial, bowel and uterine rupture 67.
vEDS passportAn individual emergency plan with the diagnosis and management, recommended by the 2017 consensus 67.
Villefranche nosologyThe 1997 classification (published 1998) with 6 named types and major and minor criteria 35.
WOSIWestern Ontario Shoulder Instability Index, 0–2100 with lower better; minimal important difference 252 268.
Wrist sign (Walker-Murdoch)Thumb and little finger overlap when wrapped around the opposite wrist 368.
ZebraThe EDS community symbol, from "think horses, not zebras": the rare answer is sometimes right 28,359.
