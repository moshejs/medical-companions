Part 1 · The syndromes

Collagen, gene to fibril

Collagen is the body's main "rope" protein: about a third of all protein, and about three-quarters of the dry weight of skin. Follow one collagen molecule from its gene to a cross-linked fibril, then switch on an EDS type to see which step it breaks. 292,432

Which step breaks in each type

The 2017 classification groups the types by the pathway their proteins work in. Most known EDS genes encode collagen I, III or V, an enzyme that modifies or processes them, or an enzyme that builds the sugar chains of proteoglycans. Pick a type (row) or a dot: the animation jumps to that step, and the strip at its top rings the place in orange. 291,292

Two ways one changed gene copy can harm

Haploinsufficiency

Too little of a normal protein

One copy makes nothing usable (a "null allele"), so the cell has about half the normal amount. About one-third of people with classical EDS have COL5A1 haploinsufficiency, and most COL5A1 variants in a 126-person series created a null allele. 456,500

In vascular EDS, COL3A1 null alleles act through reduced protein, not through faulty molecules built into fibrils. In 54 people from 19 families, the first complication came almost 15 years later than with missense or exon-skipping variants, and only 28% of relatives had a complication. 262,424

Dominant negative

A faulty chain spoils the molecule it joins

A changed chain is still made and is built into molecules alongside normal chains, so the fault spreads beyond its own share. In a Dutch cohort of 142 people with vEDS, 43 of 56 variants (76.8%) were predicted to act this way. 118

Glycine substitutions were the commonest COL3A1 class in a French cohort (71 of 126 variants), and the replacing amino acids were biased toward those that most destabilise the helix. Glycine-substitution and splice-site variants were more severe than null variants. 169

Ages at first complication by variant class are cohort statistics, not predictions for one person. More in Vascular EDS. 118,364

What this picture cannot show

Established

Hypermobile EDS has no known gene

No genetic, epigenetic or metabolomic cause has been found, so hEDS cannot be placed on this pathway, and its diagnosis rests on the 2017 clinical criteria. 199,292 See The 2017 hEDS criteria.

Early evidence

KLK15, one group's lead

Exome sequencing of 200 people with hEDS found rare KLK15 variants enriched against controls, and one variant segregated with hEDS in two families. It has not been independently replicated and is not a clinical test. The same study found no enrichment of the common MTHFR variants. 178

Established

A skin biopsy rarely names the type

Electron microscopy shows "collagen flowers" in most genetically confirmed classical EDS, but in 177 biopsies no finding was specific to one type. Gene panels are now the standard way to confirm the monogenic types. 13,480

Established

No therapy repairs the pathway yet

No specific medical or genetic therapy exists for any type of EDS. Care targets the tissues and the risks, as later modules explain. 292 See Toward better treatment.
