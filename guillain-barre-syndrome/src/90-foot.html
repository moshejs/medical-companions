GBS Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on Guillain-Barré syndrome, made for medical, nursing and therapy courses and for people who have or have had GBS, their families and carers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Myasthenia Gravis Companion, on the other autoimmune attack on nerve and muscle that GBS can be mistaken for, the MS Companion, on demyelination inside the brain and spinal cord rather than in the nerves, and the Sepsis Companion, on the intensive care that some people with severe GBS also need.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
