Part 1 · Understanding GBS

The course over time

GBS rises over days, reaches its worst point within four weeks, holds on a plateau, then improves over months and sometimes years. [[Leonhard2019,vanDoorn2023]] Scrub the timeline to watch one schematic course alongside what large groups of people actually did.

EmergencyWeakness that is getting worse quickly, or any trouble breathing or swallowing, needs emergency care. About 20% of people with GBS develop breathing failure, up to 22% within the first week in hospital. [[Leonhard2019]]

In plain wordsGBS usually gets worse over days, and most people reach their weakest point within about 2 weeks. After that comes a flat period, then slow recovery. Recovery can go on for more than a year.

How weak at the worst point?

The GBS disability score runs from 0 (no disability) to 6 (death). [[vanTilburg2024]] A score above 2 means a person cannot walk independently. [[Papri2022mEGOS]] This is how 815 people in IGOS scored at their worst. [[Doets2018]]

Getting worse again: fluctuation or CIDP?

GBS is usually monophasic: it happens once. A minority get worse again after first improving or stabilising on treatment. [[Leonhard2019]] When worsening keeps coming back, or goes on past 8 weeks, the diagnosis may be acute-onset chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyradiculoneuropathy (A-CIDP). [[vanDoorn2023]] Pick a pattern and play it.

Does treatment change the speed?

In trials, treatment shortened the time to recovery. Results are reported as published, not as instructions. Treatments are covered in IVIg and Plasma exchange.

Key numbers

Mean time to nadir in 297 Italian people; 80% of Dutch adults by 2 weeks, 97% by 4 weeks, all by 6 weeks. [[Italian1996,Fokke2014]]

Plateau4 days

Median plateau (IQR 3 to 10) in the French Polynesia outbreak, the best direct figure; plateau length is thinly reported. [[CaoLormeau2016]]

Mean time in the Italian cohort; improvement had begun in 36% by 1 week and in 85% by 4 weeks. [[Italian1996]]

A slow road is still a roadMost people recover extensively, especially in the first year, including people who were completely paralysed at their worst or needed a ventilator for a long time. Remaining problems can still improve more than 5 years after onset. [[Leonhard2019]]

Exam hookUp to two-thirds of deaths occur in the recovery phase, so monitoring continues after the nadir. [[Leonhard2019]] First treatment-related fluctuation within 8 weeks, median 18 days; A-CIDP with 3 or more fluctuations or worsening 8 weeks or more after onset. [[Ruts2010,vanDoorn2023]]
In plain wordsSome people get a bit worse again after treatment. This can happen in GBS and does not always mean something new. If weakness keeps getting worse after about 8 weeks, or worse again three or more times, doctors may look at a long-term condition called CIDP.

How doctors estimate a group's chances of walking again is in Prognosis: EGOS & mEGOS; the work of getting there is in Rehabilitation & recovery.
