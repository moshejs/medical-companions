Research frontier

New medicines for Guillain-Barré syndrome aim either to clear the harmful antibodies faster or to switch off complement, the protein cascade that damages the nerve. See where each one acts, explore what the trials found, and how strong each result is.

This module reports research, not treatment advice. Doses appear only as trial design facts. Outcome figures are group statistics from trials or cohorts and do not predict what happens to one person [[Wiegers2025,Freiha2026]]. Each research claim carries a label:

Established shown in randomised trials or settled by repeated evidenceEarly evidence small, early, uncontrolled or not yet peer reviewedHypothesis a proposed explanation, not yet tested in people with GBS

Tanruprubart: reading a promising result carefully

Primary result, week 82.4-fold

higher odds of functional improvement with 30 mg/kg than placebo; the 75 mg/kg dose did not meet the endpoint [[Crescioli2026]].

Walking independently31 days

earlier (median) with 30 mg/kg, as reported in a manufacturer-funded review based on a congress poster [[Gorson2025]].

On a ventilator28 days

fewer with 30 mg/kg than placebo, from the same review [[Gorson2025]].

Nerve damage marker11.2%

reduction in neurofilament light chain relative to placebo between weeks 2 and 4, company-reported [[Crescioli2025]].

For. A single 30 mg/kg dose improved week-8 disability and later outcomes against placebo, with separation in MRC sum score from week 1 and response kept in both AMAN and AIDP subgroups; infusion-related reactions were the commonest adverse events Early evidence [[Gorson2025,Crescioli2026]].

Against. Neither arm received IVIg or plasma exchange, because in both countries many people get supportive care only; the full paper is unpublished, and results may not transfer to IVIg-treated populations [[Wiegers2025,Freiha2026]].

What happens next: after feedback from the FDA, the company began comparing trial participants with matched IGOS patients treated with IVIg or plasma exchange [[Crescioli2025,Wiegers2025]]. FORWARD, an open-label phase 3 study, gives a single 30 mg/kg dose to about 30 people aged 8 to 85 in the US, UK, Denmark, France and Spain; its main outcomes are drug levels, not recovery [[NCT07020819]]. Approval status after early 2026 was not checked for this guide [[Crescioli2026]].

A negative trial that changed practice: the second IVIg course

SID-GBS, NetherlandsOR 1.4

Adjusted common odds ratio for improvement at 4 weeks (95% CI 0.6–3.3; p=0.45) in 93 people with a poor prognosis (mEGOS 6 or more), 49 given a second 2 g/kg course and 44 placebo Established [[Walgaard2021]].

Serious adverse events, 30 days35% vs 16%

with the second course against placebo, including thromboembolic events; 4 people died in the second-course group [[Walgaard2021]].

Raising IgG is not enough

A lower IgG rise after IVIg goes with a poorer outcome, but the second course raised IgG as predicted without improving outcome Established [[vanTilburg2025,Walgaard2021]].

Still open

A systematic review of 422 IVIg-unresponsive patients found no superiority of repeat IVIg or plasma exchange [[Roe2025]]; a synthesis of 10 small studies reported improvement after a second dose in 70% of studies, low-quality evidence [[Alnaeem2025]]. Switching non-responders to plasma exchange is undefined [[Wiegers2025,Roe2025]].

Re-treating a treatment-related fluctuation, getting better then worse within 8 weeks of onset, is a different question and is still recommended; about 10% of people have one [[Wiegers2025]]. More in Steroids, repeats & relapses.

A blood marker of nerve damage: neurofilament light

Press Play to watch an axon break and its neurofilament light chain (NfL) reach the blood. Switch the strip below the chart between the published medians and the proposed cut-offs.

Teaching toolThe cut-offs were proposed for high specificity in one cohort: unable to walk above 319 pg/mL, unable to run above 248, able to run below 34 Early evidence. They are not validated for any one person and never for diagnosis, prognosis or treatment decisions [[MartinAguilar2020]].

Adds to mEGOSC 0.88

In 293 SID-GBS patients, a model of NfL at entry, disability score at entry and MRC sum score at week 2 predicted inability to walk at 26 weeks (95% CI 0.83–0.94). NfL did not predict respiratory failure [[Thomma2025]].

Later NfL, later walkingOR 2.79

High NfL at 4 weeks went with inability to walk at 26 weeks (1.72–4.90); the authors propose NfL as an intermediate trial endpoint Hypothesis [[vanTilburg2024]].

Albumin falls4.2 → 3.7

g/dL, median serum albumin before and 2 weeks after IVIg in 174 people; low albumin was associated with respiratory failure, inability to walk and weaker muscles at 6 months [[Fokkink2017]].

Better trials, better predictions: IGOS

The International GBS Outcome Study (IGOS) enrols people within 2 weeks of weakness, whatever their age, severity, variant or treatment, and stores serial samples; the registry lists 2,000 participants [[Jacobs2017,NCT01582763]]. In its first 1,000, outcomes differed by region; Bangladesh was reported separately from the rest of Asia [[Doets2018]]:

RegionWalking unaided at 1 yearMortality
Asia (not Bangladesh)91% (31 of 34)2%

Scores need local tuning

mEGOS discriminated well in every region (AUC above 0.7) but outcomes were worse than predicted in Europe and North America and better in Asia [[Doets2022mEGOS]]. In Bangladesh, EGRIS predicted ventilation in 41% when 20% were ventilated, fixed by updating the intercept [[Papri2024]].

New trial designs

Proposals include IGOS as an external control arm, Bayesian adaptive platform trials, and analysing the full disability scale instead of a walk or no-walk split, which loses power [[Wiegers2025]]. Unobserved confounding is the risk with external controls [[Wiegers2025,Verboon2020]].

What the scale misses

The GBS disability score mainly measures walking and breathing; it does not capture arm function, pain, mood or quality of life, which drives interest in patient-reported outcomes [[Wiegers2025]].

Early evidence

Affordable care

Lack of affordable effective treatment contributes to worse outcomes in low- and middle-income countries [[Papri2021]]. Small-volume plasma exchange removed 8 L in 15 of 20 adults; 14 of 20 improved a grade at 4 weeks, with no control arm [[Islam2018]].

Imaging and outbreaks

Hypothesis

Nerve ultrasound

At days 2 to 3, 27 people with GBS had enlarged nerves; the vagus was enlarged (median 3.0 against 2.0 mm²), most in those with autonomic dysfunction, and stayed enlarged at 6 months when dysautonomia persisted [[Grimm2016]]. MRI and ultrasound are not routine diagnostic tests [[Freiha2026]].

Established

Zika

In French Polynesia, 41 of 42 people with GBS (98%) had anti-Zika antibodies against 56% of hospital controls: the first evidence that Zika causes GBS [[CaoLormeau2016]]. In Colombia 66 of 68 had Zika-like symptoms first, a median 7 days before [[Parra2016]].

SARS-CoV-2

In the UK, GBS incidence fell from March to May 2020 and did not track COVID-19 regionally (r=0.06) [[Keddie2021]]. In IGOS, 11 of 49 early-pandemic cases had confirmed or probable infection; GBS may rarely follow it [[Luijten2021]].
