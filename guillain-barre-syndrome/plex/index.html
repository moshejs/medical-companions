Part 3 · Acute care

Plasma exchange

Plasma exchange (plasmapheresis) filters the liquid part of the blood out of the body, taking circulating antibodies with it. It was the first treatment proven to speed recovery from Guillain-Barré syndrome. Watch the circuit, see why it takes several sessions, and explore what the trials found.

Plasma exchange beat supportive care in randomised trials in the 1980s, and IVIg was later tested against it 75,199. Today guidelines rank the two as equally effective 473. Step 1 · access

A line into a large vein

Plasma exchange draws blood out, separates the plasma, and returns the blood cells with a replacement fluid. It removes high-molecular-weight substances from plasma, including autoantibodies, immune complexes and immunoglobulin paraproteins 509. It often needs a central venous catheter, whose risks include severe sepsis and catheter-related deep vein thrombosis 207.

In plain wordsPlasma exchange takes out the liquid part of the blood and replaces it, to remove harmful antibodies. It often needs a large line in a vein 207,473.

How many exchanges?

The French Cooperative Group randomised 556 people by severity to different numbers of exchanges 142. Choose a severity group.

In moderately affected people, four exchanges beat two: median time to walk with aid 20 against 24 days, and full strength at 1 year in 64% against 46% 142.

Combining and sequencing

Established

Both, one after the other

Plasma exchange followed by IVIg gave 0.2 grade more improvement than plasma exchange alone (95% CI -0.14 to 0.54; 249 people): not clinically significant 199. The guideline strongly recommends against it 473. In US practice, 6.1% of 2,325 people still received combination therapy 428.

Switching after no response

The guideline does not suggest switching to the other treatment, for lack of trial evidence, and notes that exchanging plasma right after IVIg washes out the treatment just given 473.

Early evidence

The zipper method

Alternating plasma exchange and IVIg for five cycles in nine ventilated children, uncontrolled: all nine walked unaided by day 28 221. A modified version was used in four children after standard therapy failed 332. The guideline says it needs a proper randomised trial before any effect can be claimed 473.

Immunoadsorption

Not recommended: one randomised trial was stopped early without significant benefit 473.

When treatment is out of reach

In Bangladesh, a full IVIg course for a 60 kg adult was estimated at US$12,000 to 16,000 and conventional plasma exchange at US$4,500 to 5,000, about 4,000 and 1,250 days of mean income 207. Only 10 to 12% of people with GBS there receive either treatment 347; in IGOS, 83% of 189 Bangladeshi participants were untreated, whereas 92% of 1,023 participants elsewhere received immunotherapy 483.

Small-volume plasma exchange (SVPE) separates plasma by letting blood settle in a bag, then returns the cells, three to six times a day for eight days 207. It was designed as an affordable alternative 206. In a single-arm study of 20 adults in Dhaka, the median exchange was 140 mL/kg, there was one serious adverse event (severe sepsis) and no deaths, and the expected cost was about US$500. 14 of 20 improved a grade by 4 weeks, but without a control group this is not an efficacy result 207.

Position A: feasible and cheap

SVPE was feasible and seemed safe in 20 adults Early evidence 207.

Position B: not yet proven

Efficacy is unknown; the guidelines do not endorse it for general use, and the EAN/PNS panel rated the evidence very low certainty 266,473.

In plain wordsPlasma exchange takes out the liquid part of the blood and replaces it, to remove harmful antibodies. It is usually done four or five times over 1 to 2 weeks 75,473. It works about as well as IVIg, and having both one after the other has not been shown to work better 473. Blood pressure can drop during a session, and the team monitors this 80.
Exam hookPlasma exchange within 4 weeks: 4 to 5 exchanges, 12 to 15 L; French 1997 (mild 2 better than 0, moderate 4 better than 2, ventilated 6 no better than 4); albumin, not fresh frozen plasma; autonomic instability a relative contraindication to discontinuous machines 142,143,473.
