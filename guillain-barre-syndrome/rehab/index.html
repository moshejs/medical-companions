Part 4 · Recovery

Rehabilitation and the long recovery

Recovery from Guillain-Barré syndrome is measured in months and years, and rehabilitation runs through all of it. Follow one Swedish cohort for two years, see the two ways a nerve gets back to work, and compare exercise programmes from the trials.

Recovery from remaining problems can still happen more than 5 years after onset 266.

The ten-step guideline calls arranging rehabilitation with a rehabilitation specialist, physiotherapist and occupational therapist a crucial step: first to reduce disability, and later to restore movement, sensation and fitness 266. A consensus group recommends that disabled people be treated by a multidisciplinary rehabilitation team with an assistive exercise programme 197.

2 weeks · acute ward

The start: weakest, and rehabilitation begins

In a Swedish prospective cohort of 42 adults (mean age 52; 21% ventilated), 62% could not walk 10 m independently at 2 weeks, overall strength was below normal in everyone, and sensation was affected in 93% 133. 76% depended on others for personal care, and all who had been working were unable to work 134. The guideline calls arranging rehabilitation a crucial step, first to reduce disability 266.

In plain wordsIn the first weeks most people need help with their personal care. Rehabilitation starts early, first to reduce disability 134,266.

Two ways a nerve gets back to work

How fast strength returns depends partly on what the immune attack did. Where antibodies only stopped the signal at the nodes of Ranvier, conduction can come back within weeks 232,252. Where the axon itself broke down, the nerve has to regrow, and that takes months or longer 70,254. Choose a route and press Play. The nerve damage itself is drawn in Subtypes: AIDP & AMAN.

Where antibodies (for example IgG anti-GM1) stop conduction at the node without destroying the axon, blocks disappeared or began to resolve within 3 weeks, with no sign of remyelination 232,252. In northern China, people with AMAN walked 5 m with help after a median of 31 days, as fast as with AIDP: too fast for regrowth of the whole nerve. Reversal at the node and regrowth of nerve terminals in the muscle are the proposed reasons Early evidence 168,191.

Exam hookAMAN can recover as fast as AIDP (median 31 against 32 days to walk 5 m with help in northern China): too fast for regrowth of the whole nerve. Reversal of changes at the node and regrowth of nerve terminals in the muscle are the proposed reasons 168,191.
In plain wordsIf the signal was only blocked, strength can come back fairly quickly. If the nerve fibre itself was damaged, it has to regrow, which can take many months, and some people keep improving for years 188,254.

From the ward to home

In an Israeli rehabilitation cohort of 70 people, the average stay was 20 days on acute wards and 61 days in rehabilitation. The total Functional Independence Measure (FIM) rose by 30 points and the GBS disability score improved by a mean of 1.23, with similar gains after IVIg or plasma exchange 50. In 47 inpatients, the Spinal Cord Independence Measure rose from 50 at admission to 80 at discharge and 95 at a mean of 7.5 years 418.

Not every skill comes back at the same speed. In US data from 2019, the share independent at the end of inpatient rehabilitation was 19:

Communication87.5%
Social cognition74.8%
Self-care35.9%
Transfers34.2%
Walking and moving about24.7%

Of 1,304 people in US inpatient rehabilitation, 81.8% went home or to the community, and gains in bed, chair and wheelchair transfers were the strongest predictor of going home 249. After discharge the gaps show: in a UK survey of 216 people, over a quarter did not receive community physiotherapy, and age and body pain predicted falls 93. In Colombia, 22% had no rehabilitation in the acute episode and 16% none after discharge 4.

Exercise programmes compared

Teaching toolThis compares a made-up schedule with the programmes described in published studies. It is not an exercise prescription. Exercise intensity must be monitored, because overwork can cause fatigue 266; therapists set and adjust any real programme.

The length is at least the 12 weeks the review described. The 2026 review (13 studies, 173 people, GBS and CIDP) linked the best results to supervised, individual, multicomponent sessions of 45 to 60 minutes, 3 to 4 times a week, for at least 12 weeks, and cautioned that study designs differed widely 96. The Shah trial used 60 minutes, 2 to 3 times a week, for 12 weeks 408; the bicycle study also ran 12 weeks 148.

In plain wordsPushing too hard can make tiredness worse, so therapists adjust the effort as you go. Programmes that were supervised and built up over about three months did best in the studies 96,266,408.

Therapy should not overfatigue the motor unit, which has been associated with paradoxical weakening, according to an expert review 294. That warning rests on expert opinion, not trials 266,294.

How the first two years go

Fastest early

In 6 treated people, strength measured on machines rose fastest in the first 6 months and more slowly from 6 to 18 months, still improving at 18 months; the authors suggest at least 24 months of follow-up. A longer plateau went with less recovery of knee and elbow strength 112.

Quality of life

In a systematic review of 14 studies, the three that followed people over time found large gains in quality of life in the first year, but not clearly after it; most studies were cross-sectional and of low quality 90.

Work at two years

Everyone working before GBS was unable to work at 2 weeks; 17% were still unable to work at 2 years 134. More in Life after GBS.
