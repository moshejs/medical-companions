Research frontier

New medicines for Guillain-Barré syndrome aim either to clear the harmful antibodies faster or to switch off complement, the protein cascade that damages the nerve. See where each one acts, explore what the trials found, and how strong each result is.

This module reports research, not treatment advice. Doses appear only as trial design facts. Outcome figures are group statistics from trials or cohorts and do not predict what happens to one person 139,507. Each research claim carries a label:

Established shown in randomised trials or settled by repeated evidenceEarly evidence small, early, uncontrolled or not yet peer reviewedHypothesis a proposed explanation, not yet tested in people with GBS

Step 1 · why look further

Two treatments for 30 years

IVIg and plasma exchange have been the only proven immune treatments for GBS for 30 years, and a substantial proportion of people still recover incompletely Established 507. New trials aim at two steps of the attack: the IgG antibodies that bind the nerve, and complement, the protein cascade those antibodies switch on 507. Such trials are hard to run: GBS is acute and rare, people must be enrolled fast, the disease varies, and testing a new drug without standard care is ethically difficult 507.

In plain wordsDoctors have used the same two treatments for GBS for about 30 years. They help, but some people still recover slowly or not fully 507.

Nerve damage marker11.2%

For. A single 30 mg/kg dose improved week-8 disability and later outcomes against placebo, with separation in MRC sum score from week 1 and response kept in both AMAN and AIDP subgroups; infusion-related reactions were the commonest adverse events Early evidence 86,163.

Against. Neither arm received IVIg or plasma exchange, because in both countries many people get supportive care only; the full paper is unpublished, and results may not transfer to IVIg-treated populations 139,507.

What happens next: after feedback from the FDA, the company began comparing trial participants with matched IGOS patients treated with IVIg or plasma exchange 85,507. FORWARD, an open-label phase 3 study, gives a single 30 mg/kg dose to about 30 people aged 8 to 85 in the US, UK, Denmark, France and Spain; its main outcomes are drug levels, not recovery 322. Approval status after early 2026 was not checked for this guide 86.

A negative trial that changed practice: the second IVIg course

SID-GBS, NetherlandsOR 1.4

Adjusted common odds ratio for improvement at 4 weeks (95% CI 0.6–3.3; p=0.45) in 93 people with a poor prognosis (mEGOS 6 or more), 49 given a second 2 g/kg course and 44 placebo Established 499.

Serious adverse events, 30 days35% vs 16%

with the second course against placebo, including thromboembolic events; 4 people died in the second-course group 499.

Raising IgG is not enough

A lower IgG rise after IVIg goes with a poorer outcome, but the second course raised IgG as predicted without improving outcome Established 477,499.

Still open

A systematic review of 422 IVIg-unresponsive patients found no superiority of repeat IVIg or plasma exchange 379; a synthesis of 10 small studies reported improvement after a second dose in 70% of studies, low-quality evidence 12. Switching non-responders to plasma exchange is undefined 379,507.

Re-treating a treatment-related fluctuation, getting better then worse within 8 weeks of onset, is a different question and is still recommended; about 10% of people have one 507. More in Steroids, repeats & relapses.

A blood marker of nerve damage: neurofilament light

Press Play to watch an axon break and its neurofilament light chain (NfL) reach the blood. Switch the strip below the chart between the published medians and the proposed cut-offs.

High early: in a Spanish IGOS cohort, median serum NfL was 55.49 pg/mL in GBS against 9.83 in healthy controls, and 190.55 in AMAN against 46.79 in other variants 283. In 281 SID-GBS patients, high NfL at 2 weeks was associated with being unable to walk at 4 weeks (OR 1.74, 95% CI 1.27–2.45) 476.

Teaching toolThe cut-offs were proposed for high specificity in one cohort: unable to walk above 319 pg/mL, unable to run above 248, able to run below 34 Early evidence. They are not validated for any one person and never for diagnosis, prognosis or treatment decisions 283.

Better trials, better predictions: IGOS

The International GBS Outcome Study (IGOS) enrols people within 2 weeks of weakness, whatever their age, severity, variant or treatment, and stores serial samples; the registry lists 2,000 participants 211,315. In its first 1,000, outcomes differed by region; Bangladesh was reported separately from the rest of Asia 102:

Scores need local tuning

mEGOS discriminated well in every region (AUC above 0.7) but outcomes were worse than predicted in Europe and North America and better in Asia 105. In Bangladesh, EGRIS predicted ventilation in 41% when 20% were ventilated, fixed by updating the intercept 350.

New trial designs

Proposals include IGOS as an external control arm, Bayesian adaptive platform trials, and analysing the full disability scale instead of a walk or no-walk split, which loses power 507. Unobserved confounding is the risk with external controls 484,507.

What the scale misses

The GBS disability score mainly measures walking and breathing; it does not capture arm function, pain, mood or quality of life, which drives interest in patient-reported outcomes 507.

Early evidence

Affordable care

Lack of affordable effective treatment contributes to worse outcomes in low- and middle-income countries 347. Small-volume plasma exchange removed 8 L in 15 of 20 adults; 14 of 20 improved a grade at 4 weeks, with no control arm 207.

Imaging and outbreaks

Hypothesis

Nerve ultrasound

At days 2 to 3, 27 people with GBS had enlarged nerves; the vagus was enlarged (median 3.0 against 2.0 mm²), most in those with autonomic dysfunction, and stayed enlarged at 6 months when dysautonomia persisted 170. MRI and ultrasound are not routine diagnostic tests 139.

Established

Zika

In French Polynesia, 41 of 42 people with GBS (98%) had anti-Zika antibodies against 56% of hospital controls: the first evidence that Zika causes GBS 68. In Colombia 66 of 68 had Zika-like symptoms first, a median 7 days before 353.

SARS-CoV-2

In the UK, GBS incidence fell from March to May 2020 and did not track COVID-19 regionally (r=0.06) 220. In IGOS, 11 of 49 early-pandemic cases had confirmed or probable infection; GBS may rarely follow it 278.
