## Findings

### frontier · Imlifidase phase 2 trial published
- The single-arm phase 2 trial of imlifidase followed by IVIg (NCT03943589) was published in NEJM Evidence on 24 November 2026. Until now the site knew it only from registry-posted results (Early evidence; single arm, no control) [[Attarian2026]].
- Design per the abstract: open-label, single-arm, multicentre; adults with severe GBS (GBS disability scale grades 3 to 5); one dose of imlifidase within 12 days of symptom onset, followed by standard-dose IVIg; 27 participants with confirmed GBS were assessed [[Attarian2026]].
- Results per the abstract: median time to improve at least one GBS-DS grade 6 days (95% CI 3.0–16.0); median time to independent walking 16 days (4.0–92.0); 37% walked independently within 1 week; mean MRC sum score improvement 10.7 points within 1 week [[Attarian2026]]. These agree with the registry figures the site already shows (6 days; 10 of 27 walking unaided at day 7; MRC sum score 39.1 to 50.0 by day 8) [[NCT03943589]].
- Safety per the abstract: 25 participants (83%) had at least one treatment-emergent adverse event, most grade 1 or 2; five events were grade 3 and two grade 4, including one death [[Attarian2026]].
- The authors conclude that the results support a randomised controlled trial of IgG-depleting strategies in GBS [[Attarian2026]].
- This replaces "registry results" and "registry-posted" as the description of the evidence. The label stays Early evidence: there is still no control arm.

### Exam hooks
- Imlifidase then IVIg in severe GBS: a published single-arm phase 2 study, so no randomised evidence yet [[Attarian2026]].

### In plain words
- The small imlifidase study has now been checked by other scientists and published. It still had no comparison group, so it cannot show whether the medicine works better than standard care [[Attarian2026]].

## Open questions and controversies
- The registry gives the intervals around the two medians as full ranges (3–16 and 8–92 days, the second for a two-grade improvement); the abstract gives 95% confidence intervals (3.0–16.0, and 4.0–92.0 for independent walking). The site keeps the registry wording for the figures it took from the registry [[NCT03943589,Attarian2026]].
- The abstract does not report the planned comparison with matched IVIg-treated IGOS patients. The full text was not read.
