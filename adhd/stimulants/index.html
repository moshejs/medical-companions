Part 3 · Medication

How stimulants work

Two families, one target: the dopamine synapse. Methylphenidate jams the recycling pump; amphetamine slips in through it, empties the stores and makes the pump run backwards. Switch between no medicine, methylphenidate and amphetamine and watch the cleft.

Synapse

Schematic. Pulse shapes and heights are drawn to show the mechanisms, not measured. Transporter occupancy figures are from PET in healthy adults. 435,462

Before the medicine

Baseline: release and reuptake

Each impulse releases a brief puff of dopamine into the cleft, and the dopamine transporter (DAT) pulls it back within moments, so each pulse on the graph is short. In the prefrontal cortex the noradrenaline transporter (NET) does the same job for noradrenaline and dopamine. These transporters are where the stimulants act. 56,154

In plain wordsEvery signal is a quick squirt of dopamine that is vacuumed back up straight away.
Exam hookUS labels: the mode of therapeutic action of methylphenidate and of amphetamines in ADHD "is not known". 137,154

Not a "paradoxical" effect. In 1978, a single dose of dextroamphetamine in 14 typically developing boys reduced motor activity and reaction time and improved test performance, which "casts doubt" on the idea of a special stimulant response in ADHD. In 1980, typically developing boys, boys with hyperactivity and men all showed less motor activity, more vigilance and better learning; the men reported euphoria, the boys felt "tired" or "different". A response to a stimulant therefore cannot confirm or rule out ADHD. Established 369,370

One family, several medicines

Every US stimulant label says its mode of therapeutic action in ADHD is not known, and every one carries a boxed warning for "abuse, misuse, and addiction". Stimulants are Schedule II controlled substances. 137,140,146,154,157,159 Doses and release curves are in Formulations & the daily curve.

Prodrug

Lisdexamfetamine

Examples
Vyvanse
Mechanism
Inactive itself: the parent molecule does not bind dopamine or noradrenaline reuptake sites. Red blood cells split it into dextroamphetamine and l-lysine, even at 33% of normal haematocrit, without CYP450. 159
Timing
In children 6–12, lisdexamfetamine peaks at about 1 hour and dextroamphetamine at about 3.5 hours. 159
Status
Schedule II, "a prodrug of amphetamine". 159

Speed matters: by mouth versus snorted or injected

Route

Schematic curves built around published anchors: oral methylphenidate peaks in the brain at about 60 minutes; reinforcing effects of intravenous doses appear above about 60% DAT blockade. 439,462

By mouth · slow rise

Slow and steady

Taken by mouth, methylphenidate reached peak brain concentration only after about 60 minutes (baboon PET), matching the time to peak behavioural effect. Oral clinical doses also exceed about 60% DAT blockade but rarely produce reinforcing effects. Hypothesis Slow, steady rises mimic tonic dopamine firing and are linked to therapeutic effects. 439,462,464

In plain wordsTaken by mouth at prescribed doses, stimulants raise dopamine slowly. 464
Exam hookFour variables separate therapeutic from reinforcing effects: dose, speed of rise (route), individual sensitivity and context. 464
