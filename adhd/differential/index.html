Part 2 · Recognising & diagnosing

Look-alikes & co-occurring conditions

Poor sleep, seizures, hearing loss, anxiety, low mood, trauma and substance use can all look like ADHD, and most people with ADHD also have at least one other condition. Follow the questions a clinician asks, then compare ADHD with each look-alike.

A teaching path, not a clinical algorithm: real assessments ask these questions together, and a "consider" box means look for it as well, not instead. 16,493

Question 1

Constant or episodic?

ADHD is a persistent, trait-like pattern from childhood. In bipolar disorder, increased activity comes in discrete episodes, tied to elevated mood and grandiosity. The female ADHD consensus advises asking whether problems are trait-like or episodic when weighing mood conditions. The two can co-occur: 7.95% of adults with ADHD also have bipolar disorder, and 17.11% of adults with bipolar disorder have ADHD. When both are present, a narrative review advises stabilising mood before starting ADHD medication. 16,264,388,499

In plain wordsADHD is there most days, year after year. Mood conditions tend to come and go in episodes.
Exam hookBipolar disorder is episodic; ADHD is persistent from childhood. 16

ADHD and anxiety

Anxiety

Anxiety can look like ADHD, and the two often co-occur: parents reported anxiety in 18% of children with ADHD vs 2% without. Adults with ADHD score higher on anxiety measures (Hedges' g 0.77), less so against clinical controls (0.437). In girls, self-reported anxiety best distinguished those with ADHD from control girls. ADHD is not diagnosed when symptoms are better explained by an anxiety disorder (Criterion E). 16,209,243,406,493

Relative age

Youngest in the class

Across 25 studies with over 8 million participants, children young for their school year were more likely to be diagnosed with ADHD, and in high-prescribing countries more likely to be medicated (RR 1.27). Yet younger relative age did not predict whether ADHD persisted (OR 1.02). Age matters when comparing a child with classmates. 129,203,402

Adults

Treated for the other condition

In US and WHO surveys, adult ADHD was highly comorbid with anxiety, mood, behaviour and substance disorders, and people more often sought treatment for those conditions than for ADHD. 134,225

Girls & women

Another diagnosis first

Females with ADHD are more likely to first receive an internalising or personality-disorder diagnosis, which can delay recognition of ADHD. 499 See girls, women & late diagnosis.

Lead

An environmental look-alike

Across 33 studies (10,232 children), lead exposure correlated with inattention (r = 0.16) and hyperactivity-impulsivity (r = 0.13). 176 Other risks are covered in environment, risk & myths.
