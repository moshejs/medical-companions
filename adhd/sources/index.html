Not medical adviceThe ADHD Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and for understanding ADHD. It does not replace a doctor, nurse or psychologist, and its screeners and calculators cannot diagnose anyone. Doses, criteria and guideline statements are reported as published, not as instructions. Do not start, stop or change a medicine without the prescriber.

Part 6 · Context & review

Sources

Every reference behind the ADHD Companion: journal articles, guidelines, drug labels, trial registrations and regulator pages. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Eight research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology, presentation and history; genetics and environmental risk; neurobiology and how the medicines act; diagnosis and assessment; stimulants and non-stimulants; behavioural, psychological and school treatment; course, outcomes and research; and living, family and nursing. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked not matched in a research database are regulator or guideline pages that have no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are used only for what they state, so treat them with extra care.

ADHD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on attention deficit hyperactivity disorder in children and adults, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with ADHD and their families.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose ADHD, and nothing here should start, stop or change a treatment without your own clinician. Rating scales and the criteria calculator are teaching tools, not tests. Criteria, doses and guideline statements are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
