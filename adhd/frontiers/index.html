Medicines

New medicines

Three medicines in the adult pipeline. A review of 90 phase 2/3 trials found centanafadine the only candidate with replicated efficacy, with small effect sizes (0.28–0.40). 459 It was approved in July 2026. 156 Solriamfetol and mazindol had large effects in small early trials. 437,482

In plain wordsOne new medicine has made it to approval. Two more look promising in small trials, which often shrink when larger trials are done.

Every piece on the board

Each card gives the evidence label, the key trial with its ClinicalTrials.gov number where there is one, and the question still open. Cards for the pieces moving on the board are outlined.

Established

Centanafadine

A norepinephrine, dopamine and serotonin reuptake inhibitor. In two adult phase 3 trials it lowered the AISRS score more than placebo at day 42 (by 2.74 to 4.47 points; effect sizes −0.24 to −0.40, small). 9 In adolescents, 328.8 mg beat placebo at week 6 (−18.50 v −14.15) while 164.4 mg did not. 472 Approved by the FDA as Simtriyo, status date 24 July 2026. 156

Key trial: NCT03605680, NCT03605836 (adults); NCT05257265 (adolescents); NCT05428033 (children 6–12, completed 2025) 311,312,315,317

Open question: A review of 90 phase 2/3 adult trials found no pipeline candidate matching stimulants or atomoxetine for efficacy with better tolerability; centanafadine was the only one with replicated efficacy. 459 In a 52-week open-label study, 12.3% stopped because of adverse events. 280

Early evidence

Solriamfetol

A wake-promoting agent. In a 6-week pilot RCT in 60 adults, 45% met the response definition versus 6.9% on placebo (AISRS effect size 1.09), with no significant change in mean heart rate or blood pressure. 437

Key trial: NCT04839562 (pilot); FOCUS NCT05972044 (516 enrolled, completed, no posted results); FOCUS-2 NCT07717879 (recruiting from July 2026) 314,318,319

Open question: Will the large effect in a 60-person pilot hold up in phase 3? The FOCUS results are not yet posted. 318

Early evidence

Mazindol CR

Controlled-release mazindol in a phase 2 adult trial (85 people, 6 weeks) reduced ADHD-RS-DSM5 by 13.2 points more than placebo (effect size 1.09); 42.9% versus 11.9% had at least a 50% reduction. 482

Key trial: NCT02808104 309

Open question: A single small phase 2 trial; no phase 3 result is reported in this guide's research.

Early evidence

EndeavorRx (AKL-T01)

Video-game-like attention training played 25 minutes a day, 5 days a week, for 4 weeks. In STARS-ADHD (348 unmedicated children aged 8–12) it improved the TOVA Attention Performance Index more than a digital control (median difference 0.88). 231 FDA De Novo DEN200026, 15 June 2020, as an adjunct to clinician-supervised treatment. 143

Key trial: STARS-ADHD, NCT02674633 308

Open question: The primary outcome was a computerised attention test, not parent- or clinician-rated ADHD symptoms. 231

Early evidence

Monarch eTNS

External trigeminal nerve stimulation through forehead electrodes, nightly for 4 weeks. In a double-blind, sham-controlled pilot in 62 children aged 8–12, ADHD-RS improved (week 4 Cohen d 0.5) with a number needed to treat of 3 on CGI-I. 282 FDA De Novo DEN180041, 19 April 2019. 148

Key trial: Pilot NCT02155608; larger trial NCT05374187 (218 enrolled, completed April 2026, no posted results) 307,316

Open question: Will the larger trial replicate the pilot? Responders had lower baseline working memory and right-frontal EEG power (a Hypothesis about who benefits). 258

Early evidence

NEBA EEG aid

An EEG theta/beta ratio assessment aid, authorised by De Novo DEN110019 on 15 July 2013 for use only alongside other valid diagnostic methods. 150 A sponsor-linked 13-site study reported accuracy rising from 61% to 88% when the ratio was added to clinician evaluation. 410

Key trial: No NCT number in this guide's research.

Open question: A meta-analysis concluded the ratio "cannot be considered a reliable diagnostic measure"; 17 in two multicentre datasets, none of five processing methods separated children with and without ADHD. 457

Established

Neurofeedback

Training people to change their own EEG patterns. A 2025 meta-analysis of 38 RCTs (2,472 participants) found no improvement on probably blinded ratings (SMD 0.04). 477 In a double-blind sham-controlled trial of 144 children, both groups improved strongly but neurofeedback was not superior (d = 0.01). 321

Key trial: Sham-controlled trial reported by the Neurofeedback Collaborative Group 321,322

Open question: A small effect remained for standard protocols (SMD 0.21). 477 Brain stimulation (rTMS, tDCS) also shows little clinical benefit so far. 475,476

Hypothesis

Polygenic scores

A score adding up many common genetic variants. The 2023 GWAS (38,691 people with ADHD) found 27 loci, with 84–98% of ADHD-influencing variants shared with other psychiatric disorders. 104 Adding a polygenic score to the ASRS raised variance explained by only 0.58 percentage points. 201

Key trial: Not a treatment trial.

Open question: "Currently not a clinically useful diagnostic aid" (Hypothesis for future use). 201 More in Genes & heritability.

Established

CYP2D6 dosing

Precision dosing that exists today: the CPIC guideline gives atomoxetine recommendations by CYP2D6 genotype. 53 The Strattera label states that exposure (AUC) is about 10-fold higher in CYP2D6 poor metabolisers, about 7% of White and 2% of Asian and Black populations, and describes a longer titration interval for them, quoted as published. 158

Key trial: Guideline and label, not a trial.

Open question: Whether genotype-guided dosing of other ADHD medicines adds value is not shown in this guide's research.

How to read a new claim

Headlines about ADHD treatments appear every month. Four questions sort most of them: was there a sham or placebo, were the raters blind, how big was the effect, and has anyone repeated it? Pick an example and step through. The meter is a teaching device, not a validated score.

Example

Bell curves: two groups whose averages differ by the reported standardised effect size. The overlap shows how many people in the two groups score alike even when the averages differ.

Where the evidence is argued over

Controversy

Over-diagnosis or under-diagnosis?

Position AA scoping review of 334 studies found evidence of over-diagnosis and over-treatment in young people, with added cases at the milder end; only 5 studies weighed benefits and harms for them. 222 August-born children in states with a 1 September cut-off were diagnosed more often than September-born classmates (85.1 v 63.6 per 10,000). 245
Position BThe AAP guideline states that "ADHD goes undiagnosed in a considerable number of children and adolescents". 493 Adult diagnosis rates were lowest in Asian American and Pacific Islander adults, suggesting lower detection in minority groups. 74 Younger relative age did not predict whether ADHD persisted later. 402

Status: both can be true in different groups at the same time. 74,222

Controversy

The rise in adult diagnoses and telehealth

Position AAdult diagnosis in one US health system rose from 0.43% in 2007 to 0.96% in 2016. 74 US ADHD medication fills rose 23.8% from 2019 to 2023. 211 A first stimulant prescription at a telehealth visit was linked to higher stimulant use disorder risk (aOR 6.18, 95% CI 1.34–28.46), a wide interval that needs replication. 368
Position BRising prevalence reflects broader criteria and greater awareness, including in women and under-represented groups. 2 A purely telehealth care relationship was not linked to later substance use disorder (aOR 0.85). 368 About half of US adults with ADHD had used telehealth for ADHD care, and 71.5% of recent stimulant users had trouble filling a prescription. 425

Status: single-system telehealth data; replication needed. The consensus statement attributes rising diagnosis to changes in practice, not a true rise in prevalence. 129,368

Controversy

How good is the long-term evidence for medication?

Position ARegister studies that compare people with themselves, or emulate a trial, find fewer transport accidents, suicidal behaviour, substance misuse and criminality during treatment; 505 starting medication was associated with lower 2-year all-cause mortality (HR 0.79). 251 Stopping methylphenidate after 2 years led to worsening in 40.4% versus 15.9% who continued. 279
Position BAn umbrella review found "no high certainty, long term evidence" at 52 weeks for any ADHD intervention. 178 The largest network meta-analysis found insufficient data at 26 and 52 weeks. 85 In the MTA follow-up, extended use was not associated with lower adult symptom severity. 442 Register findings "are not causal" in the strict sense. 233

Status: the association is robust; its causal size is uncertain. See The MTA study and Course & outcomes.

Hypothesis

Is there a late-onset adult ADHD?

Position AIn the Dunedin cohort, 90% of adult cases had no history of childhood ADHD; 287 in Pelotas, only 12.6% of young adults with ADHD had it in childhood. 63
Position BDSM-5-TR still requires several symptoms before age 12; late-onset cases may reflect missed childhood ADHD or other conditions. 16,356

Status: unresolved. 63,287,356

Exam hookEndeavorRx (2020) is an FDA-authorised digital therapeutic and Monarch eTNS (2019) an FDA-authorised forehead nerve stimulator; blinded neurofeedback trials show no specific benefit over sham; the theta/beta ratio is not a reliable diagnostic test; no ADHD treatment has high-certainty evidence at 52 weeks. 17,143,148,178,477
In plain wordsNew treatments are being studied, and a new medicine, centanafadine, was approved in the US in 2026. Brain-wave training and gene tests are not proven ways to diagnose or treat ADHD yet. 156,201,477
