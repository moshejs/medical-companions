ADHD Companion · animated and cited

ADHD, explained

ADHD, attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, affects attention, activity and self-control in children and adults. Here it is in moving pictures: see how brain signalling works, try the DSM-5-TR criteria as a teaching tool, watch how each medicine acts, and compare what the trials show.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Schematic, not to scale.

For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. Finish with flashcards, mnemonics and a quiz.

For people with ADHD, parents and families

Look for the In plain words boxes. The rating scales and the criteria calculator are for learning, not for testing yourself or your child: only a clinician's assessment can diagnose ADHD. Doses are quoted from labels and guidelines, never as instructions.

How sure is each claim?

Research claims carry a label: Established Early evidence Hypothesis. Small numbers link to the Sources list.
