ADHD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on attention deficit hyperactivity disorder in children and adults, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with ADHD and their families.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: BPD Companion, a guide to borderline personality disorder, Autism Companion, on a condition that often occurs alongside ADHD, Bipolar Companion, on a condition ADHD is sometimes mistaken for, and AUD Companion, on alcohol use disorder, which is more common in people with ADHD.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
