ADHD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on attention deficit hyperactivity disorder in children and adults, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with ADHD and their families.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: BPD Companion, a guide to borderline personality disorder, and Autism Companion, on a condition that often occurs alongside ADHD, and Bipolar Companion, on a condition ADHD is sometimes mistaken for.
