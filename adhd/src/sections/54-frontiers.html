How to read a new claim

Headlines about ADHD treatments appear every month. Four questions sort most of them: was there a sham or placebo, were the raters blind, how big was the effect, and has anyone repeated it? Pick an example and step through. The meter is a teaching device, not a validated score.

Example

Bell curves: two groups whose averages differ by the reported standardised effect size. The overlap shows how many people in the two groups score alike even when the averages differ.

Where the evidence is argued over

Controversy

Over-diagnosis or under-diagnosis?

Position AA scoping review of 334 studies found evidence of over-diagnosis and over-treatment in young people, with added cases at the milder end; only 5 studies weighed benefits and harms for them. [[Kazda2021]] August-born children in states with a 1 September cut-off were diagnosed more often than September-born classmates (85.1 v 63.6 per 10,000). [[Layton2018]]
Position BThe AAP guideline states that "ADHD goes undiagnosed in a considerable number of children and adolescents". [[Wolraich2019]] Adult diagnosis rates were lowest in Asian American and Pacific Islander adults, suggesting lower detection in minority groups. [[Chung2019]] Younger relative age did not predict whether ADHD persisted later. [[SIMBA2023]]

Status: both can be true in different groups at the same time. [[Kazda2021,Chung2019]]

Controversy

The rise in adult diagnoses and telehealth

Position AAdult diagnosis in one US health system rose from 0.43% in 2007 to 0.96% in 2016. [[Chung2019]] US ADHD medication fills rose 23.8% from 2019 to 2023. [[Huskamp2025]] A first stimulant prescription at a telehealth visit was linked to higher stimulant use disorder risk (aOR 6.18, 95% CI 1.34–28.46), a wide interval that needs replication. [[Rao2025]]
Position BRising prevalence reflects broader criteria and greater awareness, including in women and under-represented groups. [[Abdelnour2022]] A purely telehealth care relationship was not linked to later substance use disorder (aOR 0.85). [[Rao2025]] About half of US adults with ADHD had used telehealth for ADHD care, and 71.5% of recent stimulant users had trouble filling a prescription. [[Staley2024]]

Status: single-system telehealth data; replication needed. The consensus statement attributes rising diagnosis to changes in practice, not a true rise in prevalence. [[Rao2025,Faraone2021]]

Controversy

How good is the long-term evidence for medication?

Position ARegister studies that compare people with themselves, or emulate a trial, find fewer transport accidents, suicidal behaviour, substance misuse and criminality during treatment; [[Zhang2025BMJ]] starting medication was associated with lower 2-year all-cause mortality (HR 0.79). [[Li2024JAMA]] Stopping methylphenidate after 2 years led to worsening in 40.4% versus 15.9% who continued. [[Matthijssen2019]]
Position BAn umbrella review found "no high certainty, long term evidence" at 52 weeks for any ADHD intervention. [[Gosling2025]] The largest network meta-analysis found insufficient data at 26 and 52 weeks. [[Cortese2018]] In the MTA follow-up, extended use was not associated with lower adult symptom severity. [[Swanson2017MTA]] Register findings "are not causal" in the strict sense. [[Kozhevnikova2026]]

Status: the association is robust; its causal size is uncertain. See The MTA study and Course & outcomes.

Hypothesis

Is there a late-onset adult ADHD?

Position AIn the Dunedin cohort, 90% of adult cases had no history of childhood ADHD; [[Moffitt2015]] in Pelotas, only 12.6% of young adults with ADHD had it in childhood. [[Caye2016]]
Position BDSM-5-TR still requires several symptoms before age 12; late-onset cases may reflect missed childhood ADHD or other conditions. [[Posner2020,APA2022DSM]]

Status: unresolved. [[Moffitt2015,Caye2016,Posner2020]]

Exam hookEndeavorRx (2020) is an FDA-authorised digital therapeutic and Monarch eTNS (2019) an FDA-authorised forehead nerve stimulator; blinded neurofeedback trials show no specific benefit over sham; the theta/beta ratio is not a reliable diagnostic test; no ADHD treatment has high-certainty evidence at 52 weeks. [[FDA-EndeavorRx,FDA-MonarchETNS,Westwood2025,Arns2013,Gosling2025]]
In plain wordsNew treatments are being studied, and a new medicine, centanafadine, was approved in the US in 2026. Brain-wave training and gene tests are not proven ways to diagnose or treat ADHD yet. [[FDA-Simtriyo,Westwood2025,Hoberg2024]]
