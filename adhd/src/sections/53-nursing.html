Before the first dose: when does NICE ask for a cardiology opinion?

NICE says an ECG "is not needed before starting stimulants, atomoxetine or guanfacine" unless a cardiac risk feature is present or another medicine raises cardiac risk. Tick what the history and examination found. [[NICE2018F]]

Safe handling and teaching

Controlled drugs

Storage and disposal

Stimulants are Schedule II controlled substances in the US. Their boxed warning asks clinicians to teach patients and families about "proper storage" and "proper disposal of any unused drug". Labels advise storing the medicine "in a safe place, preferably locked", and not giving it to anyone else. [[FDA-Concerta,FDA-Vyvanse,FDA-AdderallXR]]

Storage temperatures as labelled: Concerta at 25 °C, protected from humidity; Vyvanse and Adderall XR at 20 to 25 °C in a tight, light-resistant container. Prescribers should know the controlled-drug law that governs stimulants. [[FDA-Concerta,FDA-Vyvanse,FDA-AdderallXR,NICE2018F]]

School

Medicines at school

The AAP's school medication policy says the best arrangement is full-time registered nurses giving routine and emergency medicines, with any delegation to unlicensed staff supervised by the nurse under state nurse practice acts. [[AAP2009School]] The AAP and National Association of School Nurses (2024) call for a collaborative model with student and family, safe storage and administration, and protocols to prevent school-specific medication errors. [[Miotto2024]] NICE lists fewer "problems of storing and administering controlled drugs at school" among the reasons to consider once-daily modified-release stimulants. [[NICE2018F]]

Teaching points

How each product is taken, as labelled

  • Concerta: once daily in the morning, swallowed whole; "Do not split, crush, or chew". [[FDA-Concerta]]
  • Adderall XR and Focalin XR: may be opened and sprinkled on applesauce, eaten at once without chewing. [[FDA-AdderallXR,FDA-FocalinXR]]
  • Vyvanse: may be opened and mixed with yogurt, water or orange juice and taken at once; "A single dose should not be divided." [[FDA-Vyvanse]]
  • Jornay PM: "only in the evening", between 6:30 and 9:30 p.m.; "Do not take JORNAY PM in the morning." [[FDA-JornayPM]]
  • Strattera: "Take STRATTERA capsules whole; do not open the capsules." [[FDA-Strattera]]
  • Intuniv: "Do not crush, chew, or break tablets"; not with high-fat meals; stopped by tapering no more than 1 mg every 3 to 7 days. [[FDA-Intuniv]]
Missed and double doses

What the labels say

Strattera: "take the dose as soon as possible, but do not take more than the prescribed total daily amount of STRATTERA in any 24-hour period." Intuniv: after two or more missed doses, "consider titration based on patient tolerability". [[FDA-Strattera,FDA-Intuniv]] US poison centres recorded 124,383 out-of-hospital ADHD medication errors in under-20s from 2000 to 2021, up 299%; 53.9% were a dose taken or given twice. [[DeCoster2023]]

NICE suggests picture or written instructions kept with the medicine, alarms or apps, and linking doses to routines such as meals or brushing teeth. [[NICE2018F]]

What to report

Tell the team promptly about

  • New sad, hopeless or suicidal thoughts on atomoxetine or viloxazine (boxed warnings). [[FDA-Strattera,FDA-Qelbree]]
  • A sustained or painful erection: immediate medical help. [[FDA-Concerta]]
  • Changes in the fingers or toes: the labels warn of peripheral vasculopathy, including Raynaud's phenomenon. [[FDA-Concerta]]
  • Fainting or dizziness on standing with guanfacine. [[NICE2018F]]
  • New tics, seizures, sleep changes, worse behaviour, poor weight gain. [[NICE2018F]]
Interactions

MAOIs and more

Concerta, Vyvanse, Adderall XR, Strattera and Qelbree are contraindicated with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor or within 14 days of stopping one, because of hypertensive crisis; the Vyvanse and Adderall XR labels name linezolid and intravenous methylene blue as MAOIs. Qelbree is also contraindicated with sensitive or narrow-range CYP1A2 substrates. Strattera is contraindicated in narrow-angle glaucoma and phaeochromocytoma. [[FDA-Concerta,FDA-Vyvanse,FDA-AdderallXR,FDA-Strattera,FDA-Qelbree]]

Sleep hygiene

A bedtime routine is a treatment

In a trial of 244 children with ADHD, two sessions of sleep hygiene and behavioural strategies cut moderate to severe sleep problems at 3 months from 56% to 30% (number needed to treat 3.9), and also eased ADHD symptoms a little. [[Hiscock2015]] NICE suggests a sleep diary to track changes and adjust medication. [[NICE2018F]] Melatonin brought sleep onset 26.9 minutes earlier in one trial; in the US it is a supplement, and child ingestions rose 530% from 2012 to 2021, so it is stored out of reach. [[VanderHeijden2007,Lelak2022]]

Misuse & diversion

Watch, without suspicion

The AAP advises monitoring adolescents' symptoms and refill requests for misuse or diversion, "including by parents, classmates, or other acquaintances", and notes prescription drug monitoring programmes can help. [[Wolraich2019]] NICE asks clinicians and parents to watch for changes in that risk with age and circumstances. Common misunderstandings

BeliefWhat the sources say
"Everyone needs an ECG first."NICE: not needed unless there are cardiac risk features. Screening 1,470 children found confirmed heart disease in 0.3%. [[NICE2018F,Mahle2009]]
"A rating scale can diagnose ADHD."NICE: "a diagnosis of ADHD should not be made solely on the basis of rating scale or observational data". Scales track progress. [[NICE2018F]]
"Parent training means bad parenting."NICE says parent training "does not imply bad parenting". [[NICE2018F]]
"Stimulants cause tics."In 22 trials, tics were as common on placebo (6.5%) as on stimulants (5.7%). [[Cohen2015Tics]]
"Stopping is harmless once things are going well."After 2+ years on methylphenidate, 40.4% worsened after withdrawal versus 15.9% who continued, though some did not worsen; hence planned, reviewed trials off medicine. [[Matthijssen2019,NICE2018F]]
"ADHD ends at 18."NICE asks for reassessment at school-leaving age and transfer by 18 if treatment is still needed; in UK surveillance only 22% attended a first adult appointment. [[NICE2018F,Eke2020]]
