Look-alikes & co-occurring conditions

Poor sleep, seizures, hearing loss, anxiety, low mood, trauma and substance use can all look like ADHD, and most people with ADHD also have at least one other condition. Follow the questions a clinician asks, then compare ADHD with each look-alike.

A teaching path, not a clinical algorithm: real assessments ask these questions together, and a "consider" box means look for it as well, not instead. [[Wolraich2019,APA2022DSM]]

Relative age

Youngest in the class

Across 25 studies with over 8 million participants, children young for their school year were more likely to be diagnosed with ADHD, and in high-prescribing countries more likely to be medicated (RR 1.27). Yet younger relative age did not predict whether ADHD persisted (OR 1.02). Age matters when comparing a child with classmates. [[Faraone2021,Holland2019,SIMBA2023]]

Adults

Treated for the other condition

In US and WHO surveys, adult ADHD was highly comorbid with anxiety, mood, behaviour and substance disorders, and people more often sought treatment for those conditions than for ADHD. [[Kessler2006,Fayyad2017]]

Girls & women

Another diagnosis first

Females with ADHD are more likely to first receive an internalising or personality-disorder diagnosis, which can delay recognition of ADHD. [[Young2020Females]] See girls, women & late diagnosis.

Lead

An environmental look-alike

Across 33 studies (10,232 children), lead exposure correlated with inattention (r = 0.16) and hyperactivity-impulsivity (r = 0.13). [[Goodlad2013]] Other risks are covered in environment, risk & myths.
