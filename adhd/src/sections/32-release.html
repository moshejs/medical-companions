Same molecule, different day

Wearing off

When the curve falls

Labels pin duration to trials, not to a promise: Concerta's paediatric results "were maintained through 12 hours after dosing"; Vyvanse separated from placebo from hours 2 to 12 in children and 2 to 14 in adults; Mydayis is taken on waking "because the effects may last up to 16 hours". In a review of 15 randomised driving trials, immediate-release methylphenidate lost its effect in the evening. [[FDA-Concerta,FDA-Vyvanse,FDA-Mydayis,Gobbo2014]]

Planning the day around the curve (which product, what time) is part of the conversation with the prescriber.

Not interchangeable

Why one ER product is not simply another

Release depends on the device. Concerta must be swallowed whole: splitting, crushing or chewing "will compromise the extended-release characteristics". Jornay PM should not be swapped for other methylphenidate products milligram for milligram. A methylphenidate patch on inflamed skin gave about 3-fold higher Cmax and AUC. Extended-release guanfacine is not interchangeable mg for mg with the immediate-release tablet (see non-stimulants). [[FDA-Concerta,FDA-JornayPM,FDA-Daytrana,FDA-Intuniv]]

Prodrug

Lisdexamfetamine waits for red blood cells

Lisdexamfetamine is inactive until it is split into dextroamphetamine and l-lysine, mainly in the blood by red blood cells. Substantial hydrolysis still happened at low haematocrit (33% of normal). The prodrug peaks at about 1 hour and its own half-life is under 1 hour; the dextroamphetamine it releases peaks at about 3.5 hours in children. Heal and colleagues describe this rate-limited route as central to its sustained effect and to a lower potential for recreational abuse. [[FDA-Vyvanse,Pennick2010,Heal2013]]

Food

Breakfast moves some curves

A high-fat meal pushed Mydayis's d-amphetamine Tmax from 7.0 to 12.0 hours and Focalin's from 1.5 to 2.9 hours. Food delays Vyvanse's Tmax by about 1 hour without changing exposure. A high-fat breakfast did not change Concerta's pharmacokinetics, and food has no relevant effect on IR methylphenidate. [[FDA-Mydayis,FDA-Focalin,FDA-Vyvanse,FDA-Concerta,FDA-RitalinC]]

Patch

A day you can switch off

The methylphenidate patch (ages 6 to 17) goes on the hip 2 hours before an effect is needed and comes off 9 hours after application. Peak levels come about 10 hours after a single application and 8 hours after repeated ones. [[FDA-Daytrana]]

Supply

The 2022–2024 shortages

A national shortage of immediate-release mixed amphetamine salts was announced in October 2022, and dispensing to children shifted toward dexmethylphenidate. In 2023, 71.5% of US adults taking a stimulant had difficulty getting a prescription filled because it was unavailable. A shortage database counted 26 ADHD stimulant shortages from 2001 to 2023, lasting a mean 20.7 months. Early evidence A 2026 analysis links the shortage to simultaneous manufacturing cutbacks and a fall in raw amphetamine imports, not only to DEA quotas. [[He2025Pediatrics,Staley2024,Sheykhsoltan2025,Currie2026]]

In plain words. Long-acting pills release the medicine slowly, so one morning dose can last a school or work day. Some capsules can be opened and sprinkled on applesauce for people who cannot swallow pills; some tablets must never be crushed or chewed. [[FDA-Concerta,FDA-Vyvanse,FDA-AdderallXR]]
