Assessing girls and women

Assessment

What a careful adult assessment includes

  • Collateral history from a parent or carer, or someone who has known the person well for 5 years or more, plus school, college or work reports where available. [[Young2020Females]]
  • Female norms on rating scales where they exist, with cut-offs read cautiously: rigid use gives both false positives and false negatives. [[Young2020Females]]
  • Questions about compensatory strategies, emotional dysregulation, self-harm and eating patterns, and a look for co-occurring autism, tics, mood, anxiety and eating disorders. [[Young2020Females]]
  • Whether problems are trait-like (more typical of ADHD) or episodic. [[Young2020Females]] Satisfactory academic achievement should not rule out ADHD. [[Quinn2014]]
Informants

Who reports changes the answer

In hyperactive children followed to age 19–25, ADHD persisted in 5% by self-report but 46% by parent report. [[Barkley2002]] In 283 children with high symptoms, parents under-rated diagnosed girls' hyperactive-impulsive symptoms compared with an interviewer, and over-rated boys'; emotional problems especially distinguished the girls who met full criteria. [[Mowlem2019]]

The authors suggest girls' ADHD may need extra emotional or behavioural problems before it is "made more prominent" enough to meet criteria. [[Mowlem2019]]

Hormones, pregnancy and later life

Early evidence

Puberty and hormonal change

The female ADHD consensus states that hormonal transitions, starting with puberty, may change ADHD symptoms, and suggests that women track their symptoms across these periods. [[Young2020Females]]

Early evidence

Postpartum and menopause

The same consensus lists the postpartum period and menopause as transitions that may change symptoms, again with a suggestion to track them rather than firm evidence of how often or how much. [[Young2020Females]]

Established

Pregnancy and medicines

Among 4,717 Danish pregnancies with ADHD medication use, about 60% stopped or paused. [[BangMadsen2024]] In US Medicaid data, stimulants were linked to small absolute increases in preeclampsia and preterm birth; the authors conclude women with significant ADHD "should not be counseled to suspend their ADHD treatment based on these findings". [[Cohen2017Placenta]] In 861,650 Swedish children, exposure in the womb was not linked to neurodevelopmental disorders. [[BangMadsen2025]] Decisions are individual and made with the prescriber; the labels point to a pregnancy registry. [[FDA-Concerta,FDA-Vyvanse]]

Established

Why recognition matters

In a 10-year follow-up of 140 girls with ADHD, those with childhood ADHD had more impairment and higher rates of suicide attempts and self-injury than comparison girls, concentrated in the combined type. [[Hinshaw2012Girls]] Females with ADHD have heightened risks in close relationships, self-harm, intimate partner violence and unplanned pregnancy. [[Hinshaw2022]] In Denmark, the ADHD-linked mortality rate ratio was 2.85 in girls and women versus 1.27 in boys and men, after excluding major co-occurring conditions (population statistics). [[Dalsgaard2015]]

Both risks are real

Under-diagnosis

Missed or misdiagnosed

Clinic ratios of up to 16:1 against about 3:1 in the community point to under-recognition of girls. [[Young2020Females,Biederman2002Gender]] Females are recorded with ADHD about 4 years later, after more contact with services for other problems. [[Skoglund2024]] Adults' ADHD symptoms are often mistaken for depression, anxiety or alcohol problems. [[Ginsberg2014]] Established for under-referral; the size of any true sex difference is debated.

Over-diagnosis

Labels that may not fit

A scoping review of 334 studies found Early evidence of overdiagnosis at the milder end in children. [[Kazda2021]] With repeated assessments from several informants, about 95% of apparent late-onset cases were excluded, most often because symptoms occurred only with heavy substance use. [[Sibley2018LateOnset]] Feigned ADHD is hard to detect on self-report questionnaires. [[Musso2014]] A careful history guards against both errors.
