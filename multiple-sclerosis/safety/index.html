Natalizumab and PML: how the risk is estimated

In plain wordsMost people on natalizumab never get PML. The risk is very low without JC virus antibodies, and climbs with a high antibody index, earlier immune-suppressing drugs and more than two years of treatment.
Exam hookNatalizumab PML risk factors: anti-JCV antibodies (index), prior immunosuppressants, >24 months. Highest Bloomgren group: 11.1 per 1,000; overall 2.1 per 1,000.
Controversy

How often to retest a negative antibody result

The US label reports seroconversion of 3–8% a year and asks for antibody-negative patients to be retested periodically, without an interval. A UK expert group reports that the EMA's CHMP recommends testing every 6 months, and suggests MRI at least every 6 months (index ≤1.5) or every 3–4 months (index >1.5) after 18 months of treatment in antibody-positive patients. Practice follows regional regulation. 307,474

“A negative JCV test means no risk”

The label states antibody-negative patients remain at risk, from new infection or a false-negative result; the assay's false-negative rate is 3%. In Bloomgren 2012, all 54 PML cases with an earlier blood sample had been antibody-positive. 42,474

What the label says to watch for

The natalizumab label describes PML symptoms progressing over days to weeks: one-sided weakness or clumsiness of a limb, disturbed vision, and changes in thinking, memory and orientation leading to confusion and personality change. Dosing is withheld at the first suggestive sign. ECTRIMS/EAN 2018 R13 recommends MRI every 3–6 months for people at high PML risk (JCV positive, natalizumab beyond 18 months). 324,474

Controversy

Extended-interval dosing

Six-weekly dosing is used to lower PML risk, and Ho 2017 shows how strongly risk depends on index and duration. NOVA, which tested lesions rather than PML, found more new lesions on 6-weekly dosing (significant under one estimand), driven by two outliers, with one asymptomatic PML case in the 6-weekly arm. The US label still states every 4 weeks. 151,209,474

What each label asks to be checked

Pick a drug, then press Play to walk through the label's schedule. Checks light up as the timeline passes them. The time scale differs by drug: hours for the first fingolimod dose, years for alemtuzumab.

Before starting, the label lists a CBC, liver tests within 6 months, VZV antibodies (vaccinate if negative), a fundus examination at baseline and again at 3–4 months, and a skin examination. Symptomatic bradycardia occurred in 0.6% (placebo 0.1%); heart rate returns to baseline within a month. 163

Fingolimod · label

Watching the first dose

The label says to observe every patient for bradycardia for at least 6 hours after the first dose, with hourly pulse and blood pressure and an ECG before and at the end. Observation is extended if the adult heart rate is below 45 bpm at 6 hours, if there is AV block, or if the lowest rate falls at the end of the period. Heart rate starts falling within an hour, is lowest within 6 hours and dips again within 24 hours. Macular oedema occurred in 0.5% (4/783) on 0.5 mg and 0.4% on placebo, mostly in the first 3–4 months. 163

In plain wordsThe first fingolimod tablet slows the heart for a few hours, so the label asks for a day of checks: a heart tracing before and after, and pulse and blood pressure every hour.
Exam hookFingolimod: ≥6 h first-dose observation, hourly pulse and BP, ECG before and after; repeat after a break of more than 14 days.

Safety cards, drug by drug

Fingolimod

  • First-dose observation for at least 6 hours; repeat after a break of more than 14 days.
  • Before starting: CBC, liver tests, VZV antibodies, fundus examination (again at 3–4 months), skin examination.
  • Macular oedema 0.5% on 0.5 mg versus 0.4% on placebo.
  • In TRANSFORMS, two fatal herpesvirus infections, both on the unapproved 1.25 mg dose. 82,163

Fumarates

  • CBC with lymphocytes before, at 6 months, then every 6–12 months.
  • Consider interruption if lymphocytes stay below 0.5 × 10⁹/L for more than 6 months.
  • Warnings include PML and liver injury.
  • On dimethyl fumarate, flushing 40% versus 6% on placebo; 3% stopped for flushing. 459,487

Cladribine tablets

  • Lymphopenia in 87%; nadir below 500 cells/µL in 26% on 3.5 mg/kg.
  • In CLARITY, lymphocytopenia as an adverse event in 21.6% versus 1.8%.
  • Boxed warning: malignancy (0.27 events per 100 patient-years) and teratogenicity.
  • Handled as a cytotoxic: dry hands, swallowed at once, at least 3 hours apart from other oral drugs. 165,303
Boxed warning

Alemtuzumab

  • Secondary autoimmunity: thyroid disorders 36.8%, with new cases more than 7 years after the first dose; immune thrombocytopenia 2%.
  • Monthly CBC, creatinine and urinalysis, and 3-monthly thyroid tests, until 48 months after the last infusion.
  • Stroke, ischaemic or haemorrhagic, and cervicocephalic arterial dissection within 3 days of dosing; infusion reactions in 92% in the trials. 280
  • Reports date an FDA safety communication on stroke to November 2018; that date is unverified, because the FDA page no longer loads.
  • EU: an Article 20 review (CHMP 14 November 2019; Commission decision 16 January 2020) restricted Lemtrada to highly active RRMS despite at least one DMT, or rapidly evolving severe disease, added contraindications including prior angina, myocardial infarction, stroke, cervicocephalic arterial dissection and uncontrolled hypertension, and limited infusion to hospitals with ready access to intensive care. 126
Boxed warning

Teriflunomide

  • Hepatotoxicity and embryofetal toxicity.
  • Transaminases and bilirubin within 6 months before starting, then ALT at least monthly for 6 months; CBC, tuberculosis screening, blood pressure.
  • Accelerated elimination: cholestyramine 8 g every 8 hours or charcoal 50 g every 12 hours for 11 days.
  • In TOWER, hair thinning 13% versus 4% and ALT increase 14% versus 8%. 14,92

Ozanimod, siponimod, ponesimod

  • Ozanimod is labelled 0.92 mg; the trials call the same dose “1 mg” (as ozanimod hydrochloride).
  • Titration: ozanimod over 8 days, ponesimod over 14 days; baseline ECG and CBC.
  • Siponimod: CYP2C9 genotype first; *3/*3 is a contraindication.
  • All four S1P labels warn of a severe increase in disability after stopping. 163,305,386,512

Anti-CD20 antibodies

  • Hepatitis B screening and immunoglobulins before the first dose; immunoglobulins monitored during and after.
  • Consider stopping for serious opportunistic or recurrent infections, or prolonged hypogammaglobulinaemia.
  • Infusion reactions: 34.3% on ocrelizumab in OPERA; 47.7% on ublituximab in ULTIMATE. Serious infections 5.0% versus 2.9% (ULTIMATE) and 2.5% versus 1.8% (ASCLEPIOS). 186,187,364,453
  • Early evidence Inflammatory bowel disease: in a Swedish register cohort, rituximab was linked to 110 new cases per 100,000 person-years, versus 13 on natalizumab, dimethyl fumarate or fingolimod and 41 in the general population (adjusted HR for the other drugs versus rituximab 0.10); 62% of cases on rituximab were Crohn disease. Ocrelizumab, ofatumumab and ublituximab were not studied. 231
Boxed warning

Natalizumab

  • PML: anti-JCV antibodies, duration and prior immunosuppressants are the named risk factors; prescribed only within a REMS programme.
  • 300 mg IV over one hour every 4 weeks; observation for 1 hour after each of the first 12 infusions.
  • Infusion reactions about 24% versus 18% on placebo; serious hypersensitivity under 1%. 474

Interferons and glatiramer acetate

  • Interferon labels: liver injury (monitor liver tests), depression and suicide, injection-site necrosis, low blood counts (monitor CBC), thrombotic microangiopathy, pulmonary arterial hypertension, heart failure.
  • Glatiramer acetate carries a boxed warning for anaphylaxis, which can occur from as early as after the first dose, up to years after starting. 15,35,95,399
Historic

Mitoxantrone

  • No dosing if LVEF is below 50% or the lifetime dose exceeds 140 mg/m²; LVEF before every dose.
  • Heart failure may occur ... months to years after termination; the drug increases the risk of developing secondary acute myeloid leukemia. 318

Rebound after stopping

Two drugs stand out for what can happen in the weeks after the last dose. Move through the 28 weeks after stopping to see when the published windows open. 324,395

Window bars are the published time spans. The icons fill in step with those windows for illustration; only the final counts are published. 154,163,184,395

Week 28 after stopping

Six months on

In RESTORE, 175 relapse-free people on natalizumab were randomised to a 24-week interruption: MRI activity returned in 0% of those who continued versus 46% on placebo, and relapses occurred in 4% who continued versus 15–29% in the other arms, from 4–8 weeks after the last dose. AAN 2018 (Level A) says patients must be counselled that relapse or MRI activity risk rises within six months of stopping natalizumab. The fingolimod label reports severe increases in disability, usually within 12 weeks but up to 24, and most patients did not return to their previous function. In a UCSF series, 5 of 46 people (10.9%) had severe rebound relapses 4–16 weeks after stopping, with a median of 9 new gadolinium-enhancing lesions that persisted 3–6 months despite steroids or B-cell depletion. The siponimod and ozanimod labels say severe exacerbation including disease rebound has been rarely reported after stopping an S1P modulator. 154,163,184,305,395,512

In plain wordsStopping natalizumab or fingolimod can let MS come back within weeks, sometimes more strongly than before. That is why guidelines plan the next drug before the old one stops.
Exam hookNatalizumab: activity returns from 4–8 weeks, risk raised within 6 months. Fingolimod: severe rebound usually within 12 weeks. ECTRIMS/EAN R16: consider another highly efficacious drug.

What the guidelines add

ECTRIMS/EAN 2018 R19 (consensus): DMDs are not licensed during pregnancy, except glatiramer acetate 20 mg/ml. R21 (weak): for persistent high activity, natalizumab throughout pregnancy may be considered after full discussion, and alemtuzumab is an option for planned pregnancy if a 4-month interval is strictly observed before conception. AAN 2018 Switching 10 (Level B): counsel stopping DMT before a planned conception unless the risk of MS activity during pregnancy outweighs the risk associated with the specific DMT. 324,395

The 2026 UK consensus update

The updated UK consensus on pregnancy in MS stresses family planning when choosing a treatment. It describes the transfer of monoclonal antibodies into breast milk as generally low, so treatment during breastfeeding can be considered, and vaccines in pregnancy as generally safe. 334

In plain wordsSome MS medicines are fine to stop just before trying for a baby, some need months to clear, and one needs a blood test to prove it has gone. Labels spell this out, and planning ahead with the MS team is what the guidance stresses.

Vaccines on DMTs

Timing and live vaccines

  • ECTRIMS/EAN 2023 (53 recommendations): before an immunosuppressive DMT, inactivated vaccines ideally at least 2 weeks before treatment onset; live attenuated vaccines at least 4 weeks before treatment onset, 6 weeks for ocrelizumab and alemtuzumab. 374
  • Live attenuated vaccines can be used untreated or on interferon or glatiramer acetate, and should be avoided on dimethyl fumarate, teriflunomide, S1P modulators, natalizumab, cladribine, alemtuzumab and anti-CD20 antibodies. 374
  • On 6-monthly anti-CD20 therapy, inactivated vaccines at least 3 months after the last anti-CD20 treatment and 4–6 weeks before the next infusion (the window is drawn in the anti-CD20 calendar above). 374
  • The fingolimod label says the drug reduces the immune response to vaccination, which may be less effective during and for up to 2 months after stopping. 163
  • AAN 2019: follow local schedules with yearly influenza vaccine, vaccinate at least 4–6 weeks before starting immunosuppressive therapy, and delay vaccination during a relapse. 131
Blunted responses · VELOCE

Vaccine responses on ocrelizumab

  • Tetanus response: 23.9% on ocrelizumab, 54.5% in controls
  • 12 or more pneumococcal serotypes: 37.3% on ocrelizumab, 97.1% in controls

People on ocrelizumab responded less than controls: a response to tetanus in 23.9% versus 54.5%, and to 12 or more pneumococcal serotypes in 37.3% versus 97.1%. In an Italian COVID-19 cohort of 844 people with MS, anti-CD20 therapy was associated with severe COVID-19 (OR 2.37, 95% CI 1.18–4.74). 374,447

Exam hookECTRIMS/EAN 2023 on live vaccines: usable untreated or on interferon or glatiramer, to be avoided on the other DMTs listed, and given at least 4 weeks before starting (6 weeks for ocrelizumab and alemtuzumab).
