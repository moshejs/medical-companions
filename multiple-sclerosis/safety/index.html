Controversy

How often to retest a negative antibody result

The US label reports seroconversion of 3–8% a year and asks for antibody-negative patients to be retested periodically, without an interval. A UK expert group reports that the EMA's CHMP recommends testing every 6 months, and suggests MRI at least every 6 months (index ≤1.5) or every 3–4 months (index >1.5) after 18 months of treatment in antibody-positive patients. Practice follows regional regulation. 306,473

“A negative JCV test means no risk”

The label states antibody-negative patients remain at risk, from new infection or a false-negative result; the assay's false-negative rate is 3%. In Bloomgren 2012, all 54 PML cases with an earlier blood sample had been antibody-positive. 42,473

What the label says to watch for

The natalizumab label describes PML symptoms progressing over days to weeks: one-sided weakness or clumsiness of a limb, disturbed vision, and changes in thinking, memory and orientation leading to confusion and personality change. Dosing is withheld at the first suggestive sign. ECTRIMS/EAN 2018 R13 recommends MRI every 3–6 months for people at high PML risk (JCV positive, natalizumab beyond 18 months). 323,473

Controversy

Extended-interval dosing

Six-weekly dosing is used to lower PML risk, and Ho 2017 shows how strongly risk depends on index and duration. NOVA, which tested lesions rather than PML, found more new lesions on 6-weekly dosing (significant under one estimand), driven by two outliers, with one asymptomatic PML case in the 6-weekly arm. The US label still states every 4 weeks. 151,209,473

What the guidelines add

ECTRIMS/EAN 2018 R19 (consensus): DMDs are not licensed during pregnancy, except glatiramer acetate 20 mg/ml. R21 (weak): for persistent high activity, natalizumab throughout pregnancy may be considered after full discussion, and alemtuzumab is an option for planned pregnancy if a 4-month interval is strictly observed before conception. AAN 2018 Switching 10 (Level B): counsel stopping DMT before a planned conception unless the risk of MS activity during pregnancy outweighs the risk associated with the specific DMT. 323,394

The 2026 UK consensus update

The updated UK consensus on pregnancy in MS stresses family planning when choosing a treatment. It describes the transfer of monoclonal antibodies into breast milk as generally low, so treatment during breastfeeding can be considered, and vaccines in pregnancy as generally safe. 333

In plain wordsSome MS medicines are fine to stop just before trying for a baby, some need months to clear, and one needs a blood test to prove it has gone. Labels spell this out, and planning ahead with the MS team is what the guidance stresses.

Vaccines on DMTs

Timing and live vaccines

  • ECTRIMS/EAN 2023 (53 recommendations): before an immunosuppressive DMT, inactivated vaccines ideally at least 2 weeks before treatment onset; live attenuated vaccines at least 4 weeks before treatment onset, 6 weeks for ocrelizumab and alemtuzumab. 373
  • Live attenuated vaccines can be used untreated or on interferon or glatiramer acetate, and should be avoided on dimethyl fumarate, teriflunomide, S1P modulators, natalizumab, cladribine, alemtuzumab and anti-CD20 antibodies. 373
  • On 6-monthly anti-CD20 therapy, inactivated vaccines at least 3 months after the last anti-CD20 treatment and 4–6 weeks before the next infusion (the window is drawn in the anti-CD20 calendar above). 373
  • The fingolimod label says the drug reduces the immune response to vaccination, which may be less effective during and for up to 2 months after stopping. 163
  • AAN 2019: follow local schedules with yearly influenza vaccine, vaccinate at least 4–6 weeks before starting immunosuppressive therapy, and delay vaccination during a relapse. 131
Blunted responses · VELOCE

Vaccine responses on ocrelizumab

    People on ocrelizumab responded less than controls: a response to tetanus in 23.9% versus 54.5%, and to 12 or more pneumococcal serotypes in 37.3% versus 97.1%. In an Italian COVID-19 cohort of 844 people with MS, anti-CD20 therapy was associated with severe COVID-19 (OR 2.37, 95% CI 1.18–4.74). 373,446

    Exam hookECTRIMS/EAN 2023 on live vaccines: usable untreated or on interferon or glatiramer, to be avoided on the other DMTs listed, and given at least 4 weeks before starting (6 weeks for ocrelizumab and alemtuzumab).
