Part 6 · Context & review

Sources

Every reference behind MS Companion: journal articles, guidelines, drug labels, trial registrations and regulator pages. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Eight research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology and history, genetics and risk, mechanism, diagnosis, disease-modifying therapies, living with MS, research toward a cure, and nursing care. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov, ISRCTN or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked not matched in a research database are regulator pages, press releases and society pages that have no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are used only for what they state, so treat them with extra care.
