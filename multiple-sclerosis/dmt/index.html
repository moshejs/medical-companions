Part 4 · Treatment

How the drugs work

Each MS disease-modifying therapy blocks a different step of the immune attack: holding cells in lymph nodes, stopping them entering the brain, or removing them. Pick a drug class to watch where it acts, then compare continuous and pulsed treatment over two years.

Published label and trial information only. Doses below are quoted from the US labels or trial reports so the mechanisms make sense. They are not instructions. Which medicine suits a particular person is a decision for that person and their MS team.

Step 1 of 4 · No treatment

S1P receptor modulators

Without treatment, T cells (orange) and B cells (violet) are activated in a lymph node, leave it through the S1P1 receptor, travel in the blood, grip VCAM-1 on the blood-brain barrier with the integrin VLA-4, squeeze through and attack myelin. 205,303,505 The whole story is in 1.3 The immune attack.

Drug class card

fingolimod · siponimod · ozanimod · ponesimod

Mechanism
Lymphocytes need the receptor S1P1 to leave lymph nodes. 303 These drugs first activate S1P1, then pull it inside the cell (functional antagonism), so cells cannot exit and blood lymphocyte counts fall. 81,164 Fingolimod-phosphate binds S1P1, 3, 4 and 5; siponimod and ozanimod bind S1P1 and S1P5; ponesimod binds S1P1. 164,306,387,513
First FDA approval
Fingolimod 21 Sep 2010; siponimod 26 Mar 2019; ozanimod 25 Mar 2020; ponesimod 18 Mar 2021. 135
Label indication
"Relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis (MS), to include clinically isolated syndrome, relapsing-remitting disease, and active secondary progressive disease, in adults" (the wording shared by most current US labels). 14,50,241,306,475 Fingolimod's label extends this to patients 10 years and older. 164
Route and dosing as published
Fingolimod 0.5 mg once daily (0.25 mg at 40 kg or less), with at least 6 hours of first-dose observation for bradycardia. 164 Siponimod needs CYP2C9 genotyping and titration; CYP2C9*3/*3 is a contraindication. 306 Ozanimod is titrated from 0.23 mg to 0.92 mg by day 8; ponesimod over 14 days from 2 mg to 20 mg. 387,513
Pivotal trial headline
FREEDOMS (fingolimod against placebo, 24 months): ARR 0.18 versus 0.40. 235 TRANSFORMS (against IM interferon): 0.16 (95% CI 0.12–0.21) versus 0.33 (0.26–0.42). 82 SUNBEAM and RADIANCE (ozanimod against IM interferon): rate ratio 0.52 (0.41–0.66) and 0.62 (0.51–0.77). 84,86 OPTIMUM (ponesimod against teriflunomide): ARR 0.202 versus 0.290, 30.5% lower. 238 EXPAND (siponimod in SPMS): disability HR 0.79 (0.65–0.95). 236
Efficacy tier
Moderate to higherAAN 2018 lists fingolimod with alemtuzumab, natalizumab and ocrelizumab as more effective than the older self-injectables, so its own table row is tagged higher. Siponimod, ozanimod and ponesimod were approved after that statement; ozanimod and ponesimod beat active comparators head to head. 86,135,238,396
In plain wordsS1P modulators lock the exit door of the lymph nodes. The immune cells are kept in, not destroyed, so the effect wears off when the drug stops.
Exam hookS1P modulators = sequestration, not depletion. Fingolimod: first-dose bradycardia observation, macular oedema (fundus check at 3–4 months), rebound after stopping. 164,185

The untreated pathway, step by step, is in 1.3 The immune attack. Choosing between these medicines is covered in 4.2 The DMT ladder, and their monitoring in 4.3 Safety & monitoring.

Continuous or pulsed: lymphocyte counts over 24 months

Continuous therapies work only while they are being given. Pulsed immune reconstitution therapies are given as two short yearly courses: they knock lymphocyte counts down, and the immune system then rebuilds itself. Drag through two years and watch the four patterns side by side. Numbered dots mark the published timing anchors the curves pass through.

Month 0

First dose

Anti-CD20 infusions and daily S1P modulator doses begin continuous treatment. Cladribine (course 1: two short cycles of tablets) and alemtuzumab (5 days of infusions) begin a pulse. 83,164,304,365

In plain wordsSome medicines work only while they keep being given. Others are given as a short burst that removes many immune cells, and then the body rebuilds them.
Exam hookContinuous (anti-CD20, S1P modulators) versus pulsed immune reconstitution therapy (cladribine, alemtuzumab). S1P: sequestration, reversible, rebound risk on stopping. Anti-CD20: depletion that spares CD20-negative plasma cells. Pulsed: deplete, then rebuild.

What is still debated

Debated

Why does anti-CD20 work?

One view: through B cells presenting antigen and making cytokines, not antibody, because the benefit arrives before immunoglobulin falls and oligoclonal bands persist. The other gives antibody and complement a role in at least some lesions. Both may operate. 48,87,284,290,402

Regulators differ

Tolebrutinib

The EU authorised it in June 2026 for SPMS without relapses in the last 2 years; the FDA issued a complete response letter in December 2025. Both looked at the same HERCULES data, which carry a liver-enzyme signal. 126,157,421

Off-label

Rituximab

RIFUND-MS found it superior to dimethyl fumarate, yet it remains unapproved for MS in the US and EU, and AAN 2018 mentions it only in PML counselling. 396,457

Mixed

Relapses fall, but disability?

CARE-MS I and ULTIMATE showed no significant disability benefit over their comparators; CARE-MS II, OPERA and ASCLEPIOS did. Cochrane rates the cladribine disability evidence as low certainty and possibly null, despite the CLARITY hazard ratio of 0.67. 74,83,85,166,187,188,454

In plain wordsDisease-modifying therapies act on the immune attack, not on damage that is already done. They differ in where they act: some keep immune cells inside the lymph nodes, one stops them climbing into the brain, and some remove whole groups of immune cells for a while.
Exam hookMatch drug to step. S1P modulators: node exit (sequestration, reverses on stopping). Natalizumab: VLA-4 docking on VCAM-1 at the barrier. Anti-CD20: B-cell lysis that spares CD20-negative plasma cells. Cladribine and alemtuzumab (CD52): pulsed immune reconstitution. Teriflunomide: DHODH. BTK inhibitors: B cells and microglia behind the barrier. 14,164,176,259,281,475
