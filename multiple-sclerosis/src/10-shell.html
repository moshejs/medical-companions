Multiple sclerosis, explained

Multiple sclerosis affects an estimated 2.8 to 3.1 million people worldwide, most of them diagnosed as young adults.[[Walton2020,King2026]] Here it is in moving pictures: watch a nerve signal jump along myelin and stall where it is stripped, follow relapses and silent progression over decades, try the McDonald criteria for yourself, and see where each medicine acts.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Schematic, not to scale. The top fibre is myelinated; the bottom one has lost myelin over part of its length.
