MS Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on multiple sclerosis, made for health-science and nursing courses and for patient understanding.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should change a treatment without your own clinician. Criteria, doses and guideline statements are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one disease each. Also there: Alzheimer's Companion and FMF Companion.
