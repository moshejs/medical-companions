Seeing the delay: the visual evoked potential

Show a flashing pattern to one eye at a time and record over the back of the head. A positive wave arrives about 100 ms after the flash, named P100. When one optic nerve is demyelinated its wave comes late. Pick a state for the right optic nerve and press play.

Who makes the myelin: brain and spinal cord versus peripheral nerves

In the CNS one oligodendrocyte myelinates 40–60 different axons, one internode on each, so every axon depends on many oligodendrocytes. In the PNS each Schwann cell myelinates one internode of one axon. [[Kister2022]] Oligodendrocytes also feed the axon through myelinic channels, so losing them can harm axons. [[Kister2022]]

Exam hookSaltatory conduction needs myelin. Demyelination causes slowing (a delayed VEP), conduction block and temperature sensitivity (Uhthoff). [[Smith1999,Halliday1972,Frohman2013]] CNS myelin = oligodendrocyte; PNS myelin = Schwann cell, and MS spares the PNS. [[Kister2022]]
In plain wordsMyelin lets nerve signals jump quickly along the wire. Where it is stripped, signals crawl or stop, which is why symptoms can come and go and can get worse in the heat. Some repair happens, but fibres that are cut through are lost.
