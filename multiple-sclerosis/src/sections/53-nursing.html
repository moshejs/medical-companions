Part 5 · Living with MS

Nursing care

What drug labels, guidelines and nursing studies report on MS nursing care: assessment scales, infusion and first-dose monitoring, lab calendars, teaching and notes. Each part has an animation or a tool to try.

ScopeThis module reports what labels, guidelines and published studies say. It is not a care plan and does not replace local protocols, your scope of practice or the prescriber. Drug details come from current US labels unless stated. Where the sources name a gap, the module says so instead of filling it.
