Everything else the criteria ask about

Clinical picture
Attacks
Attacks
Objective clinical evidence of

An attack means symptoms with corroborating objective findings, lasting at least 24 hours, without fever or infection [[Solomon2026]]. Accepting attacks without objective evidence was a leading cause of misdiagnosis [[Solomon2016]].

Cerebrospinal fluid

κ-FLC index = (CSF κ-FLC ÷ serum κ-FLC) ÷ (CSF albumin ÷ serum albumin). Positive CSF in 2024 means OCB or an index ≥6.1; each laboratory should verify that cut-off [[Deisenhammer2025,Konen2026]]. The 2017 criteria use OCB only [[Thompson2018]].

Optic nerve evidence (2024)

With rigorous quality control, an inter-eye difference of ≥6 μm in peripapillary RNFL or ≥4 μm in GCIPL supports optic nerve involvement; an MRI optic nerve lesion also counts (place it on the map). Any of these needs no better explanation for the optic nerve finding [[Saidha2025,IACCTMS2025Slides]].

Susceptibility MRI (2024)

Set "with central vein sign" and "with paramagnetic rim" per site in the lesion details above. Select-6: at least 6 white-matter lesions with a central vein or, if fewer than 10 white-matter lesions are seen, more CVS-positive than CVS-negative lesions. PRL: at least 1 paramagnetic rim lesion [[Montalban2025,Solomon2026,Samara2025]]. The official slide deck is internally inconsistent here: one slide applies the majority rule below 6 lesions and another below 10. The published figure says fewer than 10, which this calculator uses [[IACCTMS2025Slides]].

Step 4 · No better explanation

"No better explanation" means the presentation is typical of MS and nothing clinical or paraclinical suggests another diagnosis, or the workup for those diagnoses is negative [[Solomon2026]]. A calculator cannot decide this. See The differential.

The same case under 2017 and 2024

Both rule sets run on every change. The highlighted card is the version the map is showing. Apart from some people with primary progressive MS who lack several spinal cord lesions, everyone who meets 2017 also meets 2024, and a diagnosis made under older criteria is not changed by the revision [[Solomon2026]].

2024 dissemination in time follows the paper's Figure 3 footnote: "one or more new T2 lesions or one or more gadolinium-positive lesions or a clinical attack" [[Montalban2025]]. The companion glossary reads it more strictly, as new T2 lesions over time or enhancing and non-enhancing lesions together on one scan, as in 2017 [[Solomon2026]]. The sources do not settle this; the verdict tells you when the stricter reading would change the answer.

How well the criteria and tests perform

Where experts disagree

Debated

Diagnosing without dissemination in time

2024 allows a diagnosis on 4–5 sites alone, or with a central vein sign, to speed diagnosis [[Montalban2025,VanDerWalt2026]]. Real-world cohorts found many more diagnoses, specificity of 57.8% against 2017 at follow-up, and low 2017–2024 agreement in one cohort (κ=0.112) [[Brownlee2026,Hamdy2025]]. The London authors note that their "specificity" counts 2017-negative CIS and RIS as false positives. No long-term prospective validation yet.

Unresolved

Calling RIS multiple sclerosis

About 51% of people with RIS develop clinical symptoms within 10 years [[Rocca2024,IACCTMS2025Slides]], and OCB (HR 5.09) and κ-FLC ≥6.1 (HR 2.79) predict it [[Fissolo2025]]. Critics point to overdiagnosis, the consequences of treatment and labelling, and gaps in implementation [[Konen2026,Samara2025,Brownlee2026]]. Letters raising concerns were published with an authors' reply [[Montalban2026Reply]].

Varies by centre

The optic nerve as a fifth site

Adding it raised sensitivity without losing specificity by VEP in one cohort [[VidalJordana2021]] and gave similar accuracy across MRI, OCT and VEP in another [[VidalJordana2024]]. Other cohorts found lower specificity, and asymptomatic optic nerve findings in non-optic-neuritis CIS added no patients [[Filippi2018,Brownlee2018]]. OCT and VEP thresholds depend on quality control, centre and device [[Saidha2025]].

Threshold choice

Central vein sign and κ-FLC cut-offs

Earlier work used a 40% lesion rule; Select-3 was sensitive but not specific, and 2024 adopted Select-6 [[Cagol2024,Toljan2024,Montalban2025]]. Published κ-FLC cut-offs range from 2.4 to 20; 6.1 is a weighted mean, and κ-FLC and OCB disagree in about 10%–13% of cases [[Deisenhammer2025,IACCTMS2025Slides]].

Exam hook2017: DIS = 2 of 4 areas (PV, cortical/JC, IT, cord); DIT = enhancing + non-enhancing together, or a new lesion, or a second attack; CSF OCB can stand in for DIT; symptomatic lesions count except in the optic nerve; PPMS = 1 year of progression + 2 of 3 (brain, ≥2 cord, OCB). 2024: optic nerve is the 5th site; DIS + positive CSF (OCB or κ-FLC ≥6.1) is enough; one site needs (CSF or DIT) and (CVS or PRL); 4–5 sites alone only after an attack or progression; RIS can qualify with ≥2 sites + CSF, CVS or DIT. [[Thompson2018,Montalban2025]]
In plain wordsTo diagnose MS, doctors look for damage in more than one part of the nervous system, signs that it happened at more than one time (or a spinal fluid test that stands in for that), and then make sure nothing else explains it better. The 2024 rules add new scan and eye tests that can make the answer faster, but the final check still belongs to a person.
