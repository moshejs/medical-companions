How the drugs work

Each MS disease-modifying therapy blocks a different step of the immune attack: holding cells in lymph nodes, stopping them entering the brain, or removing them. Pick a drug class to watch where it acts, then compare continuous and pulsed treatment over two years.

Continuous or pulsed: lymphocyte counts over 24 months

Continuous therapies work only while they are being given. Pulsed immune reconstitution therapies are given as two short yearly courses: they knock lymphocyte counts down, and the immune system then rebuilds itself.     What is still debated

    Debated

    Why does anti-CD20 work?

    One view: through B cells presenting antigen and making cytokines, not antibody, because the benefit arrives before immunoglobulin falls and oligoclonal bands persist. The other gives antibody and complement a role in at least some lesions. Both may operate. [[Reich2018,Comi2021,Li2015,Breij2008,Lucchinetti2000]]

    Regulators differ

    Tolebrutinib

    The EU authorised it in June 2026 for SPMS without relapses in the last 2 years; the FDA issued a complete response letter in December 2025. Both looked at the same HERCULES data, which carry a liver-enzyme signal. [[EMACenrifki2026,Sanofi2025,Fox2025]]

    Off-label

    Rituximab

    RIFUND-MS found it superior to dimethyl fumarate, yet it remains unapproved for MS in the US and EU, and AAN 2018 mentions it only in PML counselling. [[Svenningsson2022,RaeGrant2018]]

    Mixed

    Relapses fall, but disability?

    CARE-MS I and ULTIMATE showed no significant disability benefit over their comparators; CARE-MS II, OPERA and ASCLEPIOS did. Cochrane rates the cladribine disability evidence as low certainty and possibly null, despite the CLARITY hazard ratio of 0.67. [[Cohen2012,Steinman2022,Coles2012,Hauser2017,Hauser2020Ofatumumab,Celani2026,Giovannoni2010]]

    In plain wordsDisease-modifying therapies act on the immune attack, not on damage that is already done. They differ in where they act: some keep immune cells inside the lymph nodes, one stops them climbing into the brain, and some remove whole groups of immune cells for a while.
