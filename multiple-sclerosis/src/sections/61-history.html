What it was called

Charcot's French name was sclérose en plaques, "sclerosis in patches". Dawson's 1916 paper used the name disseminated sclerosis. Click a dated name to jump the timeline. [[Zalc2018,Dawson1916]]

Exam hookFive dates: 1868 Charcot, "sclérose en plaques" and the triad of nystagmus, dysarthria and ataxia with intention tremor · 1981 first MRI of MS · 23 July 1993 Betaseron (interferon beta-1b) approved by the FDA · 20 December 1996 Copaxone (glatiramer acetate) · 2001 McDonald criteria bring MRI into diagnosis. In plain wordsPeople have recognised MS for about 200 years, first from a patient's diary and drawings of damaged brains. Scanners made the damage visible in life in the 1980s. Treatments that change the course of the disease only arrived in the 1990s.
