The immune attack

How immune cells switched on in a lymph node leave it, cross the blood-brain barrier and strip myelin, then smoulder behind a closed barrier. Step through the attack, or turn on where each drug class acts.

Teal numbered badges mark the step each drug class targets. Mechanism only; trial results are in How the drugs work.

Why removing B cells works before antibodies fall

Anti-CD20 antibodies empty the blood of B cells within two weeks, yet antibody levels and the plasma cells that make them barely change. Scrub through the first year after a first infusion to see the order of events.

Schematic curves through the published anchor points (B cells low by day 14; response as early as 8–12 weeks; IgG generally unchanged with initial courses). Heights are not measured values.
