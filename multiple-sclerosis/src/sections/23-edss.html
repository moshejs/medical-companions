Try it: a teaching calculator

A teaching tool, not a clinical score. A real EDSS comes from a full neurological examination by a trained examiner, who grades each functional system and applies Kurtzke's detailed rules. This simplified version does not reproduce those rules: it uses only the published walking and care anchors, and places any examination findings with full walking in the 1.0–3.5 range. Even trained raters disagree: reported inter-rater kappa is 0.32–0.76 for the total score and 0.23–0.58 for individual functional systems. [[MeyerMoock2014]]

When does worsening count? Confirmed disability progression

In trials, confirmed disability accumulation is typically an EDSS rise of at least 1.0 point from a baseline of 5.5 or less, or at least 0.5 point from a baseline above 5.5, that is still there at a visit 12 or 24 weeks later. Composite definitions add a 20% or greater worsening on the timed 25-foot walk or the 9-hole peg test. Watch one person's visits below: a short-lived rise and a lasting one. [[Kappos2020]]

Beyond EDSS: other measures

MSFC

MS Functional Composite

Built in 1999 from three timed tests: the Timed 25-Foot Walk (legs), the 9-Hole Peg Test (arms) and the PASAT (cognition), combined as a z-score. Change over one year predicted later change in EDSS, which suggests it is more sensitive to change. Its main limitations are learning effects and the z-score scoring. [[Cutter1999,MeyerMoock2014]]

T25FW · 9HPT

Timed walk and peg test

The Timed 25-Foot Walk and the 9-Hole Peg Test are used alone too. In trial composites, a 20% or greater worsening on either counts toward confirmed disability progression. Walking speed is also how fampridine response was measured: 35% timed-walk responders vs 8% on placebo. [[Kappos2020,Goodman2009]]

SDMT

Symbol Digit Modalities Test

Measures processing speed. A change of about 4 points or 10% has been proposed as clinically meaningful. It is brief, reliable and sensitive enough to monitor cognition, including during acute disease activity. [[Benedict2017,Benedict2020]]

Limitations

Why EDSS is criticised

It is ordinal, not a ruler: the lower range rests on the examination, 4–6 depends heavily on walking, and above 6 it measures handicap. Variability between raters is greatest at 1.0–3.5. Across 48 progression definitions in MSBase, concordance was only 17.3%, and 11–34% of progression events regressed within 5 years. [[MeyerMoock2014,Kalincik2015]]
