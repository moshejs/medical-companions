## Findings

### cure · BTK inhibitors
- REMODEL-1 and 2 (remibrutinib 100 mg twice daily vs teriflunomide 14 mg daily; relapsing MS, aged 18–55, EDSS 0–5.5; 2007 randomised at 407 centres in 46 countries; up to 30 months; NCT05147220, NCT05156281): annualised relapse rate 0.073 vs 0.112 (RR 0.648, 95% CI 0.453–0.927; p=0.018) and 0.080 vs 0.116 (RR 0.688, 0.487–0.972; p=0.034). Discontinuation for adverse events 3.1% vs 5.8%; alopecia 5.8% vs 13.5%; "no liver safety concerns" per the authors (Established) [[Wiendl2026]].
- This replaces the claim that only a Novartis release existed and no numbers were published. The pooled disability-progression result remains sponsor-reported only [[Novartis2026REMODEL]].

### safety · Anti-CD20 antibodies
- Swedish MS register cohort (13,748 treatment starts in RRMS 2011–2023; 6481 rituximab; 61,065 matched general-population episodes): crude inflammatory bowel disease incidence per 100,000 person-years 110 (95% CI 76–147) on rituximab, 13 (3–30) on natalizumab, dimethyl fumarate or fingolimod, and 41 (34–48) in the general population. Adjusted HR for the comparator drugs vs rituximab 0.10 (0.04–0.22). 62% of IBD on rituximab was Crohn disease vs 29% in the general population. NNH at 5 years 131 (93–228). Only rituximab studied (Early evidence) [[Kallin2026]].

### Exam hooks
- REMODEL: remibrutinib beat teriflunomide on relapse rate in two phase 3 trials (RR 0.648 and 0.688) [[Wiendl2026]].

### In plain words
- A new pill in the BTK inhibitor family cut relapses by about a third compared with an older pill in two large trials [[Wiendl2026]].
- In Sweden, people with MS on rituximab developed bowel inflammation (IBD) more often than people on other MS drugs. It is still uncommon [[Kallin2026]].

## Open questions and controversies
- Does the IBD signal extend to ocrelizumab, ofatumumab and ublituximab? Not studied in [[Kallin2026]]; open.
