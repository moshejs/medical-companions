Part 3 · Diagnosis

The differential

NMOSD, MOGAD, small-vessel disease, migraine and other conditions can look like MS. Watch MRI patterns morph from MS to NMOSD to MOGAD, then click red flags to see which mimics they point to.

MS is a diagnosis of exclusion: the criteria only apply when there is no better explanation 219. Across studies, 5%–41% of MS diagnoses were wrong; the pooled figure was 15% 515. A 2008 consensus scored 79 red flags (36 major, 32 intermediate, 11 minor), and a 2023 consensus updated them 317,444.

Pattern 1

Typical MS

Periventricular MS lesions follow the deep medullary veins, so their long axis runs perpendicular to the lateral ventricles and they look ovoid ("Dawson's fingers"). Cord lesions are short: under two vertebral segments, under half the cross-section, mostly peripheral in the lateral and dorsal columns, cigar-like on sagittal and wedge-shaped on axial images. Multiple short cord lesions are highly specific for MS 141.

In plain wordsIn MS the spots in the brain are small ovals that point away from the fluid spaces in the middle, like fingers. In the spinal cord they are short and sit near the edge.
Exam hookMS cord lesion: under 2 segments, under half the cross-section, peripheral. Cord lesions are not seen with normal ageing, migraine or most cerebrovascular disease 141.
Why the label matters Several MS drugs may make NMOSD worse. The international NMOSD panel noted observational data that interferon-β, natalizumab and fingolimod may worsen NMO 501. Early evidence In 5 AQP4-positive people first treated as MS with natalizumab, 9 relapses occurred on treatment, 4 people accumulated disability and 1 died 250. Relapses with rising AQP4 antibody titres on interferon beta-1a, and a catastrophic relapse after one natalizumab dose, have been reported; interferon-β, glatiramer acetate, fingolimod, natalizumab, alemtuzumab and dimethyl fumarate have all been linked to unexpected NMOSD exacerbations 247,249,376.

How misdiagnosis happens

Established

What people misdiagnosed with MS really had

110 patients at 4 US centres (Solomon 2016) 443:

Migraine22%Fibromyalgia15%Non-specific symptoms + abnormal MRI12%Conversion / psychogenic11%NMOSD6%

70% had received a disease-modifying therapy, 31% had unnecessary morbidity, and 33% had carried the wrong diagnosis for 10 years or more 443.

Established

Referrals to two Los Angeles centres

17% and 19% of referrals already carrying an MS diagnosis were misdiagnosed (Kaisey 2019). Commonest true diagnoses: migraine 16%, RIS 9%, spondylopathy 7%, neuropathy 7%. About 110 patient-years of unnecessary DMT had been given 229.

Early evidence In 2024, 21% (73 of 347) of referrals for suspected MS in London had other disorders, most often migraine or functional neurological disorder with non-specific or vascular white-matter lesions 54.

The three commonest errors

  • Treating symptoms atypical for demyelination as attacks.
  • Accepting attacks without objective evidence of a CNS lesion.
  • Over-relying on MRI spots in people with non-specific symptoms 443.
Exam hookCord lesion over ≥3 vertebral segments (LETM), area postrema syndrome (intractable hiccups or vomiting), severe or bilateral optic neuritis, CSF cells >50/μL or no OCB: think NMOSD or MOGAD; their criteria rest on AQP4-IgG and MOG-IgG by cell-based assay. Interferon-β, natalizumab and fingolimod may worsen NMOSD. 19,501
In plain wordsSeveral other conditions can leave spots on a scan that look like MS: migraine, ageing blood vessels, low vitamin B12, other immune diseases and infections. Some, like NMOSD, need different medicines, and some MS medicines can make them worse. That is why doctors look hard for clues that point elsewhere before they say "MS".
