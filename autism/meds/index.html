Read this firstNo medicine treats autism itself. The 2026 drug-development review states: "There are no approved drugs for autism core features". Monitoring an antipsychotic

Both US labels tell prescribers to monitor weight, and to monitor glucose regularly in people with, or at risk of, diabetes. Both describe dyslipidaemia as a class effect. 134,141 NICE guidance for under-19s says to consider an antipsychotic for behaviour that challenges only when psychosocial or other interventions are insufficient or cannot be delivered because of the severity of the behaviour; to review effectiveness and side effects after 3–4 weeks; and to stop if there is no clinically important response at 6 weeks. 279

Things worth knowing

Combined care

Parent training alongside medicine

In a 24-week trial of 124 children aged 4–13, risperidone plus parent training beat risperidone alone on the Home Situations Questionnaire (d=0.34) and on ABC-Irritability (d=0.48), and the final dose was lower: 1.98 vs 2.26 mg/day. Medicine works best as one part of a plan; see Supports & the evidence. 13

Placebo

Many children improve on placebo

Pooling 86 trials, 19% (95% CI 16–22%) of participants given a placebo were rated at least "much improved". Caregiver ratings, flexible dosing and large multisite trials predicted a bigger placebo response. This is why a medicine needs a placebo-controlled trial before anyone can say it works. 546

Polypharmacy

Prescribing runs ahead of evidence

In 33,565 commercially insured US children with autism (2001–2009), 35% had overlapping prescriptions from at least 2 classes, with a median length of 346 days. The authors found "minimal evidence" for multidrug treatment and called for standards of care. 557

Guidelines

What not to use for core features

NICE guidance for under-19s says do not use antipsychotics, antidepressants, anticonvulsants or exclusion diets to manage the core features of autism. 279 The adult guidance adds chelation, secretin, oxytocin, hyperbaric oxygen, testosterone regulation and supplements, among others. 461 See Unproven & harmful treatments.

Sleep

Behavioural steps come first

The American Academy of Neurology guideline asks clinicians first to look for medicines and conditions that could cause the sleep problem, then counsel parents on behavioural strategies "as a first-line treatment approach", then offer melatonin if those have not helped, using pharmaceutical-grade melatonin. 636 Parent sleep education improved time to fall asleep within a few sessions. 353

Anxiety & depression

Treated as co-occurring conditions

Antidepressants should not be used "for the routine management of core features of autism in adults". Anxiety and depression in autistic people are conditions in their own right, covered in Co-occurring conditions. 461
