12–24 months

Volume, and the head-size debate

Early evidence Brain-volume overgrowth at 12–24 months was linked to the emergence and severity of autistic social differences. 215 Established Volume rose another 15% in year 2 in typical development, and synaptic density in prefrontal cortex does not peak until after 15 months. 248,297 Hypothesis, later contested: a 2003 chart study reported accelerated head growth in 59% of infants with autistic disorder against 6% of healthy infants. 95 A 2013 reappraisal showed that typical children also "overgrew" against classical population norms, so much of early overgrowth reflected norm bias. 476 A meta-analysis found macrocephaly in 15.7% of autistic people: a subgroup. 502

In plain wordsSome autistic toddlers have larger heads or brains, but most do not. Head size is not a useful sign of autism.
Exam hookEarly overgrowth: present in a subgroup (macrocephaly 15.7%), largely a norm artefact in head-circumference charts (Raznahan 2013). 337

How big is a "brain difference"?

Group studies can find real average differences that say almost nothing about one person. Move the effect size and watch the two groups overlap.

Effect size d = 0.20

Small: the groups overlap almost entirely

Established The ENIGMA mega-analysis of 1,571 autistic and 1,651 non-autistic people (age 2–64) found smaller pallidum, putamen, amygdala and nucleus accumbens volumes, thicker frontal and thinner temporal cortex, with effect sizes between d −0.21 and 0.20. 601 At d = 0.2 the two curves overlap by about 92%, and one measure alone could sort people correctly only about 54% of the time. In plain wordsA real average difference between groups can still be far too small to say anything about one person.
Exam hookGroup difference ≠ diagnostic test. Brain-structure effects in autism are small (|d| ≤ 0.21 in ENIGMA).

The hypothesis board

Each idea below has an evidence label and two sources. Filter by label, and open the counterpoint: most of these ideas are debated, and none of them is "the autistic brain".

Filter
Established

No brain scan or blood test diagnoses autism

Because there are no reliable biomarkers, autism is diagnosed from behaviour and developmental history. MRI needs more work before it could be a reliable biomarker, because replication has been difficult. Gross brain pathology is not characteristic of autism. 337,338

Eye-tracking of social visual engagement in 475 children aged 16–30 months had 71.0% sensitivity and 80.7% specificity against expert diagnosis. It was evaluated as an aid to specialist diagnosis, not a stand-alone test. Early evidence 264

Hypothesis

Excitation/inhibition balance

Rubenstein and Merzenich proposed in 2003 that some forms of autism arise from an increased ratio of excitation to inhibition in sensory, memory, social and emotional systems. 498 In mice, raising E/I balance in medial prefrontal cortex impaired social behaviour, and boosting inhibitory cells partly rescued it. 650

Across four autism mouse models the E/I ratio rose but spiking stayed largely normal, suggesting the shift may be homeostatic compensation rather than a cause. 22 A review stresses that compensation makes a primary defect hard to separate. 419 Human support is indirect, including fewer parvalbumin interneurons postmortem. 212

Hypothesis

Predictive coding: the world "too real"

Pellicano and Burr proposed "hypo-priors": weaker expectations, so perception is shaped more by raw input than by past experience. 450 Related accounts put too much weight (precision) on sensory evidence, or uniformly high weight on prediction errors, which could make sameness a way to make the world predictable. 317,599

The accounts disagree with each other, and experimental support is early: autistic adults in one study overestimated how volatile the environment is. Early evidence 318,450

Early evidence

Synaptic pruning and mTOR

Postmortem autistic temporal cortex had higher spine density with less developmental pruning, tracking overactive mTOR signalling and impaired autophagy. 585 Golgi staining also found higher spine densities, mainly in layer II and temporal layer V. 247

The tissue samples are small, and the mechanism (rapamycin correcting pruning and behaviour) was shown in Tsc2 mice, an animal model, not in people. 585 Postmortem neuron counts in 7 autistic boys were called "small" and "preliminary" by their own authors. 96

Early evidence

Early brain overgrowth

In infant-sibling MRI, surface-area hyperexpansion at 6–12 months came before brain-volume overgrowth at 12–24 months in infants later diagnosed. 215 A meta-analysis found macrocephaly in 15.7% of autistic people and brain overgrowth in 9.1%, largest in early childhood. 502

Studies against population norms found "overgrowth" far more often than studies with local controls; typical children also overgrew against classical norms. The best-replicated parts reflect norm bias, not a disease marker. 476 Head circumference has little use as a predictor of autism. 337

Early evidence

Social attention from 2 to 6 months

In infants later diagnosed, eye fixation began at typical levels at 2 months and then declined (−4.8% a month, against +3.6% in typical infants), reaching about half the typical level by 24 months. 263 A meta-analysis of 122 studies found the largest gaze differences for eyes and whole faces during interaction. 154

The infant finding comes from a single cohort of 12 later-diagnosed infants. 263 And social difficulty may be two-way: autistic-to-autistic chains passed on information as well as non-autistic chains, while mixed chains did worse (the double empathy problem). Hypothesis 102,383

Early evidence

Maternal immune activation

In animal models, immune activation in pregnancy alone can cause lifelong changes in offspring, through maternal IL-17a in mice. 85,132 In humans, a meta-analysis of 15 studies found maternal infection associated with autism (OR 1.13; 1.30 with hospitalisation). 261

The animal work shows a mechanism; the human data show modest associations, and most children exposed to maternal infection are not autistic. 132,261 In all Danish births 1980–2005, any infection over the whole pregnancy was not associated with autism. 33

Hypothesis

The gut microbiome

Mice given gut microbiota from autistic donors showed autism-like behaviours. 539 An open-label microbiota transfer study in 18 children reported lower GI symptom scores and autism ratings at 2 years, with no control group. 270

Established for its design: in 247 people, diagnosis had a negligible direct link with the microbiome; restricted interests went with a less varied diet, which went with lower microbial diversity. 649 The autism-linked profile appeared in age- and sex-matched cohorts but not in sibling-matched ones. 389 Causal role: not shown.

Established

Convergent gene-expression patterns

Postmortem cortex shows attenuated frontal-versus-temporal gene-expression differences, a downregulated neuronal module and an upregulated immune-glial module, replicated in a larger cohort. 448,618 Changes span the whole cortex and are largest in primary visual cortex. Early evidence 163

The immune-glial module was not enriched for autism genetic signal, so it may be a consequence rather than a cause. 618 Autism shares many of these changes with schizophrenia and bipolar disorder. 162

Established

Connectivity: both under and over

ABIDE pooled 1,112 resting-state scans: both under- and over-connectivity appeared, with under-connectivity dominant for cortico-cortical and interhemispheric links. 120

No single connectivity method gave reproducible results across sites, and consistent findings needed large samples. 291 Large overlap with controls limits diagnostic use. 229

Established

Vaccines do not cause autism

In 657,461 Danish children, MMR-vaccinated children had an adjusted autism hazard ratio of 0.93 (95% CI 0.85–1.02) against unvaccinated children. 250 A meta-analysis of 1,256,407 children found no link between vaccination and autism (OR 0.99). 588 More in Risk factors & myths.

There is no credible counterpoint: the 1998 paper that started the scare was retracted and found to be fraudulent. 175,312
