Flashcards & mnemonics

Flashcards on autism, from prevalence, identity and the sensory world to genes, vaccines, M-CHAT-R/F, DSM-5-TR and ICD-11, the ABA debate, medicines, school, adulthood and nursing care. Tap a card or press Space to flip it; arrow keys move through the deck. Filter by part, or shuffle.

A note on words: many autistic people prefer identity-first language ("autistic person"), and many families and clinicians use person-first language ("person with autism"). Surveys find no single consensus, so this guide uses both and the best rule is to ask each person [[Kenny2016,Schuck2025]]. The screening tools and criteria on these cards are teaching tools for understanding, never for diagnosing yourself or your child [[Hyman2020,Hirota2023]].

Mnemonic in motion: all 3 A, any 2 of 4 B

Play the checklist to watch the DSM-5-TR rule assemble for one teaching example: all three social-communication criteria, two of the four restricted or repetitive ones, then the C, D and E gates, the support levels, and the ICD-11 6A02 codes. Sources

Every reference behind the Autism Companion: journal articles, guidelines, drug labels, device decisions and trial registrations. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Eight research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology, identity, language and history; genetics, causes and risk factors, including what does not cause autism; neuroscience, brain development and sensory processing; early signs, screening, DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 criteria and assessment; supports and non-drug interventions, including the ABA debate; medication, unproven treatments and research; co-occurring conditions, physical health, course and outcomes; and family, school, work, adulthood and nursing. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked check are regulator pages, laws and society statements that have no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are listed for completeness; treat them with extra care.
