The developing brain

From mid-pregnancy to age 4: the brain grows, wires itself, then trims. Scrub the timeline to see what typical development looks like, what infant studies found in groups of children later diagnosed with autism, and why none of it adds up to a brain test for one person.

The hypothesis board

Each idea below has an evidence label and two sources. Filter by label, and open the counterpoint: most of these ideas are debated, and none of them is "the autistic brain".

Filter
Established

No brain scan or blood test diagnoses autism

Because there are no reliable biomarkers, autism is diagnosed from behaviour and developmental history. MRI needs more work before it could be a reliable biomarker, because replication has been difficult. Gross brain pathology is not characteristic of autism. [[Lord2018,Lord2020NRDP]]

Eye-tracking of social visual engagement in 475 children aged 16–30 months had 71.0% sensitivity and 80.7% specificity against expert diagnosis. It was evaluated as an aid to specialist diagnosis, not a stand-alone test. Early evidence [[Jones2023]]

Hypothesis

Excitation/inhibition balance

Rubenstein and Merzenich proposed in 2003 that some forms of autism arise from an increased ratio of excitation to inhibition in sensory, memory, social and emotional systems. [[Rubenstein2003]] In mice, raising E/I balance in medial prefrontal cortex impaired social behaviour, and boosting inhibitory cells partly rescued it. [[Yizhar2011]]

Across four autism mouse models the E/I ratio rose but spiking stayed largely normal, suggesting the shift may be homeostatic compensation rather than a cause. [[Antoine2019]] A review stresses that compensation makes a primary defect hard to separate. [[Nelson2015]] Human support is indirect, including fewer parvalbumin interneurons postmortem. [[Hashemi2017]]

Hypothesis

Predictive coding: the world "too real"

Pellicano and Burr proposed "hypo-priors": weaker expectations, so perception is shaped more by raw input than by past experience. [[Pellicano2012]] Related accounts put too much weight (precision) on sensory evidence, or uniformly high weight on prediction errors, which could make sameness a way to make the world predictable. [[Lawson2014,VandeCruys2014]]

The accounts disagree with each other, and experimental support is early: autistic adults in one study overestimated how volatile the environment is. Early evidence [[Lawson2017,Pellicano2012]]

Early evidence

Synaptic pruning and mTOR

Postmortem autistic temporal cortex had higher spine density with less developmental pruning, tracking overactive mTOR signalling and impaired autophagy. [[Tang2014]] Golgi staining also found higher spine densities, mainly in layer II and temporal layer V. [[Hutsler2010]]

The tissue samples are small, and the mechanism (rapamycin correcting pruning and behaviour) was shown in Tsc2 mice, an animal model, not in people. [[Tang2014]] Postmortem neuron counts in 7 autistic boys were called "small" and "preliminary" by their own authors. [[Courchesne2011PFC]]

Early evidence

Early brain overgrowth

In infant-sibling MRI, surface-area hyperexpansion at 6–12 months came before brain-volume overgrowth at 12–24 months in infants later diagnosed. [[Hazlett2017]] A meta-analysis found macrocephaly in 15.7% of autistic people and brain overgrowth in 9.1%, largest in early childhood. [[Sacco2015]]

Studies against population norms found "overgrowth" far more often than studies with local controls; typical children also overgrew against classical norms. The best-replicated parts reflect norm bias, not a disease marker. [[Raznahan2013]] Head circumference has little use as a predictor of autism. [[Lord2018]]

Early evidence

Social attention from 2 to 6 months

In infants later diagnosed, eye fixation began at typical levels at 2 months and then declined (−4.8% a month, against +3.6% in typical infants), reaching about half the typical level by 24 months. [[Jones2013Eyes]] A meta-analysis of 122 studies found the largest gaze differences for eyes and whole faces during interaction. [[Frazier2017Gaze]]

The infant finding comes from a single cohort of 12 later-diagnosed infants. [[Jones2013Eyes]] And social difficulty may be two-way: autistic-to-autistic chains passed on information as well as non-autistic chains, while mixed chains did worse (the double empathy problem). Hypothesis [[Milton2012,Crompton2020]]

Early evidence

Maternal immune activation

In animal models, immune activation in pregnancy alone can cause lifelong changes in offspring, through maternal IL-17a in mice. [[Estes2016,Choi2016]] In humans, a meta-analysis of 15 studies found maternal infection associated with autism (OR 1.13; 1.30 with hospitalisation). [[Jiang2016Infection]]

The animal work shows a mechanism; the human data show modest associations, and most children exposed to maternal infection are not autistic. [[Jiang2016Infection,Estes2016]] In all Danish births 1980–2005, any infection over the whole pregnancy was not associated with autism. [[Atladottir2010]]

Hypothesis

The gut microbiome

Mice given gut microbiota from autistic donors showed autism-like behaviours. [[Sharon2019]] An open-label microbiota transfer study in 18 children reported lower GI symptom scores and autism ratings at 2 years, with no control group. [[Kang2019]]

Established for its design: in 247 people, diagnosis had a negligible direct link with the microbiome; restricted interests went with a less varied diet, which went with lower microbial diversity. [[Yap2021]] The autism-linked profile appeared in age- and sex-matched cohorts but not in sibling-matched ones. [[Morton2023]] Causal role: not shown.

Established

Convergent gene-expression patterns

Postmortem cortex shows attenuated frontal-versus-temporal gene-expression differences, a downregulated neuronal module and an upregulated immune-glial module, replicated in a larger cohort. [[Voineagu2011,Parikshak2016]] Changes span the whole cortex and are largest in primary visual cortex. Early evidence [[Gandal2022]]

The immune-glial module was not enriched for autism genetic signal, so it may be a consequence rather than a cause. [[Voineagu2011]] Autism shares many of these changes with schizophrenia and bipolar disorder. [[Gandal2018]]

Established

Connectivity: both under and over

ABIDE pooled 1,112 resting-state scans: both under- and over-connectivity appeared, with under-connectivity dominant for cortico-cortical and interhemispheric links. [[DiMartino2014]]

No single connectivity method gave reproducible results across sites, and consistent findings needed large samples. [[King2019FC]] Large overlap with controls limits diagnostic use. [[Holiga2019]]

Established

Vaccines do not cause autism

In 657,461 Danish children, MMR-vaccinated children had an adjusted autism hazard ratio of 0.93 (95% CI 0.85–1.02) against unvaccinated children. [[Hviid2019]] A meta-analysis of 1,256,407 children found no link between vaccination and autism (OR 0.99). [[Taylor2014Vaccine]] More in Risk factors & myths.

There is no credible counterpoint: the 1998 paper that started the scare was retracted and found to be fraudulent. [[Lancet2010Retraction,Godlee2011]]
