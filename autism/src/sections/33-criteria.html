DSM-5-TR & ICD-11 criteria

Both manuals describe autism with two core domains: social communication and interaction, and restricted, repetitive patterns. Switch criteria on and off to see how the published structure fits together, then switch to ICD-11 to see how it subdivides the same diagnosis.

Points students get asked about

Differential

Social (pragmatic) communication disorder

DSM-5 introduced SCD for persistent difficulty using verbal and nonverbal communication socially, without restricted and repetitive behaviours or interests. If those behaviours were present at any point in development, autism must be considered first. [[Norbury2014,APA2022]] Hypothesis SCD lacks well-validated measures, overlaps with ADHD, conduct disorder and genetic syndromes, and may be better seen as a dimensional profile across conditions. [[Norbury2014,Swineford2014]]

2013 change

Who kept a diagnosis?

Early field-trial reanalysis: only 60.6% of people with a DSM-IV diagnosis met DSM-5 (Asperger's disorder 0.25, PDD-NOS 0.28). [[McPartland2012]] Using parent and clinician data: 91%. [[Huerta2012]] A five-year follow-up meta-analysis put the fall in diagnoses at 20.8%, and fewer than a third (28.8%) of those not meeting DSM-5 qualified for SCD. [[Kulage2020]]

Support levels

"Requiring support" to "very substantial support"

Levels are rated separately for social communication and for restricted, repetitive behaviours: Level 1 "requiring support", Level 2 "requiring substantial support", Level 3 "requiring very substantial support". [[APA2022]] The ESCAP guidance criticises them because DSM-5 does not define what some, substantial or very substantial support means. [[Fuentes2021ESCAP]]

Criterion C

Masking and late diagnosis

Criterion C allows that differences may not be fully apparent until social demands exceed capacities, or may be masked by learned strategies. [[APA2022,Fuentes2021ESCAP]] Camouflaging, described by autistic adults as masking plus compensation, has costs including exhaustion. [[Hull2017]] See Autistic adults.

2022 wording

"All of the following"

DSM-5-TR clarified criterion A to say all three A symptoms are needed. Some had read the 2013 phrase as allowing fewer; the intended rule, all three A and at least two B, did not change. [[APA2022,Fuentes2021ESCAP]]

Identity

When a name disappears

ICD-11, like DSM-5, no longer separates Asperger syndrome. Some people for whom "Asperger" was part of their identity found the change confusing or unwelcome. [[Chahboun2022]] Historians have also documented Hans Asperger's cooperation with Nazi race-hygiene policy. [[Czech2018]] How people describe themselves is their own choice.
