The diagnostic assessment

Who is in the room

Team

A multidisciplinary evaluation

The reference standard is a comprehensive multidisciplinary evaluation that combines semistructured direct observation with a semistructured caregiver interview. [[Hirota2023]] The ESCAP minimum assessment adds clinical history, developmental level and language, a physical and sensory examination (hearing, vision, neurological screen), emotional and behavioural difficulties and socio-environmental factors. [[Fuentes2021ESCAP]]

ADI-R

The caregiver interview

The Autism Diagnostic Interview–Revised is a semistructured interview with a parent or carer, linked to ICD-10 and DSM-IV criteria, usable from a mental age of about 18 months into adulthood. [[Lord1994]]

ADOS-2

The observation

The ADOS is a semistructured, standardised observation of social interaction, communication, play and imaginative use of materials, with four modules of about 30 minutes chosen by expressive language. [[Lord2000]] A Toddler Module covers children under 30 months with a "range of concern" instead of one cutoff, [[Luyster2009]] and calibrated severity scores let severity be compared across modules and over time. [[Gotham2009]]

Medical workup

Tests that can help, and ones that cannot

No autism-specific biomarker exists. [[Hirota2023]] If the examination finds nothing specific, EEG and neuroimaging are usually not needed; epilepsy or unexplained loss of skills warrants more. [[Fuentes2021ESCAP]] Chromosomal microarray finds a cause in 15–20%, against about 3% for a karyotype, [[Miller2010CMA]] and ESCAP estimates genetic causes can now be found in 25–35% of autistic people. [[Fuentes2021ESCAP]] More in Genes & heritability.

Why the score never decides alone

Established

Clinical judgement

No supporting instrument should be used on its own to make the diagnosis; it needs expert clinical judgement using information from all relevant sources. [[Fuentes2021ESCAP]] Across 68 studies, ADI-R and ADOS had the largest evidence base, and together classified 80.8% correctly against the gold standard. [[Falkmer2013]]

Mixed findings

Two tools or one?

In 595 children aged 12–47 months, using both the ADI-R and the ADOS improved specificity over either alone, with sensitivity and specificity both above 80%. [[Kim2012Lord]] In the Cochrane review, one study found adding the ADI-R to the ADOS did not improve accuracy. [[Randall2018]]

Other estimates

Numbers move with the setting

A 2021 meta-analysis gave ADOS-2 sensitivity 0.89–0.92 and specificity 0.81–0.85; ADI-R specificity was lower in clinical than research samples (0.72 vs 0.85). [[Lebersfeld2021]] A 2023 review cites ADOS-2 sensitivity 91% and specificity 76%. [[Hirota2023]]

Stability

Does an early diagnosis hold?

In 1,269 toddlers first evaluated at 12–36 months, overall stability was 0.84, rising from 0.50 at 12–13 months to 0.83 at 16 months; 23.8% not identified at the first visit were identified later. [[Pierce2019]] Screening and review may need repeating. [[Ozonoff2015]]

Screening questionnaires come before all this, and a screen is not a diagnosis: see Screening tools and the DSM-5-TR & ICD-11 criteria. Conditions often found during the assessment are in Co-occurring conditions.
