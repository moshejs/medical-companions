# U · Research update 2026-11-20

Pass date: 2026-11-20. Window: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-20 (PubMed entry dates). Modules served: therapies (CBT and talking therapy), frontier (medicines timeline notes).

## Findings

### therapies · Guided self-help for depression, adapted for autistic adults
- The site's only statement on talking therapy for mood was that, across 48 studies of CBT in autistic people, the self-reported effect on affective disorders was 0.24 and not significant [[Weston2016]].
- ADEPT-2, a two-arm multi-centre phase 3 RCT (ISRCTN17547011), randomised 266 adults with a confirmed autism diagnosis and depressive symptoms (PHQ-9 ≥ 10), recruited from community settings, to guided self-help adapted for autism (n = 132) or treatment as usual (n = 134). The intervention was psychoeducation and behavioural activation supported by nine meetings with a coach. [[Russell2026]]
- The between-group difference in BDI-II at 16 weeks, analysed by intention to treat, was −5.05 (95% CI −7.85 to −2.24; P < 0.001; d = 0.47) in favour of guided self-help, "with the difference sustained at 52-weeks". [[Russell2026]]
- Limits: one trial, comparator was treatment as usual, and the primary outcome is self-rated. (Early evidence)
- The abstract's completion figures are internally inconsistent (110 of 132 given as 81%) and are not quoted.

### frontier · Cannabidiol for severe behaviour problems
- A double-blind RCT at 3 Australian children's hospitals randomised 131 participants aged 6–18 with intellectual disability and severe behavioural problems to CBD isolate in oil 10 mg/kg/d (n = 65) or placebo (n = 66) for 8 weeks. 110 participants (84.0%) were autistic. [[Efron2026]]
- Mean ABC-Irritability at the end of treatment was 21.7 (SD 11.0) with CBD and 19.2 (SD 10.7) with placebo: mean difference 2.4 (95% CI −1.5 to 6.3). At least one adverse event occurred in 63.1% and 60.6%. [[Efron2026]]
- The authors conclude that CBD "should not be used to treat severe behavioral problems in this population". The population was defined by intellectual disability, not autism, so the site's cannabinoid row stays "mixed" and the trial is reported in the 2024–2026 note only. (Early evidence; negative)

### Exam hooks
- ADEPT-2: adapted guided self-help vs usual care in 266 autistic adults, BDI-II difference −5.05 at 16 weeks (d 0.47). [[Russell2026]]
- CBD vs placebo in 131 youths with intellectual disability (84% autistic): no difference on ABC-Irritability. [[Efron2026]]

### In plain words
- A self-help programme for low mood, changed to suit autistic adults and supported by a coach, worked better than usual care in a large study.
- In a careful study, CBD oil did not help severe behaviour problems in children with intellectual disability, most of whom were autistic.
