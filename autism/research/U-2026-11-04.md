## Findings

### frontier · Syndrome-specific therapies (Angelman syndrome)
- The GTX-102 phase 3 trial (Aspire, 129 children with Angelman syndrome) is terminated; the registry gives the reason as "Lack of efficacy" (primary completion 2026-07-10, study completion 2026-10-30, record updated 2026-11-03) [[NCT06617429]].
- The other two phase 3 antisense trials continue: obudanersen (REVEAL, 156 enrolled, active, not recruiting) and rugonersen (BEACON, recruiting) [[NCT06914609,NCT07605429]].
- This corrects the site's "three phase 3 trials are registered" to say that one stopped. The broader statement, that any such approach for autism in general is a hypothesis, is unchanged (Hypothesis) [[Hipp2025]].

### Exam hooks
- Angelman ASOs: GTX-102 phase 3 (Aspire) stopped for lack of efficacy in 2026; obudanersen and rugonersen phase 3 trials continue [[NCT06617429,NCT06914609,NCT07605429]].

### In plain words
- One of three big trials of a gene-targeted medicine for Angelman syndrome was stopped in 2026 because it did not work well enough. Two others are still going [[NCT06617429]].
