16p11.2 deletion and duplicationCopy-number changes on chromosome 16 that together account for about 1% of autism 623.
22q11.2 deletion syndromeA deletion syndrome in which about 11% of people show autism features 480.
AAC (augmentative and alternative communication)Pictures, symbol systems or speech-generating devices that add to or replace speech; they do not impede speech 163,525.
ABA (applied behaviour analysis)Behavioural approaches built on learning principles; early intensive forms (EIBI) run 20–40 hours a week. Evidence and ethics are debated 293,478.
Ableist languageLanguage that "assumes disabled people are inferior to nondisabled people" 52.
ABC-IrritabilityThe Aberrant Behavior Checklist irritability subscale, the main outcome in risperidone and aripiprazole trials 367,439.
ADDM NetworkCDC's Autism and Developmental Disabilities Monitoring Network, reviewing records of 8-year-olds at selected US sites; not nationally representative 35.
ADHDAttention deficit hyperactivity disorder, the most common co-occurring condition (pooled 28%) 309.
ADI-RAutism Diagnostic Interview–Revised, a semistructured caregiver interview 333.
ADOSAutism Diagnostic Observation Schedule: a standardised observation in four modules chosen by language level 334; a Toddler Module covers under 30 months 344.
ADOS calibrated severity scoreA score that standardises ADOS totals across modules and ages, so severity can be compared over time 178.
Adverse events reportingOnly 11 of 150 early-intervention reports mentioned adverse events 53.
AlexithymiaDifficulty identifying and describing one's own emotions; about half of autistic people (49.93%) vs 4.89% of controls 291.
Angelman syndromeA single-gene condition (loss of maternal UBE3A) in which many children meet autism criteria; antisense oligonucleotide trials are under way 220.
Antisense oligonucleotide (ASO)A short synthetic strand that alters gene expression; in Angelman syndrome it aims to switch on the silent paternal UBE3A 220.
AripiprazoleAn antipsychotic FDA-labelled for irritability associated with autistic disorder, ages 6–17 134.
ARFIDAvoidant/restrictive food intake disorder; 11.41% of autistic groups in a meta-analysis, with a very wide interval 502.
Asperger syndromeA category introduced to English readers by Wing in 1981 638, added to DSM-IV in 1994 and merged into autism spectrum disorder in DSM-5 36.
AQ (Autism-Spectrum Quotient)A 50-item self-report trait scale 39. In referred adults it did not predict diagnosis 30; not for self-diagnosis.
AQ-10A 10-item short form; case-control accuracy at a cut-point of 6 overstates real-clinic accuracy 10,30.
Autism alert cardA card explaining a person's autism to others; about half of 272 users used it, mostly on transport and in healthcare 124.
Autism spectrum disorderThe single DSM-5 diagnosis that replaced autistic disorder, Asperger's disorder and PDD-NOS in 2013 22,36.
Autistic burnoutChronic exhaustion, loss of skills and reduced tolerance to stimuli from cumulative load 474.
BalovaptanA vasopressin V1a antagonist; negative phase 3 trials in adults and children 229,255.
BumetanideAn NKCC1 chloride-importer blocker; phase 3 SIGN trials stopped for lack of efficacy 157.
BullyingPooled victimisation of 44% in autistic youth 352.
Camouflaging (masking)Masking and compensating for autistic traits to fit in; exhausting and linked to poorer mental health 91,241.
Canvas DxAn FDA-authorised AI diagnosis aid for clinicians; it gave a determinate result in 31.8% of children tested 135,374.
CARSChildhood Autism Rating Scale; Cochrane sensitivity 0.80 and specificity 0.88 470.
CAT-QCamouflaging Autistic Traits Questionnaire: 25 items, three factors; a research measure 242.
CatatoniaA movement and behaviour syndrome affecting about 10.4% of autistic people, usually from adolescence 604.
Cerebral folate deficiencyLow folate in the brain; a 2021 meta-analysis estimated 38% prevalence in autism (Early evidence) 494.
ChelationDrugs that bind metals; no trial evidence of benefit in autism and deaths from hypocalcaemia 59,258.
Chromosomal microarray (CMA)A first-tier test for copy-number changes; yield 15–20% in unexplained DD, ID or autism 379.
Co-occurringSuggested in place of "comorbid": conditions that occur alongside autism 52. More than 70% have at least one 306.
Complementary and alternative medicine (CAM)Non-mainstream treatments; used by a median 54% of autistic people across studies 227.
Conflict of interestAbout 70% of early-intervention reports had one, but only about 6% fully disclosed it 54.
Copy-number variant (CNV)A deleted or duplicated stretch of DNA; six de novo CNV loci were confirmed in the Simons Simplex Collection 511.
Cord blood infusionAn unproven treatment; a 180-child RCT found no benefit on socialisation 114.
Criterion A (DSM-5-TR)Social communication and interaction: all three of A1–A3 required 23.
Criterion B (DSM-5-TR)Restricted, repetitive patterns: at least two of B1–B4 required 23.
DALYDisability-adjusted life-year, a measure of health loss; it does not judge the worth of autistic lives 518.
De novo mutationA new variant not inherited from either parent; explains 2.6% of population variance 167.
Dementia in autistic adultsMore frequent than in the general population in US claims data (aHR 1.96 for autism only) 613.
Developmental surveillanceOngoing monitoring of development at every health visit, with screening at set ages 329.
Diagnostic overshadowingA new symptom being put down to autism itself and not assessed (Hypothesis) 425.
Double empathy problemMilton's account of social difficulty as a two-way mismatch 382.
DSM-5-TRThe 2022 text revision of DSM-5 23.
Dunn's modelFour sensory patterns from threshold × strategy: low registration, seeking, sensitivity, avoiding 122.
EarliPointAn FDA-cleared eye-tracking diagnosis aid; sensitivity 71.0% and specificity 80.7% vs expert diagnosis 139,263.
EHCPEducation, Health and Care Plan, the legal framework for extra school support in England 282.
EIBIEarly intensive behavioural intervention: ABA-based, 20–40 hours a week for years; evidence low or very low certainty 478.
Elopement (wandering)Leaving a safe place; 49% of autistic children tried at least once after age 4 16.
EpilepsySeizure disorder; median 12.1% in autism 341, 21.5% with intellectual disability 14.
Equality Act 2010In Great Britain, autism may count as a disability and bring an entitlement to reasonable adjustments 553.
ESDMEarly Start Denver Model, an NDBI; in a 2010 RCT IQ rose 17.6 vs 7.0 points 111, with a partial replication 487.
Excitation/inhibition (E/I) balanceThe hypothesis that excitation is raised relative to inhibition 497; the shift may be compensatory 21.
Exome sequencingSequencing all protein-coding genes; yield 36% in neurodevelopmental disorders 559.
Facilitated communication (FC)Physical support while a person types; messages are authored by the facilitator 526.
Female protective effectThe idea that girls need a higher genetic load to be autistic; siblings of high-trait girls had more traits (Early evidence) 20,484.
FLACCA pain scale: Face, Legs, Activity, Cry, Consolability 377.
Folic acidAlready advised around conception; some studies link it with a lower chance of autism 326,573.
Fragile X syndromeMore than 200 CGG repeats silence FMR1 196; about half of males meet autism criteria 277.
Functional analysisTesting which environmental events maintain a behaviour by varying conditions 202,254.
Functioning labels"High-" and "low-functioning"; IQ-based labels can hide real needs 12.
GBD (Global Burden of Disease)The study estimating 61.8 million autistic people worldwide in 2021 518.
GFCF dietGluten-free, casein-free diet; "little evidence" of benefit in 6 RCTs 462.
GI symptomsGastrointestinal symptoms are more common in autistic children (OR 4.42; constipation OR 3.86) 369.
Grandfather clauseDSM-5 says a well-established DSM-IV diagnosis should become autism spectrum disorder 156.
Guanfacine extended releaseAn ADHD medicine; 50% vs 9.4% responders for hyperactivity in autistic children 521.
HeritabilityThe share of variation in liability due to genetic differences; about 80% for autism 34.
Hospital passportA document recording a person's communication, sensory and care needs; formats vary widely 217,429.
HyperacusisDecreased sound tolerance; about 41% of autistic people currently 634.
Hyperbaric oxygenPressurised oxygen; one RCT reported gains, a controlled study found no consistent effects 259,492.
Hypo-priorsPellicano and Burr's account: perception less shaped by prior expectations 449.
iBASIS-VIPPA video-feedback parenting programme for infants showing early signs 184,628.

ICD-11 6A02The ICD-11 code for autism spectrum disorder, subdivided by intellectual development and functional language 156.
IDEAThe US Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, providing special education to eligible students up to age 21 372.
Identity-first language"Autistic person"; preferred by many autistic adults 278,575.
IEPIndividualised Education Program; school-based IEP services varied widely across ADDM sites 240.
InclusionThe school adapting to the student, as opposed to "integration", where the student adapts 452.
Infant-sibling studyA prospective study of younger siblings of autistic children, followed from infancy 214,441.
Intellectual disabilityRecorded in 39.6% of autistic 8-year-olds with IQ data in ADDM 2022 541.
InteroceptionSensing internal body signals; a meta-analysis found no clear difference in adult cardiac interoceptive accuracy 294.
Interpregnancy intervalTime between pregnancies; under 12 months was associated with autism (OR 1.90) 89.
JASPERA joint-attention and symbolic-play NDBI; targeted skills improved 274,275.
Judge Rotenberg CenterThe US school whose use of skin-shock devices was the main target of campaigns against painful aversives 293.
Landau–Kleffner syndromeAn acquired epileptic aphasia: sudden language loss with EEG abnormalities after typical development 156.
Lancet Commission (2021)A 32-author commission calling for personalised, stepped care; it proposed "profound autism" 338.
Late diagnosisDiagnosis in adolescence or adulthood; in England 59–72% of autistic people may be undiagnosed 432.
Leucovorin (folinic acid)Its 2026 US label indication is FOLR1 cerebral folate transport deficiency, not autism 142.
Loss of autism diagnosisProposed replacement for "optimal outcome"; 9% by age 19 in one cohort 17,126.

Maternal immune activationInfection or inflammation in pregnancy; in humans only modestly linked to autism (OR 1.13) 260.

Autism Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on autism, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses, and for autistic people, families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose anyone, and the questionnaires and criteria it explains are teaching tools, not tests to use on yourself or your child. Nothing here should change a support plan or a medicine without your own clinician. Criteria, doses and guideline statements are quoted as published.
