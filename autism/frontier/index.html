Research toward better support

Sixteen years of trials, devices and community voices on one timeline. Many medicines that looked promising in small studies failed in large ones; some supports and new diagnostic aids held up. Every item carries its evidence label.

What people want studied, and what trials measured

UK community consultation found a "clear disparity" between how autism research was funded and what the community prioritised: people wanted research that makes a difference to day-to-day lives. 450 A review of 7 priority-setting studies found the most common priorities were real-world impact on daily life and lifelong skill-building. 485 Pick a priority to see which medicine-trial outcomes connect to it.

Reading the research

Established

Why so many drug trials fail

Placebo responses are large: across 86 trials, 19% of participants on placebo were rated "much improved", more with caregiver ratings, flexible dosing and big multisite trials. 546 A 2026 industry–academic review recommends to "measure what matters to patients and caregivers", enrich trials by mechanism, avoid diagnostic tools as efficacy endpoints and design trials that reduce placebo response. 445

Early evidence

Leucovorin: a live controversy

A 12-week RCT of 48 children with language impairment found better verbal communication (d=0.70). 154 The 2026 US label indication is cerebral folate transport deficiency with a confirmed FOLR1 variant, not autism, and rests on 46 patients in case reports. 142 After public promotion in September 2025, prescribing to US children with autism rose more than 24-fold, from 34.1 to 835.4 per 100,000 encounters a month. 495 No large independent trial exists yet. 494

Hypothesis

Syndrome-specific therapies

Angelman syndrome is caused by loss of maternal UBE3A, and many affected children meet autism criteria. Antisense oligonucleotides aim to switch on the silent paternal copy. A phase 1 trial (61 children) met its safety endpoint, and three phase 3 trials are registered. Whether anything similar could apply to autism in general is a hypothesis. 220,415,416,417

Ethics

Consent, genetics and trust

Spectrum 10K, a UK genetic study planning to recruit 10,000 autistic people, was postponed in 2021 after backlash from the autism community. An interview study of 22 autistic adults recorded concerns about trust, data reuse and autistic representation. 127 Autistic stakeholders want priorities and trust built in from the start. 450,485

Practical

How to find a trial

Every trial in the timeline has a public registry record on ClinicalTrials.gov, with its status, size and any posted results; for example SOARS-B is NCT01944046. 401 Registry records show what a study planned to measure, and whether results were ever posted: a European arbaclofen trial completed in January 2023 with no results posted. 409 A 2025 INSAR group of autistic people, clinicians and researchers recommended that autism journals require trial registration, published protocols and analysis plans. 312

Diagnostics

Aids, not stand-alone tests

Canvas Dx gave a result in only 31.8% of children tested; when it did, sensitivity was 98.4% and specificity 78.9%. 374 EarliPoint eye-tracking had 71.0% sensitivity and 80.7% specificity in all 475 children. 263 Both are cleared as aids for clinicians. No device or questionnaire here is for self-diagnosis: see The diagnostic assessment.

Autism Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on autism, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses, and for autistic people, families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose anyone, and the questionnaires and criteria it explains are teaching tools, not tests to use on yourself or your child. Nothing here should change a support plan or a medicine without your own clinician. Criteria, doses and guideline statements are quoted as published.
