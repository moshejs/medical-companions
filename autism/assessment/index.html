Station 1 · Primary care

Surveillance & screening

Development is watched at every routine visit, and the AAP recommends an autism-specific screen at 18 and 24 months on top of that surveillance. 250 A screen only raises the possibility of autism and cannot confirm it; a negative screen does not rule autism out when other information points to developmental differences. 156 In a UK survey of 1,047 parents, families typically waited about a year after first concerns before seeking help. 98

In plain wordsA questionnaire at a check-up is a first look, not an answer. A parent's worry is a good reason to ask for a closer look.
Exam hookAAP: autism-specific screening at 18 and 24 months, plus developmental surveillance at every visit. 250

Who is in the room

Team

A multidisciplinary evaluation

The reference standard is a comprehensive multidisciplinary evaluation that combines semistructured direct observation with a semistructured caregiver interview. 222 The ESCAP minimum assessment adds clinical history, developmental level and language, a physical and sensory examination (hearing, vision, neurological screen), emotional and behavioural difficulties and socio-environmental factors. 156

ADI-R

The caregiver interview

The Autism Diagnostic Interview–Revised is a semistructured interview with a parent or carer, linked to ICD-10 and DSM-IV criteria, usable from a mental age of about 18 months into adulthood. 333

ADOS-2

The observation

The ADOS is a semistructured, standardised observation of social interaction, communication, play and imaginative use of materials, with four modules of about 30 minutes chosen by expressive language. 334 A Toddler Module covers children under 30 months with a "range of concern" instead of one cutoff, 344 and calibrated severity scores let severity be compared across modules and over time. 178

Medical workup

Tests that can help, and ones that cannot

No autism-specific biomarker exists. 222 If the examination finds nothing specific, EEG and neuroimaging are usually not needed; epilepsy or unexplained loss of skills warrants more. 156 Chromosomal microarray finds a cause in 15–20%, against about 3% for a karyotype, 379 and ESCAP estimates genetic causes can now be found in 25–35% of autistic people. 156 More in Genes & heritability.

With sensitivity 0.94 and specificity 0.80, ADOS picks up 70 of 74 autistic people and wrongly flags 5 of 26 who are not. Lower the share of autistic people being assessed and false positives make up more of the flags: the review warns that false positives would be more common in lower-prevalence settings. 470

What overlaps

Late or limited speech. No single words by 16 months and no two-word phrases by 24 months are red flags for autism, but they predict more when combined with social differences. 25

Points towards autism

Autism

Social-communication differences together with restricted, repetitive behaviours, now or in the past. 23,428 Requiring both parent report and clinician observation improved specificity against non-autism diagnoses such as language disorder. 238

Why the score never decides alone

Established

Clinical judgement

No supporting instrument should be used on its own to make the diagnosis; it needs expert clinical judgement using information from all relevant sources. 156 Across 68 studies, ADI-R and ADOS had the largest evidence base, and together classified 80.8% correctly against the gold standard. 133

Mixed findings

Two tools or one?

In 595 children aged 12–47 months, using both the ADI-R and the ADOS improved specificity over either alone, with sensitivity and specificity both above 80%. 284 In the Cochrane review, one study found adding the ADI-R to the ADOS did not improve accuracy. 470

Other estimates

Numbers move with the setting

A 2021 meta-analysis gave ADOS-2 sensitivity 0.89–0.92 and specificity 0.81–0.85; ADI-R specificity was lower in clinical than research samples (0.72 vs 0.85). 322 A 2023 review cites ADOS-2 sensitivity 91% and specificity 76%. 222

Stability

Does an early diagnosis hold?

In 1,269 toddlers first evaluated at 12–36 months, overall stability was 0.84, rising from 0.50 at 12–13 months to 0.83 at 16 months; 23.8% not identified at the first visit were identified later. 460 Screening and review may need repeating. 442
