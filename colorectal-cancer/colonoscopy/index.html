Quality: adenoma detection rate and withdrawal time

The adenoma detection rate (ADR) is the share of an endoscopist's screening colonoscopies that find at least one adenoma. It is the best-studied marker of a careful examination. In Kaiser Permanente (314,872 colonoscopies), physician ADRs ranged from 7.4% to 52.5%, and each 1% rise in ADR was linked to a 3% lower risk of interval cancer (HR 0.97). 109 Move the sliders to compare an endoscopist with the published targets.

2024 targets

ACG/ASGE quality indicators

Screening ADR ≥35% overall (≥40% in men, ≥30% in women), raised from 25% in 2015; ADR ≥50% after a positive stool test; sessile serrated lesion detection ≥6%; adenomas per colonoscopy ≥0.6; caecal intubation ≥95% with photo documentation; adequate prep ≥90%; average withdrawal ≥8 minutes in normal colonoscopies; cold snare for 4–9 mm lesions ≥90%. 451

Europe

ESGE 2017 minimum standards

Adequate bowel prep 90%, caecal intubation 90%, ADR 25%, appropriate polypectomy technique 80%. In Poland's programme, endoscopists with an ADR below 20% had interval-cancer hazard ratios of 10.75–12.50. 275,276

Improvement works

Raising ADR lowers risk

Endoscopists who raised their ADR had fewer interval cancers (HR 0.63) and interval-cancer deaths (HR 0.50). Reaching or staying in the top quintile gave HRs of 0.27 and 0.18. 277

Misses

No colonoscopy sees everything

In tandem studies, miss rates were 26% for adenomas, 9% for advanced adenomas and 27% for serrated polyps. Cancers within 5 years of a colonoscopy were more often CIMP-high and MSI-high, suggesting fast-growing pathways. Early evidence 404,600

After a positive FIT: does waiting matter?

In 70,124 people with a positive FIT at Kaiser Permanente, colonoscopy within 2–9 months carried no excess risk compared with 8–30 days. Beyond that, the bars climb. Press Play to grow them. 110

Why it works

The National Polyp Study

After polypectomy, CRC incidence was 76–90% lower than in three reference groups. With a median 15.8 years of follow-up, there were 12 CRC deaths against 25.4 expected: a 53% reduction. 583,598

Comfort

CO2 instead of air

Using carbon dioxide rather than air to inflate the bowel cut moderate or severe pain after the procedure from 16.7% to 4.2%. 76

Definitions

Post-colonoscopy cancer

The World Endoscopy Organization standardised "post-colonoscopy colorectal cancer" (PCCRC). In England the 3-year PCCRC rate fell from 10.6% for colonoscopies done in 2001 to 7.3% in 2007, depending heavily on the method used. 379,464
