Part 4 · Treatment

Rectal cancer pathway

Rectal cancer is treated differently from colon cancer: it sits low in the pelvis, close to the sphincters, and an MRI scan plans everything. Follow one schematic course month by month, from staging through radiotherapy and chemotherapy to surgery, or to watch-and-wait when the tumour disappears.

Month 0 · staging

MRI plans everything

High-resolution pelvic MRI shows how deep the tumour goes, which nodes look involved and how close it comes to the mesorectal fascia, the future circumferential margin. In MERCURY (408 people), MRI predicted a clear margin with 92% specificity. The 2022 NCCN rectal update rebuilt its stage II–III algorithms around total neoadjuvant therapy and added watch-and-wait for complete responders. 55,360

In plain wordsThe MRI scan is the map. It tells the team whether surgery alone is enough or whether treatment should come first.
Exam hookMRI-predicted CRM decides whether a tumour is "high risk" before treatment. 360

Watch-and-wait: what happens next

Year 5.0: about 28.5% regrown. The curve is drawn through the published 2-year point and flattens because 88% of regrowths came in the first 2 years; its exact shape is not published here. Registry data. 555

IWWD · van der Valk 2018

About 1 in 4 regrow, mostly early

Among 880 people with a clinical complete response managed without surgery (47 institutes, 15 countries, median follow-up 3.3 years), the 2-year cumulative local regrowth was 25.2% (95% CI 22.2–28.5). 88% of regrowths were found in the first 2 years and 97% were in the bowel wall. Distant metastases occurred in 8%; 5-year overall survival was 85% and disease-specific survival 94%. 555

In plain wordsMost regrowths show up in the first 2 years, which is why checks are most frequent then.
Exam hookIWWD: 25.2% local regrowth at 2 years; 88% of regrowths within 2 years. 555

dMMR rectal cancer: immunotherapy instead of everything else?

A small group of rectal cancers are mismatch-repair deficient (dMMR; see Tumour biomarkers). In a Memorial Sloan Kettering trial, people with stage II–III dMMR rectal cancer were given the PD-1 antibody dostarlimab, every 3 weeks, as their only first treatment. Early evidence 91,92 The global AZUR-1 trial (154 people) has since reported similar results, and in October 2026 the US label added dostarlimab alone for stage II/III dMMR or MSI-H locally advanced rectal cancer under accelerated approval. 93,164

Cercek 2025 · NEJM

49 of 49 who completed treatment

Early evidence In the update (dostarlimab 500 mg IV every 3 weeks for 9 cycles), the rectal cohort had 50 people. All 49 who completed treatment had a clinical complete response and chose nonoperative management; 37 had a sustained response of at least 12 months at data cut. 2-year recurrence-free survival was 96% (median follow-up 30.2 months). The global AZUR-1 trial (154 people, interim analysis) found a clinical complete response sustained for 12 months in 68% of 144 people followed for at least 12 months. Regulatory status: since October 2026 the JEMPERLI US label includes dostarlimab alone for stage II/III MSI-H or dMMR locally advanced rectal cancer (500 mg every 3 weeks for 9 cycles), "approved under accelerated approval based on sustained clinical complete response for 12 months"; "the long-term risk of relapse is unknown". 92,93,164

In plain wordsIn the US this is now an approved option, on an accelerated basis: how long the responses last over many years is not yet known. It applies only to the small dMMR group.

Where the evidence disagrees

Controversy

Which TNT sequence?

RAPIDO lowered disease-related treatment failure but raised 5-year locoregional recurrence after resection (10% vs 6%), more often with a breached mesorectum. PRODIGE 23 improved DFS with FOLFIRINOX before chemoradiation. Which sequence is best remains unresolved. 33,108,131

Established

Skipping radiotherapy

PROSPECT supports FOLFOX with selective chemoradiation for T2N+ and T3 tumours suitable for sphincter-sparing surgery: only 9.1% needed chemoradiation before surgery, and 6.5% missed the 20% shrinkage threshold. It does not apply to high-risk tumours such as those threatening the margin, the population of RAPIDO. 33,479

Controversy

Is watch-and-wait safe?

About a quarter regrow by 2 years, yet survival in registries is high. A pooled analysis of CAO/ARO/AIO-12 (mandatory TME) and OPRA (selective watch-and-wait) found no difference in survival, but it is not randomised. Watch-and-wait is offered in expert centres with intensive surveillance. Early evidence 555,580

Early evidence

Dostarlimab and durability

Every patient who completed treatment had a clinical complete response, and 2-year recurrence-free survival was 96%. In the global AZUR-1 trial (interim analysis), 68% of 144 people followed for at least 12 months had a clinical complete response sustained for 12 months, with no recurrences reported in those with a complete response. The US label added this use in October 2026 under accelerated approval and states: The long-term risk of relapse is unknown. 92,93,164

Recurrence and survival numbers on this page are population statistics from trials and registries. They cannot predict what will happen to one person.
