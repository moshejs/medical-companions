Colorectal Cancer Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on colorectal (bowel) cancer, made for health-science and nursing courses and for patient understanding.

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one disease each. Also there: FMF Companion, another guide with an inherited-disease genetics module, and Gout Companion.

Another project by the same author: Bugs & Cells, a microbiology and pathology study guide for students, with its card on Streptococcus gallolyticus (bovis).

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
