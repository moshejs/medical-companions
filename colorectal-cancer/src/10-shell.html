Colorectal Cancer Companion · animated and cited

Colorectal cancer, explained

Colorectal cancer is among the most common cancers worldwide, and most begin as a small polyp that grows over years. Here it is in moving pictures: watch a polyp become a cancer, compare screening tests, try the TNM staging calculator, and see how each treatment works.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Schematic, not to scale; the years are illustrative.

For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. Finish with flashcards, mnemonics and a quiz.

For people facing screening or a diagnosis

Look for the In plain words boxes. Bring questions to your own doctor or nurse: the app reports what guidelines and trials say, not what you should do. Survival figures describe large groups, not any one person.

How sure is each claim?

Research claims carry a label: Established Early evidence Hypothesis. Small numbers link to the Sources list.
