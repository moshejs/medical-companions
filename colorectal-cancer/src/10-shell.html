Colorectal Cancer Companion · animated and cited

Colorectal cancer, explained

Colorectal cancer is the third most common cancer worldwide and the second leading cause of cancer death, with about two million new diagnoses a year.[[Bray2024,Sung2026]] Most begin as a polyp that changes slowly over years, which is why screening can prevent it.[[Fearon1990,Jones2008]] Here it is in moving pictures: watch a polyp become a cancer, compare the screening tests, try the TNM staging calculator, and see where each treatment fits.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Schematic, not to scale; the years are illustrative. In one sequencing study, a large adenoma took about 17 years to become an advanced cancer.[[Jones2008]]

For people facing screening or a diagnosis

Look for the In plain words boxes. Bring questions to your own doctor or nurse: the app reports what guidelines and trials say, not what you should do. Survival figures describe large groups, not any one person.
