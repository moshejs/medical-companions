Toward a cure

Where research is pushing next: a blood test for tumour DNA that decides who needs chemotherapy, immunotherapy before surgery, vaccines, new RAS drugs, exercise, aspirin and smarter screening. Each route carries an evidence label.

Routes toward a cure, with evidence labels

Show
Established

ctDNA-guided adjuvant therapy, stage II

DYNAMIC: chemotherapy only for ctDNA-positive patients cut chemotherapy use to 15% vs 28%, with non-inferior 2-year recurrence-free survival (93.5% vs 92.4%). [[Tie2022]]

One RCT, in stage II colon cancer

Established

ctDNA as a recurrence warning

In GALAXY, ctDNA positivity 4 weeks after surgery carried an HR of 10.0 for recurrence; in the 2240-patient update, HR 11.99 for DFS and 9.68 for OS. Sustained clearance on chemotherapy gave 24-month DFS of 89.0%, transient clearance 3.3%. True spontaneous clearance was rare (1.9%). [[Kotani2023,Nakamura2024]]

Prognostic, stage II–IV resectable CRC

Early evidence

ctDNA de-escalation, stage III

In DYNAMIC-III ctDNA-negative patients, de-escalation cut oxaliplatin use (34.8% vs 88.6%) and hospitalisation (8.5% vs 13.2%), but RFS was slightly lower (85.3% vs 88.1%) and missed the non-inferiority margin. Not standard. [[Tie2025]]

Phase 2/3 randomised

Early evidence

Treating ctDNA-positive disease harder

Escalated chemotherapy in DYNAMIC-III did not improve 2-year RFS (51% vs 61%). In ALTAIR, 6 months of trifluridine/tipiracil for ctDNA-positive patients without visible disease did not significantly improve DFS (HR 0.79, 0.60–1.05), with grade ≥3 haematological events of 73.0% vs 3.3%. CIRCULATE-US is recruiting (estimated n=1912). [[Tie2025,Bando2026,NCT05174169]]

New strategies needed

Early evidence

Immunotherapy before surgery, dMMR colon cancer

NICHE-2: neoadjuvant nivolumab plus ipilimumab gave a major pathological response in 95% and a complete response in 68% of 111 patients. 98% had timely surgery, 4% grade 3–4 immune-related events, and no recurrence at 26 months' median follow-up. [[Chalabi2024]]

Single-arm

Early evidence

Organ preservation in dMMR rectal cancer

Dostarlimab gave a clinical complete response in all of the first 12 patients, and in all 49 who completed treatment in the expanded rectal cohort, without chemoradiotherapy or surgery; 2-year RFS across 117 early-stage dMMR tumours was 92%. For mostly pMMR rectal cancer, OPRA's 3-year organ preservation was 53% vs 41%. [[Cercek2022,Cercek2025,GarciaAguilar2022]]

Single-arm; OPRA randomised phase 2

Early evidence

A KRAS vaccine (ELI-002)

AMPLIFY-201 (25 patients, 5 with colorectal cancer, all with residual mutant-KRAS disease): the lymph-node-targeted vaccine induced mKRAS-specific T cells in 84%. Strong T-cell responders had median RFS not reached vs 3.02 months (HR 0.12) in exploratory, non-randomised comparisons. [[Pant2024,Wainberg2025]]

Phase 1

Hypothesis

KRAS inhibitors beyond G12C

MRTX1133, a non-covalent KRAS G12D inhibitor, caused regression in preclinical models, enhanced by co-targeting EGFR or PI3Kα. RMC-7977, a RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor, worked across RAS genotypes in preclinical models; the related daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) is in a recruiting phase 1/2 trial (Early evidence). [[Hallin2022,Holderfield2024,NCT05379985]]

Preclinical; first-in-human trial

Established

Exercise after chemotherapy

CHALLENGE (889 patients after adjuvant chemotherapy for colon cancer): a 3-year structured exercise programme improved DFS (HR 0.72); 5-year DFS 80.3% vs 73.9% and 8-year OS 90.3% vs 83.2%. Musculoskeletal adverse events 18.5% vs 11.5%. [[Courneya2025]]

Phase 3 RCT

Established

Aspirin for everyone after CRC: no

ASCOLT (1550 patients): aspirin 200 mg daily for 3 years after standard adjuvant therapy did not improve 5-year DFS (77.0% vs 74.8%; HR 0.91). An established null result. [[Chia2025]]

Phase 3 RCT

Early evidence

Aspirin for PI3K-altered tumours

ALASCCA: low-dose aspirin cut 3-year recurrence to 7.7% vs 14.1% in PIK3CA exon 9/20 hotspot tumours (HR 0.49), and 7.7% vs 16.8% with other PI3K-pathway alterations. Severe adverse events 16.8% vs 11.6%. Add-Aspirin has no colorectal efficacy results yet. [[Martling2025,NCT02804815]]

Single positive RCT

Established

Blood-based screening tests

ECLIPSE (7861 evaluable average-risk adults): a cfDNA blood test detected 83.1% of colorectal cancers and 87.5% of stage I–III cancers, but only 13.2% of advanced precancerous lesions. Specificity for advanced neoplasia was 89.6%. [[Chung2024]]

Established performance; see the screening module

Early evidence

AI-assisted colonoscopy (CADe)

Across 44 RCTs, computer-aided detection raised the adenoma detection rate from 36.7% to 44.7%, but advanced neoplasia per colonoscopy was unchanged and it removed almost 2 extra non-neoplastic polyps per 10 colonoscopies. Whether it lowers cancer deaths is not yet shown. [[Soleymanjahi2024,Hassan2021]]

Detection established; effect on deaths unknown

Hypothesis

Why cancer is rising under 50

Incidence under 55 has risen 1–2% a year since the mid-1990s. Colibactin mutational signatures from pks+ E. coli were 3.3 times more common in people diagnosed before 40 than after 70, acquired early; the authors suggest early-life exposure "may contribute". [[Siegel2023CRC,PleguezuelosManzano2020,DiazGay2025]]

Mechanism shown in organoids; cause unproven
