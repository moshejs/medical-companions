Bowel function after rectal surgery: the LARS score

Low anterior resection syndrome (LARS) is the cluster of urgency, frequency, clustering and leakage that many people notice after part of the rectum is removed. The LARS score was built from questionnaires returned by 961 Danish patients and has five items, scored 0–42. It was validated in 801 patients in four countries. A meta-analysis put major LARS at 41% (95% CI 34–48%) of patients at least a year after surgery or stoma closure, with radiotherapy and a low join as the most consistent risk factors. [[Emmertsen2012,Juul2014,Croese2018]]

Living with a stoma

A stoma brings bowel out through the skin of the abdomen, where a pouch collects its output. An ileostomy uses the end of the small bowel (ileum); a colostomy uses the colon. After a low rectal join, surgeons often make a temporary defunctioning stoma to limit the harm if the join leaks: in the Dutch TME trial, 57% of patients who had low anterior resection got one. [[denDulk2007]]

Parastomal hernia

A bulge beside the stoma

In the older literature, rates were 1.8–28.3% after end ileostomy and 4.0–48.1% after end colostomy, depending on stoma type and follow-up. The European Hernia Society states end colostomy carries the higher risk and strongly recommends a prophylactic synthetic non-absorbable mesh when an end colostomy is made, with no such recommendation for other stomas. In a small trial, 17 of 21 people without mesh had a hernia at 5 years vs 2 of 15 with mesh. [[Carne2003,Antoniou2018,Janes2009]]

High output

Ileostomy and dehydration

High-output stoma is usually taken as more than about 1.5–2.0 L in 24 hours, draining water, sodium and often magnesium; it occurs in up to 31% of small-bowel stomas. About 1 in 5 people are readmitted within 30 days of ileostomy creation, 6% for dehydration. Reported consequences include delayed or abandoned chemotherapy and kidney injury. [[Nightingale2022,Vogel2022,Borucki2021]]

Reversal

Will it be closed?

In the TME trial, 19% of stomas were never reversed; complications after surgery predicted a permanent stoma. In a Swedish series, 24% had a permanent stoma at the end of follow-up, and 9% of reversals had a major complication. In Swedish registry data, 13.6–15.2% still had a stoma 2 years after anterior resection. [[denDulk2007,Holmgren2017,Back2021]]

Numb fingers: oxaliplatin neuropathy over time

Oxaliplatin causes two kinds of nerve symptoms. The acute form starts within hours to 2 days of a dose, is triggered or worsened by cold, and resolves within 14 days, often returning with the next dose. The delayed form persists for more than 14 days and can impair position sense and daily activities. Drag along the months after the last adjuvant cycle. [[FDA-OxaliplatinGland]]

The rest of life

Fear of recurrence

The most common need

Across 54 studies of supportive care needs, emotional support for fear of recurrence was the most prominent need at every stage, with diet information, help with fatigue and a trusted professional contact close behind. In 5515 long-term survivors, the Fear of Cancer Recurrence Inventory–Short Form worked as a screen, with a cut-off of 22. [[Kotronoulas2017,Ingeman2026]]

Mood

Anxiety and low mood

In PROFILES (2625 survivors), depression symptoms were reported by 19.0% vs 12.8% of a normative sample, and anxiety by 20.9% vs 11.8%. Being single, low education and other illnesses raised the risk. [[Mols2018]]

Work and money

Return to work

In 32,174 German working-age patients after rehabilitation, 31% were back at work within a month and 65% had returned for 6 months or more by 2 years. The highest income third had three times the odds of a lasting return (aOR 3.06). Financial toxicity is common across 65 studies, and was more likely in women, people under 60 and those with stage IV disease. [[Soff2026,Shao2026]]

Fertility

Ask before treatment starts

In one US centre, only 15.5% of 194 people with early-onset colorectal cancer got fertility counselling, less often in lower-income areas. In an Italian survey, 45% had fertility-preservation counselling and 11.9% had discussed sexual issues. Pelvic surgery can cause adhesions and nerve damage that affect fertility. [[Peng2025,Spring2025,Savitch2024]]

Sex and bladder

After rectal treatment

More than half of people have worse sexual function after rectal cancer treatment, and about a third have urinary problems, mainly from nerve damage at surgery; radiotherapy adds to sexual but not urinary dysfunction. In the TME trial cohort, 79.8% of men reported more erectile dysfunction and 59.1% of women dyspareunia. [[Lange2011,Lange2009]]

Caregivers

Families carry weight too

In 185 family caregivers in China, burden rose and then fell, peaking mid-chemotherapy; almost 88% had moderate or high burden, and the main strain shifted from daily schedules to money. [[Wang2024CG]]

Early evidence

Diet after diagnosis

In a stage III cohort, a Western dietary pattern (meat, fat, refined grains, dessert) was linked to worse disease-free survival (HR 3.25, highest vs lowest fifth). Following American Cancer Society guidance was linked to lower mortality (HR 0.58). These are observational, unlike the randomised exercise evidence. [[Meyerhardt2007,VanBlarigan2018,Rock2022]]

Palliative care

Extra support, alongside treatment

Palliative care helps with symptoms and planning, and can start early. In early integrated palliative care for incurable lung and GI cancers, quality of life was better at week 24, more people discussed end-of-life wishes (30.2% vs 14.5%), and caregivers had less distress. In ALLAN, early home-based palliative care for advanced GI cancer improved quality of life by 13 points at week 24. [[Temel2017,ElJawahri2017,Bojesson2024]]

Caveats: the landmark trial was in lung cancer; the 2017 trial enrolled non-colorectal GI cancers; ALLAN was small and GI-wide (Early evidence). No colorectal-only early palliative care trial was found, so the evidence is extrapolated. [[Temel2010,Temel2017,Bojesson2024]]
