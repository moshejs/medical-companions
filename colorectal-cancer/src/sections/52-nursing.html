DPYD and fluoropyrimidines: what the guideline states

Dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD) breaks down about 80% of a 5-fluorouracil dose; only 1–3% becomes the active, cell-killing metabolites. Variants in its gene, DPYD, slow that breakdown, so the drug builds up. CPIC turns the genotype into an activity score from 0 to 2. Choose a score to see what the 2017 CPIC guideline states for it. [[Amstutz2018]]

Boxed warning (US labels)The capecitabine and fluorouracil labels state: "Test patients for genetic variants of DPYD prior to initiating" treatment "unless immediate treatment is necessary". They say to avoid the drug with certain homozygous or compound heterozygous variants that cause complete DPD deficiency, that no dose has been proven safe in complete deficiency, and that data are insufficient to recommend a dose in partial deficiency. The Xeloda label adds that no FDA-authorized DPYD test is currently available, and that serious reactions can still occur when no variant is found. [[FDA-Xeloda,FDA-FluorouracilFK]]

Teaching points

TopicWhat the source statesEvidence
Bowel prepSplit-dose preparation; a 2 L regimen may be enough; same-day prep acceptable for afternoon but inferior for morning colonoscopy; diet restriction limited to the day before (clear liquids or low-fibre meals for breakfast and lunch); simethicone suggested. ESGE: start the last dose within 5 hours of colonoscopy and finish at least 2 hours before. [[Jacobson2025,Hassan2019]]Split vs day-before: OR 2.51 for adequate cleansing. Enhanced instructions: OR 2.35. One day of low-residue diet as good as three. [[Martel2015,Guo2017,Wang2024LRD]]
Inadequate-prep risk factorsFactors linked to inadequate prep: cirrhosis (OR 3.41), stroke or dementia (2.09), constipation (1.97), diabetes (1.79), opioid use (1.70), low education (1.49). [[Gandhi2018]]67 studies, 75,818 patients. Poor prep lowers early-adenoma detection (OR 0.53). [[Gandhi2018,Sulz2016]]
Stoma site and classMark the best site before surgery; colorectal surgeons and certified ostomy nurses are best placed to mark. [[Salvadalena2015]]Preoperative education: peristomal skin complications 9.5% vs 19.4% (very low certainty). [[Shi2025]]
Ileostomy outputCall the team if output is much higher than usual (around 1.5 L a day or more), or with thirst, dizziness or little urine. [[Messaris2012,Vogel2022]]Dehydration was the commonest cause of readmission after diverting ileostomy (43.1% of readmissions). [[Messaris2012]]
Oxaliplatin and coldThe label advises avoiding cold drinks, ice and cold on the skin, and avoiding topical ice for mucositis prophylaxis. [[FDA-OxaliplatinGland]]N08CB: 71% sensitive to touching cold items with cycle 1. [[Pachman2015]]
Capecitabine at homeLabel patient instructions: stop and call for grade 2 hand-foot syndrome or worse, for grade 2 diarrhoea or worse, immediately for grade 2 or higher dehydration; call promptly for fever of 100.5°F or more. [[FDA-Xeloda]]Persistent or severe hand-foot syndrome can lead to loss of fingerprints. [[FDA-Xeloda]]
SurvivorshipFollow-up schedule, exercise, fear of recurrence, fertility and money worries are all things people can ask for help with. [[Meyerhardt2013,Courneya2025,Kotronoulas2017,Peng2025,Shao2026]]Fear of recurrence was the most prominent need at every stage. [[Kotronoulas2017]]
