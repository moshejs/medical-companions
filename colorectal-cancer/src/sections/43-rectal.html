Rectal cancer pathway

Rectal cancer is treated differently from colon cancer: it sits low in the pelvis, close to the sphincters, and an MRI scan plans everything. Follow one schematic course month by month, from staging through radiotherapy and chemotherapy to surgery, or to watch-and-wait when the tumour disappears.

Watch-and-wait: what happens next

dMMR rectal cancer: immunotherapy instead of everything else?

A small group of rectal cancers are mismatch-repair deficient (dMMR; see Tumour biomarkers). In a Memorial Sloan Kettering trial, people with stage II–III dMMR rectal cancer were given the PD-1 antibody dostarlimab, every 3 weeks, as their only first treatment. Early evidence [[Cercek2022,Cercek2025]]

Where the evidence disagrees

Controversy

Which TNT sequence?

RAPIDO lowered disease-related treatment failure but raised 5-year locoregional recurrence after resection (10% vs 6%), more often with a breached mesorectum. PRODIGE 23 improved DFS with FOLFIRINOX before chemoradiation. Which sequence is best remains unresolved. [[Bahadoer2021,Dijkstra2023,Conroy2021]]

Established

Skipping radiotherapy

PROSPECT supports FOLFOX with selective chemoradiation for T2N+ and T3 tumours suitable for sphincter-sparing surgery: only 9.1% needed chemoradiation before surgery, and 6.5% missed the 20% shrinkage threshold. It does not apply to high-risk tumours such as those threatening the margin, the population of RAPIDO. [[Schrag2023,Bahadoer2021]]

Controversy

Is watch-and-wait safe?

About a quarter regrow by 2 years, yet survival in registries is high. A pooled analysis of CAO/ARO/AIO-12 (mandatory TME) and OPRA (selective watch-and-wait) found no difference in survival, but it is not randomised. Watch-and-wait is offered in expert centres with intensive surveillance. Early evidence [[vanderValk2018,Williams2025]]

Early evidence

Dostarlimab and durability

Every patient who completed treatment had a clinical complete response, and 2-year recurrence-free survival was 96%. The study is a single-centre-led phase 2; how durable responses are over many years is not yet known, and the US label has no localised rectal indication. [[Cercek2025,FDA-Jemperli]]

Recurrence and survival numbers on this page are population statistics from trials and registries. They cannot predict what will happen to one person.
