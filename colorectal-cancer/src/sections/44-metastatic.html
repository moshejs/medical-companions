The biomarker-matched treatment ladder

Before first-line treatment, the tumour is tested for mismatch repair (MMR/MSI), RAS (KRAS and NRAS, exons 2–4), BRAF V600E and HER2, and its side matters. ASCO 2023 recommends multidisciplinary care and shared decision-making. Pick a tumour profile and a line of treatment: matching regimens light up with their pivotal trial result. Label wording from openFDA is quoted as published, not as instructions. [[Morris2023,VanCutsem2015]]

Safety signals from the labels

Boxed warning

Regorafenib

The Stivarga label states: "Severe and sometimes fatal hepatotoxicity has occurred in clinical trials", with liver tests before and during treatment. [[FDA-Stivarga]]

Boxed warning

Panitumumab

Dermatologic toxicities occurred in 90% of patients and were severe (grade ≥3) in 15% on monotherapy, per the Vectibix label. [[FDA-Vectibix]]

Boxed warning

Cetuximab

The Erbitux boxed warning covers serious and fatal infusion reactions and cardiopulmonary arrest. [[FDA-Erbitux]]

Boxed warning

Trastuzumab deruxtecan

The Enhertu label carries a boxed warning for interstitial lung disease and embryo-fetal toxicity. [[FDA-Enhertu]]

About the numbers. Median survival is the time by which half of a group has died; half live longer, some much longer. Trial and cohort figures describe groups of people treated in a particular place and era. They cannot say how one person will do. [[Zeineddine2023,vanderGeest2015]]
