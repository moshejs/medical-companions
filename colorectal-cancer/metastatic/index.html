Step 1 · Breaking away

Cells leave the primary tumour

A cancer that has invaded the bowel wall can shed cells into its veins. Sequencing of paired lesions suggests that cells within a cancer gain the ability to metastasise in under 2 years, with few if any extra selective events 270. Modelling of biopsies suggested that seeding often happened while the primary was still clinically undetectable, typically under 0.01 cm³ Early evidence 242. In the Burgundy registry, 14.5% of people already had liver metastases at diagnosis, and the 5-year cumulative rate of later liver metastases was also 14.5% 348.

In plain wordsSpread happens when a few cancer cells get into the bloodstream and settle in another organ, sometimes before the bowel tumour is even found.
Exam hookSynchronous metastases are present at diagnosis; metachronous ones appear later. For the liver, both were 14.5% in Burgundy.

The biomarker-matched treatment ladder

Before first-line treatment, the tumour is tested for mismatch repair (MMR/MSI), RAS (KRAS and NRAS, exons 2–4), BRAF V600E and HER2, and its side matters. ASCO 2023 recommends multidisciplinary care and shared decision-making. Pick a tumour profile and a line of treatment: matching regimens light up with their pivotal trial result. 5 matching regimens

For MSS/pMMR, left-sided, RAS wild-type disease, ASCO 2023 recommends chemotherapy plus anti-EGFR first-line. RAS testing must cover KRAS and NRAS exons 2–4: other RAS mutations were found in 14.7% of KRAS exon 2 wild-type tumours in CRYSTAL, and those did not benefit from cetuximab. 380,550

Chemotherapy + anti-EGFR (panitumumab or cetuximab) (1st line, PARADIGM · CRYSTAL). CRYSTAL, KRAS wild-type: FOLFIRI plus cetuximab OS 23.5 vs 20.0 months. 549,574

Safety signals from the labels

Boxed warning

Regorafenib

The Stivarga label states: "Severe and sometimes fatal hepatotoxicity has occurred in clinical trials", with liver tests before and during treatment. 174

Boxed warning

Panitumumab

Dermatologic toxicities occurred in 90% of patients and were severe (grade ≥3) in 15% on monotherapy, per the Vectibix label. 177

Boxed warning

Cetuximab

The Erbitux boxed warning covers serious and fatal infusion reactions and cardiopulmonary arrest. 160

Boxed warning

Trastuzumab deruxtecan

The Enhertu label carries a boxed warning for interstitial lung disease and embryo-fetal toxicity. 159

About the numbers. Median survival is the time by which half of a group has died; half live longer, some much longer. Trial and cohort figures describe groups of people treated in a particular place and era. They cannot say how one person will do. 552,599
