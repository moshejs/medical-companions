An animated, cited guide for students, people facing screening or a diagnosis, and their families. Educational only, not medical advice. Your place is saved on this device.

Not medical adviceThe Colorectal Cancer Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and patient understanding. It does not replace a doctor, nurse or screening programme. Doses, criteria, survival figures and guideline statements are reported as published, not as instructions or a prognosis for any individual. Anyone with symptoms should see a clinician, whatever their screening history.

Part 6 · Context & review

Sources

Every reference behind the Colorectal Cancer Companion: journal articles, guidelines, drug labels, trial registrations and regulator pages. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Eight research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology, symptoms and history; risk, prevention and hereditary syndromes; biology and the adenoma–carcinoma sequence; screening and colonoscopy; diagnosis, staging and biomarkers; treatment of localised disease; metastatic disease and research; and living, survivorship and nursing. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked not matched in a research database are regulator pages, press releases and society pages that have no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are used only for what they state, so treat them with extra care.
