# U · Research update 2026-10-29

Pass date: 2026-10-29. Window: 2026-10-25 to 2026-10-29, plus two US label changes published 2026-10-23 that the companion's 2026-10-25 pass did not reflect. Modules served: rectal, cure, chemo, metastatic, biomarkers, review.

## Findings

### rectal · dMMR rectal cancer and dostarlimab
- AZUR-1 (global single-arm registrational trial, NCT05723562; dostarlimab 500 mg every 3 weeks for 9 cycles; previously untreated stage II/III dMMR/MSI-H locally advanced rectal cancer; 154 enrolled; median follow-up 26.3 months): of 144 with ≥12 months' follow-up, 68% had a clinical complete response sustained for 12 months (investigator-assessed); pCR 12/15 (80%) among those who had surgery after dostarlimab alone; cCR12 + pCR 76%; no recurrences reported in patients with cCR or pCR; grade ≥3 immune-mediated adverse events 4 (3%) (Early evidence: interim analysis of a single-arm trial) [[Cercek2026]].
- JEMPERLI US label, version 15 (effective 2026-10-19): new indication 1.2, single agent for adults with stage II/III MSI-H or dMMR locally advanced rectal cancer, 500 mg every 3 weeks for 9 cycles. "This indication is approved under accelerated approval based on sustained clinical complete response for 12 months." "The long-term risk of relapse is unknown" [[FDA-Jemperli]].
- Overturned claim: the site said the US label had no localised rectal indication (43-rectal.html, 43-rectal.js, 62-review.js). Corrected.

### chemo · Adjuvant therapy for high-risk stage III colon cancer
- IROCAS (795 patients with resected pT4 and/or pN2 stage III colon cancer; mFOLFIRINOX vs mFOLFOX6, 12 cycles; median follow-up 55 months; NCT02967289): 3-year DFS 65.6% vs 66.3% (HR 1.07, 95% CI 0.84–1.36; P=0.61); 3-year OS 91.2% vs 86.7% (HR 0.85, 0.61–1.21; immature); grade ≥3 adverse events 62.2% vs 62.1%; grade ≥3 diarrhoea 15.6% vs 3.8% (Established: negative phase 3) [[Bennouna2026]].

### metastatic, biomarkers · KRAS G12C
- KRAZATI US label, version 14 (effective 2026-08-31, published 2026-10-23): "KRAS G12C Mutated Locally Advanced or Metastatic Colorectal Cancer - Accelerated Approval Indication Removed 08/2026". The label now covers NSCLC only [[FDA-Krazati]].
- Overturned claim: the site presented adagrasib plus cetuximab as labelled for colorectal cancer (44-metastatic.js, 33-biomarkers.js). Corrected; the KRYSTAL-1 trial data stay as trial evidence [[Yaeger2023,Yaeger2024]].

### Exam hooks
- AZUR-1: dostarlimab alone, 68% sustained 12-month clinical complete response in dMMR locally advanced rectal cancer; FDA accelerated approval October 2026 [[Cercek2026,FDA-Jemperli]].
- IROCAS: a third drug (irinotecan) adds toxicity but not DFS in high-risk stage III colon cancer [[Bennouna2026]].

### In plain words
- For the small group of rectal cancers called dMMR, a single immunotherapy drug is now approved in the US. How long the results last over many years is not yet known [[FDA-Jemperli]].
- Adding a third chemotherapy drug after surgery for high-risk colon cancer did not lower the chance of the cancer coming back [[Bennouna2026]].
