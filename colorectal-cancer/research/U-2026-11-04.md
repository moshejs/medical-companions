## Findings

### cure · Neoadjuvant immunotherapy in pMMR colon cancer
- pMMR colon cancer "has been consistently considered resistant to first-generation ICIs" [[Ghelardi2026]].
- UNICORN (phase 2, 56 patients with resectable, locally advanced colon cancer; 28 pMMR and 28 dMMR, each sequentially allocated to botensilimab alone, n=14, or botensilimab plus balstilimab, n=14): centrally assessed major pathological response (≤10% residual viable tumour) 0% (95% CI 0–23.2%) pMMR botensilimab; 36% (12.8–64.9%) pMMR combination; 36% (12.8–64.9%) dMMR botensilimab; 100% (76.8–100%) dMMR combination (Early evidence) [[Ghelardi2026]].
- Immune-related adverse events: 8 (29%) with botensilimab and 15 (54%) with the combination, most commonly flu-like syndrome and diarrhoea [[Ghelardi2026]].
- ctDNA clearance before surgery correlated with pathological response and no recurrence at data cutoff [[Ghelardi2026]].
- This adds to, without changing, the NICHE-2 dMMR card [[Chalabi2024]].

### Exam hooks
- UNICORN: botensilimab + balstilimab before surgery, 36% major pathological response in pMMR colon cancer, where first-generation checkpoint inhibitors fail [[Ghelardi2026]].

### In plain words
- A new two-drug immunotherapy shrank some colon cancers before surgery even in the common "pMMR" type, where older immunotherapy does not work. The trial was small [[Ghelardi2026]].
