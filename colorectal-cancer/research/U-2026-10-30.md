## Findings

### screening · Blood tests
- SimpleScreen CRC (Freenome; cell-free DNA blood test) received FDA premarket approval (P250029) on 24 July 2026. Its indication is "colorectal cancer screening in adults aged 45 years and older who are at average risk"; positive results "should be followed by colonoscopy", and it "is not a replacement for diagnostic colonoscopy or for surveillance colonoscopy in high-risk individuals" (Established, regulatory fact) [[FDA-SimpleScreen]].
- Shield is therefore no longer the only FDA-approved blood test for screening. ACS 2026 still recommends blood tests only for people who decline or do not complete the preferred tests [[Wolf2026]].

### In plain words
- A second blood test for bowel cancer screening is now approved in the US. A positive result still needs a colonoscopy [[FDA-SimpleScreen]].
