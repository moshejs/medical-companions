## Findings

### chemo · What the labels say
- TECENTRIQ US label, version 46 (effective 2026-10-13, published 2026-10-16): new indication 1.7, atezolizumab "in combination with a fluoropyrimidine and oxaliplatin, is indicated for the adjuvant treatment of adult and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with Stage III, mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) colon cancer", based on ATOMIC (NCT02912559) [[FDA-Tecentriq,Sinicrope2026]].
- The label states: "An FDA-authorized test for detection of dMMR tumor status in patients with colon cancer is not currently available." Established (regulatory) [[FDA-Tecentriq]].
- The FDA's oncology approvals list dates the approval 8 Oct 2026, before the previous passes' windows; the site already showed ATOMIC's results but not the approval.

### Exam hooks
- Atezolizumab + FOLFOX is US-approved (Oct 2026) as adjuvant therapy for stage III dMMR colon cancer, on ATOMIC (3-y DFS 86.3% vs 76.2%) [[FDA-Tecentriq,Sinicrope2026]].

### In plain words
- For a small group of stage III colon cancers (called dMMR), an immunotherapy drug added to chemotherapy after surgery is now approved in the US [[FDA-Tecentriq]].
