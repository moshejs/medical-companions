# U · Research update 2026-11-13

Pass date: 2026-11-13. Window: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13. Modules served: chemo, cure.

## Findings

### chemo, cure · NCCN Guidelines Insights: Colon Cancer, Version 3.2026
- DPYD: "The panel recommends discussing testing for DPYD genetic variants with patients prior to fluoropyrimidine therapy and considering testing in the context of each patient’s circumstances." The article notes that the FDA amended the capecitabine boxed warning in October 2025 and applied the same warning to fluorouracil in February 2026 (Established) [[Benson2026]].
- Adjuvant therapy: "Based on the results of the ATOMIC trial, the NCCN panel added atezolizumab, in combination with FOLFOX or CAPEOX, as a preferred adjuvant treatment option for stage III dMMR/MSI-H colon cancer" [[Benson2026]].
- Aspirin: "the NCCN panel recommends that 100–162 mg aspirin should be given daily for 3 years following surgery for stage II–III colon cancer harboring PI3K pathway alterations" [[Benson2026]]. The trial behind it is ALASCCA [[Martling2025]].
- Exercise: the panel added that "a structured exercise program should be implemented as part of an adjuvant treatment strategy, with the goal of achieving at least 150 minutes of moderate- to vigorous-intensity physical activity per week" [[Benson2026]].
- BRAF V600E metastatic disease: systemic therapy is reorganised by biomarker. The panel lists CAPEOX with encorafenib and cetuximab or panitumumab as an "other recommended" option, treats cetuximab and panitumumab as interchangeable in this combination, and prefers the BRAF regimen in the first-line setting [[Benson2026]].
- "All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated" [[Benson2026]].

### Exam hooks
- NCCN 2026: discuss DPYD testing before any fluoropyrimidine; atezolizumab plus FOLFOX or CAPEOX is a preferred adjuvant option for stage III dMMR colon cancer; aspirin for 3 years for PI3K-altered stage II–III disease [[Benson2026]].

### In plain words
- US cancer guidelines now advise talking about a gene test (DPYD) before starting fluorouracil or capecitabine, because some people break these medicines down slowly and can be badly harmed by a normal dose [[Benson2026]].

## Open questions and controversies
- NCCN says discuss and consider DPYD testing; the FDA boxed warning says test before starting unless treatment is urgent. Which variants to test is left to other bodies [[Benson2026,FDA-Xeloda]]. Status: open.
