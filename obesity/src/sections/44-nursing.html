In plain wordsThe cuff has to fit the arm. If it is too small, the reading comes out higher than the real blood pressure.

Body-weight descriptors: total, ideal and adjusted

No single body-size descriptor works for all drugs: volume of distribution guides a loading dose and clearance a maintenance dose, and both change with obesity in drug-specific ways. [[Hanley2010]] The AAGBI/SOBA guideline defines total body weight (actual weight), ideal body weight (height in cm minus 105 for women or minus 100 for men, as given in the guideline), lean body weight (weight without fat) and adjusted body weight, IBW + 0.4 × (TBW − IBW). [[Nightingale2015]] Try the formulas.

Teaching toolThis shows how the published descriptors relate. It gives no dose and is not for choosing one: drug dosing follows the product label, local protocol and a pharmacist. The IBW formula is the simplified one printed in the guideline, not the Devine formula.

In 3928 inpatients over 100 kg with BMI 40 or more, high-dose prophylaxis (enoxaparin 40 mg twice daily or heparin 7500 units three times daily) went with less VTE than standard doses (0.77% vs 1.48%, OR 0.52, 95% CI 0.27 to 1.00) without more bleeding (OR 0.84). [[Wang2014]] After gastric bypass, a BMI-stratified regimen gave a VTE rate of 0.45%. [[Borkgren2008]] These are observational data; there is no definitive trial. Doses are quoted, never instructions.

Exam hookAdjusted body weight = IBW + 0.4 × (TBW − IBW); lean body weight rarely exceeds 100 kg in men or 70 kg in women. [[Nightingale2015]]
In plain wordsMedicines spread through the body differently at a higher weight, so doctors and pharmacists use different body-weight measures for different medicines.

    Talking without stigma: the 5As

    1 · Ask

    Ask permission to discuss weight, without judgement. [[Vallis2013]]

    2 · Assess

    Assess BMI, waist and obesity stage, and look at drivers and complications. [[Vallis2013]]

    3 · Advise

    Advise on health risks, the benefits of modest loss and treatment options. [[Vallis2013]]

    4 · Agree

    Agree on realistic expectations and a plan. [[Vallis2013]]

    5 · Assist

    Assist with barriers, resources, referral and follow-up. [[Vallis2013]]
