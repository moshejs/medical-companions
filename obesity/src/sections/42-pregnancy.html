In a meta-analysis of 1,309,136 women, gain was below the IOM range in 23% of pregnancies and above it in 47%. Gain above the range went with more large-for-gestational-age babies (OR 1.85; absolute risk difference 4%), macrosomia (OR 1.95) and caesarean delivery (OR 1.30); gain below it with more small-for-gestational-age babies (OR 1.53) and preterm birth (OR 1.70). [[Goldstein2017]]

A pooled analysis of 196,670 women in 25 cohorts found the lowest risk with smaller gains in obesity (grade 1: 2 to under 6 kg; grade 2: a loss or gain of 0 to under 4 kg; grade 3: a gain of 0 to under 6 kg), but these ranges predicted outcomes only modestly (AUC 0.55 to 0.76), and the IOM ranges have not changed. [[Voerman2019LifeCycle]]

If eating or weight is distressingIf eating or weight causes distress, or someone restricts, binges or purges, talk to a clinician or an eating-disorder service. The ranges above are published population guidance, not a target for any one pregnancy; the Zepbound label advises that weight loss is not recommended during pregnancy, and the Wegovy label says weight loss offers no benefit in pregnancy. Exam hookZepbound can reduce oral contraceptive efficacy: the label advises a non-oral method or a barrier method for 4 weeks after starting and after each dose increase. [[FDA-Zepbound]]
In plain wordsBefore pregnancy, ask your clinician about folic acid and about any weight medicines. Some weight medicines need to be stopped two months before trying to get pregnant.

Pregnancy after bariatric surgery

Group results from observational studies. [[Johansson2015NEJM,Akhter2019PLoSMed]]

The 2019 consensus also says breastfeeding can be recommended after bariatric surgery, with micronutrients monitored during lactation. Because of dumping and accuracy concerns, it advises an oral glucose tolerance test at 24 to 28 weeks only after a band; after bypass, sleeve or biliopancreatic diversion it suggests a seven-point capillary glucose profile or 1 week of continuous glucose monitoring instead. [[Shawe2019ObesRev]] Surgery itself is in Metabolic & bariatric surgery and vitamins in Life after surgery.

Exam hookAfter surgery: less gestational diabetes (1.9% vs 6.8%) and fewer large babies, but more small-for-gestational-age babies (15.6% vs 7.6%). Avoid retinol-form vitamin A; the consensus lists beta-carotene. [[Johansson2015NEJM,Shawe2019ObesRev]]
In plain wordsIf you might become pregnant after weight-loss surgery, talk to your team about timing and contraception, and about the special vitamins you will need.
