Part 4 · Across life & care

Research frontier

The next medicines for obesity act on two or three hormone signals at once, come as monthly injections or daily tablets, or try to spare muscle. Scrub the pipeline to see what trials have shown so far, each result with its evidence label.

Obesity is a chronic, relapsing disease. The WHO 2025 guideline states that it needs lifelong care and that medication alone cannot solve the global burden of obesity. [[Celletti2026WHO]] Most of the agents below are not approved anywhere yet. Trial results do not mean a medicine is available, or right for any one person. [[NCT05882045,NCT06858839]]

How to read the labels

  • EstablishedAt least one completed phase 3 randomised trial, or a regulatory approval, for the stated use.
  • Early evidencePhase 1 or 2 trials, single trials in small groups, or observational data.
  • HypothesisA mechanism or signal not yet tested in an adequately powered human outcome trial.

Beyond weight: outcome trials

The bigger question is whether these medicines prevent the illnesses that come with clinical obesity. Some answers are in; several are years away.

Heart · SELECT

Fewer heart attacks, strokes and cardiovascular deaths

6.5% vs 8.0%
semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo, mean 39.8 months

17,604 people with established cardiovascular disease, BMI 27 or more and no diabetes: HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90). Adverse events led 16.6% to stop, versus 8.2% on placebo. [[Lincoff2023SELECT]]

HR 0.89 (95% CI 0.67–1.18) met non-inferiority: the drug did not raise cardiovascular risk. That is a safety result, not proof of benefit. Gastrointestinal events led about one in five on survodutide to stop. [[Bozkurt2026SYNCHRONIZECVOT]]

Other routes being explored

Rare genetic and hypothalamic obesity

Setmelanotide: repairing a broken fullness signal

Setmelanotide activates the melanocortin-4 receptor, downstream of the leptin pathway. The label covers acquired hypothalamic obesity from age 4, and Bardet-Biedl syndrome or genetically confirmed POMC, PCSK1 or LEPR deficiency from age 2; it is not for common obesity. [[FDA-Imcivree]] At about 1 year, 80% with POMC deficiency and 45% with LEPR deficiency lost at least 10% of body weight. [[Clement2020]] In acquired hypothalamic obesity, BMI changed −16.5% versus +3.3% on placebo. [[Miller2026TRANSCEND]] More in Other weight-loss medicines.

Established
Endoscopy · MERIT

Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty

In class 1–2 obesity, total body weight loss at 52 weeks was 13.6% with the procedure plus lifestyle versus 0.8% with lifestyle alone; serious procedure-related events in 2% (3 of 131). [[AbuDayyeh2022MERIT]] For operations, see Metabolic & bariatric surgery.

Established for class 1–2
Devices that disappointed

Vagal nerve blockade and duodenal resurfacing

Vagal blockade gave 9.2% versus 6.0% weight loss with a sham device and missed both prespecified efficacy targets. [[Ikramuddin2014ReCharge]] Duodenal mucosal resurfacing, a diabetes procedure, did not change HbA1c significantly overall versus sham. [[Mingrone2022REVITA2]]

Early evidence
Precision medicine

Matching medicine to phenotype

A Mayo Clinic group sorted adults into "hungry brain", "emotional hunger", "hungry gut" and "slow burn" phenotypes. With older medicines, phenotype-guided prescribing gave 15.9% versus 9.0% weight loss at 12 months (single centre, before modern incretins). [[Acosta2021Phenotype]]

Early evidence

Open questions

Are the newest agents better than tirzepatide?

Cross-trial numbers favour retatrutide, but network meta-analysis intervals overlap and head-to-head trials are few; the CagriSema versus tirzepatide trial has not reported. [[Jastreboff2026TRIUMPH1,Chen2026NMA,NCT06131437]]

Open

Is more weight loss worth more side effects?

Retatrutide 9 mg led 12% to stop for adverse events versus 5% on placebo, with dysaesthesia and hypotension as new signals; [[Bellido2026TRIUMPH2]] semaglutide 7.2 mg also raised dysaesthesia. [[Wharton2025STEPUP]] The answer depends on the person. See Side effects, safety & stopping.

Open

Activate GIP or block it?

Tirzepatide activates the GIP receptor; MariTide blocks it; both lower weight. Why both work is not settled. [[Jastreboff2022SURMOUNT1,Jastreboff2025MariTide]]

Hypothesis

Lifelong treatment, or stepping down?

Regain after stopping is expected biology: hunger hormones stay shifted a year after weight loss. [[Sumithran2011]] Switching to a daily tablet kept most of the loss for a year, without an arm that stayed on the injection. [[Aronne2026ATTAINMAINTAIN]] In US records, 64.8% of people without diabetes stopped a GLP-1 medicine within a year. [[Rodriguez2025Discontinuation]]

Open

Does losing muscle matter?

An MRI sub-study of STEP UP found a non-significant fall in thigh muscle with preserved sit-to-stand performance. [[Hjelmesaeth2026STEPUPMuscle]] No outcome trial has tested whether sparing lean mass improves strength, falls or long-term health. [[Heymsfield2026Bimagrumab]]

Hypothesis

Can health systems pay for them?

One US lifetime model put tirzepatide at $197,023 and semaglutide at $467,676 per quality-adjusted life year. [[Hwang2025CostEffect]] ICER, as reported in a 2026 commentary, found them cost-effective but unaffordable at scale. [[Ramachandran2026ICER]] The WHO made its recommendations conditional partly for cost and equity. [[Celletti2026WHO]]

Compounded and online products

After the US semaglutide and tirzepatide shortages ended, a 2025 secret-shopper study of 75 weight-loss clinics and medical spas found 56.0% still offering compounded GLP-1 products mixed with B vitamins, and 4 of 21 supplying pharmacies were not licensed for sterile compounding. [[DiStefano2026Compounded]] A poison-centre case series described 10-fold dosing errors with compounded semaglutide drawn up with syringes, partly from confusion between millilitres, units and milligrams. [[Lambson2023Compounded]] Compounded products are not FDA-approved medicines.
