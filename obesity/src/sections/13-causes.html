Causes: genes & environment

Obesity is a chronic disease with many causes. An international consensus states that it results "primarily from a combination of genetic, epigenetic, and environmental factors", and that the idea of obesity as a choice is a misconception. [[Rubino2020Stigma]] Build the picture layer by layer.

Medicines that can cause weight gain

Many medicines for diabetes, depression and other chronic diseases affect weight. The Endocrine Society guideline states that, where possible, options with favourable weight profiles should be chosen; that is quoted as published, not as an instruction. [[Apovian2015]] The figures below are trial averages compared with placebo. [[Domecq2015]]

MedicinesThese medicines treat serious conditions. Do not start, stop or change any medicine without a clinician. Weight effects can be discussed with them, and other options may exist.

Olanzapine label5.6 kg

The US label states a mean gain of 2.6 kg in short placebo-controlled trials in adults (placebo −0.3 kg), and 5.6 kg with at least 48 weeks of use; in adolescents, 11.2 kg with at least 24 weeks. [[FDA-Zyprexa]]

Mirtazapine label7.5%

The label states that 7.5% of adults in US controlled trials gained at least 7% of body weight (placebo 0%), and 17% reported increased appetite (placebo 2%). [[FDA-Remeron]]

Young people on antipsychotics4.4–8.5 kg

After a median of 10.8 weeks, gains were 4.4 kg (aripiprazole) to 8.5 kg (olanzapine), against 0.2 kg in untreated young people. [[Correll2009]]

Insulin and glucocorticoids4.0 kg

In UKPDS 33, weight gain over 10 years was greatest with insulin (4.0 kg). [[UKPDS33]] Prednisone 5 mg a day or more for over 6 months was linked to more weight gain, rising with dose. [[Huscher2009]]

Exam hookClozapine and olanzapine cause the most weight gain of the antipsychotics (+4.45 and +4.15 kg at 10 weeks vs −0.74 kg on placebo). [[Allison1999]] Metformin and GLP-1 receptor agonists go the other way. [[Domecq2015]]
In plain wordsSome medicines, such as certain mental-health, diabetes and steroid medicines, can cause weight gain. Never stop a medicine on your own: ask your clinician if there are other options. [[Domecq2015,Apovian2015]]

More influences across a lifetime

Before birth and in infancyOR 4.47

Maternal obesity before pregnancy was linked to overweight or obesity in children aged 10–18 (OR 4.47). [[Voerman2019]] Each 1 SD of faster weight gain in the first year roughly doubled the odds of childhood obesity (OR 1.97). [[Druet2012]] Established

Sleep, tested−270 kcal

Two nights of short sleep lowered leptin by 18% and raised ghrelin by 28%. [[Spiegel2004]] In a randomised trial, adults who usually slept under 6.5 hours slept about 1.2 hours more and ate 270 kcal a day less than controls. [[Tasali2022]]

Stopping smoking4.67 kg

People who quit without treatment gained 4.67 kg on average at 12 months, mostly in the first 3 months; 16% lost weight and 13% gained more than 10 kg. [[Aubin2012]] These are averages; people vary widely.

Neighbourhood−3.38 points

In a randomised social experiment, offering 4,498 women in high-poverty housing a voucher to move to a lower-poverty area lowered the prevalence of BMI 40 or more by 3.38 percentage points. [[Ludwig2011MTO]]

Gut microbesRR 1.21

Microbes from a twin with obesity made germ-free mice gain more fat. [[Ridaura2013]] Early-life antibiotics are linked to childhood obesity (RR 1.21), [[Shao2017]] but a placebo-controlled faecal transplant did not change insulin sensitivity or body composition. [[Yu2020FMT]] Hypothesis in humans

Hormone conditionsa few kg

Across thyroid function within the normal range, weight differences were modest, a few kilograms at most. [[Fox2008Thyroid]] Cushing's syndrome, from chronic excess glucocorticoids, is rare compared with common obesity. [[Lacroix2015]]

Energy balance or carbohydrate–insulin?The carbohydrate–insulin model says high-glycaemic-load diets drive fat storage first; [[Ludwig2021CIM]] the energy balance model puts the brain at the centre of regulation, responding to the body and the food environment.
