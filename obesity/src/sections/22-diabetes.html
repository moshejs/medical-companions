Obesity & type 2 diabetes

Type 2 diabetes is the complication most tightly bound to excess adiposity. Watch fat spill into the liver and pancreas until the insulin-making cells fall behind, then see what happened in the trials that prevented diabetes (DPP) and put it into remission (DiRECT).

Among 18 comorbidities pooled from 89 cohorts, type 2 diabetes had the strongest link to obesity (IRR 12.41 in women, 6.74 in men). [[Guh2009]] Even a moderate adult weight gain of 2.5 to under 10 kg went with more diabetes: 207 vs 110 cases per 100 000 person-years in women. [[Zheng2017]] Genetic studies support a causal role (OR 1.67). [[Riaz2018]]

Exam hookDPP: lifestyle (goal of at least 7% weight loss and 150 minutes of activity a week) cut diabetes incidence by 58% and metformin by 31%, over a mean 2.8 years. [[Knowler2002DPP]] DiRECT: remission 46% vs 4% at 12 months. [[Lean2018DiRECT]]
In plain wordsIn a large study of people at high risk, a lifestyle programme cut the chance of getting type 2 diabetes by more than half over about three years. [[Knowler2002DPP]] Diabetes that does come can sometimes go into remission, especially early on. [[Lean2018DiRECT]]

Preventing diabetes: the Diabetes Prevention Program

The DPP randomised 3234 adults with raised fasting and post-load glucose (mean BMI 34.0) to placebo, metformin 850 mg twice daily, or a lifestyle programme with goals of at least 7% weight loss and at least 150 minutes of activity a week. [[Knowler2002DPP]] These are the trial's arms as published, not a prescription. Toggle the arms and drag through 15 years.

USPSTF (grade B): clinicians should offer or refer adults with a BMI of 30 or more to intensive, multicomponent behavioural interventions, which lowered diabetes incidence in adults with obesity and raised glucose; harms were "small to none". How long does remission last?

DiRECT remission fell from 46% at 1 year to 36% at 2 years, and only 26% of those in remission at year 2 still were at 5 years. [[Lean2018DiRECT,Lean2019DiRECT,Lean2024DiRECT]] Weight regain is biology, not a lack of effort; remission is real but needs long-term support.

Which figure for "kept off 10 kg"?

The 2-year paper reports remission in 64% (29 of 45, whole study) of those who kept off at least 10 kg [[Lean2019DiRECT]]; the 5-year paper reports 81% (29 of 36, intervention group). [[Lean2024DiRECT]] Same people, different denominators.

Fewer serious problems

Over 5 years, the original DiRECT intervention group had fewer serious adverse events than controls: 4.8 vs 10.2 per 100 patient-years. [[Lean2024DiRECT]]
