Other weight-loss medicines

Before the weekly incretin injections there were other medicines, and they are still prescribed: one blocks fat digestion in the gut, two act on appetite and reward in the brain, one replaces a missing signal in rare genetic obesity, and one is a daily GLP-1 injection. In plain wordsThese medicines work in different places, which is why their side effects differ too. None of them is a test of willpower: they act on the body's own systems for hunger, fullness and digestion.

In plain wordsEach label sets a check-in point. If the medicine has not helped enough by then, the label says to stop it, so nobody stays on a medicine that is not working for them.

Guidelines weigh these options differently. The AGA's 2022 guideline suggested semaglutide 2.4 mg, liraglutide 3.0 mg, phentermine–topiramate ER and naltrexone–bupropion ER for long-term management (moderate-certainty evidence), and phentermine or diethylpropion with low certainty; it suggested against orlistat. [[Grunvald2022AGA]] Yet orlistat is the one medicine shown in XENDOS to cut diabetes incidence over 4 years in people with impaired glucose tolerance. [[Torgerson2004XENDOS]]

Setmelanotide: one gene, one receptor

POMC deficiency8 of 10

lost at least 10% at about 1 year in an open-label trial; "most hunger" scores fell by 27.1%. [[Clement2020]]

LEPR deficiency5 of 11

lost at least 10% at about 1 year; "most hunger" scores fell by 43.7%. [[Clement2020]]

Bardet-Biedl syndrome32.3%

of patients aged 12 and over reached at least 10% weight reduction at 52 weeks in a randomised trial; skin darkening (hyperpigmentation) occurred in 61%. [[Haqq2022Setmelanotide]]

Acquired hypothalamic obesity−16.5%

BMI change at 52 weeks vs +3.3% on placebo in TRANSCEND (ages 4 to 66, 120 people). [[Miller2026TRANSCEND]]

Why it works only in these conditions: in POMC or LEPR deficiency the chain that normally ends at the MC4 receptor is broken upstream, so a drug that switches the receptor on directly can stand in for the missing signal. [[FDA-Imcivree]] The genetics are in Causes: genes & environment, and the circuit in Appetite: the gut-brain loop.

Medicines that were withdrawn: a cautionary timeline

Several earlier weight-loss medicines were taken off the market when harm appeared, which is one reason today's medicines are tested in large, long safety trials. [[CDC1997Fenfluramine,James2010SCOUT]] Scrub through the decades.

Exam hookFenfluramine and dexfenfluramine: withdrawn in 1997 for heart-valve disease. Sibutramine: withdrawn after SCOUT (more non-fatal heart attacks and strokes). Rimonabant: withdrawn in Europe for psychiatric harm. [[CDC1997Fenfluramine,James2010SCOUT,Christensen2007]]
In plain wordsSome past weight-loss medicines turned out to harm the heart or mood and were taken off the market. That history is why new ones now go through very large safety studies before and after approval.

MedicinesDo not start, stop or change a medicine without a clinician. Products sold online as "compounded" GLP-1 medicines are not the same as FDA-approved ones: in the FDA's adverse event reports they had higher reporting odds of preparation errors and contamination, though reporting data cannot show cause.
