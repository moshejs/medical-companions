Life after surgery

Surgery is the start of lifelong care, not the end of treatment. The 1991 NIH consensus already called for lifelong medical surveillance after surgery. [[NIH1991Consensus]] Scrub from the day of the operation to 20 years later to see when each problem tends to appear.

UrgentAfter surgery, get urgent medical help for a fast heartbeat that does not settle, fever, belly or shoulder pain, or changes in breathing: these can be signs of a leak. [[Kim2026Complications,Hamilton2003]] Persistent vomiting needs prompt care because it can cause thiamine deficiency, which can damage the brain. [[Lupoli2017]] Confusion or seizures can be a sign of very low blood sugar. [[Kim2026Complications]] If you feel low, have thoughts of harming yourself, or feel unsafe, contact your care team or an emergency service straight away.

In plain wordsSurgery is safe for most people, but it has risks: leaks, bleeding, clots, ulcers, reflux, gallstones and blocked bowel. Many of these are found early when follow-up continues. [[Kim2026Complications]]
Exam hookLABS 30-day mortality 0.3%; modern perioperative mortality 0.03 to 0.2%. [[Flum2009LABS,Arterburn2020JAMA]] Predictors of a bad early outcome: previous clots, sleep apnoea, impaired function and extreme BMI, but not age, sex or race. [[Flum2009LABS]]

Vitamins and minerals, for life

Many people already have at least one vitamin or mineral deficiency before surgery, and the ASMBS guideline covers screening, prevention and repletion of thiamine, B12, folate, iron, vitamins A, E, K and D, calcium, copper and zinc. [[Parrott2017ASMBS]] Deficiencies are becoming more common while follow-up monitoring declines. [[Parrott2017ASMBS]] Pick a nutrient and an operation; rows about other operations fade.

Next steps

Revisional surgery achieves further weight loss in selected people but carries more complications than a first operation. [[Eisenberg2022ASMBS]] Medicines are another option; see How GLP-1 medicines work and Why lost weight comes back.

If eating or weight is distressingIf eating or weight causes distress, or someone restricts, binges or purges, talk to a clinician or an eating-disorder service. Some weight returning after surgery is common and is not a personal failure.

Alcohol and mental health

Alcohol after bypass20.8%

Cumulative new alcohol use disorder symptoms by 5 years after bypass, versus 11.3% after a band (AHR 2.08). [[King2017SOARD]] In SOS, bypass raised the risk of alcohol abuse diagnoses (adjusted HR 4.97); banding did not. [[Svensson2013SOS]]

Self-harmRR 1.54

Self-harm emergencies rose from 2.33 to 3.63 per 1000 patient-years after surgery in Ontario; 93% of events were in people with a mental health diagnosis in the 5 years before. [[Bhatti2016]] A meta-analysis found 3.8 times the odds of self-harm or suicide attempt compared with matched controls. [[Castaneda2019]]

Suicide4.1

Estimated suicides per 10,000 person-years after bariatric surgery, higher than in the general population. [[Peterhansel2013]] Whether surgery causes this or reflects earlier risk is unresolved. [[Bhatti2016]]

Support is part of careThese findings support psychological screening and follow-up before and after surgery, and alcohol education and screening. [[Peterhansel2013,King2017SOARD]] Feeling low, struggling with drinking or with how you eat are reasons to reach out, not reasons for shame. More in Living with obesity.

Pregnancy after surgery

A 2019 international consensus advises postponing pregnancy until weight is stable, typically about 1 year after sleeve or bypass and 2 years after a band, and contraceptive counselling before surgery. [[Shawe2019ObesRev]] In a Swedish register, pregnancies after surgery had less gestational diabetes (1.9% versus 6.8%) and fewer large babies, but more small-for-gestational-age babies (15.6% versus 7.6%). [[Johansson2015NEJM]] Details are in Pregnancy & fertility.

Exam hookThiamine deficiency with vomiting after surgery risks Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome. [[Lupoli2017]] Alcohol use disorder risk is about twice as high after bypass as after a band. [[King2012JAMA,King2017SOARD]]
