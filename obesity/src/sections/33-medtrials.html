Semaglutide & tirzepatide trials

Large randomised trials compared these medicines with placebo, with each other and against heart, lung and joint outcomes. Every number here is an average for a group of people in a trial, never a prediction for one person: some people lose much more, some much less. [[Wilding2021STEP1,Jastreboff2022SURMOUNT1]]

Averages, not a forecastWeight-change results are population averages from trials, with lifestyle support in both arms. They are not a target or a prediction for any one person, and nobody should start, stop or change a medicine without a clinician.

In plain wordsIn the big trials, people on semaglutide lost about 15% of their weight on average, and on tirzepatide about 20%. When the medicine stopped, much of the weight came back. That is biology, not failure. [[Wilding2021STEP1,Jastreboff2022SURMOUNT1,Wilding2022STEP1ext,Aronne2024SURMOUNT4]]

Beyond the scales: outcome trials

Weight is a means, not the goal. These trials asked whether the medicines change heart events, heart-failure symptoms, sleep apnoea, knee pain or diabetes.

SELECT · semaglutide 2.4 mgHR 0.80heart attack, stroke or CV death: 6.5% vs 8.0%

17,604 adults aged 45 or over with established cardiovascular disease, BMI 27 or more and no diabetes, followed for a mean 39.8 months (95% CI 0.72 to 0.90). Adverse events led 16.6% vs 8.2% to stop. [[Lincoff2023SELECT]] Weight change at 4 years was −10.2% vs −1.5%. [[Ryan2024SELECT]] Established

STEP-HFpEF · semaglutide 2.4 mg+16.6KCCQ symptom score vs +8.7 on placebo

529 people with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction and BMI 30 or more, 52 weeks. The 6-minute walk improved by 21.5 vs 1.2 m; serious adverse events 13.3% vs 26.7%. [[Kosiborod2023STEPHFpEF]] Established

Trials and everyday life

Outside trials, many people stop. In US insurance data on 195,915 new users in 2021, 36.5% had stopped a GLP-1 medicine by 12 months, and 50.3% of those with obesity but no diabetes. [[Do2024Discontinuation]] In US health records, 1-year discontinuation was 64.8% without type 2 diabetes vs 46.5% with it, and higher income went with staying on treatment. [[Rodriguez2025Discontinuation]]

In an Ohio and Florida health system, mean 1-year weight loss on injectable semaglutide or tirzepatide was 8.7%: 3.6% in those who stopped within 3 months, 6.8% in those who stopped at 3 to 12 months and 11.9% in those who stayed on; 80.8% were on lower maintenance doses. [[Gasoyan2025Weight]] Among people who stopped, the reasons were cost or insurance (47.6%), side effects (14.6%), shortages (11.8%), a switch to a compounded product (2.4%) and unsatisfactory weight loss (1.7%). In plain wordsObesity is a long-term condition. When the medicine stops, the body tends to regain weight. Cost is the most common reason people stop, so ask the care team about coverage before starting. [[Wilding2022STEP1ext,Gasoyan2025Reasons]]

If eating or weight is distressingIf eating or weight causes distress, or someone restricts, binges or purges, talk to a clinician or an eating-disorder service.
