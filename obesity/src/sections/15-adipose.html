Part 1 · Understanding obesity

Fat tissue as an organ

Fat tissue is not a passive store. It holds fat cells, blood vessels, nerves and immune cells, and it sends hormones and other signals around the body. [[Kershaw2004]] The Lancet Commission defines obesity as excess adiposity "with or without abnormal distribution or function of adipose tissue". [[Rubino2025Commission]] Scrub the timeline to zoom from the body into the tissue and watch what changes as it grows.

Schematic, not to scale. Signal bars show direction, not measured levels. [[Kershaw2004,Arita1999,Weisberg2003]]

In plain wordsFat is an active organ that sends hormones. When fat cells get very large they can become inflamed, and fat can end up stored in places such as the liver, where it causes more trouble. [[Kershaw2004,Cinti2005,Neeland2019]]
Exam hookCrown-like structures are macrophages surrounding dead, enlarged fat cells; more than 90% of fat-tissue macrophages in obesity sit around dead adipocytes. [[Cinti2005]] Adiponectin is made only by fat cells, yet it is lower in obesity. [[Arita1999]]

White, beige and brown fat

White fat stores surplus energy. Brown and beige fat burn energy as heat through uncoupling protein 1 (UCP1). [[Becher2021,Wu2012]] Pick a type, then switch the cold on and off.

How many fat cells? A slow turnover

Carbon-14 from nuclear bomb tests, built into DNA, let researchers date human fat cells. About 10% are renewed each year at all adult ages and body sizes, yet the total number stays constant through adulthood, even after marked weight loss. Where fat sits, and what it does there

Visceral vs subcutaneous4.7 vs 3.0

Odds ratio for metabolic syndrome per 1-SD more visceral or subcutaneous fat, women in the Framingham cohort (4.2 vs 2.5 in men). Only visceral fat added information after BMI and waist. [[Fox2007Framingham]]

Heritability57% / 36%

Estimated heritability of subcutaneous and visceral fat in the same Framingham cohort. [[Fox2007Framingham]] Genes are covered in Causes: genes & environment.

Liposuction~10 kg

Large-volume liposuction removed 9.1 to 10.5 kg of fat under the skin in 15 women with obesity, but did not change insulin sensitivity of muscle, liver or fat, or inflammatory markers, at 10 to 12 weeks. [[Klein2004]] Removing fat is not the same as weight loss.

Liver fat falls first12.8% → 2.9%

Liver fat in 11 people with type 2 diabetes on a 600 kcal/day research diet over 8 weeks; pancreatic fat fell from 8.0% to 6.2% and first-phase insulin secretion recovered toward normal. [[Lim2011]] A research protocol, not advice.

Brown fat and healthEarly evidence

Across 134,529 PET-CT scans from 52,487 people, those with detectable brown fat had lower odds of type 2 diabetes, heart disease and high blood pressure, most clearly in people with overweight or obesity. Retrospective and observational. [[Becher2021]] Whether switching brown fat on can change body weight is unproven.

The personal fat threshold

Many people develop type 2 diabetes at a BMI that is not in the obesity range. In the UKPDS the median BMI at diagnosis was 28 kg/m², and one in three had a BMI under 25. [[Taylor2015PFT]] One idea to explain this is the personal fat threshold.

Hypothesis

Each person has their own limit

The personal fat threshold hypothesis says each person has a level of fat beyond which type 2 diabetes becomes likely, that this level is independent of BMI. [[Taylor2015PFT]] It fits the wider picture of fat stored in the wrong places, such as the liver. [[Tchernof2013]]

Early evidence

A small test at "normal" BMI

In 20 people with type 2 diabetes and BMI under 27, repeated 5% weight-loss cycles lowered BMI from 24.8 to 22.5 kg/m² and returned liver fat to normal. 14 of 20 (70%) reached remission after a median initial loss of 6.5%. Small and uncontrolled. [[Taylor2023PFT]]

Early evidence

Who recovers insulin secretion

In a DiRECT subgroup, liver fat fell from 16.0% to 3.1% after weight loss. Return of first-phase insulin secretion separated people whose diabetes went into remission from those whose did not, and responders had shorter diabetes duration (2.7 vs 3.8 years). [[Taylor2018CellMetab]] See Obesity & type 2 diabetes.

Exam hookAbout 10% of adult fat cells are replaced each year; the total number is set by the end of adolescence and does not fall with weight loss. [[Spalding2008]] Visceral fat outranks subcutaneous fat for metabolic risk. [[Fox2007Framingham]]
