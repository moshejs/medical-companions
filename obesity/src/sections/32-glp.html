How GLP-1 medicines work

After a meal, cells in the gut release GLP-1, a hormone that helps the pancreas release insulin, slows the stomach and tells the brain that food has arrived. [[Holst2007,Drucker2018]] The body clears it within minutes. The medicines copy it, last far longer and, in tirzepatide's case, act on a second gut-hormone receptor as well. [[Kieffer1995,FDA-Wegovy,FDA-Zepbound]]

In plain wordsThese medicines act on the brain's appetite system, so people feel full sooner and think about food less. They are not a test of willpower. [[FDA-Wegovy,FDA-Zepbound]] Natural GLP-1 lasts only minutes in the blood; medicines like semaglutide are built to last about a week. [[Kieffer1995,FDA-Wegovy]]
Exam hookGLP-1: 30 amino acids, made by intestinal L cells from proglucagon, inactivated by DPP-4. [[Holst2007]] Its insulin effect is glucose-dependent. [[Holst2007,Drucker2018]] Tirzepatide is a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist. [[FDA-Zepbound]]

Weight loss is not just nausea

A common question is whether people lose weight because the medicine makes them feel sick. In STEP 1 to 3, mean weight loss on semaglutide 2.4 mg was similar in people who had gastrointestinal side effects (11.4 to 17.7%) and in people who did not (9.6 to 17.1%). Less than 1 percentage point of the extra weight loss was explained by those side effects. [[Wharton2022GItolerability]]

What changes is how much people choose to eat. In 72 adults with obesity, 20 weeks of semaglutide 2.4 mg cut the energy eaten at a free-choice lunch by 35% compared with placebo. [[Friedrichsen2021]] In a 12-week crossover trial, semaglutide 1.0 mg cut food intake across the day by 24%, reduced hunger and cravings and did not change resting metabolic rate adjusted for lean mass. [[Blundell2017]] The label says semaglutide lowers calorie intake, likely through appetite. [[FDA-Wegovy]]

Dose steps as printed in the US labels

Each label starts with a low dose and steps it up. Gastrointestinal side effects are most frequent during or shortly after a dose increase. [[Wharton2022GItolerability]] Choose a product and scrub through the first months to see the steps as the label prints them. Wegovy now comes as a weekly injection with a maximum of 7.2 mg and as a daily tablet, and orforglipron was approved in 2026 as Foundayo. [[FDA-Wegovy,FDA-Foundayo]]

Which receptors each medicine targets

Every medicine here acts on the GLP-1 receptor. Newer molecules add a second or third target: GIP (the other incretin), glucagon, or amylin, a hormone released with insulin. The research behind the investigational ones is in Research frontier.
