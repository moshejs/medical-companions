Appetite: the gut-brain loop

Hunger and fullness are not just decisions. The brain combines hormone and nerve signals about the body's fuel stores to match intake to need over time, mostly below conscious awareness. [[Schwartz2000,Hall2022EBM]] Follow one loop from an empty stomach to the signals that report the body's stores.

Schematic, not to scale: particle numbers and meter levels show direction and balance, not measured amounts. The hypothalamus inset: POMC fullness neurons (circles), AgRP/NPY hunger neurons (triangles), and the paraventricular nucleus (PVN) whose neurons carry MC4R. Numbers in the notes come from the studies cited.

Exam hookThe arcuate nucleus holds POMC/CART neurons, which reduce intake through alpha-MSH acting on MC4R, and NPY/AgRP neurons, which increase it. Leptin excites POMC and inhibits NPY/GABA input. [[Cowley2001]] In mice, switching on about 800 AgRP neurons triggered intense feeding within minutes. [[Aponte2011]]
In plain wordsYour brain has a "hunger team" and a "fullness team" of nerve cells. Hormones from your fat and gut tell these teams how much energy you have stored and what you just ate. [[Cowley2001,Cummings2007]]

Leptin: more is not always heard

Blood vs brain4.3×

Serum leptin was 318% higher in obesity (40.2 vs 9.6 ng/mL) but CSF leptin only about 30% higher (0.337 vs 0.259 ng/mL), so the CSF/serum ratio was 4.3-fold lower. [[Caro1996]] Early evidence

Cause or consequence??

"Leptin resistance" covers several phenomena; obesity itself weakens leptin signalling, so it is hard to tell which defects cause weight gain and which follow it. [[Myers2010]] Established concept Mechanisms debated

A starvation signal↓

In fasting mice, preventing the fall in leptin largely blunted the changes in the gonadal, adrenal and thyroid axes but had little effect on body weight. [[Ahima1996]] Leptin may matter most when it falls.

Leptin in common obesity−0.7 to −7.1 kg

In a randomised dose-escalation trial, 24-week weight change ranged from −0.7 kg at the lowest dose to −7.1 kg at the highest, and baseline leptin did not predict response. [[Heymsfield1999]]

Beyond the hypothalamus: reward

Dopamine receptors2.47 vs 2.99

Striatal D2 receptor availability was lower in 10 people with obesity than in controls; within the group, the lowest values went with the highest BMI. [[Wang2001Lancet]] Early evidence Small and cross-sectional: cause and effect cannot be separated.

Food-cue responsesfMRI

A blunted dorsal-striatum response to food related to current and future weight gain, mainly in carriers of the TaqIA A1 allele. [[Stice2008]] Early evidence

Two systems talking↔

Hormones that act on the hypothalamus also act on dopamine neurons, so reward, conditioning and self-control circuits interact with energy balance. [[Volkow2011]] Hypothesis for a reward deficit in common obesity.

After weight lossn = 6

After 10% weight loss, food pictures caused more activity in the brainstem and parahippocampal gyrus and less in the hypothalamus; leptin injections reversed many of these changes. [[Rosenbaum2008]] Early evidence

Exam hookMC4R-deficient mice: hyperphagia, hyperinsulinaemia, hyperglycaemia. [[Huszar1997]] Chemogenetic AgRP activation in mice increased feeding and lowered energy expenditure; silencing reduced eating: AgRP is necessary and sufficient. [[Krashes2011]]
In plain wordsNatural fullness hormones work in people with obesity, too: PYY cut eating by about the same amount in people with and without obesity. [[Batterham2003]] Many signals, not willpower alone, set how hungry someone feels.
