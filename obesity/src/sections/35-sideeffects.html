Side effects, safety & stopping

Most side effects of the incretin medicines are in the stomach and gut, come while the dose is going up, and settle. A few rarer problems carry label warnings, and stopping the medicine usually brings weight back. Scrub through 120 weeks of one trial group to meet them in the order they tend to matter.

UrgentSevere stomach pain that does not go away, sometimes spreading to the back, needs urgent medical review. [[FDA-Wegovy]] The Wegovy label also warns that stomach and gut reactions can be severe, and of acute kidney injury from fluid loss (volume depletion). [[FDA-Wegovy]]

Weight line: the STEP 1 extension group (327 people), published at weeks 0, 68 and 120 (dots); the shape between them is schematic. [[Wilding2022STEP1ext]] Dose steps as published in the Wegovy label, quoted, not instructions. [[FDA-Wegovy]] The symptom curve is a schematic shape based on the finding that GI events were most frequent during or shortly after escalation; [[Wharton2022GItolerability]] its running totals end at the label's pooled rates. [[FDA-Wegovy]] Trial averages, not predictions for any person.

Exam hookThe Wegovy label says that if 2 or more consecutive weekly doses are missed, escalation restarts at a lower dose to reduce GI adverse reactions. [[FDA-Wegovy]] GI events explained less than 1 percentage point of the extra weight loss in STEP 1–3. [[Wharton2022GItolerability]]
In plain wordsThe slow step-by-step start is there to let the stomach get used to the medicine. The weight loss does not come from feeling sick: people without nausea lost about as much as people with it. [[Wharton2022GItolerability]]

Side effects by medicine

Each medicine has its own pattern, so choose one. Rates are the share of people who reported each effect at any time in the trials, against placebo, as printed in the labels unless the note says otherwise. Mechanisms are in Other weight-loss medicines and How GLP-1 medicines work.

Sources: Wegovy, [[FDA-Wegovy]] Zepbound, [[FDA-Zepbound,Jastreboff2022SURMOUNT1]] Saxenda, [[FDA-Saxenda]] Qsymia [[FDA-Qsymia]] and Contrave (COR-I trial). [[Greenway2010CORI]] Trials differ in length and design, so compare within a medicine, not across them.

Staying on, and stopping

Trials keep people on treatment; everyday life often does not. Real-world results are lower than trial averages, largely because many people stop. [[Gasoyan2025Weight]]

Stopped within a year36.5%

of 195,915 new GLP-1 receptor agonist users in US insurance data (26.2% by 3 months); 50.3% of people with obesity but no diabetes. [[Do2024Discontinuation]]

Why people stopped47.6%

cost or insurance; side effects 14.6%; shortages 11.8%; switching to a compounded product 2.4%; unsatisfactory weight loss 1.7% (288 people). [[Gasoyan2025Reasons]]

One-year loss by persistence3.6 → 11.9%

for those who stopped within 3 months vs those who stayed on (6.8% if stopped at 3–12 months); 8.7% overall in 7881 adults. [[Gasoyan2025Weight]]

After stopping a GLP-1 medicine5.63 kg

average regain across 18 trials (95% CI 3.52–7.73), more with longer follow-up and after semaglutide than liraglutide. [[Tzang2025Rebound]]

Restarting is common: among people who stopped, 36.3% without diabetes and 47.3% with diabetes restarted within a year, and higher income was linked to staying on treatment, which raises equity concerns. [[Rodriguez2025Discontinuation]] Withdrawal trials in detail are in Semaglutide & tirzepatide trials, and the biology of regain in Why lost weight comes back.

Exam hookSURMOUNT-4: after a 36-week lead-in (−20.9%), switching to placebo gave +14.0% vs −5.5% with continued tirzepatide. [[Aronne2024SURMOUNT4]] STEP 4: +6.9% vs −7.9%. [[Rubino2021STEP4]]
In plain wordsCost is the most common reason people stop. Ask the care team about coverage before starting, and plan together what happens if the medicine has to stop. [[Gasoyan2025Reasons]]

MedicinesDo not start, stop or change a medicine or a dose without a clinician. Products sold online or "compounded" are not the same as FDA-approved medicines: compounded GLP-1 products had higher reporting odds of preparation errors, contamination and hospitalisation in FDA adverse event reports, though reporting data cannot show cause.
