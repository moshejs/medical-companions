Part 1 · Understanding obesity

BMI, waist & body fat

BMI is a quick, cheap screening number with real blind spots. Follow a point across the height and weight plane through the WHO classes, see what BMI misses, and why waist and a second measure now matter.

Bands follow BMI = weight ÷ height²; the moving point is an imaginary adult. [[WHO2000TRS894,Flegal1998,WHO2004Asian]]

BMI and waist-to-height: a teaching calculator

Teaching toolThis works the published formulas and cut-offs for an imaginary adult. It is not a diagnosis, not a target and not advice: BMI cannot tell fat from muscle, cut-offs differ by population, and the Lancet Commission asks for a second measure before calling body fat excessive [[Okorodudu2010,Rubino2025Commission]]. For children and adolescents, BMI is read against age- and sex-specific growth charts, so this calculator does not apply [[Kuczmarski2002,Ogden2025]].

If eating or weight is distressingIf eating or weight causes distress, or someone restricts, binges or purges, talk to a clinician or an eating-disorder service.

How well does BMI find excess body fat?

Adults · 31,968 people

0.42 · 0.97

Sensitivity and specificity of BMI ≥30 for excess body fat measured by body-composition methods. BMI "fail[s] to identify half of the people with excess BF%" [[Okorodudu2010]].

NHANES III · 13,601 adults

19% vs 44%

Obesity in men by BMI against body-fat percentage (women 24.7% vs 52.3%). BMI did worst in the 25–29.9 range, in men and in older adults, and in men tracked lean mass better than fat [[RomeroCorral2008]].

Children · 53,521 aged 4–18

0.73 · 0.93

Pooled sensitivity and specificity; BMI cut-offs missed "over a quarter of children with excess body fat percentage" [[Javed2015]].

Populations

r = 0.955

Correlation of W/H² with measured fat mass per height² in women (0.943 in men): "both a convenient and reliable indicator of obesity" for groups [[Garrow1985]].

Waist beyond BMI · 259,388 adults

5% · 39%

Share with a high waist among people with normal BMI and with overweight. In those BMI groups, a high waist or waist-to-hip ratio meant 15–50% greater risk for most cardiovascular outcomes [[Dardari2026]].

EPIC · 359,387 adults

2.05 · 1.78

Relative risk of death in the highest waist fifth (men, women) after adjusting for BMI [[Pischon2008EPIC]].

Sarcopenic obesity

Excess adiposity together with low skeletal muscle mass and function (ESPEN/EASO 2022). Screening uses BMI or waist plus signs of low muscle; diagnosis rests on muscle-function tests, then body composition. Stage I has no complications, stage II has complications [[Donini2022]].

The open question

Meta-analyses favour waist-to-height ratio over waist or BMI [[Ashwell2012,Browning2010]], but waist cut-offs remain population-specific and international harmonisation is unfinished [[Alberti2009Harmonizing,Ross2020IASICCR]]. Status: unresolved. How a clinician puts measures together is covered in Assessment & staging; children in Children & adolescents.
