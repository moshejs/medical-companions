Living with obesity

Obesity is a chronic, relapsing disease, so the work does not end when a programme does. After weight loss the body pushes back for years, and ongoing support changes what happens next. [[Sumithran2011,Hall2018Maintenance]] This module follows the years after weight loss, then turns to work, travel, equipment, relationships, mood and binge eating.

    Regain is biologyAfter weight loss, hunger hormones stay changed for at least a year and appetite rises with each kilogram lost. [[Sumithran2011,Polidori2016]] Weight coming back is the disease acting, not a personal failure. More in Why lost weight comes back.

    If eating or weight is distressingIf eating or weight causes you distress, or you restrict, binge, purge or exercise compulsively, talk to a clinician or an eating-disorder service before changing how you eat. Some diet trials excluded people with eating disorders, so their results may not apply. Help works.

    A binge-eating screen: what a positive result means

    Binge-eating disorder (BED) had a lifetime prevalence of 3.5% in women and 2.0% in men in a US national survey; it was associated with severe obesity, and only a minority ever sought treatment. [[Hudson2007]] The 7-item Binge-Eating Disorder Screener (BEDS-7) flags possible BED for full evaluation. In its development it had 100% sensitivity and 38.7% specificity, [[Herman2016BEDS7]] so it misses almost no one but flags many people who do not have BED.     BED is a treatable health condition. A meta-analysis of 81 trials (7515 people) found large effects of psychotherapy, mostly CBT, on binge episodes; medicines had small effects, and evidence quality was generally very low. [[Hilbert2019]] In 125 adults with obesity and BED, 12-month binge-eating remission was 51% with CBT and 36% with behavioural weight loss. [[Grilo2011]] The US label lists lisdexamfetamine for moderate to severe BED in adults and states it "is not indicated or recommended for weight loss"; it carries a boxed warning for abuse, misuse and addiction. [[FDA-Vyvanse]]

    Exam hookMaintenance trials: diet-plus-activity behavioural programmes cut regain by 1.56 kg at 12 months (45 trials). [[Dombrowski2014]] Obesity and depression run both ways: OR 1.55 for later depression, OR 1.58 for later obesity. [[Luppino2010]]
    In plain wordsKeeping weight off is hard because the body pushes back, not because people are weak. Regular support, help with mood and sleep, and treatment that continues all help. Health gains can last even if some weight returns.
