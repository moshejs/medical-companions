Obesity Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on obesity, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people living with obesity, their families and caregivers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Heart Attack Companion, on the coronary heart disease that obesity makes more likely, the Cirrhosis Companion, on where fatty liver disease can lead, the Stroke Companion, on another vascular complication, and the Gout Companion, on a form of arthritis that is more common at higher weight.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
