An animated, cited guide for students, people living with obesity, and their families and caregivers. Educational only, not medical advice. Your place is saved on this device.

Not medical adviceThe Obesity Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. Obesity is a chronic disease with genetic, biological, environmental and social causes, not a failure of willpower. The guide does not replace a doctor, nurse, dietitian, psychologist, surgeon or the rest of a care team. BMI, waist and staging tools are teaching aids, never a diagnosis or a target. Doses, schedules and guideline statements are quoted as published, never as instructions: do not start, stop or change a medicine without a clinician. Weight-change results are averages from trials, not predictions for any person.

Ectopic fatFat stored in the wrong places, such as the liver and around the heart, adding cardiometabolic risk 394.

Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty (ESG)An organ-sparing procedure through the mouth; 13.6% total weight loss against 0.8% at 52 weeks in MERIT 3.

Gastric bypass (Roux-en-Y)A small pouch joined to a jejunal Roux limb, bypassing most of the stomach and the duodenum 284.
Gestational weight gainWeight gained in pregnancy. IOM 2009 published 5-9 kg for pre-pregnancy BMI 30 or more 452.

Hypertrophy (adipose)Fat growth by larger cells; linked to higher insulin and lower insulin sensitivity, independent of fat mass 24.
Hypothalamic obesityObesity after a hypothalamic tumour, lesion or injury; setmelanotide gave BMI -16.5% against +3.3% 367.
Ideal body weightIn the AAGBI/SOBA guideline, height in cm minus 105 (women) or 100 (men); used for tidal volume, as published 399.

PCOSPolycystic ovary syndrome; women with PCOS have higher obesity prevalence (RR 2.77) 326.

Obesity Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on obesity, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people living with obesity, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose. Nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, a diet, an operation or a care plan without your own doctor or care team. BMI, waist-to-height, the Edmonton Obesity Staging System and other tools are for learning, never a diagnosis or a target for any person. Doses, escalation schedules, thresholds and guideline statements are quoted as published by drug labels, the AGA, AAP, ASMBS/IFSO, the Lancet Commission and other guidelines, never as instructions, and weight-change figures are averages from trials, not predictions for any person. Compounded or online "GLP-1" products are described only as the FDA describes them. If eating or weight causes distress, or someone restricts, binges or purges, talk to a clinician or an eating-disorder service.
