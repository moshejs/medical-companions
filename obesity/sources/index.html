An animated, cited guide for students, people living with obesity, and their families and caregivers. Educational only, not medical advice. Your place is saved on this device.

Not medical adviceThe Obesity Companion is an educational synthesis of published literature for study and understanding. Obesity is a chronic disease with genetic, biological, environmental and social causes, not a failure of willpower. The guide does not replace a doctor, nurse, dietitian, psychologist, surgeon or the rest of a care team. BMI, waist and staging tools are teaching aids, never a diagnosis or a target. Doses, schedules and guideline statements are quoted as published, never as instructions: do not start, stop or change a medicine without a clinician. Weight-change results are averages from trials, not predictions for any person.

Part 5 · Context & review

Sources

Every reference behind the Obesity Companion: randomised trials such as the DPP, Look AHEAD, DiRECT, STEP, SURMOUNT and SELECT, cohorts such as SOS and LABS, meta-analyses, the 2025 Lancet Commission, guidelines from the AHA/ACC/TOS, USPSTF, AGA, AAP and ASMBS/IFSO, and US drug labels. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Research passes ran in parallel, one per area: definitions, measurement, epidemiology and history; causes, genetics and environment; physiology of appetite, fat tissue and energy balance; complications and clinical assessment; lifestyle and behavioural treatment; anti-obesity medications; metabolic and bariatric surgery; children, pregnancy, stigma, living with obesity and nursing; and the research frontier. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Some guideline texts could only be read in part; where a module relies on a statement reported through a secondary source, it says so. Drug labels change, so check the current label on the regulator's site. Entries marked not matched in a research database are pages with no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed.

Obesity Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on obesity, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people living with obesity, their families and caregivers.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose. Nothing here should start, stop or change a medicine, a diet, an operation or a care plan without your own doctor or care team. BMI, waist-to-height, the Edmonton Obesity Staging System and other tools are for learning, never a diagnosis or a target for any person. Doses, escalation schedules, thresholds and guideline statements are quoted as published by drug labels, the AGA, AAP, ASMBS/IFSO, the Lancet Commission and other guidelines, never as instructions, and weight-change figures are averages from trials, not predictions for any person. Compounded or online "GLP-1" products are described only as the FDA describes them. If eating or weight causes distress, or someone restricts, binges or purges, talk to a clinician or an eating-disorder service. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
