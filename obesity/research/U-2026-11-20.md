## Findings

### children · Tirzepatide in adolescents (SURMOUNT-ADOLESCENTS)
- Phase 3, randomised, double-blind, 72-week trial at 31 centres in Canada, the Czech Republic, Israel, Italy, Poland and the US in adolescents aged 12 to under 18 with obesity, or overweight with at least one obesity-related complication. 160 were randomised 2:1 to weekly tirzepatide escalated to a maximum tolerated dose (5, 10 or 15 mg) or placebo, both with a healthy lifestyle intervention. Mean age 14.6 years, 61.9% female, mean BMI 38.6. [[Kelly2026SURMOUNTADOLESCENTS]]
- Primary end point, percent change in BMI at week 72: −18.5% with tirzepatide vs +0.5% with placebo (estimated treatment difference −19.0 percentage points, 95% CI −23.9 to −14.1; P < .001). [[Kelly2026SURMOUNTADOLESCENTS]]
- BMI reduction of at least 5%: 79.6% vs 28.5% (risk difference 51.2 percentage points, 95% CI 36.3 to 66.1). All 10 key secondary outcomes favoured tirzepatide. The most common adverse events were gastrointestinal, mostly mild to moderate. [[Kelly2026SURMOUNTADOLESCENTS]]
- The Zepbound label still states that safety and effectiveness "have not been established in pediatric patients" (openFDA, effective 2026-08-28, read 2026-11-20). [[FDA-Zepbound]]

### children · Semaglutide in children aged 6 to under 12 (STEP Young)
- Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial: 167 children aged 6 to under 12 with obesity were randomised 2:1 to weekly subcutaneous semaglutide (112) or placebo (55) for 104 weeks, both with lifestyle counselling, followed by a 7-week washout. (Early evidence: one trial, not a labelled use) [[Jastreboff2026STEPYOUNG]]
- Primary end point, percent change in BMI at week 68: −13.5% vs +2.7% (difference −16.2 percentage points, 95% CI −19.4 to −12.9; P < 0.001). At week 104: −9.4% vs +8.4% (difference −17.8 points, 95% CI −22.2 to −13.3). [[Jastreboff2026STEPYOUNG]]
- A reduction in BMI category at week 68 occurred in 79% vs 23% (OR 7.8, 95% CI 3.5 to 17.6); BMI fell below the obesity threshold (95th percentile for age and sex) in 38% vs 2%. [[Jastreboff2026STEPYOUNG]]
- Adverse events occurred in 96% vs 93%. Gastrointestinal events were the most common, were more frequent with semaglutide and led one participant on semaglutide to stop. [[Jastreboff2026STEPYOUNG]]
- The Wegovy weight indication covers "pediatric patients aged 12 years and older with obesity"; children under 12 are not on the label. [[FDA-Wegovy]]

### sideeffects · NAION: FDA requires a class-wide label warning
- On 2026-11-09 the FDA issued a Drug Safety Communication requiring application holders of all GLP-1 receptor agonist medicines (the list includes Wegovy, Saxenda, Zepbound and Foundayo) to add information on a potential increased risk of non-arteritic anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy (NAION) to the Warnings and Precautions section. (Early evidence: the FDA describes a potential increased risk) [[FDA2026NAION]]
- FDA meta-analysis of 126 randomised trials (134,253 patients; 288,879 person-years): 15 probable NAION events, 10 with GLP-1 receptor agonists and 5 with placebo; incidence 6.4 per 100,000 person-years with GLP-1 receptor agonists vs 3.8 with comparators. [[FDA2026NAION]]
- Sentinel cohort of adults with type 2 diabetes: GLP-1 receptor agonist vs SGLT-2 inhibitor HR 1.19 (95% CI 0.91 to 1.54); vs DPP-4 inhibitor HR 1.43 (95% CI 1.02 to 2.01). [[FDA2026NAION]]
- The FDA advises people to seek care at once for sudden vision loss in one eye, and clinicians to refer suspected NAION promptly to an ophthalmologist and to consider stopping the medicine unless benefits clearly outweigh risks; quoted as published, not as an instruction. [[FDA2026NAION]]
- The openFDA label records for Wegovy (effective 2026-06-18), Zepbound, Saxenda and Foundayo did not yet contain NAION text on 2026-11-20.

### Exam hooks
- SURMOUNT-ADOLESCENTS: tirzepatide lowered BMI by 18.5% vs +0.5% with placebo at 72 weeks in adolescents; not a labelled use. [[Kelly2026SURMOUNTADOLESCENTS,FDA-Zepbound]]
- STEP Young: semaglutide lowered BMI by 13.5% vs +2.7% at 68 weeks in children aged 6 to under 12. [[Jastreboff2026STEPYOUNG]]
- November 2026: the FDA required a NAION warning on all GLP-1 receptor agonist labels (6.4 vs 3.8 per 100,000 person-years in trials). [[FDA2026NAION]]

### In plain words
- Trials have now tested tirzepatide in teenagers and semaglutide in children aged 6 to 11. Both lowered BMI much more than placebo. Neither use is on the US label today.
- A rare kind of sudden vision loss in one eye may be slightly more common in people taking GLP-1 medicines. It is very rare. Sudden vision loss needs urgent care.

## Open questions and controversies
- Should obesity medicines be used before age 12? One phase 3 trial shows a large BMI effect over two years [[Jastreboff2026STEPYOUNG]]; long-term growth, puberty and safety data and a regulatory decision are still to come; AAP guidance offers medicines from age 12 [[Hampl2023AAP]]. Status: open.
- Do GLP-1 medicines cause NAION? A single-centre cohort found large hazard ratios [[Hathaway2024NAION]]; pooled trials show few events and a small absolute difference, and the FDA calls the risk potential [[FDA2026NAION]]. Status: open.
