Sepsis Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on sepsis (the body's harmful response to an infection), septic shock and the other types of shock, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people who survive sepsis, their families and caregivers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Heart Attack Companion, on the commonest cause of cardiogenic shock, and the TB Companion and Cirrhosis Companion, on an infection and a liver disease that can both lead to sepsis, and the Stroke Companion, another time-critical emergency.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
