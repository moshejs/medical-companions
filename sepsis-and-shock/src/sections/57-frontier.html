Research frontier

For thirty years, drugs that calmed the immune storm in animals failed in people with sepsis. Walk the timeline from the "graveyard" of failed trials to today's lab bench: phenotypes and endotypes, immunotherapy matched to a patient's immune state, vitamin C, rapid diagnostics and AI alerts, each with an evidence label you can filter by.

Filter the bench and the graveyard

Evidence

Why so many trials failed

More than 100 randomised trials have tested whether modulating the septic response improves survival, and "with one short-lived exception" (drotrecogin alfa) none produced a new treatment. [[Marshall2014]] Explanations offered are that the response is complex and redundant, the same pathway can do good and harm, and trials enrolled unselected patients; the proposed fix is to stratify patients and target those likely to benefit. [[Marshall2014,Angus2012Drotrecogin]] Animal models may also mislead: animals in classic models die of shock within 72 hours, while many patients die later, with opportunistic infections and low monocyte HLA-DR, a sign of immune exhaustion. [[Docke1997]] See Infection to dysregulated host response for the biology.

Established

Drotrecogin alfa: the drug that came and went

PROWESS (2001, 1,690 people): 28-day mortality 24.7% vs 30.8%, with more serious bleeding (3.5% vs 2.0%). [[Bernard2001PROWESS]] Approved after that single trial, which was stopped early for efficacy, it was withdrawn worldwide in 2011 when PROWESS-SHOCK (1,697 people) found 26.4% vs 24.2%. [[MedLett2011Xigris,Angus2012Drotrecogin,Ranieri2012PROWESSSHOCK]]

Established

Harm, not just failure

An anti-TNF fusion protein raised 28-day mortality with dose, from 30% on placebo to 53% at the highest dose. [[Fisher1996TNFR]] A non-selective nitric oxide synthase inhibitor raised blood pressure but increased mortality (59% vs 49%). [[Lopez2004NOS]] In LOVIT, IV vitamin C increased death or persistent organ dysfunction (RR 1.21). [[Lamontagne2022LOVIT]]

Early evidence

Subgroups that did not hold up

HA-1A looked strongly positive in Gram-negative bacteraemia (30% vs 49% mortality) but not overall, and the confirmatory CHESS trial found no benefit. [[Ziegler1991HA1A,McCloskey1994CHESS]] A p55 anti-TNF subgroup signal (23% vs 36%, P = .07) was not confirmed. [[Abraham1997p55]] Post hoc subgroups generate hypotheses; they need a new trial.

Not one disease: phenotypes and endotypes

Early evidence Clustering 29 routine variables in 20,189 people with Sepsis-3 sepsis gave four clinical phenotypes with very different 28-day mortality. In simulations, changing the phenotype mix of an early goal-directed therapy trial swung its result from a more than 33% chance of benefit to a more than 60% chance of harm. Early evidence

SRS endotypes

White-cell gene expression in pneumonia sepsis defined SRS1 (immunosuppressed: endotoxin tolerance, T-cell exhaustion, HLA class II loss) and SRS2; SRS1 had higher 14-day mortality (HR 2.4), and a 7-gene set classifies patients. [[Davenport2016]] Post hoc, hydrocortisone was linked to higher mortality in SRS2 in the VANISH trial (OR 7.9; interaction P = 0.02). [[Antcliffe2019]]

Early evidence

MARS endotypes

Mars1 to Mars4: Mars1 had the worst outcome (39% 28-day mortality) and was the only endotype consistently linked with death across cohorts. [[Scicluna2017]]

Early evidence

MALS

Macrophage activation-like syndrome was found in 3.7–4.3% of more than 5,000 people with infection and SIRS; ferritin above 4,420 ng/mL carried about 66–67% 28-day mortality. [[Kyriazopoulou2017MALS]]

Reading research news

How to read a headline

Questions worth asking

  • Which outcome? A better SOFA score or immune marker is not the same as more people surviving: ImmunoSep met its SOFA end point without a significant mortality difference. [[GiamarellosBourboulis2026ImmunoSep]]
  • Subgroup or whole trial? Anakinra's signal came from 43 people in a reanalysis. [[Shakoory2016]]
  • Confirmed? PROWESS was positive; PROWESS-SHOCK was not. [[Bernard2001PROWESS,Ranieri2012PROWESSSHOCK]]
  • Randomised? The TREWS mortality result is observational. [[Adams2022TREWS]]
Early evidence

Endotoxin removal, again

EUPHRATES found no benefit from polymyxin B haemoperfusion, and SSC 2021 suggests against it. Tigris (2026, 157 people, open label, Bayesian) in a narrower endotoxin band reported a 95.3% posterior probability of benefit at 28 days; the guideline position predates it. [[Dellinger2018EUPHRATES,Evans2021SSC,Neyra2026Tigris]]

Hypothesis

Is shutdown protective?

Low muscle ATP tracked with death in sepsis, yet others frame tubular-cell and mitochondrial changes as an adaptive, hibernation-like response. [[Brealey2002,Gomez2014]]

Call nowNone of the research on this page changes what to do in an emergency. Sepsis is a medical emergency. If someone with an infection becomes very unwell (confused or very drowsy, breathing very fast, cold, mottled or bluish skin, passing little or no urine), call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department now and ask: could this be sepsis?
