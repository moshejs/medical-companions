Part 4 · Treatment

Vasopressors & steroids

When fluid alone does not bring the blood pressure back, intensive care teams add medicines that tighten the blood vessels or help the heart squeeze. See how each drug works, how the guideline orders them, what blood-pressure target the trials support, and what hydrocortisone adds.

Call nowSepsis is a medical emergency. If someone with an infection becomes very unwell (confused or very drowsy, breathing very fast, cold, mottled or bluish skin, passing little or no urine), call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department now and ask: could this be sepsis?

Vasopressors are part of the definition of septic shock: Sepsis-3 identifies it by "a vasopressor requirement to maintain a mean arterial pressure of 65 mm Hg or greater and serum lactate level greater than 2 mmol/L" after fluids 447. Vasoplegia, the failure of vessels to constrict, is classically attributed to nitric oxide, ATP-sensitive potassium channels in vascular smooth muscle and relative vasopressin deficiency 249. Blocking nitric oxide production does not help: a nitric oxide synthase inhibitor raised blood pressure but increased mortality (59% vs 49%) 268. Doses on this page are quoted from labels and guidelines as published. They are not instructions.

Infusion · Noradrenaline

Noradrenaline

Its label describes α-adrenergic vasoconstriction with β-adrenergic inotropy: it tightens the vessels and helps the heart contract. Levophed "is indicated to raise blood pressure in adult patients with severe, acute hypotension" 137. SSC 2021: "we recommend using norepinephrine as the first-line agent over other vasopressors" (strong; evidence high versus dopamine, moderate versus vasopressin) 133. In SOAP II, dopamine caused more arrhythmias (24.1% vs 12.4%) 112.

In plain wordsThe usual first choice. It tightens blood vessels and helps the heart pump, so blood pressure rises 133,137.

Step 1 · first line

Noradrenaline first

SSC 2021, recommendation 37: "we recommend using norepinephrine as the first-line agent over other vasopressors" (strong) 133. SOAP II (1679 patients with shock): 28-day mortality 52.5% with dopamine versus 48.5% with norepinephrine (P=0.10), with twice the arrhythmias 112. A meta-analysis of 32 trials found lower mortality with norepinephrine than dopamine (RR 0.89) 32.

In plain wordsNoradrenaline (norepinephrine) is the usual first medicine to raise blood pressure 133.
Exam hookNorepinephrine over dopamine: arrhythmias 12.4% vs 24.1% (SOAP II) 112.

What blood-pressure target? MAP and the trials

Mean arterial pressure (MAP) is the average pressure driving blood into the organs. SSC 2021 explains that organ perfusion falls below a MAP threshold "usually understood to be approximately 60 mm Hg" 133. Step through the trials that tested higher and lower targets.

Step 1 · the definition

Septic shock and MAP 65

Sepsis-3 identifies septic shock by "a vasopressor requirement to maintain a mean arterial pressure of 65 mm Hg or greater and serum lactate level greater than 2 mmol/L (>18 mg/dL) in the absence of hypovolemia" 447. In the derivation data, these patients had a mortality of 42.3% (95% CI 41.2–43.3%), a figure for large groups, not one person 440.

In plain wordsDoctors call it septic shock when medicines are needed to keep the average blood pressure at 65 or more, and a blood test called lactate is high 447.
Exam hookSeptic shock = vasopressor for MAP ≥65 + lactate >2 mmol/L despite fluids 447.

The traditional estimate is diastolic pressure plus one third of the pulse pressure (systolic minus diastolic). In 1878 people, a factor of 0.412 (MAP = DBP + 0.412 × PP) correlated better with target-organ damage than the traditional one-third 338. The ESICM consensus notes that shock does not require hypotension, which it gives as SBP <90, MAP <65 or a fall of ≥40 mm Hg from baseline 85.

SSC 2021 remark

The usual regimen, as published

"The typical corticosteroid used in adults with septic shock is IV hydrocortisone at a dose of 200 mg/day given as 50 mg intravenously every 6 h or as a continuous infusion. It is suggested that this is commenced at a dose of norepinephrine or epinephrine ≥ 0.25 mcg/kg/min at least 4 h after initiation" 133. The Solu-Cortef label gives no septic-shock-specific dose: the regimens come from trials and guidelines 138.

Established

Faster shock reversal

Cochrane 2019 (61 trials, 12,192 people): corticosteroids probably slightly reduce 28-day mortality (RR 0.91; moderate certainty), and increase muscle weakness (RR 1.21) and hypernatraemia, without clearly more superinfection 25. A 2018 meta-analysis found higher shock reversal at day 7 (RR 1.26, high certainty), with more hypernatraemia and hyperglycaemia 397.

Early evidence

Dose and fludrocortisone

A 2024 dose-response meta-analysis (45 RCTs) found short-term mortality RR 0.93 and an optimum near 260 mg/day of hydrocortisone or equivalent 356. Whether fludrocortisone matters, and the best dose, timing and threshold, remain unresolved 133. The SCCM/ESICM 2024 update recommends against high-dose, short-duration corticosteroids for septic shock 93.

Starting in an arm vein: how safe is it?

SSC 2021 (kept in 2026): "we suggest starting vasopressors peripherally to restore MAP rather than delaying initiation until a central venous access is secured" (weak, very low quality). The 2021 remark: give them "only for a short period of time and in a vein in or proximal to the antecubital fossa" 133,366.

In one medical ICU (734 patients, 783 peripheral infusions, mean 49 h), extravasation occurred in 2% (19 patients), with no tissue injury after treatment; 13% eventually needed central access 81.

Each agent: guideline, trials and label

First line

Noradrenaline (norepinephrine)

SSC 2021: "we recommend using norepinephrine as the first-line agent over other vasopressors" (strong) 133. SSC 2026 recommends it over dopamine, epinephrine or selepressin (strong) and suggests it over vasopressin or angiotensin II (conditional) 366. Where it is unavailable, "epinephrine or dopamine can be used as an alternative", with attention to arrhythmia risk 133.

Not first line

Dopamine

SOAP II (1679 with shock): 28-day mortality 52.5% with dopamine versus 48.5% with norepinephrine (P=0.10), arrhythmias 24.1% versus 12.4%, and excess deaths in cardiogenic shock 112. Pooled, norepinephrine beat dopamine for mortality (RR 0.89) 32. "Renal-dose" dopamine did not protect the kidneys 51.

Second agent

Vasopressin

SSC 2021 suggests "adding vasopressin instead of escalating the dose of norepinephrine" (weak, moderate) 133. VASST (778): 28-day mortality 35.4% versus 39.3% (P=0.26) 401. VANISH (409): less RRT (25.4% vs 35.3%) 175. Pooled (1453): no mortality effect, more digital ischaemia, fewer arrhythmias 318. SSC suggests against terlipressin 133,366.

Third agent

Adrenaline (epinephrine)

For inadequate MAP "despite norepinephrine and vasopressin, we suggest adding epinephrine" (SSC 2021, weak, low; kept in 2026) 133,366. CATS (330): epinephrine alone versus norepinephrine plus dobutamine, 28-day mortality 40% versus 34% (RR 0.86, not significant) 22. In CAT, 12.9% were withdrawn for transient metabolic effects 315.

Add-on

Angiotensin II

ATHOS-3 (321 on more than 0.2 µg/kg/min norepinephrine): MAP response at 3 h 69.9% versus 23.4%; 28-day mortality 46% versus 54% (HR 0.78; P=0.12) 226. The label warns of thromboembolic events (12.9% vs 5.1%) and says to use VTE prophylaxis. At entry, 97% were on norepinephrine and 67% on vasopressin 136.

Weak heart

Inotropes

For cardiac dysfunction with persistent hypoperfusion "despite adequate volume status and arterial blood pressure, we suggest either adding dobutamine to norepinephrine or using epinephrine alone" (SSC 2021, weak, low) 133. SSC 2026 suggests inotropes over none, added to vasopressors, and both versions suggest against levosimendan 133,366. LeoPARDS found no SOFA benefit and more tachyarrhythmia with levosimendan 174.

What is still argued

Open

When to add vasopressin

The SSC 2021 practice remark is 0.25–0.5 µg/kg/min of norepinephrine 133. VASSPR (3959 patients) found no mortality difference between start thresholds of 0.1 and 0.4 (31.8% vs 33.0%) 350. In one cohort, odds of death rose 20.7% for every 10 µg/min of norepinephrine-equivalent at vasopressin start (association only) 403.

Early evidence

Several pressors at once

TRICYCLE (79 patients, phase II) started angiotensin II, vasopressin and norepinephrine early together: fewer tachyarrhythmias (15.6% vs 68.6%) and similar mortality, not powered for it 220. Methylene blue within 24 h shortened time to vasopressor discontinuation (69 vs 94 h) in a 91-patient trial 208.

Early evidence

Steroids plus vasopressin

In a post hoc VASST analysis, patients also given corticosteroids had lower mortality with vasopressin than norepinephrine (35.9% vs 44.7%), and corticosteroids raised plasma vasopressin levels 402.

Debated

Individual MAP targets

Lower targets in older adults appear at least as safe 18,247, while people with chronic hypertension needed less RRT at the higher SEPSISPAM target 31. ESICM recommends individualising the target 85.

In plain wordsIf fluid alone does not raise blood pressure enough, doctors use medicines that tighten blood vessels. The usual first choice is noradrenaline (norepinephrine), and others can be added 133,137. These medicines are usually given in intensive care with close monitoring, and sometimes they are started in an arm vein so treatment is not delayed 133,474. Doctors usually aim for an average blood pressure (the "MAP") of about 65; for older adults, slightly lower may be fine 133,247,366. Some people also get a steroid called hydrocortisone, which can help blood pressure recover sooner, though its effect on survival is small and still debated 24,491.

The numbers on this page describe large groups of people in trials, not what will happen to any one person. Fluids come first: see Fluids. Treating the infection itself continues in Source control & antimicrobial stewardship.
