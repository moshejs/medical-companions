TB infection tests: TST & IGRA

The skin test and the blood test both ask one question: has this person's immune system met the TB bacterium? Neither can say whether TB is active now. Watch both run side by side over 72 hours, then change the person.

Worth knowing

TST

Boosting and conversion

BCG-induced reactivity can be boosted by repeated tuberculin testing, and no method reliably tells a BCG reaction from infection, so in a BCG-vaccinated person at increased risk a positive TST is taken to show infection. In someone retested after a negative result, an increase of 10 mm or more within 2 years counts as a conversion and suggests recent infection. 17

False negatives

When a negative test is less reassuring

TST sensitivity is about 43% in people with HIV. 284 In children with HIV in Cape Town, the TST was positive in fewer than 20%. 305 A negative test never excludes TB disease in someone with symptoms. 284

BCG

How much does BCG matter?

Across 24 studies of 240,203 people given BCG in infancy, 8.5% had a TST of 10 mm or more because of BCG, and only 1% when tested 10 or more years later. After BCG given past the first birthday, 41.8% did, and 21.2% still did after 10 years. Non-tuberculous mycobacteria caused false positives in only 0.1–2.3%. 158

Established

A positive test is not TB disease

In 13 studies of 32,034 people, positive predictive values for TB disease in lower-incidence countries were 2.74% (TST 15 mm) and 2.46% (QFT-GIT), with negative predictive values above 99.4%. Across 15 cohorts, TB incidence in IGRA-positive people was 4–48 per 1,000 person-years. 387 In the UK PREDICT cohort, 1.0% of 9,610 recent contacts and migrants developed TB over a median 2.9 years. 4,217 See Treating TB infection.

WHO 2022

Antigen-based skin tests (TBSTs)

Cy-Tb, C-Tb, Diaskintest and C-TST inject ESAT-6 and CFP-10 into the skin, so BCG does not affect them. WHO states that "Mycobacterium tuberculosis antigen-based skin tests (TBSTs) may be used to test for TB infection" (conditional recommendation, very low certainty). C-Tb had a sensitivity of 74.5% (IGRA 71.7%) and specificity of 97.9%; in Spanish contact tracing it agreed with QFT in 94%. 216,276,405,513

Early evidence

Which predicts disease better?

A pooled hazard ratio for incident TB was 4.15 for QFT-GIT and 2.88 for a BCG-stratified TST, but much of the difference came from one study, and absolute predictive values were almost the same. In PREDICT, a 5 mm TST cut-off identified the most people who progressed (83%), at the cost of specificity. 4,217
