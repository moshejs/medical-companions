## Findings

### frontier · PARADIGM4TB (UNITE4TB-01), first stage
- Phase 2 randomised controlled trial in people with rifampin-susceptible pulmonary TB: 24 weeks of HRZE, 16 weeks of bedaquiline-delamanid-moxifloxacin, or one of 10 new 16-week four-drug combinations containing ganfeborole, BTZ-043 or both, with combinations of bedaquiline, delamanid, moxifloxacin, pyrazinamide, pretomanid and linezolid. 401 were randomised; 371 were in the modified intention-to-treat population and 400 in the safety population (Early evidence) [[Esmail2026]].
- Primary end point: the slope of log10 time to positivity of sputum culture from baseline to week 12, assessed as the Bayesian posterior probability that a new regimen is superior to HRZE. The authors state the results "were used to select regimens for further evaluation, rather than for characterization of efficacy" [[Esmail2026]].
- Two regimens were selected: ganfeborole-bedaquiline-pretomanid-moxifloxacin (BPMG) and BTZ-043-bedaquiline-delamanid-moxifloxacin (BDMT). Slopes: 0.129 log10 days per week (95% credible interval 0.112–0.146) with BPMG and 0.142 (0.126–0.160) with BDMT, against 0.118 (0.105–0.131) with HRZE. Relative differences: 9.1% (−8.6 to 29.0) and 20.5% (2.7 to 41.1); posterior probabilities of superiority 0.83 and 0.99 [[Esmail2026]].
- Adverse events of grade 3 or higher: 38% with BPMG (no discontinuations), 28% with BDMT (one discontinuation, for a drug reaction with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms) and 24% with HRZE (no discontinuations). The bedaquiline-moxifloxacin-pyrazinamide-BTZ-043 arm was discontinued because of hepatotoxic effects [[Esmail2026]].
- The ClinicalTrials.gov record still lists the platform as recruiting (last update posted 2026-10-15; no results posted) [[NCT06114628]].

### Exam hooks
- PARADIGM4TB chose two 16-week four-drug regimens for further testing, one with ganfeborole and one with BTZ-043; the phase 2 stage selected regimens and did not measure cure [[Esmail2026]].

### In plain words
- A large trial that tests many new TB drug combinations at once has picked two to study further. It does not yet show that they cure TB faster [[Esmail2026]].

## Open questions and controversies
- Do BPMG and BDMT cure TB in 16 weeks without more relapse than 6 months of HRZE? The phase 2 stage used a culture-based marker over 12 weeks, and BPMG's credible interval for the difference from HRZE includes zero [[Esmail2026]]. Status: open.
