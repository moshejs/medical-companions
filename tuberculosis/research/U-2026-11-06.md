# U · Research update 2026-11-06

Pass date: 2026-11-06. Window: 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-06, plus a catch-up check of WHO Module 4, second edition (issued 2026-09-21). Modules served: drtb, resistance, living.

## Findings

### drtb · WHO Module 4: treatment and care, second edition (issued 21 Sep 2026)
- The second edition carries forward all valid 2025 recommendations, merges the 2022 child and adolescent treatment recommendations, and adds new and updated drug-resistant TB recommendations from the November 2025 GDG. All DS-TB recommendations, the 9-month and longer regimens, the Hr-TB regimen, monitoring, ART timing, surgery and HCV co-treatment are unchanged (Established) [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- Pooled BEAT Tuberculosis and endTB-Q data, plus extended TB-PRACTECAL follow-up, led WHO to choose the regimen for pulmonary pre-XDR-TB by disease severity [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- B1.1 (UPDATED): BPaLM remains suggested for MDR/RR-TB (conditional, very low certainty). B1.1a (NEW): BPaL "in patients with non-severe pulmonary pre-XDR-TB … rather than the BPaLC regimen". B1.1b (NEW): BPaLC (bedaquiline, pretomanid, linezolid, clofazimine) "in patients with severe pulmonary pre-XDR-TB … rather than the BPaL regimen". Both are conditional, very low certainty [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- Severity, as defined for this choice: cavitary lesions on chest radiography with sputum smear 1+ or higher, or smear 2+ or higher (or an equivalent molecular result) even without cavities. Everything else counts as non-severe [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- If fluoroquinolone susceptibility is unknown, start BPaLM and adjust after DST. Fluoroquinolone-susceptible TB continues as BPaLM whatever the severity [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- BPaL/M/C eligibility as published: aged 14 or older; any HIV status; pulmonary TB and all extrapulmonary TB except CNS, osteoarticular or disseminated multiorgan TB; less than 1 month of previous bedaquiline, linezolid, pretomanid, delamanid or clofazimine (clofazimine is new to this list); not in pregnancy or breastfeeding. The linezolid dose is 600 mg once daily in BPaLM, BPaL and BPaLC [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- B1.2 (UPDATED): BDLLfx/C is kept for MDR/RR-TB with or without fluoroquinolone resistance (conditional, very low certainty). B1.2a (NEW): 6-month BDLC is suggested in non-severe pulmonary pre-XDR-TB over 18-month or longer regimens. B1.2b (NEW): WHO "suggests against using the 6-month BDLC regimen in patients with severe pulmonary pre-XDR-TB". Both are conditional, very low certainty. BDLLfx/C still covers all ages, people with HIV, and pregnant and breastfeeding women [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- Evidence behind B1.2b: in severe pre-XDR-TB, BDLC (n=114) against longer standard-of-care regimens (n=58) in BEAT Tuberculosis and endTB-Q had failure or recurrence of 12.3% vs 1.7% (RD 106 more per 1000, 95% CI 22 to 182 more) and amplified resistance of 9.6% vs 3.4% (very low certainty) [[WHO-Mod4-2026]]. This fits endTB-Q's own conclusion that noninferiority was not shown overall [[Guglielmetti2025LRM]].

### living · Palliative care (new section C4)
- C4.1 (NEW): "In patients with pulmonary MDR/RR-TB, in addition to effective treatment, WHO recommends integrating palliative care assessment and interventions by trained providers" (conditional, moderate certainty). It is WHO's first recommendation on palliative care in TB, and it is to be given alongside effective treatment, not instead of it (Established) [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- The evidence is the Ugandan nurse-led RCT: 76 people with palliative care vs 78 without. At month 4, palliation was 0.61 SD higher (0.35–0.86) and quality of life 0.79 SD higher (0.44–1.14). No missed doses in the past 30 days: 932 vs 559 per 1000 (OR 10.77, 3.99–35.10) [[WHO-Mod4-2026,Buyinza2025]].

### Exam hooks
- WHO 2026: pre-XDR-TB (fluoroquinolone-resistant) gets BPaL if the lung disease is non-severe and BPaLC (clofazimine instead of moxifloxacin) if it is severe [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- WHO 2026: BDLC only for non-severe pulmonary pre-XDR-TB. WHO suggests against it in severe disease because of failure and amplified resistance [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- WHO 2026: palliative care is recommended alongside, not instead of, MDR/RR-TB treatment [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].

### In plain words
- If your TB also resists the fluoroquinolone medicines, the 6-month tablet regimen now depends on how much of your lungs is affected. People with cavities or many germs in their sputum get an extra medicine, clofazimine [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
- Care for symptoms, worry and family needs is now part of WHO's advice for drug-resistant TB. It sits alongside the TB medicines and does not replace them [[WHO-Mod4-2026]].
