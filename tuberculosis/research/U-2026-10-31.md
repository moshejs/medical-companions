## Findings

### resistance · Genotypic resistance testing
- WHO's third mutation catalogue (published 30 Oct 2026) draws on "more than 71 000" isolates "from over 75 countries" with paired genomic and phenotypic data, and extends the catalogue to pretomanid and cycloserine (Established) [[WHO-MutCat3-2026]].
- Bedaquiline, groups 1+2, all isolates: sensitivity 47.5% (95% CI 44.6–50.4) in the second edition; 55.0% (52.0–57.9) with specificity 98.9% (98.8–99.1) in the third at the 75% allele-frequency threshold; 66.3% (63.5–69.1) with specificity 98.7% (98.5–98.8) at the 25% threshold (Table 10) [[WHO-MutCat3-2026]].
- "The catalogue does not constitute a standalone clinical decision-support tool" [[WHO-MutCat3-2026]].
- This updates, without replacing, the claim that the 2021 catalogue graded only two mutations each for the newest drugs [[Walker2022]].

### Exam hooks
- WHO mutation catalogue, 3rd edition (2026): >71,000 isolates; adds pretomanid and cycloserine; bedaquiline sensitivity 55.0% (66.3% counting low-frequency variants) [[WHO-MutCat3-2026]].

### In plain words
- Gene tests for resistance to the newest TB drugs got better in 2026 but still miss some resistant strains [[WHO-MutCat3-2026]].
