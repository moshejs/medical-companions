Is 4 months as good as 6? Reading the trials

A non-inferiority trial asks whether a new regimen is not worse than the standard by more than a pre-set margin. Pick a trial: the bars show unfavourable outcomes in each arm, and the right-hand panel shows the difference from the standard with its confidence interval. If the whole interval sits left of the margin line, non-inferiority is shown.

Regimens and recommendations are quoted as WHO and ATS/CDC/ERS/IDSA publish them, for teaching. They are not instructions for anyone's treatment, which is decided with the clinician and national TB programme, usually free of charge.

Global outcome

Treatment works

88%

Treatment success for drug-susceptible TB on first-line regimens, 2023 cohort, up from 81% in 2016. It ranged from 71% in the Americas to 93% in the Eastern Mediterranean Region, and was 79% for people with HIV. These are population figures, not a prediction for one person. [[WHO-GTR2025]]

Established

Rifampicin all the way through

Across 57 trials and 21,472 people, using rifampicin only in the first 1 to 2 months meant more failure, relapse and acquired resistance. WHO's analysis: relapse 8.2% vs 3.1% with fully susceptible TB, about 112 relapses averted per 1,000 people treated. [[Menzies2009,WHO-Mod4-2025]]

Established

Daily, not three times a week

WHO recommends daily dosing throughout. Thrice-weekly dosing throughout carried 3.3 times the acquired-resistance rate. In Union Study A, unfavourable outcomes 12 months after treatment were 5% with 2EHRZ/4HR against 14% with a thrice-weekly intensive phase plus 6HE. [[WHO-Mod4-2025,Jindani2004]]

Established

One tablet or four?

WHO prefers fixed-dose combination tablets (conditional, low certainty). In Study C, favourable outcomes were 93.9% with a 4-drug combination tablet and 94.6% with separate drugs. The 4-month rifapentine regimen has no combination tablet, so more pills a day. [[WHO-Mod4-2025,Lienhardt2011]]

Early evidence

Treat by strategy, not by fixed length?

TRUNCATE-TB started with 8 weeks and extended or retreated as needed. The bedaquiline-linezolid strategy averaged 85 days of treatment but 12.7% to 22.8% of strategy participants needed retreatment, and it uses core drugs for drug-resistant TB. It is not in WHO Module 4 (2025). [[Paton2023]]

Established

Steroids for meningitis

In 545 people over 14 with TB meningitis, dexamethasone lowered death at 9 months (RR 0.69) but not severe disability among survivors. In TB pericarditis (IMPI, 1,400 adults), prednisolone did not change the main outcome but lowered constriction (4.4% vs 7.8%). [[Thwaites2004,Mayosi2014]]

Before the 4-month regimen, fluoroquinolone resistance must be excluded: see Drug resistance. Side effects and the checks during treatment are in Side effects & monitoring; support during treatment in Living with TB.
