    Teaching points and common misunderstandings

    "My urine is orange"

    Expected, and harmless

    Rifampicin can colour urine, sweat, sputum and tears yellow, orange, red or brown and permanently stain soft contact lenses. Tell people before the first dose so it does not frighten them into stopping. [[FDA-Rifampin]]

    "I feel fine, so I can stop"

    Feeling better is not cure

    The rifampin label asks staff to stress the full course and not missing doses. [[FDA-Rifampin]] In cohort studies, self-administered treatment without support went with more acquired drug resistance than DOT (RR 4.19). [[Alipanah2018]] If a dose is missed, the person should tell the team: how a programme responds to missed doses may matter more than any device. [[Mohamed2025]]

    "I'm on the pill"

    Contraception

    Rifamycins can reduce the effect of oral hormonal contraception; the rifampin label says alternative measures should be considered. Offer pregnancy testing and family-planning referral free of charge and without judgement. [[FDA-Rifampin,Maugans2023]]

    "Just a drink or two"

    Alcohol and the liver

    The rifampin label asks people to avoid alcohol and liver-toxic drugs or herbs; daily alcohol raises the risk of isoniazid hepatitis. Alcohol use roughly doubled the odds of a poor outcome. [[FDA-Rifampin,FDA-Isoniazid,Ragan2020]]

    "Why do you watch me swallow?"

    Observation is support

    Explain DOT or video-observed treatment as help to finish, not suspicion. In New York, electronic DOT completed 89.8% of doses against 87.2% in person, and was non-inferior. [[Burzynski2022]] A nurse-led community health worker programme reached 91.8% completion of weekly 3HP among adults experiencing homelessness. Early evidence [[Nyamathi2021]]

    Sputum

    Coach the cough

    Simple instructions on producing sputum raised smear positivity in women 1.63-fold (8% to 13% detected) and cut saliva samples. [[Khan2007Lancet]] Across 23 studies, instructions raised microscopy yield (OR 1.6); early-morning collection alone did not improve yield significantly. [[Datta2017LGH]]

    Stigma-free communication

    Say this, not that

    Instead ofSay
    TB suspectperson with presumptive TB
    defaulterperson lost to follow-up
    TB controlTB prevention and care
    latent TBTB infection
    TB patient (as an identity)person with TB

    The first three are from authors in TB programmes on five continents, who called the old terms coercive and blaming. [[Zachariah2012]]

    Why it matters

    • Fear of infection is the most common cause of TB stigma; it is perceived to delay diagnosis and disrupt treatment, though measured effects are mixed. [[Courtwright2010]]
    • Stigma was linked to lower uptake of testing and poorer adherence to treatment for TB infection. [[Kilic2025]]
    • WHO notes that staff education may reduce the stigma health workers themselves hold. [[WHO-Mod4-2025]]
    • Triage should fast-track people with presumed TB in dialogue with staff and patients, so it does not add to stigma or alienation. [[WHO-IPC2019]]
    • Interview people in their own language, with an interpreter if needed, and explain how confidentiality is protected and where its limits are. [[NTCA2005]]
    • Infection control should not usually separate a parent from their newborn; fear-based practices deepen stigma and harm adherence. [[Maugans2023]]
