Treating drug-resistant TB

In ten years, treatment for MDR/RR-TB went from about two years with daily injections to six months of tablets. Step down the ladder of regimens and watch the duration shrink, cure rates rise and the harms change.

Safety, support and the gap

Monitoring

Heart, blood, nerves and eyes

  • Bedaquiline carries a boxed warning for QTc prolongation; the label requires an ECG, liver enzymes and electrolytes before starting, and stopping if QTc exceeds 500 ms or a significant arrhythmia develops [[FDA-Sirturo]].
  • ATS/CDC/ERS/IDSA list ECGs at baseline and at 2, 12 and 24 weeks on BPaL, and monthly when moxifloxacin or another QT-prolonging drug is added [[Saukkonen2025]].
  • Linezolid: weekly blood counts; peripheral and optic neuropathy, mainly beyond 28 days [[FDA-Zyvox]]. The dose is reduced or paused if they appear [[FDA-Pretomanid]].
  • WHO adds checking potassium, calcium and magnesium when QTc is prolonged, liver tests, and audiometry when certain agents are used [[WHO-Mod4-2025]].
Established

QT effects add up, but no more

In ACTG A5343, mean QTcF rose 12.3 ms with bedaquiline, 8.6 ms with delamanid and 20.7 ms with both, with no grade 3–4 QTc events: no more than additive [[Dooley2021LID]]. Delamanid added to background treatment did not significantly shorten time to culture conversion (51 vs 57 days) [[VonGroote2019]].

Counselling and support

Treatment is more than tablets

WHO recommends health education and counselling for everyone on TB treatment, and suggests material and psychological support, with MDR-TB treated mainly in ambulatory, decentralised care [[WHO-Mod4-2025]]. Depression affected 52.34% of people with MDR-TB in pooled studies [[Duko2020]]. In Uganda, nurse-led palliative care improved patient-reported outcomes for adults with MDR-TB [[Buyinza2025]]. See Living with TB.

The gap

Most people still do not get the best regimen

Only about 42% of the estimated 390 000 people with MDR/RR-TB were enrolled on treatment in 2024 (calculated from WHO figures), and 54% of those treated were still on 18–20-month regimens [[WHO-GTR2025]]. Second-line regimens cost about US$ 200–600 per person at Global Drug Facility prices, against about US$ 50 for first-line treatment, and 82% of affected households face catastrophic costs [[WHO-GTR2025]].

Contacts

Preventing MDR-TB in households

WHO recommends 6 months of daily levofloxacin for contacts exposed to MDR/RR-TB (strong, moderate certainty); pooled, it cut TB risk by 62% over a year. Neither VQUIN nor TB-CHAMP was significant on its own [[WHO-Mod1-2024,Fox2024VQUIN,Hesseling2024]]. More in Treating TB infection.

Controversy

Bedaquiline and deaths

C208 recorded 10 deaths with bedaquiline against 2 with placebo, with no causal pattern, and the US label still reports the imbalance (11.4% vs 2.5%) [[Diacon2014,FDA-Sirturo]]. Observational data link bedaquiline to lower mortality, and STREAM stage 2 found no excess deaths at 132 weeks [[Ahmad2018Lancet,Goodall2024]]. Most evidence now points away from harm.

Controversy

Same evidence, different strength

TB-PRACTECAL stopped early and its per-protocol population was small (90), giving a wide CI. WHO graded its BPaLM recommendation conditional, very low certainty; ATS/CDC/ERS/IDSA made a strong recommendation on the same evidence [[Nyangwa2022,WHO-Mod4-2025,Saukkonen2025]].

Early evidence

Pregnancy and drug-resistant TB

BPaLM excludes pregnancy and breastfeeding because pretomanid safety data are limited. BEAT enrolled pregnant and breastfeeding people and WHO extends BDLLfxC to them, but pregnancy safety data remain thin [[WHO-Mod4-2025,Conradie2026]]. See TB with HIV, in children & pregnancy.

In plain words. Drug-resistant TB can be cured with other medicines. Today most people can take tablets only, for 6 to 9 months, and the new regimens cured about nine in ten people in trials [[WHO-Mod4-2025,Nyangwa2022,Conradie2026]]. Tingling in the hands or feet, vision changes or tiredness from low blood counts should be reported early, because the linezolid dose can be adjusted [[Conradie2022,FDA-Pretomanid]]. Heart tracings and blood tests are routine safety checks, not a sign that something is wrong [[WHO-Mod4-2025,FDA-Sirturo]].
