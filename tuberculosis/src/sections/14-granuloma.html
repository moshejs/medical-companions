Part 1 · The infection

Infection to granuloma

One to five bacilli reach an air sac. What happens over the next weeks — a macrophage that swallows but cannot digest, a ten-day wait before T cells are primed, and a ball of cells built around the bacteria — decides whether a person stays well, and whether the lesion later breaks open. Scrub the whole sequence, then weaken the immune system and watch the granuloma loosen.

Inside the granuloma: pick a part

A granuloma is an organised aggregate of macrophages, often with characteristic changes in shape, together with other immune cells. It forms in response to a persistent stimulus that a single macrophage cannot clear, and it unfolds as a stepwise programme of macrophage activations and transformations that recruit further cells and change the structure. Select a part to light it up. [[Pagan2018]]

Primary and post-primary disease

Radiologists split TB into two patterns. Primary disease appears shortly after infection, is commonest in children and in people whose immunity is suppressed, and presents with enlarged lymph nodes, pulmonary consolidation and pleural effusion. Post-primary disease appears after a long period of infection and presents with cavities, consolidation and centrilobular nodules. Teaching texts attach eponyms to several of these lesions; the evidence pass behind this page could not verify that terminology from an indexed source, so the lesions are described here rather than named. [[Nachiappan2017]]

Container, or niche?

Established

It limits the infection

Granulomas form in response to persistent stimuli a single macrophage cannot eradicate, and the structure is the hallmark of TB. Interferon-gamma-deficient mice still form granulomas but cannot restrict bacillary growth, show heightened necrosis and die quickly; TNF-deficient mice form granulomas in equal numbers, but necrosis occurs only in them. [[Pagan2018,Flynn1993,Flynn1995]]

Established

It also shelters the bacteria

Intravital imaging in zebrafish showed that mycobacteria use the ESX-1/RD1 locus to recruit new macrophages into the nascent granuloma; arriving macrophages engulf infected macrophages that are dying, producing iterative expansion, and the primary granuloma then seeds secondary ones. Inside it the bacillus switches to a non-replicating but energy-generating state. [[Davis2009,Ramakrishnan2012,Ehlers2012]]

Heterogeneity

Every granuloma is its own experiment

In macaques most lung lesions appear to be founded by a single bacterium and reach similar maximum burdens, yet some are sterilised by the host while others progress — in the same animal, even in animals with active disease. On average only about 8% of granuloma T cells produce cytokine after stimulation, and blood responses did not reflect granuloma responses. [[Lin2014,Gideon2015]]

Architecture

Centre and rim differ

Laser-capture microdissection with mass spectrometry across six people with TB found a pro-inflammatory centre — antimicrobial peptides, reactive oxygen species, pro-inflammatory eicosanoids — and a comparatively anti-inflammatory rim around the caseum. Lesion types coexist in one lung and differ in cellular composition and immune activity. [[Marakalala2016,Dorhoi2016]]
