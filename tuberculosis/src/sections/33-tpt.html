Treating TB infection

TB preventive treatment (TPT) stops TB infection from turning into TB disease. Six regimens run side by side here in real weeks: from 28 daily doses to 9 months of daily isoniazid. Pick a group, then press Play.

Before, during and after

First step

Rule out TB disease first

The skin test and blood tests cannot tell TB infection from TB disease, so disease must be excluded before preventive treatment. [[Lewinsohn2017]] For HIV-negative household contacts aged 5 or over, WHO says no symptoms and a normal chest X-ray may be used to rule it out; for contacts under 15, a symptom screen (cough, fever or poor weight gain), a chest X-ray, or both. [[WHO-Mod1-2024]] In VQUIN, 61 of 3,948 people screened (1.5%) already had TB disease. [[Fox2024VQUIN]] TB disease needs full treatment, described in Treating TB disease.

MDR/RR-TB contacts

Levofloxacin for contacts of drug-resistant TB

WHO 2024 recommends 6 months of daily levofloxacin for contacts exposed to MDR/RR-TB (strong, moderate certainty). [[WHO-Mod1-2024]] Alone, neither trial was significant: in TB-CHAMP (922 children, 91% under 5) TB by week 48 was 1.1% vs 2.6% on placebo (HR 0.44); in VQUIN (2,041 household contacts) confirmed TB was 0.6% vs 1.1% (IRR 0.55). [[Hesseling2024,Fox2024VQUIN]] Pooled by WHO, risk fell 62% (RR 0.38). Completion was 86% in TB-CHAMP and 70% in VQUIN, and tolerability fell with age; any-grade adverse events were 31.9% vs 13.0% in VQUIN. [[WHO-Mod1-2024,Fox2024VQUIN]] See Treating drug-resistant TB.

What to expect

Side effects to report

  • 3HP: a flu-like reaction in 3.5% (vs 0.4% on 9H), starting after a median of 3 doses, about 4 hours after a dose, and settling in about a day. Severe reactions were 0.3%. [[Sterling2015]]
  • Liver: yellow eyes or skin, dark urine, stomach pain or loss of appetite should be reported at once. The isoniazid label's hepatitis rates rise with age, from under 1 per 1,000 under 20 to 23 per 1,000 at 50 to 64. [[FDA-Isoniazid]]
  • Rifamycins can stop hormonal contraception working; the labels advise considering another method. [[FDA-Rifampin,FDA-Priftin]]
  • Tingling or burning in the hands or feet: isoniazid can cause neuropathy, and vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) is often given with it. [[FDA-Isoniazid,Saukkonen2025]]
HIV

Rifamycins and antiretrovirals

Rifampicin induces CYP3A4 and P-glycoprotein; combining it with indinavir, nelfinavir or saquinavir is contraindicated, and the induction takes about 2 weeks to wear off after stopping. [[Niemi2003]] The Priftin label warns about rifapentine with protease inhibitors and some reverse transcriptase inhibitors. [[FDA-Priftin]] In DOLPHIN, weekly rifapentine-isoniazid raised dolutegravir clearance by 36%, yet viral load stayed below 40 copies/mL in all 60 participants; the authors concluded 3HP can be given with dolutegravir without dose adjustment. [[Dooley2020LancetHIV]] More in TB with HIV, in children & pregnancy.

Support

Watched, self-given, or with reminders

In iAdhere, 3HP completion was 87.2% with direct observation, 74.0% self-administered and 76.4% self-administered with text reminders; in the US subgroup self-administration was non-inferior. [[Belknap2017]] Observation is a form of support, not suspicion. In most programmes TB testing and treatment are free.

The gap

The evidence is ahead of the reach

Preventive treatment reached 5.3 million people in 2024, including 58% of eligible people living with HIV but only 25% of eligible household contacts. [[Zenner2027]] Contact investigation is high-yield because risk is early and concentrated: incidence among contacts peaks in the first year, and children under 5 and people with HIV are at highest risk. [[Fox2013]] See Infection control & contacts.

TB is a notifiable disease. Anyone with a cough for more than 2 weeks, fever, night sweats or weight loss should see a clinician. Decisions about preventive treatment are made with a clinician and the national TB programme.
