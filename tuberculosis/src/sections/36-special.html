The exception

TB meningitis: early ART did not help

In a Vietnamese trial of 253 people with HIV-associated TB meningitis, immediate ART did not reduce 9-month mortality (HR 1.12) and caused more grade 4 adverse events (102 vs 87) [[Torok2011]]. WHO therefore allows a delay of 4–8 weeks when there are signs of meningitis [[WHO-Mod4-2025]]. The trial was small and from before dolutegravir, so the question is not closed. For people with drug-resistant TB on second-line drugs, WHO recommends ART as early as possible, within the first 8 weeks [[WHO-Mod4-2025]].

Pregnancy, drugs and other conditions

Pregnancy and after birth

Risk peaks after delivery

An estimated 216 500 pregnant women had TB disease worldwide in 2011 [[Sugarman2014]]. In UK primary care, TB rates rose postpartum (adjusted IRR 1.95), not significantly during pregnancy (1.29) [[Zenner2012]]. In Sweden, rates were 9, 12 and 17 per 100 000 person-years outside pregnancy, during pregnancy and postpartum, with the excess concentrated in women from high-incidence countries [[Jonsson2020]].

Why treat

TB in pregnancy affects two people

TB disease in pregnancy was associated with maternal morbidity (OR 2.8), anaemia (3.9), preterm birth (1.7), low birth weight (1.7), birth asphyxia (4.6) and perinatal death (4.2) [[Sobhy2017]]. TB in pregnancy can be treated, and treating it protects both mother and baby [[Sobhy2017,WHO-Mod4-2025]].

Early evidence

Preventive treatment in pregnancy: TB APPRISE

In 956 pregnant women with HIV on ART, starting isoniazid preventive therapy in pregnancy was noninferior to waiting until 12 weeks postpartum for maternal toxicity (15.1% vs 15.2%), but adverse pregnancy outcomes were more common with antepartum isoniazid (23.6% vs 17.0%) [[Gupta2019NEJM]]. WHO still lists pregnant women among people with HIV for whom TPT is strongly recommended [[WHO-Mod1-2024]].

Drugs, as published

What applies in pregnancy and breastfeeding

  • BPaLM is not recommended in pregnancy or breastfeeding (limited pretomanid data); WHO's 6-month BDLLfxC covers pregnant and breastfeeding women [[WHO-Mod4-2025]].
  • The 4-month rifapentine–moxifloxacin regimen is not for pregnancy [[WHO-Mod4-2025]].
  • The rifampin label: use in the last weeks of pregnancy can cause postnatal bleeding in mother and infant, for which vitamin K may be indicated [[FDA-Rifampin]].
  • There is no absolute contraindication to breastfeeding during TB treatment, and a parent should not usually be separated from their newborn [[Maugans2023]].
Interactions

Rifamycins and HIV medicines

Dolutegravir is given twice daily with rifampicin: the label says so for INSTI-naive adults [[FDA-Tivicay]]. In INSPIRING, twice-daily dolutegravir with rifampicin-based TB treatment gave 75% viral suppression at 48 weeks, with TB-IRIS uncommon [[Dooley2020CID]]. With weekly rifapentine–isoniazid (3HP), DOLPHIN found no dose change was needed [[Dooley2020LancetHIV]]. Rifamycins can also weaken hormonal contraception [[FDA-Rifampin,Maugans2023]].

Diabetes

About three times the risk

Diabetes was associated with a roughly threefold risk of TB disease (RR 3.11) [[Jeon2008]]. During treatment it was linked to failure or death (RR 1.69), death (adjusted RR 4.95 in 4 studies) and relapse (RR 3.89), but not to drug-resistant recurrence [[Baker2011]]. Keeping blood sugar under control helps TB treatment work [[Baker2011]].

Children's medicines

Formulations made for children

WHO encourages dispersible fixed-dose combinations (HRZ 50/75/150 mg and HR 50/75 mg) for children up to 25 kg, and describes dispersible or small-strength bedaquiline (20 mg), delamanid (25 mg) and ethionamide (125 mg) for drug-resistant TB [[WHO-Mod5-2022]]. SHINE showed 4 months is enough for children with non-severe TB [[Turkova2022]]. Treatment success for drug-susceptible TB in children aged 0–14 was 92% (2023 cohort) [[WHO-GTR2025]].

The child gap

Most child deaths are in children never treated

An estimated 239 000 children died of TB in 2015, 80% under 5, and more than 96% were not receiving TB treatment [[Dodd2017]]. WHO estimated 174 000 TB deaths in children under 15 in 2024 [[WHO-GTR2025]]. For MDR/RR-TB, 72.0% of 23 369 children and adolescents were treated successfully [[GarciaPrats2025]].

In plain words. If you have HIV and TB, HIV medicines usually start within two weeks of TB medicines, and this saves lives [[WHO-Mod4-2025,Blanc2011]]. Young children who live with someone with TB should be checked quickly [[Martinez2020Lancet,Marais2004]]. TB during pregnancy can be treated, and you can usually keep breastfeeding [[Sobhy2017,Maugans2023]]. TB is a notifiable disease; anyone with a long cough, fever, night sweats or weight loss should see a clinician.
