How TB drugs work

TB bacteria live in three very different places inside a lung, and no single medicine reaches and kills all of them. Switch the four first-line drugs on and off and watch which bacteria die, which linger and when a lone drug lets resistant mutants take over.

Where each drug hits the bacillus

Tap a card to flip it. The bacillus on the right lights up the structure that drug attacks. Most targets sit in the thick, waxy envelope or in the machinery that copies genes and makes energy, which is also where resistance mutations arise (see Drug resistance).

Open questions

Established

A drug has to reach the bacteria

Drugs must cross three barriers in series: from blood into the lesion, through the waxy cell wall, then to the target inside. Imaging of human and animal lesions showed rifampicin and pyrazinamide reaching the lesions well, with rifampicin building up in caseum, while moxifloxacin diffused poorly into caseum. That matches its failure to shorten treatment in trials. [[Dartois2014,Prideaux2015,Gillespie2014]]

Early evidence

Caseum bacilli are the hardest to kill

In rabbit cavity caseum, bacilli were largely non-replicating and extremely tolerant of many drugs. Only the rifamycins fully sterilised caseum, and pyrazinamide killed there even though it shows no activity in the classic laboratory models of dormancy. [[Sarathy2018]]

Early evidence

Is the rifampicin dose too low?

Over 14 days, sputum bacterial load fell by 0.176 log10 CFU/mL a day at 10 mg/kg and by 0.265 at 30 mg/kg. Exposure rose more than in proportion to dose, and 35 mg/kg was well tolerated for 2 weeks. [[Boeree2015]]

Early evidence

Does ethambutol get in the way?

In 100 people given 22 different regimens, ethambutol's effect on days 2 to 14 ran opposite to rifampin's, which suggests it may blunt the sterilising activity of the other drugs. [[Jindani2003]]

Early evidence

Dosing isoniazid by genotype

In a Japanese trial of 172 people, 78% of slow acetylators on standard dosing had isoniazid liver injury, against none on 2.5 mg/kg. Early treatment failure in rapid acetylators fell from 38% to 15.0% at about 7.5 mg/kg. This is not in WHO or US guidance. [[Azuma2013]]

Established

Food works in opposite directions

A high-fat meal cut rifampin's peak level by 36% and delayed it (Tmax 2.42 to 4.43 h), while the total dose absorbed fell by only 6%. Rifapentine works the other way: food raised its levels by 40 to 50%. That is why Study 31 gave rifapentine after food and rifampin on an empty stomach. [[Peloquin1999,FDA-Priftin,Dorman2021]]
