TB Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on tuberculosis, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for patient and family understanding.

It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should change a test, treatment or isolation decision without your own clinician or TB programme. TB is a notifiable disease. Criteria, regimens, doses and guideline statements are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
