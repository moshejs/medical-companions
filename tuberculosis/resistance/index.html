Drug resistance

Resistance is not caused by the drugs: rare mutants are already in every large population of TB bacteria. Pick a scenario and watch what one drug, four drugs, or stopping early does to them, then how a resistant strain reaches someone new.

Why combinations work: the arithmetic

Each resistance mutation arises independently, so the chance that one bacillus resists two drugs at once is roughly the product of the two single-drug rates 110,121. Name the resistance: WHO 2021 categories

In 2021 WHO redefined pre-XDR-TB and XDR-TB around the drugs that matter most today. The older XDR definition was based on fluoroquinolone plus second-line injectable resistance, and trials that enrolled before 2021, such as Nix-TB and ZeNix, used it 86,498. Tick what a strain resists.

WHO Group A medicines: levofloxacin or moxifloxacin, bedaquiline and linezolid. Group B: clofazimine and cycloserine/terizidone. Group C includes ethambutol, delamanid, pyrazinamide, imipenem–cilastatin, meropenem and amikacin 515.

Which gene, which test

Most resistance comes from mutations in a handful of genes, so molecular tests can read resistance in hours or days instead of the weeks that growing the bacteria takes. In one implementation study the median time to a resistance result was 20 days by line probe assay against 106 days by conventional drug-susceptibility testing (DST) 45. Sensitivity / specificity are pooled estimates from the studies reviewed, not the chance that one person's result is right. WHO conditionally recommends tNGS on respiratory samples from people with rifampicin-resistant TB, covering isoniazid, fluoroquinolones, bedaquiline, linezolid, clofazimine, pyrazinamide, ethambutol, amikacin and streptomycin 514. Across all drugs, tNGS was 94.1% sensitive and 98.1% specific 416. Established The 2021 WHO mutation catalogue graded only two resistance mutations each for bedaquiline, delamanid, clofazimine and linezolid, so genotypic testing for the newest drugs is still incomplete 503. The third edition (October 2026), built from more than 71,000 isolates from over 75 countries, adds pretomanid and cycloserine and raised the sensitivity of the bedaquiline mutations from 47.5% to 55.0% at specificity 98.9%, or 66.3% when lower-frequency variants are counted. WHO says the catalogue is not a standalone clinical decision-support tool 518. More on these tests in Diagnosing TB disease.

The global picture

New MDR/RR-TB, 2024390 000

Estimated people who developed MDR/RR-TB in 2024 (95% UI 360 000–430 000), falling since 2015. MDR/RR-TB caused an estimated 150 000 deaths 510. About a quarter of deaths caused by antimicrobial resistance are due to rifampicin-resistant TB 114.

Who has it3.2% · 16%

Share of MDR/RR-TB among people never treated before (down from 4.7% in 2015) and among previously treated people (down from 19%). In new cases it ranged from 2.0% (African and Eastern Mediterranean regions) to 23% (European Region) 510.

Whereover half

Four countries had over half of incident MDR/RR-TB in 2024: India 32%, China 7.1%, the Philippines 7.1% and the Russian Federation 6.7%. An estimated 18% of MDR/RR-TB was pre-XDR, and 1.5 million people developed isoniazid-resistant TB 510.

The treatment gap≈ 42%

164 545 people were enrolled on MDR/RR-TB treatment in 2024, down from 179 491 in 2022: roughly 42% of the estimated 390 000 (calculated here from the two WHO figures). Ten countries made up 74% of the gap; India alone 33% 510.

Testing83% · 58%

In 2024, 83% of people with bacteriologically confirmed TB were tested for rifampicin resistance, but only 58% of people with rifampicin-resistant TB were tested for fluoroquinolone resistance 510.

Cost82%

Share of households affected by drug-resistant TB facing catastrophic costs, against 47% for all TB, even where treatment is free 510. Drug-resistant TB often uses more than 50% of national TB budgets while making up less than 5–10% of cases 121.

Resistance is curable, and it is not a fault. Most people with drug-resistant TB were infected with a strain that was already resistant 268,422. All-oral regimens of 6 to 9 months now cure most people in trials; see Treating drug-resistant TB. Contacts of people with MDR/RR-TB can be offered preventive treatment: WHO recommends 6 months of daily levofloxacin 512.
