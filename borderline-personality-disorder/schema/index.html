Part 3 · Psychotherapies

Schema therapy & TFP

Two long-term therapies with very different maps of the mind. Schema therapy pictures a cast of "modes" that take the stage in turn; transference-focused psychotherapy pictures a world split into all-good and all-bad. Switch views, scrub the timeline, then see how they did head to head.

The trigger

A text cancels the plans

A friend cancels plans by text an hour before. Young's schema-mode model proposes that people with BPD flip between four maladaptive modes: the abandoned/abused ("vulnerable") child, the angry/impulsive child, the punitive parent and the detached protector. 14 The healthy adult mode, on the right, starts small: it was lowest in BPD and highest in non-patients in two experimental studies. 14,313

In plain wordsSchema therapy describes "modes": different states, almost like different parts of you, that can take over in a moment of stress.
Exam hookBPD modes: detached protector, punitive parent, abandoned/abused child, angry/impulsive child; healthy adult is weak. 14

Head to head: schema therapy vs TFP

The Dutch trial randomised 88 patients at 4 community mental health centres to 3 years of schema-focused therapy or TFP, both twice a week. Both helped on all measures; more schema therapy patients recovered. 177 The 4-year recovery percentages come from the trial's cost-effectiveness paper. 542

Giesen-Bloo 2006 · 88 patients

Schema therapy recovered more people

After 3 years, more schema therapy patients had recovered (relative risk 2.18; P = .04) or reliably improved (relative risk 2.33; P = .009), and TFP carried a higher dropout risk (P = .01). 177 The cost-effectiveness paper reported 52% vs 29% recovered over 4 years, at lower mean cost with schema therapy. 542

In plain wordsIn this trial, about half the people in schema therapy had recovered after 4 years, compared with about 3 in 10 in TFP. Both groups improved.
Exam hookGiesen-Bloo 2006: schema therapy beat TFP on recovery (RR 2.18) and retention. Note: a commonly quoted "45.5% vs 23.8%" is not in the abstract; the checked figures are RR 2.18 and 52% vs 29% at 4 years. 177,542

Group schema therapy: the 2022 trial

495 outpatients at 15 sites in 5 countries were randomised to mainly group schema therapy (PGST), combined individual and group schema therapy (IGST) or optimal usual care, over 2 years. 15

Mainly-group schema therapy alone did not beat usual care: its interval crosses zero. Combined individual and group schema therapy did best. 15

Exam hook

Modes are states; splitting is a defence

Schema modes are the states a person with BPD flips between, measurable in experiments: a stress film raised the detached protector more in BPD than in either control group. 14 Splitting is a primitive defence in Kernberg's borderline personality organisation. 259 Do not mix up the two vocabularies.

Established

No clear winner overall

In the BOOTS trial, 204 outpatients had 2 years of DBT or schema therapy: no significant difference in BPD severity at 3 years (d 0.15; 95% CI −0.17 to 0.47), with large improvements in both; dropout at 2 years was 52% vs 46%. 553 Schema therapy beat TFP in one trial, but most head-to-head trials find no difference. See Therapies compared. 177,300

Early evidence

Schema therapy in an app

In 580 adults in Germany, an unguided schema-therapy-based app plus usual care improved BSL-23 scores more at 3 months (d 0.24) and was followed by fewer suicide attempts (7 vs 21), against usual care alone. One trial; it needs replication. 17

Cost

Cheaper and more effective

Over 4 years, mean costs were EUR 37,826 with schema therapy and EUR 46,795 with TFP, while more people recovered with schema therapy, so it was "dominant" for recovery. 542 Adding therapist phone availability outside office hours added nothing in regular care. 359

In plain words: both therapies are long and demanding, and both helped people in trials. Most people with BPD reach symptom remission over the years: by 10 years, 93% had a remission lasting at least 2 years in one long cohort. See Course & recovery. 207,588
