Research toward better care

New ideas for BPD care are being tested: earlier help for young people, shorter therapies, apps, brain stimulation and new medicines. Most are early. Here is how a treatment earns its place, and how to read the next headline.

Hype filter

Read the headline a news story might write, guess the evidence level, then see the study behind it. Established = consistent results from good trials or reviews; Early evidence = a few or small trials, mixed or untested results; Hypothesis = an idea, a lab finding or an open-label pilot.

Open questions and controversies

Open

Remission is common; recovery is not

In the long cohorts 85–99% reached remission and relapse was low, but recovery of social and vocational functioning stayed around 40–60%. The meta-analytic remission range is 50–70%. Functional recovery is the unsolved problem. [[Zanarini2012,Gunderson2011,AlvarezTomas2019Meta]]

Open

Does treatment change the long-term course?

Short-term trials show psychotherapy beats usual care, but a meta-analysis found no significant link between treatment moderators and long-term outcome, and the McLean cohort reflects usual treatment. Long-term treatment effects are unproven. [[Storebo2020,AlvarezTomas2019Meta,Zanarini2003Course]]

Open

Service or therapy brand?

In MOBY, youth-oriented case management carried the benefit, not specialist psychotherapy, and in HYPE cognitive analytic therapy and good clinical care were similar. Larger trials are pending. [[Chanen2022MOBY,Chanen2008HYPE,Kaess2025]]

Open

Why do BPD drug trials keep failing?

Large placebo responses and high dropout are common. The BI 1358894 authors suggest more targeted populations and new outcome measures. [[Grant2022Brexpiprazole,Rothman2024Brexpiprazole,Dwyer2025BI1358894]]

Open

How long must treatment last?

6 months of DBT matched 12 on self-harm and 5 months of MBT matched 14, but critics question the MBT trial's design and NICE still warns against BPD-specific interventions under 3 months outside a structured service. Which patients need longer care is unknown. [[McMain2022,Juul2023,Philips2024,NICE2009,Laporte2018Stepped]]

Open

Who was studied?

The McLean cohort recruited severely ill, mostly white, mostly female inpatients in 1992–1995 using DSM-III-R criteria. Community and youth samples may differ. [[Zanarini2012,Temes2019,Winograd2008,Tomko2014]]

Early evidence

Matching person to therapy

A model built on baseline traits matched 156 patients to DBT or GPM with a moderate advantage (d 0.36) when they had been randomised to their predicted best treatment, but it has not been tested prospectively. [[Keefe2021Selection]]

Plain words

New is not the same as proven

Brain stimulation, ketamine and psilocybin are still being tested and are not proven treatments for BPD. Talking therapies remain the main treatment, and no medicine is approved for BPD itself. [[Konstantinou2021rTMS,Fineberg2023Ketamine,Grant2026Psilocybin,StoffersWinterling2022]]
