Often both: co-occurring conditions

A differential is not always "either, or". In inpatients with BPD, most also had at least one other lifetime diagnosis; rates were lower in adolescents. When BPD remits, rates of co-occurring disorders fall substantially. [[Zanarini2021Comorbidity,Zanarini2004AxisI,Shah2018]]

Lifetime rates in inpatients with BPD, as published. Adult PTSD is printed as 53.3% although the table's count (169 of 290) would give 58.3%. [[Zanarini2021Comorbidity]]

Bipolar

About 1 in 5, each way

BPD was found in 21.6% of people with bipolar disorder (37.7% in bipolar II), and bipolar disorder in 18.5% of people with BPD. [[Fornaro2016]] Yet in 80–90% of cases each occurs without the other, and depression, substance use and PTSD are all more common in BPD than bipolar disorder is. [[Zimmerman2013DCN]]

PTSD

Common, not universal

In the US population, 30.2% of people with BPD also had PTSD and 24.2% of people with PTSD had BPD; having both meant poorer quality of life and more childhood trauma than either alone. [[Pagura2010PTSD]] PTSD being common but not universal argues against seeing BPD as simply chronic PTSD. [[Zanarini1998AxisI]] Latent class analysis separated PTSD, complex PTSD and BPD classes. [[Cloitre2014]]

ADHD

An impulsive overlap

In 118 women seeking treatment for BPD, 41.5% had childhood ADHD and 16.1% adult ADHD. [[Philipsen2008]] In another adult sample, 38.1% had adult ADHD; that group had more substance use disorders (59.4% vs 38.4%) and fewer mood disorders. [[Ferrer2010]] Among adults with ADHD, 21.9% had BPD, with very high heterogeneity between studies. [[Adamis2026]]

Autism

Autistic women and BPD

Pooled overlap is low: BPD in 4% of autistic people and autism in 3% of people with BPD, within population estimates. [[May2021]] In women, identity disruption was much higher in BPD and sensory processing differences favoured autism; five features together classified 95.1% correctly. [[Barnicot2026]] Late-diagnosed autistic women may be misread as having BPD, so a full developmental history is advised. Hypothesis [[McQuaid2024]]

Voices

Hearing voices is not schizophrenia

Hallucinations were present in 43% of 324 outpatients with BPD, mostly distressing voices. Their severity tracked delusions and unusual thoughts but not negative symptoms or disorganisation, which helps separate them from primary psychotic disorders. [[Niemantsverdriet2017]]

Harms

Misdiagnosis cuts both ways

Calling BPD bipolar disorder can deprive a person of effective psychotherapy; calling bipolar disorder BPD can deprive them of effective medication. [[Paris2015]] Of 82 outpatients whose earlier bipolar diagnosis was not confirmed, 24.4% had BPD, against 6.1% of those never so diagnosed. [[Zimmerman2010JCP]] Missing BPD leads to drug treatment that rarely succeeds. [[Gunderson2018]]

Exam hooks

Remember

  • Hours (BPD) versus days to weeks (bipolar). [[Paris2015,Kaess2025]]
  • Abandonment fears and identity disturbance best separate BPD from bipolar disorder. [[Bayes2020]]
  • Self-concept: alternating in BPD, stably negative in complex PTSD. [[Frost2020]]
  • "Complex comorbidity", a disorder of affect plus a disorder of impulse, is a strong marker of BPD. [[Zanarini1998AxisI]]
  • About half of people with BPD have a current substance use disorder. [[Trull2018]]
