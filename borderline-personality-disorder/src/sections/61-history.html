History & people

In about 85 years, "borderline" went from a loose psychoanalytic word for patients between neurosis and psychosis to a defined diagnosis with specific psychotherapies and long-term studies showing that most people recover. [[Gunderson2009Ontogeny,Bohus2021]]

The people

Adolph Stern

In 1938 described the "border line group of neuroses", patients who seemed to sit between neurosis and psychosis. [[Stern1938]]

Robert Knight

Published "Borderline states" in 1953; he and Stern are named as the psychiatrists most responsible for introducing the label. [[Knight1953,Gunderson2009Ontogeny]]

Otto Kernberg

In 1967 defined "borderline personality organization": primitive defences such as splitting, identity diffusion and lapses in reality testing. [[Kernberg1967,Gunderson2009Ontogeny]]

John Gunderson

With Singer proposed six features for diagnosis at a first interview (1975), then published the Diagnostic Interview for Borderline Patients (1981); he was first author of the CLPS 10-year report. [[Gunderson1975,Gunderson1981,Gunderson2011]]

Marsha Linehan

Published the first randomised trial of dialectical behaviour therapy in 1991: fewer and less severe self-harm acts and fewer inpatient days than usual treatment. [[Linehan1991]] More in DBT.

Anthony Bateman & Peter Fonagy

Their 1999 trial of psychoanalytically oriented partial hospitalisation cut suicidal acts, self-harm and inpatient days; it was the forerunner of mentalization-based treatment. [[Bateman1999]] More in MBT.

Mary Zanarini

Led the McLean Study of Adult Development from 1992, whose remission findings began the "recovery era". [[Zanarini2003Course,Temes2019]] More in Course & recovery.

Should the name change?

Keep it dimensional

The ICD-11 working group

Members argued that only a dimensional system fits the evidence and that the borderline pattern "adds little": its features are already captured by severity and the trait domains. [[Tyrer2019,Mulder2020]] ICD-11's shift was called "the most radical change in the classification history of personality disorders". [[Tyrer2019]]

Keep the word

Clinicians and societies

After lengthy debate, clinicians and personality-disorder societies won an optional borderline pattern in ICD-11, and DSM-5-TR keeps the category. [[Tyrer2019,APA2022]] The treatment trials and recovery studies were built on the BPD diagnosis. [[Linehan1991,Zanarini2012]]

The label itself

Does the word carry stigma?

The personality-disorder label alone shifted psychiatrists' judgements in 1988, and the BPD label drew less sympathy from nurses than other labels for the same behaviour. [[Lewis1988,Markham2003]] Messages pairing recovery with biological causes may reduce stigma most.
