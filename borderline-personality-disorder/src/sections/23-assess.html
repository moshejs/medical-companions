Telling someone the diagnosis

Do

Share it well

  • Name the diagnosis. Withholding a psychiatric diagnosis to protect the patient is now generally seen as unethical, and non-disclosure harms integrated care. [[Sims2022]]
  • Explain it, then talk about treatments together: disclosure opens the way to psychoeducation and to specific, effective therapies. [[Lequesne2004]]
  • Offer hope. NICE asks clinicians to explore options "in an atmosphere of hope and optimism, explaining that recovery is possible and attainable". [[NICE2009]]
  • Follow with psychoeducation: a workshop within a week of disclosure reduced impulsivity and relationship "storminess" over 12 weeks. Early evidence [[Zanarini2008Psychoed]]
Avoid

What not to do

  • Do not keep it secret. In a survey of 134 US psychiatrists, 57% had at some point not disclosed a BPD diagnosis and 37% had not documented it, mostly because of stigma or uncertainty. [[Sisti2016]]
  • Do not use the diagnosis to exclude. People with BPD should not be excluded from any service because of their diagnosis or because they have self-harmed. [[NICE2009]]
  • Do not miss it. A missed diagnosis leads to misleading drug treatment that rarely succeeds. [[Gunderson2018]]
Reliability

Interviews are reliable

The DIB-R reached kappas above 0.75 for the diagnosis. [[Zanarini2002DIBR]] The IPDE performed about as reliably as interviews for psychosis and mood disorders in a WHO trial in 11 countries. [[Loranger1994]] Self-report gives higher endorsement rates, and using interview and self-report together identified BPD criteria best. [[Hopwood2008]]

Exam hook

Screening is not diagnosis

A screen flags, an interview diagnoses. Self-report questionnaires are for screening or rating severity, and a structured or semi-structured interview is for the diagnosis. [[Kaess2025,Leichsenring2024WP]] Screening "is not a replacement for clinical diagnosis". [[Chanen2008]] With sensitivity 0.81 and specificity 0.85, the PPV of an MSI-BPD score of 7 or more would be about 5% at 1% prevalence and about 57% at 20%. [[Zanarini2003MSI,Ellison2018]]

Recovery

The message behind the hope

In 290 people with severe BPD followed prospectively, 34.5% were in remission at 2 years, 49.4% at 4 years and 68.6% at 6 years; only 5.9% of remissions were followed by recurrence. [[Zanarini2003Course]] These are population statistics from one inpatient cohort.

Is it BPD, or something else?

Mood episodes in BPD usually last hours, rarely more than a few days; sustained episodes point to bipolar disorder. [[Kaess2025,Paris2015]] Abandonment fears, an unstable self, unstable relationships and impulsiveness separate BPD from complex PTSD. [[Cloitre2014]]
