Three ways mentalizing goes offline

The MBT literature describes three "pre-mentalizing" modes. Everyone uses them sometimes, especially when upset; the model proposes that in BPD they take over faster and more often under attachment stress. Hypothesis [[Chung2026,FonagyLuyten2009]]

ModeDefinition, as publishedHow it can sound (illustrative)
Psychic equivalenceTreating inner experience as if it were outer reality. [[Chung2026]]"I feel rejected, so you have rejected me."
Teleological thinkingFocusing on physical, goal-directed actions as the only proof of what someone feels or intends. [[Chung2026]]"If you cared, you would come over right now."
Pretend modeAppearing to mentalize while cut off from real inner states and reality. [[Chung2026]]Long, fluent talk about feelings that changes nothing and feels empty.
Mentalizing"The capacity to understand others and oneself in terms of internal mental states", along four dimensions: automatic–controlled, internal–external focus, self–other, cognitive–affective. [[Luyten2020]]"You seem quiet. Was it a hard day, or is it something between us?"

In a Korean sample of 90 people with BPD and 47 controls, psychic equivalence was linked both to having the diagnosis and to symptom severity. Early evidence [[Chung2026]]

How MBT is delivered

Structure

Individual plus group, over months

The first trial used psychoanalytically oriented partial hospitalisation with individual and group psychotherapy for up to 18 months. [[Bateman1999]] The 2009 outpatient trial ran for 18 months. [[Bateman2009]] A Danish trial gave 2 years of twice-weekly combined MBT. [[Jorgensen2013MBT]]

MBT is "a time-limited treatment which structures interventions that promote the further development of mentalizing", and worked when delivered by mental health professionals with limited extra training and moderate supervision. [[Bateman2010WP]]

Stance

Too much guessing about minds

People with BPD traits tend to hypermentalize, making excessive, inaccurate guesses about others' mental states, and in adolescent inpatients emotion-regulation difficulties accounted for 43.5% of the hypermentalizing-to-BPD-traits path. Early evidence [[Sharp2011]] Across 36 studies, hypermentalizing was linked to psychopathology in general (r 0.24), not more to BPD (r 0.26). [[McLaren2022]]

Young people

MBT-A and MBT in groups

In 80 adolescents who self-harmed and were depressed, MBT-A reduced self-harm and depression more than usual treatment over 12 months; the benefit was explained by better mentalizing and less attachment avoidance. [[Rossouw2012MBTA]] Group MBT for 112 adolescents did not beat usual care: 29% remitted in both groups, and 29% in MBT-G attended fewer than half their sessions (7% of controls). [[Beck2020MBTG]]

Early evidence

How long does it need to be?

In 166 outpatients, 5 months of MBT matched 14 months on BPD symptoms at 16 months (mean difference 0.99; 95% CI −1.06 to 3.03) [[Juul2023]] and at 24 months (−0.56; −2.67 to 1.54); the longer group had better quality of life, but 40% of that data was missing. [[Juul2025]] Other researchers argue the trial was flawed in design. [[Philips2024]]

Certainty

What the pooled evidence says

The 2020 Cochrane review found MBT reduced self-harm (RR 0.62; 95% CI 0.49–0.80; 3 trials) and suicidality (RR 0.10; 0.04–0.30; 3 trials) against usual care, all low-certainty evidence. [[Storebo2020]] A 2022 focused meta-analysis gave RR 0.51 for self-harm and 0.10 for suicide-related outcomes for standalone MBT, again low certainty. [[StoffersWinterling2022BJP]] No therapy has been shown superior to another: see Therapies compared. [[Leichsenring2024]]

Exam hook

Name the mode

  • Psychic equivalence: inner = outer. "I feel it, so it is true."
  • Teleological: only actions count as proof.
  • Pretend: mentalizing talk cut off from real feeling.

Bateman 2008: 8 years after entry, 13% vs 87% still met BPD criteria after MBT partial hospitalisation vs usual care. [[Chung2026,Bateman2008]]

Where the model comes from: the developmental mentalizing model links early attachment and later stress to unstable mentalizing; it is covered in Causes & the biosocial model and Brain & stress systems. Most people with BPD reach symptom remission over time, and treatment can speed it up: see Course & recovery. [[Gunderson2018]]
