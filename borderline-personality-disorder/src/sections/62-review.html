Every card below is tagged memory aid: a short phrase written for this guide to hold a published fact in mind. None of them is an official grouping from DSM-5-TR, ICD-11 or a treatment manual, and none replaces the source.

Mnemonic in motion: R-S-A-I + 9

DSM-5-TR describes BPD as a pervasive pattern of instability in relationships, self-image and affects, with marked impulsivity [[APA2022,Kaess2025]]. Play the animation to sort the nine criteria under those four words, plus a ninth for stress, then see why "5 of 9" gives 256 different ways to meet the diagnosis and where self-harm is, and is not, counted.

Criterion wording shortened for study. The five-row grouping is a memory aid, not a DSM-5-TR category. [[APA2022,Kaess2025,Mulay2019]]

These cards are for learning, not for diagnosing yourself or anyone else. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123) [[SAMHSA988,Samaritans116123]].

Sources

Every reference behind the BPD Companion: journal articles, Cochrane reviews, guidelines, drug labels, trial registrations and crisis-line pages. The small numbers throughout the app point here; filter by research area or search.

Eight research passes ran in parallel, one per area: epidemiology, presentation, stigma and history; causes, genes and the biosocial model; emotion dysregulation, neurobiology and mentalizing; diagnosis and assessment; the psychological treatments; medication evidence and suicide and self-harm safety; course, comorbidity, recovery and research; and living, families and nursing care. Before inclusion each source was checked, where possible, against PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov or openFDA, and the green tag on an entry names the service that confirmed it. Works found by more than one pass appear once, with any extra keys listed beside them.

Entries marked check are pages with no PMID or DOI, or works that could not be confirmed. They are used only for what they state, so treat them with extra care.
