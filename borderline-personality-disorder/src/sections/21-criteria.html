Try it: the criteria counter

A teaching tool, not a self-test. Ticking tiles here cannot tell anyone whether they have BPD. In practice each criterion has to be pervasive and long-standing, and a clinician confirms it in a semi-structured interview, which is how BPD is diagnosed reliably. [[Leichsenring2024WP,Kaess2025]] Tap a tile to count it and read its full wording; then work through the general personality disorder criteria.

Reading the criteria well

Pervasive

What counts as pervasive

A criterion counts only when it belongs to an enduring, inflexible pattern that appears across many personal and social situations, causes distress or impairment, and dates back to adolescence or early adulthood. A stormy month after a break-up is not a personality pattern. [[APA2022]] ICD-11 states the duration explicitly: more than 2 years. See ICD-11 & dimensional models. [[Bach2018BMC]]

Adolescence

Teenagers can be diagnosed

DSM-5-TR uses the same nine criteria for adolescents; under 18, the features must have been present for at least 1 year. Evidence supports diagnosis from age 12, the diagnosis is as reliable and valid as in adults, and an expert review found "no justification for failing to make an early diagnosis". [[Kaess2025,Kaess2014Adolescence,Fonagy2015]] Among help-seeking 12–17-year-olds, most (86.8%) fitted a broad "borderline" profile and 13.2% an "impulsive" profile high only on impulsivity and anger. [[Cavelti2021]]

Judgement

The clinician's job, not the checklist's

Counting is the easy part. A clinician asks whether each feature is pervasive and long-standing, rules out other explanations (see BPD or something else?), and combines interview and self-report, which together identify criteria best. [[Hopwood2008]] Missing the diagnosis can lead to drug treatment that rarely helps, so clinicians are urged to ask actively about relationships and identity. [[Gunderson2018]]

Below 5

Fewer than five still matters

In 1,976 outpatients, people with a single BPD criterion had more co-occurring disorders, hospital admissions and time off work than people with none. [[Zimmerman2012JCP]] Chronic emptiness on its own marked impairment on every index of psychosocial functioning, [[Ellison2016]] and in youth the number of criteria predicted poor functioning even below the threshold. [[Thompson2020]]

Recovery

A diagnosis describes now, not forever

In a cohort of 290 people with severe BPD, 68.6% were in remission by 6 years and only 5.9% of remissions were followed by recurrence; by 16 years, 78% to 99% had reached sustained remission. These are population figures from one inpatient cohort. [[Zanarini2003Course,Zanarini2012]] More in Course & recovery.
