ICD-11 & dimensional models

ICD-11 no longer sorts people into named personality disorders. It asks how severe the difficulties are, then describes their style, and keeps "borderline" only as an optional pattern. Scrub from the old boxes to the new dimensions, and compare with the DSM's Alternative Model.

Why it changed, and what it means

Why

No clear line between types

The ICD-11 rationale: there is no clear threshold between types and degrees of personality dysfunction, so pathology is best placed on one dimension from normal personality to severe disorder. [[Tyrer2015Lancet]] Its authors called it "the most radical change in the classification history of personality disorders". [[Tyrer2019]] In a bifactor analysis of 966 inpatients, BPD criteria loaded only on a general personality-pathology factor. Early evidence [[Sharp2015JAP]]

Records

What it means for a person's notes

ICD-11 has been used for health reporting since 1 January 2022. [[Reed2019]] A full ICD-11 description can combine every layer, for example "Moderate personality disorder, with borderline pattern, with Negative Affectivity, Disinhibition and Dissociality". [[Bach2018BMC]] ICD-11 also says personality disorders are only "relatively" stable after young adulthood and may lessen by middle age. [[Bach2018BMC]] NICE CG78 is under review because of the change. [[NICE2009]]

Debate

Keep "borderline" or drop it?

Some argue BPD is a spurious category that should give way to severity plus traits, [[Mulder2023]] and that the borderline pattern is redundant because severity and three domains already capture it. [[Mulder2020]] Others point out that BPD is diagnosed reliably, its criteria form a coherent construct, and it links to effective specific treatments. [[Leichsenring2024WP,Sanislow2002]] After lengthy debate, ICD-11 added the pattern as an optional descriptor. [[Tyrer2019]] Status: unresolved.

Two systems

DSM-5-TR still has the category

The DSM-5 work group proposed the Alternative Model and the Task Force endorsed it, but the APA Board placed it in Section III. [[Morey2013]] So DSM-5-TR runs two systems in parallel: the nine-criterion category in Section II, and the AMPD, which aligns closely with ICD-11. [[Mulder2021]] The ICD-11 and DSM-5 trait models share four domains; ICD-11 adds Anankastia, DSM-5 adds Psychoticism. [[Bach2018ANZJP]]

Exam hooks

Remember

  • ICD-11: severity first (6D10.0–.2), then domains (6D11.0–.4), then the optional borderline pattern (6D11.5). [[Sharan2023]]
  • Harm to self or others: typically not (mild), sometimes (moderate), often (severe). [[Bach2018BMC]]
  • No psychoticism domain in ICD-11: schizotypal features sit with the psychotic disorders. [[Bach2018BMC]]
  • The ICD-11 domains can be scored from 16 PID-5 facets, replicated in Danish and US samples. [[Bach2017]]
