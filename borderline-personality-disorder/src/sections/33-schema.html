Part 3 · Psychotherapies

Schema therapy & TFP

Two long-term therapies with very different maps of the mind. Schema therapy pictures a cast of "modes" that take the stage in turn; transference-focused psychotherapy pictures a world split into all-good and all-bad. Switch views, scrub the timeline, then see how they did head to head.

View

Head to head: schema therapy vs TFP

The Dutch trial randomised 88 patients at 4 community mental health centres to 3 years of schema-focused therapy or TFP, both twice a week. Both helped on all measures; more schema therapy patients recovered. [[GiesenBloo2006]] The 4-year recovery percentages come from the trial's cost-effectiveness paper. [[vanAsselt2008]]

Endpoints published, shape drawn. The circled 4-year values are from van Asselt 2008; the curves between entry and year 4 are a schematic rise, not measured data. Group averages from one trial, not predictions for anyone. [[vanAsselt2008,GiesenBloo2006]]

Group schema therapy: the 2022 trial

495 outpatients at 15 sites in 5 countries were randomised to mainly group schema therapy (PGST), combined individual and group schema therapy (IGST) or optimal usual care, over 2 years. [[Arntz2022GST]]

Exam hook

Modes are states; splitting is a defence

Schema modes are the states a person with BPD flips between, measurable in experiments: a stress film raised the detached protector more in BPD than in either control group. [[Arntz2005Modes]] Splitting is a primitive defence in Kernberg's borderline personality organisation. [[Kernberg1967]] Do not mix up the two vocabularies.

Exam hook

The four BPD modes

  • Punitive parent
  • Abandoned/abused (vulnerable) child
  • Angry/impulsive child

Healthy adult: low in BPD, high in non-patients. [[Arntz2005Modes,Lobbestael2005]]

Established

No clear winner overall

In the BOOTS trial, 204 outpatients had 2 years of DBT or schema therapy: no significant difference in BPD severity at 3 years (d 0.15; 95% CI −0.17 to 0.47), with large improvements in both; dropout at 2 years was 52% vs 46%. [[Wibbelink2026BOOTS]] Schema therapy beat TFP in one trial, but most head-to-head trials find no difference. See Therapies compared. [[GiesenBloo2006,Leichsenring2024]]

Early evidence

Schema therapy in an app

In 580 adults in Germany, an unguided schema-therapy-based app plus usual care improved BSL-23 scores more at 3 months (d 0.24) and was followed by fewer suicide attempts (7 vs 21), against usual care alone. One trial; it needs replication. [[Assmann2025]]

Cost

Cheaper and more effective

Over 4 years, mean costs were EUR 37,826 with schema therapy and EUR 46,795 with TFP, while more people recovered with schema therapy, so it was "dominant" for recovery. [[vanAsselt2008]] Adding therapist phone availability outside office hours added nothing in regular care. [[Nadort2009]]

In plain words: both therapies are long and demanding, and both helped people in trials. Most people with BPD reach symptom remission over the years: by 10 years, 93% had a remission lasting at least 2 years in one long cohort. See Course & recovery. [[Gunderson2018,Zanarini2010Recovery]]
