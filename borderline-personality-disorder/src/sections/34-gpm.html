Good psychiatric management

Most people with BPD will never see a specialist DBT or MBT team. Good psychiatric management (GPM) is a generalist approach built for ordinary services, and stepped care adds more only when it is needed. Follow one person up and down the ladder over two years.

Why generalist care matters

Access

Most people never reach a specialist

Fewer than 25% of patients with BPD receive guideline-adherent psychotherapy, according to the authors of a digital-therapeutic trial. [[Assmann2025]] Reviews describe a move toward generalist care models and "pared down" forms of intensive therapies, for example case management plus DBT skills groups. [[ChoiKain2017]]

Hypothesis

GPM as the first step

GPM is "a once-weekly, generalist model that medicalizes the disorder, emphasizes psychoeducation, and focuses on social adaptation". Its authors propose it as the primary intervention, with more intensive therapies kept for people who do not respond. [[Gunderson2018GPM]]

Early evidence

GPM for adolescents (GPM-A)

A one-day GPM-A workshop for 32 child and adolescent psychiatry staff improved willingness to disclose a BPD diagnosis, reduced negative attitudes and increased confidence, with effects lasting 6 months. It was uncontrolled. [[Usher2024GPMA]] In young people, the MOBY trial found that youth-oriented case management and psychiatric care, not necessarily specialist psychotherapy, carried the benefit. [[Chanen2022MOBY]]

Good enough

What "good enough" means

In the treatment-as-usual arms of 16 trials, BPD symptoms improved by a small-to-moderate amount (g 0.371) but self-harm and suicidality did not change (g 0.003). [[Finch2019TAU]] Structured generalist care is different: GPM matched DBT on every outcome. [[McMain2009]] On this evidence, "good enough" care is structured generalist care, not simply whatever is usual.

Disclosure

Say the diagnosis

APA 2024 recommends (1C) "a collaborative discussion about their diagnosis and treatment, which includes psychoeducation related to the disorder". [[APA2024Guideline]] In a survey of 134 US psychiatrists, 57% had at some point not disclosed a BPD diagnosis. [[Sisti2016]] Withholding a psychiatric diagnosis to protect the patient is now generally seen as unethical. [[Sims2022]]

Exam hook

DBT = GPM

McMain 2009: 180 people, 1 year of DBT vs general psychiatric management: "No significant differences across any outcomes were found between groups", and gains held 2 years later. [[McMain2009,McMain2012]] STEPPS is a 20-week add-on group with a "systems" component involving the person's support network. [[Blum2008STEPPS]]

Functional recovery lags behind symptom remission: even after successful treatment, many people remain out of work or study. [[McMain2012,Bateman2008]] GPM focuses on social adaptation for this reason. [[Gunderson2018GPM]] See also Suicide & self-harm safety, Dialectical behaviour therapy and Therapies compared.
