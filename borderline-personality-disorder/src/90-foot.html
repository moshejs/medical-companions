BPD Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on borderline personality disorder, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people with BPD, their families and carers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: ADHD Companion, a guide to a condition that often co-occurs with BPD, and Autism Companion, and Bipolar Companion, on a mood disorder that is often part of the differential diagnosis, and the Depression Companion, on major depressive disorder, which often occurs alongside BPD.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
