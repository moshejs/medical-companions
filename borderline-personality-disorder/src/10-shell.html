BPD Companion · animated and cited

Borderline personality disorder, explained

Borderline personality disorder (BPD) affects about 1 to 3 adults in 100 at some point in life, and about 1 in 5 people in psychiatric hospitals. [[Ellison2018,Tomko2014]] It is treatable: psychotherapy reduces symptoms, and in the longest follow-up study 99% of people had a remission lasting at least 2 years by year 16. [[Storebo2020,Zanarini2012]] Scrub the animations, check every number against its source, and use the plain-words and exam-hook boxes to learn at your own level.

Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Need help now?If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123).

For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. The criteria and assessment tools are teaching aids, not self-tests. Finish with flashcards, mnemonics and a quiz.

For people with BPD, families and carers

Look for the In plain words boxes. BPD is treatable and many people improve a great deal over time. Bring questions to your own clinician: the app reports what guidelines and trials say, not what you should do.

How sure is each claim?
