Research toward better care

New ideas for BPD care are being tested: earlier help for young people, shorter therapies, apps, brain stimulation and new medicines. Most are early. Here is how a treatment earns its place, and how to read the next headline.

Into a guideline

NICE CG78: "For women with borderline personality disorder for whom reducing recurrent self-harm is a priority, consider a comprehensive dialectical behaviour therapy programme." Note how narrow the recommendation is: who (women), what goal (recurrent self-harm), what form (comprehensive). The same year, DBT and general psychiatric management gave equal outcomes. Established for this use. 338,379

Hype filter

Read the headline a news story might write, guess the evidence level, then see the study behind it. Established = consistent results from good trials or reviews; Early evidence = a few or small trials, mixed or untested results; Hypothesis = an idea, a lab finding or an open-label pilot.

A headline might sayMagnetic brain stimulation calms BPD

Sham-controlled trials so far are negative or underpowered: iTBS added to DBT had no significant effect, and 10-Hz rTMS did not beat sham in 30 women. A review of 8 small studies found efficacy not established. 231,278,287

A headline might saySix months of DBT works as well as a full year

A non-inferiority RCT of 240 people found 6 months non-inferior to 12 for self-harm up to 24 months. One large, well-designed trial: established for this question, though which people need longer care is unknown. 341

A headline might sayKetamine: a fast fix for BPD?

The only RCT (22 people, terminated early) found no significant difference from midazolam on suicidal ideation or depression. A review advises caution, citing reports of self-harm after stopping and possibly more dissociation. 157,467

A headline might sayThe ‘love hormone’ could fix trust in BPD

Single doses changed threat processing and empathy in the lab, but one study found oxytocin decreased trust and cooperation in anxiously attached, rejection-sensitive people. No clinical trial has shown a benefit. 32,50,123

A headline might sayAn app cut suicide attempts in BPD

In 580 adults, an unguided app plus usual care gave a small benefit (d 0.24) and fewer suicide attempts (7 vs 21). An earlier trial of the same app missed its primary outcome. Needs replication. 17,265

A headline might sayTalking therapy helps people with BPD

75 randomised trials, 4507 participants: BPD-tailored psychotherapy reduced BPD severity vs usual care (SMD −0.52), with moderate certainty and a clinically relevant size. 514

A headline might sayPsychedelic therapy for BPD

An open-label, single-dose psilocybin study in 9 adults with BPD and depression: depression fell, BPD scores did not change significantly. No control group. A phase 1 trial with DBT has not started. 186,375

A headline might sayA computer model picks your best therapy

A model matched 156 past patients to DBT or GPM with a moderate advantage, but only in a re-analysis; it has not been tested in new patients. 255

Open questions and controversies

Open

Remission is common; recovery is not

In the long cohorts 85–99% reached remission and relapse was low, but recovery of social and vocational functioning stayed around 40–60%. The meta-analytic remission range is 50–70%. Functional recovery is the unsolved problem. 5,204,592

Open

Does treatment change the long-term course?

Short-term trials show psychotherapy beats usual care, but a meta-analysis found no significant link between treatment moderators and long-term outcome, and the McLean cohort reflects usual treatment. Long-term treatment effects are unproven. 5,514,574

Open

Service or therapy brand?

In MOBY, youth-oriented case management carried the benefit, not specialist psychotherapy, and in HYPE cognitive analytic therapy and good clinical care were similar. Larger trials are pending. 91,97,251

Open

Why do BPD drug trials keep failing?

Large placebo responses and high dropout are common. The BI 1358894 authors suggest more targeted populations and new outcome measures. 137,185,439

Open

How long must treatment last?

6 months of DBT matched 12 on self-harm and 5 months of MBT matched 14, but critics question the MBT trial's design and NICE still warns against BPD-specific interventions under 3 months outside a structured service. Which patients need longer care is unknown. 246,294,341,379,412

Open

Who was studied?

The McLean cohort recruited severely ill, mostly white, mostly female inpatients in 1992–1995 using DSM-III-R criteria. Community and youth samples may differ. 523,528,556,592

Early evidence

Matching person to therapy

A model built on baseline traits matched 156 patients to DBT or GPM with a moderate advantage (d 0.36) when they had been randomised to their predicted best treatment, but it has not been tested prospectively. 255

Plain words

New is not the same as proven

Brain stimulation, ketamine and psilocybin are still being tested and are not proven treatments for BPD. Talking therapies remain the main treatment, and no medicine is approved for BPD itself. 157,186,278,512
