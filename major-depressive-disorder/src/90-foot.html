Depression Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on major depressive disorder, made for health-science, medical and nursing courses and for people living with depression, their families and caregivers.

%t:foot.not_advice%

Part of Medical Companions, animated and cited guides to one condition each. Also there: the Bipolar Companion, because depression that alternates with mania is a different condition and is treated differently, the BPD Companion, on a condition in which low mood is common and which can occur alongside depression, the AUD Companion, on alcohol use disorder, which often occurs together with depression, the ADHD Companion, on a condition that often comes with depression, and the Heart Attack Companion, because depression is common after a heart attack.

Edited by Moshe Malka, who is not a clinician. These guides are written with AI from published sources and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician. How they are made · Privacy
