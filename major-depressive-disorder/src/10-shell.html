Every animation can be driven from the keyboard: Tab to its timeline, then arrow keys to scrub and Enter to play or pause.

Schematic, not to scale.

In crisis?If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland Samaritans 116 123).

A common, treatable illnessDepression is an illness with biological, psychological and social causes, not weakness, laziness or a flaw of character. With treatment and support, most people recover. This guide uses person-first language: people with depression, a person living with depression.

For students

Every module has an Exam hook box and a citation trail. The PHQ-9, the EPDS and the criteria counter are teaching aids that show how clinicians score them, worked exactly as published, not self-tests. Finish with flashcards, memory aids and a quiz.

For people living with depression, families and caregivers

Look for the In plain words boxes. The scales here are teaching aids, not self-tests, and trial results are averages for groups, not predictions for one person. The guide reports what guidelines, trials and drug labels say, not what you should take, stop or change: talk to your own care team.

How sure is each claim?

Research claims carry a label: Established Early evidence Hypothesis. Small numbers link to the Sources list.
